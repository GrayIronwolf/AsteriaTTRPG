# Increase Number of Charges

## Overview
Increases the number of charges available to Artificer devices.

## Rank 1
Increase available charges for eligible Artificer creations.

## Rank 2
Improve charge storage and field reliability.

## Rank 3
Expand charge capacity for more advanced devices.

## Rank 4
Boost charge retention for larger inventions.

## Rank 5
Maximize charge capacity for masterwork Artificer devices.

## Prerequisites
Gadgeteer's Gambit or an Artificer device feature

## Scaling
Increases the number of charges available to Artificer devices.

## Synergy
Supports gadgets, reactors, enchanted tools, and charge-based inventions.
