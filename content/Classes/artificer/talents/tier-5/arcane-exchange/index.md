# Arcane Exchange

## Overview
Transmutation scope, safety, and value ceiling improve by rank.

## Rank 1
Transmute simple and common materials through equivalent exchange.

## Rank 2
Improve exchange efficiency and material range.

## Rank 3
Transmute more complex crafted or refined materials.

## Rank 4
Work with rare materials under stricter exchange limits.

## Rank 5
Master high-tier Arcane Exchange with GM-approved limits.

## Prerequisites
Arcane Reactor Rank 4 or Scaling Creation Rank 4
