## Description
A substantial travelling meal consisting of dense hard bread, generous strips of salted and dried jerky, and a large portion of dried fruit. Sized specifically for Large creatures and humanoids, the ration provides a filling meal while remaining durable enough for extended travel.
A standard Large Food Ration contains:

- **Hard Bread** — A large portion of dense baked bread designed to remain edible during extended travel.
- **Jerky** — A large portion of salted and dried meat providing the main protein portion of the meal.
- **Dried Fruit** — A large portion of preserved fruit providing sweetness and variety while remaining suitable for long-term travel.

## Lore
A heavy travelling provision prepared for the larger peoples and creatures of Asteria, commonly stocked by expeditions expecting sizeable companions.

### Sustenance
One complete **Large Food Ration provides enough food for one Large-sized creature or humanoid for one meal**.

### Storage & Spoilage
Large Food Rations are specifically prepared for travel and remain edible considerably longer than fresh food when properly stored.

Exposure to the following can reduce their usable lifespan:

- Water or excessive moisture
- Extreme heat
- Insects and vermin
- Contaminated storage
- Damaged packaging
- Magical or environmental corruption

A ration that becomes sufficiently spoiled or contaminated is no longer safe to consume.
