## Description
A small travelling meal consisting of dense hard bread, strips of salted and dried jerky, and a portion of dried fruit. Sized specifically for Small creatures and humanoids, the ration provides a dependable meal while remaining compact and lightweight for travel.
A standard Small Food Ration contains:

- **Hard Bread** — A small portion of dense baked bread designed to remain edible during extended travel.
- **Jerky** — A small portion of salted and dried meat providing the main protein portion of the meal.
- **Dried Fruit** — A small portion of preserved fruit providing sweetness and variety while remaining lightweight and easy to store.

## Lore
A common provision prepared for the smaller peoples and creatures of Asteria, particularly those travelling far from settled lands.

### Sustenance
One complete **Small Food Ration provides enough food for one Small-sized creature or humanoid for one meal**.

### Storage & Spoilage
Small Food Rations are specifically prepared for travel and remain edible considerably longer than fresh food when properly stored.

Exposure to the following can reduce their usable lifespan:

- Water or excessive moisture
- Extreme heat
- Insects and vermin
- Contaminated storage
- Damaged packaging
- Magical or environmental corruption

A ration that becomes sufficiently spoiled or contaminated is no longer safe to consume.
