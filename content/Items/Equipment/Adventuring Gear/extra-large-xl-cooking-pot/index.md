## 📝 Description

A massive iron cooking pot with a deep, broad body and heavily reinforced carrying handle for suspension over a large cooking fire. Designed for substantial group cooking, its considerable capacity makes it ideal for large adventuring parties, caravans, military camps, and expeditions.

## 📜 Lore

These imposing cooking pots are commonly found in large camps where feeding many hungry travellers quickly is more important than travelling light.

### Cooking

An Extra Large Cooking Pot can be placed over or suspended above a substantial heat source to prepare large quantities of food.

It can be used for:

- Cooking large stews
- Cooking soups
- Boiling vegetables
- Heating prepared food
- Preparing grains
- Making broth
- Boiling large quantities of water
- Preparing hot drinks
- Cooking meals for large adventuring parties
- Preparing large batches of food

### Capacity

The pot can hold approximately **12 litres** of liquid or food.

Under normal circumstances, this is sufficient to prepare approximately **12–16 standard Medium-sized servings**, depending on the food being prepared.

The actual number of servings may vary according to creature size, recipe, ingredients, and portion requirements.

### Water Boiling

The Extra Large Cooking Pot can be used to boil up to approximately **12 litres of water** when an appropriate heat source is available.

Boiling may make naturally sourced water safer to consume where heat treatment is sufficient, but it does **not** automatically remove:

- Poisons
- Chemical contamination
- Heavy metals
- Magical contamination
- Curses
- Other supernatural effects

### Heat Source

The pot requires a substantial and sustained source of heat, such as:

- Large campfire
- Dedicated cooking fire
- Large stove
- Large bed of hot coals
- Suitable magical heat source

Due to its considerable size and volume, a small campfire may be insufficient to heat a fully loaded XL Cooking Pot effectively.

### Transport

An XL Cooking Pot is technically portable but is significantly heavier and bulkier than smaller cooking pots.

It is most practical when transported using:

- Pack animals
- Carts
- Wagons
- Expedition equipment
- Multiple party members
- Creatures capable of carrying heavy loads

### Environmental Limitations

Cooking may become more difficult when exposed to:

- Heavy rain
- Strong wind
- Extreme cold
- Insufficient fuel
- Unstable terrain
- Poorly maintained fires

Shelter or specialised cooking equipment may be required in severe conditions.

### Limitations

An Extra Large Cooking Pot provides exceptional cooking capacity at the cost of considerable weight and bulk.

It is best suited to large adventuring parties, caravans, expedition camps, military groups, and semi-permanent camps where feeding many creatures outweighs the difficulty of transporting the pot.
