## Description
A 30-metre length of tightly twisted hemp fibre woven into a strong, flexible rope. Its extended length makes it particularly useful for climbing, hauling, securing large loads, constructing shelters, and navigating difficult terrain where shorter ropes may be insufficient.

## Lore
Long coils of hemp rope are favoured by explorers, sailors, climbers, labourers, and adventurers expecting difficult or unpredictable terrain.

### Common Uses
Hemp Rope can be used for a wide variety of adventuring and everyday tasks, including:

- Climbing
- Descending
- Securing equipment
- Tying loads
- Creating basic shelters
- Constructing temporary structures
- Making basic traps
- Tethering animals
- Hauling equipment
- Crossing difficult terrain
- Creating rope lines
- Binding objects

### Environmental Limitations

Hemp Rope is a natural fibre product and can deteriorate when repeatedly exposed to poor conditions.

Potential hazards include:

- Prolonged water exposure
- Fire
- Extreme heat
- Sharp surfaces
- Heavy abrasion
- Excessive loads
- Rot
- Chemical damage
- Magical or environmental corruption

Wet hemp rope should be properly dried before long-term storage to reduce the risk of deterioration.

### Limitations

Hemp Rope is strong and dependable but is not indestructible.

Damaged, heavily frayed, burned, rotten, or overloaded rope may weaken or break and should be repaired or replaced before being relied upon for climbing or load-bearing tasks.
