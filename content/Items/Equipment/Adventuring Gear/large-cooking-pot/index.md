## Description
A large iron cooking pot with a deep, broad body and reinforced carrying handle for suspending it over a campfire. Built for group cooking, its generous capacity allows substantial meals to be prepared for adventuring parties, expedition camps, military groups, and travelling communities.

## Lore
A familiar sight around larger camps, these sturdy pots have fed countless adventurers, soldiers, labourers, and travellers throughout Asteria.


### 🔧 Maintenance
- **Cleaning Requirement:** Clean thoroughly after cooking and remove food residue before storage.
- **Upkeep Needed:** Keep dry when stored and periodically oil exposed iron surfaces to reduce rust.
- **Durability Loss per Use:** Minimal during normal cooking; increased by severe overheating, heavy impacts, corrosion, or improper use.

### Cooking

A Large Cooking Pot can be placed over or suspended above a suitable heat source to prepare substantial quantities of food while travelling or camping.

It can be used for:

- Cooking stews
- Cooking soups
- Boiling vegetables
- Heating prepared food
- Preparing grains
- Making broth
- Boiling water
- Preparing hot drinks
- Cooking meals for adventuring parties
- Preparing large batches of food

### Capacity

The pot can hold approximately **6 litres** of liquid or food.

Under normal circumstances, this is sufficient to prepare approximately **6–8 standard Medium-sized servings**, depending on the food being prepared.

The actual number of servings may vary according to creature size, recipe, ingredients, and portion requirements.

### Water Boiling

The Large Cooking Pot can be used to boil up to approximately **6 litres of water** when an appropriate heat source is available.

Boiling may make naturally sourced water safer to consume where heat treatment is sufficient, but it does **not** automatically remove:

- Poisons
- Chemical contamination
- Heavy metals
- Magical contamination
- Curses
- Other supernatural effects

### Heat Source

The pot requires an appropriate source of heat, such as:

- Campfire
- Cooking fire
- Stove
- Hot coals
- Suitable magical heat source

Due to its larger size, a **stronger and more sustained heat source** may be required compared with smaller cooking pots.

### Environmental Limitations

Cooking may become more difficult when exposed to:

- Heavy rain
- Strong wind
- Extreme cold
- Insufficient fuel
- Unstable terrain
- Poorly maintained fires

Shelter or specialised cooking equipment may be required in severe conditions.

### Limitations

A Large Cooking Pot provides substantial cooking capacity but is considerably heavier and bulkier than Small or Medium Cooking Pots.

It is best suited to adventuring parties, expedition camps, caravans, and other groups where its increased capacity outweighs the additional carrying weight.
