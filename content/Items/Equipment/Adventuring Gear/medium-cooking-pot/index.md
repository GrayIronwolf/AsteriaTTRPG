## Description

A sturdy iron cooking pot with a broad body and reinforced carrying handle for suspending it over a campfire. Its moderate capacity provides enough room for preparing meals for several travellers while remaining practical to carry as part of an adventuring group's camp equipment.

## 📜 Lore

A dependable piece of cookware commonly carried by adventuring parties, travelling families, merchants, soldiers, hunters, and expedition camps throughout Asteria.

### Cooking

A Medium Cooking Pot can be placed over or suspended above a suitable heat source to prepare food while travelling or camping.

It can be used for:

- Cooking stews
- Cooking soups
- Boiling vegetables
- Heating prepared food
- Preparing grains
- Making broth
- Boiling water
- Preparing hot drinks
- Cooking meals for small groups
- Other basic camp cooking

### Capacity

The pot can hold approximately **3 litres** of liquid or food.

Under normal circumstances, this is sufficient to prepare approximately **3–4 standard Medium-sized servings**, depending on the food being prepared.

The actual number of servings may vary according to creature size, recipe, ingredients, and portion requirements.

### Water Boiling

The Medium Cooking Pot can be used to boil up to approximately **3 litres of water** when an appropriate heat source is available.

Boiling may make naturally sourced water safer to consume where heat treatment is sufficient, but it does **not** automatically remove:

- Poisons
- Chemical contamination
- Heavy metals
- Magical contamination
- Curses
- Other supernatural effects

### Heat Source

The pot requires an appropriate source of heat, such as:

- Campfire
- Cooking fire
- Stove
- Hot coals
- Suitable magical heat source

### Environmental Limitations

Cooking may become more difficult when exposed to:

- Heavy rain
- Strong wind
- Extreme cold
- Insufficient fuel
- Unstable terrain
- Poorly maintained fires

Shelter or specialised cooking equipment may be required in severe conditions.

### Limitations

A Medium Cooking Pot provides a practical balance between **cooking capacity and portability**.

It is well suited to small adventuring groups but may require multiple batches when preparing meals for larger parties or Large-sized creatures.
