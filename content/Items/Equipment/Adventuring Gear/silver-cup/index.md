# 🥛 Silver Cup

## 📝 Description

A solid silver drinking cup with a smooth polished surface, gently flared rim, and weighted base. Simple shaping and restrained detailing make it suitable for everyday use among wealthy households while remaining elegant enough for feasts, ceremonies, and formal occasions.

## 📜 Lore

Silver cups are common symbols of comfortable wealth, frequently found in noble households, prosperous taverns, temples, and ceremonial collections.

### Tools Required
- Forge
- Crucible
- Casting Mould
- Smithing Hammer
- Tongs
- Metalworking Tools
- Polishing Tools

### Crafting Difficulty
- **Skill Check:** Smithing
- **Required Profession & Rank:** Blacksmith — Novice
- **Failure Consequences:** Uneven shaping, cracks, excessive material loss, poor balance, cosmetic defects, or reduced durability.

---

### 🔧 Maintenance

- **Cleaning Requirement:** Wash after use and remove residue before storage.
- **Upkeep Needed:** Periodically polish the silver to remove tarnishing and maintain its appearance.
- **Durability Loss per Use:** Negligible during normal use; increased by heavy impacts, crushing, extreme heat, or improper handling.

### Drinking Vessel

A Silver Cup can hold approximately **300 mL of liquid** and is suitable for serving:

- Water
- Ale
- Wine
- Mead
- Milk
- Juice
- Herbal drinks
- Other consumable liquids

The cup provides no inherent benefit to the liquid it contains unless modified through craftsmanship, alchemy, magic, or enchantment.

---

## ✨ Enchantment Use

As a crafted metal object, a Silver Cup may be used as the target item for compatible enchantments.

Potential enchanted cups could be created for purposes such as:

- Purifying liquids
- Maintaining liquid temperature
- Detecting contaminants
- Ceremonial rituals
- Magical drinking effects
- Religious or arcane functions

Any enchantment applied to the cup must follow the normal **Asteria Enchanting System**, including the enchantment limitations of the material used.

---

## 💰 Material Value

A significant portion of a Silver Cup's value comes from the silver used in its construction.

Damaged or unwanted cups may therefore still retain value as recyclable silver and can potentially be melted down and reforged into other silver items.

The amount of usable silver recovered depends upon the item's condition and the effectiveness of the recycling or smithing process.

---

## ⚠️ Limitations

A Silver Cup is more resistant to normal wear than wooden or ceramic drinking vessels but may still:

- Dent under heavy impacts
- Scratch during rough handling
- Tarnish over time
- Bend under excessive force
- Melt when exposed to sufficient heat

Damage that does not destroy the cup may potentially be repaired by a suitable metalworker.
