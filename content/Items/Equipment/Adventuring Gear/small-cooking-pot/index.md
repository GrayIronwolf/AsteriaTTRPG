## Description
A compact iron cooking pot with a sturdy body and curved carrying handle for suspending it over a campfire. Its small capacity makes it ideal for preparing individual meals, heating water, cooking simple stews, and travelling without the weight of larger cookware.

## Lore
A practical piece of cookware commonly carried by lone travellers, scouts, hunters, small adventuring parties, and wandering cooks throughout Asteria.

### Cooking

A Small Cooking Pot can be placed over or suspended above a suitable heat source to prepare food while travelling or camping.

It can be used for:

- Cooking stews
- Cooking soups
- Boiling vegetables
- Heating prepared food
- Preparing grains
- Making broth
- Boiling water
- Preparing hot drinks
- Other basic camp cooking

### Capacity

The pot can hold approximately **1.5 litres** of liquid or food.

Under normal circumstances, this is sufficient to prepare approximately **1–2 standard Medium-sized servings**, depending on the food being prepared.

The actual number of servings may vary according to creature size, recipe, ingredients, and portion requirements.

### Water Boiling

The Small Cooking Pot can be used to boil water when an appropriate heat source is available.

Boiling may make naturally sourced water safer to consume where heat treatment is sufficient, but it does **not** automatically remove:

- Poisons
- Chemical contamination
- Heavy metals
- Magical contamination
- Curses
- Other supernatural effects

### Heat Source

The pot requires an appropriate source of heat, such as:

- Campfire
- Cooking fire
- Stove
- Hot coals
- Suitable magical heat source

### Environmental Limitations

Cooking may become more difficult when exposed to:

- Heavy rain
- Strong wind
- Extreme cold
- Insufficient fuel
- Unstable terrain
- Poorly maintained fires

Shelter or specialised cooking equipment may be required in severe conditions.

### Limitations

A Small Cooking Pot is designed primarily for lightweight camp cooking.

Its limited capacity makes it unsuitable for preparing large quantities of food for sizeable groups without cooking multiple batches.
