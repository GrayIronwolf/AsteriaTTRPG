## 📝 Description
Alpha Rat Beast Fang is a large, heavily curved fang harvested from an Alpha Rat Beast. Dense, yellowed, and exceptionally hard, these formidable teeth retain potent organic properties and are prized by alchemists, spellcasters, craftsmen, and collectors.

## ✨ Material Properties
- Large, dense, and naturally pointed.
- Exceptionally hard and resistant to chipping.
- Stronger and more durable than [[Dire Rat Fang]].
- Can be polished, drilled, carved, ground, or engraved.
- Retains potent organic properties after harvesting.
- Remains stable for long periods once properly cleaned.

## ⚒️ Crafting Use
- Alchemy component.
- Spell component.
- Advanced jewellery and pendants.
- Charms and trinkets.
- Carved ornaments.
- Trophy decorations.
- Decorative additions to weapons, armour, and equipment.
