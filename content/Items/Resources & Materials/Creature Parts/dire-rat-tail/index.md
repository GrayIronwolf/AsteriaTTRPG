## 📝 Description
Dire Rat Tail is a thick, muscular tail harvested from a Dire Rat. Dark grey and heavily segmented, it is considerably tougher than the tails of lesser rats. Its unusual biological properties make it useful to alchemists and spellcasters as a component.

## ✨ Material Properties
- Thick, muscular, and highly flexible.
- Tougher and more durable than [[Great Rat Tail]].
- Retains useful biological properties after harvesting.
- Can be dried or preserved for long-term storage.
- Valued as both an alchemical and spellcasting component.

## ⚒️ Crafting Use
- Alchemy Component.
- Spell Component.
- Can be preserved for use in alchemical preparations.
- Can be prepared for use in spellcasting.
- May also be used for trophies, charms, and decorative crafting.

## 🧪 Processing
- **Processing Required:** Clean and preserve or thoroughly dry depending on its intended use.
- **Tools Required:** Small Knife and basic preservation equipment.
- **Required Profession & Rank:** None for basic preparation.
- **Processed Into:** Preserved Dire Rat Tail or Dried Dire Rat Tail.
- **Processing Yield:** 1x Dire Rat Tail → 1x Prepared Dire Rat Tail.

## 🔮 Enchantment Use
- **Enchantment Affinity:** Low
- **Elemental Alignment:** None
- **Compatible Enchantments:** None
- **Special Properties:** Can be used as a Spell Component.
