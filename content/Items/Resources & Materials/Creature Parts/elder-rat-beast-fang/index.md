## 📝 Description
Elder Rat Beast Fang is an enormous, heavily curved fang harvested from an Elder Rat Beast. Dense, aged, and exceptionally hard, the fang retains concentrated beast essence and is highly valued by alchemists, spellcasters, Beast Tamers, and specialised craftsmen.

## ✨ Material Properties
- Enormous, dense, and naturally pointed.
- Exceptionally hard and resistant to chipping or cracking.
- Stronger and more durable than [[Alpha Rat Beast Fang]].
- Can be polished, drilled, carved, ground, or engraved.
- Retains concentrated beast essence after harvesting.
- Remains stable for extended periods once properly cleaned.

## ⚒️ Crafting Use
- Alchemy component.
- Spell component.
- Beast Tamer component.
- Advanced charms and talismans.
- Jewellery and carved ornaments.
- Trophy decorations.
- Decorative additions to weapons, armour, and equipment.
