## 📝 Description
Elder Rat Beast Tail is an enormous, heavy tail harvested from an Elder Rat Beast. Thick hide, powerful connective tissue, and a covering of coarse grey-black hairs make it remarkably durable, while the accumulated beast essence within makes it valuable to specialised practitioners.

## ✨ Material Properties
- Exceptionally long, thick, and heavy.
- Covered in sparse, coarse grey-black hairs.
- Extremely tough while retaining natural flexibility.
- More durable than [[Alpha Rat Beast Tail]].
- Retains concentrated beast essence after harvesting.
- Can be preserved for extended periods when properly prepared.

## ⚒️ Crafting Use
- Alchemy component.
- Spell component.
- Beast Tamer component.
- Advanced charms and trinkets.
- Specialised magical preparations.
- Trophy and equipment decoration.

## 🧪 Processing
- **Processing Required:** Thoroughly clean, treat, and preserve.
- **Tools Required:** Skinning Knife and preservation equipment.
- **Required Crafting Rank:** Journeyman
- **Processed Into:** Preserved Elder Rat Beast Tail
- **Processing Yield:** 1x Elder Rat Beast Tail → 1x Preserved Elder Rat Beast Tail
