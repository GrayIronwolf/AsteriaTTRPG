## 📝 Description

Dire Rat Hide is an exceptionally thick, dark reddish-brown pelt covered in coarse, dense fur. Its tough underlying skin is naturally resistant to tearing and puncturing, providing considerably greater protection than the hides harvested from ordinary or Great Rats.

---

## ✨ Material Properties

- Thick, dense hide with strong natural resistance to tearing.
- Tougher and more durable than [[Great Rat Hide]].
- Provides **+2 Armour Modifier** when used as the protective material of crafted armour.
- Remains reasonably flexible despite its considerable thickness.
- Well suited to rugged equipment exposed to frequent wear.

---

## ⚒️ Crafting Use

- Leather armour.
- Reinforced protective clothing.
- Bracers, boots, gloves, and other protective equipment.
- Heavy-duty straps, harnesses, packs, and equipment components.
- Can be processed into [[Dire Rat Leather]].

---

## 🧪 Processing

- **Processing Required:** Clean, scrape, cure, and thoroughly tan the hide.
- **Tools Required:** Skinning Knife, Scraping Knife, Tanning Rack.
- **Required Profession & Rank:** Leatherworking — Apprentice.
- **Processed Into:** [[Dire Rat Leather]]
- **Processing Yield:** 1x Dire Rat Hide → 1x Dire Rat Leather.

---

## 🔮 Enchantment Use

- **Enchantment Affinity:** Low
- **Elemental Alignment:** None
- **Compatible Enchantments:** Basic defensive and reinforcement enchantments.
- **Special Properties:** Its dense structure provides a more stable enchanting base than lesser rat hides.
