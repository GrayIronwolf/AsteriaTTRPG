## 📝 Description
Dire Rat Leather is a thick, dark reddish-brown leather produced from carefully tanned Dire Rat Hide. Tougher than lesser rat leathers while retaining good flexibility, it provides a durable protective material suitable for reliable armour and rugged adventuring equipment.

## ✨ Material Properties
- Thick, tough, and naturally resilient.
- Provides **+1 Armour Modifier** when used as the protective material of crafted armour.
- Resistant to tearing, puncturing, and general wear.
- More flexible and workable than unprocessed [[Dire Rat Hide]].
- Maintains good durability when shaped into fitted equipment.

## ⚒️ Crafting Use
- Leather armour.
- Reinforced protective clothing.
- Bracers, boots, gloves, and other protective equipment.
- Durable packs, harnesses, straps, and adventuring equipment.
- Reinforced components for Leatherworking projects.
