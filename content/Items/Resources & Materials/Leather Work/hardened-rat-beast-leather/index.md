## 📝 Description

Hardened Beast Rat Leather is armour-grade leather produced from the treated leather of Alpha or Elder Rat Beasts. Through additional hardening, the thick leather becomes considerably more rigid and resistant to impact while retaining enough flexibility for protective equipment.

---

## ✨ Material Properties

- Thick, tough armour-grade leather.
- Provides **+1 Armour Modifier** when used as an armour material.
- More rigid and impact-resistant than untreated Beast Rat Leather.
- Retains enough flexibility for fitted armour and protective equipment.
- Resistant to tearing, splitting, and general wear.
- Can be cut, shaped, layered, and reinforced during crafting.
- Can be produced from either Alpha or Elder Rat Beast Leather.

---

## ⚒️ Crafting Use

- Leather armour.
- Reinforced clothing.
- Bracers and greaves.
- Protective gloves and boots.
- Armour reinforcement.
- Shields and protective coverings.
- Durable equipment components.

## 🧪 Processing

- **Processing Required:** Beast Rat Leather must be soaked, treated, shaped, hardened, and thoroughly dried.
- **Tools Required:** Leatherworking Tools, Hardening Solution, Moulds or Forms, and Drying Equipment.
- **Required Crafting Rank:** Journeyman
- **Processed Into:** Hardened Beast Rat Leather
- **Processing Yield:** 1x suitable Beast Rat Leather → 1x Hardened Beast Rat Leather
