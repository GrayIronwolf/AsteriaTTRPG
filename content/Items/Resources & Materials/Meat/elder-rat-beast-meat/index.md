## 📝 Description
Elder Rat Beast Meat is a heavy portion of dense, dark red flesh harvested from an Elder Rat Beast. Exceptionally tough and strongly flavoured, the meat retains concentrated organic properties accumulated throughout the creature's long life, making it valuable to both experienced cooks and alchemists.

## ✨ Material Properties
- Dense, heavy, and exceptionally tough.
- Dark red in colour with a strong flavour.
- More substantial than [[Alpha Rat Beast Meat]].
- Highly perishable when left untreated.
- Requires thorough preparation before consumption.
- Can be dried, smoked, salted, or otherwise preserved.
- Retains concentrated organic properties useful in alchemy.

## ⚒️ Crafting Use
- Alchemy component.
- Advanced cooked meat dishes.
- Stews, soups, and broths.
- Dried or smoked travel rations.
- Ingredient for Cooking recipes.
- Animal feed where appropriate.

## 🧪 Processing
- **Processing Required:** Clean and cook, dry, smoke, salt, or otherwise preserve.
- **Tools Required:** Knife and appropriate cooking or preservation equipment.
- **Required Crafting Rank:** Journeyman
- **Processed Into:** Cooked Elder Rat Beast Meat, Preserved Elder Rat Beast Meat, or appropriate food recipes.
- **Processing Yield:** 1x Elder Rat Beast Meat → 1x prepared portion.
