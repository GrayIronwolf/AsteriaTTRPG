## 📝 Description
Great Rat Meat is a sizeable portion of dark red meat harvested from a Great Rat. Tougher and stronger in flavour than ordinary rat meat, it is rarely considered desirable fare, though proper preparation can turn it into a filling and inexpensive meal.

## ✨ Material Properties
- Dense, tough raw meat.
- Provides a larger portion than ordinary [[Rat Meat]].
- Highly perishable when left untreated.
- Requires thorough cleaning and cooking before consumption.
- Can be preserved through drying, smoking, or salting.

## ⚒️ Crafting Use
- Cooked meat dishes.
- Stews, soups, and broths.
- Dried or smoked travel rations.
- Animal feed where appropriate.
- Ingredient for basic Cooking recipes.

## 🧪 Processing
- **Processing Required:** Clean and cook, dry, smoke, salt, or otherwise preserve the meat.
- **Tools Required:** Knife and appropriate cooking or preservation equipment.
- **Required Profession & Rank:** Cooking — Novice
- **Processed Into:** Cooked Great Rat Meat, Dried Great Rat Meat, or appropriate food recipes.
- **Processing Yield:** 1x Great Rat Meat → 1x prepared portion.
