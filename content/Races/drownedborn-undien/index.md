## Racial Traits
### Amphibious Nature
Uniden can breathe both air and water, easily adapting between land and aquatic environments.

### Waterborne Healing
When immersed in a natural source of water (like rivers or rain), Undines are able to regenerate health quicker:
- In combat: +3 HP per round  
- Out of combat: +10 HP per round 

---
### Drowned Soul
#### Description
The sea carries countless memories of those it has claimed. Drownedborn Undien possess an innate connection to these lingering echoes.

#### Gameplay Effect
- Gain +2 on Spirit-related checks.
- Can sense locations where death by drowning has occurred.
- Gain advantage when interacting with water-bound spirits.

---
### Echoes of the Lost
#### Description
Water remembers. By touching natural bodies of water, a Drownedborn may perceive fragments of memories carried within the current.

#### Gameplay Effect
- Gain advantage when investigating shipwrecks, submerged ruins, and missing persons cases.
- The GM may provide visions, emotions, sounds, or symbolic clues tied to events that occurred in nearby waters.
- Receive a +2 bonus to Investigation checks involving aquatic mysteries.

---

### Call of the Depths

#### Description
The ocean whispers warnings and secrets to its children.

#### Gameplay Effect
- Gain +2 Perception while within 30 metres of a natural body of water.
- Detect hidden submerged objects within 9 metres.
- Gain advantage when searching for underwater structures, shipwrecks, or hidden passages.
