# Earth Pixie

## Overview
# Pixie Lore

Pixies are among the **oldest manifested fae** known to exist, born not of flesh but of **Avalon’s will and magic**. They are living expressions of elemental harmony, curiosity, and the unstable boundary between realms.

Though small in stature, pixies have left **outsized marks on history**, often unnoticed by mortals until centuries later.

---

## Origins of the Pixie

Pixies first emerged during the **Early Bloom of Avalon**, when the Fae Realm stabilised after its initial formation. Unlike other fae who formed courts or hierarchies, pixies manifested as **free-roaming spirits**, each bound to a single elemental truth.

Pixies were never meant to rule, conquer, or guide.  
They were created to **observe, influence, and subtly correct** the flow of magic between realms.

From their first moments, pixies were incomplete.

Each pixie was born with **half a soul**, the other half forming separately as a **Sprite**. Together they form a complete metaphysical whole, though separated by form and purpose.

---

## The Split Soul Doctrine

Pixie lore teaches that a soul divided can better perceive reality.

- Pixies experience the **active world** — emotion, action, impulse
- Sprites experience the **reflective world** — memory, intuition, balance

Ancient pixies believe this division prevents corruption, arrogance, and stagnation. A whole soul, they claim, grows rigid. A split soul remains adaptable.

This belief has never been universally accepted among the fae.

---

## The Worldroot Gate (Tree Portal)

Pixies do not freely cross between realms.

Instead, they rely on a living construct known as the **Worldroot Gate** — an immense, ancient tree whose roots exist simultaneously in **Avalon** and **Asteria**.

### Key Facts
- The gate opens **once every 50 years**
- It remains open for **a single full lunar cycle**
- Only pixies, sprites, and bonded fae may pass freely
- Mortals who attempt passage rarely survive unchanged

To pixies, this event is known as **The Crossing Bloom**.

---

## The Crossing Bloom

The Crossing Bloom is a time of:
- Celebration
- Mourning
- Oaths
- Long journeys

Pixies born in Avalon may choose to:
- Visit Asteria
- Remain behind permanently
- Escort bonded sprites or fae envoys
- Observe the state of magic in the mortal realm

Pixies who pass through often do not return for centuries — if at all.

Those who miss the Crossing Bloom must wait **another 50 years**, a delay that can feel unbearable to beings defined by curiosity.

---

## Pixies in Asterian History

Pixies rarely appear in written mortal records, but their influence is undeniable.

They prefer to act as:
- Whispered guides
- Unseen helpers
- Saboteurs of great evils
- Inspirers of invention and art

Many pivotal moments in Asterian history coincide with **Crossing Bloom years**, though few mortals recognise the pattern.

---

## Notable Pixie Figures

### ✦ Queen Lythiriel of the Shimmergrove
The only pixie ever acknowledged as a **queen**.

Lythiriel united scattered pixie enclaves during a time when the Worldroot Gate was damaged by planar instability. Her reign lasted only one Crossing Bloom, after which she dissolved her crown and vanished into Avalon’s upper canopies.

Pixies still swear rare oaths **“by Lythiriel’s Leaves.”**

---

### ✦ King Bramblethorn (The Unwilling King)
A title bestowed by mortals, not pixies.

Bramblethorn led a resistance against a demonic incursion that attempted to corrupt the Worldroot Gate during a Crossing Bloom. Though victorious, he rejected all authority afterward and shattered his staff into seeds scattered across Asteria.

Some believe those seeds still grow into **warded trees**.

---

### ✦ Nyx-of-the-Falling-Ash
A pixie hero bound to fire.

Nyx is credited with:
- Diverting a volcanic eruption
- Burning a lich’s phylactery without destroying the soul cycle
- Teaching early fire-mages restraint

Nyx never returned to Avalon and is believed to have **faded into pure flame**.

---

## Pixie Artisans & Crafters

Pixies rarely craft physical goods, but when they do, their work is legendary.

### ✦ The Glimmerhands
A collective of pixie artisans known for crafting:
- Ever-burning lanterns
- Floating instruments
- Soul-safe crystal foci

Each item crafted by a Glimmerhand is said to contain a **single truthful memory** of its maker.

---

### ✦ Thistlewick the Binder
An eccentric pixie who specialised in **binding magic to form**.

Thistlewick helped design early enchantment frameworks still used by Asterian artificers today. Many do not realise the “odd inefficiencies” in these designs were intentional safety limits.

---

## Pixies & Mortals

Pixies regard mortals with fascination and caution.

They admire:
- Creativity
- Defiance
- Emotional depth

They fear:
- Obsession
- Exploitation of magic
- Attempts to control the Worldroot Gate

Pixies may aid mortals they find interesting, but will abandon those who attempt to command or bind them.

---

## Modern Pixie Beliefs

In the current age, pixies believe:
- The realms are drifting further apart
- Crossing Blooms feel more dangerous
- Magic in Asteria is becoming volatile

Some whisper that the Worldroot Gate may **one day fail entirely**.

If that happens, pixies will be forced to choose:
- Remain forever in Avalon  
- Or become permanent residents of the mortal realm

---

## Final Words of Pixie Lore

> — Old Pixie Proverb

## Racial Traits
### Magical Affinity (Innate)
- Born with **100 Affinity** in one elemental type  
- Cannot be changed or reduced  
- Reduced backlash from aligned magic  

### Racial Bond – Split Soul
Pixies are born with **half a soul**.

- The other half manifests as a **Sprite**
- Pixie and Sprite can sense one another across great distances
- When within close proximity, magic flows more smoothly

If the bond is damaged or severed, the Pixie and/or Sprite becomes **Unstable**, suffering narrative and mechanical consequences.

### Elemental Embodiment
- Emotions influence environment slightly  
- Enhances nearby aligned magic  
- Discomfort when exposed to opposing elements  

### Fae Perception
- Detect:
  - Illusions  
  - Glamours  
  - Magical distortions  
- Strong intuition for hidden intent  
- Struggles with rigid systems or logic
