## Racial Traits
### Flow Sense

#### Description
Flowborn can instinctively feel the movement of water around them. Rivers, streams, rainfall, and currents act as extensions of their awareness.

#### Gameplay Effect
- Gain +2 Perception while within 30 metres of flowing water.
- Can determine the direction of nearby water sources.
- Gain advantage on Survival checks involving rivers, streams, and wetlands.
- Cannot become naturally lost while following a waterway.

---

### Spirit of Renewal

#### Description
Flowborn embody the restorative nature of rivers and freshwater springs.

#### Gameplay Effect
- Healing effects received are increased by 10%.
- Recover Stamina 25% faster during short rests.
- Gain +1 to all healing magic rolls.
- Natural healing is doubled while resting near fresh water.

---

### River Walker

#### Description
The currents support the Flowborn wherever they travel.

#### Gameplay Effect
- Ignore movement penalties caused by shallow water, mud, marshes, and wetlands.
- Gain advantage on Balance checks.
- Swim speed equals double normal movement speed.
- Can move across flowing water surfaces for up to 3 seconds before sinking.

---

### Breath of the Current

#### Description
Flowborn are equally comfortable above and below the water's surface.

#### Gameplay Effect
- Can breathe underwater indefinitely.
- Ignore underwater combat penalties.
- Gain resistance to exhaustion caused by swimming.
- Never suffer penalties from strong river currents.
