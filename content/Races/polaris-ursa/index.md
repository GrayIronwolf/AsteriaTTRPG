# Polaris Ursa

## Overview
### Population

**Current Population:** 10,000–12,000

The Polaris Ursa are considered a declining race. Though still respected throughout the frozen regions of Asteria, their numbers have never fully recovered from the devastation of the Great War approximately 1,000 years ago.

Before the war, the Polaris Ursa population was estimated between **30,000 and 35,000 individuals**, spread across numerous clans, settlements, and noble families throughout the southern polar regions of Altarin.

The war resulted in:

- The destruction of dozens of clans
- The loss of countless settlements
- The extinction of many noble bloodlines
- The near-collapse of Polaris Ursa civilization

Today every Polaris Ursa birth is celebrated as a blessing from Nevara and a sign that their people continue to endure.

---

### Homeland

**Original Homeland:** Altarin

**Secondary Homeland:** Noran

All Polaris Ursa were originally born and raised within the frozen southern continent of **Altarin**, where they lived alongside the Ice Elves for thousands of years.

Following the Great War, the surviving leaders feared that keeping their remaining population concentrated within a single continent could eventually lead to their extinction.

To preserve the future of their people, the surviving clans divided their population between two continents.

Today the Polaris Ursa maintain homes in both Altarin and Noran.

---

### The Four Great Clans

Only four major clans survived the Great War.

These clans became the foundation upon which modern Polaris Ursa civilization was rebuilt.

#### Frostclaw Clan
**Ruling Family:** House Frostbjorn

Known for:
- Leadership
- Nobility
- Military command
- Diplomacy

The Frostclaw Clan is considered the oldest and most politically influential of the surviving clans.

---

#### Glacierheart Clan

Known for:

- Lorekeepers
- Runecrafters

The Glacierheart Clan preserves much of the race's surviving history, traditions, and religious teachings.

---

#### Snowshadow Clan
**Ruling Family:** House Skjoldr

Known for:
- Hunters
- Rangers
- Scouts
- Explorers

The Snowshadow Clan serves as the eyes and ears of the Polaris Ursa people.

---

#### Stormmaw Clan
**Ruling Family:** House Hrimvald

Known for:
- Warriors

- Monster hunters

The Stormmaw Clan led much of the migration to Noran and remains renowned for its courage and martial traditions.

### Creation Myth

According to Polaris Ursa tradition, the **Ice Elves were the first children of Nevara**, Goddess of Winter.

The Ice Elves were gifted beauty, wisdom, magic, and longevity.

Yet Nevara soon realized that her first children lacked the physical strength necessary to defend the frozen world she had created.

From the heart of a glacier, the breath of a blizzard, and the spirit of the polar winds, she forged her second children:

**The Polaris Ursa.**

Where the Ice Elves were graceful, the Polaris Ursa were mighty.

Where the Ice Elves were scholars, the Polaris Ursa were guardians.

Where the Ice Elves preserved knowledge, the Polaris Ursa protected it.

Together they became the Twin Children of Winter.

---

### Sacred Relationship with the Ice Elves

The Polaris Ursa do not view the Ice Elves as rivals.

Instead they refer to them as:

> **The Firstborn of Winter**

Many Ice Elves in turn refer to the Polaris Ursa as:

> **The Wardens of Winter**

For thousands of years the two races lived side by side throughout the frozen regions of Altarin, forming one of the oldest alliances in Asterian history.

---

### The Great War

Approximately **1,000 years ago**, the Great War devastated the southern polar regions of Altarin.

The conflict nearly wiped out both the Ice Elves and the Polaris Ursa.

Entire settlements disappeared beneath ice and snow.

Ancient temples were destroyed.

Countless bloodlines vanished forever.

By the war's conclusion, the Polaris Ursa population had fallen from approximately **30,000–35,000 individuals** to fewer than **2,000 survivors**.

Only four great clans remained.

---

### The Divine Command

Following the war, the surviving shamans and priests of the Aetherion Faith gathered to seek guidance.

Legend states that Nevara herself appeared to them through dreams and visions.

She warned that if all her remaining children stayed together, another catastrophe could one day destroy them entirely.

At that same time, an Ice Elf Prince was preparing an expedition to the distant northern continent of Noran.

Nevara commanded:

> *"Let winter take root in two lands, so that neither may perish."*

Obeying her decree, the Polaris Ursa divided their remaining population.

#### Remained in Altarin
- Frostclaw Clan
- Glacierheart Clan

#### Journeyed to Noran
- Snowshadow Clan
- Stormmaw Clan

Accompanied by the Ice Elf Prince and his followers, the northern clans crossed the seas and established new settlements upon the frozen continent of Noran.

---

### Modern Beliefs

The Polaris Ursa believe they have two sacred responsibilities:

#### Preserve the Legacy of Altarin
The homeland of their ancestors and birthplace of their people.

#### Protect the Future of Noran
The refuge granted to them by Nevara after the Great War.

This belief is reflected in one of their most famous sayings:

> *"One paw remains in the south. One paw stands in the north."*

A proverb symbolizing the eternal bond between the Polaris Ursa of Altarin and Noran.

---

> *"We endure the storm. We honor our ancestors. We protect what is sacred."*
>
> — Ancient Polaris Ursa Oath

## Racial Features
- Height Range:
  - 180 cm to 230 cm (Head to Foot)

- Weight Range:
  - 140 kg to 300 kg  

- Fur:
  - Thick, dense fur designed for extreme cold survival  
  - Colour Range:
    - White → cream → pale grey  
    - Often tinted with faint blue or silver  

- Skin:
  - Tough, insulated beneath fur  
  - Colour Range:
    - Deep black → bluish-grey  

- Eyes:
  - Calm, heavy-lidded with strong focus  
  - Colour Range:
    - Ice blue → grey → silver → pale brown  

- Hands:
  - Large, powerful hands with thick claws  
  - Claws can be used for:
    - Climbing  
    - Combat (natural weapon potential)  

- Face:
  - Broad facial structure with strong jawline  
  - Heavy brow ridge giving a stern expression  

- Ears:
  - Rounded and fur-covered  
  - Highly sensitive to environmental sounds  

- Markings:
  - Often decorated with:
    - Bone beads  
    - Carved tusks  
    - Runic tattoos (ash + oil pigments)  

- Body:
  - Massive, muscular build  
  - Built for endurance and strength  
  - Movement is heavy but controlled

## Racial Traits
### Frostborn Resilience
- Resistant to cold damage  
- Immune to:
  - Natural frostbite  
  - Hypothermia  

### Ice Strider
- No movement penalties on:
  - Snow  
  - Ice  
- Cannot slip on frozen terrain  

### Polar Endurance
- Advantage on saving throws against:
  - Exhaustion  
  - Starvation  
  - Extreme cold  

### Mighty Swimmer
- Swim speed: 15m  
- Can hold breath for up to 20 minutes  

### Guardian’s Fury
- When an ally within 3m is attacked:
  - Gain advantage on next melee attack against attacker
