# Artificer Discipline

## Overview
Crafting-related D100 skill checks improve as the discipline ranks up.

## Rank 1
Gain +5 to all crafting-related D100 skill checks.

## Rank 2
Increase the discipline bonus and broaden reliable crafting applications.

## Rank 3
Improve complex crafting, salvaging, and analysis checks.

## Rank 4
Strengthen advanced creation workflows and specialist crafting checks.

## Rank 5
Master the discipline of creation across Artificer systems.

## Prerequisites
None

## Scaling
Crafting-related D100 skill checks improve as the discipline ranks up.

## Synergy
Foundation talent for crafting, smithing, alchemy, enchanting, runecrafting, salvaging, construct creation, reverse engineering, and item analysis.
