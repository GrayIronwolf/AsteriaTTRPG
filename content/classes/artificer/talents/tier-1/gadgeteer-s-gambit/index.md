# Gadgeteer's Gambit

## Overview
Adds stronger and more flexible mechanical effects to crafted items by rank.

## Rank 1
Unlock Gadgeteering and add 1 simple mechanical effect to a crafted item.

## Rank 2
Improve gadget reliability and add more practical mechanical options.

## Rank 3
Create more complex linked gadget functions.

## Rank 4
Build advanced multi-step device effects.

## Rank 5
Master compact gadget systems and high-complexity mechanical effects.

## Prerequisites
None

## Scaling
Adds stronger and more flexible mechanical effects to crafted items by rank.

## Synergy
Pairs with gadgets, triggers, timers, devices, and engineered equipment.
