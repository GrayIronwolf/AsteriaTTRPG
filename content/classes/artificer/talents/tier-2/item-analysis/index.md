# Item Analysis

## Overview
Analysis accuracy and detail improve by rank.

## Rank 1
Identify basic item function, construction, and likely use.

## Rank 2
Improve insight into materials and craftsmanship.

## Rank 3
Reveal deeper mechanical, magical, or alchemical design clues.

## Rank 4
Analyze advanced enchantments, devices, and hidden construction features.

## Rank 5
Master item diagnosis and design interpretation.

Artificer Discipline Rank 2

Analysis accuracy and detail improve by rank.
