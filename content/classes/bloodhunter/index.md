# Bloodhunter

## Overview
The Bloodhunter is unlike any other warrior. Rather than relying solely on strength, mana, or stamina, they wield **Sangramancy**—the forbidden art of blood magic. Every technique fueled through Sangramancy draws upon the emotions carried within blood, leaving behind a lingering resonance known as **Blood Points (BP)**.

Blood Points do not represent physical blood. Instead, they represent the emotional burden accumulated through repeated use of Sangramancy. Rage, hatred, fear, sorrow, vengeance, and countless other emotions become trapped within the Bloodhunter's soul.

Learning Sangramancy is not simply mastering blood magic—it is mastering yourself.

Every Bloodhunter talent generates Blood Points unless otherwise stated.

---

# Blood Point Limit

Every Bloodhunter has a maximum Blood Point Limit representing how much emotional resonance they can safely contain before losing control.

As a Bloodhunter grows stronger, they do not generate fewer Blood Points. Instead, they learn to withstand greater emotional burdens before their mind finally fractures.

| Progression | Maximum Blood Points |
|------------|---------------------:|
| Bloodhunter | 20 BP |
| Tier II | 35 BP |
| Tier III | 50 BP |
| Tier IV | 70 BP |
| Tier V | 100 BP |

Some Bloodhunter talents and magical equipment may further increase this limit.

---

# Gaining Blood Points

Every Bloodhunter talent lists how many Blood Points it generates.

As a general guideline:

- Minor techniques generate **2–5 BP**
- Standard techniques generate **5–8 BP**
- Advanced techniques generate **8–12 BP**
- Legendary techniques generate **10–20 BP**

Blood Points are accumulated immediately after successfully activating an ability unless stated otherwise.

---

# Recovering Blood Points

Blood Points are **persistent** and do not disappear when combat ends.

### End of Combat

- Blood Points remain unchanged.

### Short Rest

- Reduce your current Blood Points by **25% (rounded up).**

### Long Rest

- Reduce your current Blood Points to **0**.

Certain Bloodhunter talents, relics, rituals, or blessings may restore additional Blood Points.

---

# Bloodlust

When a Bloodhunter's current Blood Points exceed their maximum Blood Point Limit, the emotions carried within the blood finally overwhelm their own.

The Bloodhunter immediately enters a state known as **Bloodlust**.

Bloodlust is not blind rage.

It is absolute emotional detachment.

The Bloodhunter becomes unnaturally calm. Their breathing slows. Their movements become precise. Compassion, hesitation, and mercy disappear, replaced only by an overwhelming instinct to hunt and kill.

To witness Bloodlust is to watch someone become less human with every passing moment.

---

# Effects of Bloodlust

While Bloodlust is active:

- The player immediately loses control of their character.
- The Game Master controls the Bloodhunter until Bloodlust ends.
- The Bloodhunter identifies the greatest hostile threat they can perceive and relentlessly attempts to kill it.
- The Bloodhunter ignores pain, fear, and self-preservation.
- Any creature physically preventing them from reaching their prey becomes a valid target, including allies.
- The Bloodhunter cannot willingly perform social interactions, negotiations, or acts of mercy.
- Sangramancy abilities may still be used if they aid in eliminating the chosen prey.
- Bloodlust cannot occur outside of combat.

---

# Ending Bloodlust

Bloodlust ends immediately when one of the following occurs:

- Combat ends.
- The Bloodhunter falls unconscious.
- The Bloodhunter dies.
- A powerful magical effect specifically suppresses Bloodlust.
- Another ability or talent states that it ends Bloodlust.

Once Bloodlust ends, the Bloodhunter retains their current Blood Points unless another effect removes them.

---

# Mastering Sangramancy

Every Bloodhunter walks a dangerous path.

Some spend their lives learning to increase their Blood Point Limit.

Others develop techniques to reduce Blood Point generation.

The greatest Bloodhunters are not those who possess the most power.

They are those who can stand upon the edge of Bloodlust, stare into the darkness within themselves, and choose not to fall.
