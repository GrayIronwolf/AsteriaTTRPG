## Description

Blood Rite is the first Sangramancy technique taught to every Bloodhunter. By willingly shedding their own blood, a Bloodhunter awakens the latent magical energy carried within it, binding their life force directly to a chosen weapon. This ritual transforms an ordinary weapon into a conduit of blood magic, allowing every strike to carry supernatural power.

Unlike traditional enchantments fuelled by mana, Blood Rite demands sacrifice. The Bloodhunter must willingly wound themselves, offering part of their own vitality to awaken the rite. This sacrifice is never symbolic—the pain is real, and every activation reminds the Bloodhunter that true power always demands a price.

As the ritual takes hold, crimson runes spread across the weapon before sinking beneath its surface. To outsiders the weapon appears stained with fresh blood, while those capable of sensing magic witness violent currents of crimson energy flowing through the steel. Each strike tears not only flesh but also the life essence of the target, leaving wounds that continue to bleed long after the blow has landed.

Although Blood Rite is considered the most fundamental Sangramancy technique, mastering it is a lifelong pursuit. Every future Bloodhunter ability builds upon the discipline, sacrifice, and control first learned through this ritual.

## Effects

- As an **Action**, you may activate Blood Rite by sacrificing **5 HP**.
- Activating Blood Rite generates **5 Blood Points (BP)**.
- Blood Rite may be activated **twice per Long Rest**.
- Choose one weapon you are currently wielding.
- The chosen weapon becomes empowered with Sangramancy for **3 Combat Rounds**.
- While Blood Rite is active, the weapon deals an additional **1d6 Blood Damage** on each successful hit.
- The first successful attack against a creature each turn inflicts **Minor Bleeding**.
- Minor Bleeding deals **1d4 Blood Damage** at the start of the target's next **2 turns**.
- Reapplying Minor Bleeding refreshes its duration but does not increase its damage.
- Blood Damage dealt by Blood Rite is considered **Magical Damage**.
- Only one Blood Rite may be active at a time.
- Blood Rite immediately ends if the empowered weapon leaves your possession for more than one round.
- Blood Rite may be dismissed at any time without requiring an action.

## Lore

The first Blood Rite is remembered by every Bloodhunter. It is the moment they cease relying solely upon steel and instead place their trust in their own life force. The scar left by the ritual is traditionally never healed through magic, serving as a permanent reminder that Sangramancy is founded upon sacrifice.

Among veteran Bloodhunters, Blood Rite is often described as a conversation between the hunter and the blood itself. Every activation stirs the countless emotions carried within their lifeblood—anger, grief, determination, fear, and vengeance. With each use, these emotions linger as Blood Points, slowly testing the hunter's resolve.

Those who fail to respect the burden of Sangramancy eventually lose themselves to Bloodlust. Those who endure learn that the true enemy is not the monster before them, but the darkness growing within.

> *"The first lesson of Sangramancy is not how to bleed. It is how to stop the blood from commanding you."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor
