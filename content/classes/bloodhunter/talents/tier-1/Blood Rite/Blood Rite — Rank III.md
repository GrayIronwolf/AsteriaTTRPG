## Description

Blood Rite reaches a turning point at Rank III. No longer simply a ritual of sacrifice, it becomes a true extension of the Bloodhunter's will. The blood willingly spilled no longer merely empowers a weapon—it answers the call of its master, surging through the blade with controlled ferocity. The hunter has begun to understand that Sangramancy is not about commanding blood, but existing in harmony with the emotions carried within it.

As mastery grows, so too does the burden placed upon both body and mind. The Bloodhunter can invoke Blood Rite more frequently than before, yet every activation draws increasingly powerful emotional echoes into their soul. Hatred, grief, vengeance, and fear all linger within the growing pool of Blood Points, testing the hunter's discipline with every battle.

Veterans warn that Rank III is where many Bloodhunters fall. The rite becomes dependable enough to use in nearly every encounter, creating the dangerous illusion that Sangramancy has finally been mastered. Those who grow overconfident often discover that the blood remembers every sacrifice ever made, and eventually demands to be heard.

Only those who remain disciplined can continue walking the path without surrendering themselves to Bloodlust.

## Effects

- As an **Action**, you may activate Blood Rite by sacrificing **12 HP**.
- Activating Blood Rite generates **8 Blood Points (BP)**.
- Blood Rite may be activated **3 times per Short Rest**.
- Choose one weapon you are currently wielding.
- The chosen weapon becomes empowered with Sangramancy for **5 Combat Rounds**.
- While Blood Rite is active, the weapon deals an additional **1d8 Blood Damage** on each successful hit.
- The first successful attack against a creature each turn inflicts **Moderate Bleeding**.
- Moderate Bleeding deals **1d6 Blood Damage** at the start of the target's next **3 turns**.
- Reapplying Moderate Bleeding refreshes its duration but does not stack its damage.
- Blood Damage dealt by Blood Rite is considered **Magical Damage**.
- Only one Blood Rite may be active at a time.
- Blood Rite immediately ends if the empowered weapon leaves your possession for more than one round.
- Blood Rite may be dismissed at any time without requiring an action.

## Lore

Within every Bloodhunter Order, Rank III is known as the Threshold. It marks the point where apprentices become true hunters, trusted to undertake contracts without constant supervision. The ability to invoke Blood Rite repeatedly is seen as a privilege earned through discipline rather than a reward for strength alone.

Senior Bloodhunters teach that every drop of blood used in Sangramancy leaves behind an emotional imprint. Over time these echoes begin to accumulate, speaking louder with every battle. Some hunters describe hearing whispers at the edge of their thoughts, while others experience an unsettling calm that precedes Bloodlust.

Those who survive this stage emerge with a newfound respect for Sangramancy. They realise that true mastery will never come from greater power alone, but from maintaining control when every instinct urges them to surrender to the blood.

> *"The blood grows louder with every battle. Mastery is not silencing it—it is refusing to answer its call."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor
