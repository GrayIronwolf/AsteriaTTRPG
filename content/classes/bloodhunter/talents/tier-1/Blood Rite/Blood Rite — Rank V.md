## Description

Blood Rite has reached its ultimate form. The Bloodhunter has become one of the few living masters capable of wielding Sangramancy without hesitation, instinctively weaving blood magic into every movement of battle. The ritual that once demanded complete concentration has become as natural as drawing breath, allowing the Bloodhunter to invoke it repeatedly throughout even the longest encounters.

The sacrifice demanded is immense. A master Bloodhunter willingly opens a grievous wound to unleash overwhelming power, allowing the blood itself to flood their weapon with ancient crimson energy. Every heartbeat fuels the rite, every strike carries devastating force, and every wound inflicted leaves behind echoes of suffering that refuse to fade.

Yet no Bloodhunter ever escapes the price of Sangramancy. While others see only unmatched skill, the master knows that each activation pushes them closer to Bloodlust. Their greater understanding has not silenced the emotions carried within the blood—it has merely allowed them to shoulder a far heavier burden before those emotions threaten to consume them.

Only the greatest Bloodhunters in history have achieved this level of mastery. Their names are remembered not because they wielded Blood Rite more powerfully than anyone else, but because they stood upon the edge of Bloodlust countless times and never surrendered their humanity.

## Effects

- As an **Action**, you may activate Blood Rite by sacrificing **20 HP**.
- Activating Blood Rite generates **15 Blood Points (BP)**.
- Blood Rite may be activated **4 times per Combat**.
- Choose one weapon you are currently wielding.
- The chosen weapon becomes empowered with Sangramancy for **8 Combat Rounds**.
- While Blood Rite is active, the weapon deals an additional **1d10 Blood Damage** on each successful hit.
- The first successful attack against a creature each turn inflicts **Severe Bleeding**.
- Severe Bleeding deals **1d10 Blood Damage** at the start of the target's next **4 turns**.
- Reapplying Severe Bleeding refreshes its duration but does not stack its damage.
- Blood Damage dealt by Blood Rite is considered **Magical Damage**.
- While Blood Rite is active, your weapon counts as magical for the purpose of overcoming resistances and immunities.
- Only one Blood Rite may be active at a time.
- Blood Rite immediately ends if the empowered weapon leaves your possession for more than one round.
- Blood Rite may be dismissed at any time without requiring an action.

## Lore

Among Bloodhunters, Rank V Blood Rite is regarded as the pinnacle of Sangramancy. Very few survive long enough to achieve it, and fewer still possess the discipline required to wield such immense power responsibly. Those who do are often feared as much as the monsters they hunt, for the line separating hunter and horror has become dangerously thin.

The greatest masters teach that Sangramancy is not a battle against Bloodlust, but a lifelong negotiation with it. Every ritual, every sacrifice, and every drop of blood strengthens both the hunter and the darkness waiting within. Those who believe they have conquered Bloodlust have already begun to lose themselves.

Legends tell of Bloodhunters who stood alone against dragons, demon lords, and ancient horrors, their weapons burning crimson until the battlefield itself became stained with blood. Survivors rarely remembered what those hunters said during the battle. They remembered only the terrifying silence, the precise movements, and the overwhelming certainty that death had come—not as a raging beast, but as a calm and methodical executioner.

> *"Mastery is not reaching the edge of Bloodlust. Mastery is standing upon its edge, hearing it whisper your name... and choosing to take one more step forward without falling."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor
