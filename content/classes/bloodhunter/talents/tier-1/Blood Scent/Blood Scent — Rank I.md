## Description

Blood Scent is the first true hunting technique taught to every Bloodhunter. Through the practice of Sangramancy, their senses evolve beyond those of ordinary mortals, allowing them to perceive the lingering scent carried within freshly spilled blood. To a Bloodhunter, every drop of blood tells a story, revealing the direction a creature has fled and hinting at the emotions it carried when it was shed.

Unlike common trackers who rely upon footprints or broken branches, a Bloodhunter follows the scent of blood itself. Rain may wash away tracks, wind may erase scent, and darkness may hide a quarry, but fresh blood leaves behind a trail that Sangramancy can awaken. Once the Bloodhunter discovers that trail, the hunt truly begins.

Should the Bloodhunter wish to know more than the scent alone can reveal, they may perform a sacred Sangramancy ritual known as **Blood Communion**. By absorbing a fragment of a creature's blood into themselves, the Bloodhunter experiences echoes of the creature's life force, committing its unique Blood Signature to memory. This knowledge comes at a price, for every Blood Communion strengthens the emotions carried within the blood and brings the hunter one step closer to Bloodlust.

Every great hunt begins with a single drop of blood.

## Effects

- Blood Scent is a **Passive Talent** and is always active.
- You can detect the scent of **fresh blood** within **10 metres**.
- You can follow trails created by fresh blood, even when ordinary tracks have been lost.
- You may identify the general direction a bleeding creature has travelled by following the scent of its blood.
- While actively following a blood trail, gain:
  - **+2 to Survival checks** made to track the creature.
  - **+2 to Perception checks** made to locate the creature.
- Blood Scent cannot detect creatures that have left **no blood trail**.
- You may perform **Blood Communion** on fresh blood no older than **24 hours**.
- Blood Communion requires:
  - **10 MP**
  - **1 minute of uninterrupted concentration**
- Upon completing Blood Communion:
  - You memorise that creature's unique **Blood Signature**.
  - If this is the first creature of its species you have communed with, you permanently add that species to your **Blood Codex**.
  - Gain **5 Blood Points (BP)** as you absorb the emotional resonance carried within the blood.
- You may maintain **one Active Blood Signature** at a time.
- While hunting a creature whose Blood Signature you have memorised, gain **+1 to Attack Rolls** against that creature.

## Lore

Among the Bloodhunter Orders, apprentices are forbidden from performing Blood Communion until they have first mastered Blood Scent. Elders believe that a hunter who cannot follow blood has no right to absorb it. Only after proving they can complete a hunt through patience and discipline are they permitted to experience the deeper mysteries of Sangramancy.

The ritual of Blood Communion is both revered and feared. As the blood dissolves into crimson mana, fragments of the creature's emotions briefly become the Bloodhunter's own. Terror from fleeing prey, rage from a wounded beast, or the unnatural hunger of a vampire may all be felt for a fleeting moment. Every communion leaves behind another echo within the Bloodhunter's soul.

Veteran Bloodhunters maintain a mental archive known as the **Blood Codex**, an ever-growing collection of Blood Signatures gathered throughout their lives. To them, every hunt is more than a victory—it is another lesson preserved forever. Yet every lesson carries a cost, for no Bloodhunter can absorb another creature's essence without also carrying a piece of its burden.
