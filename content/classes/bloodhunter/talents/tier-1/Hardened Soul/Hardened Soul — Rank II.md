## Description

The soul of a Bloodhunter is forged through countless encounters with horrors that would shatter ordinary minds. Every monster faced, every Blood Communion performed, and every step taken toward Bloodlust tempers their spirit into something far stronger than mortal resolve.

At this stage, the Bloodhunter no longer steels themselves against fear alone. They have learned that charm, temptation, and false comfort are simply different weapons wielded by the darkness. Rather than allowing these emotions to cloud their judgement, they willingly feed them into the ever-growing storm within their blood.

To outsiders, this appears reckless. A Bloodhunter knowingly embraces the very corruption that threatens to consume them. Yet experienced hunters understand that every choice is calculated. Sometimes allowing Blood Points to rise is preferable to surrendering control of the hunt.

Masters often remind their apprentices that Hardened Soul is not about becoming fearless—it is about deciding which battle is worth risking your humanity. Every use strengthens the Bloodhunter's resolve, but also brings them one step closer to the cold certainty of Bloodlust.

## Effects

- Hardened Soul remains a **Passive Talent**.
- Whenever you are required to make a Saving Throw against becoming **Frightened** or **Charmed**, you may choose to activate Hardened Soul **before making the Saving Throw**.
- If you do, immediately gain **10 Blood Points (BP)**.
- You gain a **+4 bonus** to the Saving Throw.
- This decision is entirely optional and must be declared before the Saving Throw is rolled.
- Blood Points gained through Hardened Soul count towards your **Blood Point Limit** and may contribute to triggering **Bloodlust**.
- Hardened Soul may be used any number of times, provided you are willing to accept the Blood Point cost.

## Lore

Experienced Bloodhunters eventually realise that monsters rarely rely upon brute strength alone. Vampires seduce, fey deceive, demons tempt, and eldritch horrors whisper promises that slowly erode the will of even the strongest heroes. The greatest danger is often not the monster's claws, but its ability to convince its prey to surrender willingly.

Through relentless discipline, the Bloodhunter learns to recognise these manipulations before they take hold. Instead of resisting through force of will alone, they deliberately awaken the darkness already sleeping within their blood. Terror and temptation are swallowed by Sangramancy, becoming fuel for determination rather than chains upon the mind.

This practice is considered one of the most dangerous teachings within every Bloodhunter Order. Apprentices are warned that each time they embrace the darkness, it becomes easier to do so again. One day they may discover that resisting fear is no longer difficult—not because they have mastered it, but because they have begun to feel nothing at all.

> *"The darkness offered me comfort. I answered by making it carry my burden instead."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor
