## Description

A Bloodhunter who reaches the Third Rank of Hardened Soul has begun to understand one undeniable truth—every emotion is a weapon. Fear, desire, despair, and doubt are no longer obstacles to overcome, but sources of strength waiting to be embraced. Rather than resisting the darkness invading their mind, they willingly pull it into themselves, allowing Sangramancy to consume its influence before it can take hold.

The Bloodhunter's soul has become a battlefield unlike any other. Countless Blood Communions, dangerous hunts, and moments spent standing on the edge of Bloodlust have forged a spirit that refuses to break. Every choice to embrace Blood Points instead of surrendering control strengthens this inner resolve, teaching the Bloodhunter that true courage is not the absence of fear, but the willingness to weaponise it.

Yet this newfound mastery carries a frightening consequence. The Bloodhunter begins to rely upon the darkness. Each successful act of defiance makes it easier to accept more Blood Points, and easier still to justify walking ever closer to Bloodlust. Masters teach that the greatest danger is no longer the monsters outside—but the growing belief that the monster within can always be controlled.

Only the wisest Bloodhunters remember that every choice has a cost.

## Effects

- Hardened Soul remains a **Passive Talent**.
- Whenever you are required to make a Saving Throw against becoming **Frightened**, **Charmed**, or **Mentally Influenced**, you may choose to activate Hardened Soul **before making the Saving Throw**.
- If you do, immediately gain **15 Blood Points (BP)**.
- You automatically **succeed** on the Saving Throw.
- Mental Influence includes magical or supernatural effects that alter your thoughts, emotions, or judgement, as determined by the GM.
- This decision is entirely optional and must be declared before the Saving Throw is rolled.
- Blood Points gained through Hardened Soul count towards your **Blood Point Limit** and may contribute to triggering **Bloodlust**.
- Hardened Soul may be used any number of times, provided you are willing to accept the Blood Point cost.

## Lore

Among the Bloodhunter Orders, the Third Rank is known as **The Iron Mind**. It is the stage where a hunter no longer fears the whispers of darkness because they have learned to answer them with their own.

Veteran Bloodhunters describe the experience as opening a gate within the soul. For a fleeting moment, every fear, temptation, and doubt is swallowed by Sangramancy and transformed into unwavering resolve. The hunter walks forward untouched while lesser warriors falter.

But every gate opened becomes easier to open again.

The elders caution that many Bloodhunters who eventually succumb to Bloodlust never lost a battle of will—they won too many. Each victory convinced them they remained in control, until one day the darkness no longer needed permission to answer in their place.

> *"The mind can be conquered only once. Mine was conquered by my own will long before any monster had the chance."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor
