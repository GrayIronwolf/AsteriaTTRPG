# 

## Description

A Bloodhunter's obsession grows stronger with every successful hunt. What once began as hatred evolves into unparalleled understanding, allowing the hunter to anticipate the instincts, habits, and behaviour of their chosen prey with frightening accuracy. Every battle, every Blood Communion, and every page added to the Blood Codex strengthens the bond between hunter and quarry.

At this stage, the Bloodhunter no longer studies their Hunter's Bane simply to destroy it—they begin to think like it. They recognise territorial patterns, preferred methods of attack, weaknesses hidden beneath instinctive behaviour, and the subtle signs that reveal when a creature is preparing to flee, ambush, or strike.

This deeper understanding comes at an equally dangerous cost. The emotions carried within the blood of their sworn prey resonate far more violently than before. Every encounter awakens powerful feelings of determination, vengeance, and obsession, causing Sangramancy to react with greater intensity. The Bloodhunter becomes increasingly efficient at hunting their chosen enemy, but every victory brings them closer to surrendering themselves to Bloodlust.

Among the Orders, veterans often warn that a Bloodhunter should never allow their Hunter's Bane to become their only purpose. Those who do frequently forget why they became hunters in the first place, until the hunt itself becomes all that remains.

## Effects

- Your Hunter's Bane remains the creature type chosen at Rank I.
- Gain **+3 to Survival checks** made to track creatures of your Hunter's Bane.
- Gain **+3 to Monster Knowledge checks** regarding creatures of your Hunter's Bane.
- Blood Communion performed on your Hunter's Bane now requires only **15 seconds**.
- Blood Communion performed on your Hunter's Bane reveals:
  - The creature's approximate health.
  - Signs of disease, corruption, curses, or magical influence.
  - Whether the creature has recently fought or consumed another creature.
- If a creature is both your **Hunter's Bane** and your **Marked Prey**, gain:
  - **+2 to Attack Rolls**.
  - **+2 to Damage Rolls**.
- Whenever you gain Blood Points (BP) from an ability, talent, Blood Rite, Blood Communion, Blood Curse, or any other Sangramancy effect while interacting with your Hunter's Bane, the Blood Points gained are **doubled**.
- Once your Hunter's Bane has been permanently recorded within your Blood Codex, you immediately recognise blood belonging to that creature type without needing to perform Blood Communion again.

## Lore

Only seasoned Bloodhunters reach the point where instinct begins to rival experience. The Blood Codex grows beyond a simple record of hunts and becomes a detailed study of a single enemy, documenting generations of knowledge gathered by countless hunters before them.

Many Bloodhunter Orders pass these records from master to apprentice, ensuring that no lesson earned in blood is ever forgotten. Over decades, the Bloodhunter develops an uncanny familiarity with their Hunter's Bane, recognising patterns invisible to everyone else and predicting behaviour with remarkable precision.

Yet obsession is never without consequence. Sangramancy rewards dedication with power, but it also magnifies every emotion tied to that dedication. The Bloodhunter must constantly ask themselves whether they are still hunting their prey—or whether their prey has begun hunting them from within.
