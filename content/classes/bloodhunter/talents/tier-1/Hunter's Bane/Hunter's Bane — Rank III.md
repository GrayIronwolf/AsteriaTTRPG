## Description

By the Third Rank, your Hunter's Bane has become more than a sworn enemy—it has become your life's work. Years of study, countless Blood Communions, and relentless pursuit have transformed your Blood Codex into a comprehensive record of your chosen prey. Every hunt refines your understanding, allowing you to predict behaviour before it happens and recognise weaknesses invisible to ordinary hunters.

Your Blood Scent reacts instinctively whenever your Hunter's Bane is near. Even the faintest trace of fresh blood can reveal the creature's passing, while your Sangramancy resonates more violently than ever before. Every encounter awakens powerful emotions that sharpen your focus while simultaneously drawing you ever closer to Bloodlust.

Experienced Bloodhunters often describe this stage as becoming one with the hunt. They no longer simply pursue their prey—they begin to anticipate its every decision. Every failed ambush, every attempted escape, and every desperate last stand becomes another lesson added to the Blood Codex.

Yet this mastery carries a terrible burden. The stronger your obsession becomes, the more Sangramancy responds to it. Every drop of blood belonging to your Hunter's Bane burns like fire within your soul, granting incredible strength while threatening to consume your reason. Few Bloodhunters can maintain such discipline for long, and many legendary hunters have ultimately fallen to the very obsession that once made them great.

## Effects

- Your Hunter's Bane remains the creature type chosen at Rank I.
- Gain **+4 to Survival checks** made to track creatures of your Hunter's Bane.
- Gain **+4 to Monster Knowledge checks** regarding creatures of your Hunter's Bane.
- Blood Communion performed on your Hunter's Bane requires only **1 Action**.
- If a creature is both your **Hunter's Bane** and your **Marked Prey**, gain:
  - **+3 to Attack Rolls**.
  - **+3 to Damage Rolls**.
- Gain **Advantage** on checks made to identify, investigate, or recall information about your Hunter's Bane.
- Blood Communion now reveals:
  - Approximate remaining vitality.
  - Active magical effects.
  - Curses, diseases, corruption, mutations, and bloodline abnormalities.
  - Whether the creature has previously been encountered by you.
- Whenever you gain Blood Points (BP) from an ability, talent, Blood Rite, Blood Communion, Blood Curse, or any other Sangramancy effect while interacting with your Hunter's Bane, the Blood Points gained are **doubled**.
- Once recorded within your Blood Codex, you immediately recognise fresh blood belonging to your Hunter's Bane by scent alone.

## Lore

The Third Rank is known among the Bloodhunter Orders as the **Hunter's Awakening**. By this stage, a Bloodhunter has accumulated enough knowledge that hunting their chosen prey becomes almost instinctive. Stories tell of masters recognising a single drop of blood upon stone, immediately identifying not only the creature that shed it but also the direction it travelled and the wounds it carried.

The Blood Codex grows ever larger with every successful hunt. Some Bloodhunters dedicate entire libraries to documenting the habits of a single creature type, ensuring that future generations never begin their hunt without centuries of accumulated knowledge guiding them.

But obsession is a dangerous companion. The Orders teach that every Bloodhunter eventually reaches a point where they must choose whether they control the hunt—or whether the hunt has begun to control them. Those who fail that choice often become legends... remembered as warnings rather than heroes.

> *"The day you understand your prey better than it understands itself is the day the hunt truly begins."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor
