## Description

Your Hunter's Bane is no longer simply a sworn enemy—it has become an obsession that defines your existence. Years of relentless pursuit have transformed your Blood Codex into an unrivalled archive of knowledge, containing every weakness, instinct, hunting pattern, and behaviour your chosen prey has ever revealed. Every encounter reinforces what you know, making each hunt more efficient than the last.

At this level, your Sangramancy instinctively reacts whenever your Hunter's Bane is near. The scent of their blood immediately sharpens your focus, while your Blood Signature and Blood Codex work together to predict their movements before they are made. Few creatures can deceive a Bloodhunter who has dedicated their life to understanding them.

The Bloodhunter Orders teach that this is the point where hunters stop reacting to monsters and instead begin controlling the hunt itself. Every trap, every pursuit, and every confrontation unfolds according to the Bloodhunter's expectations, forcing even the most intelligent prey into a losing battle.

Yet obsession has now become inseparable from power. Every encounter with your Hunter's Bane causes your Blood Points to surge violently, feeding Sangramancy with emotions that grow stronger with every victory. Many Bloodhunters who reach this level begin to fear not their enemies, but the calm certainty they feel whenever the hunt begins.

## Effects

- Your Hunter's Bane remains the creature type chosen at Rank I.
- Gain **+5 to Survival checks** made to track creatures of your Hunter's Bane.
- Gain **+5 to Monster Knowledge checks** regarding creatures of your Hunter's Bane.
- Blood Communion performed on your Hunter's Bane is completed as a **Bonus Action**.
- If a creature is both your **Hunter's Bane** and your **Marked Prey**, gain:
  - **+4 to Attack Rolls**.
  - **+4 to Damage Rolls**.
- Gain **Advantage** on Initiative rolls whenever your Hunter's Bane is present.
- You cannot be surprised by your Hunter's Bane while it is within the range of your Blood Scent.
- Blood Communion additionally reveals:
  - The creature's approximate remaining Health.
  - Active magical enchantments or curses.
  - Recent injuries and their severity.
  - Whether the creature has consumed, transformed into, or been influenced by another creature.
- Whenever you gain Blood Points (BP) from an ability, talent, Blood Rite, Blood Communion, Blood Curse, or any other Sangramancy effect while interacting with your Hunter's Bane, the Blood Points gained are **doubled**.
- Once recorded within your Blood Codex, blood belonging to your Hunter's Bane is instantly recognised by scent, regardless of age, provided some trace of its essence remains.

## Lore

Within the Bloodhunter Orders, Rank IV is known as the **Master Hunter's Oath**. Hunters who reach this level have devoted so much of their lives to a single enemy that they often understand their prey better than the creatures understand themselves. Every victory becomes another lesson, and every lesson strengthens both the Bloodhunter and the obsession driving them forward.

Many legendary Bloodhunters have ended ancient bloodlines through nothing more than patience. They waited, observed, studied, and struck only when every possible outcome had already been calculated. To such hunters, the battle itself is merely the final chapter of a hunt won long before blades are ever drawn.

The elders warn, however, that the line between mastery and obsession has all but disappeared. The Bloodhunter's hatred has become an inexhaustible source of strength, but Sangramancy eagerly feeds upon that hatred. One careless moment is all that separates a legendary hunter from becoming another victim of Bloodlust.

> *"The greatest hunters do not chase monsters. They become the fate those monsters can never escape."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor
