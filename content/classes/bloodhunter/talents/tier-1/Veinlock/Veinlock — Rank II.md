## Description

With greater mastery of Sangramancy, the Bloodhunter learns that blood does not merely power movement—it dictates timing, balance, and reaction. By exerting finer control over a target's circulatory system, the Bloodhunter can cause muscles to seize at precisely the wrong moment, disrupting even the most disciplined warriors.

The victim feels as though their own body has begun to betray them. Their limbs become sluggish, reactions slow, and every attempt to defend themselves is delayed by a heartbeat. While the effect lasts only moments, those moments are often all a Bloodhunter requires to end the hunt.

Experienced Bloodhunters rarely use Veinlock to simply immobilise prey. Instead, they exploit hesitation. Every delayed step, every failed dodge, and every missed opportunity creates another opening for Sangramancy to inflict greater suffering. When combined with Blood Rite and a well-maintained Bleeding effect, Veinlock transforms even the swiftest enemy into easy prey.

However, manipulating another creature's blood demands greater sacrifice from the Bloodhunter. More blood is offered, more Blood Points are accumulated, and the whispers of Bloodlust grow louder with every successful use.

## Effects

- As an **Action**, target one creature with circulating blood within **20 metres**.
- Spend **20 MP**, sacrifice **8 HP**, and gain **10 Blood Points (BP)**.
- The target must succeed on a **Constitution Saving Throw** against your **Sangramancy DC**.
- On a failed save, the target becomes **Veinlocked** for **3 Combat Rounds**.
- While Veinlocked, the target:
  - Has its **Movement Speed reduced by 50%**.
  - Cannot take the **Dash** action.
  - Cannot take **Reactions**.
  - Has **Disadvantage** on Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks.
- If the target is suffering from a **Bleeding** effect, it has **Disadvantage** on the Constitution Saving Throw.
- If the target is both **Bleeding** and your **Marked Prey**, it also suffers **-2 to Dexterity Saving Throws** while Veinlocked.
- A creature may only be affected by one instance of Veinlock at a time.
- Veinlock has **no effect** on creatures without functioning blood unless another Bloodhunter ability states otherwise.

## Lore

Bloodhunter veterans describe the Second Rank of Veinlock as the moment Sangramancy changes from simple manipulation into complete battlefield control. Rather than merely slowing prey, the Bloodhunter begins controlling the rhythm of combat itself. A delayed parry, a missed opportunity, or a failed escape often determines the outcome of an entire hunt.

Many Bloodhunters compare the circulatory system to a river. At first they merely slowed its current. Now they learn to create sudden surges and blockages, forcing the body to move against its own instincts. To onlookers, the victim appears exhausted or clumsy. Only another Sangramancer understands that every heartbeat is being manipulated by an unseen hand.

The Orders warn that every successful Veinlock reinforces the Bloodhunter's confidence in controlling another living being. Used carelessly, that confidence may one day tempt the hunter to believe every creature exists only to have its blood commanded—a dangerous belief that has led many promising Bloodhunters toward Bloodlust.
