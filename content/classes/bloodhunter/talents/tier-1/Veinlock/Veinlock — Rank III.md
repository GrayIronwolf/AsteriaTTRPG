## Description

By the Third Rank, Veinlock has evolved beyond simply slowing an opponent. The Bloodhunter can now disrupt the rhythm of a creature's entire circulatory system, causing muscles to seize, reflexes to falter, and limbs to respond sluggishly to even the simplest commands. Every heartbeat becomes a battle between the creature's will and the Bloodhunter's Sangramancy.

At this level, experienced Bloodhunters no longer use Veinlock merely to prevent escape—they use it to dictate the pace of combat. Enemies find themselves unable to reposition, react, or unleash their full strength before the Bloodhunter closes the distance. The technique is especially feared amongst monsters that rely upon speed, overwhelming attacks, or coordinated movements.

The stronger the Bloodhunter's connection to their prey, the more devastating Veinlock becomes. A bleeding target offers countless paths through which Sangramancy may flow, allowing the Bloodhunter to tighten their control with terrifying precision. Many veteran hunters deliberately inflict wounds before invoking Veinlock, ensuring their prey becomes trapped both physically and psychologically.

Despite its effectiveness, Veinlock remains a dangerous technique. Every manipulation of another creature's lifeblood demands a greater sacrifice from the Bloodhunter, feeding Blood Points into the growing storm within their own soul.

## Effects

- As an **Action**, target one creature with circulating blood within **25 metres**.
- Spend **30 MP**, sacrifice **12 HP**, and gain **15 Blood Points (BP)**.
- The target must succeed on a **Constitution Saving Throw** against your **Sangramancy DC**.
- On a failed save, the target becomes **Veinlocked** for **4 Combat Rounds**.
- While Veinlocked, the target:
  - Has its **Movement Speed reduced by 75%**.
  - Cannot take the **Dash** action.
  - Cannot take **Reactions**.
  - Suffers **-1 Action Point** (minimum of 1 Action Point remaining).
  - Has **Disadvantage** on Dexterity Saving Throws.
- If the target is suffering from a **Bleeding** effect, it has **Disadvantage** on the Constitution Saving Throw.
- If the target is both **Bleeding** and your **Marked Prey**, it also suffers **-2 to Constitution Saving Throws** while Veinlocked.
- A creature may only be affected by one instance of Veinlock at a time.
- Veinlock has **no effect** on creatures without functioning blood unless another Bloodhunter ability specifically states otherwise.

## Lore

Among the Bloodhunter Orders, the Third Rank is known as **The Crimson Grip**. Few techniques inspire greater fear amongst intelligent monsters, for those who have witnessed it know that escape becomes nearly impossible once the Bloodhunter has seized control of their blood.

Masters teach that blood is the language spoken by every living creature. A skilled Sangramancer does not force the blood to obey—they convince it to flow differently, causing the body to betray itself one heartbeat at a time. To outsiders, the victim simply appears exhausted or slow. Only another Bloodhunter understands the invisible battle taking place beneath the skin.

Veterans caution their apprentices that Veinlock should never be used carelessly. Every successful manipulation strengthens the Bloodhunter's confidence in commanding another creature's lifeblood, and that confidence can easily become arrogance. Those who forget that blood is sacred often discover too late that Sangramancy is always willing to command its master as readily as it commands its prey.

> *"The body obeys the blood. Command the blood... and the body has no choice but to follow."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor
