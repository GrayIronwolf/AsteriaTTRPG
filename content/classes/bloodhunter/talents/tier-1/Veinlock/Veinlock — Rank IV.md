## Description

Master Bloodhunters understand that blood is more than a source of life—it is the thread that binds every movement together. At the Fourth Rank, Veinlock allows the Bloodhunter to seize that thread with frightening precision, disrupting the flow of blood throughout an opponent's body until every movement becomes a struggle.

Victims often describe the sensation as though invisible chains have wrapped themselves around every muscle. Their limbs refuse to respond, their heartbeat becomes uneven, and every attempt to fight against the Sangramancy only causes their own blood to resist them further. Even the strongest warriors find themselves trapped inside bodies that no longer obey.

Against bleeding prey, the technique becomes devastating. Every open wound strengthens the Bloodhunter's connection, allowing Sangramancy to spread effortlessly through the target's circulatory system. Veteran Bloodhunters deliberately combine Blood Rite, Blood Scent, and Veinlock into a relentless sequence—find the prey, wound the prey, then ensure the prey can never escape.

Only creatures with functioning blood can be affected. Those without a living circulatory system remain beyond the reach of this technique, a limitation every Bloodhunter quickly learns to respect.

## Effects

- As an **Action**, target one creature with circulating blood within **30 metres**.
- Spend **40 MP**, sacrifice **16 HP**, and gain **20 Blood Points (BP)**.
- The target must succeed on a **Constitution Saving Throw** against your **Sangramancy DC**.
- On a failed save, the target becomes **Veinlocked** for **5 Combat Rounds**.
- While Veinlocked, the target:
  - Has its **Movement Speed reduced to 0**.
  - Cannot take the **Dash** action.
  - Cannot take **Reactions**.
  - Suffers **-1 Action Point** (minimum of 1 Action Point remaining).
  - Has **Disadvantage** on Dexterity and Constitution Saving Throws.
- At the end of each of its turns, the target may repeat the Constitution Saving Throw, ending Veinlock on a success.
- If the target is suffering from a **Bleeding** effect, it has **Disadvantage** on all Saving Throws made to end Veinlock.
- If the target is both **Bleeding** and your **Marked Prey**, it suffers **1d8 Blood Damage** at the start of each of its turns while Veinlocked.
- A creature may only be affected by one instance of Veinlock at a time.
- Veinlock has **no effect** on creatures without functioning blood unless another Bloodhunter ability specifically states otherwise.

## Lore

Within the Bloodhunter Orders, the Fourth Rank is known as **The Crimson Prison**. Few techniques inspire greater terror among monsters, for those who survive it often speak of being fully conscious while their own bodies refused every command.

Masters teach that Veinlock is not about overwhelming force. Instead, it is absolute precision. A Bloodhunter learns exactly which veins to constrict, which muscles to starve of blood, and which heartbeat to interrupt. To observers, the victim appears frozen by fear. In truth, they are imprisoned by their own blood.

The greatest Bloodhunters consider Veinlock an act of mercy. A fleeing monster endangers countless innocent lives. By ending the chase before it truly begins, the Bloodhunter ensures the hunt concludes swiftly and with as little unnecessary suffering as possible. Yet every use reminds the hunter how easily Sangramancy can command another living being—and how dangerous such power becomes when restraint is forgotten.

> *"The fastest prey still carries blood. That is all the advantage I require."*
