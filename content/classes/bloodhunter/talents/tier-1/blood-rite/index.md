# Blood Rite

## Overview
Blood Rite is the first Sangramancy technique taught to every Bloodhunter. By willingly shedding their own blood, a Bloodhunter awakens the latent magical energy carried within it, binding their life force directly to a chosen weapon. This ritual transforms an ordinary weapon into a conduit of blood magic, allowing every strike to carry supernatural power.

Unlike traditional enchantments fuelled by mana, Blood Rite demands sacrifice. The Bloodhunter must willingly wound themselves, offering part of their own vitality to awaken the rite. This sacrifice is never symbolic—the pain is real, and every activation reminds the Bloodhunter that true power always demands a price.

As the ritual takes hold, crimson runes spread across the weapon before sinking beneath its surface. To outsiders the weapon appears stained with fresh blood, while those capable of sensing magic witness violent currents of crimson energy flowing through the steel. Each strike tears not only flesh but also the life essence of the target, leaving wounds that continue to bleed long after the blow has landed.

Although Blood Rite is considered the most fundamental Sangramancy technique, mastering it is a lifelong pursuit. Every future Bloodhunter ability builds upon the discipline, sacrifice, and control first learned through this ritual.

## Rank 1
### Description

### Effects

- As an **Action**, you may activate Blood Rite by sacrificing **5 HP**.
- Activating Blood Rite generates **5 Blood Points (BP)**.
- Blood Rite may be activated **twice per Long Rest**.
- Choose one weapon you are currently wielding.
- The chosen weapon becomes empowered with Sangramancy for **3 Combat Rounds**.
- While Blood Rite is active, the weapon deals an additional **1d6 Blood Damage** on each successful hit.
- The first successful attack against a creature each turn inflicts **Minor Bleeding**.
- Minor Bleeding deals **1d4 Blood Damage** at the start of the target's next **2 turns**.
- Reapplying Minor Bleeding refreshes its duration but does not increase its damage.
- Blood Damage dealt by Blood Rite is considered **Magical Damage**.
- Only one Blood Rite may be active at a time.
- Blood Rite immediately ends if the empowered weapon leaves your possession for more than one round.
- Blood Rite may be dismissed at any time without requiring an action.

### Lore

The first Blood Rite is remembered by every Bloodhunter. It is the moment they cease relying solely upon steel and instead place their trust in their own life force. The scar left by the ritual is traditionally never healed through magic, serving as a permanent reminder that Sangramancy is founded upon sacrifice.

Among veteran Bloodhunters, Blood Rite is often described as a conversation between the hunter and the blood itself. Every activation stirs the countless emotions carried within their lifeblood—anger, grief, determination, fear, and vengeance. With each use, these emotions linger as Blood Points, slowly testing the hunter's resolve.

Those who fail to respect the burden of Sangramancy eventually lose themselves to Bloodlust. Those who endure learn that the true enemy is not the monster before them, but the darkness growing within.

> *"The first lesson of Sangramancy is not how to bleed. It is how to stop the blood from commanding you."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 2
### Description

Having survived the first teachings of Sangramancy, the Bloodhunter learns to deepen their Blood Rite by sacrificing more of their own vitality. The additional blood offered strengthens the bond between hunter and weapon, allowing the crimson energy to remain stable for longer while striking with greater consistency.

At this stage, the Bloodhunter begins to understand that Blood Rite is not merely an enchantment but a living extension of their own emotions. Every heartbeat fuels the rite, every wound strengthens it, and every enemy struck feeds the growing resonance within their soul. What once felt like an act of desperation slowly becomes a deliberate act of discipline.

Veteran Bloodhunters teach that Rank II is where complacency becomes the greatest danger. The rite feels more natural to invoke, tempting hunters to rely upon its power in every battle. Those who forget that every activation feeds the growing burden of Blood Points often discover too late that Sangramancy demands payment long after the battle has ended.

Although the increased sacrifice grants greater control over the rite, it also draws stronger emotional echoes from the Bloodhunter's blood. Mastery lies not in resisting these emotions, but in acknowledging them without allowing them to take control.

### Effects

- As an **Action**, you may activate Blood Rite by sacrificing **8 HP**.
- Activating Blood Rite generates **6 Blood Points (BP)**.
- Blood Rite may be activated **twice per Long Rest**.
- Choose one weapon you are currently wielding.
- The chosen weapon becomes empowered with Sangramancy for **4 Combat Rounds**.
- While Blood Rite is active, the weapon deals an additional **1d6 Blood Damage** on each successful hit.
- The first successful attack against a creature each turn inflicts **Minor Bleeding**.
- Minor Bleeding deals **1d4 Blood Damage** at the start of the target's next **3 turns**.
- Reapplying Minor Bleeding refreshes its duration but does not increase its damage.
- Blood Damage dealt by Blood Rite is considered **Magical Damage**.
- Only one Blood Rite may be active at a time.
- Blood Rite immediately ends if the empowered weapon leaves your possession for more than one round.
- Blood Rite may be dismissed at any time without requiring an action.

### Lore

Every Bloodhunter remembers the first time they willingly shed more blood than was necessary. It is a difficult lesson to accept that greater strength can only be achieved through greater sacrifice. Many abandon their training at this stage, believing the cost too high to justify the power gained.

Those who continue soon realise that Sangramancy is not testing their endurance—it is testing their resolve. Every wound inflicted upon themselves becomes another promise that innocent lives will not bear the burden instead.

Within the Bloodhunter Orders, the scars earned through repeated Blood Rites are known as the Crimson Oath. They are proof that the hunter has chosen duty over comfort and sacrifice over fear, even while the growing weight of Blood Points whispers constantly from within.

> *"Power offered freely is rarely understood. Power bought with your own blood is never forgotten."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 3
### Description

Blood Rite reaches a turning point at Rank III. No longer simply a ritual of sacrifice, it becomes a true extension of the Bloodhunter's will. The blood willingly spilled no longer merely empowers a weapon—it answers the call of its master, surging through the blade with controlled ferocity. The hunter has begun to understand that Sangramancy is not about commanding blood, but existing in harmony with the emotions carried within it.

As mastery grows, so too does the burden placed upon both body and mind. The Bloodhunter can invoke Blood Rite more frequently than before, yet every activation draws increasingly powerful emotional echoes into their soul. Hatred, grief, vengeance, and fear all linger within the growing pool of Blood Points, testing the hunter's discipline with every battle.

Veterans warn that Rank III is where many Bloodhunters fall. The rite becomes dependable enough to use in nearly every encounter, creating the dangerous illusion that Sangramancy has finally been mastered. Those who grow overconfident often discover that the blood remembers every sacrifice ever made, and eventually demands to be heard.

Only those who remain disciplined can continue walking the path without surrendering themselves to Bloodlust.

### Effects

- As an **Action**, you may activate Blood Rite by sacrificing **12 HP**.
- Activating Blood Rite generates **8 Blood Points (BP)**.
- Blood Rite may be activated **3 times per Short Rest**.
- Choose one weapon you are currently wielding.
- The chosen weapon becomes empowered with Sangramancy for **5 Combat Rounds**.
- While Blood Rite is active, the weapon deals an additional **1d8 Blood Damage** on each successful hit.
- The first successful attack against a creature each turn inflicts **Moderate Bleeding**.
- Moderate Bleeding deals **1d6 Blood Damage** at the start of the target's next **3 turns**.
- Reapplying Moderate Bleeding refreshes its duration but does not stack its damage.
- Blood Damage dealt by Blood Rite is considered **Magical Damage**.
- Only one Blood Rite may be active at a time.
- Blood Rite immediately ends if the empowered weapon leaves your possession for more than one round.
- Blood Rite may be dismissed at any time without requiring an action.

### Lore

Within every Bloodhunter Order, Rank III is known as the Threshold. It marks the point where apprentices become true hunters, trusted to undertake contracts without constant supervision. The ability to invoke Blood Rite repeatedly is seen as a privilege earned through discipline rather than a reward for strength alone.

Senior Bloodhunters teach that every drop of blood used in Sangramancy leaves behind an emotional imprint. Over time these echoes begin to accumulate, speaking louder with every battle. Some hunters describe hearing whispers at the edge of their thoughts, while others experience an unsettling calm that precedes Bloodlust.

Those who survive this stage emerge with a newfound respect for Sangramancy. They realise that true mastery will never come from greater power alone, but from maintaining control when every instinct urges them to surrender to the blood.

> *"The blood grows louder with every battle. Mastery is not silencing it—it is refusing to answer its call."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 4
### Description

By the time a Bloodhunter attains the Fourth Rank of Blood Rite, Sangramancy has become second nature. The ritual no longer requires hesitation or careful preparation. The Bloodhunter instinctively understands how much blood must be sacrificed, allowing them to awaken the rite with frightening speed and unwavering precision.

The blood flowing through their veins has become increasingly responsive to their will. Crimson energy surges across their weapon with greater intensity than ever before, carving glowing runes into the air as each strike lands. Wounds inflicted by the empowered weapon refuse to close naturally, leaving enemies weakened by relentless blood loss while the Bloodhunter presses their advantage.

Yet this level of mastery comes with an equally dangerous burden. The greater sacrifice required to sustain such power causes Blood Points to accumulate at an alarming rate. Veteran Bloodhunters often describe this stage as walking beside a predator that is forever watching, waiting patiently for a single lapse in discipline before seizing control.

Only the most disciplined hunters can maintain such power repeatedly without surrendering to Bloodlust. Every battle becomes a contest between the Bloodhunter's will and the countless emotions awakened through Sangramancy.

- Major Bleeding deals **1d8 Blood Damage** at the start of the target's next **3 turns**.

- Only one Blood Rite may be active at a time.

### Lore

Among Bloodhunter Orders, reaching the Fourth Rank is a moment of both celebration and concern. It is proof that the hunter has survived countless battles and mastered techniques that would have claimed the lives of lesser practitioners. Yet it is also the stage where the greatest number of Bloodhunters are lost—not to monsters, but to themselves.

Masters teach that Sangramancy is never truly controlled. Instead, it is respected. Every ritual strengthens the bond between hunter and blood, but every ritual also strengthens the emotions dwelling within that blood. Those who mistake confidence for mastery often discover too late that Bloodlust has been quietly growing beneath the surface all along.

The wisest Bloodhunters learn that restraint is every bit as important as power. A hunter who knows when **not** to invoke Blood Rite is often far more dangerous than one who wields it without hesitation.

> *"The strongest Bloodhunter is not the one who bleeds the most... but the one who knows exactly when another drop is too much."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 5
### Description

Blood Rite has reached its ultimate form. The Bloodhunter has become one of the few living masters capable of wielding Sangramancy without hesitation, instinctively weaving blood magic into every movement of battle. The ritual that once demanded complete concentration has become as natural as drawing breath, allowing the Bloodhunter to invoke it repeatedly throughout even the longest encounters.

The sacrifice demanded is immense. A master Bloodhunter willingly opens a grievous wound to unleash overwhelming power, allowing the blood itself to flood their weapon with ancient crimson energy. Every heartbeat fuels the rite, every strike carries devastating force, and every wound inflicted leaves behind echoes of suffering that refuse to fade.

Yet no Bloodhunter ever escapes the price of Sangramancy. While others see only unmatched skill, the master knows that each activation pushes them closer to Bloodlust. Their greater understanding has not silenced the emotions carried within the blood—it has merely allowed them to shoulder a far heavier burden before those emotions threaten to consume them.

Only the greatest Bloodhunters in history have achieved this level of mastery. Their names are remembered not because they wielded Blood Rite more powerfully than anyone else, but because they stood upon the edge of Bloodlust countless times and never surrendered their humanity.

### Effects

- As an **Action**, you may activate Blood Rite by sacrificing **20 HP**.
- Activating Blood Rite generates **15 Blood Points (BP)**.
- Blood Rite may be activated **4 times per Combat**.
- Choose one weapon you are currently wielding.
- The chosen weapon becomes empowered with Sangramancy for **8 Combat Rounds**.
- While Blood Rite is active, the weapon deals an additional **1d10 Blood Damage** on each successful hit.
- The first successful attack against a creature each turn inflicts **Severe Bleeding**.
- Severe Bleeding deals **1d10 Blood Damage** at the start of the target's next **4 turns**.
- Reapplying Severe Bleeding refreshes its duration but does not stack its damage.
- Blood Damage dealt by Blood Rite is considered **Magical Damage**.
- While Blood Rite is active, your weapon counts as magical for the purpose of overcoming resistances and immunities.
- Only one Blood Rite may be active at a time.
- Blood Rite immediately ends if the empowered weapon leaves your possession for more than one round.
- Blood Rite may be dismissed at any time without requiring an action.

### Lore

Among Bloodhunters, Rank V Blood Rite is regarded as the pinnacle of Sangramancy. Very few survive long enough to achieve it, and fewer still possess the discipline required to wield such immense power responsibly. Those who do are often feared as much as the monsters they hunt, for the line separating hunter and horror has become dangerously thin.

The greatest masters teach that Sangramancy is not a battle against Bloodlust, but a lifelong negotiation with it. Every ritual, every sacrifice, and every drop of blood strengthens both the hunter and the darkness waiting within. Those who believe they have conquered Bloodlust have already begun to lose themselves.

Legends tell of Bloodhunters who stood alone against dragons, demon lords, and ancient horrors, their weapons burning crimson until the battlefield itself became stained with blood. Survivors rarely remembered what those hunters said during the battle. They remembered only the terrifying silence, the precise movements, and the overwhelming certainty that death had come—not as a raging beast, but as a calm and methodical executioner.

> *"Mastery is not reaching the edge of Bloodlust. Mastery is standing upon its edge, hearing it whisper your name... and choosing to take one more step forward without falling."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor
