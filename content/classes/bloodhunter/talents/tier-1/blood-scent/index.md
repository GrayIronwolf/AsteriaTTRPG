# Blood Scent

## Overview
Blood Scent is the first true hunting technique taught to every Bloodhunter. Through the practice of Sangramancy, their senses evolve beyond those of ordinary mortals, allowing them to perceive the lingering scent carried within freshly spilled blood. To a Bloodhunter, every drop of blood tells a story, revealing the direction a creature has fled and hinting at the emotions it carried when it was shed.

Unlike common trackers who rely upon footprints or broken branches, a Bloodhunter follows the scent of blood itself. Rain may wash away tracks, wind may erase scent, and darkness may hide a quarry, but fresh blood leaves behind a trail that Sangramancy can awaken. Once the Bloodhunter discovers that trail, the hunt truly begins.

Should the Bloodhunter wish to know more than the scent alone can reveal, they may perform a sacred Sangramancy ritual known as **Blood Communion**. By absorbing a fragment of a creature's blood into themselves, the Bloodhunter experiences echoes of the creature's life force, committing its unique Blood Signature to memory. This knowledge comes at a price, for every Blood Communion strengthens the emotions carried within the blood and brings the hunter one step closer to Bloodlust.

Every great hunt begins with a single drop of blood.

## Rank 1
### Description

Blood Scent is the first true hunting technique taught to every Bloodhunter. Through the practice of Sangramancy, their senses evolve beyond those of ordinary mortals, allowing them to perceive the lingering scent carried within freshly spilled blood. To a Bloodhunter, every drop of blood tells a story, revealing the direction a creature has fled and hinting at the emotions it carried when it was shed.

Every great hunt begins with a single drop of blood.

### Effects

- Blood Scent is a **Passive Talent** and is always active.
- You can detect the scent of **fresh blood** within **10 metres**.
- You can follow trails created by fresh blood, even when ordinary tracks have been lost.
- You may identify the general direction a bleeding creature has travelled by following the scent of its blood.
- While actively following a blood trail, gain:
  - **+2 to Survival checks** made to track the creature.
  - **+2 to Perception checks** made to locate the creature.
- Blood Scent cannot detect creatures that have left **no blood trail**.
- You may perform **Blood Communion** on fresh blood no older than **24 hours**.
- Blood Communion requires:
  - **10 MP**
  - **1 minute of uninterrupted concentration**
- Upon completing Blood Communion:
  - You memorise that creature's unique **Blood Signature**.
  - If this is the first creature of its species you have communed with, you permanently add that species to your **Blood Codex**.
  - Gain **5 Blood Points (BP)** as you absorb the emotional resonance carried within the blood.
- You may maintain **one Active Blood Signature** at a time.
- While hunting a creature whose Blood Signature you have memorised, gain **+1 to Attack Rolls** against that creature.

### Lore

Among the Bloodhunter Orders, apprentices are forbidden from performing Blood Communion until they have first mastered Blood Scent. Elders believe that a hunter who cannot follow blood has no right to absorb it. Only after proving they can complete a hunt through patience and discipline are they permitted to experience the deeper mysteries of Sangramancy.

The ritual of Blood Communion is both revered and feared. As the blood dissolves into crimson mana, fragments of the creature's emotions briefly become the Bloodhunter's own. Terror from fleeing prey, rage from a wounded beast, or the unnatural hunger of a vampire may all be felt for a fleeting moment. Every communion leaves behind another echo within the Bloodhunter's soul.

Veteran Bloodhunters maintain a mental archive known as the **Blood Codex**, an ever-growing collection of Blood Signatures gathered throughout their lives. To them, every hunt is more than a victory—it is another lesson preserved forever. Yet every lesson carries a cost, for no Bloodhunter can absorb another creature's essence without also carrying a piece of its burden.

> *"Steel wins battles. Blood wins hunts. Learn the blood, and no prey will remain hidden from you."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 2
### Description

As a Bloodhunter's understanding of Sangramancy deepens, Blood Scent evolves from a simple hunting instinct into a supernatural connection with the essence carried within blood. Every trail becomes clearer, every lingering scent more distinct, and every Blood Signature easier to distinguish from the countless others that stain the world.

At this level, the Bloodhunter no longer needs to dedicate themselves to a single quarry. Their growing Blood Codex allows them to retain multiple Blood Signatures simultaneously, making it possible to pursue several hunts without abandoning previous knowledge. Experienced Bloodhunters often compare this to recognising familiar voices in a crowded room—each Blood Signature possesses its own unmistakable identity.

Blood Communion also reveals more than simple identity. The Bloodhunter begins to understand the condition of the creature that shed the blood. A single drop can reveal signs of exhaustion, illness, corruption, or injury, allowing the hunter to prepare long before the prey is ever seen.

To outsiders, Bloodhunters appear impossibly persistent. They do not simply follow tracks—they follow the lingering echoes of life itself.

### Effects

- Blood Scent remains a **Passive Talent**.
- You can detect the scent of **fresh blood** within **20 metres**.
- Blood trails remain detectable for up to **48 hours**, provided they have not been completely destroyed by powerful magic.
- You may maintain **two Active Blood Signatures** at a time.
- While hunting a creature whose Blood Signature you have memorised, you gain:
  - **+2 to Survival checks** made to track that creature.
  - **+2 to Perception checks** made to locate that creature.
  - **+2 to Attack Rolls** against that creature.
- Blood Communion now reveals:
  - The creature's approximate size.
  - Whether it is healthy, wounded, diseased, corrupted, or poisoned.
  - Whether the blood belongs to a Humanoid, Beast, Monstrosity, Dragon, Fey, Fiend, Undead, Aberration, or another recognised creature type.
- Blood Communion requires:
  - **10 MP**
  - **1 minute of uninterrupted concentration**
- Completing Blood Communion generates **6 Blood Points (BP)**.
- If the creature's species is not already recorded, it is permanently added to your **Blood Codex**.

### Lore

Senior Bloodhunters often say that Rank II marks the beginning of true mastery. The hunter no longer relies solely upon instinct but begins building a lifetime of knowledge through their ever-growing Blood Codex. Every successful communion expands their understanding of the creatures that roam Asteria, making future hunts more efficient and far more dangerous for their prey.

Many Orders preserve the Blood Codices of legendary Bloodhunters after their deaths, allowing future generations to study centuries of accumulated knowledge. While the memories of the hunter fade with time, the lessons written in blood endure.

Yet each Blood Communion continues to exact its toll. Every absorbed Blood Signature leaves behind another fragment of emotion within the hunter's soul. The Bloodhunter learns to carry these burdens with discipline, knowing that one day the weight of too many memories may awaken the chilling calm of Bloodlust.

> *"Every hunt teaches a lesson. Every lesson is written in blood. The wise hunter never stops reading."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 3
### Description

At the Third Rank, Blood Scent becomes far more than an exceptional hunting instinct—it becomes an extension of the Bloodhunter's own consciousness. Every Blood Signature committed to the Blood Codex strengthens the hunter's understanding of the creatures they pursue, allowing them to distinguish one individual from another even amongst members of the same species.

A Bloodhunter no longer follows blood alone. Tiny traces left upon weapons, armour, footprints, or vegetation are enough to awaken the lingering scent carried within a creature's life essence. Once a Blood Signature has been memorised, the Bloodhunter develops an almost supernatural intuition for that prey's movements, instinctively predicting where it will flee and recognising attempts to disguise or mislead the hunt.

Blood Communion also reveals far deeper truths. As fragments of emotion and instinct flow into the Bloodhunter, they begin to understand the creature itself. They can recognise corrupted blood, unnatural mutations, lingering curses, and even signs that another Bloodhunter has previously hunted the same prey.

Veterans often describe this stage as the point where the hunt becomes inevitable. Once a Bloodhunter has learned your Blood Signature, escaping is no longer a matter of speed—it becomes a matter of luck.

### Effects

- Blood Scent remains a **Passive Talent**.
- You can detect the scent of **fresh blood** within **30 metres**.
- Blood trails remain detectable for up to **72 hours**, unless removed by powerful magical means.
- You may maintain **three Active Blood Signatures** at a time.
- While hunting a creature whose Blood Signature you have memorised, you gain:
  - **+3 to Survival checks** made to track that creature.
  - **+3 to Perception checks** made to locate that creature.
  - **+2 to Attack Rolls** against that creature.
  - **+2 to Damage Rolls** against that creature.
- You have Advantage on checks made to follow a Blood Signature through difficult terrain or poor weather.
- Blood Communion now reveals:
  - Approximate age of the blood.
  - Signs of magical corruption, curses, disease, or mutation.
  - Whether multiple creatures have mixed blood at the scene.
- Blood Communion requires:
  - **10 MP**
  - **1 minute of uninterrupted concentration**
- Completing Blood Communion generates **8 Blood Points (BP)**.
- If the creature's species is not already recorded, it is permanently added to your **Blood Codex**.

### Lore

Only experienced Bloodhunters reach the Third Rank, for it requires absolute trust in one's Sangramancy. The Blood Codex has grown into a vast archive of living memories, each Blood Signature becoming another lesson preserved within the hunter's soul. Some masters claim they can recall the scent of a creature hunted decades earlier as clearly as if the blood had been spilled moments ago.

Blood Communion becomes increasingly dangerous at this stage. The emotions absorbed from prey linger longer than before, occasionally surfacing during moments of silence or meditation. While disciplined Bloodhunters learn to separate their own thoughts from those carried within the blood, many become haunted by echoes of every creature they have ever hunted.

Despite the danger, Rank III is widely regarded as the point where a Bloodhunter truly earns the title of Master Hunter. Few creatures can escape once their blood has been committed to the Codex, and fewer still survive long enough to be hunted a second time.

> *"A trail may fade. Footprints may vanish. But blood never forgets who it belongs to."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 4
### Description

At the Fourth Rank, Blood Scent transcends mortal instinct and becomes an art perfected through decades of relentless hunts. Every Blood Signature committed to the Blood Codex deepens the Bloodhunter's understanding of life itself, allowing them to perceive not only where prey has travelled, but how it lived, fought, suffered, and survived.

The Bloodhunter no longer follows obvious blood trails alone. The faintest trace upon a weapon's hilt, a single crimson droplet caught beneath a leaf, or dried blood hidden within ancient stone is enough to awaken the memory of its owner. To the Bloodhunter, every battlefield becomes a library written in blood, each stain another page waiting to be read.

Blood Communion reaches an advanced level of mastery. By absorbing another creature's blood, the Bloodhunter can sense far more than identity. They instinctively understand the strength flowing through the creature's veins, recognise ancient bloodlines, and identify signs of magical alteration, corruption, curses, diseases, or unnatural evolution. Few secrets remain hidden from one who understands the language of blood.

Among the Bloodhunter Orders, reaching this rank is known as becoming a **Crimson Tracker**—a hunter capable of pursuing prey across kingdoms, through dense wilderness, and into the darkest ruins with unwavering certainty.

### Effects

- Blood Scent remains a **Passive Talent**.
- You can detect the scent of **fresh blood** within **50 metres**.
- Blood trails remain detectable for up to **7 days**, unless removed by powerful magical means.
- You may maintain **five Active Blood Signatures** at a time.
- While hunting a creature whose Blood Signature you have memorised, you gain:
  - **+4 to Survival checks** made to track that creature.
  - **+4 to Perception checks** made to locate that creature.
  - **+3 to Attack Rolls** against that creature.
  - **+3 to Damage Rolls** against that creature.
- You have Advantage on checks made to pursue your Marked Prey through difficult terrain, darkness, storms, or other natural obstacles.
- Blood Communion now reveals:
  - The creature's approximate power compared to your own.
  - Magical corruption, diseases, curses, mutations, and bloodline alterations.
  - Whether the blood belongs to a creature currently under magical influence or possession.
  - The approximate time since the blood was shed.
- Blood Communion requires:
  - **10 MP**
  - **1 minute of uninterrupted concentration**
- Completing Blood Communion generates **10 Blood Points (BP)**.
- If the creature's species is not already recorded, it is permanently added to your **Blood Codex**.
- Once a creature type has been permanently recorded within your Blood Codex, you automatically recognise blood belonging to that species without needing to perform Blood Communion again.

### Lore

Only the greatest Bloodhunters ever attain the Fourth Rank. By now, the Blood Codex has become a living archive of countless hunts, each Blood Signature adding another lesson to a lifetime devoted to protecting the world from horrors that ordinary warriors cannot comprehend.

Senior Bloodhunters often describe Blood Communion as less of a ritual and more of a conversation. The blood willingly reveals its history to those who have earned the right to hear it. Every triumph, every wound, every curse, and every drop of corruption leaves an echo waiting to be interpreted by an experienced Crimson Tracker.

Yet every lesson carries a burden. The Bloodhunter's own Blood Points continue to accumulate with each Communion, each hunt, and each use of Sangramancy. Veterans know that knowledge itself is dangerous, for the more blood one understands, the louder the whispers of Bloodlust become.

> *"A novice follows blood. A master listens to it. Every drop has a story—few possess the wisdom to understand what it is trying to say."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 5
### Description

At the pinnacle of Sangramancy, the Bloodhunter no longer merely follows blood—they understand it. Every Blood Signature committed to their Blood Codex becomes a permanent lesson, allowing them to perceive the hidden truths carried within every drop of blood. What once began as a hunting technique has become an unparalleled mastery of life, death, and the countless emotions that bind them together.

The Bloodhunter's Blood Codex has grown into a living archive of every species they have studied. Once the blood of a creature has been absorbed through Blood Communion, its scent can never truly be forgotten. Across years, continents, and countless hunts, the Bloodhunter instinctively recognises familiar blood as though greeting an old enemy.

A master Bloodhunter no longer needs obvious trails to pursue their prey. A single dried droplet hidden upon a weapon, armour, or stone is enough to awaken the creature's Blood Signature. Through Sangramancy, they reconstruct the hunt piece by piece, predicting movements, identifying weaknesses, and uncovering truths that ordinary trackers could never hope to perceive.

Among the Bloodhunter Orders, those who reach this level are known as **Blood Sages**. They are feared not because they cannot be escaped, but because once they have learned your blood, they will never truly lose your trail.

### Effects

- Blood Scent remains a **Passive Talent**.
- You can detect the scent of **fresh blood** within **75 metres**.
- Blood trails remain detectable for up to **30 days**, unless completely destroyed by powerful magical effects.
- You may maintain an **unlimited number of Active Blood Signatures**.
- While hunting a creature whose Blood Signature you have memorised, you gain:
  - **+5 to Survival checks** made to track that creature.
  - **+5 to Perception checks** made to locate that creature.
  - **+4 to Attack Rolls** against that creature.
  - **+4 to Damage Rolls** against that creature.
- You always know the general direction of any memorised Blood Signature while it remains within your Blood Scent range.
- Blood Communion now reveals:
  - The creature's exact species and ancestry.
  - Approximate age and physical condition.
  - Magical corruption, diseases, curses, mutations, and bloodline alterations.
  - Whether the creature is currently under magical influence, possession, or transformation.
  - The approximate strength of the creature compared to your own.
- Blood Communion requires:
  - **10 MP**
  - **1 minute of uninterrupted concentration**
- Completing Blood Communion generates **15 Blood Points (BP)**.
- Every creature species successfully communed with is permanently added to your **Blood Codex**.
- Once a species has been recorded within your Blood Codex, you automatically recognise blood belonging to that species without requiring another Blood Communion.
- The first time you encounter a previously recorded species, the GM should provide any information your Blood Codex has previously learned about that creature.

### Lore

Only a handful of Bloodhunters throughout history have reached the Fifth Rank of Blood Scent. By this stage, their Blood Codex has become one of the greatest repositories of monster lore ever assembled. Every hunt, every battle, and every Blood Communion has added another chapter to a lifetime devoted to understanding the creatures that threaten the world.

Legends tell of Blood Sages who could identify an ancient dragon from a single crimson scale, determine the age of a vampire from one dried droplet upon castle stone, or recognise a demon's blood centuries after it had first stained the earth. To such hunters, blood is no longer merely a trail—it is history itself.

Yet every Blood Communion continues to carry a price. Each absorbed Blood Signature strengthens the Bloodhunter's understanding while adding another emotional echo to the burden they carry. Even the greatest Blood Sages know that mastery over Sangramancy is never complete. They simply learn to shoulder more voices without allowing them to become their own.

> *"Names can be forgotten. Faces can change. Kingdoms can fall. But blood remembers... and so do I."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor
