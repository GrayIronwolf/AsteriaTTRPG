# Hunter's Bane

## Overview
Every Bloodhunter eventually discovers that there is one creature they cannot ignore.

Whether born from vengeance, duty, fear, or obsession, every hunter develops a relentless fixation upon a particular kind of prey. Some dedicate their lives to destroying the undead that slaughtered their family. Others swear to eradicate fiends that plague the mortal realm, while others pursue dragons, aberrations, or monsters that ordinary people dare not face.

This lifelong dedication is known as a **Hunter's Bane**.

Through years of study, relentless pursuit, and countless Blood Communions, the Bloodhunter learns every weakness, behaviour, hunting pattern, and instinct of their chosen prey. Their Blood Codex steadily grows with observations gathered from every encounter, making them one of the most dangerous adversaries that creature type will ever face.

Yet obsession comes at a terrible price.

The blood of a Hunter's Bane resonates more violently than any other. Every drop carries stronger emotions, stronger memories, and stronger hatred. Sangramancy eagerly feeds upon these emotions, granting the Bloodhunter greater focus while simultaneously accelerating the accumulation of Blood Points. Against their sworn prey, the Bloodhunter walks far closer to Bloodlust than they ever would against any other creature.

### Effects

- Choose **one creature type** from the following list. This choice is **permanent**:
  - Aberration
  - Beast
  - Celestial
  - Construct
  - Dragon
  - Elemental
  - Fey
  - Fiend
  - Giant
  - Humanoid
  - Monstrosity
  - Ooze
  - Plant
  - Undead
- This creature type becomes your **Hunter's Bane**.
- Gain **+2 to Survival checks** made to track creatures of your Hunter's Bane.
- Gain **+2 to Monster Knowledge checks** regarding creatures of your Hunter's Bane.
- Blood Communion performed on your Hunter's Bane requires only **30 seconds** instead of **1 minute**.
- The first successful Blood Communion performed on your Hunter's Bane immediately records that species within your **Blood Codex** if it has not already been documented.
- If a creature is both your **Hunter's Bane** and your **Marked Prey**, gain:
  - **+1 to Attack Rolls**.
  - **+1 to Damage Rolls**.
- **Whenever you gain Blood Points (BP) from an ability, talent, Blood Communion, Blood Rite, Blood Curse, or any other Sangramancy effect while interacting with your Hunter's Bane, the Blood Points gained are doubled.**
- This doubling is mandatory and cannot be ignored unless another ability specifically states otherwise.

### Lore

Every Bloodhunter carries a story that begins with a single monster.

For some, it is the vampire that slaughtered their village. For others, the demon that corrupted their homeland or the ancient dragon that reduced an entire kingdom to ash. These encounters leave scars deeper than flesh, shaping the hunter's purpose for the rest of their life.

The ritual of declaring a Hunter's Bane is among the oldest traditions of every Bloodhunter Order. Before their peers, the hunter speaks the name of the creatures they will dedicate their life to destroying before sealing the oath with their own blood. From that day onward, every hunt deepens their understanding, but also their obsession.

The elders teach that hatred is the sharpest weapon a Bloodhunter possesses—and the most dangerous. Sangramancy amplifies every emotion carried within blood, and no blood burns hotter than that of a sworn enemy. Many Bloodhunters who succumb to Bloodlust do so not while facing impossible odds, but while standing before the very creature they swore they would one day destroy.

> *"Beware the hunter who knows your blood... but fear the one who has sworn an oath upon it."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 2
# 

### Description

A Bloodhunter's obsession grows stronger with every successful hunt. What once began as hatred evolves into unparalleled understanding, allowing the hunter to anticipate the instincts, habits, and behaviour of their chosen prey with frightening accuracy. Every battle, every Blood Communion, and every page added to the Blood Codex strengthens the bond between hunter and quarry.

At this stage, the Bloodhunter no longer studies their Hunter's Bane simply to destroy it—they begin to think like it. They recognise territorial patterns, preferred methods of attack, weaknesses hidden beneath instinctive behaviour, and the subtle signs that reveal when a creature is preparing to flee, ambush, or strike.

This deeper understanding comes at an equally dangerous cost. The emotions carried within the blood of their sworn prey resonate far more violently than before. Every encounter awakens powerful feelings of determination, vengeance, and obsession, causing Sangramancy to react with greater intensity. The Bloodhunter becomes increasingly efficient at hunting their chosen enemy, but every victory brings them closer to surrendering themselves to Bloodlust.

Among the Orders, veterans often warn that a Bloodhunter should never allow their Hunter's Bane to become their only purpose. Those who do frequently forget why they became hunters in the first place, until the hunt itself becomes all that remains.

### Effects

- Your Hunter's Bane remains the creature type chosen at Rank I.
- Gain **+3 to Survival checks** made to track creatures of your Hunter's Bane.
- Gain **+3 to Monster Knowledge checks** regarding creatures of your Hunter's Bane.
- Blood Communion performed on your Hunter's Bane now requires only **15 seconds**.
- Blood Communion performed on your Hunter's Bane reveals:
  - The creature's approximate health.
  - Signs of disease, corruption, curses, or magical influence.
  - Whether the creature has recently fought or consumed another creature.
- If a creature is both your **Hunter's Bane** and your **Marked Prey**, gain:
  - **+2 to Attack Rolls**.
  - **+2 to Damage Rolls**.
- Whenever you gain Blood Points (BP) from an ability, talent, Blood Rite, Blood Communion, Blood Curse, or any other Sangramancy effect while interacting with your Hunter's Bane, the Blood Points gained are **doubled**.
- Once your Hunter's Bane has been permanently recorded within your Blood Codex, you immediately recognise blood belonging to that creature type without needing to perform Blood Communion again.

### Lore

Only seasoned Bloodhunters reach the point where instinct begins to rival experience. The Blood Codex grows beyond a simple record of hunts and becomes a detailed study of a single enemy, documenting generations of knowledge gathered by countless hunters before them.

Many Bloodhunter Orders pass these records from master to apprentice, ensuring that no lesson earned in blood is ever forgotten. Over decades, the Bloodhunter develops an uncanny familiarity with their Hunter's Bane, recognising patterns invisible to everyone else and predicting behaviour with remarkable precision.

Yet obsession is never without consequence. Sangramancy rewards dedication with power, but it also magnifies every emotion tied to that dedication. The Bloodhunter must constantly ask themselves whether they are still hunting their prey—or whether their prey has begun hunting them from within.

> *"Know your enemy so completely that they believe escape is possible... right until the moment they realise you were always one step behind them."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 3
### Description

By the Third Rank, your Hunter's Bane has become more than a sworn enemy—it has become your life's work. Years of study, countless Blood Communions, and relentless pursuit have transformed your Blood Codex into a comprehensive record of your chosen prey. Every hunt refines your understanding, allowing you to predict behaviour before it happens and recognise weaknesses invisible to ordinary hunters.

Your Blood Scent reacts instinctively whenever your Hunter's Bane is near. Even the faintest trace of fresh blood can reveal the creature's passing, while your Sangramancy resonates more violently than ever before. Every encounter awakens powerful emotions that sharpen your focus while simultaneously drawing you ever closer to Bloodlust.

Experienced Bloodhunters often describe this stage as becoming one with the hunt. They no longer simply pursue their prey—they begin to anticipate its every decision. Every failed ambush, every attempted escape, and every desperate last stand becomes another lesson added to the Blood Codex.

Yet this mastery carries a terrible burden. The stronger your obsession becomes, the more Sangramancy responds to it. Every drop of blood belonging to your Hunter's Bane burns like fire within your soul, granting incredible strength while threatening to consume your reason. Few Bloodhunters can maintain such discipline for long, and many legendary hunters have ultimately fallen to the very obsession that once made them great.

### Effects

- Your Hunter's Bane remains the creature type chosen at Rank I.
- Gain **+4 to Survival checks** made to track creatures of your Hunter's Bane.
- Gain **+4 to Monster Knowledge checks** regarding creatures of your Hunter's Bane.
- Blood Communion performed on your Hunter's Bane requires only **1 Action**.
- If a creature is both your **Hunter's Bane** and your **Marked Prey**, gain:
  - **+3 to Attack Rolls**.
  - **+3 to Damage Rolls**.
- Gain **Advantage** on checks made to identify, investigate, or recall information about your Hunter's Bane.
- Blood Communion now reveals:
  - Approximate remaining vitality.
  - Active magical effects.
  - Curses, diseases, corruption, mutations, and bloodline abnormalities.
  - Whether the creature has previously been encountered by you.
- Whenever you gain Blood Points (BP) from an ability, talent, Blood Rite, Blood Communion, Blood Curse, or any other Sangramancy effect while interacting with your Hunter's Bane, the Blood Points gained are **doubled**.
- Once recorded within your Blood Codex, you immediately recognise fresh blood belonging to your Hunter's Bane by scent alone.

### Lore

The Third Rank is known among the Bloodhunter Orders as the **Hunter's Awakening**. By this stage, a Bloodhunter has accumulated enough knowledge that hunting their chosen prey becomes almost instinctive. Stories tell of masters recognising a single drop of blood upon stone, immediately identifying not only the creature that shed it but also the direction it travelled and the wounds it carried.

The Blood Codex grows ever larger with every successful hunt. Some Bloodhunters dedicate entire libraries to documenting the habits of a single creature type, ensuring that future generations never begin their hunt without centuries of accumulated knowledge guiding them.

But obsession is a dangerous companion. The Orders teach that every Bloodhunter eventually reaches a point where they must choose whether they control the hunt—or whether the hunt has begun to control them. Those who fail that choice often become legends... remembered as warnings rather than heroes.

> *"The day you understand your prey better than it understands itself is the day the hunt truly begins."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 4
# 

### Description

Your Hunter's Bane is no longer simply a sworn enemy—it has become an obsession that defines your existence. Years of relentless pursuit have transformed your Blood Codex into an unrivalled archive of knowledge, containing every weakness, instinct, hunting pattern, and behaviour your chosen prey has ever revealed. Every encounter reinforces what you know, making each hunt more efficient than the last.

At this level, your Sangramancy instinctively reacts whenever your Hunter's Bane is near. The scent of their blood immediately sharpens your focus, while your Blood Signature and Blood Codex work together to predict their movements before they are made. Few creatures can deceive a Bloodhunter who has dedicated their life to understanding them.

The Bloodhunter Orders teach that this is the point where hunters stop reacting to monsters and instead begin controlling the hunt itself. Every trap, every pursuit, and every confrontation unfolds according to the Bloodhunter's expectations, forcing even the most intelligent prey into a losing battle.

Yet obsession has now become inseparable from power. Every encounter with your Hunter's Bane causes your Blood Points to surge violently, feeding Sangramancy with emotions that grow stronger with every victory. Many Bloodhunters who reach this level begin to fear not their enemies, but the calm certainty they feel whenever the hunt begins.

### Effects

- Your Hunter's Bane remains the creature type chosen at Rank I.
- Gain **+5 to Survival checks** made to track creatures of your Hunter's Bane.
- Gain **+5 to Monster Knowledge checks** regarding creatures of your Hunter's Bane.
- Blood Communion performed on your Hunter's Bane is completed as a **Bonus Action**.
- If a creature is both your **Hunter's Bane** and your **Marked Prey**, gain:
  - **+4 to Attack Rolls**.
  - **+4 to Damage Rolls**.
- Gain **Advantage** on Initiative rolls whenever your Hunter's Bane is present.
- You cannot be surprised by your Hunter's Bane while it is within the range of your Blood Scent.
- Blood Communion additionally reveals:
  - The creature's approximate remaining Health.
  - Active magical enchantments or curses.
  - Recent injuries and their severity.
  - Whether the creature has consumed, transformed into, or been influenced by another creature.
- Whenever you gain Blood Points (BP) from an ability, talent, Blood Rite, Blood Communion, Blood Curse, or any other Sangramancy effect while interacting with your Hunter's Bane, the Blood Points gained are **doubled**.
- Once recorded within your Blood Codex, blood belonging to your Hunter's Bane is instantly recognised by scent, regardless of age, provided some trace of its essence remains.

### Lore

Within the Bloodhunter Orders, Rank IV is known as the **Master Hunter's Oath**. Hunters who reach this level have devoted so much of their lives to a single enemy that they often understand their prey better than the creatures understand themselves. Every victory becomes another lesson, and every lesson strengthens both the Bloodhunter and the obsession driving them forward.

Many legendary Bloodhunters have ended ancient bloodlines through nothing more than patience. They waited, observed, studied, and struck only when every possible outcome had already been calculated. To such hunters, the battle itself is merely the final chapter of a hunt won long before blades are ever drawn.

The elders warn, however, that the line between mastery and obsession has all but disappeared. The Bloodhunter's hatred has become an inexhaustible source of strength, but Sangramancy eagerly feeds upon that hatred. One careless moment is all that separates a legendary hunter from becoming another victim of Bloodlust.

> *"The greatest hunters do not chase monsters. They become the fate those monsters can never escape."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 5
### Description

You have become the embodiment of the hunt.

Your Hunter's Bane is no longer merely a creature type you specialise in—it has become the purpose around which your Sangramancy has evolved. Every Blood Communion, every Blood Signature committed to your Blood Codex, and every battle fought against your sworn prey has reshaped both your body and mind into the ultimate predator.

The Blood Codex has become a living archive of generations of knowledge, allowing you to understand your Hunter's Bane with frightening clarity. Their instincts, behaviours, strengths, and weaknesses are no longer mysteries. You read them as easily as others read a book, often predicting their actions moments before they occur.

Yet no Bloodhunter reaches this level without sacrifice.

The blood of your Hunter's Bane resonates with overwhelming intensity. Every drop fuels your Sangramancy, causing your Blood Points to surge faster than ever before. To lesser Bloodhunters this would guarantee Bloodlust, but masters willingly walk that razor's edge, accepting the growing burden because no one else can stand against the horrors they were born to destroy.

Among the Orders, those who achieve this rank are known as **Apex Hunters**. Legends claim entire monsters species have altered their migration routes after learning one of these hunters has entered their territory.

### Effects

- Your Hunter's Bane remains the creature type chosen at Rank I.
- Gain **+6 to Survival checks** made to track creatures of your Hunter's Bane.
- Gain **+6 to Monster Knowledge checks** regarding creatures of your Hunter's Bane.
- Blood Communion performed on your Hunter's Bane is **instantaneous** and requires no additional action beyond interacting with the blood.
- If a creature is both your **Hunter's Bane** and your **Marked Prey**, gain:
  - **+5 to Attack Rolls**.
  - **+5 to Damage Rolls**.
- Gain **Advantage** on Initiative rolls whenever your Hunter's Bane is present.
- Your Hunter's Bane cannot gain Advantage against you through being hidden, invisible, or disguised while within the range of your Blood Scent.
- Your Blood Codex immediately identifies any blood belonging to your Hunter's Bane, regardless of its age, provided a trace of its essence remains.
- Blood Communion reveals everything known through previous ranks, as well as:
  - The creature's approximate age.
  - Its bloodline or lineage.
  - Magical mutations or inherited traits.
  - Signs of previous Bloodhunter encounters.
- **Whenever you gain Blood Points (BP) from an ability, talent, Blood Rite, Blood Communion, Blood Curse, or any other Sangramancy effect while interacting with your Hunter's Bane, the Blood Points gained are doubled.**
- This effect is mandatory and cannot be prevented unless another ability specifically overrides it.

### Lore

Very few Bloodhunters survive long enough to become an Apex Hunter. Those who do are spoken of with equal measures of admiration and fear, for they have devoted an entire lifetime to understanding a single enemy so completely that the hunt itself has become instinct.

Entire Bloodhunter Orders preserve the journals of Apex Hunters within their Blood Codices. These writings contain centuries of accumulated knowledge, allowing future generations to continue the hunt where others left off. It is said that no Bloodhunter truly dies while their Blood Codex remains, for every lesson learned in blood continues to guide those who follow.

The greatest irony of the Apex Hunter is that they stand closer to Bloodlust than any other Bloodhunter. Every encounter with their sworn prey floods their soul with overwhelming emotion, pushing them to the brink of losing themselves. Yet they willingly embrace that burden, believing there is no greater sacrifice than becoming the very weapon needed to destroy the monsters that threaten the world.

> *"Every hunter dreams of slaying the monster. Only an Apex Hunter understands that the greatest victory is surviving the monster within."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor
