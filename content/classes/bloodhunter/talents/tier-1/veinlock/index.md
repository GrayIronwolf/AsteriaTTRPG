# Veinlock

## Overview
A Bloodhunter soon learns that blood is more than life—it is movement. Every heartbeat drives muscles, every pulse fuels strength, and every flowing vein keeps a creature alive. Through Sangramancy, the Bloodhunter can momentarily seize control of that flow, causing the blood within an enemy's body to thicken and resist its natural movement.

This technique is known as **Veinlock**. Rather than inflicting overwhelming pain, the Bloodhunter briefly restricts circulation, causing muscles to stiffen and reactions to slow. The effect is subtle but devastating, robbing even the fastest prey of the speed they rely upon to escape.

Veinlock is most effective against creatures already suffering from open wounds. Fresh blood provides the Sangramancer with a stronger connection to the target, allowing the manipulation to spread more easily through the circulatory system. Veteran Bloodhunters often combine Blood Rite with Veinlock, weakening their prey before closing in for the kill.

The technique is completely ineffective against creatures without circulating blood. Constructs, most undead, oozes, and similar beings simply possess no living blood for the Bloodhunter to command.

## Rank 1
### Description

A Bloodhunter soon learns that blood is more than life—it is movement. Every heartbeat drives muscles, every pulse fuels strength, and every flowing vein keeps a creature alive. Through Sangramancy, the Bloodhunter can momentarily seize control of that flow, causing the blood within an enemy's body to thicken and resist its natural movement.

This technique is known as **Veinlock**. Rather than inflicting overwhelming pain, the Bloodhunter briefly restricts circulation, causing muscles to stiffen and reactions to slow. The effect is subtle but devastating, robbing even the fastest prey of the speed they rely upon to escape.

Veinlock is most effective against creatures already suffering from open wounds. Fresh blood provides the Sangramancer with a stronger connection to the target, allowing the manipulation to spread more easily through the circulatory system. Veteran Bloodhunters often combine Blood Rite with Veinlock, weakening their prey before closing in for the kill.

The technique is completely ineffective against creatures without circulating blood. Constructs, most undead, oozes, and similar beings simply possess no living blood for the Bloodhunter to command.

### Effects

- As an **Action**, target one creature with circulating blood within **15 metres**.
- Spend **15 MP**, sacrifice **5 HP**, and gain **5 Blood Points (BP)**.
- The target must succeed on a **Constitution Saving Throw** against your **Sangramancy DC**.
- On a failed save, the target becomes **Veinlocked** for **2 Combat Rounds**.
- While Veinlocked, the target:
  - Has its **Movement Speed reduced by 50%**.
  - Cannot take the **Dash** action.
  - Has **Disadvantage** on Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks.
- If the target is currently suffering from a **Bleeding** effect, it has **Disadvantage** on the Constitution Saving Throw.
- A creature may only be affected by one instance of Veinlock at a time.
- Veinlock has **no effect** on creatures without functioning blood unless another Bloodhunter ability specifically states otherwise.

### Lore

Among the Bloodhunter Orders, Veinlock is often the first true display of offensive Sangramancy taught to apprentices. Unlike Blood Rite, which channels one's own blood into a weapon, Veinlock reaches outward, proving that another creature's blood can be manipulated just as easily as one's own.

Many apprentices struggle with the technique at first. They instinctively try to force the blood to stop completely, only to find the connection breaks under such strain. Masters instead teach them to think of a river rather than a dam—slowing the current just enough that the prey begins to stumble under its own weight.

Experienced Bloodhunters know that a fleeing monster is often more dangerous than a cornered one. Veinlock was created not to kill, but to ensure that once the hunt begins, the prey can never outrun the hunter.

> *"Every creature believes its legs will carry it to safety. They forget who commands the blood that moves them."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 2
### Description

With greater mastery of Sangramancy, the Bloodhunter learns that blood does not merely power movement—it dictates timing, balance, and reaction. By exerting finer control over a target's circulatory system, the Bloodhunter can cause muscles to seize at precisely the wrong moment, disrupting even the most disciplined warriors.

The victim feels as though their own body has begun to betray them. Their limbs become sluggish, reactions slow, and every attempt to defend themselves is delayed by a heartbeat. While the effect lasts only moments, those moments are often all a Bloodhunter requires to end the hunt.

Experienced Bloodhunters rarely use Veinlock to simply immobilise prey. Instead, they exploit hesitation. Every delayed step, every failed dodge, and every missed opportunity creates another opening for Sangramancy to inflict greater suffering. When combined with Blood Rite and a well-maintained Bleeding effect, Veinlock transforms even the swiftest enemy into easy prey.

However, manipulating another creature's blood demands greater sacrifice from the Bloodhunter. More blood is offered, more Blood Points are accumulated, and the whispers of Bloodlust grow louder with every successful use.

### Effects

- As an **Action**, target one creature with circulating blood within **20 metres**.
- Spend **20 MP**, sacrifice **8 HP**, and gain **10 Blood Points (BP)**.
- The target must succeed on a **Constitution Saving Throw** against your **Sangramancy DC**.
- On a failed save, the target becomes **Veinlocked** for **3 Combat Rounds**.
- While Veinlocked, the target:
  - Has its **Movement Speed reduced by 50%**.
  - Cannot take the **Dash** action.
  - Cannot take **Reactions**.
  - Has **Disadvantage** on Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks.
- If the target is suffering from a **Bleeding** effect, it has **Disadvantage** on the Constitution Saving Throw.
- If the target is both **Bleeding** and your **Marked Prey**, it also suffers **-2 to Dexterity Saving Throws** while Veinlocked.
- A creature may only be affected by one instance of Veinlock at a time.
- Veinlock has **no effect** on creatures without functioning blood unless another Bloodhunter ability states otherwise.

### Lore

Bloodhunter veterans describe the Second Rank of Veinlock as the moment Sangramancy changes from simple manipulation into complete battlefield control. Rather than merely slowing prey, the Bloodhunter begins controlling the rhythm of combat itself. A delayed parry, a missed opportunity, or a failed escape often determines the outcome of an entire hunt.

Many Bloodhunters compare the circulatory system to a river. At first they merely slowed its current. Now they learn to create sudden surges and blockages, forcing the body to move against its own instincts. To onlookers, the victim appears exhausted or clumsy. Only another Sangramancer understands that every heartbeat is being manipulated by an unseen hand.

The Orders warn that every successful Veinlock reinforces the Bloodhunter's confidence in controlling another living being. Used carelessly, that confidence may one day tempt the hunter to believe every creature exists only to have its blood commanded—a dangerous belief that has led many promising Bloodhunters toward Bloodlust.

> *"A warrior's greatest strength is timing. Steal a single heartbeat... and you've already won the battle."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 3
### Description

By the Third Rank, Veinlock has evolved beyond simply slowing an opponent. The Bloodhunter can now disrupt the rhythm of a creature's entire circulatory system, causing muscles to seize, reflexes to falter, and limbs to respond sluggishly to even the simplest commands. Every heartbeat becomes a battle between the creature's will and the Bloodhunter's Sangramancy.

At this level, experienced Bloodhunters no longer use Veinlock merely to prevent escape—they use it to dictate the pace of combat. Enemies find themselves unable to reposition, react, or unleash their full strength before the Bloodhunter closes the distance. The technique is especially feared amongst monsters that rely upon speed, overwhelming attacks, or coordinated movements.

The stronger the Bloodhunter's connection to their prey, the more devastating Veinlock becomes. A bleeding target offers countless paths through which Sangramancy may flow, allowing the Bloodhunter to tighten their control with terrifying precision. Many veteran hunters deliberately inflict wounds before invoking Veinlock, ensuring their prey becomes trapped both physically and psychologically.

Despite its effectiveness, Veinlock remains a dangerous technique. Every manipulation of another creature's lifeblood demands a greater sacrifice from the Bloodhunter, feeding Blood Points into the growing storm within their own soul.

### Effects

- As an **Action**, target one creature with circulating blood within **25 metres**.
- Spend **30 MP**, sacrifice **12 HP**, and gain **15 Blood Points (BP)**.
- The target must succeed on a **Constitution Saving Throw** against your **Sangramancy DC**.
- On a failed save, the target becomes **Veinlocked** for **4 Combat Rounds**.
- While Veinlocked, the target:
  - Has its **Movement Speed reduced by 75%**.
  - Cannot take the **Dash** action.
  - Cannot take **Reactions**.
  - Suffers **-1 Action Point** (minimum of 1 Action Point remaining).
  - Has **Disadvantage** on Dexterity Saving Throws.
- If the target is suffering from a **Bleeding** effect, it has **Disadvantage** on the Constitution Saving Throw.
- If the target is both **Bleeding** and your **Marked Prey**, it also suffers **-2 to Constitution Saving Throws** while Veinlocked.
- A creature may only be affected by one instance of Veinlock at a time.
- Veinlock has **no effect** on creatures without functioning blood unless another Bloodhunter ability specifically states otherwise.

### Lore

Among the Bloodhunter Orders, the Third Rank is known as **The Crimson Grip**. Few techniques inspire greater fear amongst intelligent monsters, for those who have witnessed it know that escape becomes nearly impossible once the Bloodhunter has seized control of their blood.

Masters teach that blood is the language spoken by every living creature. A skilled Sangramancer does not force the blood to obey—they convince it to flow differently, causing the body to betray itself one heartbeat at a time. To outsiders, the victim simply appears exhausted or slow. Only another Bloodhunter understands the invisible battle taking place beneath the skin.

Veterans caution their apprentices that Veinlock should never be used carelessly. Every successful manipulation strengthens the Bloodhunter's confidence in commanding another creature's lifeblood, and that confidence can easily become arrogance. Those who forget that blood is sacred often discover too late that Sangramancy is always willing to command its master as readily as it commands its prey.

> *"The body obeys the blood. Command the blood... and the body has no choice but to follow."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 4
### Description

Master Bloodhunters understand that blood is more than a source of life—it is the thread that binds every movement together. At the Fourth Rank, Veinlock allows the Bloodhunter to seize that thread with frightening precision, disrupting the flow of blood throughout an opponent's body until every movement becomes a struggle.

Victims often describe the sensation as though invisible chains have wrapped themselves around every muscle. Their limbs refuse to respond, their heartbeat becomes uneven, and every attempt to fight against the Sangramancy only causes their own blood to resist them further. Even the strongest warriors find themselves trapped inside bodies that no longer obey.

Against bleeding prey, the technique becomes devastating. Every open wound strengthens the Bloodhunter's connection, allowing Sangramancy to spread effortlessly through the target's circulatory system. Veteran Bloodhunters deliberately combine Blood Rite, Blood Scent, and Veinlock into a relentless sequence—find the prey, wound the prey, then ensure the prey can never escape.

Only creatures with functioning blood can be affected. Those without a living circulatory system remain beyond the reach of this technique, a limitation every Bloodhunter quickly learns to respect.

### Effects

- As an **Action**, target one creature with circulating blood within **30 metres**.
- Spend **40 MP**, sacrifice **16 HP**, and gain **20 Blood Points (BP)**.
- The target must succeed on a **Constitution Saving Throw** against your **Sangramancy DC**.
- On a failed save, the target becomes **Veinlocked** for **5 Combat Rounds**.
- While Veinlocked, the target:
  - Has its **Movement Speed reduced to 0**.
  - Cannot take the **Dash** action.
  - Cannot take **Reactions**.
  - Suffers **-1 Action Point** (minimum of 1 Action Point remaining).
  - Has **Disadvantage** on Dexterity and Constitution Saving Throws.
- At the end of each of its turns, the target may repeat the Constitution Saving Throw, ending Veinlock on a success.
- If the target is suffering from a **Bleeding** effect, it has **Disadvantage** on all Saving Throws made to end Veinlock.
- If the target is both **Bleeding** and your **Marked Prey**, it suffers **1d8 Blood Damage** at the start of each of its turns while Veinlocked.
- A creature may only be affected by one instance of Veinlock at a time.
- Veinlock has **no effect** on creatures without functioning blood unless another Bloodhunter ability specifically states otherwise.

### Lore

Within the Bloodhunter Orders, the Fourth Rank is known as **The Crimson Prison**. Few techniques inspire greater terror among monsters, for those who survive it often speak of being fully conscious while their own bodies refused every command.

Masters teach that Veinlock is not about overwhelming force. Instead, it is absolute precision. A Bloodhunter learns exactly which veins to constrict, which muscles to starve of blood, and which heartbeat to interrupt. To observers, the victim appears frozen by fear. In truth, they are imprisoned by their own blood.

The greatest Bloodhunters consider Veinlock an act of mercy. A fleeing monster endangers countless innocent lives. By ending the chase before it truly begins, the Bloodhunter ensures the hunt concludes swiftly and with as little unnecessary suffering as possible. Yet every use reminds the hunter how easily Sangramancy can command another living being—and how dangerous such power becomes when restraint is forgotten.

> *"The fastest prey still carries blood. That is all the advantage I require."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 5
### Description

At the pinnacle of Sangramancy, the Bloodhunter no longer merely influences the blood of another creature—they command it. Every heartbeat, every contraction of muscle, and every surge of adrenaline becomes another thread woven into the Bloodhunter's control. To those who witness the technique, it appears as though time itself has slowed around the victim. In reality, it is their own blood refusing to obey.

Master Bloodhunters use Veinlock only when a hunt must end. Ancient dragons attempting to take flight, legendary beasts preparing their final charge, and powerful fiends readying devastating magic have all been brought to a standstill by a hunter who understands the language of blood better than the creature carrying it.

The connection forged through Blood Rite, Blood Scent, and Blood Communion reaches its absolute peak at this rank. A wounded Marked Prey becomes almost impossible to escape with, as every drop of blood flowing through its veins strengthens the Bloodhunter's control.

Yet such mastery demands an immense sacrifice. The Bloodhunter pours an overwhelming amount of Sangramancy into another living being, flooding themselves with Blood Points in the process. Every use brings them dangerously close to Bloodlust, forcing them to decide whether ending the hunt is worth risking the monster growing within.

### Effects

- As an **Action**, target one creature with circulating blood within **35 metres**.
- Spend **50 MP**, sacrifice **20 HP**, and gain **25 Blood Points (BP)**.
- The target must succeed on a **Constitution Saving Throw** against your **Sangramancy DC**.
- On a failed save, the target becomes **Veinlocked** for **6 Combat Rounds**.
- While Veinlocked, the target:
  - Has its **Movement Speed reduced to 0**.
  - Cannot take the **Dash** action.
  - Cannot take **Reactions**.
  - Suffers **-2 Action Points** (minimum of 1 Action Point remaining).
  - Has **Disadvantage** on all Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution Saving Throws.
- At the end of each of its turns, the target may repeat the Constitution Saving Throw, ending Veinlock on a success.
- If the target is suffering from a **Bleeding** effect, it has **Disadvantage** on all Saving Throws made to end Veinlock.
- If the target is both **Bleeding** and your **Marked Prey**, it suffers **2d8 Blood Damage** at the start of each of its turns while Veinlocked.
- Veinlock has **no effect** on creatures without functioning blood unless another Bloodhunter ability specifically states otherwise.
- A creature may only be affected by one instance of Veinlock at a time.

### Lore

The Fifth Rank of Veinlock is spoken of with equal parts admiration and fear throughout every Bloodhunter Order. It is a technique so precise that masters claim it is no longer the Bloodhunter controlling another creature's blood—it is the blood itself recognising the authority of Sangramancy.

Stories tell of Apex Bloodhunters stopping charging giants mid-stride, grounding dragons before they could take flight, and freezing ancient vampires just long enough for the final strike to be delivered. These tales have become legends, yet every Bloodhunter knows they were purchased through immense sacrifice. Such absolute control can only be achieved by allowing the darkness within to rise alongside the power.

The elders warn that Veinlock is the ultimate temptation of Sangramancy. To command another creature's lifeblood so completely is to stand upon the edge of believing all life exists to be controlled. The greatest Bloodhunters never forget that blood is sacred, for the moment they do, the hunt ends—and Bloodlust begins.

> *"Every heart beats to the rhythm of blood. Learn that rhythm... and even legends shall stand still."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor
