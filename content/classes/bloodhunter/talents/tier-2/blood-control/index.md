# Blood Control

## Overview
True mastery of Sangramancy begins not with controlling another creature's blood, but with mastering your own. Every Bloodhunter must learn to regulate the blood flowing through their body, slowing its loss, resisting corruption, and preventing panic from overwhelming instinct. Blood Control is the first discipline that teaches this vital lesson.

Through rigorous training, the Bloodhunter learns to consciously direct the flow of their own blood. Wounds clot more rapidly, circulation becomes easier to regulate, and the body instinctively prioritises survival. While these techniques cannot prevent injury, they greatly reduce the effects of blood-based afflictions that would cripple an ordinary warrior.

Blood Control also strengthens the Bloodhunter's tolerance to Sangramancy itself. As they become more accustomed to manipulating the lifeblood within their own body, they are capable of safely containing greater amounts of Blood Points before Bloodlust begins to threaten their mind.

In moments of absolute desperation, a Bloodhunter may force their blood into complete obedience. This technique, known as **Emergency Blood Control**, suppresses the effects of a blood-based affliction through sheer force of will. The effort is immense, pushing the Bloodhunter closer to Bloodlust, but it may provide the precious seconds needed to survive the hunt.

## Rank 1
### Description

### Effects

- Permanently increase your **Maximum Blood Point Limit** by **5 BP**.
- Reduce the severity of all **Bleeding** effects affecting you by **1 Rank** (to a minimum of **Rank I**).
- **Emergency Blood Control:** After you are afflicted with **Bleeding**, you may choose to activate Emergency Blood Control **before the start of your next turn**.
- Gain **10 Blood Points (BP)**.
- The Bleeding effect deals **no damage** until the start of your next turn.
- This **does not remove** the Bleeding condition. If the condition has not been cured, its effects resume normally after the suppression ends.
- Emergency Blood Control may only be activated **once for each application** of a condition.

### Lore

The Bloodhunter Orders teach that every apprentice first learns to command the blood within themselves before attempting to influence the blood of another. Those unable to master their own body inevitably lose control when Sangramancy reaches its peak, becoming victims of Bloodlust rather than its master.

Veteran Bloodhunters often describe Blood Control as listening to the rhythm of their own heartbeat. With enough discipline, they can slow bleeding, strengthen weakened muscles, and force their body to continue fighting through wounds that would leave others helpless.

The elders consider this technique the foundation of every advanced Sangramantic discipline. A Bloodhunter who cannot command their own blood has no right to command the blood of another.

> *"Master your own blood before you ever dare command another's."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 2
### Description

With greater experience, the Bloodhunter develops an even deeper understanding of the blood flowing through their body. Every heartbeat becomes deliberate, every pulse controlled, and every wound is met with instinctive resistance. Sangramancy is no longer simply a source of power—it has become a means of preserving the Bloodhunter's own life.

Through relentless training, the Bloodhunter learns to identify foreign substances entering their bloodstream and instinctively slow their spread. Poisons that would rapidly overwhelm another adventurer are instead met by blood that actively resists corruption, buying valuable moments to finish the hunt or seek treatment.

This growing mastery also expands the Bloodhunter's tolerance to Blood Points. The emotions carried within their blood remain dangerous, but the hunter has learned to endure a greater burden before Bloodlust threatens to consume them. Every battle becomes another lesson in restraint, proving that true mastery is measured not by how much power one possesses, but by how well one controls it.

When faced with overwhelming injury or venom, the Bloodhunter can once again force their blood into complete obedience. Though this momentary suppression comes at the cost of Blood Points, it allows the hunter to delay the effects long enough to continue fighting. Every use is a reminder that control is never free.

### Effects

- Permanently increase your **Maximum Blood Point Limit** by **10 BP**.
- Reduce the severity of all **Bleeding** effects affecting you by **1 Rank** (minimum **Rank I**).
- Reduce the severity of all **Poison** effects affecting you by **1 Rank** (minimum **Rank I**).
- **Emergency Blood Control:** After you are afflicted with **Bleeding** or **Poison**, you may activate Emergency Blood Control **before the start of your next turn**.
- Gain **10 Blood Points (BP)**.
- The chosen condition deals **no damage** until the start of your next turn.
- This **does not remove** the condition. If it has not been cured, its effects resume normally after the suppression ends.
- Emergency Blood Control may only be activated **once for each application** of a condition.

### Lore

Among the Bloodhunter Orders, the Second Rank is considered the point where Sangramancy becomes as much a defensive discipline as an offensive one. Apprentices are no longer merely surviving their wounds—they are actively commanding the blood within their own bodies to resist corruption and maintain control under impossible circumstances.

Veterans often compare Blood Control to commanding an army. Every drop of blood answers the Bloodhunter's will, slowing poisons, sealing wounds, and preserving strength through sheer determination. To outsiders, these feats appear impossible, but Bloodhunters know they are earned only through countless battles and painful sacrifice.

The elders remind every hunter that Blood Control is not invincibility. It merely grants the opportunity to survive long enough to complete the hunt. Every time the blood is forced into submission, Bloodlust watches patiently, waiting for the moment discipline finally falters.

> *"The blood obeys only because I have taught it discipline through pain."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 3
### Description

By the Third Rank, the Bloodhunter's mastery over their own blood has become instinctive. Sangramancy no longer serves merely as a weapon against monsters—it has become a means of preserving the hunter's own life. Every heartbeat is consciously regulated, every drop of blood obeys the hunter's will, and the body instinctively resists afflictions that would overwhelm even seasoned adventurers.

The Bloodhunter has learned to isolate foreign contaminants within their bloodstream, slowing the spread of disease and preventing severe wounds from worsening as quickly. Their body becomes remarkably resilient, adapting to trauma through sheer force of discipline rather than natural endurance. To outside observers, it appears almost unnatural how quickly a Bloodhunter regains control after suffering devastating injuries.

This level of mastery also allows the Bloodhunter to contain a far greater amount of Sangramantic energy. The emotions carried within their blood remain dangerous, but the hunter has learned to suppress them beneath unwavering determination. Every battle strengthens this control, delaying the onset of Bloodlust without ever eliminating the threat entirely.

When the situation becomes desperate, the Bloodhunter can once again invoke **Emergency Blood Control**, forcing every drop of blood to obey. The effort is immense, flooding their body with Blood Points, but granting them precious moments to continue the hunt before the darkness threatens to consume them.

### Effects

- Permanently increase your **Maximum Blood Point Limit** by **15 BP**.
- Reduce the severity of all **Bleeding** effects affecting you by **1 Rank** (minimum **Rank I**).
- Reduce the severity of all **Poison** effects affecting you by **1 Rank** (minimum **Rank I**).
- Reduce the severity of all **Disease** effects affecting you by **1 Rank** (minimum **Rank I**).
- **Emergency Blood Control:** After you are afflicted with **Bleeding**, **Poison**, or **Disease**, you may activate Emergency Blood Control **before the start of your next turn**.
- Gain **15 Blood Points (BP)**.
- The chosen condition deals **no damage** until the start of your next turn.
- This **does not remove** the condition. If it has not been cured, its effects resume normally after the suppression ends.
- Emergency Blood Control may only be activated **once for each application** of a condition.

### Lore

Among the Bloodhunter Orders, Rank III is known as **The Crimson Discipline**. It marks the point where a Bloodhunter no longer merely survives their wounds—they command them. Through countless battles and relentless training, the hunter learns to slow the spread of corruption, regulate their own circulation, and force their body to endure hardships that would claim lesser warriors.

Masters often teach that true Blood Control is not about denying pain, but about refusing to let pain dictate one's actions. Every scar becomes another lesson, every wound another opportunity to strengthen the bond between hunter and blood. This unwavering discipline is what separates experienced Bloodhunters from those who eventually surrender to Bloodlust.

The elders caution that mastery is never permanent. Every hunt tests the Bloodhunter anew, and every use of Emergency Blood Control reminds them that commanding their blood requires constant vigilance. A single lapse in discipline is all Bloodlust needs to reclaim what has been held at bay.

> *"The blood remembers every wound. I simply remind it who gives the orders."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 4
### Description

The Fourth Rank of Blood Control represents a level of Sangramantic mastery that few Bloodhunters ever achieve. Their blood no longer simply responds to commands—it anticipates them. Every heartbeat is deliberate, every vein carries the will of its master, and the body instinctively resists corruption before it can fully take hold.

Years of relentless training have taught the Bloodhunter to recognise the subtle changes caused by venom, disease, and catastrophic injuries. The moment these afflictions enter the bloodstream, the Bloodhunter instinctively redirects circulation, slowing their spread and reducing their severity. What would cripple another warrior becomes little more than another obstacle to overcome.

This mastery also greatly expands the Bloodhunter's capacity to contain Blood Points. The emotions that once threatened to erupt into Bloodlust are held in check through absolute discipline, allowing the hunter to draw upon far greater reserves of Sangramantic power before losing control.

In moments where death seems inevitable, the Bloodhunter may invoke **Emergency Blood Control**, overwhelming their own body with Sangramantic force. Every drop of blood obeys without hesitation, suppressing the effects of even the deadliest afflictions. The effort comes at a terrible price, rapidly increasing Blood Points, but it grants the Bloodhunter one more opportunity to finish the hunt.

### Effects

- Permanently increase your **Maximum Blood Point Limit** by **20 BP**.
- Reduce the severity of all **Bleeding** effects affecting you by **1 Rank** (minimum **Rank I**).
- Reduce the severity of all **Poison** effects affecting you by **1 Rank** (minimum **Rank I**).
- Reduce the severity of all **Disease** effects affecting you by **1 Rank** (minimum **Rank I**).
- Reduce the severity of all **Necrosis**, **Venom**, or other blood-affecting conditions by **1 Rank** (minimum **Rank I**).
- **Emergency Blood Control:** After you are afflicted with **Bleeding**, **Poison**, **Disease**, **Necrosis**, **Venom**, or another blood-affecting condition, you may activate Emergency Blood Control **before the start of your next turn**.
- Gain **20 Blood Points (BP)**.
- The chosen condition deals **no damage** until the start of your next turn.
- This **does not remove** the condition. If it has not been cured, its effects resume normally after the suppression ends.
- Emergency Blood Control may only be activated **once for each application** of a condition.

### Lore

Within the Bloodhunter Orders, Rank IV is known as **The Crimson Sovereignty**. Bloodhunters who achieve this mastery are said to rule over their own lifeblood with absolute authority. Their veins no longer serve merely as vessels—they become conduits of unwavering discipline, capable of resisting horrors that would overwhelm even legendary heroes.

Many stories tell of Bloodhunters continuing to fight after suffering venom capable of killing giants or diseases that emptied entire villages. Their survival is not due to immunity, but because their blood refuses to surrender while its master still stands. Every heartbeat becomes a declaration that Sangramancy answers only the will of the Bloodhunter.

The elders caution that this mastery walks a dangerous path. Every time Emergency Blood Control is invoked, the Bloodhunter forces their emotions deeper beneath the surface instead of confronting them. Bloodlust does not disappear—it waits patiently beneath the calm, growing stronger with every act of suppression until the hunter is once again forced to choose between control and surrender.

> *"My blood knows every poison, every wound, and every curse. It has learned to fear only one thing... my command."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 5
### Description

The Fifth Rank of Blood Control is regarded as one of the greatest achievements in Sangramancy. At this stage, the Bloodhunter has attained absolute mastery over the blood flowing through their body. Every heartbeat, every vein, and every drop of blood responds instantly to their will, allowing them to resist afflictions that would claim even the mightiest of heroes.

To the Bloodhunter, blood is no longer merely a life-giving fluid—it has become a disciplined servant. The spread of toxins is slowed before they can take hold, catastrophic wounds clot with unnatural speed, and corruption carried through the bloodstream struggles to gain purchase. While these afflictions remain dangerous, the Bloodhunter has learned to deny them the opportunity to reach their full potential.

This mastery also represents the greatest control over Bloodlust that a mortal can achieve. The Bloodhunter has learned to contain immense amounts of Blood Points before surrendering to the darkness within. They understand that Bloodlust can never truly be conquered, only restrained through unwavering discipline and sacrifice.

When death appears inevitable, the Bloodhunter may invoke the ultimate expression of this mastery—**Emergency Blood Control**. Every drop of blood is forced into complete obedience, suppressing even the most devastating blood-borne afflictions for a brief moment. The strain is immense, flooding the Bloodhunter with Blood Points, but granting one final opportunity to finish the hunt before Bloodlust demands its price.

### Effects

- Permanently increase your **Maximum Blood Point Limit** by **25 BP**.
- Reduce the severity of all **Bleeding** effects affecting you by **1 Rank** (minimum **Rank I**).
- Reduce the severity of all **Poison** effects affecting you by **1 Rank** (minimum **Rank I**).
- Reduce the severity of all **Disease** effects affecting you by **1 Rank** (minimum **Rank I**).
- Reduce the severity of all **Necrosis**, **Venom**, and all other **blood-affecting conditions** by **1 Rank** (minimum **Rank I**).
- **Emergency Blood Control:** After you are afflicted with a blood-affecting condition, you may activate Emergency Blood Control **before the start of your next turn**.
- Gain **25 Blood Points (BP)**.
- The chosen condition deals **no damage and its effects are suppressed** until the start of your next turn.
- This **does not remove** the condition. If it has not been cured, its effects immediately resume after the suppression ends.
- Emergency Blood Control may only be activated **once for each application** of a condition.

### Lore

Within the Bloodhunter Orders, Rank V is known as **The Crimson Dominion**. Only the oldest and most disciplined masters ever attain this level of control, where Sangramancy ceases to be a dangerous art and instead becomes an extension of the hunter's own will. Their blood obeys without hesitation, resisting corruption, preserving life, and carrying them through battles that should have ended in death.

Legends speak of Bloodhunters who continued fighting while suffering wounds that would kill ordinary warriors many times over. They did not survive because they were immune to pain or poison, but because every drop of blood within them refused to yield until their master's task was complete. To witness such a hunter in battle is to watch determination itself take physical form.

Yet every Grandmaster teaches the same final lesson. Absolute control over one's blood does not mean victory over Bloodlust. The darkness is never destroyed—it is merely held in chains forged through discipline, sacrifice, and relentless resolve. The Bloodhunter who forgets this truth will discover that even the strongest chains can one day break.

> *"My blood no longer asks permission to fight. It waits only for my command."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor
