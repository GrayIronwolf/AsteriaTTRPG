# Blood Rush

## Overview
Not every hunt is won through patience. Sometimes the Bloodhunter must close the distance before their prey can flee, cast a spell, or recover from its wounds. Blood Rush is the first technique that teaches a Bloodhunter to turn the power of Sangramancy inward, accelerating their own body instead of manipulating the blood of another.

By flooding their veins with mana and forcing their heart to beat beyond its natural rhythm, the Bloodhunter enters a brief state of heightened speed and awareness. Muscles respond instantly, breathing becomes effortless, and every instinct screams for the hunt to continue. To onlookers, the Bloodhunter appears to burst forward with unnatural acceleration, driven by a determination that borders on obsession.

Unlike Blood Rite, Blood Rush requires no blood sacrifice. Instead, the strain is placed upon the body itself. Mana fuels the Sangramancy, stamina keeps the body moving, and the emotional intensity generated by the technique feeds the Bloodhunter's growing Blood Points. Every activation is another reminder that even the pursuit of prey carries the risk of Bloodlust.

The Orders teach that Blood Rush is never to be used for retreat. Once the hunt begins, the Bloodhunter commits to seeing it through.

## Rank 1

### Effects

- As a **Bonus Action**, activate Blood Rush.
- Spend **15 MP**, **15 SP**, and gain **5 Blood Points (BP)**.
- Blood Rush lasts for **2 Combat Rounds**.
- While Blood Rush is active:
  - Your movement speed increases by **3 metres**.
  - If you move at least **3 metres** towards your **Marked Prey**, gain **+1 to your first melee Attack Roll** against that target each turn.
- While Blood Rush is active, if you willingly end your movement **farther away from your Marked Prey** than where you started your movement, Blood Rush immediately ends.
- Blood Rush may only have one active instance at a time.

### Lore

Blood Rush is one of the first mobility techniques taught to Bloodhunters after they master Blood Rite. While many warriors rely upon disciplined footwork or physical training, Bloodhunters instead trust the power flowing through their own veins. Every heartbeat becomes another step in the hunt, every breath another promise that the prey will not escape.

Veteran hunters often describe Blood Rush as surrendering to instinct without surrendering to Bloodlust. For a few precious moments, the Bloodhunter allows the predatory nature awakened through Sangramancy to guide every movement, trusting years of training to keep the darkness in check.

Among the Orders, apprentices who misuse Blood Rush to flee from battle are required to retrain the technique from the beginning. The lesson is simple: Blood Rush was never created to escape danger. It exists so that no worthy prey can ever outrun the hunter sworn to pursue it.

> *"The hunt ends when my heart does—not when yours finds somewhere to hide."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 2
### Description

With greater experience, the Bloodhunter learns to sustain Blood Rush for longer without allowing the violent surge of Sangramancy to overwhelm their body. Their heart beats with unnatural speed, muscles are flooded with oxygen, and every movement becomes sharper and more deliberate. What once felt like a desperate burst of speed now becomes a controlled state of relentless pursuit.

The Bloodhunter no longer chases their prey—they pressure it. Every step forces the quarry into making mistakes, every burst of speed closes another avenue of escape, and every heartbeat reminds the Bloodhunter that the hunt is drawing to its inevitable conclusion.

Although Blood Rush no longer tears at the Bloodhunter's body, it still feeds the emotional storm within. The exhilaration of the chase, the anticipation of the kill, and the instinct to pursue all generate Blood Points, bringing the hunter ever closer to Bloodlust.

Veteran Bloodhunters teach that Blood Rush is not about moving faster than your prey. It is about making them realise that no matter how quickly they run, you are always gaining ground.

### Effects

- As a **Bonus Action**, activate Blood Rush.
- Spend **20 MP**, **20 SP**, and gain **10 Blood Points (BP)**.
- Blood Rush lasts for **2 Combat Rounds**.
- While Blood Rush is active:
  - Your movement speed increases by **4 metres**.
  - If you move at least **3 metres** towards your **Marked Prey**, gain **+1 to your first Attack Roll** and **+1 Damage** against that target each turn.
- While Blood Rush is active, if you willingly end your movement **farther away from your Marked Prey** than where you started your movement, Blood Rush immediately ends.
- Blood Rush may only have one active instance at a time.

### Lore

Among experienced Bloodhunters, Blood Rush is no longer viewed as a simple movement technique. It becomes a declaration that the hunt has entered its final stage. Once activated, the Bloodhunter abandons hesitation entirely, trusting instinct, training, and Sangramancy to guide every step toward their quarry.

Many monsters have mistaken Blood Rush for reckless aggression. Those that survive quickly realise the truth—it is a calculated pursuit. Every movement is measured, every path anticipated, and every escape route methodically closed until surrender or death becomes the prey's only remaining choice.

The elders remind every Bloodhunter that speed alone never wins a hunt. Discipline does. Blood Rush simply ensures that discipline arrives before the prey has the chance to disappear.

> *"You think I am chasing you. I stopped chasing long ago. Now I am simply deciding where this hunt ends."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 3
### Description

By the Third Rank, Blood Rush has become second nature to the Bloodhunter. Their body has adapted to the immense strain placed upon it by Sangramancy, allowing them to enter a state of heightened speed without sacrificing precision. Every heartbeat echoes through their veins like a battle drum, sharpening their instincts and preparing them for the hunt before blades are ever drawn.

Veteran Bloodhunters often activate Blood Rush the instant they sense their prey. Their heightened awareness allows them to react before others have even recognised the threat, turning hesitation into overwhelming momentum. Few creatures are prepared for a hunter that seems to move the very moment combat begins.

This heightened state does not simply increase speed—it awakens the Bloodhunter's predatory instincts. Every movement is focused on closing the distance, every attack is delivered with greater confidence, and every heartbeat drives them relentlessly toward their quarry.

Yet every accelerated heartbeat feeds the Bloodhunter's Blood Points. The exhilaration of the chase is intoxicating, and many Bloodhunters have learned too late that becoming addicted to the hunt is the first step towards Bloodlust.

### Effects

- As a **Bonus Action**, activate Blood Rush.
- Spend **25 MP**, **25 SP**, and gain **15 Blood Points (BP)**.
- Blood Rush lasts for **2 Combat Rounds**.
- While Blood Rush is active:
  - Your movement speed increases by **5 metres**.
  - Gain **+2 Initiative**.
  - If you move at least **3 metres** towards your **Marked Prey**, gain **+2 to your first Attack Roll** and **+1 Damage** against that target each turn.
- While Blood Rush is active, if you willingly end your movement **farther away from your Marked Prey** than where you started your movement, Blood Rush immediately ends.
- Blood Rush may only have one active instance at a time.

### Lore

Within the Bloodhunter Orders, Rank III marks the moment a hunter truly earns the title of predator. No longer reacting to danger, they become the one dictating the pace of every encounter. Their heartbeat quickens, their senses sharpen, and their prey suddenly finds itself fighting a hunter who was ready before the battle even began.

Masters often compare Blood Rush to a wolf beginning its chase. The first few steps determine the outcome of the hunt, and hesitation is the difference between success and starvation. A Bloodhunter who has mastered this technique understands that momentum is a weapon every bit as deadly as steel.

The elders caution that Blood Rush is addictive. The thrill of moving faster than any ordinary warrior and reaching prey before it can escape is exhilarating. Those who become obsessed with that feeling often find themselves activating Blood Rush long before it is necessary, unknowingly feeding the Bloodlust that waits patiently within.

> *"The battle begins when others draw their weapons. My hunt begins with the first heartbeat."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 4
### Description

At the Fourth Rank, Blood Rush becomes a terrifying display of controlled Sangramancy. The Bloodhunter has learned to regulate the violent surge of blood racing through their body, allowing them to move with incredible speed while maintaining absolute focus on the hunt. Every heartbeat is deliberately accelerated, flooding muscles with strength and sharpening instincts beyond mortal limits.

To witnesses, the Bloodhunter appears almost impossible to pin down. They weave between enemies, close impossible distances, and remain relentlessly fixed upon their prey. Those attempting to intercept them often strike nothing but empty air as the Bloodhunter slips past in pursuit of their target.

Despite this mastery, the technique remains dangerous. The Bloodhunter willingly drives their body beyond its natural limits, embracing the exhilaration of the chase while Blood Points rapidly accumulate. Veterans understand that the greatest threat is no longer physical exhaustion, but the growing temptation to remain in this heightened state forever.

Within the Bloodhunter Orders, Rank IV is known as **The Crimson Pursuit**, for it is said that once a Bloodhunter enters this state, even the fastest prey begins to realise there is nowhere left to run.

### Effects

- As a **Bonus Action**, activate Blood Rush.
- Spend **35 MP**, **30 SP**, and gain **20 Blood Points (BP)**.
- Blood Rush lasts for **2 Combat Rounds**.
- While Blood Rush is active:
  - Your movement speed increases by **6 metres**.
  - Gain **+3 Initiative**.
  - Opportunity Attacks made against you suffer **-2 to their Attack Rolls**.
  - If you move at least **3 metres** towards your **Marked Prey**, gain **+2 to your first Attack Roll** and **+2 Damage** against that target each turn.
- If you willingly end your movement **farther away from your Marked Prey** than where you started your movement, Blood Rush immediately ends.
- Blood Rush may only have one active instance at a time.

### Lore

The Fourth Rank is where Blood Rush ceases to be a burst of speed and becomes an extension of the Bloodhunter's identity. Their pursuit is no longer driven by anger or desperation, but by unwavering purpose. Every movement is efficient, every stride calculated, and every heartbeat serves a single goal—to end the hunt.

Many monsters speak of encountering Bloodhunters capable of appearing wherever escape seemed possible. They are not teleporting, nor are they bending time. They simply understand momentum so completely that every movement flows seamlessly into the next, leaving no opportunity for their prey to recover.

Masters caution that this level of Sangramancy can become intoxicating. The thrill of overwhelming speed, perfect control, and absolute pursuit is seductive. Those who begin chasing the feeling instead of the prey often discover that Bloodlust has quietly begun chasing them in return.

> *"You can outrun my footsteps... but you'll never outrun my heartbeat."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 5
### Description

The Fifth Rank of Blood Rush represents the pinnacle of physical Sangramancy. Every beat of the Bloodhunter's heart becomes a catalyst for overwhelming speed, allowing them to pursue their prey with relentless precision. Their blood surges through every vein with incredible force, pushing their body to the very limits of mortal endurance while their mind remains unnervingly calm.

To those watching the hunt unfold, the Bloodhunter appears almost impossible to escape. They cover impossible distances in moments, effortlessly weaving through the chaos of battle while their eyes never leave their chosen prey. Every movement is purposeful, every stride calculated, and every heartbeat carries the certainty that the hunt will end exactly where the Bloodhunter chooses.

Yet this mastery demands immense discipline. The rush of power is intoxicating, and the Bloodhunter willingly embraces a torrent of Blood Points every time they unleash their full potential. Veterans understand that Blood Rush is no longer simply a technique—it is a constant test of self-control. One careless activation while already nearing Bloodlust can transform the hunter into the very monster they have spent their life pursuing.

Among the Bloodhunter Orders, this mastery is known as **The Crimson Pursuit**, for once a Bloodhunter reaches this stage, very few creatures ever escape alive.

### Effects

- As a **Bonus Action**, activate Blood Rush.
- Spend **45 MP**, **35 SP**, and gain **25 Blood Points (BP)**.
- Blood Rush lasts for **2 Combat Rounds**.
- While Blood Rush is active:
  - Your movement speed increases by **8 metres**.
  - Gain **+4 Initiative**.
  - Opportunity Attacks made against you suffer **-4 to their Attack Rolls**.
  - If you move at least **3 metres** towards your **Marked Prey**, gain **+3 to your first Attack Roll** and **+3 Damage** against that target each turn.
- If you willingly end your movement farther away from your **Marked Prey** than where you started your movement, Blood Rush immediately ends.
- Blood Rush may only have one active instance at a time.

### Lore

Only the greatest Bloodhunters ever master the Fifth Rank of Blood Rush. Their heartbeat has become as much a weapon as the blade they carry, allowing them to relentlessly pursue prey across battlefields, forests, and ruined cities without ever surrendering momentum. Many monsters have mistaken the sudden silence before their death as safety, only to find the Bloodhunter already standing behind them.

Masters teach that Blood Rush is not about becoming the fastest warrior alive. It is about becoming the one creature your prey can never escape. Every lesson, every sacrifice, and every hunt leads to this singular truth: the Bloodhunter decides when the chase ends.

The elders leave every apprentice with the same warning. Speed is intoxicating, and the thrill of the hunt is one of the strongest emotions Sangramancy can amplify. The Bloodhunter who begins chasing that feeling instead of their prey has already taken the first step towards Bloodlust. True masters never forget that they command the hunt—the hunt must never command them.

> *"Run until your heart bursts if you wish. Mine has already decided where yours will stop beating."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor
