# Blood Shield

## Overview
Blood Shield is one of the first defensive applications of Sangramancy taught within the Bloodhunter Orders. Rather than turning their blood into a weapon, the Bloodhunter willingly sacrifices a portion of their own life force, drawing fresh blood from beneath their skin and suspending it in the air through the power of mana.

The blood rapidly hardens into a thin crimson barrier that circles the Bloodhunter like a living shield. Although delicate in appearance, the enchanted blood is capable of deflecting blades, arrows, and magical attacks before instantly reforming around its master. Every strike absorbed by the shield reminds the Bloodhunter that their own blood is both their greatest weapon and their greatest defence.

Unlike conventional armour, Blood Shield demands sacrifice. The Bloodhunter willingly weakens themselves to create a barrier capable of preserving their life during the most dangerous moments of battle. Veterans often describe the technique as placing complete trust in Sangramancy, believing that a small wound now is worth surviving a fatal blow later.

The Orders teach that Blood Shield should never become a crutch. Every activation sheds blood, fuels Blood Points, and edges the Bloodhunter closer to Bloodlust. A wise hunter knows that the strongest shield is not one that is used constantly, but one that is summoned only when death is certain to follow without it.

### Effects

- As a **Bonus Action**, activate Blood Shield.
- Spend **20 MP**, sacrifice **5 HP**, and gain **5 Blood Points (BP)**.
- Blood Shield lasts for **2 Combat Rounds**.
- While Blood Shield is active, gain **+2 Armour Class (AC)**.
- Blood Shield is considered a **magical effect**.
- Only one Blood Shield may be active at a time.
- Blood Points gained through Blood Shield count towards your **Blood Point Limit** and may contribute to triggering **Bloodlust**.

### Lore

Among experienced Bloodhunters, Blood Shield is considered a symbol of absolute faith in Sangramancy. The hunter willingly spills their own blood before the enemy has the chance, trusting that the sacrifice made now will preserve their life long enough to complete the hunt.

The crimson shield has become an unmistakable sight upon battlefields across Asteria. Monsters that recognise its appearance know they face a Bloodhunter willing to sacrifice their own life to ensure the hunt continues. Many have broken their weapons against the shimmering barrier, only to watch the blood reform as though it possessed a will of its own.

The elders remind every apprentice that blood is sacred. To shape it into a shield is not merely a display of power, but a promise that every drop sacrificed will be repaid through the successful completion of the hunt.

> *"The first blood I spill in every battle is my own. Every drop after that belongs to my prey."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 2
### Description

As a Bloodhunter's mastery of Sangramancy deepens, so too does their ability to shape their own lifeblood into a resilient defensive barrier. The once thin crimson shield becomes denser and more stable, its surface constantly flowing and reforming as fresh mana courses through the Bloodhunter's veins.

The shield no longer merely deflects attacks—it anticipates them. Each strike causes the living blood to ripple across its surface before instantly hardening again, dispersing the force of incoming blows. To an observer, it appears as though the Bloodhunter is surrounded by a crimson halo that instinctively moves to intercept danger.

Maintaining such a shield requires a greater sacrifice than before. More blood must be offered, more mana must sustain its form, and the Bloodhunter's emotions surge alongside the flow of Sangramancy. Every heartbeat strengthens the barrier while simultaneously feeding the Blood Points accumulating within the hunter.

Veterans often teach that Blood Shield should not be viewed as armour, but as a promise. Every drop of blood willingly sacrificed is another declaration that the hunt will continue, regardless of the wounds yet to come.

- Blood Shield lasts for **3 Combat Rounds**.
- While Blood Shield is active, gain **+2 Armour Class (AC)**.
- Blood Shield is considered a **magical effect**.
- Only one Blood Shield may be active at a time.
- Blood Points gained through Blood Shield count towards your **Blood Point Limit** and may contribute to triggering **Bloodlust**.

### Lore

Among the Bloodhunter Orders, Rank II is seen as the point where the shield begins to feel alive. Apprentices no longer force the blood into shape through sheer will alone. Instead, they learn to allow Sangramancy to guide its movement, creating a barrier that responds almost instinctively to danger.

Many Bloodhunters describe hearing the rhythm of their own heartbeat within the shield itself. Every pulse reinforces its strength, reminding the hunter that the barrier exists only because they willingly gave a part of themselves to create it.

The elders caution that Blood Shield is never truly free. Every activation strengthens the Bloodhunter's resolve, but it also deepens their connection to the darkness carried within their blood. A shield born from sacrifice will always demand another sacrifice before the hunt is over.

> *"Steel may guard the body, but only sacrifice can protect the soul."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 3
### Description

At the Third Rank, Blood Shield becomes a true manifestation of Sangramancy. The Bloodhunter has mastered the delicate balance between sacrifice and control, allowing greater quantities of their own blood to leave the body without losing cohesion. The crimson barrier grows thicker, stronger, and far more resilient, rotating around the Bloodhunter in a constant orbit of living blood.

Each impact against the shield sends crimson ripples across its surface before it reforms almost instantly. Blades glance away, arrows are deflected, and spells collide against a barrier sustained by both blood and willpower. Every heartbeat reinforces the shield, binding it ever closer to the Bloodhunter's life force.

The shield is not merely protection—it is a declaration. Every enemy that witnesses Blood Shield understands they face a warrior willing to sacrifice their own life to survive long enough to complete the hunt. Such determination alone has broken the morale of many monsters before the first blow is ever struck.

Yet greater protection demands greater sacrifice. More blood is drawn from the body, more mana is consumed to sustain the barrier, and the Bloodhunter's Blood Points rise with every activation. The stronger the shield becomes, the louder the call of Bloodlust grows.

- Blood Shield lasts for **3 Combat Rounds**.
- While Blood Shield is active, gain **+3 Armour Class (AC)**.
- Blood Shield is considered a **magical effect**.
- Only one Blood Shield may be active at a time.
- Blood Points gained through Blood Shield count towards your **Blood Point Limit** and may contribute towards triggering **Bloodlust**.

### Lore

Bloodhunters who master the Third Rank are recognised throughout the Orders as warriors capable of standing against creatures far stronger than themselves. Their Blood Shield is no longer viewed as a desperate defence, but as an extension of their own resolve. Every drop of blood sacrificed becomes another promise that the hunt will not end until justice has been delivered.

Experienced Bloodhunters often describe the sensation as carrying a second heartbeat around their body. The crimson barrier pulses in perfect rhythm with their own heart, responding instinctively to danger as though it possesses a life of its own.

The elders continue to remind every apprentice that no shield lasts forever. Blood Shield is not intended to make a Bloodhunter invincible—it exists to buy precious moments when death seems inevitable. Those moments are often all a true hunter needs.

> *"Every strike against my shield reminds me that I chose this pain before my enemy ever could."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 4
### Description

The Fourth Rank of Blood Shield marks the point where Sangramancy becomes an unshakable bastion rather than a simple defence. The Bloodhunter can now draw forth a far greater quantity of their own blood, shaping it into a dense crimson barrier that constantly circles their body like a vigilant guardian. Every heartbeat strengthens the shield, reinforcing its structure before the enemy can exploit even the smallest weakness.

Unlike steel or enchanted armour, Blood Shield is alive. It shifts, twists, and flows around incoming attacks, instinctively placing itself between the Bloodhunter and danger. Blades glance away, arrows are deflected from their mark, and magical energy crashes harmlessly against the crimson barrier before dissolving into crimson mist.

Such mastery demands tremendous sacrifice. The Bloodhunter willingly offers more of their own life to sustain the shield, trusting that every drop of blood given now will preserve countless more before the hunt is over. The emotions stirred by this sacrifice surge through Sangramancy, rapidly increasing the Bloodhunter's Blood Points and testing their resolve against the ever-present threat of Bloodlust.

Within the Bloodhunter Orders, reaching this rank is considered proof that a hunter has learned to trust their blood completely, knowing that the strongest defence is often forged from the greatest sacrifice.

### Lore

Among the Bloodhunter Orders, the Fourth Rank is known as **The Crimson Bastion**. Bloodhunters who achieve this level no longer fear standing before overwhelming foes, for they know that every sacrifice strengthens the shield protecting them. Their confidence does not come from arrogance, but from absolute faith in the Sangramancy flowing through their veins.

Stories tell of Bloodhunters standing against charging giants, dragonfire, and swarms of monsters while their crimson barrier absorbed blow after blow. Those who witnessed these battles often believed the Bloodhunter was protected by divine intervention, never realising the shield was sustained solely by the hunter's own blood and unwavering determination.

The elders caution that every shield eventually fades. A Bloodhunter who believes themselves untouchable has already taken the first step toward Bloodlust. The shield exists to preserve the hunter long enough to complete the hunt—not to replace skill, discipline, or wisdom.

> *"A fortress built of stone will one day crumble. A fortress built of sacrifice stands for as long as its guardian draws breath."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 5
### Description

The Fifth Rank of Blood Shield represents the pinnacle of defensive Sangramancy. Through complete mastery over their own lifeblood, the Bloodhunter can create a magnificent crimson barrier that continuously circles their body, moving with a will that mirrors their own. Every heartbeat feeds the shield, allowing it to reshape itself in an instant to intercept blades, arrows, spells, and monstrous claws before they can reach the Bloodhunter.

The barrier appears almost ethereal, formed from countless strands of enchanted blood flowing together in perfect harmony. It is neither armour nor magic alone, but a living extension of the Bloodhunter's soul. Each strike against the shield sends crimson ripples across its surface before it reforms without hesitation, standing as a testament to the hunter's willingness to sacrifice themselves for victory.

Such mastery comes at an immense price. The Bloodhunter must willingly surrender a significant portion of their own life force, trusting that Sangramancy will preserve them long enough to complete the hunt. Every activation floods their body with powerful emotions, dramatically increasing their Blood Points and bringing them perilously close to Bloodlust.

Among the Bloodhunter Orders, only the greatest masters ever achieve this technique. They are known as the **Crimson Wardens**, hunters whose blood alone is said to be stronger than steel.

### Effects

- As a **Bonus Action**, activate Blood Shield.
- Spend **60 MP**, sacrifice **20 HP**, and gain **25 Blood Points (BP)**.
- Blood Shield lasts for **5 Combat Rounds**.
- While Blood Shield is active, gain **+5 Armour Class (AC)**.
- Blood Shield is considered a **magical effect**.
- Only one Blood Shield may be active at a time.
- Blood Points gained through Blood Shield count towards your **Blood Point Limit** and may contribute to triggering **Bloodlust**.

### Lore

To become a Crimson Warden is to place absolute faith in Sangramancy. The Bloodhunter no longer questions whether their blood will protect them—they know it will. Every sacrifice strengthens the bond between hunter and shield until the barrier becomes as natural as the heartbeat that sustains it.

Legends tell of Bloodhunters standing alone against overwhelming armies while crimson shields danced around them like living guardians. Arrows shattered, enchanted weapons glanced away, and monstrous blows were turned aside by nothing more than blood willingly given in the name of the hunt. Those fortunate enough to witness such feats rarely forget the sight.

Yet every master teaches the same final lesson. The Blood Shield is not the source of a Bloodhunter's strength—it is merely a reflection of it. The true shield has always been the unwavering resolve to sacrifice everything necessary to protect others from the horrors that stalk the world. Blood may form the barrier, but conviction is what truly makes it unbreakable.

> *"Steel protects the body. Blood protects the hunter. Conviction protects the soul."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor
