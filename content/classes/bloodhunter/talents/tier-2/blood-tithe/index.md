# Blood Tithe

## Overview
Every Bloodhunter understands that Sangramancy is a cycle of sacrifice and reward. Blood fuels power, power fuels Bloodlust, and Bloodlust must eventually be brought back under control. Blood Tithe is the sacred ritual that maintains this balance.

Rather than drawing upon mana, the Bloodhunter willingly offers a portion of their own life force as a tithe. The sacrificed blood is consumed by Sangramancy, calming the turmoil raging within the hunter's soul while restoring the strength needed to continue the hunt. It is a painful exchange, but one every experienced Bloodhunter learns to master.

Unlike Blood Rite or Blood Shield, Blood Tithe is not an offensive technique. It is an act of discipline. The Bloodhunter acknowledges the darkness building within themselves and willingly pays a greater price before it can consume them. Many hunters perform this ritual between clashes in battle, choosing to bleed by their own hand rather than allow Bloodlust to decide their fate.

Within the Bloodhunter Orders, apprentices are taught that every hunt is measured not by the monsters slain, but by how many times the hunter successfully mastered the monster growing within themselves.

### Effects

- As a **Bonus Action**, activate Blood Tithe.
- Sacrifice **10 HP**.
- Choose **one** of the following:
  - Restore **15 Mana Points (MP)**.
  - Restore **15 Stamina Points (SP)**.
- Reduce your current **Blood Points (BP) by 5**.
- Blood Tithe cannot restore HP.
- Blood Tithe cannot restore MP or SP beyond their normal maximum values.
- Blood Tithe may only be used while you have at least **5 Blood Points**.

### Lore

Among the Bloodhunter Orders, Blood Tithe is regarded as one of the most important techniques a hunter can learn. Every Bloodhunter inevitably walks the razor's edge between mastery and Bloodlust, and only those who learn to temper their emotions survive long enough to become veterans.

The ritual is performed differently by every Order. Some carve symbols into their palms, others allow blood to drip onto ancient runestones, while some simply whisper a silent oath before offering their sacrifice. Regardless of tradition, the purpose remains the same—to remind the Bloodhunter that power without restraint is the quickest path to ruin.

The elders often tell apprentices that Blood Tithe is not an act of weakness. It is the greatest display of strength a Bloodhunter can perform, for it proves they still command their own blood rather than allowing their blood to command them.

> *"The greatest sacrifice is not the blood you spill upon the battlefield... it is the blood you willingly offer to remain yourself."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 2
### Description

As a Bloodhunter grows stronger, so too does their understanding of balance. Every use of Sangramancy pushes them closer to Bloodlust, but Blood Tithe allows them to reclaim a measure of control through deliberate sacrifice. Rather than fearing the darkness within, experienced Bloodhunters learn to manage it, paying a blood tithe before it can overwhelm them.

At this stage, the ritual becomes far more efficient. The Bloodhunter has refined the flow of Sangramancy through their body, allowing a greater portion of the sacrificed life force to be transformed into renewed strength. Mana is restored with greater clarity, stamina returns more quickly, and the emotional burden carried within their blood begins to settle.

Veterans often perform Blood Tithe in the brief moments between battles, recognising that the hunt is rarely won through brute strength alone. Endurance, discipline, and restraint are every bit as important as the blade carried into combat.

Although the ritual calms the Bloodhunter's spirit, it never comes without consequence. The blood offered cannot be reclaimed, and every sacrifice leaves another reminder that true power is earned through suffering rather than granted freely.

### Lore

Within the Bloodhunter Orders, Rank II is considered the first step towards true emotional discipline. Apprentices learn that surviving a hunt requires more than defeating monsters—it requires surviving the conflict raging within themselves. Every successful Blood Tithe is proof that the Bloodhunter still commands their own destiny rather than surrendering to Bloodlust.

Many masters describe the ritual as a conversation with Sangramancy itself. The Bloodhunter willingly offers blood, and in return receives only enough strength to continue the hunt—never more than they have earned. This sacred exchange reminds every hunter that sacrifice and discipline must always remain in balance.

The elders caution that Blood Tithe should never become a crutch. A hunter who constantly relies upon it without learning restraint will eventually discover that no amount of sacrifice can restore a soul that has already surrendered to Bloodlust.

> *"Power is easy to claim. Wisdom is knowing when to surrender a little of it to remain yourself."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 3
### Description

The Third Rank of Blood Tithe marks the moment a Bloodhunter truly understands the balance between sacrifice and restraint. Sangramancy is no longer viewed as an uncontrollable force that threatens to consume them, but as a living current that can be guided through discipline, experience, and unwavering resolve.

When performing Blood Tithe, the Bloodhunter willingly opens a deeper wound, offering a greater portion of their own life force to restore what has been exhausted. The ritual is painful, but it clears the mind and steadies the heart, allowing the Bloodhunter to continue the hunt with renewed focus. Every drop of blood offered is a conscious decision to remain in control rather than surrender to Bloodlust.

Veteran Bloodhunters often perform this ritual in the brief moments between fierce exchanges, knowing that the greatest enemy is not always standing before them. Sometimes the true battle is fought within, where every Blood Point tempts the hunter to abandon reason for overwhelming power.

The Orders teach that Blood Tithe is not a means of avoiding Bloodlust forever. It is simply another tool that allows a disciplined hunter to decide when they are willing to embrace the darkness—and when they are not.

### Lore

Within every Bloodhunter Order, Rank III is considered the beginning of true mastery over Sangramancy. The Bloodhunter has learned that strength is not measured by how much blood they can spill, but by how much they are willing to sacrifice without losing themselves.

Masters often describe Blood Tithe as a ritual of humility. The hunter willingly weakens themselves so that they may continue protecting others, proving that sacrifice remains the foundation of every Bloodhunter's path. Those who perform the ritual without respect quickly discover that Sangramancy rewards discipline, not recklessness.

The elders remind every apprentice that Bloodlust is never defeated—it is only held at bay. Blood Tithe is one of the few techniques capable of quieting its whispers, but even the greatest Bloodhunters know those whispers will always return when the next hunt begins.

> *"Control is not the absence of darkness. It is choosing, every single day, not to let the darkness choose for you."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 4
### Description

Few Bloodhunters ever master the Fourth Rank of Blood Tithe. At this stage, the ritual is no longer simply an exchange of blood for strength—it becomes an act of absolute discipline. The Bloodhunter has learned to willingly surrender a significant portion of their own life force, trusting that Sangramancy will return only what is necessary while calming the turmoil threatening to erupt into Bloodlust.

The ritual is performed with unwavering precision. A deep cut is made, blood is offered, and ancient Sangramantic runes briefly ignite around the wound before fading into crimson mist. As the offering is accepted, the Bloodhunter feels exhaustion melt away and the relentless pressure of Blood Points begin to subside. Their breathing steadies, their heartbeat slows, and clarity returns to a mind that moments before stood dangerously close to losing control.

Veterans often reserve Blood Tithe for the most desperate moments of a hunt. They understand that sacrificing more blood now may be the only reason they survive long enough to finish what they started. Every use is a reminder that true mastery is measured not by how much power one possesses, but by knowing when to relinquish it.

### Lore

Within the Bloodhunter Orders, Rank IV is known as **The Crimson Offering**. It represents the moment a Bloodhunter proves they can command Sangramancy without allowing Sangramancy to command them. Every offering made through this ritual demonstrates a level of discipline few hunters ever achieve.

Many masters teach that Blood Tithe is not merely a recovery technique—it is a reminder of why the Bloodhunter fights. Every sacrifice reinforces the oath taken upon entering the Orders: to willingly endure suffering so that others never have to face the horrors lurking beyond civilisation alone.

The elders warn that even the greatest Bloodhunter must never become complacent. Bloodlust is patient. It waits beyond every battle, every victory, and every sacrifice. Blood Tithe does not destroy that darkness—it merely reminds it who remains in control.

> *"Every drop I surrender is another promise that the darkness will serve me... never the other way around."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 5
### Description

At the Fifth Rank, Blood Tithe becomes the ultimate expression of a Bloodhunter's discipline over Sangramancy. Few warriors possess the resolve to willingly surrender so much of their own life force, yet the greatest Bloodhunters understand that true mastery has never been about gaining power—it has always been about knowing when to relinquish it.

The ritual has become instinctive. A single deliberate wound is enough to begin the ancient exchange. Blood leaves the body, crimson runes flare to life around the offering, and the restless emotions feeding Bloodlust are drawn away with it. The Bloodhunter feels their breathing steady, their heartbeat return to a measured rhythm, and the chaos within their soul settle into calm determination.

This technique is often performed during the most desperate moments of battle. A Bloodhunter standing on the brink of Bloodlust may willingly sacrifice their own life to regain the strength needed to continue the hunt while restoring control over the darkness within. Every use is a reminder that the greatest enemy a Bloodhunter will ever face is not standing before them—it lives within their own blood.

Among the Orders, only the most disciplined masters are entrusted with this ritual. They teach that Blood Tithe is not a means of escaping Bloodlust forever, but a sacred reminder that every hunter must choose, time and time again, whether they will command their blood or become enslaved by it.

- As a **Bonus Action**, activate Blood Tithe.
- Sacrifice **30 HP**.
- Choose **one** of the following:
  - Restore **80 Mana Points (MP)**.
  - Restore **80 Stamina Points (SP)**.
- Reduce your current **Blood Points (BP)** by **25**.
- Blood Tithe cannot restore HP.
- Blood Tithe cannot restore MP or SP beyond their normal maximum values.
- Blood Tithe may only be used while you have at least **25 Blood Points**.

### Lore

The Fifth Rank of Blood Tithe is known throughout the Bloodhunter Orders as **The Final Offering**. It is regarded as the highest demonstration of restraint a Bloodhunter can achieve, proving that even after embracing unimaginable power through Sangramancy, they still possess the wisdom to temper it through sacrifice.

Legends tell of Bloodhunters who stood alone against impossible odds, repeatedly offering their own lifeblood to remain standing while Bloodlust clawed at the edges of their soul. Their victories were never remembered because they possessed greater strength than other warriors, but because they possessed greater control over themselves.

The elders leave every Bloodhunter with one final lesson before teaching this ritual. Sangramancy will always answer the call of blood, but it cares nothing for the one who makes the offering. Only discipline separates a master from a monster, and Blood Tithe exists to ensure that, no matter how powerful a Bloodhunter becomes, they never forget that sacrifice has always been the true price of their strength.

> *"The strongest Bloodhunter is not the one who spills the most blood... but the one who knows exactly how much of their own they are willing to give."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor
