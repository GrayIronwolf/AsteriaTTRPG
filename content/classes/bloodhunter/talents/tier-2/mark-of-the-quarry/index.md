# Mark of the Quarry

## Overview
Every Bloodhunter understands that a hunt truly begins the moment prey is chosen. Through Sangramancy, the Bloodhunter offers a small portion of their own blood, weaving it into the life force of a single creature. This crimson bond is known as the **Mark of the Quarry**—a supernatural connection that allows the hunter to relentlessly pursue their chosen prey.

Unlike ordinary tracking magic, the mark is not placed upon the creature's body alone. It binds to the creature's blood itself. As long as the creature lives, the Bloodhunter can instinctively feel the connection, making every strike more precise and every movement more deliberate. To the Bloodhunter, there is no longer a battlefield filled with enemies—only the quarry.

The mark also serves as the foundation of every advanced Sangramancy technique. Blood Scent becomes easier to follow, Veinlock grips tighter, and the Bloodhunter's predatory instincts sharpen whenever confronting their chosen target. Every lesson taught within the Bloodhunter Orders begins with the same principle: one hunter, one quarry.

The ritual is not without cost. Every mark requires a willing blood sacrifice and stirs the emotions carried within the Bloodhunter's soul. The hunt becomes deeply personal, and the satisfaction of finally bringing down the marked creature only fuels the growing danger of Bloodlust.

## Rank 1

### Effects

- As a **Bonus Action**, mark one creature within **20 metres** as your **Marked Quarry**.
- Spend **15 MP**, sacrifice **5 HP**, and gain **5 Blood Points (BP)**.
- You may only have **one Marked Quarry** at a time.
- The mark remains until:
  - The target dies.
  - You mark another creature.
  - You complete a **Long Rest**.
- Gain **+1 to Attack Rolls** against your **Marked Quarry**.
- Gain **+2 Damage** against your **Marked Quarry**.
- Your **Blood Scent** automatically recognises the blood of your Marked Quarry while the mark remains active.
- Any Bloodhunter talent that references your **Marked Quarry** may target this creature.
- When your Marked Quarry is reduced to **0 HP**, immediately gain **10 Blood Points (BP)**.

### Lore

The first lesson taught to every Bloodhunter apprentice is simple: never divide your attention between multiple hunts. A distracted hunter is a dead hunter. Mark of the Quarry embodies this philosophy, teaching absolute focus upon a single foe until the hunt reaches its conclusion.

Veteran Bloodhunters often describe the mark as a quiet whisper carried through the blood. It is not a voice, nor a vision, but an instinct that constantly reminds the hunter where their purpose lies. Every heartbeat strengthens the connection until hunter and quarry become inseparably bound.

Many Bloodhunters have fallen to Bloodlust after allowing the thrill of a successful hunt to consume them. For this reason, the elders teach that the mark is more than a hunting technique—it is a test of discipline. The Bloodhunter must learn to end the hunt without allowing the hunt to consume them.

> *"A hunter without a quarry is merely a warrior. A Bloodhunter chooses one life... and does not stop until its final heartbeat."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 2
### Description

As a Bloodhunter's mastery of Sangramancy grows, so too does the strength of the bond forged between hunter and quarry. The Mark of the Quarry no longer serves merely as a declaration of intent—it becomes a supernatural tether that strengthens every instinct the Bloodhunter possesses. The marked creature's blood resonates with the hunter's own, making it increasingly difficult for the quarry to escape the inevitable conclusion of the hunt.

The Bloodhunter begins to anticipate the movements of their quarry with frightening accuracy. Every subtle shift in posture, every desperate attempt to flee, and every hesitation in combat seems almost instinctive to read. To outsiders, this appears to be incredible skill, but experienced Bloodhunters know the truth—it is the blood itself revealing the path of the hunt.

The connection between hunter and quarry also strengthens the Bloodhunter's Sangramancy. Techniques such as Blood Scent, Blood Rush, Veinlock, and Hunter's Bane become increasingly effective when directed towards the marked creature, rewarding the Bloodhunter for maintaining focus on a single target.

The Bloodhunter Orders teach that this unwavering focus is both a strength and a danger. Obsession is never far from purpose, and every successful hunt feeds the emotions that slowly build towards Bloodlust.

### Effects

- As a **Bonus Action**, mark one creature within **25 metres** as your **Marked Quarry**.
- Spend **20 MP**, sacrifice **8 HP**, and gain **10 Blood Points (BP)**.
- You may only have **one Marked Quarry** at a time.
- The mark remains until:
  - The target dies.
  - You mark another creature.
  - You complete a **Long Rest**.
- Gain **+2 to Attack Rolls** against your **Marked Quarry**.
- Gain **+3 Damage** against your **Marked Quarry**.
- Your **Blood Scent** automatically recognises the blood of your Marked Quarry while the mark remains active.
- Any Bloodhunter talent that references your **Marked Quarry** may target this creature.
- You always know the **general direction** of your Marked Quarry while it remains on the same plane of existence.
- When your Marked Quarry is reduced to **0 HP**, immediately gain **15 Blood Points (BP)**.

### Lore

Within the Bloodhunter Orders, the Second Rank is known as the point where the hunter truly begins to understand the meaning of pursuit. The mark is no longer simply placed upon a creature—it becomes a bond written into blood itself. No matter how desperately the quarry runs, the Bloodhunter feels the faint pull of the connection guiding them onward.

Veteran Bloodhunters describe the sensation as hearing a distant heartbeat that never truly fades. It is subtle, almost impossible for others to comprehend, yet unmistakable to the hunter who forged the bond. Every step taken by the quarry strengthens the Bloodhunter's resolve to see the hunt through to its inevitable end.

The elders often remind apprentices that Mark of the Quarry is not permission to become obsessed. A hunter who cannot let go after the hunt is finished has already begun walking the path towards Bloodlust. The mark exists to focus the hunt—not to consume the hunter.

> *"The quarry may lose sight of me... but it can never escape the blood that binds us together."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 3
### Description

By the Third Rank, the Mark of the Quarry has become an inseparable extension of the Bloodhunter's Sangramancy. The moment the ritual is completed, a fragment of the Bloodhunter's lifeblood intertwines with that of their chosen prey, creating a bond that neither distance nor deception can easily sever. To the Bloodhunter, the hunt has already begun—the only question remaining is how long the quarry can survive.

The Bloodhunter's instincts sharpen dramatically while confronting their Marked Quarry. Every movement, every hesitation, and every drop of blood spilled reinforces the supernatural connection between hunter and prey. The Bloodhunter no longer reacts to the battle; they anticipate it, predicting the quarry's actions through the rhythm of its blood.

This deeper bond also strengthens every Sangramancy technique the Bloodhunter possesses. Blood Rush drives them relentlessly towards the quarry, Veinlock seizes their blood with greater precision, Blood Scent recognises even the faintest trace of their lifeblood, and Hunter's Bane pushes the Bloodhunter ever closer to perfection against their chosen foe.

Yet such focus carries a terrible danger. The stronger the bond becomes, the greater the exhilaration when the hunt is finally complete. Every successful kill floods the Bloodhunter with powerful emotions, feeding Bloodlust and reminding them that obsession is never far removed from purpose.

### Effects

- As a **Bonus Action**, mark one creature within **30 metres** as your **Marked Quarry**.
- Spend **30 MP**, sacrifice **12 HP**, and gain **15 Blood Points (BP)**.
- You may only have **one Marked Quarry** at a time.
- The mark remains until:
  - The target dies.
  - You mark another creature.
  - You complete a **Long Rest**.
- Gain **+3 to Attack Rolls** against your **Marked Quarry**.
- Gain **+4 Damage** against your **Marked Quarry**.
- Your **Blood Scent** automatically recognises the blood of your Marked Quarry while the mark remains active.
- You always know the **general direction and approximate distance** of your Marked Quarry while it remains on the same plane of existence.
- Any Bloodhunter talent that references your **Marked Quarry** may target this creature.
- When your Marked Quarry is reduced to **0 HP**, immediately gain **20 Blood Points (BP)**.

### Lore

Among veteran Bloodhunters, the Third Rank is known as **The Crimson Bond**. At this stage, the connection between hunter and quarry transcends ordinary tracking, becoming an instinct that few outside the Orders could ever understand. The Bloodhunter no longer searches for the quarry—they simply follow the pull of blood.

Many monsters have attempted to flee across kingdoms, hide beneath ancient ruins, or vanish into enchanted forests, only to discover that the Bloodhunter never truly lost them. As long as the bond endures, every heartbeat of the quarry echoes faintly within the hunter's own blood.

The elders teach that the Crimson Bond is one of Sangramancy's greatest gifts—and one of its greatest temptations. A Bloodhunter who allows the bond to become obsession may eventually forget why they hunt at all. The greatest masters remember that the mark exists to protect the innocent, never to satisfy the darkness within.

> *"The mark does not tell me where you are. It reminds me that the hunt is not finished."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 4
### Description

At the Fourth Rank, the Mark of the Quarry becomes an unbreakable crimson covenant between hunter and prey. Sangramancy no longer simply identifies a target—it entwines the Bloodhunter's soul with the rhythm of the quarry's lifeblood, creating a supernatural bond that grows stronger with every passing heartbeat.

The Bloodhunter no longer needs to actively search for weakness. Their instincts naturally guide every strike toward vulnerable openings, every movement is calculated to deny escape, and every wound inflicted strengthens the relentless pressure of the hunt. To the quarry, it feels as though the Bloodhunter already knows every decision before it is made.

The mark also amplifies every Sangramantic technique the Bloodhunter possesses. Blood Rush becomes an unstoppable pursuit, Veinlock grips tighter as blood answers the Bloodhunter's will, and Blood Scent can distinguish the quarry's blood from every other creature, even after countless battles. There is no confusion, no hesitation—only the certainty that the hunt has already been decided.

Masters warn that this level of focus borders dangerously on obsession. The stronger the bond becomes, the greater the exhilaration when the quarry finally falls. Only the most disciplined Bloodhunters remember that the mark exists to serve justice, not to satisfy Bloodlust.

### Effects

- As a **Bonus Action**, mark one creature within **35 metres** as your **Marked Quarry**.
- Spend **40 MP**, sacrifice **16 HP**, and gain **20 Blood Points (BP)**.
- You may only have **one Marked Quarry** at a time.
- The mark remains until:
  - The target dies.
  - You mark another creature.
  - You complete a **Long Rest**.
- Gain **+4 to Attack Rolls** against your **Marked Quarry**.
- Gain **+5 Damage** against your **Marked Quarry**.
- Your **Blood Scent** automatically recognises the blood of your Marked Quarry while the mark remains active.
- You always know the **direction and approximate distance** of your Marked Quarry while it remains on the same plane of existence.
- Your **Marked Quarry** has **Disadvantage** on ability checks made to hide, disguise, or conceal evidence of its blood from you.
- Any Bloodhunter talent that references your **Marked Quarry** may target this creature.
- When your **Marked Quarry** is reduced to **0 HP**, immediately gain **25 Blood Points (BP)**.

### Lore

Within the Bloodhunter Orders, Rank IV is known as **The Crimson Oath**. Bloodhunters who achieve this mastery are feared not because they are stronger than other warriors, but because once they have chosen their quarry, they rarely fail to complete the hunt. Escape becomes an illusion, and every passing hour only strengthens the hunter's resolve.

Legends tell of Bloodhunters pursuing their quarry across continents, through ancient ruins, and into the darkest corners of the world. Distance becomes meaningless when blood itself remembers the bond that was forged. Even after weeks of pursuit, the Bloodhunter can still sense the faint pull guiding them toward the creature they swore to destroy.

The elders teach that this rank represents the greatest test of discipline before true mastery. The Bloodhunter must never allow the hunt to define who they are. They are protectors first, hunters second. Those who forget this truth eventually discover that Bloodlust no longer cares who has been marked—it only cares that blood continues to flow.

> *"Once I write your name in blood, the world itself becomes your prison."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 5
### Description

The Fifth Rank of Mark of the Quarry is the pinnacle of the Bloodhunter's hunting discipline. Through absolute mastery of Sangramancy, the Bloodhunter no longer merely marks a creature—they forge an unbreakable covenant written in blood itself. The moment the ritual is complete, the quarry becomes the sole focus of the hunt, bound to the Bloodhunter until one of them falls.

At this level, the Bloodhunter's instincts transcend ordinary perception. Every heartbeat of the quarry seems to echo through the hunter's own veins, guiding their movements with terrifying precision. Every strike naturally seeks weakness, every pursuit is relentless, and every attempt to escape feels futile. To the quarry, it is as though death itself has chosen to follow.

The Mark strengthens every Sangramantic technique the Bloodhunter possesses. Blood Rush drives the hunter ever closer, Blood Scent can identify the quarry from the faintest trace of blood, Veinlock manipulates its lifeblood with frightening efficiency, and Hunter's Bane reaches its full destructive potential. Every talent exists to serve the hunt.

Yet this mastery carries the greatest temptation of all. The overwhelming exhilaration of pursuing and finally defeating the Marked Quarry floods the Bloodhunter with Blood Points, pushing them dangerously close to Bloodlust. Masters teach that reaching this rank is not about learning to hunt monsters—it is about proving that the monster within will never become the one choosing the quarry.

### Effects

- As a **Bonus Action**, mark one creature within **40 metres** as your **Marked Quarry**.
- Spend **50 MP**, sacrifice **20 HP**, and gain **25 Blood Points (BP)**.
- You may only have **one Marked Quarry** at a time.
- The mark remains until:
  - The target dies.
  - You mark another creature.
  - You complete a **Long Rest**.
- Gain **+5 to Attack Rolls** against your **Marked Quarry**.
- Gain **+6 Damage** against your **Marked Quarry**.
- You always know the **direction and approximate distance** of your Marked Quarry while it remains on the same plane of existence.
- Your **Blood Scent** automatically recognises your Marked Quarry regardless of how old the blood trail is.
- Your **Marked Quarry** cannot gain Advantage on checks or abilities made to hide, disguise, or evade you through non-magical means.
- All Bloodhunter talents that reference your **Marked Quarry** gain their maximum benefits against this creature.
- When your **Marked Quarry** is reduced to **0 HP**, immediately gain **30 Blood Points (BP)**.

### Lore

Among the Bloodhunter Orders, the Fifth Rank is known as **The Crimson Verdict**. Very few hunters ever achieve this level of mastery, for it demands complete understanding of both Sangramancy and the discipline required to wield it. Those who do are spoken of in hushed voices, for once they have chosen their quarry, the hunt is considered all but inevitable.

Ancient records tell of Bloodhunters pursuing dragons across mountain ranges, vampires through forgotten kingdoms, and eldritch horrors into realms untouched by mortal hands. Distance, deception, and time become meaningless when blood itself remembers the oath that has been sworn.

The elders teach one final lesson before granting this knowledge. The Mark of the Quarry is not permission to kill—it is a promise to finish what must be done. A Bloodhunter who marks out of hatred, vengeance, or obsession has already begun surrendering to Bloodlust. The greatest hunters are remembered not because they never failed, but because they always remained masters of their own blood.

> *"The mark is not your sentence. It is my promise that this hunt will have an end."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor
