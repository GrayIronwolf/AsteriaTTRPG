# Blood Reprisal

## Overview
Every Bloodhunter knows that pain is inevitable. The difference between an ordinary warrior and a Bloodhunter is what they choose to do with that pain. Through Sangramancy, the Bloodhunter learns to transform the shock of an enemy's strike into an immediate and devastating retaliation.

The instant the Bloodhunter is wounded, the blood spilled from the injury surges with crimson energy instead of falling harmlessly to the ground. That surge of Sangramancy fuels a burst of instinctive aggression, allowing the hunter to answer the attack before the enemy can recover their footing.

Blood Reprisal is not born from anger alone. It is the culmination of discipline, timing, and absolute trust in one's own blood. Rather than recoiling from injury, the Bloodhunter steps into it, accepting the wound as the price of creating an opening. To outsiders this appears reckless, but to experienced Bloodhunters every wound is simply another opportunity to end the hunt.

The Bloodhunter Orders teach that this technique must never be used carelessly. Every reprisal feeds Bloodlust, and the exhilaration of answering pain with violence is one of the quickest paths toward losing control.

## Rank 1
### Description

### Effects

- **Reaction:** Activate Blood Reprisal immediately after taking damage from a creature within your melee reach.
- Spend **20 MP** and gain **10 Blood Points (BP)**.
- Immediately make **one melee weapon attack** against the creature that damaged you.
- This attack gains **+2 to the Attack Roll**.
- If the attack hits, it deals an additional **+2 damage**.
- Blood Reprisal may only be used **once every 3 Combat Rounds**.
- This attack benefits from all bonuses against your **Marked Quarry**, if applicable.

### Lore

Among the Bloodhunter Orders, Blood Reprisal is often described as the moment a hunter stops fearing pain. Apprentices spend months learning not to flinch after receiving a blow, instead conditioning themselves to recognise that every committed attack leaves the enemy exposed for the briefest of moments.

Veteran Bloodhunters become infamous for this technique. Many monsters quickly realise that striking a Bloodhunter is often the most dangerous decision they can make, for every successful wound is answered by an immediate counterstroke empowered by Sangramancy itself.

The elders warn that Blood Reprisal is intoxicating. There is a dangerous satisfaction in turning suffering into violence, and many Bloodhunters who fall to Bloodlust first became addicted to the thrill of retaliation rather than the discipline required to master it.

> *"Every wound you give me is another chance for me to end this hunt."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 2
### Description

As the Bloodhunter's understanding of Sangramancy deepens, Blood Reprisal becomes more than an instinctive counterattack—it becomes a calculated punishment for those foolish enough to draw the hunter's blood. Every wound suffered is immediately analysed, every opening recognised, and every heartbeat prepares the Bloodhunter to answer with lethal precision.

The Bloodhunter no longer recoils from pain. Instead, injury fuels their focus, allowing them to retaliate with greater speed and accuracy than before. The blood spilled from their wounds surges with Sangramantic energy, briefly strengthening both body and mind before exploding into a devastating counterstrike.

Experienced Bloodhunters deliberately remain close to dangerous foes, knowing that every committed attack creates a fleeting weakness. Their enemies soon realise a terrifying truth: striking the Bloodhunter often places them in greater danger than the hunter themselves.

Yet every reprisal strengthens the emotional current flowing through the Bloodhunter's blood. Satisfaction, anger, determination, and exhilaration all feed Bloodlust, reminding the hunter that even justified vengeance comes with a price.

### Effects

- **Reaction:** Activate Blood Reprisal immediately after taking damage from a creature within your melee reach.
- Spend **25 MP** and gain **15 Blood Points (BP)**.
- Immediately make **one melee weapon attack** against the creature that damaged you.
- Gain **+3 to the Attack Roll** for this attack.
- If the attack hits, it deals an additional **+4 damage**.
- If the target is your **Marked Quarry**, gain an additional **+1 Attack Roll**.
- Blood Reprisal may only be used **once every 3 Combat Rounds**.
- This attack benefits from all bonuses granted by **Blood Rite**, **Hunter's Bane**, and other Bloodhunter talents.

### Lore

Within the Bloodhunter Orders, Rank II marks the point where Blood Reprisal becomes a feared combat technique rather than a desperate reaction. Veteran hunters no longer view taking a wound as failure—they recognise it as an opportunity. Every enemy that overextends themselves has unknowingly offered the Bloodhunter the perfect opening.

Many monsters learn this lesson only once. Those that survive often become hesitant to strike first, wary that every successful blow may be answered by an even deadlier retaliation. This hesitation alone has allowed countless Bloodhunters to seize control of battles that once seemed unwinnable.

The elders teach that Blood Reprisal must never become an excuse to seek injury. A Bloodhunter survives not because they enjoy pain, but because they have mastered the discipline to transform suffering into victory. The moment pain becomes something to chase, Bloodlust has already begun tightening its grip.

> *"Every scar teaches the same lesson... strike me once, and you'll remember why you should have struck harder."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 3
### Description

By the Third Rank, Blood Reprisal has become second nature to the Bloodhunter. The moment blood is spilled, Sangramancy instinctively courses through the wound, transforming pain into purpose before the Bloodhunter has time to think. Every strike suffered becomes an opportunity to retaliate with ruthless efficiency.

The Bloodhunter no longer views incoming attacks as moments to endure, but as openings to exploit. As an enemy's weapon completes its swing, their balance shifts, their guard weakens, and for a single heartbeat they become vulnerable. Sangramancy amplifies the Bloodhunter's instincts, allowing them to seize that fleeting opportunity with terrifying speed.

Those who witness a master of Blood Reprisal often describe it as though the Bloodhunter welcomes injury. This is not recklessness, but supreme confidence. They know exactly how much pain they can endure, and they understand that every wound accepted on their own terms can become the beginning of their enemy's downfall.

Yet this technique carries a dangerous temptation. The exhilaration of answering pain with violence becomes increasingly addictive, and every successful reprisal drives Blood Points higher, bringing the Bloodhunter ever closer to Bloodlust.

### Effects

- **Reaction:** Activate Blood Reprisal immediately after taking damage from a creature within your melee reach.
- Spend **35 MP** and gain **20 Blood Points (BP)**.
- Immediately make **one melee weapon attack** against the creature that damaged you.
- Gain **+4 to the Attack Roll** for this attack.
- If the attack hits, it deals an additional **+6 damage**.
- If the target is your **Marked Quarry**, gain an additional **+2 to the Attack Roll**.
- If Blood Reprisal reduces your **Marked Quarry** to **0 HP**, immediately reduce your current **Blood Points** by **10 BP**, as the hunt reaches its satisfying conclusion.
- Blood Reprisal may only be used **once every 2 Combat Rounds**.
- This attack benefits from all bonuses granted by **Blood Rite**, **Hunter's Bane**, **Mark of the Quarry**, and other Bloodhunter talents.

### Lore

Within the Bloodhunter Orders, Rank III is known as **The Crimson Retaliation**. It is the point where a Bloodhunter no longer fears the enemy's first strike, for they know it will almost certainly be answered before their opponent can recover. Many monsters have won the opening exchange of a battle, only to lose the fight moments later to the Bloodhunter's relentless counterattack.

Masters teach that Blood Reprisal is not a technique born from anger, but from discipline. Every movement has been practised countless times, every counterstrike refined until it becomes instinct. The Bloodhunter does not retaliate because they are enraged—they retaliate because they have already accepted the pain as part of the hunt.

The elders warn that Blood Reprisal represents one of Sangramancy's greatest temptations. There is a dangerous satisfaction in turning suffering into victory, and only those with absolute self-control can wield this technique without allowing Bloodlust to transform justice into vengeance.

> *"Pain is merely the signal. Retribution is the answer."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 4
### Description

The Fourth Rank of Blood Reprisal is feared even among veteran monster hunters. At this level, Sangramancy no longer merely responds to pain—it anticipates it. The instant an enemy's attack lands, the Bloodhunter's blood surges with crimson power, their body instinctively moving to exploit the opening left behind by the attacker.

Every wound becomes a calculated exchange. The Bloodhunter willingly accepts the enemy's strike, trusting in years of discipline to transform that moment of vulnerability into overwhelming retaliation. To the untrained eye, it appears reckless. To a master Bloodhunter, it is absolute control over both body and blood.

As Sangramancy flows through the Bloodhunter's veins, the exhilaration of immediate vengeance grows stronger than ever. Every successful reprisal feeds the emotions carried within the blood, rapidly increasing Blood Points while reinforcing the dangerous satisfaction of answering pain with greater violence. Only those with unwavering discipline can prevent this intoxicating cycle from becoming Bloodlust.

Within the Bloodhunter Orders, Rank IV is considered the final trial before true mastery. Those who survive long enough to perfect Blood Reprisal become living reminders that the greatest mistake a monster can make is believing a wounded Bloodhunter has become weaker.

### Effects

- **Reaction:** Activate Blood Reprisal immediately after taking damage from a creature within your melee reach.
- Spend **45 MP** and gain **25 Blood Points (BP)**.
- Immediately make **one melee weapon attack** against the creature that damaged you.
- Gain **+5 to the Attack Roll** for this attack.
- If the attack hits, it deals an additional **+8 damage**.
- If the target is your **Marked Quarry**, gain an additional **+2 to the Attack Roll** and **+2 Damage**.
- If Blood Reprisal reduces your **Marked Quarry** to **0 HP**, immediately reduce your current **Blood Points** by **15 BP**.
- Blood Reprisal may only be used **once every 2 Combat Rounds**.
- This attack benefits from all bonuses granted by **Blood Rite**, **Hunter's Bane**, **Mark of the Quarry**, and other Bloodhunter talents.

### Lore

Among the Bloodhunter Orders, Rank IV is known as **The Crimson Counterstroke**. Hunters who reach this level have mastered the art of turning suffering into opportunity. Their enemies soon learn that every successful blow only increases the danger they face, as each wound fuels a retaliation of terrifying precision.

Stories tell of Bloodhunters who stood against creatures many times their size, allowing a single strike to land before delivering the decisive counterattack that ended the battle. Such victories are remembered not because the Bloodhunter endured the pain, but because they possessed the discipline to transform it into purpose.

The elders teach that Blood Reprisal is never an excuse to seek injury. A master does not invite pain—they simply refuse to waste it. The Bloodhunter who forgets this distinction will eventually stop hunting monsters and begin chasing the thrill of retaliation itself, the first true step toward Bloodlust.

> *"Every wound is an invitation. Every reprisal is my reply."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

## Rank 5
### Description

The Fifth Rank of Blood Reprisal is the pinnacle of retaliatory Sangramancy. At this stage, the Bloodhunter has forged an unbreakable connection between pain and purpose. Every wound suffered becomes a catalyst for immediate vengeance, allowing the Bloodhunter to answer violence with greater violence before their enemy has even recovered from the strike.

The Bloodhunter no longer reacts consciously. The moment blood is drawn, Sangramancy surges through every vein, driving muscles into motion with supernatural precision. Every counterattack is swift, deliberate, and devastating, transforming what should have been a moment of weakness into the greatest danger the enemy will face.

To seasoned monsters, the sight of a Bloodhunter refusing to stagger after suffering a grievous wound is deeply unsettling. They know what comes next. The crimson glow spreading through the Bloodhunter's veins is not a sign of desperation—it is the warning that Blood Reprisal has already begun.

Only the greatest Bloodhunters are entrusted with this technique. The exhilaration of turning agony into triumph is intoxicating, flooding the body with Blood Points at an alarming rate. Masters constantly remind their apprentices that the purpose of Blood Reprisal is to end the hunt swiftly—not to become addicted to the satisfaction of revenge.

### Effects

- **Reaction:** Activate Blood Reprisal immediately after taking damage from a creature within your melee reach.
- Spend **60 MP** and gain **30 Blood Points (BP)**.
- Immediately make **one melee weapon attack** against the creature that damaged you.
- Gain **+6 to the Attack Roll** for this attack.
- If the attack hits, it deals an additional **+10 damage**.
- If the target is your **Marked Quarry**, gain an additional **+2 to the Attack Roll** and **+4 Damage**.
- If Blood Reprisal reduces your **Marked Quarry** to **0 HP**, immediately reduce your current **Blood Points** by **20 BP**.
- Blood Reprisal may be used **once every Combat Round**, but only **once per triggering attack**.
- This attack benefits from all bonuses granted by **Blood Rite**, **Hunter's Bane**, **Mark of the Quarry**, and all other applicable Bloodhunter talents.

### Lore

Within the Bloodhunter Orders, the Fifth Rank is known as **The Crimson Reckoning**. It is spoken of with equal measures of admiration and caution, for only those who possess absolute discipline can wield such overwhelming retaliation without allowing Bloodlust to consume them. Every strike against these masters becomes a gamble few enemies survive.

Legends recount Bloodhunters standing alone against dragons, elder vampires, and eldritch horrors, each devastating blow answered by an even more terrifying reprisal. Their enemies soon learned that drawing a Bloodhunter's blood did not bring victory—it merely awakened the deadliest part of the hunt.

The elders teach one final truth before revealing this technique. A Bloodhunter is not defined by how much pain they endure, nor by how fiercely they retaliate. They are defined by their ability to choose when vengeance serves justice and when restraint serves it better. Only those who never forget this distinction can truly master Blood Reprisal.

> *"You mistake my blood for a weakness. It is simply the price you pay to earn my full attention."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor
