# Crimson Ritual

## Overview
The **Crimson Ritual** is one of the oldest ceremonies preserved by the Bloodhunter Orders, performed before every hunt worthy of remembrance. Rather than strengthening the Bloodhunter directly, the ritual empowers the tools that will carry out the hunt. Through ancient Sangramantic runes carved into the earth with their own blood, the Bloodhunter creates a **Blood Circle**—a sacred ritual site where weapons are infused with the essence of sacrifice.

As blood fills the engraved sigils, the circle awakens with a slow, rhythmic pulse, echoing the heartbeat of its creator. Weapons laid within the circle gradually absorb this crimson energy, binding a fragment of the Bloodhunter's life force into their steel, wood, or stone. Every empowered weapon becomes an extension of the Bloodhunter's resolve, carrying the promise that blood has already been offered before the battle has begun.

Bloodhunter Orders teach that monsters are defeated long before the first strike is made. Preparation, discipline, and sacrifice are the true foundations of every successful hunt. A Bloodhunter who neglects the Crimson Ritual is seen as reckless, while one who performs it honours both the traditions of their Order and every hunter who shed their blood before them.

Though simple compared to the legendary rituals performed by Bloodhunter Masters, every Crimson Ritual is a solemn oath: *the Bloodhunter will always be the first to bleed.*

### Description

The **Crimson Ritual** is one of the oldest ceremonies preserved by the Bloodhunter Orders, performed before every hunt worthy of remembrance. Rather than strengthening the Bloodhunter directly, the ritual empowers the tools that will carry out the hunt. Through ancient Sangramantic runes carved into the earth with their own blood, the Bloodhunter creates a **Blood Circle**—a sacred ritual site where weapons are infused with the essence of sacrifice.

As blood fills the engraved sigils, the circle awakens with a slow, rhythmic pulse, echoing the heartbeat of its creator. Weapons laid within the circle gradually absorb this crimson energy, binding a fragment of the Bloodhunter's life force into their steel, wood, or stone. Every empowered weapon becomes an extension of the Bloodhunter's resolve, carrying the promise that blood has already been offered before the battle has begun.

Bloodhunter Orders teach that monsters are defeated long before the first strike is made. Preparation, discipline, and sacrifice are the true foundations of every successful hunt. A Bloodhunter who neglects the Crimson Ritual is seen as reckless, while one who performs it honours both the traditions of their Order and every hunter who shed their blood before them.

Though simple compared to the legendary rituals performed by Bloodhunter Masters, every Crimson Ritual is a solemn oath: *the Bloodhunter will always be the first to bleed.*

### Effects

- Performing the ritual requires **5 minutes** outside of combat.
- Spend **50 MP**, sacrifice **20 HP**, and gain **15 Blood Points (BP)**.
- Create a **Blood Circle** with a **2 metre radius** centred on yourself.
- The Blood Circle remains active for **5 minutes**.
- Up to **3 weapons or items** may be placed inside the Blood Circle at one time.
- A weapon or item must remain within the Blood Circle for **1 minute** to become **Bloodbound**.
- Bloodbound weapons retain their enchantment for **1 hour**, provided they are not used in combat.
- Once a Bloodbound weapon is used in combat, the enchantment lasts for the **first 4 successful attacks** made with that weapon before fading.
- Bloodbound weapons gain the following benefits:
  - Count as **Magical Weapons**.
  - Deal an additional **+3 Blood Damage** on each successful attack.
- If a Bloodbound ranged weapon is used, any ammunition fired from it while the enchantment remains active also gains the Bloodbound effect.
- A Bloodbound weapon or item may only benefit from one **Crimson Ritual** at a time.
- Only **one Blood Circle** may exist at any time.
- This talent synergises with **Blood Rite**, **Blood Control**, and other Bloodhunter talents.

### Lore

Every Bloodhunter Order maintains at least one ancient Blood Circle carved into the stone beneath its sanctuary floor. These circles have been stained by generations of hunters, each adding their own blood to an unbroken tradition stretching back centuries. Apprentices are taught their first Crimson Ritual within these sacred halls, learning that sacrifice is not an act of desperation, but one of purpose.

Many Bloodhunters perform the ritual in complete silence, laying each weapon carefully within the circle before whispering the names of those they have lost. By the time the ceremony is complete, the weapons are no longer ordinary tools—they have become symbols of the Bloodhunter's oath to stand between innocent lives and the horrors that threaten them.

The Blood Circle is never a place of celebration. It is a place where resolve is forged, where fear is accepted, and where the first sacrifice of every hunt is willingly made by the hunter themselves.

> *"Every hunt begins with blood. I simply choose that the first blood spilled will be my own."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor

### Description

As a Bloodhunter's mastery of Sangramancy grows, so too does the potency of the Crimson Ritual. The Blood Circle expands, its runes becoming more intricate as greater quantities of blood and mana are woven into the ancient sigils. What was once a simple blessing of weapons evolves into a refined ceremony capable of preparing an entire hunting party for the dangers ahead.

The Blood Circle no longer merely empowers steel—it binds each weapon to the Bloodhunter's will. Every blade, bow, shield, or tool placed within the circle absorbs a fragment of the hunter's life essence, becoming Bloodbound through sacrifice rather than enchantment. The ritual leaves faint crimson veins beneath the surface of each item, visible only to those sensitive to magical energy.

Among the Orders, Rank II is often called **The Hunter's Preparation**, as experienced Bloodhunters rarely venture into dangerous territory without first gathering their companions around the Blood Circle. It serves as a reminder that victory is earned through discipline and preparation long before the first monster is encountered.

Although more powerful than before, the ritual still demands a price. Every weapon blessed carries a fragment of the Bloodhunter's own vitality, ensuring that every successful strike is paid for with blood willingly given.

### Lore

Within every Bloodhunter Order, reaching the Second Rank of Crimson Ritual marks the first time an apprentice is trusted to prepare the weapons of others. This is considered a great honour, for the Bloodhunter is no longer sacrificing blood solely for themselves, but for every companion who will stand beside them.

Many veteran hunters claim they can identify a skilled Bloodhunter simply by examining the weapons they prepare. A perfectly performed Crimson Ritual leaves behind barely visible crimson markings beneath the surface of the weapon, evidence that the Sangramantic energy has been bound cleanly into the material without wasting a single drop of blood.

Some Bloodhunters perform this ritual before every expedition, regardless of the danger ahead. Others reserve it only for hunts worthy of legend. Regardless of tradition, every Order agrees on one truth—the Blood Circle is not merely a ritual of power, but a promise that the Bloodhunter's blood will always be the first offered in defence of their allies.

> *"Steel cuts flesh. Blood gives steel purpose."*  
> — Grand Bloodhunter Serik Vaelor
