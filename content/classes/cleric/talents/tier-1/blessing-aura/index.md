# Blessing Aura

## Overview
Through unwavering faith, you become a living conduit for your patron's divine presence. Rather than calling upon a single miracle, you invite a fragment of your deity's essence into the mortal world, surrounding yourself with a sacred aura that reflects their domains and ideals.

No two Blessing Auras are ever the same. A Cleric devoted to a god of life may radiate gentle warmth and flourishing vitality, while a servant of death may be cloaked in silent shadows and drifting souls. Patrons of fate may weave shimmering threads through the air, whereas patrons of war may surround their followers with the echoes of distant battle.

At this rank, your connection is still developing, allowing only a small manifestation of your patron's power. Even so, those who stand within the aura quickly realise they are no longer standing beside a mere mortal, but within the presence of something far greater.

## Rank 1
### Description

Through unwavering faith, you become a living conduit for your patron's divine presence. Rather than calling upon a single miracle, you invite a fragment of your deity's essence into the mortal world, surrounding yourself with a sacred aura that reflects their domains and ideals.

No two Blessing Auras are ever the same. A Cleric devoted to a god of life may radiate gentle warmth and flourishing vitality, while a servant of death may be cloaked in silent shadows and drifting souls. Patrons of fate may weave shimmering threads through the air, whereas patrons of war may surround their followers with the echoes of distant battle.

At this rank, your connection is still developing, allowing only a small manifestation of your patron's power. Even so, those who stand within the aura quickly realise they are no longer standing beside a mere mortal, but within the presence of something far greater.

### Effects

- As an Action, expend **20 MP** to manifest your **Blessing Aura**.
- Your Blessing Aura is centred on you and moves with you.
- The aura has a **5 metre radius**.
- The aura lasts for **2 rounds**.
- Allies and enemies are immediately affected upon entering the aura and cease being affected when they leave it.
- The effects of the Blessing Aura are **entirely determined by your chosen patron**.
- Blessing Aura has no innate effects of its own and instead acts as the vessel through which your patron's divine influence is expressed.
- If multiple Blessing Auras overlap, a creature can only be affected by one Blessing Aura at a time. If a conflict occurs, the GM determines which aura takes precedence.
- Blessing Aura is considered **Divine Magic**.

### Lore

The oldest temples teach that a Cleric does not command divine power—they are entrusted with it. Blessing Aura is often the first miracle granted to newly ordained priests, marking the moment their patron acknowledges them as a worthy vessel.

Many theologians believe every Blessing Aura is unique, carrying subtle signs of the deity behind it. Some are unmistakable, while others are understood only by devoted followers. To those who witness them, these manifestations are reminders that the gods are never truly absent from the world.

As a Cleric's faith deepens, so too does the strength of their Blessing Aura. What begins as a faint divine presence may one day become a manifestation so overwhelming that entire battlefields are transformed by the will of a single god.

> *"Do not mistake the aura for the miracle. The aura is merely the doorway through which the divine chooses to enter our world."*
>
> — High Hierophant Elaris, Keeper of the Sacred Testament

## Rank 2
### Description

Your bond with your patron grows stronger, allowing their divine presence to linger longer and reach farther across the battlefield. What was once a faint manifestation now becomes an unmistakable symbol of your deity's influence, inspiring both reverence and caution from those who witness it.

As your faith deepens, your patron entrusts you with a greater measure of their power. The aura expands naturally around you, extending its influence over more allies and enemies alike. Every step you take carries your patron's presence, making you the centre of a moving sanctuary—or a warning to those who oppose your deity.

Although every Blessing Aura remains unique to the patron it represents, all share one truth: the faithful do not stand alone. Wherever a Cleric walks, the will of their deity walks with them.

- The aura has a **7.5 metre radius**.
- The aura lasts for **3 rounds**.
- Allies and enemies are immediately affected upon entering the aura and cease being affected when they leave it.
- The effects of the Blessing Aura are **entirely determined by your chosen patron**.
- Blessing Aura has no innate effects of its own and instead serves as the vessel through which your patron expresses their divine influence.
- If multiple Blessing Auras overlap, a creature can only be affected by one Blessing Aura at a time. If a conflict occurs, the GM determines which aura takes precedence.
- Blessing Aura is considered **Divine Magic**.

### Lore

Senior priests often describe this stage of mastery as the moment a Cleric ceases to merely invoke their patron and instead begins to embody their presence. Those familiar with the signs of the gods can often identify a Cleric's patron long before a word is spoken, simply by observing the aura that surrounds them.

Across Asteria, temples preserve countless accounts of battlefields transformed by the arrival of a devoted Cleric. Soldiers speak of impossible courage, unnatural silence, blooming flowers, spectral ravens or radiant light appearing moments before the tide of battle changed. Whether these tales are miracles or legends is a matter of faith, but few deny the profound influence a true Blessing Aura can have.

> *"The faithful need not announce whom they serve. Their patron speaks through every step they take."*
>
> — Archbishop Maelor, Voice of the Divine Conclave

## Rank 3
### Description

Your unwavering devotion has strengthened the bond between yourself and your patron, allowing a greater portion of their divine presence to manifest within the mortal realm. Your Blessing Aura no longer feels like a fleeting miracle, but a sacred domain where your deity's influence is undeniable.

Those who enter the aura instinctively sense the presence of something beyond mortal understanding. To the faithful, it is a place of comfort, purpose or reassurance. To those who stand opposed to your patron's ideals, it may become a place of unease, judgement or overwhelming reverence. Every manifestation remains unique, shaped entirely by the deity who grants your power.

As your mastery grows, your Blessing Aura becomes a symbol recognised by clergy and scholars alike. It is no longer merely evidence of your faith—it is proof that your patron trusts you to carry a greater fragment of their divine will into the world.

- The aura lasts for **4 rounds**.
- Allies and enemies are immediately affected upon entering the aura and cease being affected when they leave it.
- The effects of the Blessing Aura are **entirely determined by your chosen patron**.
- Blessing Aura has no innate effects of its own and instead serves as the vessel through which your patron expresses their divine influence.
- If multiple Blessing Auras overlap, a creature can only be affected by one Blessing Aura at a time. If a conflict occurs, the GM determines which aura takes precedence.
- Blessing Aura is considered **Divine Magic**.

### Lore

Within the great temples of Asteria, reaching this level of mastery is considered a significant milestone in a Cleric's journey. It marks the point where the faithful cease to simply invoke miracles and instead become recognised as enduring vessels of divine influence.

Some believe the gods themselves can perceive the world through these sacred manifestations, watching over their followers from beyond the veil. Whether this belief is truth or tradition, few can deny that those who stand within a powerful Blessing Aura often feel the unmistakable presence of something far greater than themselves.

The appearance of such an aura has become a symbol of hope, judgement or destiny throughout history, its meaning forever shaped by the patron whose power it carries.

> *"Faith is not measured by the prayers you offer, but by the presence your patron entrusts you to bear."*
>
> — Saint Aveline, First Oracle of the Divine Concord

## Rank 4
### Description

The divine presence surrounding you has become a beacon of your patron's authority. No longer a simple manifestation of faith, your Blessing Aura now establishes a sacred domain wherever you stand. Within its boundaries, the influence of your deity is undeniable, and those who enter it instinctively recognise they have crossed into ground touched by the divine.

Your patron's power flows effortlessly through you, requiring little conscious effort to maintain. Every movement extends their reach, every prayer strengthens their presence, and every ally standing beside you shares in the blessings your deity chooses to bestow. Likewise, those who oppose your patron cannot escape the consequences of entering their sacred domain.

Veteran Clerics are often identified long before they reveal themselves. Their Blessing Aura becomes synonymous with their reputation, leaving behind stories of miraculous salvation, unwavering courage, inevitable judgement or peaceful serenity—each one reflecting the ideals of the patron they faithfully serve.

### Effects

- As an Action, expend **35 MP** to manifest your **Blessing Aura**.
- Your Blessing Aura is centred on you and moves with you.
- The aura has a **12.5 metre radius**.
- The aura lasts for **5 rounds**.
- Allies and enemies are immediately affected upon entering the aura and cease being affected when they leave it.
- The effects of the Blessing Aura are **entirely determined by your chosen patron**.
- Blessing Aura has no innate effects of its own and instead serves as the vessel through which your patron expresses their divine influence.
- Your Blessing Aura is considered a **Sacred Domain** for the purpose of patron abilities and future Cleric talents.
- If multiple Blessing Auras overlap, a creature can only be affected by one Blessing Aura at a time. If a conflict occurs, the GM determines which aura takes precedence.
- Blessing Aura is considered **Divine Magic**.

### Lore

Only the most devoted servants are entrusted with carrying such a powerful manifestation of their patron's presence. Across Asteria, these Clerics are regarded not merely as priests but as living sanctuaries, capable of bringing a fragment of the divine into even the darkest corners of the world.

Pilgrims often travel great distances simply to witness a master Cleric invoke their Blessing Aura. Some seek healing, others guidance or judgement, while many come only to experience what it feels like to stand within the unmistakable presence of a god.

The oldest scriptures teach that the strength of a Blessing Aura is not measured by its brilliance or size, but by the depth of the bond between patron and servant. Those chosen to wield such power are forever changed, carrying a part of their deity wherever they walk.

> *"When the faithful gather around their Cleric, they do not stand beside a priest—they stand within the shadow of a god."*
>
> — Grand Hierophant Solenar, Keeper of the Eternal Sanctum

## Rank 5
### Description

Your faith has reached its pinnacle, allowing you to become a true vessel of your patron's divine presence. Your Blessing Aura no longer represents a fleeting miracle—it is a manifestation of your deity's will upon the mortal plane. Wherever you walk, the influence of your patron follows, transforming the battlefield into sacred ground shaped by their domains, ideals and purpose.

The aura itself is unique to every Cleric, reflecting the patron they serve. It may appear as radiant light, blooming life, drifting souls, roaring storms, flowing waters, shimmering threads of fate or countless other divine manifestations. Those who stand within its boundaries cannot mistake its origin, for the presence of a god is unmistakable.

To friend and foe alike, a master Cleric is more than a priest—they are the living embodiment of divine authority. Entire armies have rallied around such individuals, while others have abandoned the field rather than face the overwhelming presence of a deity made manifest through mortal hands.

### Effects

- As an Action, expend **40 MP** to manifest your **Blessing Aura**.
- Your Blessing Aura is centred on you and moves with you.
- The aura has a **15 metre radius**.
- The aura lasts for **10 rounds**.
- Allies and enemies are immediately affected upon entering the aura and cease being affected when they leave it.
- The effects of the Blessing Aura are **entirely determined by your chosen patron**.
- Blessing Aura has no innate effects of its own and instead serves as the vessel through which your patron expresses their divine influence.
- Your Blessing Aura is considered a **Sacred Domain** for the purpose of all Cleric talents, Patron abilities and divine effects.
- While your Blessing Aura is active, you are considered to be **Channeling your Patron**.
- If multiple Blessing Auras overlap, a creature can only be affected by one Blessing Aura at a time. If a conflict occurs, the GM determines which aura takes precedence.
- Blessing Aura is considered **Divine Magic**.

### Lore

Among the oldest religions of Asteria, only a handful of Clerics throughout history have achieved this level of communion with their patron. Such individuals are remembered not for the miracles they performed, but for the unmistakable presence they carried wherever they travelled. Entire cities would fall silent as they passed, sensing that the veil between the mortal and divine had grown impossibly thin.

Scholars often debate whether these legendary Clerics merely carried their patron's blessing or whether, for brief moments, the gods themselves looked upon the world through their faithful servant. Whatever the truth, every account speaks of the same feeling—a presence so overwhelming that it inspired hope, fear, peace or judgement before a single word was spoken.

To master Blessing Aura is to surrender completely to one's faith. The Cleric no longer calls upon the divine—they become the means through which the divine touches the world.

> *"The greatest miracle is not the power of the gods, but the mortal they trust to bear it."*
>
> — Saint Aurelius, First Voice of the Divine Conclave
