# Channel Divinity

## Overview
Your devotion has opened a permanent spiritual connection between yourself and your chosen patron. Divine power now flows through that bond, allowing your patron’s influence to shape your abilities, miracles and sacred presence.

Channel Divinity does not grant a universal effect of its own. Instead, it unlocks the Rank I Channel Divinity effects recorded on your patron’s page. These effects represent the unique blessings, restrictions and divine influence granted by the being you serve.

Some patrons may strengthen healing, protection or restoration, while others influence death, warfare, knowledge, nature or fate. Whatever form the connection takes, its power is determined entirely by the patron rather than by the Cleric talent itself.

## Rank 1
### Description

Your devotion has opened a permanent spiritual connection between yourself and your chosen patron. Divine power now flows through that bond, allowing your patron’s influence to shape your abilities, miracles and sacred presence.

Channel Divinity does not grant a universal effect of its own. Instead, it unlocks the Rank I Channel Divinity effects recorded on your patron’s page. These effects represent the unique blessings, restrictions and divine influence granted by the being you serve.

Some patrons may strengthen healing, protection or restoration, while others influence death, warfare, knowledge, nature or fate. Whatever form the connection takes, its power is determined entirely by the patron rather than by the Cleric talent itself.

### Effects

- Channel Divinity is a **passive talent** and requires no action or resource cost.
- Upon unlocking this talent, you gain access to the **Rank I Channel Divinity effects** listed on your chosen patron’s page.
- Channel Divinity has no universal mechanical effects of its own.
- All bonuses, penalties, triggers, restrictions and special abilities granted by Channel Divinity are determined entirely by the chosen patron.
- Patron effects granted through Channel Divinity remain active whenever their listed conditions are met.
- Channel Divinity effects may interact with Cleric talents, spells, Blessing Aura or other divine abilities when stated on the patron’s page.
- You cannot benefit from Channel Divinity effects belonging to more than one patron at the same time.
- Changing patrons removes the previous patron’s Channel Divinity effects and replaces them with those of the new patron once the change is accepted by the GM.
- Channel Divinity is considered a **Divine Patron connection** rather than a spell or activated ability.

### Lore

To the faithful, Channel Divinity marks the moment devotion becomes communion. The Cleric no longer reaches toward the divine through prayer alone; their patron has answered by forming a lasting bond through which sacred influence may pass.

This connection differs greatly between religions. Some Clerics feel their patron as warmth beneath the skin, a whisper behind every prayer or a presence that watches through their eyes. Others bear visible signs such as shifting runes, sacred markings, spectral light or changes in their voice when invoking divine authority.

The bond is not ownership, nor does it allow the Cleric to command their patron. It is an agreement of faith and purpose. The patron grants power, but also expects the Cleric to uphold the ideals through which that power was given.

> *“Prayer asks the divine to listen. Channeling begins when the divine answers.”*
>
> — The First Covenant of the Ordained

## Rank 2
### Description

Your spiritual bond with your patron deepens, allowing a greater portion of their divine essence to flow through you. The connection becomes steadier and more responsive, enabling your patron to bestow additional blessings upon those they deem worthy.

As your faith matures, your patron entrusts you with greater responsibilities and reveals more of their divine nature. Every miracle you perform, every blessing you grant and every prayer you answer becomes a reflection of their will. While all Clerics share the same path toward Channel Divinity, the power they ultimately wield remains unique to the deity they serve.

At this stage, your patron's influence extends beyond a single blessing, granting new manifestations that reinforce their domains and strengthen your identity as one of their chosen servants.

### Effects

- Channel Divinity remains a **passive talent**.
- You gain access to the **Rank II Channel Divinity effects** listed on your chosen patron's page.
- You retain all effects gained from **Channel Divinity – Rank I**.
- Channel Divinity has no universal mechanical effects of its own.
- All effects, triggers, bonuses, penalties and restrictions are determined entirely by your chosen patron.
- Patron effects may interact with your **Blessing Aura**, Cleric talents, spells and other divine abilities when specified on the patron's page.
- You cannot benefit from Channel Divinity effects belonging to more than one patron at the same time.
- If your patron changes, all previous Channel Divinity effects are lost and replaced with those granted by your new patron, subject to the GM's approval.
- Channel Divinity is considered a permanent **Divine Patron Connection**.

### Lore

Those who achieve this level of communion are recognised as trusted servants of their deity. The bond between patron and Cleric has grown beyond simple faith, becoming a lasting spiritual covenant that shapes the Cleric's every action. Many temples regard this stage as the beginning of true divine service, where miracles are no longer isolated gifts but natural expressions of the patron's presence.

Experienced Clerics often describe this connection differently. Some hear their patron's guidance as quiet whispers, while others experience vivid dreams, visions or fleeting sensations that direct their path. Regardless of how the bond manifests, all agree that the relationship becomes increasingly personal as their devotion strengthens.

The faithful believe that every act performed through Channel Divinity carries a fragment of the patron's own purpose. It is this shared purpose that separates an ordinary priest from a true Cleric, for their power is no longer borrowed—it is willingly entrusted to them.

> *"Faith opens the door. Trust invites the divine to remain."*
>
> — Archbishop Elion, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant

### Lore

To reach this level of communion is considered a rare honour among the clergy of Asteria. Senior priests often recognise these individuals not by the miracles they perform, but by the quiet confidence and unwavering faith that surrounds them. Their patron's presence has become a constant companion, guiding their actions and strengthening their resolve in times of doubt.

Ancient texts describe this stage as the beginning of true divine stewardship. The Cleric no longer simply receives blessings—they become an instrument through which their patron's purpose is fulfilled. Each act of compassion, judgement or sacrifice echoes the teachings of the deity they serve, reinforcing the sacred covenant between mortal and divine.

Many faiths believe that only those who remain steadfast in both belief and action are entrusted with such responsibility. To betray a patron's ideals is to risk weakening the very bond that grants these sacred gifts.

> *"Power is not the reward of faith. It is the responsibility entrusted to those who never abandon it."*
>
> — Saint Veridan, Keeper of the Divine Covenant

## Rank 5
### Description

Your communion with your patron has reached its highest mortal expression. The divine bond that began as an act of faith has become an unbreakable covenant, allowing your patron's influence to flow through you with unparalleled clarity. You no longer simply receive divine gifts—you have become one of your deity's most trusted representatives within the mortal world.

Every blessing you bestow, every miracle you witness and every sacred rite you perform carries the unmistakable mark of your patron. Their teachings have become inseparable from your own actions, and your unwavering devotion has earned you access to the greatest manifestations of their divine power.

Although every Cleric reaches this pinnacle through the same path of faith, no two Channel Divinities are ever alike. Each patron grants different gifts, responsibilities and expectations, ensuring that every master Cleric reflects the unique ideals and purpose of the deity they faithfully serve.

### Effects

- Channel Divinity remains a **passive talent**.
- You gain access to the **Rank V Channel Divinity effects** listed on your chosen patron's page.
- You retain all benefits gained from **Channel Divinity – Ranks I–IV**.
- Channel Divinity has no universal mechanical effects of its own.
- All effects, bonuses, penalties, triggers and restrictions are determined entirely by your chosen patron.
- Patron effects may interact with your **Blessing Aura**, Cleric talents, spells, miracles and other divine abilities whenever specified on the patron's page.
- You cannot benefit from Channel Divinity effects granted by more than one patron at a time.
- If your patron changes, all previous Channel Divinity effects are lost and replaced by those of your new patron, subject to the GM's approval.
- Channel Divinity is considered a permanent **Divine Patron Connection**.

### Lore

The highest-ranking Clerics are remembered throughout history not because of the miracles they performed, but because of the unwavering bond they shared with their patron. Their presence alone is said to inspire faith, command reverence and remind those around them that the gods remain active within the world of Asteria.

Many sacred texts describe these individuals as the "Chosen Hands" of their deity. While they never possess a god's true power, they are entrusted with carrying their patron's will further than any ordinary servant could. Their lives become examples for future generations, shaping religions, guiding kingdoms and preserving the teachings of their faith long after their mortal journey has ended.

To achieve this level of communion is both an honour and a burden. The patron's greatest gifts are reserved for those whose devotion has never faltered, for divine power without unwavering faith is a trust that can never truly be earned.

> *"The gods do not seek perfect servants. They seek faithful hearts through which their will may endure."*
>
> — Saint Elyndor, First Voice of the Divine Covenant
