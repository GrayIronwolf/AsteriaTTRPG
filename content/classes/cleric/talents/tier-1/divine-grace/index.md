# Divine Grace

## Overview
Through unwavering faith and devoted service, your patron bestows upon you their first true blessing. This Divine Grace is not a spell nor a miracle that can be learned through study, but a sacred gift granted only to those who have proven themselves worthy in the eyes of their deity.

Every patron expresses their grace differently. A god of life may grant restorative blessings, while a god of war may instill unyielding courage. Patrons of knowledge, nature, death or countless other domains each bestow gifts that embody their own divine purpose. Though the blessing itself varies, its source remains the same—a bond of trust between deity and disciple.

As your connection deepens, your patron will reveal greater aspects of their Divine Grace, allowing these blessings to grow in strength alongside your faith. The nature of these gifts is recorded within your Patron page, making every Cleric's journey unique to the deity they serve.

## Rank 1
### Description

Through unwavering faith and devoted service, your patron bestows upon you their first true blessing. This Divine Grace is not a spell nor a miracle that can be learned through study, but a sacred gift granted only to those who have proven themselves worthy in the eyes of their deity.

Every patron expresses their grace differently. A god of life may grant restorative blessings, while a god of war may instill unyielding courage. Though the blessing itself varies, its source remains the same—a bond of trust between deity and disciple.

### Effects

- Divine Grace is a **Passive Talent**.
- Unlock **Divine Grace I** on your chosen Patron page.
- The effects of Divine Grace are determined entirely by your chosen Patron.
- Divine Grace may interact with **Blessing Aura**, **Channel Divinity**, Cleric talents, spells and other Patron abilities where specified by your Patron page.
- A Cleric may only benefit from the Divine Grace of one Patron at a time.
- If your Patron changes, all Divine Grace effects are replaced by those of your new Patron.

### Lore

The oldest scriptures teach that every true servant eventually receives a sign that their devotion has been acknowledged. Some describe a comforting warmth during prayer, others hear a quiet whisper carried upon the wind, while a few experience visions that forever change the course of their lives.

These moments are known collectively as Divine Grace. Though the form differs between faiths, each blessing serves as undeniable proof that a patron has accepted a follower into their service. From that moment onward, the Cleric walks not only by faith, but with the knowledge that their patron watches over their path.

The faithful often say that miracles may inspire belief, but Divine Grace is what transforms belief into an unbreakable bond between mortal and deity.

> *"Grace is not earned through strength alone—it is entrusted to those whose faith never falters."*
>
> — The Book of Divine Accord

## Rank 2
### Description

Your devotion has strengthened your bond with your patron, allowing their Divine Grace to manifest more fully within you. What was once a single blessing begins to grow into a lasting expression of your patron's divine will, shaping both your abilities and your purpose as one of their chosen servants.

Each patron grants this grace in their own way. Some offer protection to those under their care, others inspire unwavering courage, while some reveal hidden truths or command the powers of life, death, nature or countless other divine domains. Whatever form it takes, Divine Grace is a sacred reflection of the deity who bestowed it.

As your faith deepens, your patron entrusts you with greater responsibility. Their blessings become increasingly woven into your daily life, allowing your actions to better embody the ideals they represent. Every step taken in faithful service strengthens this divine connection, preparing you for the greater gifts still to come.

### Lore

Among the faithful, it is said that Divine Grace grows alongside devotion rather than appearing all at once. The first blessing is merely the beginning of a lifelong relationship between patron and Cleric, with every act of faith strengthening the sacred bond they share.

Senior priests often recognise this growth long before the Cleric does. They see subtle changes in how the faithful carry themselves, speak with conviction and perform their duties, understanding that these are outward signs of an ever-deepening divine connection. Such individuals are often entrusted with greater responsibilities, not because of their power alone, but because they have proven themselves worthy of their patron's continued favour.

Those who continue walking this path eventually become living examples of their patron's ideals, carrying a measure of divine grace wherever they travel.

> *"The first blessing is a welcome. The second is a promise that you walk the path together."*
>
> — High Priestess Elyndra, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant

## Rank 3
### Description

The bond between you and your patron has become an enduring part of your soul. Their Divine Grace is no longer an occasional blessing but a constant presence, guiding your actions and reinforcing the ideals you have sworn to uphold. Through unwavering faith, you have earned a greater measure of your patron's trust, allowing their influence to manifest more profoundly.

Although every patron grants different blessings, each Divine Grace serves the same purpose—to prepare their chosen servant for greater responsibilities. Whether your deity embodies life, war, nature, knowledge, death or another divine domain, their grace shapes you into a more faithful representative of their will.

Those who witness your deeds begin to recognise the subtle signs of your patron's favour. Your words carry greater conviction, your resolve inspires confidence, and your actions increasingly reflect the divine principles you have devoted your life to serving. While your greatest blessings still lie ahead, there is no doubt that your patron walks beside you.

### Lore

Within every major faith across Asteria, reaching the third stage of Divine Grace is regarded as a defining milestone in a Cleric's journey. It marks the point where faith is no longer sustained by belief alone, but by a living relationship between deity and disciple. Such Clerics often become mentors, temple guardians or spiritual leaders, trusted to guide others through both triumph and hardship.

Ancient chronicles speak of champions whose Divine Grace became so evident that strangers could sense their patron's presence without a single miracle being performed. Whether comforting the fearful, standing firm against impossible odds or offering wisdom during times of uncertainty, these faithful servants became living reflections of the divine ideals they represented.

It is said that while miracles reveal a patron's power, Divine Grace reveals their trust.

> *"Power may be granted in an instant, but grace is entrusted only to those who prove worthy of carrying it."*
>
> — Archon Seraphel, Voice of the Seven Temples

## Rank 4
### Description

Your patron's Divine Grace has become deeply intertwined with your soul, marking you as one of their most trusted servants. Their presence is no longer something you merely call upon—it accompanies you in every prayer, every act of devotion and every decision made in their name. Your faith has matured into a sacred covenant, allowing your patron to reveal greater aspects of their divine blessing.

Each deity bestows this grace according to their own divine purpose. Whether granting unwavering courage, profound wisdom, restorative power, mastery over nature or blessings beyond mortal understanding, every expression of Divine Grace reflects the ideals of the patron who grants it. Though the blessings differ, they all serve to prepare the Cleric for the greater responsibilities that accompany unwavering faith.

Those who stand beside you begin to recognise that your patron's favour extends beyond miraculous acts alone. Your very presence reflects the values of the deity you serve, inspiring allies, strengthening the faithful and reminding others that true devotion is rewarded with blessings that cannot be earned through mortal effort alone.

### Lore

Among the greatest temples of Asteria, only a select few Clerics are recognised as having reached this level of Divine Grace. These individuals are often entrusted with safeguarding sacred relics, leading pilgrimages across dangerous lands and acting as the living representatives of their patron's will.

Many ancient texts describe Divine Grace as a river that flows from the divine into the mortal soul. With each act of faithful service, that river grows stronger, carrying greater blessings and deeper understanding. By the fourth stage, the connection has become unmistakable, and even those outside the faith often sense the quiet authority carried by such devoted servants.

To many believers, these Clerics stand as proof that the gods do not merely watch from afar—they continue to shape the world through those who dedicate their lives to carrying their grace.

> *"The faithful do not seek greatness. They seek only to serve, and greatness follows in the footsteps of grace."*
>
> — High Oracle Caelith, Keeper of the Divine Covenant

## Rank 5
### Description

Your Divine Grace has reached its fullest expression, marking you as one of your patron's greatest mortal champions. The sacred bond between deity and disciple has become absolute, allowing your patron's blessings to flow through you with unparalleled clarity. Their will is reflected not only through your miracles, but through your words, your actions and the unwavering faith that defines your every step.

No two manifestations of Divine Grace are alike. Every patron grants blessings that embody their own divine nature, ensuring that each Cleric's journey remains unique. Whether your patron embodies life, war, knowledge, nature, death or another sacred domain, their ultimate grace transforms you into a living testament of their ideals.

The faithful look upon you as proof that unwavering devotion is rewarded. Your presence inspires hope, courage or reverence according to the nature of your patron, while those who oppose your deity cannot deny that a higher power walks beside you. Though you remain mortal, your Divine Grace has become an enduring symbol of the covenant between yourself and the god you serve.

### Lore

Legends speak of only a handful of Clerics throughout history who have attained the highest expression of Divine Grace. These individuals became inseparable from the faith they served, their names remembered not for the miracles they performed, but for the unwavering devotion with which they carried their patron's will into the world.

Many founded great temples, ended holy wars, restored kingdoms or stood against ancient evils that threatened all of Asteria. Yet when asked about their accomplishments, they rarely spoke of themselves. Instead, they credited every victory, every miracle and every act of mercy to the grace bestowed upon them by their patron.

The greatest lesson passed down through these stories is that Divine Grace is never a reward for seeking power. It is the natural result of a lifetime spent in faithful service. Those who bear the Fifth Grace understand that they are not the source of the divine—they are its chosen vessel, carrying their patron's light, shadow or purpose into a world that will always need it.

> *"Grace is the quiet promise that no faithful soul ever walks alone."*
>
> — **The Book of Divine Accord**
