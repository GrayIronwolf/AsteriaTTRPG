# Fortified Mind

## Overview
Through disciplined prayer, meditation and unwavering devotion to your patron, your mind becomes increasingly resilient against supernatural influence. While others may falter before overwhelming terror or malicious psychic forces, your faith provides an anchor that steadies your thoughts and strengthens your resolve.

Fortified Mind does not make you fearless, nor does it render your thoughts beyond reach. Instead, it reinforces your will, allowing you to recognise and resist attempts to overwhelm, manipulate or corrupt your mind. Every trial endured strengthens your confidence that your patron walks beside you, even in moments of doubt.

This mental discipline is considered a fundamental lesson among Clerics. Before they are entrusted with powerful miracles, they must first learn to protect the sanctuary of their own mind, ensuring that fear and deception cannot easily turn them from their sacred path.

## Rank 1
### Description

While others may falter before overwhelming terror or malicious psychic forces, your faith provides an anchor that steadies your thoughts and strengthens your resolve.

Fortified Mind does not make you fearless, nor does it render your thoughts beyond reach. Instead, it reinforces your will, allowing you to recognise and resist attempts to overwhelm, manipulate or corrupt your mind. Every trial endured strengthens your confidence that your patron walks beside you, even in moments of doubt.

This mental discipline is considered a fundamental lesson among Clerics. Before they are entrusted with powerful miracles, they must first learn to protect the sanctuary of their own mind, ensuring that fear and deception cannot easily turn them from their sacred path.

### Effects

- Fortified Mind is a **Passive Talent**.
- Gain a **+2 bonus** to all saving throws made to resist the **Fear** condition.
- Gain a **+2 bonus** to all saving throws against **Psychic** spells, abilities and magical effects.
- Gain a **+2 bonus** to checks made to resist magical mental influence, including **Charm**, **Compulsion**, **Confusion**, **Possession**, or similar effects, as determined by the GM.
- This talent does not grant immunity to Fear, Psychic damage or mental conditions.
- Fortified Mind is considered a **Divine Blessing**.

### Lore

The greatest battles fought by a Cleric are not always against monsters or heretics, but within their own heart and mind. Ancient temples teach that faith without conviction is easily shaken, and that a mind left undefended becomes an easy target for those who wield fear and deception as weapons.

Every ordained Cleric spends countless hours studying sacred teachings while enduring spiritual trials designed to strengthen their resolve. These rituals teach them to remain calm in the face of terror, reject whispers of corruption and hold fast to the ideals of their patron, no matter how overwhelming the darkness before them may seem.

Those who master these teachings become renowned for their unwavering composure, standing firm where others would flee and meeting even the most horrifying supernatural threats with steadfast faith.

> *"The strongest fortress ever built is the mind that refuses to abandon its faith."*
>
> — High Inquisitor Seraphis

## Rank 2
### Description

Your unwavering devotion continues to strengthen your mind, forging a will that is increasingly difficult to break. The teachings of your patron have become second nature, allowing you to confront supernatural terror and psychic assaults with calm determination rather than panic.

As your faith deepens, your thoughts become more disciplined and resistant to outside influence. Malevolent entities find it harder to cloud your judgement, while fear loses much of its power over your actions. Though your mind remains mortal, it has become a sanctuary reinforced by conviction, prayer and divine purpose.

This growing resilience is recognised throughout the temples of Asteria as a sign of spiritual maturity. Experienced Clerics are expected to remain composed during moments of despair, inspiring allies through their unwavering resolve while resisting forces that seek to corrupt or dominate their minds.

### Lore

Among the faithful, a disciplined mind is considered one of the greatest gifts a patron can nurture within their followers. Veteran Clerics often endure long periods of meditation, isolation and spiritual reflection, strengthening their resolve before they are entrusted with greater divine responsibilities.

Stories tell of Clerics who stood before nightmarish creatures without yielding, their prayers silencing the fear that consumed those around them. Others resisted the whispers of ancient horrors that sought to twist their thoughts, emerging with their faith stronger than before.

These tales remind every initiate that true strength begins within. Divine miracles may protect the body, but only unwavering conviction can protect the soul from those who seek to claim it.

> *"The darkness first conquers the mind. Guard it well, and the rest will follow."*
>
> — Sister Elyra, Keeper of the Sacred Mind

## Rank 3

While no mortal is beyond temptation or manipulation, your conviction allows you to confront such threats with remarkable clarity and determination.

Among experienced Clerics, Fortified Mind is seen as more than resilience—it is a reflection of complete trust in one's patron. Even when confronted by horrors beyond mortal understanding, the faithful remain steadfast, knowing that courage born of faith is stronger than fear born of darkness.

Fear no longer clouds your judgement as it once did, and even the most insidious psychic assaults struggle to overcome a spirit forged through discipline, prayer and absolute faith.

Every lesson, trial and hardship has reinforced the sanctuary of your thoughts. Rather than resisting through determination alone, your faith has become an inseparable part of who you are. Attempts to manipulate, terrify or corrupt your mind are met by a will tempered through countless acts of devotion, allowing you to remain composed when others succumb to panic or despair.

Although no mortal is beyond the reach of the supernatural, few possess the mental resilience of a master Cleric. Your patron's teachings guide your thoughts, your conviction steadies your heart, and your faith serves as a constant reminder that even in the darkest moments, you are never truly alone.

> *"A mind devoted to faith cannot be conquered by fear—it can only be tested."*
