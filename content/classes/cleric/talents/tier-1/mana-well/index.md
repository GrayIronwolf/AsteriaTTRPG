# Mana Well

## Overview
Before a Spellblade can master arcane combat, they must first master the vessel that contains their power. Through relentless training, meditation, and repeated exposure to mana, the Spellblade learns to expand the natural limits of their internal mana reserves.

This expansion creates the foundation of what Spellblades call a Mana Well—a deep reservoir of magical energy that fuels both their martial techniques and arcane abilities. While many novice practitioners focus on increasing their offensive power, experienced Spellblades understand that sustained combat is won by those who can continue fighting long after others have exhausted their resources.

At Rank 1, the Mana Well begins to take shape. The increase is immediately noticeable, allowing the Spellblade to cast additional abilities, maintain magical techniques for longer periods, and remain effective during extended encounters.

Though only the beginning of the talent's growth, many masters consider Mana Well one of the most important foundations of the entire Spellblade path. A deeper well creates opportunities, endurance, and flexibility that cannot be replicated through skill alone.

## Rank 1
### Description

### Effects

- Increase your **Maximum Mana by ×3**.
- This multiplier is applied to your **Base Maximum Mana**.
- The increase is permanent while this talent is learned.
- The additional mana becomes immediately available upon learning the talent.

#### Formula

Maximum Mana = Base Mana × 3

### Lore

Among Spellblades, the cultivation of a Mana Well is considered the first true milestone on the path toward mastery. While a sword may be sharpened and spells may be memorised, neither can compensate for a lack of magical endurance.

The oldest Spellblade academies teach that mana is like water carried into a vast desert. Every technique draws from the same reserve, and once that reserve is exhausted, even the greatest warrior becomes vulnerable.

Many legendary Spellblades became famous not because they possessed the most destructive abilities, but because they could continue fighting long after their opponents had depleted their strength. Their Mana Wells sustained them through battles that would have broken lesser combatants.

For this reason, instructors often insist that apprentices strengthen their reserves before pursuing more advanced techniques. A strong foundation supports every future achievement.

> "Power is meaningless when the well runs dry."
>
> — Master Valen Arcturus, Seventh Blade of the Azure Order

## Rank 2
### Description

As the Spellblade's mastery grows, so too does the depth of their Mana Well. What was once a modest reservoir begins to expand into a substantial source of arcane power, capable of sustaining increasingly demanding techniques.

The cultivation of a Mana Well is not simply a matter of storing more mana. The Spellblade learns to strengthen the pathways through which mana flows, reinforcing their body and soul so they can safely contain greater quantities of magical energy. Without this growth, advanced techniques would place unbearable strain upon the practitioner.

At Rank 2, the Spellblade experiences a significant increase in their available reserves. Battles that once required careful conservation of mana become easier to manage, allowing the Spellblade to maintain pressure and continue casting throughout longer encounters.

Many instructors consider this the point where an apprentice begins to transition into a true Spellblade. Their reserves become large enough to support both martial combat and arcane techniques without constantly risking exhaustion.

### Effects
- Increase your **Maximum Mana by ×6**.
- This multiplier is applied to your **Base Maximum Mana**.
- The additional mana becomes immediately available upon learning the talent.

#### Formula

Maximum Mana = Base Mana × 6

### Lore

Veteran Spellblades often describe Rank 2 as the point where the Mana Well becomes impossible to ignore. The increase in magical endurance is substantial enough that even inexperienced practitioners immediately notice the difference.

The process is neither comfortable nor quick. Expanding one's Mana Well requires months of disciplined training, repeated mana circulation exercises, and careful refinement of the soul's connection to arcane energy. Many students abandon the process due to its difficulty.

Those who persevere are rewarded with reserves that can sustain far more demanding techniques. This increase allows them to remain active during prolonged battles, maintaining the balance between swordsmanship and spellcraft that defines the Spellblade path.

The ancient records of the Azure Orders speak of Mana Wells as living reservoirs. The deeper they become, the more power they can hold, and the greater the possibilities available to the one who wields them.

> "The first well grants endurance. The second grants freedom."
>
> — Grand Spellblade Aethon Vale, Master of the Sapphire Hall

## Rank 3
### Description

The Mana Well has grown beyond a simple reservoir and now resembles a vast internal lake of arcane power. The Spellblade's body and soul have adapted to contain quantities of mana that would overwhelm most practitioners, allowing them to wield magic with far greater freedom.

At this stage, mana conservation becomes less about necessity and more about efficiency. The Spellblade can maintain prolonged combat techniques, sustain enchantments for longer periods, and continue casting long after others would be forced to retreat and recover.

The cultivation of such a Mana Well requires extraordinary discipline. Every increase places additional strain upon the pathways that carry mana throughout the body. Those who fail to strengthen themselves alongside their reserves risk injury, magical exhaustion, or complete mana collapse.

For many Spellblades, Rank 3 marks the point where they truly begin to feel the potential of their path. Their magical endurance becomes a defining advantage, allowing them to fight longer, cast more frequently, and adapt to changing battlefield conditions without fear of immediate depletion.

### Lore

Ancient Spellblade texts often describe Rank 3 as the threshold between the ordinary and the exceptional. While many warriors learn to wield magic and many mages learn to fight, few possess the reserves necessary to excel at both simultaneously.

Within the halls of the oldest arcane academies, students reaching this stage were once required to undertake endurance trials designed to test the limits of their Mana Wells. Some would cast continuously for hours, while others maintained complex enchantments throughout entire days.

Stories tell of legendary Spellblades whose Mana Wells became so deep that they could sustain magical warfare across entire campaigns. Though Rank 3 falls far short of such legends, it is often the first glimpse of what might one day become possible.

The deeper the well becomes, the more responsibility it demands. Power without discipline is dangerous, and a vast reservoir can be just as destructive to its wielder as it is to their enemies.

> "A pond may sustain a traveler. A lake may sustain a kingdom."
>
> — Archblade Seraph Valorian, Keeper of the Third Wellv

## Rank 4
### Description

Few Spellblades ever reach this stage.

The Mana Well has expanded into a vast reservoir capable of holding immense quantities of arcane energy. What once required careful conservation can now be sustained through entire battles, campaigns, and prolonged magical engagements.

At Rank 4, the Spellblade's connection to mana becomes second nature. The constant awareness of dwindling reserves fades into the background, replaced by confidence in the strength of their foundation. They possess enough energy to support advanced combat techniques while still maintaining reserves for unexpected threats.

The cultivation required to reach this level is immense. The body, mind, and soul must all be strengthened to withstand the pressure of such a large concentration of mana. Without discipline and training, the strain alone would be enough to cripple lesser practitioners.

Among the Spellblade Orders, Rank 4 is often viewed as the mark of a veteran. It is proof that the practitioner has survived years of training, battle, and refinement, building a Mana Well capable of supporting truly exceptional feats.

### Effects

- Increase your **Maximum Mana by ×12**.
- This multiplier is applied to your **Base Maximum Mana**.
- The additional mana becomes immediately available upon learning the talent.

#### Formula

Maximum Mana = Base Mana × 12

### Lore

The oldest records of the Arcane Blades speak of practitioners whose Mana Wells became so deep that they were compared to inland seas. Their reserves seemed inexhaustible to those around them, allowing them to maintain powerful techniques for astonishing lengths of time.

Within many Spellblade traditions, Rank 4 is regarded as a symbol of mastery over one's foundation. While greater power still lies ahead, few can deny the significance of reaching a stage where one's reserves dwarf those of ordinary spellcasters.

Veterans often warn younger Spellblades that a vast Mana Well is both a blessing and a burden. The temptation to rely solely upon overwhelming reserves can lead to arrogance, wastefulness, and eventual defeat.

The wisest masters understand that the purpose of a deep well is not to spend more recklessly, but to create more possibilities. The greater the reserve, the more choices remain available when others have none.

> "The difference between a novice and a master is not how much power they possess. It is how much remains when the battle is not yet over."
>
> — High Spellblade Arkanis Thorne, Warden of the Azure Depthsv

## Rank 5
### Description

The Mana Well has reached its pinnacle.

What began as a modest reservoir of arcane energy has become a vast ocean contained within the Spellblade's soul. The amount of mana available at this stage far exceeds what most practitioners will ever possess, allowing the Spellblade to sustain techniques, enchantments, and magical combat styles that would be impossible for lesser combatants.

At Rank 5, the Spellblade no longer worries about whether they possess enough mana to continue fighting. Instead, they concern themselves with how best to wield the immense reserves at their disposal. The challenge is no longer endurance, but mastery.

The cultivation required to reach this level is legendary. Every pathway within the body has been strengthened, every aspect of the soul tempered through years of training, combat, and arcane refinement. Few ever achieve such depth within their Mana Well, and those who do are often regarded as living legends among their peers.

This rank represents the culmination of the Spellblade's foundation. Every spell cast, every enchanted strike, and every future talent built upon arcane power draws strength from the immense reservoir created through Mana Well.

### Effects

- Increase your **Maximum Mana by ×15**.
- This multiplier is applied to your **Base Maximum Mana**.
- The additional mana becomes immediately available upon learning the talent.

#### Formula

Maximum Mana = Base Mana × 15

### Lore

Among the oldest Spellblade Orders, Rank 5 is often called **The Endless Reservoir**. It is said that a practitioner who reaches this stage possesses enough arcane endurance to fight battles that would exhaust entire companies of lesser spellcasters.

Ancient chronicles tell of legendary Spellblades who marched for days without rest, sustaining magical wards, empowering their weapons, and continuing to cast even as entire armies exhausted their reserves. Whether these stories are entirely true or embellished through centuries of retelling remains a matter of debate.

What is certain is that those who reach Rank 5 stand among the elite of their discipline. Their Mana Wells are so deep that they cease to be measured against ordinary standards. Their reserves become a defining aspect of their identity, spoken of with the same reverence as a master swordsman's skill or an archmage's knowledge.

Yet the wisest among them know a simple truth: even an ocean can be emptied if enough is taken from it. A vast Mana Well is not permission for recklessness, but the reward for discipline. Those who remember this lesson become legends. Those who forget it become cautionary tales.

> "The purpose of a great reservoir is not to spend without thought. It is to ensure that when the final moment arrives, you still have power left to give."
>
> — Grandmaster Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well
