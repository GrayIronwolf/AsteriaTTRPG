# Divine Insight

## Overview
You offer a prayer to your patron, opening your mind to receive a fragment of their divine perception. For a short time, your senses become aligned with the ideals and domains of the deity you serve, granting insight that no mortal could achieve through their own understanding.

The form this insight takes is unique to every patron. A god of knowledge may reveal forgotten truths, a deity of life may perceive sickness and vitality, while a patron of death may sense lingering souls. Patrons of war, justice, nature and countless other domains each grant their own unique perspective upon the world.

Divine Insight does not reveal all things. Instead, it grants only the knowledge your patron chooses to share, allowing faithful servants to better carry out their divine purpose.

### Effects

- Divine Insight is an **Active Talent**.
- Activate Divine Insight on yourself.
- Gain **Divine Insight I** from your chosen Patron.
- The effects of Divine Insight are determined entirely by your chosen Patron.
- Divine Insight lasts **10 minutes**.
- Divine Insight may interact with **Blessing Aura**, **Channel Divinity**, **Divine Grace**, **Divine Inspiration**, Cleric talents, spells and other Patron abilities where specified by your Patron page.
- Divine Insight can only be activated **once per Short Rest**.

### Lore

Every faith teaches that the gods perceive the world through lenses beyond mortal comprehension. While no Cleric can truly share a deity's limitless awareness, those who have earned their patron's trust may briefly glimpse creation through divine eyes.

These sacred moments have guided pilgrims to forgotten shrines, revealed hidden dangers before catastrophe struck and uncovered truths that would otherwise have remained lost to history. Such revelations are never random; they are gifts bestowed with purpose, reminding every Cleric that true wisdom comes not from seeing everything, but from seeing what their patron wishes them to see.

> *"The gods reveal only what is needed, never what is desired."*

> — High Oracle Seraphine, Keeper of the Celestial Archives

## Rank 2
### Description

Your connection with your patron has deepened, allowing you to receive clearer and more sustained moments of divine revelation. Through focused prayer and unwavering faith, you open your mind to a greater fragment of your patron's awareness, granting insight beyond mortal understanding.

No two patrons reveal the world in the same way. A deity of knowledge may uncover hidden truths and forgotten lore, while a patron of war may reveal tactical opportunities upon the battlefield. Gods of life, death, justice, nature and countless other domains each grant their own sacred perspective, ensuring that every Cleric experiences Divine Insight according to the ideals of the deity they serve.

As your faith grows, your patron entrusts you with increasingly profound guidance. These revelations are never without purpose, providing only the knowledge necessary to fulfil your patron's divine will rather than satisfying idle curiosity.

### Lore

Among experienced Clerics, Divine Insight is regarded as one of the patron's greatest gifts. It is said that those who seek wisdom with a humble heart are occasionally granted fleeting glimpses of truths that lie beyond the understanding of ordinary mortals. These moments often alter the course of quests, uncover ancient secrets or reveal dangers before they come to pass.

Senior members of the faith teach that Divine Insight should never be used for personal gain. Every revelation carries purpose, and those who abuse their patron's trust soon find that the gods have little reason to continue sharing their wisdom. The wisest Clerics therefore approach every invocation with reverence, accepting that the greatest answers are those freely given, not demanded.

> *"Wisdom is not measured by what you discover, but by what you do with the truths entrusted to you."*
>
> — **Archsage Valerius, Keeper of the Divine Archives**

As your bond continues to deepen, your patron entrusts you with increasingly meaningful revelations. These visions are never random; they are granted to guide your actions, strengthen your faith and aid you in fulfilling the divine purpose entrusted to you.

### Lore

Throughout history, the Third Divine Insight has marked the point where a Cleric is recognised as a trusted vessel of their patron's wisdom. Many temples entrust these faithful servants with sacred relics, dangerous expeditions and matters of spiritual importance, knowing that their patron's guidance will illuminate paths hidden from ordinary eyes.

Ancient records tell of Clerics who uncovered forgotten kingdoms, exposed hidden corruption within powerful empires and discovered relics thought lost for millennia through moments of Divine Insight. Yet every account carries the same lesson: the revelation itself was never the reward. Rather, it was the responsibility that followed. Those granted divine knowledge are expected to act upon it with humility, wisdom and unwavering devotion to their patron's will.

> *"To receive divine insight is to accept the responsibility of acting upon divine truth."*
>
> — **High Hierophant Caelus, Keeper of the Eternal Scriptures**

## Rank 5
### Description

Your faith has reached such extraordinary heights that your patron entrusts you with the fullest expression of their divine perception. When you invoke Divine Insight, your mind becomes a vessel through which your deity briefly observes the mortal world, granting you profound understanding according to their divine domain and sacred purpose.

The revelations bestowed upon you are unique to your patron alone. A deity of knowledge may unveil secrets lost to history, while a patron of justice may expose deception hidden beneath the truth. Gods of war may reveal the flow of conflict, patrons of life may perceive the balance of every living soul, and deities of death may witness the unseen passage between life and the beyond. Every patron grants a perspective that no mortal could ever achieve alone.

Although this gift is immense, your patron reveals only what serves their greater purpose. Divine Insight is not omniscience—it is the privilege of seeing exactly what your patron believes you must know. Those who master this talent become trusted instruments of divine wisdom, carrying their patron's guidance into every corner of Asteria.

### Lore

Only the greatest High Clerics are ever said to attain the Fifth Divine Insight. Throughout history, these faithful servants have stood beside kings, guided heroes and uncovered truths that reshaped entire civilizations. Their wisdom was never born from superior intellect alone, but from the humility to seek guidance from the deity they served and the courage to follow wherever that guidance led.

The sacred texts teach that the highest form of Divine Insight is not the ability to see everything, but the wisdom to recognise what truly matters. Every revelation carries responsibility, every vision demands action and every truth revealed must be used in faithful service rather than personal ambition. Those who earn this blessing become living witnesses to their patron's will, forever remembered as beacons of wisdom within their faith.

> *"The greatest revelation is not to know all things, but to understand the one truth your patron asks you to carry into the world."*
>
> — **The Book of Divine Accord**
