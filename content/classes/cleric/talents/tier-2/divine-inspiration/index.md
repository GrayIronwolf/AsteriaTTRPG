# Divine Inspiration

## Overview
You call upon your patron to bestow a fragment of their divine will upon a creature within range. This blessing is not universal, but a direct expression of the deity you serve. Every patron grants inspiration differently, reflecting their ideals, domains and sacred purpose.

Whether offering hope, courage, wisdom, judgement or countless other divine gifts, Divine Inspiration allows your patron's influence to reach beyond yourself and touch another. The exact nature of this blessing is defined by your Patron page, ensuring every Cleric's Divine Inspiration remains unique to the deity they follow.

As your faith deepens, your patron entrusts you with increasingly powerful expressions of their divine inspiration, allowing this sacred gift to grow alongside your journey.

## Rank 1
### Description

You call upon your patron to bestow a fragment of their divine will upon a creature within range. This blessing is not universal, but a direct expression of the deity you serve. Every patron grants inspiration differently, reflecting their ideals, domains and sacred purpose.

Whether offering hope, courage, wisdom, judgement or countless other divine gifts, Divine Inspiration allows your patron's influence to reach beyond yourself and touch another. The exact nature of this blessing is defined by your Patron page, ensuring every Cleric's Divine Inspiration remains unique to the deity they follow.

As your faith deepens, your patron entrusts you with increasingly powerful expressions of their divine inspiration, allowing this sacred gift to grow alongside your journey.

### Effects

- Divine Inspiration is a **Cantrip**.
- Target one creature within range.
- Apply **Divine Inspiration I** from your chosen Patron.
- The effects of Divine Inspiration are determined entirely by your chosen Patron.
- Divine Inspiration may interact with **Blessing Aura**, **Channel Divinity**, **Divine Grace**, Cleric talents, spells and other Patron abilities where specified by your Patron page.
- A creature may only be affected by one instance of Divine Inspiration at a time.
- Recasting this ability refreshes its duration.

### Lore

The scriptures tell that the gods rarely speak directly to mortals. Instead, they entrust fragments of their wisdom, conviction and purpose to those who faithfully carry out their will. Through Divine Inspiration, a Cleric becomes the vessel through which that sacred influence reaches the world, allowing others to briefly experience the guiding hand of the divine.

> *"The faithful do not create inspiration—they carry it from the heavens."*
>
> — *The Book of Divine Accord*

## Rank 2
### Description

Your patron's voice grows clearer as your faith deepens, allowing you to bestow a greater measure of their divine influence upon another. What was once a fleeting blessing now lingers with increased strength, giving your patron's will more time to shape the actions of the creature you inspire.

Every patron grants Divine Inspiration according to their own divine nature. Some kindle unwavering courage before battle, others grant profound wisdom, restore hope, instil righteous conviction or pronounce sacred judgement. No two patrons inspire in the same way, for every blessing is a reflection of the deity who grants it.

As your connection to your patron strengthens, so too does your ability to act as their chosen vessel. Those touched by your Divine Inspiration become living examples of your patron's influence, carrying a fragment of their divine purpose wherever they go until the blessing fades.

### Lore

Among experienced Clerics, Divine Inspiration is regarded as one of the purest expressions of a patron's favour. Rather than performing miracles directly, the deity works through those they have chosen, allowing fragments of their will to guide faithful servants and worthy souls alike.

Many legends tell of ordinary people accomplishing extraordinary deeds after receiving a Cleric's blessing. Whether standing against overwhelming darkness, uncovering forgotten truths or showing unexpected compassion, these moments are remembered not as acts of chance, but as quiet reminders that the gods continue to shape the world through those devoted to their service.

> *"The greatest blessings are not always the loudest. Often, they are the quiet voice that gives a soul the strength to take one more step."*
>
> — **High Priest Althoran, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your bond with your patron has grown into a powerful conduit for divine influence. Through a simple prayer or sacred invocation, you may call forth a greater measure of your patron's inspiration, allowing their will to shape the destiny of another creature. The blessing has become more enduring, carrying the authority and purpose of the deity who bestowed it.

Every patron inspires in a different way. A deity of war may awaken indomitable resolve, while a patron of knowledge may illuminate the mind with unparalleled clarity. Gods of life, death, nature, justice and countless other divine domains each grant their own unique expression of Divine Inspiration, ensuring that no two Clerics wield the same blessing.

Those touched by your Divine Inspiration often find themselves accomplishing feats they once believed impossible. Whether through courage, wisdom, resilience or another sacred gift, they become temporary vessels for your patron's influence, carrying a fragment of divine purpose into the challenges that lie ahead.

### Lore

Within the oldest temples of Asteria, Divine Inspiration is described as the sacred language through which the gods encourage their faithful without ever speaking a single word. Rather than commanding their followers directly, the patrons entrust fragments of their divine purpose to devoted Clerics, who in turn pass those blessings to others.

Those who receive such inspiration rarely describe it as overwhelming power. Instead, they speak of perfect certainty—a moment where fear fades, doubt disappears and every action feels guided by something far greater than themselves. These experiences often become lifelong memories, strengthening not only the recipient's resolve but also their faith in the patron whose grace reached them through the Cleric.

> *"The gods need not raise their voices. A single moment of true inspiration can change the course of a lifetime."*
>
> — **Saint Elyndor, Herald of the Divine Path**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your patron's favour flows through you with remarkable clarity, allowing you to bestow a profound expression of their divine will upon another creature. The inspiration you invoke is no longer a fleeting blessing but a powerful manifestation of the sacred bond between deity and disciple. Those touched by your patron's grace become living reflections of the ideals your faith represents.

No two patrons inspire in the same manner. A deity of justice may embolden unwavering conviction, while a god of nature may awaken harmony with the world around them. Patrons of war, knowledge, life, death and countless other divine domains each grant unique blessings that shape the actions and destiny of those they touch.

As your connection with your patron continues to deepen, your Divine Inspiration becomes one of the clearest demonstrations of their presence within the mortal realm. Those who receive your blessing often speak of renewed purpose, unwavering certainty and a sense that a higher power walks beside them, guiding every step they take.

### Lore

The Fourth Inspiration is regarded by many temples as a sacred milestone, reserved for Clerics whose devotion has remained unwavering through countless trials. By this stage, the patron's influence has become so refined that their blessing is recognised as more than divine magic—it is a direct expression of their will entrusted to mortal hands.

Throughout history, kings, generals and humble pilgrims alike have sought the blessing of such Clerics before undertaking journeys that would shape the fate of nations. Though each patron grants different gifts, every Divine Inspiration carries the same truth: the gods continue to guide the world through those who faithfully answer their call.

To receive such a blessing is considered a privilege. To bestow it is considered one of the greatest responsibilities a Cleric can bear.

> *"The greatest honour a servant can receive is to carry the will of their patron into the hearts of another."*
>
> — **Grand Hierophant Seraphiel, Voice of the Sacred Assembly**

## Rank 5
### Description

You have mastered the sacred art of Divine Inspiration, becoming one of the few mortals capable of serving as a flawless conduit for your patron's will. With a single invocation, you may call forth the greatest expression of your deity's inspiration, allowing their divine influence to rest upon another creature with unmatched strength and clarity.

The form this inspiration takes is determined entirely by your patron. Some grant unwavering courage in the face of impossible odds, others bestow profound wisdom, righteous judgement, serene compassion or fearsome conviction. Every patron's inspiration is unique, reflecting the ideals and purpose of the deity from whom it originates. No two Clerics share the same blessing unless they serve the same divine power.

Those who receive your Divine Inspiration often describe the experience as standing beside their patron themselves. Doubt fades, purpose becomes absolute and every action feels guided by a higher calling. Though the blessing eventually passes, its effects are often remembered for a lifetime, inspiring unwavering faith in those fortunate enough to receive it.

### Lore

Among the greatest legends of Asteria are tales of High Clerics whose words alone rekindled hope in broken armies, calmed kingdoms on the brink of collapse or inspired ordinary people to perform extraordinary acts of courage. While many believed these deeds were miracles, the faithful understood they were the highest expression of Divine Inspiration—a patron working through their chosen servant.

To master this blessing is to accept that you are not the source of divine power, but its vessel. Every prayer spoken, every blessing bestowed and every soul inspired serves as a reminder that the gods continue to shape the world through those who dedicate their lives to faithful service.

The greatest Clerics do not seek glory for themselves. They simply ensure that, wherever they travel, their patron's wisdom, purpose and inspiration continue to guide those who need it most.

> *"The greatest gift a god can grant is not power, but the ability to awaken greatness within another."*
>
> — **The Book of Divine Accord**
