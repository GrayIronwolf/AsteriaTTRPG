# Divine Restoration

## Overview
You call upon your patron to restore a creature touched by divine purpose. Unlike ordinary restorative magic, Divine Restoration does not possess a universal effect. Instead, your patron grants the form of restoration most closely aligned with their divine domain and sacred ideals.

A patron of life may mend wounds and cure afflictions, while a patron of knowledge may restore clarity to a troubled mind. Gods of war may renew a warrior's endurance, patrons of nature may restore harmony to body and spirit, and deities of death may grant peaceful release from lingering curses or unnatural suffering. Every act of restoration is unique, reflecting the will of the patron who answers your prayer.

As your faith deepens, your patron entrusts you with increasingly powerful forms of Divine Restoration, allowing your blessings to better fulfil their divine purpose.

## Rank 1
### Description

You call upon your patron to restore a creature touched by divine purpose. Unlike ordinary restorative magic, Divine Restoration does not possess a universal effect. Instead, your patron grants the form of restoration most closely aligned with their divine domain and sacred ideals.

A patron of life may mend wounds and cure afflictions, while a patron of knowledge may restore clarity to a troubled mind. Gods of war may renew a warrior's endurance, patrons of nature may restore harmony to body and spirit, and deities of death may grant peaceful release from lingering curses or unnatural suffering. Every act of restoration is unique, reflecting the will of the patron who answers your prayer.

As your faith deepens, your patron entrusts you with increasingly powerful forms of Divine Restoration, allowing your blessings to better fulfil their divine purpose.

### Effects

- Divine Restoration is an **Active Talent**.
- Target one creature within range.
- Gain access to **Divine Restoration I** from your chosen Patron.
- The effects of Divine Restoration are determined entirely by your chosen Patron.
- Divine Restoration may interact with **Blessing Aura**, **Channel Divinity**, **Divine Grace**, **Divine Inspiration**, **Divine Insight**, Cleric talents, spells and other Patron abilities where specified by your Patron page.
- Divine Restoration can only be activated **once per Short Rest**.

### Lore

Every faith teaches that true restoration is more than repairing wounds—it is returning something to the state intended by the divine. Whether restoring flesh, spirit, resolve or balance, each patron heals according to their own sacred purpose.

The oldest scriptures tell of Clerics whose prayers restored far more than broken bodies. They rekindled hope within the hopeless, renewed courage in the defeated and brought harmony where corruption had taken root. Such miracles remind the faithful that restoration is not measured by what is repaired, but by what is made whole once again.

> *"The gods restore not what we desire, but what they deem worthy of renewal."*
>
> — **The Book of Divine Accord**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your patron entrusts you with a greater expression of their restorative power, allowing you to call upon blessings that reach beyond the limitations of mortal magic. As your devotion deepens, your prayers carry greater weight, enabling your patron to restore those in need according to their divine purpose.

The nature of this restoration remains unique to every deity. A patron of life may heal deeper wounds and purge greater afflictions, while a god of knowledge may restore forgotten memories or mental clarity. Patrons of war may renew a warrior's resolve, deities of nature may restore balance to corrupted lands or creatures, and gods of death may free souls from unnatural torment. Each act of restoration reflects the values of the patron who grants it rather than following a universal definition of healing.

By invoking Divine Restoration, you become a conduit through which your patron renews what they believe should be made whole. Every blessing serves a purpose beyond simple recovery, reinforcing the ideals and responsibilities of the faith you represent.

- Divine Restoration can only be activated **once per Short Rest**.

### Lore

The faithful teach that restoration is not measured by how much power is wielded, but by the wisdom behind its use. Every patron restores the world according to their own vision of balance, and every blessing is granted with deliberate purpose. Experienced Clerics learn that the greatest miracles often repair far more than flesh, mending broken spirits, shattered convictions or bonds thought forever lost.

Those who reach this stage of Divine Restoration are often entrusted with responsibilities beyond the battlefield. They become healers, judges, counsellors and guides, called upon whenever something precious has been damaged and only divine intervention can begin the process of making it whole once more.

> *"A miracle is not the act of restoring what was lost, but knowing what must be restored first."*
>
> — **High Priest Alaric, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your unwavering devotion has strengthened the sacred bond between yourself and your patron, allowing their restorative power to flow through you with far greater purpose. When you invoke Divine Restoration, you do not simply mend or recover—you become the instrument through which your deity restores what they believe should be made whole.

The form this restoration takes is entirely determined by your patron. A deity of life may mend grievous wounds or purge debilitating afflictions, while a patron of knowledge may restore forgotten memories or sharpen a clouded mind. Gods of war may rekindle a warrior's spirit, patrons of nature may restore harmony to corrupted life, and deities of death may ease unnatural suffering or return balance where death itself has been denied. Every miracle reflects the ideals of the divine being who grants it.

By this stage, your patron entrusts you with increasingly significant acts of restoration. These blessings often change the course of battles, save lives or restore that which seemed beyond recovery, demonstrating that true divine restoration extends far beyond physical healing.

### Lore

Throughout Asteria's history, the Third Divine Restoration has marked the point where a Cleric becomes recognised as a true vessel of their patron's mercy and purpose. Their prayers are sought not merely because they possess great power, but because their patron has deemed them worthy of carrying miracles that few others can invoke.

Ancient accounts tell of devoted Clerics restoring hope to kingdoms consumed by despair, freeing souls from ancient curses and renewing sacred places that had fallen into ruin. Though the miracles themselves varied according to the patron, they all shared one truth: restoration is never granted without purpose. Every blessing carries the will of the deity who bestowed it, reminding both Cleric and recipient that divine power exists to uphold the balance their patron seeks within the world.

> *"Only when you understand what has truly been lost can you appreciate the miracle of its restoration."*
>
> — **Saint Aurelian, Keeper of the Sacred Light**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your patron now entrusts you with a profound expression of their restorative power, allowing your prayers to become vessels for miracles few mortals will ever witness. When you invoke Divine Restoration, you call not upon generic divine magic, but upon the unique will of your patron, who chooses what should be restored according to their sacred purpose.

Every deity defines restoration differently. A patron of life may mend devastating injuries or cleanse powerful afflictions, while a god of knowledge may restore forgotten truths and shattered minds. Patrons of war may reignite the spirit of a weary champion, deities of nature may return balance to corrupted creatures and lands, and gods of death may release souls bound by unnatural forces. Each blessing reflects the values and philosophy of the deity who grants it.

As your faith continues to mature, your patron's miracles become increasingly influential. Whether restoring a single life, renewing a sacred place or reversing the effects of powerful supernatural forces, Divine Restoration stands as a testament to the enduring bond between patron and Cleric.

### Lore

Clerics capable of invoking the Fourth Divine Restoration are regarded as living conduits of their patron's mercy and purpose. Their presence is sought during times of great hardship, when ordinary magic has failed and only the direct blessing of a deity can restore what has been lost. Many become guardians of holy sites, advisors to rulers or leaders of sacred pilgrimages, trusted to wield miracles with wisdom and restraint.

The greatest temples teach that true restoration is never about returning the world to what it once was. Instead, it is about guiding it back toward the balance envisioned by the gods. Every miracle granted through Divine Restoration serves this higher purpose, reminding both the Cleric and those they aid that divine power exists not to defy fate, but to fulfil it.

> *"The patron restores not because the world is broken, but because they know what it was always meant to become."*
>
> — **Grand Hierophant Elyndor, Keeper of the Divine Covenant**

## Rank 5
### Description

Your patron has entrusted you with the highest expression of their restorative power, allowing you to invoke miracles that embody their divine purpose in its purest form. Through unwavering faith and absolute devotion, you become the chosen conduit through which your deity restores what they deem worthy, whether it be body, mind, spirit or another aspect of creation unique to their divine domain.

Every patron's Divine Restoration is unlike any other. A deity of life may perform miracles of extraordinary healing, while a patron of knowledge may restore memories thought forever lost. Gods of war may rekindle an indomitable fighting spirit, patrons of nature may return balance to lands scarred by corruption, and deities of death may release souls from unnatural imprisonment. Each miracle is a reflection of the patron's ideals, ensuring that no two Clerics restore the world in exactly the same way.

To master Divine Restoration is to accept that true restoration is never about reversing fate. Instead, it is about fulfilling the divine design envisioned by your patron, restoring what should endure while preserving the balance they seek to maintain throughout Asteria.

### Lore

Only a handful of Clerics throughout history have been recognised as masters of Divine Restoration. Their names are remembered in every major faith, not because of the miracles they performed, but because of the wisdom with which they chose to perform them. Kings sought their counsel, entire nations prayed for their arrival, and even rival faiths respected the sacred authority they carried.

The oldest scriptures teach that the greatest miracle is not the restoration itself, but the judgement required to know when restoration should be granted. Those who wield the Fifth Divine Restoration understand that every blessing carries immense responsibility, for they act not according to personal desire, but as faithful servants carrying out the will of their patron.

To those who witness such miracles, one truth becomes unmistakably clear: the gods continue to shape the world, not through overwhelming displays of power, but through devoted souls willing to become instruments of their divine purpose.

> *"To restore another is to carry the will of the divine with humility, wisdom and unwavering faith."*
>
> — **The Book of Divine Accord**
