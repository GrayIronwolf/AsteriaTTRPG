# Sacred Aegis

## Overview
You invoke your patron's divine protection, surrounding yourself or another creature with a sacred aegis that shields them from harm. While every Cleric can call upon this protective blessing, each patron shapes the aegis according to their own divine ideals, causing every manifestation to feel unique despite sharing the same sacred foundation.

The aegis strengthens the target against incoming attacks through divine protection rather than physical armour. Whether it manifests as radiant light, spectral armour, swirling leaves, celestial flames, shadowed wings or another sacred form depends entirely upon the patron who answers your prayer. Regardless of its appearance, the blessing serves as a visible reminder that the target stands beneath the watchful gaze of a higher power.

As your faith deepens, your Sacred Aegis grows stronger, allowing your patron to reinforce the blessing with additional divine effects unique to their domain.

## Rank 1
### Description

You invoke your patron's divine protection, surrounding yourself or another creature with a sacred aegis that shields them from harm. While every Cleric can call upon this protective blessing, each patron shapes the aegis according to their own divine ideals, causing every manifestation to feel unique despite sharing the same sacred foundation.

The aegis strengthens the target against incoming attacks through divine protection rather than physical armour. Whether it manifests as radiant light, spectral armour, swirling leaves, celestial flames, shadowed wings or another sacred form depends entirely upon the patron who answers your prayer. Regardless of its appearance, the blessing serves as a visible reminder that the target stands beneath the watchful gaze of a higher power.

As your faith deepens, your Sacred Aegis grows stronger, allowing your patron to reinforce the blessing with additional divine effects unique to their domain.

### Effects

- Sacred Aegis is a **Cantrip**.
- Target yourself or one creature within range.
- The target gains **+2 AC** for the duration.
- Sacred Aegis lasts **3 rounds**.
- The target also gains any **Sacred Aegis I** effects granted by your chosen Patron.
- Patron effects are additional benefits and do not replace the base effects of Sacred Aegis.
- A creature may only benefit from one Sacred Aegis at a time.
- Recasting Sacred Aegis refreshes its duration.

### Lore

Throughout Asteria, every faith tells stories of divine protection appearing when hope seemed lost. Some speak of radiant shields that turned aside deadly blows, while others describe ghostly guardians, living vines, celestial feathers or silent shadows that stood between the faithful and certain death. Though these manifestations differ, they all share a single truth—they are tangible signs that a patron has chosen to intervene.

Experienced Clerics understand that Sacred Aegis is more than a defensive blessing. It is a symbol of trust between deity and disciple, demonstrating that divine protection is not always about preventing harm, but ensuring the faithful endure long enough to fulfil the purpose laid before them.

> *"When faith becomes your shield, even the impossible may be endured."*
>
> — **Saint Valen, Guardian of the First Temple**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your patron's divine protection grows stronger, allowing you to reinforce yourself or another creature with a more resilient Sacred Aegis. The blessing not only endures longer but offers greater protection against the dangers of battle, serving as a steadfast reminder that your patron watches over those who remain faithful.

Although every Cleric can invoke Sacred Aegis, no two manifestations are ever identical. The blessing takes the form most befitting your patron's divine nature, whether it appears as radiant light, celestial armour, sacred vines, spectral guardians or another divine manifestation. Regardless of its appearance, the aegis always provides a reliable shield that stands between the target and approaching danger.

As your connection with your patron deepens, they further empower your Sacred Aegis with blessings unique to their divine domain, allowing the protection to become more than a simple barrier against harm.

### Effects

- Sacred Aegis remains a **Cantrip**.
- Increase the Mana Cost to **25 MP**.
- Increase the Range to **20 metres**.
- Increase the Duration to **4 rounds**.
- Target yourself or one creature within range.
- The target gains **+3 AC** for the duration.
- The target also gains any **Sacred Aegis II** effects granted by your chosen Patron.
- Patron effects are additional benefits and do not replace the base effects of Sacred Aegis.
- A creature may only benefit from one Sacred Aegis at a time.
- Recasting Sacred Aegis refreshes its duration.

### Lore

As a Cleric's faith matures, so too does the strength of the divine protection entrusted to them. Veteran members of every faith are recognised not only by the miracles they perform, but by their ability to stand as guardians of others, placing themselves between danger and those under their patron's care.

Many ancient records tell of Sacred Aegis protecting champions during impossible battles, allowing them to survive blows that should have ended their lives. While historians often argue over the appearance of these blessings, every account agrees on one truth—the protection was never born from the Cleric's power alone, but from the unwavering faith that connected them to their patron.

> *"A shield forged by faith is strengthened by every trial it endures."*
>
> — **High Protector Aleron, Warden of the Sacred Bastion**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your faith has become a steadfast bastion through which your patron's protection flows with unwavering strength. When you invoke Sacred Aegis, divine power surrounds yourself or another creature, forming a resilient ward that shields them from danger while carrying the unmistakable mark of your patron's influence.

Although every Sacred Aegis provides the same fundamental protection, each patron leaves their own divine signature upon the blessing. Some manifest as radiant halos, others as spectral armour, shimmering runes, living branches, celestial wings or countless other sacred forms. Regardless of its appearance, the aegis stands as a visible declaration that the protected creature is under the watchful care of a higher power.

As your connection to your patron deepens, the divine blessing grows more formidable. The protection itself strengthens, while your patron weaves additional miracles into the aegis, allowing it to serve as both a shield against harm and a reflection of their divine domain.

- The target also gains any **Sacred Aegis III** effects granted by your chosen Patron.
- Patron effects are additional benefits and do not replace the base effects of Sacred Aegis.

### Lore

The Third Sacred Aegis marks the point where a Cleric is recognised as a trusted protector within their faith. Veteran adventurers often seek such Clerics before embarking upon dangerous expeditions, knowing that their patron's blessing has turned aside countless fatal blows throughout history.

Ancient scriptures describe Sacred Aegis as more than a defensive miracle. It is a sacred promise between patron and disciple that the faithful will never stand alone while fulfilling their divine purpose. Though the form of the blessing differs from one deity to another, every manifestation carries the same enduring message—that courage is strongest when shielded by unwavering faith.

> *"The divine does not promise a life without hardship. It promises that the faithful shall never face it alone."*
>
> — **Archbishop Seraphis, Guardian of the Eternal Bastion**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your patron's protection has become a formidable divine safeguard, allowing you to envelop yourself or another creature within an aegis capable of withstanding even the fiercest assaults. Through unwavering faith, you have become a trusted conduit for your patron's protective power, calling upon a blessing that few Clerics are capable of wielding.

Every Sacred Aegis shares the same sacred foundation, yet each patron leaves an unmistakable mark upon the blessing. To some it appears as radiant armour forged from holy light, while others witness celestial feathers, shimmering runes, spectral guardians, sacred vines or manifestations known only to followers of that deity. Though their appearances differ, each aegis serves the same purpose—to shield the faithful while reinforcing them with the unique blessings of their patron.

As your devotion continues to deepen, the protective barrier grows ever stronger, allowing your patron to weave increasingly powerful divine gifts into the blessing. Those protected by your Sacred Aegis often find themselves standing firm against overwhelming adversity, sustained by both the universal protection of the aegis and the additional favour granted by their patron.

### Lore

Among the great temples of Asteria, those capable of invoking the Fourth Sacred Aegis are often appointed as guardians of holy relics, protectors of sacred sites and defenders of those unable to defend themselves. Their patron's blessing has become so refined that even seasoned warriors feel a renewed sense of confidence beneath its protection.

Many ancient chronicles tell of battles where a single Cleric stood at the heart of a desperate defence, their Sacred Aegis preserving allies against impossible odds until victory could be won. Though each patron manifested their protection differently, every account reached the same conclusion: divine protection is not measured by the brilliance of the shield, but by the lives preserved beneath it.

> *"The strongest fortress is not built of stone, but of faith shared between the divine and the faithful."*
>
> — **Grand Protector Althorin, Warden of the Sacred Citadel**

## Rank 5
### Description

You have mastered the sacred art of divine protection, allowing your patron's aegis to reach its fullest expression. With a single invocation, you may surround yourself or another creature in an exceptionally powerful ward, shielding them from harm while carrying the unmistakable blessing of the deity you serve. Few Clerics ever attain this level of divine favour, and those who do are recognised as living guardians of their faith.

Every Sacred Aegis shares the same unbreakable foundation of divine protection, yet every patron shapes the blessing according to their own sacred nature. One deity may cloak their faithful in radiant light, another in celestial armour, living branches, spectral wings or ancient runic sigils. Though the appearance differs, the purpose remains constant—to preserve those chosen by the divine while empowering them with blessings unique to their patron.

At its highest rank, Sacred Aegis stands as both a shield and a symbol. It represents the unwavering covenant between patron and disciple, protecting those entrusted to your care while reminding all who witness it that true strength is found through faith, devotion and the enduring favour of the divine.

- The target also gains any **Sacred Aegis V** effects granted by your chosen Patron.
- Patron effects are additional benefits and do not replace the base effects of Sacred Aegis.
- A creature may only benefit from one Sacred Aegis at a time.
- Recasting Sacred Aegis refreshes its duration.
- You have mastered **Sacred Aegis**.

### Lore

Throughout the history of Asteria, only the greatest champions of the faith have wielded the Fifth Sacred Aegis. Their names are remembered not because they stood above others, but because they stood before them. They became the first line of defence against darkness, placing themselves between danger and those unable to protect themselves, trusting completely in the blessing granted by their patron.

The oldest scriptures teach that the greatest shield is not one that makes its bearer invincible, but one that allows others to endure long enough to fulfil their purpose. Every manifestation of Sacred Aegis reflects this truth. Whether protecting a lone traveller, an entire adventuring party or the defenders of a kingdom, the blessing exists to preserve hope when all other defences have failed.

Those who master Sacred Aegis become living symbols of their patron's protection. Wherever they walk, the faithful take comfort knowing that even amidst overwhelming darkness, the divine continues to stand beside those who refuse to yield.

> *"The measure of a guardian is not how many blows they withstand, but how many lives remain standing because they chose to protect them."*
>
> — **Saint Valen, First Shield of the Divine**
