# Ritual Blessing

## Overview
Not all divine power is called upon in battle.

## Rank 1
### ✨ Description

Not all divine power is called upon in battle.

Some must be prepared… drawn slowly through prayer, symbol, and intent.

*Ritual Blessing* allows you to channel divine energy outside of combat, weaving temporary enchantments, protections, and minor blessings that prepare your allies for what lies ahead.

At Rank 1, your rituals are simple—but meaningful.

---

### ⚔️ Effects

| Type | Description |
|------|------------|
| Type | Active (Ritual / Preparation) |
| Core Effect | Apply a minor blessing to allies |
| Secondary Effect | Create a ritual space |
| Role | Pre-combat utility |

---

### 🎯 Ritual Mechanics

---

#### 🔮 Ritual Casting (Core System)

- Requires:
  - **10 seconds (non-combat use only)**  

- Creates:
  - A **Ritual Space (5m radius)**

- Allies within the space may receive:
  - **1 Blessing effect**

---

#### 🌟 Minor Blessing (Choose 1)

Apply to all allies within the ritual:

---

##### 🟡 Blessing of Vitality
- Gain:
  - **+5 Temporary HP**
- Duration:
  - **10 minutes**

---

##### 🔵 Blessing of Focus
- Gain:
  - **+1 to next skill check or saving roll**
- Duration:
  - Until triggered

---

##### 🟣 Blessing of Resistance
- Gain:
  - **+5% resistance to one damage type (chosen at cast)**
- Duration:
  - **10 minutes**

---

👉 Only **one blessing may be active at a time per target**

---

### ⚡ Channel Divinity Interaction

When consuming a **Divine Charge** during the ritual:

- Enhance the blessing:

- Effects:
  - **+50% duration**
  - OR
  - **Increase effect strength slightly (GM scaling)**

---

### ⚔️ Combat Interaction

- Cannot be cast in combat  
- Effects **persist into combat**  

- Encourages:
  - Preparation  
  - Strategic planning  
  - Team coordination  

---

### ✨ Status Effects

| Status | Description |
|--------|------------|
| Ritual Blessed | Target has an active blessing |
| Sacred Preparation | Buff applied before combat |

---

### ⚠️ Limitations

| Type | Description |
|------|------------|
| Non-Combat Only | Cannot be used mid-fight |
| Single Blessing | Cannot stack multiple rituals |
| Modest Effects | Bonuses are intentionally small |
| Time Requirement | Requires setup time |

---

### ⚠️ Ritual Interruption

If interrupted during casting:

- Ritual fails  
- Mana is still consumed  

---

### 🌍 Environmental Interactions

| Environment | Effect |
|------------|--------|
| Consecrated Ground | Ritual feels stronger (flavour) |
| Divine Shrines | May enhance ritual (GM discretion) |
| Corrupted Zones | Ritual feels unstable |

---

### 🔗 Synergy Hooks (Future Scaling)

- Future ranks may:
  - Add multiple blessing choices  
  - Allow stacking or layering  
  - Extend duration significantly  
  - Add combat-triggered effects  

- [[Channel Divinity]] → Enhances ritual strength  
- [[Blessing of Light]] → Sustained combat synergy

There are powers that answer in an instant.

And there are powers that must be invited.

Through ritual, symbol, and quiet devotion, you create a space where the divine may linger—not as force, but as presence.

And those who stand within it…

Carry that presence with them.
