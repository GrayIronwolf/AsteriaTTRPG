# Sacred Judgement

## Overview
There exists no greater authority within the mortal realm than the judgement of a god. Through your unwavering conviction and absolute devotion, your patron grants you the sacred privilege of becoming the vessel through which their divine verdict is delivered. Unlike every miracle before it, Sacred Judgement is not an act of faith—it is the direct expression of your patron's will.

When invoked, your patron looks upon the chosen target and weighs them according to their own divine ideals. Every deity judges differently. Some offer redemption, others deliver destruction. Some restore life, while others demand sacrifice. Mercy, wrath, destiny, chaos, balance, and countless other ideals are all reflected in the judgement bestowed.

No mortal can predict the outcome with certainty, for Sacred Judgement is never governed by the desires of the champion alone. It is the decision of the patron, delivered through their chosen Avatar.

## Rank 1
### Description

There exists no greater authority within the mortal realm than the judgement of a god. Through your unwavering conviction and absolute devotion, your patron grants you the sacred privilege of becoming the vessel through which their divine verdict is delivered. Unlike every miracle before it, Sacred Judgement is not an act of faith—it is the direct expression of your patron's will.

When invoked, your patron looks upon the chosen target and weighs them according to their own divine ideals. Every deity judges differently. Some offer redemption, others deliver destruction. Some restore life, while others demand sacrifice. Mercy, wrath, destiny, chaos, balance, and countless other ideals are all reflected in the judgement bestowed.

No mortal can predict the outcome with certainty, for Sacred Judgement is never governed by the desires of the champion alone. It is the decision of the patron, delivered through their chosen Avatar.

### Effects

- Once per **Campaign Session**, you may invoke **Sacred Judgement**.
- Choose a creature, object, location, or event within **30 metres**.
- Your patron immediately passes divine judgement.
- The exact effect is determined by:
  - Your patron's Divine Authority.
  - Divine Resonance.
  - Divine Opposition.
  - The nature of the target.
  - The current circumstances.
- Sacred Judgement may bless, curse, heal, punish, empower, weaken, cleanse, transform, reveal, banish, or otherwise affect the chosen target in a manner appropriate to your patron.
- The final outcome is determined by the GM and should always reflect the ideals, personality, and divine domain of your patron.
- Sacred Judgement cannot force a result that would fundamentally undermine the campaign or contradict your patron's nature.
- The manifestation of Sacred Judgement is unique to every patron.

### Lore

Throughout history, Sacred Judgement has been witnessed only a handful of times. Entire civilizations have risen or fallen beneath its divine authority, yet no two judgements have ever unfolded in the same way. Every deity views creation through a different lens, and so every verdict reflects the ideals they embody.

The oldest scriptures teach that Sacred Judgement is not an expression of power, but of absolute truth. The champion does not command the miracle—they merely stand aside while their patron's will is made manifest upon the mortal world. Those who survive such moments often find their lives forever changed, whether by blessing, punishment, or revelation.

Among the faithful, it is believed that receiving Sacred Judgement is the closest a mortal may ever come to standing before the gods themselves.

> *"When judgement falls, even kings and monsters stand equal before the eyes of the divine."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your patron now entrusts you with a greater measure of their divine authority, allowing their judgement to reach further and carry greater consequence. You no longer merely invoke a miracle—you become the voice through which your deity pronounces their verdict upon the mortal world. Every judgement reflects not your own desires, but the ideals, purpose, and wisdom of the patron you serve.

Those who witness Sacred Judgement often speak of an overwhelming silence that falls before the miracle unfolds, as though creation itself pauses to hear the will of the divine. Whether the judgement arrives as mercy, punishment, redemption, or revelation depends entirely upon the patron whose authority you carry.

No two judgements are ever identical. Even when faced with similar circumstances, a patron may respond differently according to the sincerity of those involved, the significance of the moment, and the greater purpose they seek to achieve.

### Effects

- Once per **Campaign Session**, you may invoke **Sacred Judgement**.
- Choose a creature, object, location, or event within **40 metres**.
- Your patron immediately passes divine judgement.
- The judgement may affect **multiple closely related targets** when appropriate to the miracle and approved by the GM.
- The exact effect is determined by:
  - Your patron's Divine Authority.
  - Divine Resonance.
  - Divine Opposition.
  - The nature of the target.
  - The current circumstances.
  - The significance and purpose of the judgement.
- Sacred Judgement may bless, curse, heal, punish, empower, weaken, cleanse, transform, reveal, banish, or otherwise affect the chosen target(s) in a manner appropriate to your patron.
- The final outcome is determined by the GM and should always reflect the ideals, personality, and divine domain of your patron.
- Sacred Judgement cannot produce effects that contradict your patron's nature or fundamentally undermine the campaign.
- The appearance and manifestation of Sacred Judgement are unique to your chosen patron.

### Lore

As your covenant deepens, your patron grants you greater authority to act as their earthly representative. Few mortals are ever entrusted with such responsibility, for divine judgement is never given lightly. Every verdict echoes beyond the moment it is delivered, often shaping the destiny of kingdoms, faiths, and generations yet to come.

The High Orders teach that Sacred Judgement is neither vengeance nor mercy alone. It is the perfect expression of a patron's ideals, delivered without fear, prejudice, or hesitation. Those chosen to bear such authority are remembered not for the miracles they performed, but for the wisdom with which they accepted the burden of speaking with a god's voice.

> *"To speak with the voice of the divine is not a privilege—it is a burden few souls are worthy to carry."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**

## Rank 3
### Description

The bond between you and your patron has reached such depth that your voice may now carry a fragment of true divine authority. When you invoke Sacred Judgement, the heavens do not merely observe—they answer. Your patron weighs the hearts, deeds, intentions, and destiny of those before them, delivering a verdict according to their eternal ideals.

To stand before Sacred Judgement is to stand before absolute truth as defined by your patron. Some receive redemption, others condemnation. Some are granted impossible hope, while others witness the consequences of defying the divine. No two judgements are ever identical, for every verdict is shaped by the wisdom of the deity who delivers it.

Those fortunate enough to witness such miracles often spend the remainder of their lives attempting to understand the meaning behind what they have seen.

### Effects

- Once per **Campaign Session**, you may invoke **Sacred Judgement**.
- Choose one or more creatures, objects, locations, or events within **50 metres**, subject to GM approval.
- Your patron delivers a **Greater Sacred Judgement**.
- The judgement may affect individuals, groups, sacred sites, powerful artefacts, or significant events when appropriate.
- The miracle is determined by:
  - Your patron's **Divine Authority**.
  - **Divine Resonance**.
  - **Divine Opposition**.
  - The significance of the target.
  - The current circumstances.
  - The sincerity and purpose of the judgement.
- Greater Sacred Judgements may bless, curse, heal, condemn, empower, weaken, cleanse, transform, reveal hidden truths, banish powerful beings, alter fate, or otherwise reshape events in accordance with your patron's divine portfolio.
- The final outcome is determined by the GM and should always reflect the personality, ideals, and domain of your patron.
- Sacred Judgement can never produce an outcome that contradicts your patron's nature or bypasses the importance of player agency and the ongoing narrative.
- The appearance, manifestation, and consequences of every Sacred Judgement are unique to your chosen patron.

### Lore

Very few mortals have ever spoken with such divine authority. The oldest scriptures describe these champions as living heralds whose words briefly became indistinguishable from the will of the gods themselves. Entire nations have altered their course after witnessing a Greater Sacred Judgement, believing they had seen undeniable proof that the divine still walked among mortals.

The High Orders teach that the burden of Sacred Judgement grows heavier with every rank. To wield such authority is not to command a god, but to accept the immense responsibility of becoming the instrument through which their will is expressed. Every judgement shapes not only the world around it, but also the soul of the champion chosen to bear it.

> *"Power changes kingdoms. Judgement changes history."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your patron now entrusts you with an authority possessed by only the greatest champions in recorded history. Through your unwavering conviction, the boundary between mortal and divine grows perilously thin, allowing your patron's judgement to echo across battlefields, kingdoms, and sacred places alike. When you invoke Sacred Judgement, it is no longer viewed as a miracle—it is recognised as a divine decree that few beings possess the power to challenge.

The judgement delivered is never arbitrary. Every verdict reflects the ideals, wisdom, and eternal purpose of your patron. Some deities restore what has been broken, while others condemn corruption wherever it festers. Some reward sacrifice, while others demand justice. Whatever form it takes, every Sacred Judgement reminds the world that the gods remain active participants in the fate of creation.

Those who witness such miracles often devote their lives to recording every detail, believing future generations must never forget the day a god's will touched the mortal realm.

### Effects

- Once per **Campaign Session**, you may invoke **Sacred Judgement**.
- Choose one or more creatures, objects, sacred sites, locations, or significant events within **100 metres**, subject to GM approval.
- Your patron delivers a **Supreme Sacred Judgement**.
- The judgement may influence battles, armies, cities, sacred sites, legendary artefacts, or other major events appropriate to the campaign.
- The exact miracle is determined by:
  - Your patron's **Divine Authority**.
  - **Divine Resonance**.
  - **Divine Opposition**.
  - The nature and significance of the target.
  - Your standing with your patron.
  - The current circumstances.
- Supreme Sacred Judgements may bless, condemn, heal, empower, weaken, purify, corrupt, banish, transform, reveal, restore, or otherwise reshape reality in a manner consistent with your patron's divine portfolio.
- The GM has final authority over the judgement and should ensure every miracle reflects the ideals, personality, and limitations of the patron.
- Sacred Judgement can never contradict your patron's nature or remove meaningful player choice from the campaign.
- Every manifestation of Sacred Judgement is unique to the patron invoked.

### Lore

Very few Sacred Judgements of this magnitude have ever been witnessed. The oldest chronicles speak of wars ending in a single divine decree, cursed kingdoms restored through impossible mercy, and ancient evils cast from the world by champions whose faith never wavered. Such moments became defining events in history, remembered long after empires had risen and fallen.

The High Orders teach that a Supreme Sacred Judgement is never a display of power alone. It is the greatest expression of trust a patron can place in a mortal, allowing them to become the voice through which eternal ideals are briefly spoken into the world. Every champion who reaches this stage understands that the burden of divine authority is far greater than the power it grants.

> *"The greatest judgement is not the one that ends a war, but the one that teaches the world why it began."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**

## Rank 5
### Description

There exists no greater authority that a mortal may wield than the Sacred Judgement of their patron. Through absolute conviction, unwavering devotion, and a covenant forged through countless sacrifices, you have become the chosen vessel through which your patron may deliver their ultimate verdict upon the mortal realm.

When Sacred Judgement is invoked, your patron alone decides the outcome. Mortals cannot bargain, reason, or resist through will alone. Every judgement reflects the ideals, wisdom, mercy, wrath, justice, or purpose of the deity who delivers it. Some are granted redemption, others face condemnation. Entire kingdoms have been saved through a single judgement, while others have watched centuries of corruption brought to an end in a single divine decree.

Those who witness a Rank V Sacred Judgement rarely describe the miracle itself. Instead, they speak of an overwhelming certainty that, for one fleeting moment, they stood before the unquestionable authority of the divine.

### Effects

- Once per **Campaign Session**, you may invoke **Sacred Judgement**.
- Choose one or more creatures, objects, sacred relics, locations, armies, kingdoms, or significant events, subject to GM approval.
- Your patron delivers their **Ultimate Sacred Judgement**.
- The judgement may influence any appropriate target whose scale is reasonable within the current campaign.
- The judgement is determined by:
  - Your patron's **Divine Authority**.
  - Your patron's **Divine Resonance**.
  - Your patron's **Divine Opposition**.
  - The significance of the target.
  - The sincerity of your conviction.
  - Your standing with your patron.
  - The current state of the world and campaign.
- Ultimate Sacred Judgements may bless, condemn, heal, purge, restore, transform, banish, reveal, protect, destroy, alter destiny, or otherwise perform miracles consistent with your patron's divine portfolio.
- The GM has final authority over every judgement and should ensure that each miracle reflects the patron's personality, ideals, limitations, and role within the world.
- Sacred Judgement can never contradict your patron's nature, bypass meaningful player agency, or permanently undermine the integrity of the campaign.
- Every manifestation of Sacred Judgement is entirely unique to the patron invoked.

### Lore

The scriptures speak of only a handful of champions throughout history who have ever borne the authority to invoke the Ultimate Sacred Judgement. Their names became inseparable from the gods they served, remembered not for the power they possessed, but for the unwavering conviction that earned such unimaginable trust.

The High Orders teach that Sacred Judgement is not a weapon, nor a spell, nor even a miracle. It is the moment a deity chooses to speak directly into the mortal world through the soul of their most faithful servant. Whether that voice brings hope, justice, destruction, mercy, or renewal depends entirely upon the patron who answers the call.

Those who survive such moments often spend the remainder of their lives recounting the experience, for they know they have witnessed something that few mortals ever shall—the unmistakable will of a god made manifest.

> *"Power belongs to champions. Judgement belongs to the gods. We are merely the voices they choose when the world must listen."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**
