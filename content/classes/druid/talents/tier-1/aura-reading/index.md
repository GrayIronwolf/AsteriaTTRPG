# Aura Reading

## Overview
Every magical element leaves behind a unique essence that permeates the world around it. Through meditation and years of study, Druids learn to open their senses beyond ordinary sight, allowing them to perceive these elemental auras.

Unlike other forms of magical detection, Aura Reading is limited by the Druid's own understanding. You can only perceive the elemental essences of magical elements you have personally mastered. As your knowledge of magic grows, so too does your ability to interpret the unseen world.

Because Earth is the foundation of every Druid's training, its essence speaks more clearly than any other. The land willingly reveals details that remain hidden within other elemental auras.

---

## Rank 1
### Description

Every magical element leaves behind a unique essence that permeates the world around it. Through meditation and years of study, Druids learn to open their senses beyond ordinary sight, allowing them to perceive these elemental auras.

Unlike other forms of magical detection, Aura Reading is limited by the Druid's own understanding. You can only perceive the elemental essences of magical elements you have personally mastered. As your knowledge of magic grows, so too does your ability to interpret the unseen world.

Because Earth is the foundation of every Druid's training, its essence speaks more clearly than any other. The land willingly reveals details that remain hidden within other elemental auras.

---

### Effects

- Activate **Aura Reading** by spending **10 MP**.
- While active, Aura Reading costs **5 MP every 5 seconds** to maintain.
- Aura Reading lasts for up to **5 minutes**, or until manually dismissed.
- Detect the **elemental essence** of all magical elements you currently know within **10 metres**.
- Each known magical element appears as a distinct aura, allowing you to identify its elemental affinity.
- **Earth Essence** reveals whether the aura originates from a **creature**, an **enchanted object**, or an **active Earth spell**.
- Gain **+1 to D20 Perception Checks** when locating creatures, magical objects, or magical effects emitting a known elemental essence.
- Aura Reading requires **line of sight** and cannot perceive through solid objects such as walls, dense stone, metal, or sealed containers.
- At this rank, Aura Reading only reveals the **presence** and **elemental affinity** of an aura. It does not reveal the aura's strength, duration, magical effect, or lingering magical residue.

---

### Lore

The oldest Druids teach that every element sings its own silent song. Stone hums with patience, rivers whisper with change, flames roar with passion, and the wind carries stories beyond the horizon. To those without training, these voices are forever silent. To a Druid, they become as clear as daylight.

Every apprentice begins by learning to recognise the steady heartbeat of Earth. Only after mastering this first elemental essence can they hope to understand the countless other energies that weave together the natural world.

Aura Reading is not about seeing magic—it is about recognising the elemental truths that lie beneath it. Every new magical element a Druid learns adds another voice to the chorus, slowly revealing the hidden tapestry of Asteria.

---

> *"Magic hides from the eyes, but never from the elements that gave it form."*  
> — **Grand Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, First Warden of the Emerald Wilds**

Learning to distinguish these subtle differences is the first step towards truly understanding the language of the elements.

The elder circles teach that the Earth is the oldest witness to every spell ever cast. Though other elements reveal their presence, it is the patient memory of stone and soil that often uncovers truths long after the magic itself has faded.

With practice, a Druid begins to realise that Aura Reading is not merely about seeing magic—it is about listening to the stories each element leaves behind.

The elders teach that understanding the age and strength of an aura is often more valuable than knowing its purpose. A fading essence may speak of a traveller long gone, while a powerful aura lingering in silence may warn of dangers yet to awaken.

Only those who patiently study the language of the elements begin to realise that magic is never truly fleeting—it always leaves a story for those capable of reading it.

### Effects

- Activate **Aura Reading** by spending **25 MP**.
- While active, Aura Reading costs **5 MP every 5 seconds** to maintain.

### Lore

Among the oldest Druidic Circles, reaching this level of Aura Reading is considered the mark of a true elemental scholar. Few possess the patience required to study the subtle differences between magical essences, and fewer still can interpret the countless interactions that occur whenever multiple elements converge.

The Earth is said to remember every spell ever cast upon it. Ancient standing stones, forgotten temples, and sacred groves often bear echoes of powerful rituals that only the most accomplished Druids can perceive. By reading these lingering impressions, entire histories have been uncovered without a single written record.

Those who witness a master of Aura Reading often believe they are simply staring into empty space. In truth, the Druid is observing a vibrant tapestry of elemental essences woven together across the world, each one telling a story to those capable of understanding its silent language.

## Rank 5
### Description

Your understanding of elemental essences has reached its pinnacle. Every magical element you have mastered reveals itself with remarkable clarity, allowing you to read the invisible tapestry of magic woven throughout the world. To you, elemental auras are no longer fleeting glimmers of power—they are expressions of the forces that shape life, nature, and the world itself.

Each magical element tells a different story, and your growing mastery allows you to interpret only those elements you truly understand. A Druid who has devoted themselves to Earth sees the silent memories preserved within stone, while one who has studied Water perceives its endless flow and purity. No two Druids perceive the world in exactly the same way, for Aura Reading reflects the magical knowledge of its wielder.

The Earth remains your greatest teacher. Its essence reveals truths hidden even from the most experienced practitioners of other magical disciplines, making it the cornerstone of every Druid's perception.

---

### Effects

- Activate **Aura Reading** by spending **30 MP**.
- While active, Aura Reading costs **5 MP every 5 seconds** to maintain.
- Aura Reading lasts for up to **30 minutes**, or until manually dismissed.
- Detect the **elemental essence** of all magical elements you currently know within **30 metres**.
- Each known magical element appears as a distinct aura, allowing you to immediately identify its elemental affinity.
- You can determine whether an aura is:
  - **Natural**
  - **Enchanted**
  - **Actively Channelled**
  - **Lingering**
- You can determine an aura's magical strength as:
  - **Weak**
  - **Minor**
  - **Greater**
  - **Strengthened**
- You can recognise when multiple elemental essences are interacting and identify each known element involved.
- **Earth Essence** reveals whether the aura originates from a **creature**, **object**, **spell**, **enchantment**, or **ritual**, and whether the Earth's essence is **stable**, **fractured**, or **corrupted**.
- Gain **+5 to D20 Perception Checks** when locating creatures, magical objects, enchantments, or magical effects emitting a known elemental essence.
- Aura Reading requires **line of sight** and cannot perceive through solid objects such as walls, dense stone, metal, or sealed containers.
- Aura Reading only interprets the elemental essences you have personally learned. Unknown magical elements remain visible as unidentified magical auras.

### Lore

To the Archdruids of old, Aura Reading was never considered a spell—it was a way of perceiving the world as the elements themselves do. Every stone remembers, every river carries echoes, every flame leaves passion in its wake, and every breeze whispers of distant lands. Those who master this talent become living interpreters of these silent voices.

It is said that the greatest Druids could walk through forgotten ruins and recount centuries of magical history simply by observing the elemental essences lingering within the walls. They needed no written records, for the world itself preserved every story in the language of the elements.

Though many practitioners of magic seek greater power, Druids believe true wisdom comes from understanding. Aura Reading stands as the embodiment of that belief, reminding every Druid that magic is not merely cast—it is woven into the very fabric of Asteria.

---
