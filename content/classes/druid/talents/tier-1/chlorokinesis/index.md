# Chlorokinesis

## Overview
You awaken your first true connection to the living plants around you. By weaving Mana into nearby vegetation and directing it through physical effort, you can command roots, vines, branches, shrubs, and grasses to move at your will.

Unlike conjuration magic, Chlorokinesis cannot create plant life from nothing. Instead, it manipulates vegetation that already exists within the environment, making the battlefield itself an extension of your abilities. Even the smallest patch of roots or overgrown vines can become a useful tool in the hands of a skilled Druid.

Though your control is still limited, you quickly learn that nature rarely needs overwhelming force. A single root can halt a charging enemy, a bent branch can shelter an ally, and a moving vine can uncover what was once hidden.

## Rank 1
### Description

You awaken your first true connection to the living plants around you. By weaving Mana into nearby vegetation and directing it through physical effort, you can command roots, vines, branches, shrubs, and grasses to move at your will.

Unlike conjuration magic, Chlorokinesis cannot create plant life from nothing. Instead, it manipulates vegetation that already exists within the environment, making the battlefield itself an extension of your abilities. Even the smallest patch of roots or overgrown vines can become a useful tool in the hands of a skilled Druid.

Though your control is still limited, you quickly learn that nature rarely needs overwhelming force. A single root can halt a charging enemy, a bent branch can shelter an ally, and a moving vine can uncover what was once hidden.

---

### Effects

When casting **Chlorokinesis**, choose **one** of the following techniques:

#### 🌱 Root Snare

Command nearby roots or vines to hinder a creature's movement.

- Target one creature within range.
- The target must make a **D20 Agility Saving Throw** against your **Spell Save DC**.
- **Failure:** The target's movement speed is reduced by **50%** until the end of its next turn.
- **Success:** The target's movement speed is reduced by **25%** until the end of its next turn.

#### 🌿 Living Cover

Manipulate nearby branches, shrubs, or vines to provide protection.

Choose one:

- Grant one creature **Half Cover** until the start of your next turn.
- Create a **2-metre** patch of **Difficult Terrain** until the start of your next turn.

#### 🍃 Verdant Utility

Use nearby vegetation to assist outside of combat.

Examples include:

- Moving vines into climbing ropes.
- Clearing overgrown pathways.
- Revealing hidden tracks or objects.
- Pulling a light object within range.
- Moving foliage to improve visibility.

- Gain **+10** to one **Nature**, **Survival**, or **Tracking** **D100 Skill Check** related to the action.

---

### Lore

Among the Druidic Circles, Chlorokinesis is considered the first lesson in working alongside nature rather than commanding it. Young Druids are taught that every plant possesses its own purpose, and their role is simply to guide that purpose instead of forcing it.

Many apprentices spend months practising with a single vine before they are trusted to manipulate larger plants. The exercise is less about power and more about patience, teaching that even the smallest movement can change the outcome of a journey or battle.

To an outsider, the forest may appear still. To a Druid, every leaf, root, and branch is waiting for a gentle request.

---

> *"Do not ask how strong a tree is. Ask how willing it is to help."*  
> — **Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, Warden of the Emerald Wilds**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your bond with the natural world deepens, allowing your influence over plants to become stronger, faster, and more deliberate. The vegetation around you no longer reacts with hesitation—it answers your call with growing confidence.

Roots spread farther beneath the earth, vines tighten with greater strength, and branches bend more naturally to your commands. What once required careful concentration now feels instinctive, allowing you to adapt the battlefield in moments.

Experienced Druids understand that victory is not always won through overwhelming force. Restricting a foe's movement, shielding an ally from danger, or opening a safe path through dense wilderness can prove just as decisive as any spell. Chlorokinesis teaches that nature rewards those who think ahead, shaping the environment to favour patience, preparation, and harmony.

---

### Effects

When casting **Chlorokinesis**, choose **one** of the following techniques:

#### 🌱 Root Snare

Command roots and vines to bind a creature's legs.

- Target one creature within range.
- The target must make a **D20 Agility Saving Throw** against your **Spell Save DC**.
- **Failure:** The target becomes **Snared** until the end of its next turn.
  - A Snared creature's movement becomes **0 metres**.
- **Success:** The target's movement speed is reduced by **50%** until the end of its next turn.

#### 🌿 Living Cover

Command nearby vegetation to grow into a stronger defensive position.

Choose one:

- Grant one creature **Half Cover** until the start of your next turn.
- Create a **3-metre** area of **Difficult Terrain** that lasts for **2 rounds**.
- Block a narrow passage with dense vines or branches, requiring a creature to spend **2 metres of movement for every metre travelled** through the obstacle.

#### 🍃 Verdant Utility

Manipulate surrounding plant life to assist exploration and survival.

Examples include:

- Growing stronger vine ladders.
- Retrieving objects from difficult locations.
- Revealing concealed paths.
- Stabilising loose terrain.
- Clearing dense undergrowth.
- Pulling or pushing unattended objects using vines.

- Gain **+15** to one **Nature**, **Survival**, or **Tracking** **D100 Skill Check** related to the chosen action.

---

### Lore

As Druids continue to refine Chlorokinesis, they discover that plants possess remarkable resilience and adaptability. Every root searches endlessly for water, every vine seeks something to climb, and every branch instinctively reaches toward the light.

Rather than forcing these instincts, experienced practitioners simply encourage them. This philosophy lies at the heart of Druidic teaching: nature is strongest when allowed to follow its own course.

Those who master this lesson begin to view every forest, meadow, and overgrown ruin not as scenery, but as a living companion ready to answer their call.

---

> *"Power is not making the forest obey. Power is asking so gently that it chooses to."*  
> — **Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, Warden of the Emerald Wilds**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your mastery of Chlorokinesis allows you to command multiple plants with confidence, transforming the natural world into a powerful extension of your will. What once required careful focus now flows instinctively, as roots burst from the earth, vines lash outward, and branches bend to answer your call.

The battlefield itself begins to change around you. Dense undergrowth becomes a hindrance to your enemies, towering trees become living shields, and tangled vines become relentless restraints. Every forest, meadow, and overgrown ruin offers new opportunities to shape the course of battle.

Veteran Druids know that nature does not seek to destroy—it seeks to endure. By redirecting that enduring strength, they can halt advancing foes, protect companions, and manipulate the terrain without disturbing the balance they have sworn to preserve.

---

### Effects

When casting **Chlorokinesis**, choose **one** of the following techniques:

#### 🌱 Entangling Roots

Command a network of roots and vines to erupt beneath your enemies.

- Choose a **3-metre radius** within range.
- All creatures in the area must make a **D20 Agility Saving Throw** against your **Spell Save DC**.
- **Failure:** The creature becomes **Restrained** until the end of its next turn.
- **Success:** The creature's movement speed is reduced by **50%** until the end of its next turn.
- The affected area becomes **Difficult Terrain** for **3 rounds**.

#### 🌿 Living Barrier

Weave branches, vines, and shrubs into a durable natural wall.

Choose one:

- Create a **4-metre-wide**, **2-metre-high** living barrier that grants **Three-Quarters Cover**.
- Create a **4-metre** area of **Difficult Terrain** that lasts for **3 rounds**.
- Seal a doorway, pathway, or narrow passage with dense vegetation. The obstruction can be cleared with a successful **Strength Check** against your **Spell Save DC**.

#### 🍃 Verdant Utility

Manipulate large amounts of vegetation to aid exploration or survival.

Examples include:

- Creating stable vine bridges.
- Revealing concealed trails or entrances.
- Moving fallen trees or heavy branches.
- Constructing temporary platforms.
- Clearing dense vegetation for safe travel.
- Retrieving distant objects using living vines.

- Gain **+20** to one **Nature**, **Survival**, or **Tracking** **D100 Skill Check** related to the chosen action.

---

### Lore

By the third stage of Chlorokinesis, a Druid no longer sees individual plants—they see an interconnected living system. Every root communicates through the earth, every vine searches for support, and every tree contributes to the harmony of the forest.

Experienced Druids become conductors of this silent symphony, encouraging countless plants to work together toward a single purpose. Their power does not come from domination, but from understanding how every part of nature supports another.

It is said that when an Archdruid enters an ancient forest, the woodland recognises them long before they speak. Leaves rustle without wind, branches bow gently aside, and roots shift beneath their feet—not from magic alone, but from mutual respect between guardian and grove.

---

> *"One vine may catch a traveller. A thousand vines may change the course of a battle."*  
> — **Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, Warden of the Emerald Wilds**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your communion with the natural world has reached a level where entire groves answer your call without hesitation. Roots surge through the earth with tremendous force, towering hedges rise within moments, and ancient branches twist into formidable barriers. Every living plant within your reach becomes a willing extension of your intent.

At this stage, Chlorokinesis is no longer simply about controlling individual plants—it is about shaping the battlefield itself. Dense vegetation slows advancing enemies, towering walls protect your allies, and the wilderness bends to create paths where none existed before.

Master Druids understand that nature is neither cruel nor merciful. It simply adapts. By embracing this lesson, you learn to guide the forest's endless resilience, turning even the smallest patch of greenery into a decisive advantage.

---

### Effects

When casting **Chlorokinesis**, choose **one** of the following techniques:

#### 🌱 Verdant Grasp

Command an immense network of roots and vines to erupt across the battlefield.

- Choose a **5-metre radius** within range.
- All creatures in the area must make a **D20 Agility Saving Throw** against your **Spell Save DC**.
- **Failure:** The creature becomes **Restrained** for **2 rounds**.
- **Success:** The creature's movement speed is reduced by **50%** for **2 rounds**.
- The affected area becomes **Difficult Terrain** for **4 rounds**.

#### 🌿 Living Bastion

Shape surrounding vegetation into a powerful defensive structure.

Choose one:

- Create a living wall up to **6 metres wide** and **3 metres high** that grants **Full Cover**.
- Create a **6-metre** area of **Difficult Terrain** for **4 rounds**.
- Seal a doorway, bridge, tunnel, or narrow passage with dense vegetation. Creatures must succeed on a **Strength Check** against your **Spell Save DC** to force their way through.

#### 🍃 Verdant Dominion

Command large sections of natural vegetation to assist exploration and survival.

Examples include:

- Creating broad vine bridges capable of supporting a party.
- Opening safe routes through dense forests.
- Stabilising collapsing embankments or hillsides.
- Raising natural platforms for climbing.
- Revealing hidden ruins or forgotten pathways.
- Manipulating large trees, shrubs, and vines to reshape the surrounding environment.

- Gain **+25** to one **Nature**, **Survival**, or **Tracking** **D100 Skill Check** related to the chosen action.

### Lore

Among the oldest Druidic Circles, this level of mastery is known as **The Forest's Embrace**. It marks the point where a Druid ceases to feel like an individual standing within nature and instead becomes part of its living rhythm.

Veteran Druids often describe hearing the quiet whispers of roots beneath the soil and sensing the slow heartbeat of ancient trees. To them, every forest is a community of countless lives working together, each willing to lend its strength when called upon with respect.

Few sights inspire greater caution than a master of Chlorokinesis standing calmly within an ancient woodland. For while the Druid may appear alone, the forest itself has already risen to stand beside them.

---

> *"The forest does not march to war. It simply reminds trespassers that they were never alone."*  
> — **Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, Warden of the Emerald Wilds**

## Rank 5
### Description

You have achieved true mastery over Chlorokinesis, becoming a living conduit through which the natural world expresses its will. The distinction between your command and the forest's response has all but disappeared. With little more than a thought, roots erupt from the earth, towering hedgerows rise into defensive fortifications, and ancient trees bend their branches to shield those under your protection.

Where novice Druids manipulate individual plants, you orchestrate entire ecosystems. Every vine, shrub, flower, and towering tree within your reach contributes to a unified purpose. The battlefield itself becomes alive, restricting your enemies while providing sanctuary for your allies.

To those who witness this mastery, it appears as though the forest itself has awakened. Yet experienced Druids know the truth—they are not rulers of nature, but trusted guardians who have earned its unwavering cooperation through years of respect and understanding.

---

### Effects

When casting **Chlorokinesis**, choose **one** of the following techniques:

#### 🌱 Forest's Embrace

Command an immense network of roots, vines, and vegetation to seize the battlefield.

- Choose an **8-metre radius** within range.
- All creatures in the area must make a **D20 Agility Saving Throw** against your **Spell Save DC**.
- **Failure:** The creature becomes **Restrained** for **2 rounds**.
- **Success:** The creature's movement speed is reduced by **50%** for **3 rounds**.
- The affected area becomes **Difficult Terrain** for **5 rounds**.
- A creature restrained by this ability may use its action to repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

#### 🌿 Verdant Fortress

Shape the surrounding vegetation into a formidable natural stronghold.

Choose one:

- Create a living structure up to **8 metres wide** and **4 metres high** that grants **Full Cover**.
- Create an **8-metre** area of **Difficult Terrain** lasting **5 rounds**.
- Seal a large doorway, bridge, canyon pass, or similar opening with dense vegetation. Creatures must succeed on a **Strength Check** against your **Spell Save DC** to force a passage.

#### 🍃 Nature's Dominion

Exercise complete control over nearby vegetation to overcome environmental challenges.

Examples include:

- Constructing large vine bridges capable of supporting groups and mounts.
- Clearing extensive overgrowth to reveal roads or ruins.
- Raising natural platforms and stairways through trees or cliffs.
- Moving fallen trees and dense vegetation to reshape the landscape.
- Concealing campsites or entrances beneath living foliage.
- Guiding large sections of vegetation to create safe travel routes.

- Gain **+30** to one **Nature**, **Survival**, or **Tracking** **D100 Skill Check** related to the chosen action.

---

### Lore

Only a handful of Druids within each generation reach this level of communion with the living world. They are no longer recognised merely as practitioners of Chlorokinesis, but as true Wardens of the Wild—individuals whose presence alone calms forests and stirs ancient groves into quiet life.

Legends tell of these masters halting invading armies without drawing blood, raising forests to block advancing forces, and restoring devastated lands simply by guiding nature back into balance. Such feats are remembered not because they display overwhelming power, but because they embody perfect harmony between mortal and wilderness.

The oldest circles teach that this is not the end of a Druid's journey, but the beginning of a deeper responsibility. Once the forest answers your every call, it also begins to trust you with its future.

---

> *"Mastery is not the moment the forest obeys you. It is the moment the forest believes your voice is its own."*  
> — **Grand Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, First Warden of the Emerald Wilds**
