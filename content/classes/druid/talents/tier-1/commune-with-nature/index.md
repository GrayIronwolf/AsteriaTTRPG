# Commune with Nature

## Overview
You still your thoughts and attune yourself to the rhythm of the natural world, allowing the land to share its knowledge. Every tree, stream, mountain, and blade of grass carries traces of the life that has passed through it, and by opening your spirit you begin to understand these silent echoes.

Commune with Nature does not grant perfect vision or reveal every secret. Instead, it provides instinctive awareness of your surroundings, helping you understand the health of the land, recognise natural features, and sense the presence of nearby wildlife. The stronger and more vibrant the environment, the clearer these impressions become.

To a Druid, nature is not simply a place—it is a living companion with countless stories to tell. This talent marks the first step in learning to listen.

---

## Rank 1
### Description

You still your thoughts and attune yourself to the rhythm of the natural world, allowing the land to share its knowledge. Every tree, stream, mountain, and blade of grass carries traces of the life that has passed through it, and by opening your spirit you begin to understand these silent echoes.

Commune with Nature does not grant perfect vision or reveal every secret. Instead, it provides instinctive awareness of your surroundings, helping you understand the health of the land, recognise natural features, and sense the presence of nearby wildlife. The stronger and more vibrant the environment, the clearer these impressions become.

To a Druid, nature is not simply a place—it is a living companion with countless stories to tell. This talent marks the first step in learning to listen.

---

### Effects

- Spend **10 MP** to enter a state of communion with the surrounding natural environment.
- Commune with Nature lasts for **10 minutes**.
- While active, you become aware of the natural environment within a **50-metre radius**.
- You may determine:
  - Whether the land is **healthy**, **damaged**, or **corrupted**.
  - The location of significant natural features, such as rivers, caves, cliffs, ancient trees, clearings, or dense vegetation.
  - The presence of nearby natural water sources.
- You become aware of the **general presence** of nearby beasts and naturally occurring creatures, but not their exact location, number, or species.
- Gain **+2** to **Nature**, **Survival**, and **Tracking** **D20 Skill Checks** while Commune with Nature is active.
- You can identify whether an area has recently been affected by natural events, including storms, floods, fires, landslides, or earthquakes.
- This talent cannot detect humanoids, constructs, undead, or creatures completely severed from the natural world.
- Areas protected by powerful anti-divination or anti-nature magic may prevent or distort the information received.

---

### Lore

Among the oldest Druidic teachings, it is said that the world is constantly speaking. The rustling of leaves, the movement of rivers, and the shifting of roots beneath the earth form an ancient language that predates kingdoms, dragons, and even the first mortal races.

Most travellers hear only the wind. Druids hear warnings carried through birdsong, the passing of great beasts within the soil, and the quiet sorrow of forests scarred by corruption. These impressions are subtle, but those who learn to trust them rarely lose their way.

Every great Archdruid began with a single lesson: before you can ask nature for its strength, you must first learn to listen.

---

> *"The world has whispered since the dawn of time. Wisdom belongs to those who choose to listen."*  
> — **Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, Warden of the Emerald Wilds**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your connection to the natural world strengthens, allowing the voices of the land to become clearer and more detailed. What once felt like vague instincts now forms a coherent understanding of the environment around you. The forest no longer merely tells you that life is present—it begins to reveal how that life exists within its ecosystem.

As your communion deepens, you become increasingly aware of the relationships between the creatures, plants, and terrain around you. Disturbed habitats, unnatural changes, and damaged ecosystems become immediately apparent, while the flow of nature itself guides your senses toward areas of significance.

Experienced Druids often rely upon this gift before making camp, exploring unknown wilderness, or tracking dangerous beasts. By trusting the wisdom of the land rather than their own eyes alone, they rarely find themselves surprised by the natural world.

---

### Effects

- Spend **15 MP** to enter a state of communion with the surrounding natural environment.
- Commune with Nature lasts for **15 minutes**.
- While active, you become aware of the natural environment within a **75-metre radius**.
- You may determine:
  - Whether the land is **healthy**, **damaged**, **corrupted**, or **recovering**.
  - The location of significant natural features, including rivers, lakes, caves, cliffs, ancient trees, dense forests, and clearings.
  - The presence of nearby natural water sources and whether the water is safe or polluted.
- You become aware of the **approximate size** of nearby groups of natural creatures (solitary, small group, herd, or large gathering), though not their exact location or species.
- Gain **+4** to **Nature**, **Survival**, and **Tracking** **D20 Skill Checks** while Commune with Nature is active.
- You can determine whether an area has been recently disturbed by:
  - Natural disasters.
  - Large beasts.
  - Extensive logging or deforestation.
  - Wildfires.
  - Unnatural environmental damage.
- You can sense the general direction of the strongest concentration of natural life within range.
- This talent cannot detect humanoids, constructs, undead, or creatures completely severed from the natural world.
- Areas protected by powerful anti-divination or anti-nature magic may prevent or distort the information received.

### Lore

To most people, a forest is simply a collection of trees. To a seasoned Druid, it is a living community whose countless voices weave together into a single harmonious chorus. Every creature leaves an imprint upon its surroundings, every river remembers those who drink from it, and every ancient tree silently watches over generations of life.

With experience comes clarity. Druids who reach this level of communion begin to recognise patterns invisible to others—the migration of wildlife, the scars left by ancient disasters, and the subtle imbalance caused by corruption or reckless civilisation.

Among many circles, this stage is known as the **Listening Grove**, where apprentices first realise that nature does not merely answer questions—it freely shares its wisdom with those patient enough to hear it.

---

> *"The land remembers every footprint. Listen carefully, and it will tell you who walked before you."*  
> — **Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, Warden of the Emerald Wilds**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your communion with the natural world has matured into a profound spiritual bond. The land no longer offers only glimpses of its condition—it willingly shares the stories unfolding within it. Every forest, mountain, river, and valley speaks through countless subtle impressions, allowing you to perceive the balance of life with remarkable clarity.

As your understanding grows, you begin to recognise the relationships between every living thing. Predator and prey, healthy groves and dying woodlands, thriving rivers and polluted streams all become part of a greater picture. By reading these natural currents, you can often anticipate danger long before it becomes visible.

Many Druids regard this level of mastery as the point where one truly becomes a guardian of the wild. Rather than merely surviving within nature, you begin to understand its needs, its struggles, and the delicate balance that sustains all life.

---

### Effects

- Spend **20 MP** to enter a state of communion with the surrounding natural environment.
- Commune with Nature lasts for **20 minutes**.
- While active, you become aware of the natural environment within a **100-metre radius**.
- You may determine:
  - Whether the land is **healthy**, **damaged**, **recovering**, **corrupted**, or **unnaturally altered**.
  - The location of significant natural landmarks, including rivers, lakes, caves, cliffs, ancient trees, dense forests, ruins reclaimed by nature, and natural shelters.
  - The condition of nearby water sources, including whether they are clean, polluted, poisoned, or magically tainted.
- You become aware of the **approximate location**, **size**, and **general type** of nearby natural creatures, such as beasts, birds, reptiles, insects, or aquatic life.
- Gain **+6** to **Nature**, **Survival**, and **Tracking** **D20 Skill Checks** while Commune with Nature is active.
- You can determine whether an area has recently been disturbed by:
  - Large predators.
  - Natural disasters.
  - Extensive deforestation.
  - Wildfires.
  - Corruption.
  - Powerful magical events that have affected the natural environment.
- You instinctively sense the direction of the healthiest natural area and the most environmentally damaged area within range.
- This talent cannot detect humanoids, constructs, undead, or creatures completely severed from the natural world.
- Areas protected by powerful anti-divination or anti-nature magic may prevent or distort the information received.

---

### Lore

At this stage, the distinction between the Druid and the wilderness begins to fade. The world no longer feels like a place to be explored, but a living network of countless voices working together to preserve balance.

Ancient circles teach that every stream remembers those who drank from it, every tree recalls the seasons it has endured, and every mountain silently witnesses the passage of ages. A skilled Druid learns to interpret these memories, allowing the land itself to become both guide and teacher.

Those who possess this level of communion are often entrusted with protecting sacred groves, restoring blighted forests, and advising entire communities that live alongside nature. Their greatest strength lies not in commanding the wilderness, but in understanding what it asks of them.

---

> *"When you stop searching for nature's wisdom, you will discover it has been walking beside you all along."*  
> — **Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, Warden of the Emerald Wilds**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your bond with the natural world has become so refined that the land willingly reveals far more than its surface. The forests, rivers, mountains, and plains share not only their current state, but the lingering echoes of what has shaped them. You perceive the invisible connections between life, terrain, and magic as though they were threads woven into a single tapestry.

Nature now warns you of disturbances before they become visible. Corruption stains the land like a spreading sickness, powerful magical events leave lasting scars upon the environment, and unnatural creatures stand apart from the world's natural rhythm. To you, these disruptions feel as obvious as a broken branch beneath your feet.

Only the oldest Druids are entrusted with this level of communion, for they no longer simply observe nature—they serve as its eyes, ears, and protectors.

---

### Effects

- Spend **25 MP** to enter a state of communion with the surrounding natural environment.
- Commune with Nature lasts for **25 minutes**.
- While active, you become aware of the natural environment within a **150-metre radius**.
- You may determine:
  - Whether the land is **healthy**, **recovering**, **damaged**, **corrupted**, **magically altered**, or **unnaturally transformed**.
  - The location of significant natural landmarks, water sources, caves, cliffs, ancient groves, ruins reclaimed by nature, and natural shelters.
  - Whether nearby water sources are **clean**, **polluted**, **poisoned**, **cursed**, or **magically tainted**.
- You become aware of the **approximate location**, **size**, and **general type** of nearby natural creatures and can distinguish between predators and non-predators.
- Gain **+8** to **Nature**, **Survival**, and **Tracking** **D20 Skill Checks** while Commune with Nature is active.
- You can identify areas recently affected by:
  - Powerful magical disturbances.
  - Corruption or blight.
  - Large-scale deforestation.
  - Wildfires, floods, earthquakes, or landslides.
  - The passage of exceptionally powerful natural beasts.
- You instinctively know the direction of the healthiest ecosystem and the greatest environmental imbalance within range.
- You can sense whether the surrounding environment is being influenced by an unnatural magical force, though you do not learn its exact source or identity.
- This talent cannot reveal the precise location of humanoids, constructs, undead, or creatures completely severed from the natural world.
- Areas protected by legendary anti-divination or anti-nature magic may resist or obscure this communion.

---

### Lore

Among the ancient circles, this mastery is known as **The Whispering Wilds**. It is said that a Druid who reaches this stage no longer searches for nature's wisdom—the wilderness seeks them out instead.

The oldest forests remember forgotten kingdoms, rivers recall the countless lives they have sustained, and mountains bear silent witness to the passing of ages. Through deep communion, these memories become fleeting impressions that guide a Druid toward truth without ever revealing every answer.

Many Wardens of the Wild serve as protectors of sacred lands because few others can recognise the first signs of corruption before they spread. To them, every imbalance is a plea for restoration, and every healthy grove is proof that harmony can endure.

---

> *"The wilderness speaks in countless voices. Those who hear only one have yet to truly listen."*  
> — **Grand Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, First Warden of the Emerald Wilds**
