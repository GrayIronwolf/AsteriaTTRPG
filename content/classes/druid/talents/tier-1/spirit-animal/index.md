# Spirit Animal

## Overview
Every Druid walks the wilds in search of harmony, but none walk alone forever. Through ancient rituals and quiet meditation, a Druid eventually encounters a Spirit Animal—a mystical guardian that embodies a single aspect of the natural world. This companion is neither a summoned beast nor a permanent pet, but a spiritual manifestation that willingly answers the Druid's call.

At this rank, your Spirit Animal cannot directly fight alongside you. Instead, it acts as a guide, scout, and conduit between you and the wilderness. It instinctively understands your intentions and communicates through emotions, instincts, and brief spiritual impressions rather than spoken words.

The form of your Spirit Animal is chosen with your Game Master and should reflect your character's personality, beliefs, or connection to nature. A wolf may represent loyalty, an owl wisdom, a fox cunning, or a stag guardianship. Regardless of its form, your companion remains a trusted ally throughout your journey.

## Rank 1
### Description

Every Druid walks the wilds in search of harmony, but none walk alone forever. Through ancient rituals and quiet meditation, a Druid eventually encounters a Spirit Animal—a mystical guardian that embodies a single aspect of the natural world. This companion is neither a summoned beast nor a permanent pet, but a spiritual manifestation that willingly answers the Druid's call.

At this rank, your Spirit Animal cannot directly fight alongside you. Instead, it acts as a guide, scout, and conduit between you and the wilderness. It instinctively understands your intentions and communicates through emotions, instincts, and brief spiritual impressions rather than spoken words.

The form of your Spirit Animal is chosen with your Game Master and should reflect your character's personality, beliefs, or connection to nature. A wolf may represent loyalty, an owl wisdom, a fox cunning, or a stag guardianship. Regardless of its form, your companion remains a trusted ally throughout your journey.

---

### Effects

- Spend **15 MP** to summon your **Spirit Animal**.
- The Spirit Animal remains manifested for **10 minutes**.
- Your Spirit Animal is **ethereal** and cannot attack, deal damage, manipulate physical objects, or be targeted by attacks.
- The Spirit Animal may move up to **30 metres** away from you while remaining connected through your spiritual bond.
- You may see and hear through your Spirit Animal's senses as an action. While doing so, your own body is considered **Distracted** and you have **Disadvantage** on Perception Checks made using your own senses.
- The Spirit Animal can silently communicate simple emotions, warnings, and observations to you.
- Gain **+2** to **Nature**, **Survival**, and **Tracking** **D20 Skill Checks** while your Spirit Animal is active.
- Your Spirit Animal instinctively alerts you if it notices:
  - Nearby natural hazards.
  - Dangerous terrain.
  - Wild beasts.
  - Signs of recent natural disturbances.
- The Spirit Animal cannot pass through magical barriers designed to block spirits or extraplanar beings.
- The Spirit Animal cannot enter combat or provide combat bonuses at this rank.

---

### Lore

The first meeting between a Druid and their Spirit Animal is one of the most sacred moments in Druidic tradition. Some encounter their companion beneath ancient trees, others beside tranquil rivers or beneath starlit skies, but every meeting is deeply personal and impossible to forget.

The Spirit Animal is believed to be a fragment of the world's living spirit, choosing its Druid as much as the Druid chooses it. Though incapable of physical interaction at first, it offers guidance, companionship, and perspective drawn from the untamed wilderness itself.

Among the oldest circles, it is said that a Druid who truly understands their Spirit Animal will never again feel lost, no matter how dark the forest or uncertain the path ahead.

---

> *"A Spirit Animal is not the path before you—it is the friend who reminds you why you chose to walk it."*  
> — **Grand Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, First Warden of the Emerald Wilds**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your spiritual bond with your Spirit Animal grows stronger, allowing it to venture farther from your side while sharing more of its instincts and awareness. The connection between your souls becomes increasingly natural, requiring little effort to maintain as you begin to perceive the wilderness through two sets of senses.

Your companion has become far more than a guide. It actively scouts ahead, warns of danger, and instinctively searches for safe paths through unfamiliar terrain. Whether soaring above a dense forest canopy, slipping silently through undergrowth, or climbing rugged cliffs, it serves as an extension of your own awareness.

Although it still cannot physically interact with the world, your Spirit Animal has learned to better interpret the intentions of the creatures and environments it observes. This deeper understanding allows it to provide more accurate warnings and valuable insight before danger reveals itself.

---

### Effects

- Spend **20 MP** to summon your **Spirit Animal**.
- Your Spirit Animal remains manifested for **15 minutes**.
- Your Spirit Animal remains **ethereal**, unable to attack, manipulate objects, or be targeted by conventional attacks.
- Your Spirit Animal may travel up to **60 metres** away from you while maintaining your spiritual bond.
- As an action, you may perceive the world through your Spirit Animal's senses.
- While using your Spirit Animal's senses, your physical body is considered **Distracted** and has **Disadvantage** on Perception Checks using its own senses.
- Gain **+4** to **Nature**, **Survival**, and **Tracking** **D20 Skill Checks** while your Spirit Animal is active.
- Your Spirit Animal instinctively detects:
  - Natural hazards.
  - Dangerous terrain.
  - Nearby beasts.
  - Fresh tracks.
  - Signs of recent movement through the wilderness.
- Your Spirit Animal can distinguish whether nearby creatures appear **calm**, **alert**, **frightened**, or **agitated** through their natural behaviour.
- You may dismiss or resummon your Spirit Animal at any time while the duration remains active without paying an additional Mana Cost.
- The Spirit Animal cannot pass through magical barriers designed to block spirits or extraplanar beings.
- The Spirit Animal cannot enter combat or provide combat bonuses at this rank.

---

### Lore

As the bond deepens, the Spirit Animal begins to think less as a companion and more as an extension of its Druid. Thoughts, emotions, and instincts flow effortlessly between them, allowing each to understand the other without spoken language.

Many Druids describe this stage as learning to trust another set of eyes. While the Spirit Animal explores the wilderness, its discoveries become your own, revealing hidden dangers, forgotten trails, and the subtle movements of the natural world.

The elders teach that this growing trust is essential. A Spirit Animal does not serve its Druid out of obligation—it remains because the bond between them has become one of mutual respect, shared purpose, and unwavering companionship.

---

> *"When two spirits walk as one, even the quietest forest cannot keep its secrets."*  
> — **Grand Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, First Warden of the Emerald Wilds**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your Spirit Animal has become an inseparable extension of your soul, capable of travelling great distances while maintaining an unwavering spiritual connection. The bond between you is now so refined that its instincts, senses, and emotions flow naturally into your own awareness, allowing you to perceive the wilderness with remarkable clarity.

As your companion explores, it does more than simply observe. It studies the land, watches the behaviour of wildlife, and recognises subtle changes within the environment that would escape even experienced hunters. Through your shared bond, these discoveries become your own, enabling you to make informed decisions long before danger reveals itself.

Among the Druidic Circles, reaching this stage is considered a milestone of trust. Your Spirit Animal no longer follows your commands out of duty—it acts as a true partner, anticipating your needs and carrying out your intentions with remarkable understanding.

---

### Effects

- Spend **25 MP** to summon your **Spirit Animal**.
- Your Spirit Animal remains manifested for **20 minutes**.
- Your Spirit Animal remains **ethereal**, unable to attack, manipulate physical objects, or be targeted by conventional attacks.
- Your Spirit Animal may travel up to **100 metres** away from you while maintaining your spiritual bond.
- As an action, you may perceive the world through your Spirit Animal's senses.
- While using your Spirit Animal's senses, your physical body is considered **Distracted** and has **Disadvantage** on Perception Checks made using its own senses.
- Gain **+6** to **Nature**, **Survival**, and **Tracking** **D20 Skill Checks** while your Spirit Animal is active.
- Your Spirit Animal instinctively detects:
  - Natural hazards.
  - Dangerous terrain.
  - Fresh tracks.
  - Nearby beasts.
  - Signs of recent travel through the wilderness.
  - Changes to the surrounding ecosystem caused by natural or unnatural events.
- Your Spirit Animal can distinguish the general behaviour of nearby creatures, recognising whether they are:
  - Hunting.
  - Resting.
  - Fleeing.
  - Defending territory.
  - Travelling.
- You may freely switch between your own senses and your Spirit Animal's senses without ending the ability.
- You may dismiss and resummon your Spirit Animal anywhere within **20 metres** of you while the duration remains active without paying an additional Mana Cost.
- The Spirit Animal cannot pass through magical barriers designed to block spirits or extraplanar beings.
- The Spirit Animal cannot enter combat or provide combat bonuses at this rank.

---

### Lore

Experienced Druids often describe this stage as sharing a single soul between two bodies. Distance becomes increasingly irrelevant as thoughts and instincts flow effortlessly across the spiritual bond, allowing both companions to work together without hesitation or spoken direction.

Many great Wardens relied upon their Spirit Animals to explore dangerous ruins, watch over sacred groves, and guide lost travellers through untamed wilderness. While the Spirit Animal cannot alter the physical world, its watchful presence has prevented countless disasters by revealing dangers before they could unfold.

The oldest Druidic teachings remind every practitioner that trust cannot be forced. Only through patience, respect, and shared purpose does a Spirit Animal choose to become more than a guide—it becomes family.

---

> *"A companion who shares your spirit never walks behind you or before you. They walk beside you, no matter how great the distance."*  
> — **Grand Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, First Warden of the Emerald Wilds**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your Spirit Animal has become a true extension of your spirit, capable of exploring vast distances while maintaining an unbroken bond with you. The connection has evolved beyond shared sight and hearing; emotions, instincts, and awareness flow freely between you both, allowing each to react almost as though you were a single being.

As your companion traverses the wilderness, it perceives far more than simple movement. It recognises disturbances in ecosystems, senses changes in weather before they arrive, and notices subtle shifts in the behaviour of wildlife that often foreshadow approaching danger. Through your bond, these discoveries become instinctive knowledge rather than conscious observation.

Among the ancient Druidic Circles, reaching this stage marks the beginning of true spiritual partnership. A Spirit Animal no longer waits for commands—it understands your intentions and acts independently to protect, guide, and inform, always remaining faithful to the balance of nature.

---

### Effects

- Spend **30 MP** to summon your **Spirit Animal**.
- Your Spirit Animal remains manifested for **25 minutes**.
- Your Spirit Animal remains **ethereal**, unable to attack, manipulate physical objects, or be targeted by conventional attacks.
- Your Spirit Animal may travel up to **150 metres** away while maintaining your spiritual bond.
- As an action, you may perceive the world through your Spirit Animal's senses.
- While using your Spirit Animal's senses, your physical body is considered **Distracted** and has **Disadvantage** on Perception Checks made using its own senses.
- Gain **+8** to **Nature**, **Survival**, and **Tracking** **D20 Skill Checks** while your Spirit Animal is active.
- Your Spirit Animal instinctively detects:
  - Natural hazards.
  - Dangerous terrain.
  - Fresh tracks.
  - Nearby beasts.
  - Signs of recent travel.
  - Changes caused by corruption, blight, or powerful natural disasters.
- Your Spirit Animal can determine the general behaviour of nearby creatures, including whether they are:
  - Hunting.
  - Resting.
  - Defending territory.
  - Travelling.
  - Fleeing.
  - Stalking prey.
- You may freely switch between your own senses and your Spirit Animal's senses without using an action.
- You may dismiss and resummon your Spirit Animal anywhere within **50 metres** of you while the duration remains active without paying an additional Mana Cost.
- While your Spirit Animal is within **10 metres** of you, you gain **Advantage on D20 Perception Checks** relying on sight, hearing, or smell.
- The Spirit Animal cannot pass through magical barriers specifically designed to block spirits or extraplanar beings.
- The Spirit Animal cannot enter combat or provide offensive or defensive combat abilities at this rank.

---

### Lore

To the greatest Druids, the bond between guardian and Spirit Animal becomes impossible to separate. Each instinctively understands the other's purpose, allowing them to explore, observe, and protect the wilderness with extraordinary efficiency.

Many Wardens entrusted with safeguarding sacred groves rely upon their Spirit Animals to patrol vast forests without ever disturbing the delicate balance of nature. The creatures of the wild rarely fear these spiritual companions, often treating them as though they were simply another part of the forest itself.

The oldest teachings claim that when a Druid reaches this stage, the Spirit Animal no longer follows because it was summoned. It remains because the two spirits have chosen to walk the same path for the rest of their lives.

---

> *"When trust is complete, distance loses all meaning. Two spirits become one purpose."*  
> — **Grand Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, First Warden of the Emerald Wilds**

## Rank 5
### Description

Your bond with your Spirit Animal has reached its ultimate expression, transcending that of summoner and companion. You are now two souls united by a single purpose, capable of sharing thoughts, instincts, and senses with effortless clarity. Distance is little more than an inconvenience, and your companion acts with the same wisdom and intent as if it were your own body.

Your Spirit Animal has become the perfect scout and guide, navigating even the most dangerous wilderness without disturbing the balance of nature. It understands your intentions before they are spoken, adapting to changing circumstances and uncovering paths, dangers, and opportunities that would remain invisible to others.

Among the oldest Druidic Circles, those who achieve this mastery are known as **Wildbound Guardians**. Their Spirit Animals are regarded as sacred partners, entrusted with watching over ancient groves, sacred beasts, and places where the natural world remains untouched. Though incapable of direct combat, their presence has saved countless lives through foresight, wisdom, and unwavering vigilance.

---

### Effects

- Spend **35 MP** to summon your **Spirit Animal**.
- Your Spirit Animal remains manifested for **30 minutes**.
- Your Spirit Animal remains **ethereal**, unable to attack, manipulate physical objects, or be targeted by conventional attacks.
- Your Spirit Animal may travel up to **250 metres** away while maintaining your spiritual bond.
- You may freely switch between your own senses and your Spirit Animal's senses without using an action.
- While perceiving through your Spirit Animal, your body remains aware enough to defend itself, removing the **Distracted** condition from previous ranks.
- Gain **+10** to **Nature**, **Survival**, and **Tracking** **D20 Skill Checks** while your Spirit Animal is active.
- Your Spirit Animal instinctively detects:
  - Natural hazards.
  - Dangerous terrain.
  - Fresh tracks.
  - Hidden paths.
  - Nearby beasts.
  - Environmental corruption or blight.
  - Powerful magical disturbances affecting the natural world.
- Your Spirit Animal can determine the general behaviour of nearby creatures, including whether they are:
  - Hunting.
  - Resting.
  - Travelling.
  - Defending territory.
  - Fleeing.
  - Stalking prey.
  - Injured or distressed.
- You may dismiss and resummon your Spirit Animal anywhere within **100 metres** of you while the duration remains active without paying an additional Mana Cost.
- While your Spirit Animal is within **20 metres** of you, you gain **Advantage on D20 Perception, Survival, and Tracking Skill Checks**.
- Your Spirit Animal may communicate complex emotions, instincts, mental images, and simple concepts through your spiritual bond.
- The Spirit Animal cannot pass through magical barriers specifically designed to block spirits or extraplanar beings.
- The Spirit Animal cannot attack, cast spells, manipulate physical objects, or provide direct offensive or defensive combat abilities.

---

### Lore

Few Druids ever reach this level of spiritual harmony. The ancient circles believe that, at this stage, the Spirit Animal is no longer merely a guide gifted by nature, but a reflection of the Druid's own soul. Together they embody the balance between civilisation and wilderness, instinct and wisdom, mortal and spirit.

Throughout Asteria's history, Wildbound Guardians have served as protectors of sacred forests, watchers of ancient world trees, and keepers of forgotten pathways known only to the natural world. Their Spirit Animals have guided refugees through impassable forests, warned entire villages of approaching disasters, and uncovered corruption long before it could take root.

Despite this extraordinary bond, every Druid is reminded that a Spirit Animal is never a servant. It remains a willing companion, choosing each day to walk beside its guardian. That trust, earned through years of respect and shared purpose, is the true source of the talent's power.

---

> *"Strength may win a battle, but trust is what carries two souls across a lifetime. Walk beside your spirit, and neither of you will ever walk alone."*  
> — **Grand Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, First Warden of the Emerald Wilds**
