# Wild Shape

## Overview
Wild Shape is one of the oldest and most sacred abilities practiced by the Druids of Asteria. Rather than disguising themselves as animals through illusion, Druids temporarily reshape their bodies, minds, and instincts to become one with the creatures they have come to understand.

Learning a new form requires patience, observation, and respect. Before a Druid may transform into a creature, they must first study it in its natural habitat and successfully understand its behaviour, movement, and place within the ecosystem. Only then can its spirit be welcomed into their own.

At this rank, your understanding is limited to simple creatures. Your transformations are best suited for exploration, travel, survival, and escaping danger rather than direct combat. Every new form learned expands your connection with the natural world and lays the foundation for mastering stronger beasts in the future.

---

## Rank 1
### Description

Wild Shape is one of the oldest and most sacred abilities practiced by the Druids of Asteria. Rather than disguising themselves as animals through illusion, Druids temporarily reshape their bodies, minds, and instincts to become one with the creatures they have come to understand.

Learning a new form requires patience, observation, and respect. Before a Druid may transform into a creature, they must first study it in its natural habitat and successfully understand its behaviour, movement, and place within the ecosystem. Only then can its spirit be welcomed into their own.

At this rank, your understanding is limited to simple creatures. Your transformations are best suited for exploration, travel, survival, and escaping danger rather than direct combat. Every new form learned expands your connection with the natural world and lays the foundation for mastering stronger beasts in the future.

---

### Effects

- Spend **25 MP** to transform into a learned Beast Form.
- Wild Shape lasts for **10 minutes** or until dismissed.
- You may only transform into **Small or Medium Beasts** that you have successfully learned.
- To learn a Beast Form, you must:
  - Observe the creature in its natural environment.
  - Study its behaviour and anatomy.
  - Succeed on an appropriate **D20 Nature Check** determined by the Game Master.
- Common beasts require a lower DC than rare or elusive creatures.
- Once a Beast Form has been learned, it is permanently added to your Wild Shape forms.
- While transformed:
  - You gain the beast's natural movement types (such as climbing or swimming).
  - You retain your Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma.
  - You cannot cast spells.
  - You cannot speak languages unless your Game Master determines the form is capable of doing so.
  - Your equipment merges into your new form and provides no benefits unless specifically stated otherwise.
- If your transformed form is reduced to **0 HP**, Wild Shape immediately ends and you return to your normal form. Any remaining damage carries over to your normal Hit Points.
- You may voluntarily end Wild Shape at any time without using an action.

---

### Lore

The first successful Wild Shape is celebrated within every Druidic Circle as a rite of passage. It marks the moment a Druid ceases to merely observe nature and instead experiences life through another creature's eyes.

Each transformation is considered a gift rather than a conquest. Druids do not steal the form of an animal—they earn its trust by understanding its place within the balance of the world. For this reason, many circles forbid careless or disrespectful use of Wild Shape, believing every transformation should honour the creature being emulated.

The oldest Archdruids teach that every new form reveals another lesson. To run as a wolf is to understand the strength of the pack. To soar as a hawk is to appreciate freedom. To swim as an otter is to embrace the endless flow of life itself.

---

> *"Do not become the beast to escape yourself. Become the beast to better understand the world you protect."*  
> — **Grand Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, First Warden of the Emerald Wilds**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your understanding of the natural world has deepened, allowing you to adopt more capable and specialised beast forms. Every transformation feels more natural, requiring less concentration as your body and spirit learn to move as one.

With experience comes appreciation for the unique strengths of each creature. A wolf's endurance, a mountain goat's sure footing, or an otter's graceful swimming are no longer simply observed—they become your own. Every new form teaches another lesson about survival, adaptation, and the balance of nature.

The elders remind every Druid that Wild Shape is not measured by the number of forms they possess, but by how well they understand each one. A single mastered form is often more valuable than a dozen poorly understood transformations.

---

### Effects

- Spend **30 MP** to transform into a learned Beast Form.
- Wild Shape lasts for **15 minutes** or until dismissed.
- You may transform into **Small or Medium Beasts** that you have successfully learned.
- Learning a new Beast Form requires:
  - Observing the creature in its natural habitat.
  - Studying its behaviour and anatomy.
  - Succeeding on a **D20 Nature Check** with a DC determined by the Game Master.
- More elusive or dangerous beasts require higher DCs to learn.
- Once learned, a Beast Form is permanently added to your Wild Shape forms.
- While transformed:
  - You gain the beast's movement types, including climbing, swimming, burrowing, or limited gliding where appropriate.
  - You gain the beast's natural senses, such as enhanced smell, hearing, or low-light vision.
  - You retain your **Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma**.
  - You cannot cast spells.
  - You cannot speak languages unless the chosen form is capable of doing so.
  - Your equipment merges into your new form and provides no benefits unless specifically stated otherwise.
- If your Beast Form is reduced to **0 HP**, Wild Shape immediately ends and any remaining damage carries over to your normal Hit Points.
- You may voluntarily end Wild Shape at any time without using an action.

---

### Lore

As Druids continue along the Path of Wild Shape, they begin to realise that every creature has evolved to overcome different challenges. Strength alone is rarely the answer. The swift survive where the strong cannot, while the patient endure where the swift would fail.

Many circles encourage their apprentices to spend weeks living alongside the creatures they wish to emulate. They observe hunting patterns, migration routes, nesting habits, and social behaviour until the transformation becomes more than imitation—it becomes genuine understanding.

Among experienced Druids, each new Beast Form is viewed as another chapter in an endless story written by nature itself. Every transformation is both a privilege and a lesson, revealing another way life has adapted to thrive within the world of Asteria.

---

> *"To wear a beast's form without understanding its life is to wear only its skin. Learn its heart, and the transformation becomes complete."*  
> — **Grand Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, First Warden of the Emerald Wilds**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your bond with the beasts of Asteria has matured into genuine kinship. Transformation no longer feels like assuming another shape—it feels as though you are remembering a part of yourself that has always existed within the natural world. Every heartbeat, instinct, and movement comes effortlessly, allowing you to adapt to the wilderness with remarkable confidence.

As your understanding grows, you may begin to study larger and more formidable creatures. These beasts demand greater patience and respect, for their instincts are stronger and their place within nature is more complex. Only Druids who truly understand a creature's purpose can successfully adopt its form.

Within many Druidic Circles, this stage marks the transition from student to guardian. You no longer transform simply to survive—you transform to fulfil the role each creature was born to perform, whether that is scouting, protecting, climbing, swimming, or enduring the harshest environments.

---

### Effects

- Spend **35 MP** to transform into a learned Beast Form.
- Wild Shape lasts for **20 minutes** or until dismissed.
- You may transform into **Small, Medium, or Large Beasts** that you have successfully learned.
- Learning a new Beast Form requires:
  - Observing the creature in its natural habitat.
  - Studying its behaviour, instincts, and anatomy.
  - Succeeding on a **D20 Nature Check** with a DC determined by the Game Master.
- Large or particularly elusive beasts require higher DCs and may require multiple successful observations before their form can be learned.
- Once learned, a Beast Form is permanently added to your Wild Shape forms.
- While transformed:
  - You gain the beast's movement types, natural senses, and non-magical physical abilities.
  - You retain your **Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma**.
  - You cannot cast spells.
  - You cannot speak languages unless the chosen form is physically capable of doing so.
  - Your equipment merges into your new form and provides no benefits unless specifically stated otherwise.
- If your Beast Form is reduced to **0 HP**, Wild Shape immediately ends and any remaining damage carries over to your normal Hit Points.
- You may voluntarily end Wild Shape at any time without using an action.

---

### Lore

Experienced Druids teach that every creature carries a lesson that cannot be learned through observation alone. To understand the endurance of a bear, one must feel the weight of its stride. To understand the patience of a hunting cat, one must wait without movement until the perfect moment arrives.

By this stage, Wild Shape has become more than a magical talent—it has become a spiritual discipline. Every new transformation broadens the Druid's understanding of life, revealing how countless species survive together while maintaining the balance of the natural world.

Among the oldest circles, it is believed that a Druid who truly honours every form they learn slowly becomes recognised by nature itself. Animals no longer see them as an outsider, but as another creature sharing the same ancient cycle of life.

---

> *"Every form you master teaches you how another soul survives. Respect each lesson, for nature wastes none."*  
> — **Grand Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, First Warden of the Emerald Wilds**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your mastery of Wild Shape has reached a level few Druids ever achieve. Transformation is no longer a technique requiring deliberate thought; it has become second nature. Your body, mind, and spirit move as one, allowing you to fully embrace the instincts and capabilities of the creatures whose forms you have earned.

At this stage, even the most formidable beasts begin to reveal their secrets. Apex predators, mighty herbivores, and elusive creatures that have survived for decades within the wild may now become subjects of your study. Each new form demands unwavering patience, respect, and understanding, for powerful beasts rarely tolerate careless observation.

The elders teach that a Druid who reaches this stage no longer borrows the strength of nature—they become one of its trusted guardians, able to walk among its greatest creatures as an equal rather than an outsider.

---

### Effects

- Spend **40 MP** to transform into a learned Beast Form.
- Wild Shape lasts for **25 minutes** or until dismissed.
- You may transform into **Small, Medium, or Large Beasts**, including powerful apex predators, that you have successfully learned.
- Learning a new Beast Form requires:
  - Observing the creature within its natural habitat.
  - Understanding its behaviour, instincts, and role within the ecosystem.
  - Succeeding on one or more **D20 Nature Checks** with DCs determined by the Game Master.
- Rare, dangerous, or legendary beasts may require multiple successful observations across different encounters before their form can be learned.
- Once learned, a Beast Form is permanently added to your Wild Shape collection.
- While transformed:
  - You gain the beast's movement types, natural senses, and non-magical physical abilities.
  - You retain your **Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma**.
  - You cannot cast spells.
  - You cannot speak languages unless the chosen form is physically capable of doing so.
  - Your equipment merges into your new form and provides no benefits unless specifically stated otherwise.
- If your Beast Form is reduced to **0 HP**, Wild Shape immediately ends and any remaining damage carries over to your normal Hit Points.
- You may voluntarily end Wild Shape at any time without using an action.
- You gain **Advantage on D20 Nature Checks** made to study and learn new Beast Forms.

---

### Lore

Within the oldest Druidic Circles, this stage is known as **The Mantle of the Wild**. Those who achieve it are recognised not for the number of forms they possess, but for the depth of understanding they have cultivated with every creature they have become.

Many Grand Druids spend months observing a single beast before attempting to learn its form. They witness its hunts, its struggles, its role within the food chain, and the respect it commands from other creatures. Only then do they attempt the sacred transformation.

It is said that nature willingly shares its greatest guardians only with those who have proven they will never abuse their gift. Every transformation is a reminder that strength exists to preserve balance, never to dominate it.

---

> *"The greatest beasts are not feared because they are strong. They are respected because they know precisely when strength should be used."*  
> — **Grand Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, First Warden of the Emerald Wilds**

## Rank 5
### Description

You have become one of the greatest shapeshifters of the Druidic Orders. Wild Shape is no longer simply a magical transformation—it is a perfect harmony between your spirit and the countless lives that inhabit the natural world. Every creature you have studied leaves a permanent imprint upon your soul, allowing you to call upon its form with absolute confidence.

The greatest beasts of Asteria are no longer beyond your understanding. Through years of observation, respect, and spiritual discipline, you have learned that every predator, every guardian, and every gentle giant exists to preserve the balance of nature. Their strength is not yours to command—it is yours to borrow with humility.

Those who reach this pinnacle are known throughout the ancient circles as **True Wildshapers**. Legends tell of Druids who crossed entire continents as migrating birds, defended villages as colossal bears, or vanished into the wilderness as wolves without ever revealing their true identities. To the beasts of the world, they are no longer strangers, but kindred spirits walking the same eternal cycle.

---

### Effects

- Spend **50 MP** to transform into a learned Beast Form.
- Wild Shape lasts for **30 minutes** or until dismissed.
- You may transform into **any Beast** that you have successfully learned, regardless of its size.
- Learning a new Beast Form requires:
  - Observing the creature in its natural habitat.
  - Understanding its behaviour, instincts, anatomy, and ecological role.
  - Completing one or more **D20 Nature Checks** with DCs determined by the Game Master.
- Exceptionally rare, ancient, or legendary beasts may require extended study over multiple encounters before their form can be learned.
- Once learned, a Beast Form is permanently added to your Wild Shape collection.
- While transformed:
  - You gain the beast's movement types, natural senses, and non-magical physical abilities.
  - You retain your **Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma**.
  - You cannot cast spells.
  - You cannot speak languages unless the chosen form is physically capable of doing so.
  - Your equipment merges into your new form and provides no benefits unless specifically stated otherwise.
- If your Beast Form is reduced to **0 HP**, Wild Shape immediately ends and any remaining damage carries over to your normal Hit Points.
- You may voluntarily end Wild Shape at any time without using an action.
- You have **Advantage on all D20 Nature Checks** made to study and learn new Beast Forms.
- Once per Long Rest, you may immediately transform into any previously learned Beast Form **without spending Mana**.

---

### Lore

The oldest Druidic texts describe this achievement as **The Soul of the Wild**. Few individuals in recorded history have reached this level of mastery, for it demands a lifetime of patience, compassion, and unwavering respect for every living creature. It is said that the forests themselves remember the footsteps of those who attain it.

Among the ancient circles, True Wildshapers are often entrusted with the protection of sacred groves, Worldroots, and places where the veil between the spirit world and nature grows thin. Their wisdom is sought not because they possess great power, but because they have lived countless lives through the forms they have mastered.

To witness a True Wildshaper transform is to watch nature itself flow effortlessly from one form to another. There is no flash of magic or violent surge of energy—only the quiet certainty that all living things are connected, and that the Druid has become part of that endless cycle.

---

> *"When you no longer ask how to become the beast, but instead remember that you have always been part of the wild, your journey is complete."*  
> — **Grand Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, First Warden of the Emerald Wilds**
