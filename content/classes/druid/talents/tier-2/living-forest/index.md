# Living Forest

## Overview
Your connection to the natural world has grown so strong that the forest itself begins to answer your call.

Whenever you weave powerful plant-based Earth magic into the world, you leave behind traces of primal life. On rare occasions, that lingering energy gathers into a small guardian spirit known as an **Entling**. Though young and inexperienced, these spirits are fiercely loyal to those who protect the balance of nature.

Unlike summoned creatures created through spellcraft, an Entling is born naturally from the life energy awakened by your magic. It is not forced into service, but willingly answers the call of a Druid whose actions nurture the living world.

No two Entlings are exactly alike. Some resemble tiny saplings with glowing eyes, while others appear as moss-covered roots or walking shrubs. Their personalities are as varied as the forests from which they emerge.

## Rank 1
### Description

Your connection to the natural world has grown so strong that the forest itself begins to answer your call.

Whenever you weave powerful plant-based Earth magic into the world, you leave behind traces of primal life. On rare occasions, that lingering energy gathers into a small guardian spirit known as an **Entling**. Though young and inexperienced, these spirits are fiercely loyal to those who protect the balance of nature.

Unlike summoned creatures created through spellcraft, an Entling is born naturally from the life energy awakened by your magic. It is not forced into service, but willingly answers the call of a Druid whose actions nurture the living world.

No two Entlings are exactly alike. Some resemble tiny saplings with glowing eyes, while others appear as moss-covered roots or walking shrubs. Their personalities are as varied as the forests from which they emerge.

---

### Effects

- This is a **Passive Talent**.
- Whenever you cast a **plant-based Earth spell** that alters or affects the environment, you may make a **Luck Check (DC 16)**.
- **Talent-based cantrips do not trigger this effect.**
- On a successful check, an **Entling** is summoned in an unoccupied space within the spell's affected area.
- The Entling is treated as a **Summon** and acts immediately after your turn.
- The Entling follows your verbal or telepathic commands where possible.
- Only **one Entling** may be summoned through this talent at a time.
- If an Entling is already active, additional successful Luck Checks have no effect until it is dismissed or defeated.

---

### Lore

The oldest Druidic Circles tell stories of forests so ancient that they developed spirits of their own. These spirits watched over the woodland for centuries, protecting every seedling, beast, and stream within their domain.

Most Druids never witness such beings, but those whose bond with nature is exceptionally strong occasionally attract younger forest spirits seeking purpose.

Known as Entlings, these tiny guardians are believed to be the first heartbeat of a future forest guardian. They are curious, protective, and deeply connected to the life that surrounds them.

Many Druids consider the arrival of their first Entling to be a sacred milestone, for it is said that the forest has chosen to walk beside them.

---

> *"You cannot command a forest to trust you. When an Entling chooses to stand at your side, know that the forest already has."*  
> — **Archdruid Elarion Wildroot, Guardian of the First Grove**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your bond with the forest deepens, attracting stronger guardian spirits to your side.

The young Entling that once answered your magic has matured into a **Sapling Ent**. While still considered a juvenile among its ancient kin, it possesses greater strength, resilience, and intelligence than its smaller predecessor.

The forest continues to decide when one of its guardians should answer your call. Every spell that nourishes or reshapes the natural world carries the possibility of awakening another dormant spirit.

Many Druids believe this transformation is not simply the growth of a summoned creature, but evidence that the same forest spirit continues to mature alongside its chosen guardian.

---

### Effects

- This is a **Passive Talent**.
- Whenever you cast a **plant-based Earth spell** that alters or affects the environment, you may make a **Luck Check (DC 15)**.
- **Talent-based cantrips do not trigger this effect.**
- On a successful check, a **Sapling Ent** is summoned in an unoccupied space within the spell's affected area.
- The Sapling Ent replaces the Entling gained from Rank 1.
- The Sapling Ent is treated as a **Summon** and acts immediately after your turn.
- The Sapling Ent follows your verbal or telepathic commands.
- Only **one Living Forest summon** may exist at a time.
- The Sapling Ent is larger, tougher, and more capable than an Entling, making it a more reliable forest guardian.

---

### Lore

Druidic texts describe Sapling Ents as the first true guardians of the woodland.

Unlike Entlings, which are curious and playful spirits, Sapling Ents begin to understand their purpose. They instinctively defend healthy forests, protect wildlife, and stand against those who seek to destroy the balance of nature.

Some ancient groves are said to contain Sapling Ents that have remained in the same clearing for centuries, patiently waiting until a worthy Druid awakens their dormant spirit.

Among the Verdant Circles, it is believed that a Sapling Ent chooses its Druid just as much as the Druid earns the trust of the forest.

---

> *"The first spirit follows because it is curious. The second remains because it believes in you."*  
> — **Elder Rowan Ashgrove, Keeper of the Emerald Path**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your connection to the Living Forest continues to flourish, allowing ancient woodland spirits to place greater trust in your magic.

When the forest answers your call, it now manifests as a **Young Ent**—a powerful guardian that has matured beyond its sapling years. Stronger, wiser, and deeply connected to the natural world, a Young Ent acts as both protector and caretaker of the land.

Unlike its smaller predecessors, a Young Ent possesses a keen understanding of its surroundings. It instinctively shields allies, disrupts those who threaten nature, and helps preserve the balance wherever it walks.

Many Druids believe that each time a Young Ent answers their call, it is the same spirit continuing its journey alongside them. As the Druid grows in wisdom, so too does the guardian that walks beside them.

---

### Effects

- This is a **Passive Talent**.
- Whenever you cast a **plant-based Earth spell** that alters or affects the environment, you may make a **Luck Check (DC 14)**.
- **Talent-based cantrips do not trigger this effect.**
- On a successful check, a **Young Ent** is summoned in an unoccupied space within the spell's affected area.
- The Young Ent replaces the Sapling Ent gained from Rank 2.
- The Young Ent is treated as a **Summon** and acts immediately after your turn.
- The Young Ent follows your verbal or telepathic commands.
- Only **one Living Forest summon** may exist at a time.
- The Young Ent gains improved Health, Defence, and Damage compared to the Sapling Ent.
- While the Young Ent is active, allied creatures within **5 metres** of it gain **+2 to saving throws against forced movement and knockback effects**, as the guardian steadies the ground with its living roots.

---

### Lore

Young Ents are regarded by many Druidic Circles as the first true wardens of the forest.

They patrol ancient woodlands, guide lost creatures back to safety, and silently watch over sacred groves that have stood for generations. Although peaceful by nature, they become relentless defenders whenever the balance of nature is threatened.

Old tales claim that Young Ents can recognise the footsteps of those who respect the wild, allowing them to pass unhindered, while those who seek to burn, poison, or corrupt the forest quickly earn their attention.

To stand beside a Young Ent is considered an honour, for few woodland spirits choose to accompany a mortal for long.

---

> *"A sapling grows because the forest wills it. An Ent grows because the forest trusts it."*  
> — **Archdruid Thalen Mossroot, Warden of the Elder Groves**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your bond with the Living Forest has become so profound that ancient guardian spirits willingly answer your call.

The forest now sends forth an **Ancient Ent**, a venerable protector whose presence alone commands respect from the creatures of the wild. Towering over the battlefield, it embodies centuries of growth, resilience, and unwavering devotion to the balance of nature.

An Ancient Ent is more than a guardian—it is a steward of the forest. Wherever it walks, plant life flourishes, roots awaken beneath the soil, and the surrounding woodland responds to its presence. Many Druids believe these guardians carry the memories of forgotten forests and the wisdom of countless seasons.

To summon such a being is considered one of the greatest honours a Druid can receive before earning the trust of the legendary Elder Ents.

---

### Effects

- This is a **Passive Talent**.
- Whenever you cast a **plant-based Earth spell** that alters or affects the environment, you may make a **Luck Check (DC 13)**.
- **Talent-based cantrips do not trigger this effect.**
- On a successful check, an **Ancient Ent** is summoned in an unoccupied space within the spell's affected area.
- The Ancient Ent replaces the Young Ent gained from Rank 3.
- The Ancient Ent is treated as a **Summon** and acts immediately after your turn.
- The Ancient Ent follows your verbal or telepathic commands.
- Only **one Living Forest summon** may exist at a time.
- The Ancient Ent possesses significantly greater Health, Defence, and Strength than previous forms.
- While the Ancient Ent is active, allied creatures within **10 metres** gain **+2 to Nature-based D20 Checks** and have **advantage on checks made to resist forced movement caused by natural terrain or plant-based effects.**
- Plant-based Earth spells cast within **10 metres** of the Ancient Ent have their **range increased by 5 metres**, as the guardian channels the surrounding forest to aid your magic.

---

### Lore

Ancient Ents are among the oldest living guardians of Asteria's forests. Some are believed to have stood watch since before the first kingdoms were founded, silently protecting sacred groves from war, corruption, and time itself.

Unlike younger forest spirits, Ancient Ents possess remarkable patience. They do not judge mortals by words alone but by years of action. Only Druids who have consistently shown respect for the natural world are ever deemed worthy of their companionship.

Within Druidic Circles, the appearance of an Ancient Ent is celebrated as proof that the forest no longer sees the Druid as a visitor, but as one of its own protectors.

---

> *"When an Ancient Ent answers your call, remember this—it is not obeying you. It has simply decided that your purpose is worthy of its strength."*  
> — **High Grovekeeper Sylvara Rootheart, Circle of the Living Canopy**

## Rank 5
### Description

Your bond with the Living Forest has reached its pinnacle, earning the trust of one of nature's greatest guardians—the **Elder Ent**.

These colossal beings are spoken of only in ancient Druidic legends. They are the oldest protectors of the wild, having watched forests grow, kingdoms rise, and civilizations fade beneath their branches. An Elder Ent does not answer the call of power alone. It answers only those who have proven themselves faithful stewards of the natural world.

Whenever the forest chooses to awaken, an Elder Ent emerges as a living embodiment of nature's will. Its presence inspires nearby wildlife, strengthens the land itself, and reminds all who stand before it that the forest has never truly been defenceless.

Many Druids spend their entire lives hoping to witness an Elder Ent. Those fortunate enough to fight beside one are remembered throughout history as Wardens of the Living Forest.

---

### Effects

- This is a **Passive Talent**.
- Whenever you cast a **plant-based Earth spell** that alters or affects the environment, you may make a **Luck Check (DC 12)**.
- **Talent-based cantrips do not trigger this effect.**
- On a successful check, an **Elder Ent** is summoned in an unoccupied space within the spell's affected area.
- The Elder Ent replaces the Ancient Ent gained from Rank 4.
- The Elder Ent is treated as a **Summon** and acts immediately after your turn.
- The Elder Ent follows your verbal or telepathic commands.
- Only **one Living Forest summon** may exist at a time.
- The Elder Ent possesses exceptional Health, Defence, and Strength, making it one of the most resilient natural guardians a Druid can summon.

While the Elder Ent is active:

- Allied creatures within **15 metres** gain **+3 to Nature-related D20 Checks**.
- Plant-based Earth spells cast within **15 metres** of the Elder Ent have their range increased by **10 metres**.
- Difficult Terrain created by your Earth or Plant spells does not affect you or your allies.
- Once per turn, when you cast a **Life** or **Earth** spell within the Elder Ent's aura, you may reduce its Mana Cost by **2 MP** as the Elder Ent channels the surrounding natural energy into your magic.

---

### Lore

Elder Ents are believed to be among the oldest living beings in Asteria. Some legends claim they were already ancient when the first Druids learned to speak with the forest.

Unlike younger Ents, they rarely leave the sacred groves they protect. Their appearance is considered an omen that the natural world has recognised a worthy guardian and chosen to lend its strength.

Every scar carved into an Elder Ent's bark tells the story of a forest defended, a wildfire endured, or a kingdom reclaimed by nature. They are living monuments to the resilience of the wild.

Among the oldest Druidic Circles, becoming a companion to an Elder Ent is regarded as one of the greatest honours a protector of nature can receive.

---

> *"An Elder Ent does not answer because you called. It answers because the forest believes you are worth protecting."*  
> — **Grand Archdruid Elarion Wildroot, Guardian of the First Grove**
