# Nature's Wrath

## Overview
The natural world rewards those who fight in harmony with it. Every spell you weave through the earth, every vine you command, and every blossom you nurture awakens a fragment of the forest's primal power.

This energy manifests as **Wrath Points**, representing the growing determination of the wild to preserve its balance. Rather than exploding uncontrollably, you may channel this energy to strengthen your spells at the moment they are cast.

Experienced Druids know that true strength lies not in overwhelming force, but in knowing when to release the power nature has entrusted to them.

---

## Rank 1
### Description

The natural world rewards those who fight in harmony with it. Every spell you weave through the earth, every vine you command, and every blossom you nurture awakens a fragment of the forest's primal power.

This energy manifests as **Wrath Points**, representing the growing determination of the wild to preserve its balance. Rather than exploding uncontrollably, you may channel this energy to strengthen your spells at the moment they are cast.

Experienced Druids know that true strength lies not in overwhelming force, but in knowing when to release the power nature has entrusted to them.

---

### Effects

- This is a **Passive Talent**.
- Whenever you successfully cast a **Life** or **Earth** spell, gain **1 Wrath Point**.
- You may have a maximum of **10 Wrath Points**.
- Wrath Points **resets every short rest**.
- You may only spend Wrath Points while casting a **Life** or **Earth** spell.
- Only **one Wrath Technique** may be applied to a spell.

#### Wrath Techniques

When casting a qualifying spell, you may choose **one** of the following:

**Deep Roots** *(2 Wrath Points)*
- Increase the spell's range by **5 metres**.

**Primal Focus** *(3 Wrath Points)*
- Increase the spell's **Spell Save DC by +1**.

**Verdant Surge** *(4 Wrath Points)*
- Add an additional **+1D4** damage or healing spells.

---

### Lore

The oldest Druids teach that nature is neither cruel nor kind—it simply seeks balance. Every spell cast in defence of that balance leaves behind a trace of primal energy, gathering quietly within those who respect the wild.

Unlike raw mana, this energy cannot be forced into existence. It must be earned through action, patience, and harmony with the natural world. Those who rush to spend it too quickly find only fleeting strength, while those who learn restraint discover that even the smallest seed can one day become an ancient forest.

To master Nature's Wrath is not to command the anger of the wild, but to become its trusted steward.

---

> *"The forest gives you its strength one leaf at a time. Wisdom is knowing which leaf to spend."*  
> — **Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, Warden of the Emerald Wilds**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your understanding of Nature's Wrath deepens, allowing you to shape the wild's energy with greater precision. The primal essence gathered through your spells no longer seeks only to empower your magic—it begins to respond to your intentions.

As your bond with the natural world strengthens, you learn to conserve the forest's fury until the perfect moment. Every Wrath Point becomes another root in the growing strength of your magic, ready to be woven into your next spell.

Experienced Druids rarely spend their Wrath carelessly. Instead, they allow it to build naturally, choosing the enhancement that best suits the situation before them.

### Lore

As Druids mature, they come to realise that the forest's power is not measured by how quickly it grows, but by how wisely it is used.

The oldest wardens compare Wrath to the changing seasons. It gathers slowly, almost unnoticed, before revealing its full strength when the moment is right. Those who master this balance learn to shape the battlefield without wasting the gifts the natural world has entrusted to them.

Within the Circle of the Emerald Wilds, young Druids are taught that every Wrath Point represents the trust of the forest. Spending it recklessly is seen as no different than cutting down an ancient tree for firewood.

---

> *"Power borrowed from the wild should never be wasted. The forest remembers every gift it gives."*  
> — **Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, Warden of the Emerald Wilds**

## Rank 3
### Description

The bond between you and the natural world has grown beyond simple harmony. The Wrath you gather is no longer merely energy waiting to be spent—it has become an extension of your will, flowing through your magic with remarkable control.

Experienced Druids learn that nature is at its strongest when it can endure. Rather than always seeking to strike harder, they learn to preserve life, strengthen their allies, and ensure that every spell leaves a lasting impact upon the battlefield.

Every Wrath Point spent is another expression of the forest's trust, allowing your magic to flourish in exactly the way the moment demands.

---

- This is a **Passive Talent**.
- Whenever you successfully cast a **Life** or **Earth** spell, gain **1 Wrath Point**.
- You may have a maximum of **10 Wrath Points**.
- Wrath Points are removed at the end of combat.

---

### Lore

The oldest wardens believed that true mastery over Nature's Wrath was not measured by how much destruction a Druid could unleash, but by how wisely they chose to wield the gifts of the wild.

Every root that breaks stone also supports the forest above it. Every towering tree survives because its roots protect it long before danger arrives. Druids who reach this level of mastery begin to understand that nature's greatest strength has always been its resilience.

Among the Emerald Circles, this lesson marks the transition from apprentice to guardian. From this point onward, a Druid no longer fights for the forest alone—they fight as one of its protectors.

---

> *"The strongest roots are never seen. Yet without them, no forest could ever stand."*  
> — **High Warden Elowen Briarroot, Circle of the Emerald Wilds**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your mastery over Nature's Wrath has become instinctive. The primal energy gathered through your magic flows effortlessly, allowing you to weave the forest's power into every spell with remarkable precision.

The natural world no longer simply lends you its strength—it trusts your judgement. Every Wrath Point represents the growing will of the wild, waiting to be shaped into exactly the form the moment demands.

Veteran Druids rarely exhaust their Wrath through reckless displays of power. Instead, they reserve it for the decisive moment, empowering the spell that will protect their allies, reshape the battlefield, or bring an end to the conflict.

To the oldest Druidic Circles, this level of mastery marks the beginning of becoming a true Warden of Nature.

---

### Effects

- This is a **Passive Talent**.
- Whenever you successfully cast a **Life** or **Earth** spell, gain **1 Wrath Point**.
- You may have a maximum of **15 Wrath Points**.
- Wrath Points are removed at the end of combat.
- You may only spend Wrath Points while casting a **Life** or **Earth** spell.
- Only **one Wrath Technique** may be applied to a spell.

#### Wrath Techniques

**Deep Roots** *(2 Wrath Points)*
- Increase the spell's range by **15 metres**.

**Primal Focus** *(3 Wrath Points)*
- Increase the spell's **Spell Save DC by +2**.

**Verdant Surge** *(4 Wrath Points)*
- Add an additional **+1D6** damage or healing spells.

**Enduring Growth** *(5 Wrath Points)*
- Increase the spell's duration by **2 Rounds**.

**Nature's Resilience** *(6 Wrath Points)*
- When casting a **Life** or **Earth** spell on yourself or an ally, grant **Temporary 5 Hit points** to target.
- Temporary HP gained this way lasts until the end of combat or until depleted.

**Primal Awakening** *(8 Wrath Points)*
Choose one when casting a qualifying spell:
- The spell **ignores Half Cover and Three-Quarters Cover**.
- One **Plant-based Summon** you control may immediately move up to its Speed and make **one Basic Attack** as a free action.

---

### Lore

The oldest guardians believed the forest rewards wisdom above all else. It does not ask its protectors to unleash power at every opportunity, but to recognise the single moment when that power will matter most.

Druids who reach this stage are often entrusted with defending sacred groves, ancient Worldroots, and places where the balance of nature is most fragile. Their command of Nature's Wrath is no longer driven by instinct alone, but by centuries of teachings passed from one generation of wardens to the next.

To witness such a Druid in battle is to watch the wild itself respond with perfect timing. Every spell, every vine, and every bloom seems to arrive exactly when it is needed.

---

> *"The forest does not waste a single leaf. Neither should a Druid waste a single fragment of its strength."*  
> — **Grand Warden Sylvara Thornheart, Keeper of the Emerald Wilds**

## Rank 5
### Description

You have mastered the primal energies of the natural world, becoming a true Warden of the Wild. Every spell you cast leaves an imprint upon the land, and every Wrath Point you gather represents the trust the forest has placed in you.

No longer content with minor blessings, the natural world now allows you to channel its full might through a single spell. This ultimate technique, known simply as **Nature's Wrath**, is not an attack of rage but a perfect expression of harmony between Druid and nature.

The oldest Archdruids teach that Nature's Wrath should never be used lightly. It is a gift earned through patience, wisdom, and respect for the balance of life. When unleashed, the battlefield itself seems to answer your call, amplifying your magic beyond its normal limits.

Few Druids ever reach this level of mastery, and those who do are remembered as legends among the circles of Asteria.

Legends tell of Archdruids who ended battles without drawing blood, simply by commanding the forest to reclaim the land beneath invading armies. Others speak of healers whose Life magic became so overwhelming that entire companies rose to their feet beneath a single blossom.

To wield Nature's Wrath is to carry the trust of the wild itself. Such power is never demanded—it is given only to those who have spent a lifetime protecting the balance they now command.

---

> *"The wild does not ask whether you are worthy of its strength. It watches your actions until the answer becomes obvious."*  
> — **Grand Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, Voice of the Emerald Wilds**
