# Rooted Power

## Overview
You learn to draw strength directly from the earth beneath your feet. By remaining perfectly still, your roots of mana extend into the land, allowing you to channel greater power through your Earth magic.

Although remaining stationary leaves you vulnerable, the reward is greater control and destructive potential. Experienced Druids often prepare themselves before unleashing powerful spells, allowing the land itself to amplify their magic.

Rooted Power reflects one of the oldest teachings of the Druidic Circles: the strongest tree is not the one that moves with every wind, but the one whose roots run deepest.

---

## Rank 1
### Description

You learn to draw strength directly from the earth beneath your feet. By remaining perfectly still, your roots of mana extend into the land, allowing you to channel greater power through your Earth magic.

Although remaining stationary leaves you vulnerable, the reward is greater control and destructive potential. Experienced Druids often prepare themselves before unleashing powerful spells, allowing the land itself to amplify their magic.

Rooted Power reflects one of the oldest teachings of the Druidic Circles: the strongest tree is not the one that moves with every wind, but the one whose roots run deepest.

---

### Effects

- This is a **Passive Talent**.
- If you **do not move during your turn**, you become **Rooted** until the start of your next turn.
- While Rooted:
  - Your **Earth spells** gain **+2 Damage**.
  - Any **Earth spell** that requires a **D20 Saving Throw** increases its **Save DC by +2**.
  - Earth spells that create **Difficult Terrain** or alter the battlefield have their duration increased by **1 Round**.
- Moving before casting immediately removes the Rooted bonus.
- This talent affects **Earth spells only**.

---

### Lore

Among the oldest Druids, standing motionless before casting is not seen as hesitation—it is seen as respect. They believe the earth willingly shares its strength only with those patient enough to listen.

Many ancient stone circles were built as places where Druids could root themselves to the land before performing powerful rituals. Even today, experienced practitioners pause for a brief moment before calling upon the deepest powers of the earth.

To outsiders, the Druid appears perfectly still. Beneath the surface, however, invisible currents of natural magic flow through the ground, gathering around them until released in a single overwhelming spell.

---

> *"The mountain does not seek power. It simply stands, and power gathers around it."*  
> — **Archdruid Thalen Stoneroot, Warden of the Emerald Peaks**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your roots reach deeper into the earth, allowing greater amounts of natural energy to flow through your body before being released as powerful Earth magic.

The longer you remain still, the more stable your connection becomes. Rather than forcing mana into your spells, you allow the land itself to strengthen them. Every stone, root, and grain of soil beneath your feet becomes part of your magic.

Veteran Druids often choose their ground carefully before combat begins. Once rooted, they become living conduits of the earth, capable of shaping the battlefield with remarkable precision.

---

### Effects

- This is a **Passive Talent**.
- If you **do not move during your turn**, you become **Rooted** until the start of your next turn.
- While Rooted:
  - Your **Earth spells** gain **+4 Damage**.
  - Any **Earth spell** that requires a **D20 Saving Throw** increases its **Save DC by +3**.
  - Earth spells that create **Difficult Terrain** or alter the battlefield have their duration increased by **2 Rounds**.
  - The range of your Earth spells increases by **5 metres**.
- Moving before casting immediately removes the Rooted bonuses.
- This talent affects **Earth spells only**.

---

### Lore

As Druids continue their training, they discover that true strength does not come from standing firm—it comes from becoming part of the land itself.

Every heartbeat, every breath, and every pulse of mana sinks into the earth before returning many times stronger. Ancient Druids believed that the land remembers those who stand with patience, rewarding them with power that cannot be gained through haste alone.

Many sacred stone circles throughout Asteria were built for this very purpose, allowing Druids to root themselves in places where the world's natural energies are strongest.

---

> *"The earth gives freely to those willing to stand and listen. Those who rush hear only silence."*  
> — **Stonewarden Brynn Earthsong, Circle of the Ancient Stones**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your bond with the earth has become second nature. The moment you plant your feet, invisible roots of mana spread deep beneath the ground, drawing strength from the land and feeding it into your spells.

The earth no longer simply empowers your magic—it stabilises it. Every spell is cast with greater force and precision, while the ground itself resists any attempt to move you from your chosen position.

Experienced Druids know that sacrificing mobility is a small price to pay for commanding the overwhelming power of the earth. Once rooted, they become as steadfast as the mountains they call upon.

---

### Effects

- This is a **Passive Talent**.
- If you **do not move during your turn**, you become **Rooted** until the start of your next turn.
- While Rooted:
  - Your **Earth spells** gain **+6 Damage**.
  - Any **Earth spell** that requires a **D20 Saving Throw** increases its **Save DC by +4**.
  - The range of your Earth spells increases by **10 metres**.
  - Earth spells that create **Difficult Terrain** or alter the battlefield have their duration increased by **3 Rounds**.
  - You gain **Advantage on Strength Checks and Saving Throws** made to resist being **pushed, pulled, knocked prone, or forcibly moved**.
- Moving before casting immediately removes all Rooted bonuses.
- This talent affects **Earth spells only**.

---

### Lore

The oldest Druids speak of becoming one with the mountains—not in body, but in spirit. Through years of practice, they learn to anchor themselves so completely that even powerful magic struggles to break their connection with the earth.

Many legendary battles tell of Druids standing unmoved while the world around them crumbled. Storms raged, armies charged, and the ground split beneath their feet, yet they remained steadfast, drawing endless strength from the land below.

Among the Circle of Stone, reaching this level of Rooted Power marks the beginning of true Earth mastery, where patience becomes strength and stillness becomes power.

---

> *"A mountain does not resist the storm because it is stronger. It endures because it refuses to move."*  
> — **Stonewarden Brynn Earthsong, Circle of the Ancient Stones**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your connection to the earth has become unwavering. The moment you root yourself, the land embraces you as one of its own, anchoring your body and amplifying every Earth spell you cast.

The natural mana flowing beneath the surface surges through you with incredible force, allowing your spells to reshape the battlefield with unmatched precision. Stone walls rise taller, fissures spread farther, and every tremor carries greater destructive force.

Few enemies can move a Druid who has mastered this level of Rooted Power. They stand as immovable pillars amidst chaos, drawing strength from the world itself while unleashing the fury of the earth.

---

### Effects

- This is a **Passive Talent**.
- If you **do not move during your turn**, you become **Rooted** until the start of your next turn.
- While Rooted:
  - Your **Earth spells** gain **+8 Damage**.
  - Any **Earth spell** that requires a **D20 Saving Throw** increases its **Save DC by +5**.
  - The range of your Earth spells increases by **15 metres**.
  - Earth spells that create **Difficult Terrain** or alter the battlefield have their duration increased by **4 Rounds**.
  - You gain **Advantage on Strength Checks and Saving Throws** made to resist being **pushed, pulled, knocked prone, or forcibly moved**.
  - You gain **Resistance to Forced Movement**. If an effect would move you, reduce the distance by **half** (rounded down).
- Moving before casting immediately removes all Rooted bonuses.
- This talent affects **Earth spells only**.

---

### Lore

The ancient Stone Circles teach that the earth is never truly still. Beneath every mountain and valley lies an endless current of natural power, waiting for those patient enough to become part of it.

Druids who master Rooted Power are no longer visitors upon the land—they are accepted by it. Their footsteps become lighter, yet their presence becomes impossible to ignore. Even the oldest mountains recognise them as guardians rather than wanderers.

Legends tell of Druids who stood alone against charging armies, refusing to yield a single step. Though battered by wind, steel, and magic, they remained rooted until the battle was won, their unwavering resolve becoming as legendary as the mountains themselves.

---

> *"A tree survives because of its roots. A mountain endures because it has nowhere else to be. Learn from both."*  
> — **High Stonewarden Kaelor Granitebloom, Circle of the Worldroot**

## Rank 5
### Description

Your mastery of Earth magic reaches its pinnacle. The moment you root yourself, your body becomes one with the land beneath you, drawing upon the endless strength of the world itself. The flow of natural mana is no longer merely borrowed—it is welcomed, amplified, and released through you with overwhelming force.

While rooted, every Earth spell is cast with exceptional precision and destructive power. The ground answers your call without hesitation, raising walls of stone, opening deep fissures, and reshaping the battlefield as though the land itself has become your ally.

The oldest Druids refer to this state as becoming **Worldrooted**. Few ever achieve it, for it requires complete trust in the natural world. Those who do are said to stand as living pillars between civilisation and the untamed wilds, immovable until they choose to walk once more.

---

### Effects

- This is a **Passive Talent**.
- If you **do not move during your turn**, you become **Rooted** until the start of your next turn.
- While Rooted:
  - Your **Earth spells** gain **+10 Damage**.
  - Any **Earth spell** that requires a **D20 Saving Throw** increases its **Save DC by +6**.
  - The range of your Earth spells increases by **20 metres**.
  - Earth spells that create **Difficult Terrain** or alter the battlefield have their duration increased by **5 Rounds**.
  - You gain **Advantage on Strength Checks and Saving Throws** made to resist being **pushed, pulled, knocked prone, or forcibly moved**.
  - You **cannot be forcibly moved** by non-legendary abilities or effects while Rooted.
  - Your Earth spells ignore **Half Cover** and **Three-Quarters Cover** created by natural terrain.
- Moving before casting immediately removes all Rooted bonuses.
- This talent affects **Earth spells only**.

---

### Lore

To become Worldrooted is to earn the complete trust of the earth itself. The oldest legends speak of Druids who stood unmoving for days upon sacred mountains, allowing their spirits to intertwine with the heartbeat of the world until they could no longer tell where they ended and the land began.

These Druids became living extensions of the earth. Wherever they stood, stone rose to defend them, cliffs answered their commands, and the ground itself refused to allow them to fall.

Modern Druids rarely witness such mastery, yet every Circle preserves the stories as a reminder that true power is not found in movement or speed, but in unwavering resolve. The earth has endured since the beginning of Asteria, and those who become one with it inherit a fraction of that timeless strength.

---

> *"Stand long enough for the world to know your name, and one day it will answer when you call."*  
> — **Grand Earthwarden Kaelor Granitebloom, Keeper of the Worldroot**
