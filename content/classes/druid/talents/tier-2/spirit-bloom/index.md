# Spirit Bloom

## Overview
You call forth a radiant Spirit Bloom, a magical flower infused with the essence of Life. Upon taking root, it emits a warm, soothing light that strengthens the resolve of nearby allies and reminds them of the resilience found within the natural world.

Unlike creatures summoned through Living Forest, the Spirit Bloom is stationary and serves as a beacon of protection rather than a combatant. While fragile, its presence can inspire allies to endure hardships that would otherwise overwhelm them.

Many Druids cultivate these blossoms before battles, believing that even the smallest flower can become a symbol of hope when darkness begins to close in.

---

## Rank 1
### Description

You call forth a radiant Spirit Bloom, a magical flower infused with the essence of Life. Upon taking root, it emits a warm, soothing light that strengthens the resolve of nearby allies and reminds them of the resilience found within the natural world.

Unlike creatures summoned through Living Forest, the Spirit Bloom is stationary and serves as a beacon of protection rather than a combatant. While fragile, its presence can inspire allies to endure hardships that would otherwise overwhelm them.

Many Druids cultivate these blossoms before battles, believing that even the smallest flower can become a symbol of hope when darkness begins to close in.

---

### Effects

- Summon a **Spirit Bloom** in an unoccupied space within range.
- The Spirit Bloom is **stationary** and cannot move or attack.
- The Spirit Bloom emits bright light in a **10-metre radius**.
- Allied creatures standing within the light gain:
  - **+1 to Constitution Skill Checks.**
  - **+1 to Constitution Saving Throws.**
- These bonuses are lost immediately when a creature leaves the illuminated area.
- The Spirit Bloom remains for **3 Rounds** or until destroyed.

#### Spirit Bloom Statistics

- **HP:** 5
- **AC:** 10

Only **one Spirit Bloom** may be active at a time.

---

### Lore

Spirit Blooms are said to grow where the essence of Life gathers most strongly. Though they resemble ordinary flowers, each blossom is formed from pure natural magic, carrying a fragment of the world's life force within its petals.

Druids often see these flowers as symbols of hope. Even in the aftermath of great battles, tales speak of Spirit Blooms emerging from blood-soaked earth, reminding survivors that life always finds a way to return.

Among the Verdant Circles, nurturing a Spirit Bloom is considered one of the first lessons in protecting others before oneself.

---

> *"A single flower cannot stop a storm—but it can remind us why we endure it."*  
> — **Archdruid Lysara Dawnpetal, Keeper of the Blooming Grove**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your Spirit Bloom grows stronger as more of nature's life essence flows through it. The blossom becomes larger, its petals brighter, and its roots spread further beneath the earth, allowing its restorative aura to reach more allies.

The Bloom is no longer simply a symbol of hope—it becomes a sanctuary on the battlefield. Those who stand within its radiant light find renewed determination, their bodies strengthened by the quiet resilience of the natural world.

Although it remains vulnerable to attack, experienced Druids learn to position their Spirit Bloom carefully, encouraging allies to fight around its protective aura while forcing enemies to decide whether to waste valuable attacks destroying it.

### Lore

As a Druid's bond with Life magic deepens, their Spirit Bloom begins to resemble the sacred flowers that grow within the oldest groves of Asteria. Their radiant petals are said to absorb the strength of the earth below before sharing it with every living creature sheltered beneath their light.

Many Druidic Circles plant living gardens around their sanctuaries using Spirit Blooms, believing that every blossom carries a fragment of the world's life force. Warriors who have fought beside these flowers often speak of an overwhelming sense of calm, even in the heart of battle.

Among the Keepers of the Blooming Grove, the appearance of a mature Spirit Bloom is considered a sign that the Druid has begun to truly understand that strength and compassion can flourish together.

---

> *"The brightest flowers do not bloom because the world is gentle. They bloom because life refuses to surrender."*  
> — **Archdruid Lysara Dawnpetal, Keeper of the Blooming Grove**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your Spirit Bloom blossoms into a powerful conduit of Life Essence, becoming a beacon that strengthens both body and spirit. Its roots extend deep into the earth, drawing upon the vitality of the land before radiating that energy through its brilliant petals.

The Bloom's light grows brighter and steadier, allowing allies to fight with renewed endurance while empowering their physical resolve. Warriors often rally around a mature Spirit Bloom, knowing its presence can be the difference between holding the line and falling before overwhelming odds.

Though still unable to defend itself, the Spirit Bloom has become a valuable battlefield objective. Protecting it allows your allies to benefit from its blessings for far longer, while enemies are often forced to divert their attention to destroying it.

### Lore

Within the oldest Druidic sanctuaries, Spirit Blooms of this strength are cultivated as symbols of perseverance. Their radiant light has guided travellers through dense forests, illuminated sacred ceremonies, and given exhausted defenders the strength to endure impossible battles.

Elder Druids teach that every Spirit Bloom is a reminder that life is strongest when it is shared. Just as a single flower spreads its seeds across the forest, so too does its magic spread hope to those willing to stand together beneath its light.

Many tales tell of battlefields where the only thing left standing after the fighting ceased was a solitary Spirit Bloom, still glowing peacefully amongst the silence.

---

> *"Strength may win a battle, but hope is what gives us the strength to fight the next."*  
> — **High Druid Elowen Brightbloom, Guardian of the Emerald Vale**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your Spirit Bloom matures into a sacred blossom, radiating an aura of life powerful enough to bolster both the body and the spirit of nearby allies. Its roots weave deep beneath the battlefield, drawing strength from the land while its radiant petals bathe the surrounding area in warm, restorative light.

Those who stand beneath its glow find themselves filled with renewed determination. Fatigue lessens, muscles strengthen, and even the harshest blows become easier to withstand. Veteran Druids often place a Spirit Bloom at the heart of their formation, allowing allies to rally around its protective light while enemies desperately attempt to destroy it.

Although fragile compared to living guardians such as Ents, a mature Spirit Bloom has become an invaluable source of support, capable of changing the tide of battle simply by remaining rooted in place.

---

### Effects

- Summon a **Spirit Bloom** in an unoccupied space within range.

#### Spirit Bloom Statistics

- **HP:** 20
- **AC:** 10

Only **one Spirit Bloom** may be active at a time.

---

### Lore

Among the oldest groves of Asteria, Spirit Blooms of this magnitude are regarded as sacred landmarks. Their radiant light is said to soothe wounded souls, strengthen weary defenders, and remind all living creatures that life continues even in the darkest of times.

Many Druidic Circles cultivate these blossoms around sacred sites, believing that every bloom carries a fragment of the world's life essence. During times of war, entire companies have been known to gather around a single Spirit Bloom, drawing courage from its unwavering light.

To witness such a blossom in full bloom is to witness nature's promise that hope, like life itself, will always find a way to flourish.

---

> *"When darkness gathers, even the smallest flower may become the brightest light on the battlefield."*  
> — **Archdruid Lysara Dawnpetal, Keeper of the Blooming Grove**

## Rank 5
### Description

Your Spirit Bloom reaches its final form, becoming a legendary blossom infused with the purest essence of Life. Its petals radiate a brilliant golden light, while powerful roots spread beneath the battlefield, anchoring both itself and the resolve of those who fight beneath its canopy.

The Bloom no longer serves merely as a source of encouragement—it becomes the heart of the battlefield. Allies standing within its light feel their bodies strengthened, their resolve hardened, and their spirits lifted. Even in the face of overwhelming odds, the Bloom reminds every ally that life is resilient and hope can never truly be extinguished.

Ancient Druids tell stories of these blossoms appearing after great battles, where their light guided survivors home and marked places where nature had reclaimed victory over destruction. To witness a Spirit Bloom in its perfected form is considered one of the greatest blessings a Druid can bestow.

### Lore

The perfected Spirit Bloom is regarded by the oldest Druidic Circles as one of the purest manifestations of Life magic. It is said that these blossoms first appeared where the World Tree shed its petals at the dawn of Asteria, each bloom carrying a fragment of the world's original life force.

Only the most accomplished Druids can nurture a Spirit Bloom to this stage. Wherever one blooms, wildlife gathers, wounded spirits find comfort, and even the harshest battlefields seem to soften beneath its gentle radiance.

Many believe these flowers are watched over by Life Spirits themselves, quietly preserving the balance between growth and decay. Long after a battle has ended, legends claim that the land where a perfected Spirit Bloom once stood becomes more fertile, serving as a lasting reminder that life always endures.

---

> *"The greatest miracle of nature is not that it survives hardship, but that it continues to bloom because of it."*  
> — **Grand Archdruid Elyndra Bloomheart, Voice of the World Tree**
