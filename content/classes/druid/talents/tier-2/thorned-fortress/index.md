# Thorned Fortress

## Overview
Your mastery over living plant magic allows you to reinforce your summoned guardians with dense bark, razor-sharp thorns, and resilient vines.

Whether it is an Entling, a Spirit Bloom summon, or another plant-based creature under your control, your summons become living fortresses that punish those foolish enough to engage them in close combat.

The thorns that cover their bodies are more than simple defences. They instinctively react to danger, tearing into enemies that strike them or become ensnared within their grasp.

Many Druids believe this blessing was first taught by the Elder Ents themselves, who learned long ago that the strongest defence is one that discourages an enemy from attacking in the first place.

---

## Rank 1
### Description

Your mastery over living plant magic allows you to reinforce your summoned guardians with dense bark, razor-sharp thorns, and resilient vines.

Whether it is an Entling, a Spirit Bloom summon, or another plant-based creature under your control, your summons become living fortresses that punish those foolish enough to engage them in close combat.

The thorns that cover their bodies are more than simple defences. They instinctively react to danger, tearing into enemies that strike them or become ensnared within their grasp.

Many Druids believe this blessing was first taught by the Elder Ents themselves, who learned long ago that the strongest defence is one that discourages an enemy from attacking in the first place.

---

### Effects

- This is a **Passive Talent**.
- All **Plant-based Summons** you control gain the **Thorned** trait.
- Plant-based summons gain:
  - **+10 Maximum HP**
  - **+1 Attack Damage**
- Whenever a creature hits one of your Plant-based summons with a **melee attack**, it immediately suffers **2 Piercing Damage** from the creature's thorns.
- If one of your Plant-based summons successfully **Grapples** a creature, the grappled creature suffers **2 Piercing Damage** at the **start of each of its turns** while the grapple is maintained.
- Thorn damage ignores non-magical armour but may still be reduced by abilities or resistances that reduce **Piercing Damage**.

---

### Lore

The oldest forests in Asteria are home to trees whose bark is harder than stone and whose thorns have grown for centuries. These ancient guardians survive not through aggression, but by making every attack against them costly.

Druids who master Thorned Fortress learn to weave that same natural resilience into their summoned companions. Bark thickens, vines tighten, and thorns emerge wherever an enemy dares to strike.

Among the Verdant Circles, this gift is viewed as a symbol of nature's quiet strength. A forest does not chase its enemies—it simply ensures they regret every step they take within it.

---

> *"Strike a thorn once, and you curse the thorn. Strike a living forest, and you soon realise the thorn was showing mercy."*  
> — **Elder Brambleheart, Keeper of the Ironwood Grove**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your mastery over living plant guardians continues to grow, allowing thicker bark and stronger thorned vines to form around your summoned allies.

The once modest defensive thorns now develop into dense natural armour, making your summons significantly more resilient in battle. Their grasp tightens, and every attempt to break free from their embrace comes at a painful cost.

Experienced Druids often shape their summoned guardians into the front line of battle, allowing them to absorb attacks while the forest slowly wears down those who refuse to retreat.

To many within the Druidic Circles, Thorned Fortress is proof that nature's greatest strength is not its ability to attack—but its ability to endure.

### Lore

As a Druid's bond with the forest deepens, their summoned guardians begin to resemble the ancient defenders that watch over the oldest groves of Asteria.

Layers of bark harden like living armour while thorn-covered vines instinctively wrap around any creature that ventures too close. Every wound inflicted upon these guardians is answered by the forest itself.

The oldest Ents are said to have developed these natural defences over thousands of years, their bark becoming almost impenetrable while their thorns served as a warning to those who sought to destroy the wild.

Druids who master Thorned Fortress do not simply summon allies—they summon pieces of the forest's enduring will.

---

> *"The forest does not fear those who strike first. It simply outlasts them until they strike no more."*  
> — **Grovekeeper Maelis Briarroot, Warden of the Thornwood**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your summoned guardians now embody the relentless resilience of the oldest forests. Their bark grows thicker than ironwood, while long, barbed vines coil around their limbs, waiting to punish anything that comes within reach.

Enemies quickly learn that every strike against these guardians carries a price. The deeper they become entangled, the more the living thorns tear into flesh and armour alike.

At this stage, your plant summons are no longer simple defenders. They become wardens of the battlefield, capable of locking enemies in place while the forest slowly overwhelms them.

Many Druids consider this the point where their summons truly become extensions of the Living Forest itself.

### Lore

Ancient records tell of forests where even the bravest warriors refused to tread. Not because the trees moved, but because every branch, vine, and thorn seemed determined to drive intruders away.

Druids who master Thorned Fortress begin to recreate these legendary guardians through their summons. Every layer of bark becomes stronger, every thorn sharper, and every vine more determined to hold those who threaten the balance of nature.

To stand against one of these guardians is to wage war against the patience of the forest itself. Every wound inflicted is answered in kind, until even the strongest enemies find themselves exhausted by the unyielding embrace of living wood.

---

> *"The wise fear the beast's bite. The wiser fear the tree that refuses to let go."*  
> — **Archdruid Rowan Thornbark, Warden of the Ironwood Circle**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your command over living plant guardians reaches a level few Druids ever achieve. Thick layers of enchanted bark encase your summons, while ancient thorned vines constantly regenerate across their bodies, making them exceptionally difficult to bring down.

The forest no longer merely protects its guardians—it actively punishes those who challenge them. Every strike against your summons is met with a painful reminder that nature is both patient and relentless. Enemies who become trapped within their grasp quickly find themselves bleeding beneath an endless mass of barbed vines.

Veteran Druids often position their summoned guardians at the heart of the battlefield, allowing them to hold the line while allies fight from the safety of the living fortress they have created.

### Lore

Among the oldest groves of Asteria stand trees that have endured countless wars, storms, and disasters. Their bark bears the scars of centuries, yet they continue to grow stronger with each passing season.

Druids who master Thorned Fortress begin to emulate these ancient guardians. Their summoned companions become living bastions whose purpose is not simply to fight, but to endure. Every thorn, every vine, and every layer of bark exists to protect the balance of nature and those who fight beside it.

The greatest forest wardens teach that true strength is measured not by how hard one strikes, but by how long one can stand against the darkness.

> *"An axe may wound the bark, but the forest remembers the hand that swung it."*

## Rank 5
### Description

Your mastery over the Living Forest reaches its ultimate expression. The guardians that answer your call become legendary protectors, clad in ancient bark and covered in countless enchanted thorns that have grown for centuries.

Every branch, vine, and thorn becomes a weapon of defence. Those who attack your summons quickly discover that every strike is answered by the forest itself. Their bark becomes almost impossible to penetrate, while their grasp tightens with the unyielding strength of ancient roots.

To the Druids of old, this stage represents the perfect balance between strength and patience. The forest does not pursue its enemies, nor does it seek conquest. Instead, it stands firm, allowing those who threaten the natural world to defeat themselves against its living walls.

An Elder Ent protected by Thorned Fortress is said to resemble the oldest trees in Asteria—silent, immovable, and utterly relentless.

### Effects

- This is a **Passive Talent**.
- All **Plant-based Summons** you control gain the **Thorned** trait.

- Once per round, when a Plant-based summon is reduced below **50% of its maximum HP**, thorn-covered vines surge across its body, granting it **Temporary HP equal to 10% of its maximum HP**. This effect may trigger **once per summon**.

### Lore

The oldest legends speak of forests so ancient that invading armies simply vanished beneath their canopies. Those who survived claimed that the trees themselves fought back, their living guardians refusing to yield even after suffering terrible wounds.

These stories are believed to have inspired the creation of Thorned Fortress. Rather than teaching Druids how to create stronger summons, the talent teaches them how to awaken the ancient resilience that already exists within every guardian spirit of the forest.

To witness an Elder Ent protected by Thorned Fortress is to understand why entire kingdoms once feared entering the sacred groves of Asteria. The guardian does not rage or pursue. It simply endures, standing between the innocent and those who would bring harm, until the forest itself has claimed victory.

---

> *"Strength fells a tree. Patience grows a forest. Endurance protects it for eternity."*  
> — **Grand Archdruid Elarion Wildroot, Guardian of the First Grove**
