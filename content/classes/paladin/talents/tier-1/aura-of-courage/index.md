# Aura of Courage

## Overview
A Paladin's courage is more than personal resolve—it is a beacon that strengthens the spirits of those who stand beside them. Through unwavering faith and an unbreakable oath, you project a divine aura that steels the hearts of your allies against fear and despair.

While your aura remains active, those who fight within its protective embrace find their resolve strengthened before terror can take hold. Even allies already gripped by fear may find the courage to rise again as they draw close to your presence, though true protection belongs to those who never abandon the light of your conviction.

Aura of Courage is the first manifestation of a Paladin's divine presence upon the battlefield. It marks the beginning of your journey as a guardian whose faith protects not only their own heart, but the hearts of every companion willing to stand beside them.

## Rank 1
### Description

Aura of Courage is the first manifestation of a Paladin's divine presence upon the battlefield. It marks the beginning of your journey as a guardian whose faith protects not only their own heart, but the hearts of every companion willing to stand beside them.

### Effects

- Spend **15 MP** to activate **Aura of Courage**.
- Create a **5 metre radius Aura** centred on yourself for **3 Rounds**.
- The aura moves with you.
- Allies already within the aura gain **+2 to Saving Throws against the Fear condition**.
- If an ally is targeted by a Fear effect while already inside the aura, the **+2 bonus** is applied to their Saving Throw.
- An ally that enters the aura while already suffering the **Fear** condition may immediately attempt **one new Saving Throw** to end the effect.
- This Saving Throw gains **+1** *(half the aura's normal bonus, rounded down)*.
- This reroll may only be attempted **once per aura activation** for each creature.
- Multiple **Aura of Courage** effects do not stack. If affected by more than one, only the strongest version applies.

### Lore

Within every holy order of Asteria, courage is considered a gift that grows stronger when shared. Veteran Paladins teach that fear cannot be defeated through words alone—it is overcome by standing shoulder to shoulder with those who refuse to yield.

The first lesson every initiate learns is that an aura is not a shield that follows the fearful. It is a sanctuary for those who place their trust in the Paladin's conviction. Those who remain within its light discover that even the darkest horrors lose their power when faced together.

> *"Courage is not the absence of fear. It is the decision to stand firm despite it—and to help others do the same."*  
> **— Lady Seraphine, Shieldbearer of the Eternal Dawn**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your courage grows into an unwavering beacon that shines brighter across the battlefield. Allies fighting beside you find their resolve strengthened, allowing them to face horrors that would break the spirits of lesser warriors. Your faith stands as a constant reminder that fear only has power over those who surrender to it.

As your divine presence matures, your aura reaches farther and lingers longer, encouraging companions to remain close to your side where your conviction is strongest. Those who flee may still find refuge by returning to your light, but the greatest protection belongs to those who never leave it.

Veteran Paladins often describe this stage of mastery as the moment their companions stop looking at the monsters before them and instead draw confidence from the steadfast guardian standing at their side.

### Lore

The oldest knightly orders teach that courage is not born from victory, but from trust. Warriors who stand beside a seasoned Paladin often find themselves confronting impossible odds with a calm they cannot fully explain. It is not the absence of fear that strengthens them, but the certainty that they do not stand alone.

Many commanders intentionally place experienced Paladins where enemy terror is expected to be greatest. Their presence has turned collapsing battle lines into legendary last stands, proving that a single steadfast soul can become the foundation upon which an entire army refuses to break.

> *"Stand with me, and let your courage become greater than your fear."*  
> **— Dame Elowyn, Captain of the Silver Wardens**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your unwavering faith becomes a beacon that reaches beyond courage alone. Allies who stand within your divine presence find their minds fortified against both terror and manipulation, allowing them to remain steadfast when others would falter.

The aura you project is no longer simply a source of bravery—it becomes a sanctuary of resolve. Those who fight beneath your protection discover that fear loses its grip, while attempts to cloud their judgement or sway their will are met with remarkable resistance. Although those who seek refuge after succumbing to such effects may still recover, the greatest protection is reserved for those who remain within your light from the beginning.

As your mastery grows, your companions begin to place complete trust in your presence, knowing that while your aura surrounds them, neither fear nor deception can easily break their resolve.

### Effects

- Spend **35 MP** to activate **Aura of Courage**.
- Create a **10 metre radius Aura** centred on yourself for **5 Rounds**.
- The aura moves with you.
- Allies already within the aura gain **+4 to Saving Throws against the Fear and Charm conditions**.
- If an ally is targeted by a Fear or Charm effect while already inside the aura, the **+4 bonus** is applied to their Saving Throw.
- An ally that enters the aura while suffering the **Fear** or **Charm** condition may immediately attempt **one new Saving Throw** to end one of those effects.
- This Saving Throw gains **+2** *(half the aura's normal bonus, rounded down)*.
- This reroll may only be attempted **once per aura activation** for each creature.
- Multiple **Aura of Courage** effects do not stack. If affected by more than one, only the strongest version applies.

### Lore

To witness a veteran Paladin standing firm amid impossible odds is to understand why courage is considered contagious. Their presence inspires more than confidence—it reminds those around them who they are, what they fight for, and why no force should be allowed to bend their will.

Many scholars believe this is the stage at which a Paladin's faith begins to resonate with the souls of those nearby. Whether divine miracle or simple inspiration, those who stand within the aura often recall hearing the Paladin's voice clearly above the chaos of battle, guiding them back to themselves when fear and enchantment sought to steal their resolve.

> *"A frightened heart may stumble. A faithful heart always finds its way home."*  
> **— High Justicar Alaric, Keeper of the Sacred Bastion**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your courage has become an unwavering bastion of the mind. Those who stand beside you find their thoughts steadied, their resolve strengthened, and their spirits shielded from forces that seek to overwhelm, deceive, or corrupt them. Fear no longer stands alone as your enemy—any assault upon the will itself struggles against the divine conviction you project.

The aura surrounding you radiates calm amidst chaos. Companions fighting beneath your protection discover that even the darkest horrors and most insidious enchantments lose much of their power. While those who seek refuge after succumbing to such effects may still recover, those who remain steadfast within your presence receive the greatest blessing of your unwavering faith.

The strongest Paladins understand that courage is not merely the absence of fear. It is the strength to remain true to yourself when every force in the world attempts to make you someone else.

### Effects

- Spend **40 MP** to activate **Aura of Courage**.
- Create a **12 metre radius Aura** centred on yourself for **6 Rounds**.
- The aura moves with you.
- Allies already within the aura gain **+5 to Saving Throws** against the **Fear**, **Charm**, and **Mental** conditions or effects.
- If an ally is targeted by a Fear, Charm, or Mental effect while already inside the aura, the **+5 bonus** is applied to their Saving Throw.
- An ally that enters the aura while suffering from one of these effects may immediately attempt **one new Saving Throw** to end a single qualifying effect.
- This Saving Throw gains **+2** *(half the aura's normal bonus, rounded down)*.
- This reroll may only be attempted **once per aura activation** for each creature.
- Multiple **Aura of Courage** effects do not stack. If affected by more than one, only the strongest version applies.

### Lore

By the time a Paladin reaches this level of mastery, their presence has become a sanctuary for the minds and hearts of those around them. Countless tales tell of warriors standing firm against eldritch horrors, ancient dragons, and master enchanters simply because they refused to leave the reassuring light of a Paladin's aura.

Many theologians believe this is where divine courage transcends emotion and becomes something greater—a sacred certainty that cannot easily be shaken. Those who have fought beside such champions often recall feeling as though no darkness, however terrible, could truly reach them while they remained within the Paladin's light.

> *"Darkness first seeks to conquer the mind. Stand within the light, and let your resolve answer instead."*  
> **— Saint Aurelia, Keeper of the Eternal Flame**

## Rank 5
### Description

Your courage has become a divine sanctuary upon the battlefield. Those who fight beside you stand beneath the protection of an unwavering spirit, their resolve fortified by the sacred conviction that radiates from your very soul. Terror, manipulation and despair struggle to take hold against those sheltered within your presence.

Your aura is no longer simply a source of inspiration—it is a bastion against the forces that seek to break the will. Allies who remain within your light find themselves standing firm against horrors that would send entire armies fleeing, while those who return to your protection after succumbing to darkness are given one final opportunity to reclaim themselves.

Among the greatest Paladins, Aura of Courage is remembered as the defining symbol of their leadership. It is said that when such champions stand their ground, hope itself refuses to retreat.

### Effects

- Spend **455 MP** to activate **Aura of Courage**.
- Create a **15 metre radius Aura** centred on yourself for **8 Rounds**.
- The aura moves with you.
- Allies already within the aura have **Advantage on Saving Throws** against the **Fear**, **Charm**, and **Mental** conditions or effects.
- If an ally is targeted by a qualifying effect while already inside the aura, they make the Saving Throw with **Advantage**.
- An ally that enters the aura while already suffering one of these effects may immediately attempt **one new Saving Throw** to end a single qualifying effect.
- This Saving Throw gains **+4**.
- This reroll may only be attempted **once per aura activation** for each creature.
- Multiple **Aura of Courage** effects do not stack. If affected by more than one, only the strongest version applies.

### Lore

The oldest legends speak of Paladins whose courage burned so brightly that despair itself could not enter their presence. Their companions marched into impossible battles without hesitation, not because they believed victory was certain, but because they knew they would never face the darkness alone.

To stand within the Aura of Courage is to be reminded of every oath sworn, every sacrifice made, and every reason worth fighting for. In those moments, fear becomes merely another obstacle to overcome, and hope becomes as tangible as the shield held before you.

> *"Hope is not a feeling. It is the courage to stand when every voice tells you to flee."*  
> **— Saint Aurelian, First Warden of the Radiant Host**
