# Divine Sense

## Overview
Through unwavering devotion, a Paladin learns to quiet the distractions of the physical world and listen instead to the whispers of the divine. By opening their soul to their patron, they briefly perceive the unseen spiritual currents that flow through the world, revealing sacred presences and forces that stand in direct opposition to their deity's will.

Divine Sense is not ordinary sight, nor does it reveal every hidden creature or secret. Instead, it awakens a deeper awareness that allows a Paladin to recognise the subtle resonance of divine power. Holy places, relics, miracles and supernatural beings leave impressions upon the world that only those blessed by a patron can perceive.

As your connection to your patron deepens, so too will your understanding of these spiritual echoes. What begins as a faint awareness will eventually become the ability to discern the true nature of divine and opposing forces that shape the world around you.

## Rank 1
### Description

Through unwavering devotion, a Paladin learns to quiet the distractions of the physical world and listen instead to the whispers of the divine. By opening their soul to their patron, they briefly perceive the unseen spiritual currents that flow through the world, revealing sacred presences and forces that stand in direct opposition to their deity's will.

### Effects

- Spend **25 MP** to activate **Divine Sense**.
- Divine Sense remains active for **1 Minute (10 Rounds)**.
- Sense the presence of **Divine Resonance** associated with your patron deity within **10 metres**.
- Sense the presence of **Divine Opposition** associated with your patron deity within **10 metres**.
- Divine Sense reveals only the **presence** of these forces and does not reveal their exact location, identity, strength, or source.
- While Divine Sense is active, gain **+2 to Perception Checks** made to investigate or locate sensed divine presences.
- Powerful magical barriers, divine wards, or abilities specifically designed to conceal spiritual signatures may prevent Divine Sense from detecting a target at the GM's discretion.
- The creatures, magic, relics, locations, and phenomena detected by **Divine Resonance** and **Divine Opposition** are determined by your chosen patron's Religious pages.

### Lore

Every deity leaves a unique imprint upon creation. Those who devote themselves to a patron gradually learn to recognise that sacred resonance, just as they instinctively perceive the opposing forces that seek to challenge or corrupt it. To a trained Paladin, these spiritual echoes are as unmistakable as a familiar voice carried upon the wind.

The oldest holy orders teach that Divine Sense is the first true step toward becoming a champion of one's faith. Before a Paladin can confront the enemies of their patron, they must first learn to recognise the subtle signs of divine influence that surround them, whether they come from friend or foe.

> *"The divine speaks long before steel is drawn. Those who learn to listen will never truly walk blind."*  
> **— High Oracle Seraphis, Keeper of the Sacred Vigil**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your connection to your patron grows stronger, allowing you to perceive more than a simple spiritual presence. As your awareness deepens, the countless echoes of divine energy begin to separate into distinct signatures, enabling you to recognise when multiple sources are nearby instead of sensing only that something exists.

The world becomes layered with unseen currents of divine influence. While ordinary people pass through sacred and opposing powers unaware, your patron grants you the insight to distinguish individual spiritual presences hidden beneath the surface of reality. Though the details remain beyond your understanding, you can begin to judge the scale of what surrounds you.

Veteran Paladins describe this stage as learning to hear individual voices within a crowded room. The divine still speaks in whispers, but those whispers are no longer impossible to separate.

### Effects

- Spend **30 MP** to activate **Divine Sense**.
- Divine Sense remains active for **2 Minutes (20 Rounds)**.
- Sense the presence of **Divine Resonance** associated with your patron deity within **15 metres**.
- Sense the presence of **Divine Opposition** associated with your patron deity within **15 metres**.
- Determine the **number of distinct divine presences** detected within range.
- Divine Sense does not reveal the exact location, identity, strength, or source of detected presences.
- Gain **+4 to Perception Checks** made to investigate or locate sensed divine presences while Divine Sense is active.
- Powerful magical barriers, divine wards, or abilities specifically designed to conceal spiritual signatures may prevent Divine Sense from detecting a target at the GM's discretion.
- The creatures, relics, magic, locations, and phenomena detected through **Divine Resonance** and **Divine Opposition** are determined by your chosen patron's Religious pages.

### Lore

As devotion matures into unwavering faith, a Paladin begins to perceive the harmony and discord that exist between divine powers. No longer overwhelmed by the countless echoes surrounding them, they learn to distinguish one sacred presence from another, revealing a richer understanding of the unseen world.

Within the great religious orders, this level of mastery marks the point at which initiates are trusted to investigate sacred sites and spiritual disturbances without constant guidance. Their patron's voice has become clearer, allowing them to recognise not merely that something is present, but that many unseen forces may be moving together beneath the veil.

> *"The divine rarely speaks alone. Learn to count the voices before you choose which one to follow."*  
> **— High Oracle Seraphis, Keeper of the Sacred Vigil**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your soul has become finely attuned to your patron's divine influence, allowing you to perceive not only the existence of sacred and opposing spiritual forces, but also the direction from which they emanate. What once felt like distant whispers now forms a clearer path for your spirit to follow.

As your awareness deepens, the divine currents surrounding the world become easier to navigate. You can distinguish whether a presence lies ahead, behind, above, or below, allowing you to pursue hidden relics, uncover concealed sanctuaries, or prepare for encounters with opposing divine forces before they reveal themselves.

Although the finer details remain hidden, your patron's guidance grows stronger with every step. Divine Sense begins to transform from a simple warning into a trusted companion, leading faithful champions toward both sacred discoveries and dangerous confrontations.

### Effects

- Spend **35 MP** to activate **Divine Sense**.
- Divine Sense remains active for **3 Minutes (30 Rounds)**.
- Sense the presence of **Divine Resonance** associated with your patron deity within **20 metres**.
- Sense the presence of **Divine Opposition** associated with your patron deity within **20 metres**.
- Determine the **number of distinct divine presences** detected within range.
- Determine the **general direction** of each detected presence.
- Discern whether each presence is **Near**, **Moderate**, or **Distant** relative to your current position.
- Divine Sense still does not reveal the identity, exact location, or strength of detected presences.
- Gain **+6 to Perception Checks** made to investigate or locate sensed divine presences while Divine Sense is active.
- Powerful magical barriers, divine wards, or abilities specifically designed to conceal spiritual signatures may prevent Divine Sense from detecting a target at the GM's discretion.
- The creatures, relics, magic, locations, and phenomena detected through **Divine Resonance** and **Divine Opposition** are determined by your chosen patron's Religious pages.

### Lore

The greatest Paladins often describe the world as a tapestry woven from countless unseen threads of divine influence. With experience, those threads become easier to follow, guiding faithful champions toward forgotten temples, hidden relics, and the growing presence of forces that oppose their patron's divine purpose.

Religious scholars believe this marks the moment a Paladin ceases to simply perceive the divine and instead begins to navigate by it. Many legendary journeys have begun with nothing more than a quiet whisper carried upon the soul, faithfully followed until destiny revealed itself.

> *"The divine does not point with a finger. It whispers, and the faithful learn which way to walk."*  
> **— High Oracle Seraphis, Keeper of the Sacred Vigil**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your bond with your patron has matured into a profound spiritual awareness. The divine echoes surrounding the world no longer appear as indistinct whispers but as recognisable signatures, each carrying its own strength and purpose. Your soul instinctively interprets these impressions, allowing you to better understand the unseen forces shaping the world around you.

Where younger Paladins merely sense that something sacred or opposing is nearby, you begin to distinguish the nature of each presence. Ancient relics resonate differently from living champions, while consecrated temples and corrupted ground each leave their own unmistakable imprint upon the divine tapestry.

Although many mysteries still remain hidden, your patron grants you enough insight to judge which discoveries deserve caution, reverence, or immediate action.

### Effects

- Spend **40 MP** to activate **Divine Sense**.
- Divine Sense remains active for **5 Minutes (50 Rounds)**.
- Sense the presence of **Divine Resonance** associated with your patron deity within **25 metres**.
- Sense the presence of **Divine Opposition** associated with your patron deity within **25 metres**.
- Determine the **number**, **general direction**, and **relative proximity** of each detected presence.
- Discern the **relative strength** of each presence as **Faint**, **Moderate**, **Powerful**, or **Overwhelming**.
- Determine whether each detected presence originates from a **creature**, **relic**, **location**, **divine phenomenon**, or **active magical effect**, though its exact identity remains unknown.
- Gain **+8 to Perception Checks** made to investigate or locate sensed divine presences while Divine Sense is active.
- Powerful magical barriers, divine wards, or abilities specifically designed to conceal spiritual signatures may prevent Divine Sense from detecting a target at the GM's discretion.
- The creatures, relics, magic, locations, and phenomena detected through **Divine Resonance** and **Divine Opposition** are determined by your chosen patron's Religious pages.

### Lore

Only the most devoted champions reach the point where divine echoes become distinct voices. Experienced Paladins learn that every sacred relic, every consecrated sanctuary, and every being touched by divine power leaves behind a unique resonance upon the world. To the faithful, these impressions are as recognisable as a familiar face.

Masters of Divine Sense are often entrusted with recovering lost relics, investigating miraculous events, and uncovering hidden threats that would escape even the keenest mortal senses. Their patron's guidance has become more than intuition—it has become a trusted companion upon every journey.

> *"The divine leaves its mark upon everything it touches. Wisdom lies in learning how to read those marks."*  
> **— High Oracle Seraphis, Keeper of the Sacred Vigil**

## Rank 5
### Description

Your soul has become completely attuned to the divine will of your patron. No longer limited to sensing faint echoes, you briefly perceive the spiritual tapestry that binds the world together, allowing you to witness the true resonance and opposition surrounding you.

Sacred places blaze like stars within your awareness, while opposing divine forces stand out as undeniable distortions against your patron's design. Even hidden relics, disguised champions, and concealed manifestations struggle to remain unseen beneath your divine gaze. Although your patron never grants perfect omniscience, few spiritual secrets can remain hidden from one who has reached this level of devotion.

Masters of Divine Sense are often called the Eyes of the Faith. They are entrusted with uncovering forgotten sanctuaries, locating divine relics, exposing corrupted holy sites, and recognising those who secretly serve powers beyond mortal understanding.

### Effects

- Spend **50 MP** to activate **Divine Sense**.
- Divine Sense remains active for **10 Minutes (100 Rounds)**.
- Sense the presence of **Divine Resonance** associated with your patron deity within **30 metres**.
- Sense the presence of **Divine Opposition** associated with your patron deity within **30 metres**.
- Determine the **number**, **general direction**, **relative proximity**, and **relative strength** of every detected presence.
- Determine whether each presence originates from a **creature**, **relic**, **location**, **divine phenomenon**, or **active magical effect**.
- Reveal the **approximate location** of each detected presence within your sensing range.
- Hidden, invisible, disguised, or magically concealed divine presences are revealed unless protected by an exceptionally powerful divine artifact, deity, or magical effect, at the GM's discretion.
- Gain **+10 to Perception Checks** made to investigate or locate sensed divine presences while Divine Sense is active.
- The creatures, relics, magic, locations, and phenomena detected through **Divine Resonance** and **Divine Opposition** are determined by your chosen patron's Religious pages.

### Lore

Among every holy order, only a handful of Paladins ever master Divine Sense to this degree. Those who do are said to walk with one foot in the mortal realm and the other within the divine, perceiving spiritual truths hidden from all but the gods themselves.

Legends tell of champions who uncovered forgotten temples buried beneath mountains, exposed kings secretly serving rival deities, and located sacred relics thought lost for centuries. Whether these tales are fact or embellishment matters little, for every order agrees upon one truth: those who attain this gift become living instruments of their patron's will, seeing the world through eyes forever changed by divine purpose.

> *"When the veil is lifted, the world reveals what it has always been. Only the faithful possess the courage to truly see it."*  
> **— Saint Asterion, First Divine Seeker**
