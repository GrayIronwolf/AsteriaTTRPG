# Lay on Hands

## Overview
The divine power entrusted to a Paladin is not reserved solely for battle. Through unwavering faith and a compassionate heart, you learn to channel your patron's blessing directly through your hands, mending wounds and easing suffering with a simple touch.

When you place your hand upon another creature, divine energy flows from your soul into theirs. Flesh begins to mend, pain subsides, and the spark of life is strengthened. Though the miracle is modest at first, every act of healing reflects the sacred bond between you and your patron.

Lay on Hands is more than a healing technique—it is a symbol of a Paladin's purpose. Whether tending to a wounded ally after battle or saving a life in the heat of combat, your touch reminds those around you that hope still exists even in the darkest moments.

## Rank 1
### Description

The divine power entrusted to a Paladin is not reserved solely for battle. Through unwavering faith and a compassionate heart, you learn to channel your patron's blessing directly through your hands, mending wounds and easing suffering with a simple touch.

When you place your hand upon another creature, divine energy flows from your soul into theirs. Flesh begins to mend, pain subsides, and the spark of life is strengthened. Though the miracle is modest at first, every act of healing reflects the sacred bond between you and your patron.

Lay on Hands is more than a healing technique—it is a symbol of a Paladin's purpose. Whether tending to a wounded ally after battle or saving a life in the heat of combat, your touch reminds those around you that hope still exists even in the darkest moments.

### Effects

- Spend **25 MP** to channel divine energy through your touch.
- Target one willing creature or yourself within **Touch** range.
- Restore **2d8 + your Charisma Modifier** HP.
- Lay on Hands has no effect on creatures that cannot naturally benefit from divine healing, as determined by the GM.
- A creature may only benefit from **Lay on Hands** once per round.
- This ability does not restore lost limbs, cure permanent injuries, or remove magical conditions unless another ability specifically allows it.
- If your patron grants additional healing benefits through its Religious pages, apply those effects after the healing is resolved.

### Lore

Across every holy order, the first miracle taught to aspiring Paladins is not how to smite their enemies, but how to preserve life. Masters believe that one who cannot value the lives of others has no right to wield divine judgement.

Stories tell of weary knights walking battlefields long after the fighting had ended, offering healing to friend and stranger alike. While their names may fade with time, those they saved carried their kindness forward, proving that even the smallest act of compassion can echo through history.

> *"A sword may end a battle, but a healing hand ensures someone lives to see tomorrow."*  
> **— Saint Elyra, First Hand of Mercy**

## Rank 2
### Description

As your devotion deepens, the divine energy flowing through your hands becomes stronger and more refined. No longer limited to closing wounds, your blessing is capable of reigniting fading life, offering hope to those standing at death's door.

Veteran Paladins learn that true strength is measured not by the foes they defeat, but by the lives they preserve. In the chaos of battle, a single touch can restore an ally's resolve, allowing them to rise once more and continue fighting beside their companions.

Although your gift has grown, it remains an expression of your patron's compassion rather than limitless power. Every healing miracle is a sacred responsibility, reminding the Paladin that divine power exists to protect and preserve before it is ever used to judge.

### Effects

- Spend **35 MP** to channel divine energy through your touch.
- Target one willing creature or yourself within **Touch** range.
- Restore **4d8 + your Charisma Modifier** HP.
- If the target is **Dying**, they immediately become **Stabilised** before healing is applied.
- If the target is unconscious due to reaching **0 HP**, they regain consciousness after receiving healing.
- A creature may only benefit from **Lay on Hands** once per round.
- Lay on Hands does not restore lost limbs, cure permanent injuries, or remove conditions unless another ability specifically allows it.
- If your patron grants additional healing benefits through its Religious pages, apply those effects after the healing is resolved.

### Lore

Within every Paladin Order, this level of mastery is regarded as the first true miracle. To pull a companion back from the brink of death is considered one of the highest expressions of faith, demonstrating absolute trust between champion and patron.

Many tales recount Paladins kneeling amidst ruined battlefields, laying a single hand upon a fallen ally before watching life return where moments earlier there had been only silence. Such miracles remind both friend and foe alike that hope endures wherever divine compassion is carried.

> *"Life is the greatest gift entrusted to us. To preserve it is the first duty of every faithful hand."*  
> **— Saint Elyra, First Hand of Mercy**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your patron's blessing now flows through your hands with remarkable purity, restoring not only flesh but the vitality of the body itself. Wounds knit together with greater speed, while harmful ailments begin to fade beneath the overwhelming presence of divine power.

Seasoned Paladins understand that not every battle is won with steel alone. Venoms, diseases and lingering injuries can claim as many lives as any weapon, and so your sacred gift evolves to combat these hidden threats. A single touch can now relieve suffering that would otherwise burden an adventurer long after the battle has ended.

As your mastery grows, Lay on Hands becomes recognised as one of the greatest symbols of a Paladin's compassion. It is a miracle that preserves life, restores hope, and reminds the faithful that even in suffering, the divine has not abandoned them.

### Effects

- Spend **45 MP** to channel divine energy through your touch.
- Target one willing creature or yourself within **Touch** range.
- Restore **6d8 + your Charisma Modifier** HP.
- If the target is **Dying**, they immediately become **Stabilised** before healing is applied.
- If the target is unconscious due to reaching **0 HP**, they regain consciousness after receiving healing.
- Choose **one** of the following conditions affecting the target to immediately remove:
  - **Bleeding**
  - **Poisoned**
  - **Diseased**
- The GM may rule that particularly powerful poisons or diseases require a higher rank of **Lay on Hands** or additional treatment.
- A creature may only benefit from **Lay on Hands** once per round.
- If your patron grants additional restorative blessings through its Religious pages, apply those effects after all healing and restoration have been resolved.

### Lore

To the faithful, this level of mastery marks the moment a Paladin truly becomes a guardian of life. Their touch no longer merely closes wounds—it restores the body's natural harmony, allowing it to cast off afflictions that would cripple ordinary mortals.

Throughout Asteria, stories are told of Paladins walking plague-ridden villages, tending poisoned adventurers, and saving companions who had long since been given up for dead. Such miracles are remembered not because they display overwhelming power, but because they embody the compassion that lies at the heart of every sacred oath.

> *"Mercy is the greatest miracle. Healing is simply the form it chooses to take."*  
> **— Saint Elyra, First Hand of Mercy**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your patron's blessing now flows through you as a true divine miracle. Your touch no longer simply heals wounds—it restores the body to the state it was meant to be. Broken flesh mends, lingering afflictions are driven from the soul, and those suffering from grievous injuries find renewed strength through your unwavering faith.

Veteran Paladins are often sought long after battles have ended, for they possess the ability to heal suffering beyond the reach of ordinary medicine. Venoms that would claim kingdoms, illnesses that have resisted every remedy, and debilitating injuries that would end a warrior's career all yield before the divine power flowing through your hands.

To wield such a gift is considered one of the highest responsibilities bestowed upon a Paladin. Every miracle reminds the faithful that divine compassion is measured not by power alone, but by the lives restored through its grace.

### Effects

- Spend **60 MP** to channel divine energy through your touch.
- Target one willing creature or yourself within **Touch** range.
- Restore **8d8 + your Charisma Modifier** HP.
- If the target is **Dying**, they immediately become **Stabilised** before healing is applied.
- If the target is unconscious due to reaching **0 HP**, they regain consciousness after receiving healing.
- Choose up to **two** of the following conditions affecting the target to immediately remove:
  - **Bleeding**
  - **Poisoned**
  - **Diseased**
  - **Blinded**
  - **Deafened**
  - **Paralysed**
- Mend broken bones, torn muscles, and other serious physical injuries at the GM's discretion.
- Powerful magical diseases, legendary poisons, divine afflictions, and powerful curses may require **Lay on Hands — Rank V** or another specialised ability.
- A creature may only benefit from **Lay on Hands** once per round.
- If your patron grants additional restorative blessings through its Religious pages, apply those effects after all healing and restoration have been resolved.

### Lore

Among the greatest holy orders, Paladins who master this blessing become known as Living Sanctuaries. Entire villages celebrate their arrival, for their touch has ended plagues, restored broken champions, and returned hope where despair had taken root.

Though many marvel at the miracles performed by these champions, every Paladin understands that the gift is not truly their own. They are merely the hands through which their patron chooses to extend compassion into the mortal world.

> *"Power may win a battle, but mercy ensures there is still a world worth protecting when the battle is over."*  
> **— Saint Elyra, First Hand of Mercy**

## Rank 5
### Description

Your hands have become a true conduit for your patron's divine grace. Through unwavering faith and absolute devotion, you perform miracles that few mortals will ever witness. Wounds that should prove fatal are restored, devastating afflictions are washed away, and broken bodies are renewed through the overwhelming power entrusted to you.

Those who master Lay on Hands are revered across Asteria as living instruments of divine mercy. Kings seek their counsel, armies march beneath their blessing, and entire communities celebrate their arrival, knowing that hope itself walks beside them. Though your gift borders upon the miraculous, every act of healing serves as a reminder that true power exists not to conquer, but to preserve life.

Even at its pinnacle, this blessing is not limitless. Some curses, divine judgements, and god-forged afflictions lie beyond the reach of mortal champions, requiring direct divine intervention or extraordinary relics to overcome. Such mysteries remind every Paladin that they are not the source of the miracle—they are simply its faithful vessel.

### Effects

- Spend **80 MP** to channel your patron's divine power through your touch.
- Target one willing creature or yourself within **Touch** range.
- Restore **10d8 + your Charisma Modifier** HP.
- If the target is **Dying**, they immediately become **Stabilised** before healing is applied.
- If the target is unconscious due to reaching **0 HP**, they regain consciousness after receiving healing.
- Remove **all Minor and Major Physical Conditions** affecting the target.
- Remove **all Minor and Major Diseases, Poisons, and Natural Afflictions** affecting the target.
- Remove **one Lesser or Greater Curse, Divine Affliction, or Magical Ailment**, provided it does not exceed the limits determined by the GM or your patron's Religious pages.
- Restore severe physical injuries, including broken bones, damaged organs, and other debilitating wounds, at the GM's discretion.
- A creature may only benefit from **Lay on Hands** once per round.
- If your patron grants additional restorative blessings through its Religious pages, apply those effects after all healing and restoration have been resolved.

### Lore

Throughout history, only a handful of Paladins have ever reached this level of mastery. Their names are remembered not for the battles they won, but for the countless lives they preserved when all hope had been lost. Their touch became synonymous with mercy itself, a reminder that even amidst endless conflict, compassion remains the greatest expression of divine power.

Legends tell of champions who cleansed cursed kingdoms, restored heroes thought beyond saving, and stood beside the dying until the light of life returned to their eyes. Whether these tales have grown with time or remain entirely true is known only to the gods, but every holy order agrees upon one truth: to wield such a miracle is the highest honour a Paladin can receive.

> *"The greatest miracle is not that life can be restored, but that someone chose to preserve it."*  
> **— Saint Elyra, First Hand of Mercy**
