# Paladin's Challenge

## Overview
A Paladin is more than a warrior clad in heavy armour—they are the shield that stands between the innocent and those who would bring them harm. Through unwavering conviction and absolute faith, a Paladin can issue a sacred challenge that demands the attention of friend and foe alike.

When you invoke **Paladin's Challenge**, you openly declare yourself as the greatest threat upon the battlefield. Your voice carries the authority of your oath, compelling enemies to acknowledge your presence and confront you before those who cannot defend themselves. It is not an enchantment that dominates the mind, but a divine proclamation that weighs heavily upon the conscience and instincts of those who hear it.

Experienced Paladins understand that protection is not achieved by simply enduring punishment—it begins by ensuring the enemy's focus remains where it belongs. Every successful challenge allows your allies the opportunity to reposition, recover, or strike while you hold the front line against overwhelming odds.

## Rank 1
### Description

A Paladin is more than a warrior clad in heavy armour—they are the shield that stands between the innocent and those who would bring them harm. Through unwavering conviction and absolute faith, a Paladin can issue a sacred challenge that demands the attention of friend and foe alike.

When you invoke **Paladin's Challenge**, you openly declare yourself as the greatest threat upon the battlefield. Your voice carries the authority of your oath, compelling enemies to acknowledge your presence and confront you before those who cannot defend themselves. It is not an enchantment that dominates the mind, but a divine proclamation that weighs heavily upon the conscience and instincts of those who hear it.

Experienced Paladins understand that protection is not achieved by simply enduring punishment—it begins by ensuring the enemy's focus remains where it belongs. Every successful challenge allows your allies the opportunity to reposition, recover, or strike while you hold the front line against overwhelming odds.

### Effects

- Spend **20 MP** to issue a **Divine Challenge**.
- Choose one creature within **15 metres** that can see or hear you.
- The target must make a **Wisdom Saving Throw**.
- **Saving Throw DC = 10 + your Charisma Modifier + your Proficiency Bonus.**
- On a failed save, the target becomes **Challenged** for **2 Rounds**.
- A **Challenged** creature treats you as its primary combat target whenever it is reasonable to do so.
- A **Challenged** creature suffers **−2 to Attack Rolls** made against creatures other than you.
- The challenge immediately ends if you are reduced to **0 HP**.
- A creature may ignore the challenge if you become unreachable or another ability forces it to target a different creature.
- Bosses, Legendary creatures, and creatures of a significantly higher Threat Tier may gain Advantage on their Saving Throw at the GM's discretion.
- Your chosen patron's **Divine Authority** may grant additional bonuses against creatures that oppose its divine domain. See your patron's **Divine Authority** page for details.

### Lore

Among the knightly orders of Asteria, the Divine Challenge is considered a sacred promise rather than a battle cry. By speaking these words, a Paladin willingly places themselves between danger and those under their protection, accepting whatever consequences follow.

Veterans often teach that courage is not measured by the number of enemies defeated, but by the willingness to stand before impossible odds without yielding. To answer a Paladin's challenge is to confront not only the warrior before you, but the unwavering conviction of the divine power that guides them.

> *"If your hatred seeks the innocent, then let it first break itself against my shield."*  
> **— Sir Garrick Dawnshield, High Protector of the Argent Bastion**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your conviction grows stronger, carrying your challenge beyond a single foe. Through steadfast faith and commanding presence, you draw the attention of multiple enemies, placing yourself at the centre of the battle and giving your allies the opportunity to act without constant pressure.

A seasoned Paladin understands that protecting others is not achieved through brute strength alone. It requires absolute confidence, an unbreakable will, and the courage to become the enemy's greatest obstacle. Those who answer your challenge find it increasingly difficult to ignore the warrior who stands before them, shield raised and oath unbroken.

Every successful challenge reinforces your role as the defender of your companions, allowing you to dictate the flow of combat through unwavering resolve rather than fear or domination.

- A **Challenged** creature suffers **−4 to Attack Rolls** made against creatures other than you.

- Apply any additional bonuses granted by your patron's **Divine Authority**, if applicable.

### Lore

As Paladins gain experience, their presence alone becomes enough to command the attention of those around them. Their voice carries the weight of countless vows upheld and countless battles survived, causing even disciplined warriors to hesitate before looking past them.

Among the great knightly orders, mastering the challenge of multiple foes is considered a defining milestone. It marks the moment a Paladin ceases to be merely a protector of individuals and becomes the steadfast guardian of the battlefield itself.

> *"A shield protects one life. A true Paladin protects everyone standing behind it."*  
> **— Dame Elowyn, Captain of the Silver Wardens**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your divine authority has become impossible to ignore. Your challenge now echoes across the battlefield, drawing the attention of multiple enemies and forcing them to confront the living embodiment of your oath. Those who attempt to disregard your presence find their resolve shaken as the weight of your conviction bears down upon them.

Veteran Paladins understand that a challenge is more than a declaration of courage—it is a promise. You vow to stand between danger and those under your protection, and your enemies instinctively recognise that promise. While they may resist your authority, every moment spent ignoring you becomes increasingly difficult, allowing your allies to fight under the shelter of your unwavering resolve.

### Effects

- Spend **40 MP** to issue a **Divine Challenge**.
- Choose up to **3 creatures** within **20 metres** that can see or hear you.
- Each target must make a **Wisdom Saving Throw**.
- **Saving Throw DC = 10 + your Charisma Modifier + your Proficiency Bonus.**
- On a failed save, the target becomes **Challenged** for **3 Rounds**.
- A **Challenged** creature treats you as its primary combat target whenever it is reasonable to do so.
- A **Challenged** creature suffers **−6 to Attack Rolls** made against creatures other than you.
- If a **Challenged** creature willingly attacks another target while you are a valid target, it suffers **Radiant damage equal to your Charisma Modifier** immediately after resolving its attack.
- The challenge immediately ends for a creature if you are reduced to **0 HP**.
- A creature may ignore the challenge if you become unreachable or another effect forces it to attack a different target.
- Bosses, Legendary creatures and creatures of a significantly higher Threat Tier may gain Advantage on their Saving Throw at the GM's discretion.
- Apply any additional bonuses granted by your patron's **Divine Authority**, if applicable.

### Lore

The greatest defenders are remembered not for the enemies they slew, but for the lives they preserved. A seasoned Paladin's challenge is no longer merely heard—it is felt. Those who witness it often describe an overwhelming certainty that the warrior before them cannot simply be ignored.

Many scriptures describe this as the moment a Paladin truly becomes a guardian. Their presence alone changes the course of battle, compelling allies to stand taller while forcing their enemies to confront the consequences of misplaced aggression.

> *"Ignore my warning if you must. The light will remember your choice."*  
> **— High Justicar Alaric, Keeper of the Sacred Bastion**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your divine presence has become impossible to ignore. Rather than issuing a challenge to individual foes, your conviction radiates across the battlefield, demanding the attention of every enemy that dares approach.

To stand before a veteran Paladin is to face the living embodiment of their oath. Every strike against an ally becomes a conscious rejection of your divine authority, while every step toward you is met with unwavering resolve. Even disciplined warriors find themselves instinctively drawn toward the greatest threat—the steadfast protector standing before them.

Your challenge no longer protects a single companion. It establishes a defensive bastion wherever you stand, allowing your allies to fight beneath the shelter of your conviction.

### Effects

- Spend **50 MP** to issue a **Divine Challenge**.
- All hostile creatures within a **10 metre radius** that can see or hear you must make a **Wisdom Saving Throw**.
- **Saving Throw DC = 10 + your Charisma Modifier + your Proficiency Bonus.**
- On a failed save, a creature becomes **Challenged** for **3 Rounds**.
- Challenged creatures treat you as their primary combat target whenever it is reasonable to do so.
- Challenged creatures suffer **−6 to Attack Rolls** made against creatures other than you.
- If a Challenged creature willingly attacks another target while you are a valid target, it suffers **Radiant Damage equal to twice your Charisma Modifier** after its attack resolves.
- Challenged creatures cannot willingly move further away from you unless forced by another effect or they succeed on a new Wisdom Saving Throw at the end of their turn.
- The challenge immediately ends for a creature if you are reduced to **0 HP**.
- Bosses, Legendary creatures and creatures of a significantly higher Threat Tier may gain Advantage on their Saving Throw at the GM's discretion.
- Apply any additional bonuses granted by your patron's **Divine Authority**, if applicable.

### Lore

There comes a point where a Paladin no longer needs to seek the attention of evil—evil instinctively recognises them. Veterans of countless campaigns speak of battlefields falling strangely silent as a single knight steps forward, shield raised, daring every foe to pass.

Those who have witnessed such champions often describe the experience as standing before an immovable fortress. Their conviction is so absolute that even the most bloodthirsty monsters find their instincts drawn toward the greatest obstacle before them, unable to ignore the guardian who refuses to yield.

> *"Stand behind me and fear nothing. Stand before me and fear everything."*  
> **— Grandmaster Roland Ashbourne, First Shield of the Dawn**

## Rank 5
### Description

Your challenge has become a divine command that echoes across the battlefield. The strength of your conviction radiates from your very presence, compelling enemies to acknowledge you as the greatest obstacle between them and their quarry.

Those who stand beside you fight beneath the protection of an unbreakable oath, while those who oppose you feel the crushing weight of divine judgement. Though creatures still possess the freedom to resist your challenge, few can ignore the unwavering authority carried by a legendary Paladin.

Masters of this technique are remembered as living bulwarks. Entire battle lines have broken against their shields, not because they were impossible to defeat, but because no enemy could simply walk past them without paying a terrible price.

### Effects

- Spend **60 MP** to issue a **Divine Challenge**.
- All hostile creatures within a **15 metre radius** that can see or hear you must make a **Wisdom Saving Throw**.
- **Saving Throw DC = 10 + your Charisma Modifier + your Proficiency Bonus.**
- On a failed save, creatures become **Challenged** for **4 Rounds**.
- Challenged creatures treat you as their primary combat target whenever it is reasonable to do so.
- Challenged creatures suffer **−6 to Attack Rolls** made against creatures other than you.
- If a Challenged creature willingly attacks another target while you remain a valid target, it immediately suffers **Radiant Damage equal to twice your Charisma Modifier** after resolving its attack.
- Challenged creatures provoke an **Opportunity Attack** from you if they willingly move away from you while within your reach.
- A Challenged creature may only attempt a new Wisdom Saving Throw at the end of its turn if you become unreachable or another effect prevents it from engaging you.
- The challenge immediately ends if you are reduced to **0 HP**.
- Bosses, Legendary creatures and creatures of a significantly higher Threat Tier may gain Advantage on their initial Saving Throw at the GM's discretion.
- Apply any bonuses granted by your patron's **Divine Authority**, if applicable.

### Lore

Among the greatest champions of Asteria, a Paladin's Challenge is spoken of with reverence. It is said that when such a Paladin plants their feet upon the battlefield and issues their sacred declaration, even the bravest warriors instinctively turn their attention toward them.

The challenge is not born from arrogance, but from sacrifice. Every word is a promise that no ally shall face the darkness alone while the Paladin still stands. Those who witness such resolve often discover that true courage is not measured by the enemies one defeats, but by the burdens one willingly bears for others.

> *"Come then. Let every blade, every spell and every ounce of your hatred fall upon me. My oath was forged to carry it."*  
> **— Saint Garrick, First Shield of the Radiant Host**
