# Paladin's Charge

## Overview
A Paladin does not hesitate once judgement has been made.

Paladin's Charge is a disciplined burst of speed that allows you to rapidly close the distance between yourself and an enemy or objective. Rather than relying upon agility or trickery, you commit your entire body and conviction to a single, unwavering advance.

Once your charge begins there is no turning back. Your momentum carries you forward until you reach your destination, collide with an obstacle, or strike your target. Mastering this technique teaches every Paladin that commitment is a weapon in itself—one that can break enemy lines just as surely as any blade.

## Rank 1
### Description

A Paladin does not hesitate once judgement has been made.

Paladin's Charge is a disciplined burst of speed that allows you to rapidly close the distance between yourself and an enemy or objective. Rather than relying upon agility or trickery, you commit your entire body and conviction to a single, unwavering advance.

Once your charge begins there is no turning back. Your momentum carries you forward until you reach your destination, collide with an obstacle, or strike your target. Mastering this technique teaches every Paladin that commitment is a weapon in itself—one that can break enemy lines just as surely as any blade.

### Effects

- Spend **15 Stamina** to immediately charge up to **10 metres** in a straight line.
- The direction of the charge is chosen when activated.
- Once the charge begins you:
  - Cannot voluntarily stop.
  - Cannot change direction.
  - Continue until you reach the maximum distance, collide with terrain, or hit your chosen target.
- This movement does **not** provoke Opportunity Attacks from creatures you move past during the charge.

#### Impact

If your charge ends by colliding with an enemy, that creature must make an **Agility Saving Throw**.

**Save DC = 10 + Strength Modifier**

- **Success:** The target holds its ground.
- **Failure:** The target is pushed **2 metres** directly away from you.
- **Critical Failure:** The target is pushed **2 metres**, knocked **Prone**, and must spend movement to stand on its next turn.

Large or firmly anchored creatures may receive Advantage on this saving throw at the GM's discretion.

### Lore

Among the knightly orders of Asteria, recruits spend weeks learning that a charge is not merely running towards an enemy. It is an act of complete commitment. Doubt causes hesitation, hesitation weakens momentum, and lost momentum has claimed the lives of countless warriors.

Veteran Paladins are recognised by the confidence with which they advance. When they lower their shoulder and begin their charge, few stand willingly in their path. Even creatures many times their size learn that faith, discipline, and momentum can strike with astonishing force.

> *"When your conviction is true, every step becomes a weapon."*  
> — Sir Aldren, Commander of the Dawn Guard

## Rank 2
### Description

With experience comes confidence. Your charge is no longer a reckless rush, but a disciplined advance that carries greater force and purpose. Every step is measured, every impact deliberate, allowing you to cover greater ground while maintaining complete control over your momentum.

Seasoned Paladins learn that hesitation is the enemy of conviction. Once they commit to a charge, they become an unstoppable force until their objective is reached. Whether breaking an enemy's formation or crashing into a powerful foe, their momentum alone is enough to make even seasoned warriors brace for impact.

### Effects

- Spend **15 Stamina** to immediately charge up to **15 metres** in a straight line.
- The direction of the charge is chosen when activated.
- Once the charge begins you:
  - Cannot voluntarily stop.
  - Cannot change direction.
  - Continue until you reach the maximum distance, collide with terrain, or hit your chosen target.
- This movement does **not** provoke Opportunity Attacks from creatures you move past during the charge.
- Difficult Terrain does not reduce the movement distance of your charge.

#### Impact

If your charge ends by colliding with an enemy, that creature must make an **Agility Saving Throw**.

**Save DC = 10 + Strength Modifier + Proficiency Bonus**

- **Success:** The target holds its ground.
- **Failure:** The target is pushed **3 metres** directly away from you.
- **Critical Failure:** The target is pushed **3 metres**, knocked **Prone**, and must spend movement to stand on its next turn.

Creatures one or more size categories larger than you have Advantage on this saving throw unless otherwise stated by another ability.

### Lore

As Paladins mature in both faith and discipline, their charge becomes an extension of their conviction. Every stride carries the weight of unwavering purpose, driving them forward through mud, rubble, and fear alike.

Those who have stood before an experienced Paladin often recall the same feeling—a single heartbeat where the battlefield falls silent before an unstoppable wall of steel and determination crashes into them.

> *"Conviction carries farther than the strongest legs ever could."*  
> — Dame Elowyn, Knight-Captain of the Argent Bastion

## Rank 3
### Description

Your charge has become a symbol of unwavering resolve. Every step carries the weight of your conviction, allowing you to surge through difficult terrain and lesser obstacles without breaking your momentum. To those who stand against you, it is like watching a living battering ram blessed by the divine.

A seasoned Paladin understands that hesitation is defeat. Once the charge begins there is only one destination—the enemy before them. Whether leading the front line or breaking an entrenched defence, your momentum becomes as much a weapon as the blade in your hand.

### Effects

- Spend **20 Stamina** to immediately charge up to **20 metres** in a straight line.
- The direction of the charge is chosen when activated.
- Once the charge begins you:
  - Cannot voluntarily stop.
  - Cannot change direction.
  - Continue until you reach the maximum distance, collide with an immovable obstacle, or hit your chosen target.
- This movement does **not** provoke Opportunity Attacks.
- Your charge ignores **Difficult Terrain**.
- During the charge you may smash through:
  - Wooden furniture
  - Barricades
  - Doors
  - Other fragile environmental objects
  *(GM discretion.)*

#### Impact

If your charge ends by colliding with an enemy, that creature must make an **Agility Saving Throw**.

**Save DC = 10 + Strength Modifier + Proficiency Bonus**

- **Success:** The creature holds its ground.
- **Failure:** The creature is pushed **4 metres** directly away from you.
- **Critical Failure:** The creature is pushed **4 metres**, knocked **Prone**, and is **Staggered** until the end of its next turn.

**Staggered**
- Movement Speed is reduced by **50%**.
- The creature cannot take Reactions until the condition ends.

Creatures one or more size categories larger than you have Advantage on this saving throw unless otherwise stated by another ability.

### Lore

Every Paladin Order teaches that a charge is an act of faith before it is an act of war. The knight commits completely, trusting in discipline, conviction, and the strength granted by their oath. There can be no uncertainty once the first step is taken.

Veterans are remembered not for the battles they survived, but for the moments they shattered impossible odds with a single relentless advance. Entire shield walls have broken beneath the momentum of a determined Paladin, their enemies realising too late that the charge could never be stopped.

> *"Walls are built to halt armies. Conviction was never one of them."*  
> — High Marshal Edric of the Radiant Host

## Rank 4
### Description

Your charge becomes an overwhelming display of divine resolve. Few possess the strength to halt your advance, and those who stand before you are often thrown aside as your momentum carries you relentlessly toward your chosen destination.

To seasoned Paladins, the charge is no longer simply a means of closing the distance—it is a declaration. Every step announces the arrival of unwavering justice, breaking enemy formations and forcing even veteran warriors to reconsider holding their ground.

Those who attempt to stand against your advance quickly learn that conviction possesses a force all its own.

### Effects

- Spend **25 Stamina** to immediately charge up to **25 metres** in a straight line.
- The direction of the charge is chosen when activated.
- Once the charge begins you:
  - Cannot voluntarily stop.
  - Cannot change direction.
  - Continue until you reach your maximum distance or collide with an immovable obstacle.
- Your charge ignores **Difficult Terrain**.
- Your movement during the charge does **not** provoke Opportunity Attacks.
- You may smash through fragile objects such as wooden barricades, doors, fences and similar environmental obstacles (GM discretion).

#### Relentless Momentum

Your charge no longer ends after striking the first enemy.

Creatures that fail their Agility Saving Throw are knocked aside and your charge continues until:

- You reach your maximum distance.
- A creature successfully resists your charge.
- You collide with an immovable object.

#### Impact

Each enemy struck during the charge must make an **Agility Saving Throw**.

**Save DC = 10 + Strength Modifier + Proficiency Bonus**

- **Success:** The creature holds its ground and your charge immediately ends.
- **Failure:** The creature is pushed **5 metres** directly away from you as your charge continues.
- **Critical Failure:** The creature is pushed **5 metres**, knocked **Prone**, and becomes **Staggered** until the end of its next turn.

**Staggered**

- Movement Speed is reduced by **50%**.
- The creature cannot take Reactions until the condition ends.

Creatures one or more size categories larger than you have Advantage on this saving throw unless otherwise modified by another ability.

### Lore

Only the greatest Paladins ever master the Relentless Charge. To witness one is to see discipline transformed into unstoppable momentum, where neither fear nor resistance can slow their advance.

Entire battle lines have been broken by a single Paladin driving through the heart of an enemy formation, their unwavering conviction scattering soldiers before them like leaves before a storm. Veterans often say that by the time you hear the thunder of a charging Paladin, it is already too late to stand your ground.

> *"Justice does not ask for passage. It makes one."*  
> — Grand Commander Aurelius, Keeper of the Silver Host

## Rank 5
### Description

There comes a point where a Paladin's charge ceases to be simple movement and becomes an act of divine judgement. Every step echoes with unwavering conviction, every stride carrying the full weight of their oath toward those who would oppose it.

The battlefield itself seems to part before your advance. Lesser foes are scattered, obstacles crumble beneath your momentum, and only the strongest possess the resolve to stand against your charge. To witness such a charge is to witness absolute commitment—a warrior who has surrendered every hesitation in pursuit of justice.

Among the oldest knightly orders, reaching this level of mastery is considered one of the greatest honours a Paladin can achieve. Many legends begin with a single unstoppable charge.

### Effects

- Spend **30 Stamina** to immediately charge up to **30 metres** in a perfectly straight line.
- The direction is chosen when the ability is activated.
- Once the charge begins you:
  - Cannot voluntarily stop.
  - Cannot change direction.
  - Continue until reaching your maximum distance or colliding with an immovable object.
- Your charge ignores **Difficult Terrain**.
- Your movement during the charge does **not** provoke Opportunity Attacks.
- You may smash through fragile environmental objects such as doors, barricades, fences and similar obstacles (GM discretion).

#### Divine Momentum

Your charge continues through every creature that fails its Agility Saving Throw.

Your charge only ends when:

- You reach the maximum distance.
- You collide with an immovable object.
- A creature successfully resists your charge.

#### Impact

Each creature struck during your charge must make an **Agility Saving Throw**.

**Save DC = 10 + Strength Modifier + Proficiency Bonus**

- **Success**
  - The creature stands its ground.
  - Your charge immediately ends.

- **Failure**
  - The creature is pushed **6 metres** directly away from you.
  - Your charge continues.

- **Critical Failure**
  - The creature is pushed **6 metres**.
  - The creature is knocked **Prone**.
  - The creature becomes **Staggered** until the end of its next turn.

#### Divine Impact

If your charge ends by colliding with an enemy, your **next melee attack** against that creature before the end of your turn gains:

- **Advantage** on the attack roll.
- If that attack is **Divine Smite**, its Mana Cost is reduced by **20%** (rounded up).

### Lore

The greatest Paladins are remembered not for how many battles they fought, but for the moments when they broke impossible odds through unwavering conviction. Their charge is more than speed or strength—it is the physical manifestation of faith carried to its absolute conclusion.

Stories tell of champions who crashed through monstrous hordes, shattered fortress gates, and reached tyrants thought untouchable, all because they refused to let doubt interrupt their advance. Those who survive such a charge rarely remember the impact itself. They remember only the certainty with which it came.

> *"A charge without conviction is merely running. A charge with conviction becomes destiny."*  
> — Saint Garrick, First High Paladin
