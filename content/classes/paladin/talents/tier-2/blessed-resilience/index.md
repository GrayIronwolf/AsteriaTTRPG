# Blessed Resilience

## Overview
Your unwavering devotion has begun to transform both body and soul. Through your sacred oath, your patron's divine essence becomes a permanent part of your being, strengthening your resolve against the forces that seek to oppose your faith.

This blessing is not armour forged from steel, but a quiet resilience carried within your spirit. Each trial endured strengthens the bond between champion and patron, allowing you to withstand hardships that would break ordinary mortals. Though the blessing is subtle at first, its influence grows with every act of devotion.

Every deity grants resilience in a different form. Rather than shielding all Paladins equally, your patron fortifies you against the divine forces it understands best, preparing you to confront those that stand in opposition to its will.

## Rank 1
### Description

Your unwavering devotion has begun to transform both body and soul. Through your sacred oath, your patron's divine essence becomes a permanent part of your being, strengthening your resolve against the forces that seek to oppose your faith.

This blessing is not armour forged from steel, but a quiet resilience carried within your spirit. Each trial endured strengthens the bond between champion and patron, allowing you to withstand hardships that would break ordinary mortals. Though the blessing is subtle at first, its influence grows with every act of devotion.

Every deity grants resilience in a different form. Rather than shielding all Paladins equally, your patron fortifies you against the divine forces it understands best, preparing you to confront those that stand in opposition to its will.

### Effects

- Blessed Resilience is a **Passive Talent** and is always active.
- Gain **+2 on Saving Throws** against effects originating from your patron's **Divine Opposition**.
- Gain **Resistance** to one damage type or supernatural effect determined by your patron's Religious pages.
- Gain **+2 on checks or Saving Throws** made to resist conditions specifically associated with your patron's Divine Opposition.
- If multiple abilities would grant the same resistance, only the strongest applies unless another rule states otherwise.
- The exact resistances and protections granted by Blessed Resilience are determined by your patron's Religious pages.

### Lore

Every divine champion bears the mark of their patron, whether visible to the eye or hidden within the soul. Over time, this sacred bond reshapes the Paladin into someone capable of enduring hardships that would overwhelm lesser warriors. Their resilience is not born of stubbornness alone, but from the certainty that their patron walks beside them.

Among the great religious orders, seasoned Paladins often recognise one another by this quiet strength. Though they may serve different deities, each carries a resilience uniquely shaped by the divine power that chose them, standing firm against the very forces their patron opposes.

> *"Faith is not proven when life is easy. It is proven by the strength to endure what seeks to break you."*  
> **— High Warden Cassian, Keeper of the Eternal Oath**

## Rank 2
### Description

The bond between you and your patron grows stronger with every trial you endure. Divine power no longer simply shields you from harm—it begins to temper your body and spirit, allowing you to withstand the very forces your deity was created to oppose.

Every wound survived and every temptation resisted reinforces your sacred connection. Your patron's essence quietly shapes your resilience, ensuring that the powers most hostile to your divine purpose find it increasingly difficult to overcome your resolve. While others may falter beneath such forces, you remain steadfast, carrying your faith as both armour and sanctuary.

To experienced Paladins, this blessing is a constant reminder that resilience is not the absence of suffering, but the ability to endure it without abandoning one's oath. Their strength is drawn not from themselves alone, but from the eternal bond shared with the divine.

### Effects

- **Blessed Resilience** is a **Passive Talent** and is always active.
- Gain **+4 on Saving Throws** against effects originating from your patron's **Divine Opposition**.
- Gain **Resistance** to one or more damage types, conditions, or supernatural effects determined by your patron's Religious pages.
- Once per **Short Rest**, when you fail a Saving Throw against your patron's Divine Opposition, you may immediately reroll that Saving Throw. You must use the second result.
- Gain **+4 on checks or Saving Throws** made to resist conditions specifically associated with your patron's Divine Opposition.
- If multiple abilities would grant the same resistance, only the strongest applies unless another rule states otherwise.
- The exact resistances, protections, and additional blessings granted by **Blessed Resilience** are determined by your patron's Religious pages.

### Lore

As the years pass, the divine blessing carried by a Paladin becomes inseparable from their very being. Their patron's influence no longer answers only when called upon—it quietly strengthens them every moment of every day, preparing them for the inevitable confrontation with the powers that stand in opposition to their faith.

Many scholars believe that this resilience is the first outward sign of true divine favour. Though different patrons bestow different blessings, all share a common purpose: to ensure their chosen champion can endure the trials they were destined to face.

> *"Steel is forged through fire. Faith is forged through adversity."*  
> **— High Warden Cassian, Keeper of the Eternal Oath**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your patron's blessing has become deeply woven into your soul, transforming your resilience from simple endurance into steadfast divine protection. The forces that oppose your deity find it increasingly difficult to weaken your resolve, as your body and spirit instinctively reject influences that seek to break your sacred oath.

Every trial survived strengthens the divine bond between champion and patron. What once required conscious determination has become second nature, allowing you to endure hardships that would overwhelm even seasoned warriors. Whether facing corrupting magic, overwhelming divine power, or supernatural afflictions tied to your patron's eternal enemies, your faith stands as an unyielding shield.

To the greatest Paladins, resilience is not measured by how little they suffer, but by how often they rise again without allowing darkness to take hold. Their patron's blessing ensures that no matter how fierce the opposition becomes, their resolve remains unbroken.

### Effects

- **Blessed Resilience** is a **Passive Talent** and is always active.
- Gain **+6 on Saving Throws** against effects originating from your patron's **Divine Opposition**.
- Gain the resistances and protections granted by your patron's Religious pages.
- Once per **Short Rest**, when you fail a Saving Throw against your patron's Divine Opposition, you may immediately reroll that Saving Throw. You must use the second result.
- If you successfully resist an effect originating from your patron's Divine Opposition, you become **immune to that same effect from the same source until the end of your next turn**.
- Gain **+6 on checks or Saving Throws** made to resist conditions specifically associated with your patron's Divine Opposition.
- If multiple abilities would grant the same resistance, only the strongest applies unless another rule states otherwise.
- The exact resistances, protections, and additional blessings granted by **Blessed Resilience** are determined by your patron's Religious pages.

### Lore

Veteran Paladins are recognised not by the absence of scars, but by the certainty with which they continue to stand against impossible odds. Their patron's blessing has become so deeply intertwined with their soul that opposing divine forces struggle to gain even the briefest foothold.

Religious scholars often describe these champions as living testaments to their deity's will. Each victory over corruption, temptation, or supernatural oppression reinforces the sacred bond they share, proving that true resilience is born not from invulnerability, but from unwavering devotion.

> *"A faithful soul does not become unbreakable by avoiding hardship. It becomes unbreakable by surviving it."*  
> **— High Warden Cassian, Keeper of the Eternal Oath**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your patron's blessing has become an inseparable part of your being. Divine power no longer merely protects you—it instinctively responds whenever opposing forces attempt to weaken your resolve. Every trial endured has forged your body, mind, and spirit into a vessel capable of withstanding extraordinary hardship.

The champions who reach this level of devotion are often described as immovable pillars of faith. They continue advancing through corruption, fear, divine opposition, and supernatural assaults that would overwhelm lesser warriors. Their resilience is no longer a conscious effort but an instinct born from absolute trust in their patron.

Those who witness such unwavering determination often mistake it for invulnerability. In truth, a Paladin still feels pain and hardship—but their faith refuses to let those burdens define them.

### Effects

- **Blessed Resilience** is a **Passive Talent** and is always active.
- Gain **+6 on Saving Throws** against effects originating from your patron's **Divine Opposition**.
- Gain all resistances and protections granted by your patron's Religious pages.
- Once per **Short Rest**, when you fail a Saving Throw against your patron's Divine Opposition, you may immediately reroll that Saving Throw. You must use the second result.
- If you successfully resist an effect originating from your patron's Divine Opposition, you become **immune to that same effect from the same source until the end of your next turn**.
- The first time each **Long Rest** that you would gain a condition associated with your patron's **Divine Opposition**, you may choose to ignore that condition entirely.
- If multiple abilities would grant the same resistance or immunity, only the strongest applies unless another rule states otherwise.
- The exact resistances, immunities, and blessings granted by **Blessed Resilience** are determined by your patron's Religious pages.

### Lore

The oldest scriptures speak of champions whose faith became so complete that the divine itself seemed to shield them from corruption. Though wounds still marked their bodies, their spirits remained beyond the reach of the powers that sought to break them.

Among the greatest Paladins, Blessed Resilience is viewed not as a reward, but as a sacred responsibility. Those entrusted with such unwavering protection are expected to stand where others cannot, shielding the innocent from the very forces their patron has sworn to oppose.

> *"The faithful are not those who never fall. They are those who rise every time darkness believes it has finally won."*  
> **— High Warden Cassian, Keeper of the Eternal Oath**

## Rank 5
### Description

Your soul has become a living reflection of your patron's divine will. Every hardship you have endured, every temptation resisted, and every oath upheld has strengthened the sacred bond between you and the deity you serve. What was once a blessing has become an inseparable part of your very existence.

Opposing divine forces no longer see you as merely another mortal champion—they recognise you as an extension of the power they oppose. Your faith shields your body, your spirit, and your resolve, allowing you to stand before supernatural threats that would overwhelm even the greatest heroes.

Though no mortal is beyond suffering, your patron ensures that you will never stand alone. Their presence surrounds you in every battle, every trial, and every act of devotion, making you one of the truest embodiments of their divine purpose.

### Effects

- **Blessed Resilience** is a **Passive Talent** and is always active.
- Gain **Advantage on Saving Throws** against effects originating from your patron's **Divine Opposition**.
- Gain all resistances, protections, and passive blessings granted by your patron's Religious pages.
- Once per **Short Rest**, when you fail a Saving Throw against your patron's Divine Opposition, you may immediately reroll that Saving Throw. You must use the second result.
- If you successfully resist an effect originating from your patron's Divine Opposition, you become **immune to that same effect from the same source for 1 minute**.
- The first time each **Long Rest** that you would gain a condition associated with your patron's Divine Opposition, you may ignore that condition entirely.
- Gain your patron's **Legendary Blessing**, as described in your patron's Religious pages.
- If multiple abilities would grant the same resistance, protection, or immunity, only the strongest applies unless another rule states otherwise.

### Lore

Few Paladins ever achieve such complete harmony with their patron. Those who do become living symbols of divine conviction, carrying the essence of their deity wherever they travel. Entire orders look to these champions for guidance, believing their unwavering resilience to be proof that absolute faith can overcome even the greatest darkness.

Legends speak of these heroes standing against impossible odds without yielding, not because they were invincible, but because their devotion could not be shaken. Whether defending sacred shrines, confronting ancient evils, or leading armies into hopeless battles, they become a testament to the enduring strength of the divine.

> *"The body may tire. The armour may break. Even the sword may fail. But a soul truly bound to the divine will never surrender."*  
> **— High Warden Cassian, Keeper of the Eternal Oath**
