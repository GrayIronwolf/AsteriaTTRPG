# Divine Smite

## Overview
A Paladin's weapon is more than forged steel—it is an extension of the sacred oath they have sworn. Through prayer and unwavering conviction, you call upon your patron's divine essence, allowing a single strike to carry the judgement of the deity you serve.

Every Divine Smite is unique to its wielder. The manifestation of the power reflects the nature of the chosen patron, surging through the weapon as radiant light, spectral souls, raging flame, shadow, living vines, celestial energy, infernal wrath, or countless other divine expressions. Though its appearance differs, each smite carries the unmistakable authority of a champion acting in their patron's name.

The power of Divine Smite is especially devastating against those who stand in direct opposition to your patron. Your deity's eternal conflict resonates through your weapon, causing the strike to burn brighter, strike harder, or manifest with greater force against those aligned with the opposing divine domain.

## Rank 1
### Description

A Paladin's weapon is more than forged steel—it is an extension of the sacred oath they have sworn. Through prayer and unwavering conviction, you call upon your patron's divine essence, allowing a single strike to carry the judgement of the deity you serve.

Every Divine Smite is unique to its wielder. The manifestation of the power reflects the nature of the chosen patron, surging through the weapon as radiant light, spectral souls, raging flame, shadow, living vines, celestial energy, infernal wrath, or countless other divine expressions. Though its appearance differs, each smite carries the unmistakable authority of a champion acting in their patron's name.

The power of Divine Smite is especially devastating against those who stand in direct opposition to your patron. Your deity's eternal conflict resonates through your weapon, causing the strike to burn brighter, strike harder, or manifest with greater force against those aligned with the opposing divine domain.

### Effects

- Spend **25 MP** immediately before or immediately after hitting a creature with a melee weapon attack.
- The triggering attack deals an additional **2d8 Patron Damage**.
- Your patron determines the **damage type**, visual manifestation, and other interactions of this damage through its Religious pages.
- If the target belongs to your patron's **Divine Opposition**, increase the additional damage to **3d8 Patron Damage**.
- Divine Smite may only be activated **once per turn**.
- Divine Smite cannot be activated if the triggering attack misses.
- Additional effects granted by your patron's **Divine Authority** are applied after resolving the damage, where applicable.

### Lore

Since the earliest days of Asteria, Divine Smite has stood as the defining miracle of every Paladin. It is not simply a stronger attack, but a moment where mortal conviction and divine purpose become one. For a brief instant, the champion's weapon ceases to belong solely to them and instead becomes a conduit for their patron's will.

Although every faith teaches the technique differently, all agree upon one truth: a Divine Smite is never an act of anger alone. It is a declaration of purpose. Every empowered strike reminds both ally and enemy that the Paladin does not fight for personal glory, but as a faithful servant carrying out the judgement of the deity they have sworn to uphold.

> *"The weapon is mine to wield. The judgement belongs to my patron."*  
> **— Grand Marshal Aurelius, First Champion of the Sacred Host**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your connection to your patron has deepened, allowing greater amounts of divine essence to flow through your weapon without overwhelming your mortal body. Each strike carries more than simple power—it becomes an unmistakable expression of your patron's will upon the battlefield.

As your faith strengthens, the divine energy responds more readily to your conviction. The manifestation of your patron's power burns brighter, strikes harder, and resonates more fiercely against those who stand in opposition to the deity you serve. Though every Paladin channels this miracle differently, all learn that true strength comes not from the weapon itself, but from the sacred bond that empowers it.

Experienced Paladins know that Divine Smite is not meant to be used recklessly. Every invocation is a reminder that divine power is entrusted to them for a purpose, and each empowered strike carries the weight of their oath.

### Effects

- Spend **35 MP** immediately before or immediately after hitting a creature with a melee weapon attack.
- The triggering attack deals an additional **4d8 Patron Damage**.
- Your patron determines the **damage type**, visual manifestation, and other interactions of this damage through its Religious pages.
- If the target belongs to your patron's **Divine Opposition**, increase the additional damage to **5d8 Patron Damage**.
- Divine Smite may only be activated **once per turn**.
- Divine Smite cannot be activated if the triggering attack misses.
- Additional effects granted by your patron's **Divine Authority** are applied after resolving the damage, where applicable.

### Lore

To witness a seasoned Paladin invoke Divine Smite is to see unwavering faith given physical form. The weapon becomes an extension of the deity's purpose, carrying divine judgement with every strike. Though the appearance differs between patrons, the conviction behind the miracle is always unmistakable.

Many holy orders teach that the strength of a Divine Smite is measured not by the destruction it causes, but by the righteousness of the purpose behind it. Only those who remain true to their oath can safely wield greater measures of their patron's power.

> *"Power without purpose is nothing more than destruction. A true smite carries the weight of an oath."*  
> **— Grand Marshal Aurelius, First Champion of the Sacred Host**

## Rank 3
### Description

The bond between you and your patron has grown beyond a simple blessing. When you invoke Divine Smite, your weapon no longer carries only a fleeting spark of divine power—it becomes temporarily infused with your patron's essence, allowing every movement to reflect the sacred purpose of your oath.

For a brief moment, the line between mortal champion and divine servant becomes blurred. Your weapon radiates the unmistakable presence of your patron, and those who oppose its divine will instinctively recoil from its judgement. Whether your deity manifests through radiant light, consuming shadow, spectral souls, celestial fire, or another sacred expression, every strike reminds friend and foe alike that you fight with power greater than your own.

Veteran Paladins often describe this stage of mastery as the moment Divine Smite ceases to be an ability and instead becomes an extension of their faith itself.

### Effects

- Spend **45 MP** immediately before or immediately after hitting a creature with a melee weapon attack.
- The triggering attack deals an additional **6d8 Patron Damage**.
- Your patron determines the **damage type**, visual manifestation, and additional interactions through its Religious pages.
- If the target belongs to your patron's **Divine Opposition**, increase the additional damage to **8d8 Patron Damage**.
- Your weapon remains **Divinely Infused** until the **end of your current turn**.
- If you make another successful melee weapon attack before the end of your turn (such as through an additional Action, Bonus Action, or Opportunity Attack), you may apply **half of the original Divine Smite damage** without paying an additional Mana Cost.
- Divine Smite may only be activated **once per turn**.
- Additional effects granted by your patron's **Divine Authority** are applied after resolving the damage, where applicable.

### Lore

Only the most devoted Paladins learn to sustain their patron's divine essence beyond a single strike. Rather than calling upon fleeting power, they become living conduits through which their deity's judgement briefly enters the mortal world.

Each patron manifests this miracle differently, yet every faith agrees upon one truth: a weapon blessed in this way is no longer merely steel. For those few heartbeats, it becomes a sacred instrument carrying the will of a god.

> *"A single strike may win a duel. A weapon guided by faith changes the course of history."*  
> **— Grand Marshal Aurelius, First Champion of the Sacred Host**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your weapon has become a true vessel of your patron's divine essence. With every invocation of Divine Smite, the power flowing through your weapon is no longer contained within a single blow. Instead, fragments of your deity's will linger upon the battlefield, leaving behind a manifestation that reflects the eternal purpose of the patron you serve.

To witness a master Paladin invoke Divine Smite is to witness a deity briefly touch the mortal world. Whether blazing with radiant light, engulfed in infernal flame, surrounded by spectral souls, or woven with the threads of fate itself, each strike becomes a declaration that your patron's influence cannot be ignored.

Those who stand in opposition to your deity quickly learn that surviving the initial blow is often only the beginning. The divine power released by your weapon continues to shape the battle long after the strike has landed.

### Effects

- Spend **60 MP** immediately before or immediately after hitting a creature with a melee weapon attack.
- The triggering attack deals an additional **8d8 Patron Damage**.
- Your patron determines the **damage type**, visual manifestation, and additional interactions through its Religious pages.
- If the target belongs to your patron's **Divine Opposition**, increase the additional damage to **10d8 Patron Damage**.
- Your weapon remains **Divinely Infused** until the **end of your next turn**.
- While Divinely Infused, the first successful melee weapon attack you make on each turn deals an additional **2d8 Patron Damage** without spending additional Mana.
- After resolving Divine Smite, apply your patron's **Divine Authority** effects, if applicable.
- Divine Smite may only be activated **once per turn**.
- Divine Smite cannot be activated if the triggering attack misses.

### Lore

Only the greatest champions learn to wield Divine Smite with such mastery. Their patron's essence no longer fades after a single strike but lingers around them, transforming every swing of their weapon into a reminder of the sacred oath they uphold.

Across Asteria, tales speak of battlefields forever changed by these miracles. Some tell of radiant scars burned into the earth, others of shadows that lingered for days, while still others describe spectral echoes or impossible twists of fate. Though the manifestations differ, every account agrees upon one truth—the deity had made its presence known.

> *"One strike may bear my oath. Every strike thereafter bears my patron's will."*  
> **— Grand Marshal Aurelius, First Champion of the Sacred Host**

## Rank 5
### Description

Your patron's divine power flows through you without hesitation, transforming every invocation of Divine Smite into a sacred act of judgement. Your weapon ceases to be merely a tool of war and instead becomes a flawless conduit for the will of the deity you serve.

Every strike carries the unmistakable presence of your patron. The battlefield itself bears witness as divine power erupts from your weapon in a manifestation unique to your faith. Those aligned with your patron's Divine Opposition experience the full weight of this judgement, finding themselves unable to escape the eternal conflict between opposing divine powers.

Only the greatest Paladins are entrusted with such authority. They are no longer simply champions chosen by a deity—they are recognised as living avatars of their patron's purpose upon the battlefield, delivering divine judgement with unwavering conviction and absolute faith.

### Effects

- Spend **80 MP** immediately before or immediately after hitting a creature with a melee weapon attack.
- The triggering attack deals an additional **10d8 Patron Damage**.
- Your patron determines the **damage type**, visual manifestation, and additional interactions through its Religious pages.
- If the target belongs to your patron's **Divine Opposition**, increase the additional damage to **12d8 Patron Damage**.
- Your weapon remains **Divinely Infused** until the **end of your next turn**.
- While Divinely Infused, the first successful melee weapon attack you make on each turn deals an additional **2d8 Patron Damage** without spending additional Mana.
- After resolving Divine Smite, apply all effects granted by your patron's **Divine Authority**, including any Rank V enhancements.
- Divine Smite may only be activated **once per turn**.
- Divine Smite cannot be activated if the triggering attack misses.

### Lore

Across the ages, only a select few Paladins have mastered Divine Smite to this degree. Their names are remembered not because of the devastation they wrought, but because every strike embodied the ideals, convictions, and purpose of the deity they served. Entire wars have turned upon a single blow delivered by such champions, not through overwhelming force alone, but through the undeniable authority of the divine.

The manifestation of this miracle differs between every faith, yet all share a common truth: when a legendary Paladin invokes Divine Smite, friend and foe alike understand they are witnessing the direct will of a god expressed through mortal hands.

> *"This is not my judgement. I am merely the hand that delivers it."*  
> **— Grand Marshal Aurelius, First Champion of the Sacred Host**
