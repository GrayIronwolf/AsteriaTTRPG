# Hallowed Ground

## Overview
Through unwavering devotion, you call upon your patron to sanctify the land beneath your feet. Divine power spreads across the battlefield, transforming ordinary ground into sacred territory where your patron's influence becomes tangible. Every deity manifests this miracle differently, yet each Hallowed Ground serves the same purpose—to establish a place where the patron's divine will is strongest.

Within this consecrated domain, creatures aligned with your patron's **Divine Resonance** are welcomed beneath its blessing, their vitality restored by the sacred power flowing through the ground. Those aligned with your patron's **Divine Opposition** experience the opposite, finding the very land rejecting their presence as divine judgement manifests beneath every step.

To stand within Hallowed Ground is to stand within your patron's realm. Whether that realm radiates brilliant light, echoes with restless souls, blooms with vibrant life, burns with infernal flame, or shimmers with threads of fate depends entirely upon the deity you serve. Though every manifestation is unique, all are unmistakable signs that the battlefield has been claimed by a higher power.

## Rank 1
### Description

Through unwavering devotion, you call upon your patron to sanctify the land beneath your feet. Every deity manifests this miracle differently, yet each Hallowed Ground serves the same purpose—to establish a place where the patron's divine will is strongest.

### Effects

- Spend **40 MP** to consecrate an area within **10 metres**.
- Create a **5 metre radius Hallowed Ground** that lasts for **3 Rounds**.
- The appearance, damage type, and visual effects are determined by your patron's Religious pages.
- Creatures aligned with your patron's **Divine Resonance** that begin their turn within the Hallowed Ground restore **1d8 + your Charisma Modifier HP**.
- Creatures aligned with your patron's **Divine Opposition** that begin their turn within the Hallowed Ground take **1d8 Patron Damage**.
- The exact creatures, followers, summons, and divine entities affected by **Divine Resonance** and **Divine Opposition** are determined by your patron's Religious pages.
- A creature may only benefit from or be damaged by Hallowed Ground **once per round**.
- Additional effects granted by your patron's **Divine Authority** apply while creatures remain within the Hallowed Ground, where applicable.
- Multiple Hallowed Ground effects may overlap, but creatures are only affected by the single strongest Hallowed Ground of each patron.

### Lore

Every faith teaches that the world itself remembers the footsteps of the divine. Through prayer and absolute conviction, a Paladin briefly restores a fragment of that sacred presence, allowing mortal soil to become an extension of their patron's realm. The battlefield itself bends to the deity's influence, welcoming those who walk in harmony with its purpose while rejecting those who oppose it.

Many ancient battlefields throughout Asteria are believed to have remained hallowed for centuries after legendary conflicts. Pilgrims still seek these places, claiming they can feel the lingering presence of the gods long after their champions have departed.

> *"Where my patron's will is welcomed, life is strengthened. Where it is opposed, judgement follows."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Paths**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your patron's influence spreads further across the battlefield, allowing a greater portion of the world to briefly reflect their divine domain. What was once a small sanctuary now becomes a sacred territory where your patron's presence is undeniable, strengthening those who walk in harmony with its purpose while pressing ever harder against those who oppose it.

As your faith deepens, the boundary between the mortal world and your patron's divine realm becomes thinner. Every step taken within the Hallowed Ground reminds creatures aligned with your deity that they stand upon blessed soil, while those bound to opposing divine powers feel an increasing sense of unease as the land itself rejects their presence.

Experienced Paladins learn that battles are not always won through overwhelming force. Sometimes victory comes from claiming the ground beneath your feet and compelling friend and foe alike to fight upon terrain blessed by a higher power.

### Effects

- Spend **50 MP** to consecrate an area within **15 metres**.
- Create a **7 metre radius Hallowed Ground** that lasts for **4 Rounds**.
- The appearance, damage type, and visual effects are determined by your patron's Religious pages.

- Creatures aligned with your patron's **Divine Opposition** that begin their turn within the Hallowed Ground take **2d8 Patron Damage**.
- Creatures aligned with your patron's **Divine Opposition** suffer a **−2 penalty on Saving Throws** against effects originating from your patron while they remain within the Hallowed Ground.
- The exact creatures, followers, summons, and divine entities affected by **Divine Resonance** and **Divine Opposition** are determined by your patron's Religious pages.
- A creature may only benefit from or be damaged by Hallowed Ground **once per round**.
- Additional effects granted by your patron's **Divine Authority** apply while creatures remain within the Hallowed Ground, where applicable.
- Multiple Hallowed Ground effects may overlap, but creatures are only affected by the single strongest Hallowed Ground of each patron.

### Lore

The greatest temples teach that sacred ground is more than a place of worship—it is a place where the divine feels close enough to touch. As a Paladin's devotion grows, they learn to recreate a fragment of that holy presence wherever they stand, carrying their patron's influence into the heart of battle.

Many ancient battlefields became revered pilgrimage sites because the divine essence left behind by legendary Paladins lingered long after the fighting had ended. Even centuries later, worshippers claim to feel the comforting embrace—or unsettling rejection—of the patron that once claimed that ground.

> *"The world remembers every place the gods have touched. Today, this ground shall remember my patron."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Paths**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your patron's influence no longer merely blesses the battlefield—it begins to reshape it. The sacred domain you create resonates with divine purpose, strengthening those who walk in harmony with your deity while steadily weakening the resolve of those who stand against it.

Within the Hallowed Ground, the boundary between the mortal realm and your patron's divine domain grows thin. Every heartbeat spent upon the consecrated earth reinforces the patron's will. Those aligned with your deity feel renewed with every passing moment, while opposing forces find their strength slowly eroded beneath the overwhelming presence of the divine.

To veteran Paladins, Hallowed Ground becomes more than a place of refuge. It is a declaration that this battlefield has been claimed, and all who enter must answer to the will of the patron who sanctified it.

### Effects

- Spend **60 MP** to consecrate an area within **20 metres**.
- Create a **10 metre radius Hallowed Ground** that lasts for **5 Rounds**.
- The appearance, damage type, and visual effects are determined by your patron's Religious pages.
- Creatures aligned with your patron's **Divine Resonance** that begin their turn within the Hallowed Ground restore **3d8 + your Charisma Modifier HP**.
- Creatures aligned with your patron's **Divine Opposition** that begin their turn within the Hallowed Ground take **3d8 Patron Damage**.
- Creatures aligned with your patron's **Divine Opposition** suffer a **−4 penalty on Saving Throws** against effects originating from your patron while they remain within the Hallowed Ground.
- Once per turn, creatures aligned with your patron's **Divine Resonance** may immediately attempt a new Saving Throw against one effect associated with your patron's **Divine Opposition**, gaining a **+2 bonus** on that Saving Throw.
- The exact creatures, followers, summons, and divine entities affected by **Divine Resonance** and **Divine Opposition** are determined by your patron's Religious pages.
- A creature may only benefit from or be damaged by Hallowed Ground **once per round**.
- Additional effects granted by your patron's **Divine Authority** apply while creatures remain within the Hallowed Ground, where applicable.
- Multiple Hallowed Ground effects may overlap, but creatures are only affected by the single strongest Hallowed Ground of each patron.

### Lore

As a Paladin's devotion matures, their patron's influence ceases to be a fleeting blessing and instead becomes a living domain upon the battlefield. Warriors who fight within it often describe feeling as though they stand beneath the direct gaze of the deity itself, their courage renewed while opposing powers seem strangely diminished.

Many of Asteria's holiest battlefields were said to have been transformed by Paladins of this mastery. Even centuries later, pilgrims claim those places still carry echoes of the divine presence that once sanctified them, while creatures opposed to that patron instinctively avoid crossing the ancient ground.

> *"Where my patron walks, the faithful are strengthened and the faithless find no comfort."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Paths**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your patron's influence now extends far beyond a simple blessing upon the land. When you consecrate the battlefield, the ground itself begins to reflect a fragment of your deity's divine realm, strengthening those who embody your patron's ideals while relentlessly rejecting those who stand in opposition.

Within this sacred domain, divine power flows freely. Every step taken by those aligned with your patron renews their strength and conviction, while opposing forces feel the crushing weight of entering territory that no longer welcomes their presence. To them, the very earth seems to resist their advance.

Among the greatest Paladins, Hallowed Ground is considered the ultimate expression of battlefield control. Rather than merely fighting upon the battlefield, they transform the battlefield into an extension of their patron's divine will, allowing every miracle they perform to resonate more strongly within its sacred boundaries.

### Effects

- Spend **75 MP** to consecrate an area within **25 metres**.
- Create a **12 metre radius Hallowed Ground** that lasts for **6 Rounds**.
- The appearance, damage type, and visual manifestation are determined by your patron's Religious pages.
- Creatures aligned with your patron's **Divine Resonance** that begin their turn within the Hallowed Ground restore **4d8 + your Charisma Modifier HP**.
- Creatures aligned with your patron's **Divine Opposition** that begin their turn within the Hallowed Ground take **4d8 Patron Damage**.
- Creatures aligned with your patron's **Divine Opposition** suffer a **−6 penalty on Saving Throws** against abilities originating from your patron while they remain within the Hallowed Ground.
- Creatures aligned with your patron's **Divine Resonance** gain a **+2 bonus to Saving Throws** against effects originating from your patron's Divine Opposition while they remain within the Hallowed Ground.
- All Paladin talents that reference your patron's **Divine Authority** are considered empowered while used within your Hallowed Ground. Apply any additional effects granted by your patron's Religious pages.
- The exact creatures, followers, summons, and divine entities affected by **Divine Resonance** and **Divine Opposition** are determined by your patron's Religious pages.
- A creature may only benefit from or be damaged by Hallowed Ground **once per round**.
- Multiple Hallowed Ground effects may overlap, but creatures are only affected by the single strongest Hallowed Ground of each patron.

### Lore

Only a handful of Paladins ever reach the point where the world itself bends to reflect their patron's divine influence. Their Hallowed Ground is no longer viewed as a temporary blessing, but as a sacred territory where the deity's presence can almost be felt with every breath.

Many ancient battlefields became holy sites because legendary Paladins fought upon them with such devotion that traces of their patron's influence remained long after the conflict had ended. Pilgrims still journey to these places seeking blessings, while those opposed to the patron often refuse to set foot upon the consecrated ground.

> *"This is no longer mortal soil. For a fleeting moment, you stand within my patron's realm."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Paths**

## Rank 5
### Description

Your faith has reached its pinnacle, allowing your patron to briefly manifest a fragment of their divine realm upon the mortal world. The land itself becomes an extension of your deity's will, transforming the battlefield into sacred territory where their influence reigns supreme.

Every Hallowed Ground is unique to the patron that created it. Some blossom into flourishing sanctuaries overflowing with life, while others become silent graveyards where restless spirits gather. Some shimmer with impossible threads of fate, while others burn with celestial fire or echo with infernal wrath. Though their appearance differs, they all represent the same truth—the world itself has yielded to divine authority.

Those whose souls resonate with your patron find themselves strengthened simply by standing within the domain. Those aligned against your patron feel the land reject their presence, every moment spent within it reminding them they walk upon ground claimed by a higher power.

### Effects

- Spend **90 MP** to consecrate an area within **30 metres**.
- Create a **15 metre radius Hallowed Ground** that lasts for **8 Rounds**.
- The appearance, damage type, and all additional effects are determined by your patron's Religious pages.
- Creatures aligned with your patron's **Divine Resonance** that begin their turn within the Hallowed Ground restore **5d8 + your Charisma Modifier HP**.
- Creatures aligned with your patron's **Divine Opposition** that begin their turn within the Hallowed Ground take **5d8 Patron Damage**.
- Creatures aligned with your patron's **Divine Opposition** have **Disadvantage on Saving Throws** against abilities originating from your patron while they remain within the Hallowed Ground.
- Creatures aligned with your patron's **Divine Resonance** have **Advantage on Saving Throws** against effects originating from your patron's Divine Opposition while they remain within the Hallowed Ground.
- All Paladin talents that reference your patron's **Divine Authority** are empowered while used within your Hallowed Ground. Apply all Rank V Divine Authority effects described in your patron's Religious pages.
- The exact creatures, followers, summons, and divine entities affected by **Divine Resonance** and **Divine Opposition** are determined by your patron's Religious pages.
- A creature may only benefit from or be damaged by Hallowed Ground **once per round**.
- Multiple Hallowed Ground effects may overlap, but creatures are only affected by the strongest Hallowed Ground belonging to each individual patron.

### Lore

The oldest scriptures speak of legendary champions capable of bringing a fragment of their patron's divine realm into the mortal world. Though only for a few fleeting moments, these sacred domains became places where miracles felt natural and divine judgement seemed inevitable.

Entire civilizations have built temples upon battlefields where such miracles once occurred, believing traces of the patron's presence still linger beneath the soil. Whether these stories are truth or legend, every holy order agrees that no greater expression of a Paladin's devotion exists than to allow their deity's will to reshape the very ground beneath their feet.

> *"For this brief moment, this land no longer belongs to kings or kingdoms. It belongs to my patron."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Paths**
