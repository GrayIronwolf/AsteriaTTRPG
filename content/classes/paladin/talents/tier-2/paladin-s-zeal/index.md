# Paladin's Zeal

## Overview
The unwavering faith of a Paladin does not awaken through prayer alone—it is forged upon the battlefield. Every strike, every hardship overcome, and every act of courage strengthens your resolve, building **Zeal** as combat continues. Rather than representing anger or rage, Zeal embodies your absolute conviction and the growing presence of your patron fighting beside you.

As your Zeal grows, your patron's divine essence becomes increasingly apparent. Whether it manifests as radiant light, spectral ravens, infernal embers, blooming life, celestial brilliance, or shimmering threads of fate depends entirely upon the deity you serve. Though every manifestation differs, each serves as a visible reminder that your faith grows stronger with every passing moment.

A disciplined Paladin may channel this accumulated conviction into powerful **Zeal Techniques**, sacrificing a portion of their Zeal to briefly empower themselves during the heat of battle. Knowing when to preserve your resolve and when to unleash it is the mark of a true champion.

## Rank 1
### Description

The unwavering faith of a Paladin does not awaken through prayer alone—it is forged upon the battlefield. Every strike, every hardship overcome, and every act of courage strengthens your resolve, building **Zeal** as combat continues. Rather than representing anger or rage, Zeal embodies your absolute conviction and the growing presence of your patron fighting beside you.

As your Zeal grows, your patron's divine essence becomes increasingly apparent. Whether it manifests as radiant light, spectral ravens, infernal embers, blooming life, celestial brilliance, or shimmering threads of fate depends entirely upon the deity you serve. Though every manifestation differs, each serves as a visible reminder that your faith grows stronger with every passing moment.

A disciplined Paladin may channel this accumulated conviction into powerful **Zeal Techniques**, sacrificing a portion of their Zeal to briefly empower themselves during the heat of battle. Knowing when to preserve your resolve and when to unleash it is the mark of a true champion.

### Effects

- **Paladin's Zeal** is a **Passive Talent** and is automatically active whenever combat begins.
- At the start of combat, your Zeal resets to **0**.
- Maximum Zeal: **10**.
- Zeal is lost when combat ends or if you become unconscious.

#### Generating Zeal

- Gain **1 Zeal** whenever you successfully hit with a melee weapon attack.
- Gain **1 Zeal** if your melee weapon attack misses by **2 AC or less** (**Unyielding Conviction**).
- Gain **2 Zeal** on a **Critical Hit**.
- Gain **2 Zeal** on a **Critical Success** against an effect originating from your patron's **Divine Opposition**.
- Lose **2 Zeal** on a **Critical Failure** with a melee weapon attack.
- Lose **2 Zeal** on a **Critical Failure** against an effect originating from your patron's **Divine Opposition**.
- Zeal can never exceed its maximum.

#### Broken Conviction

- If a Critical Failure reduces your Zeal to **0**, all accumulated Zeal is lost.
- If you suffer another Critical Failure while already at **0 Zeal**, you enter **Broken Conviction**.
- While suffering **Broken Conviction**, you cannot gain Zeal until the end of your next turn.
- At the end of your next turn, regain **1 Zeal** as your conviction is restored.

### Zeal Techniques

#### Divine Pursuit (2 Zeal Points)

- Spend **2 Zeal**.
- Gain **+4 metres Movement Speed** until the end of your turn.

#### Divine Strength (3 Zeal Points)

- Spend **3 Zeal**.
- Your next successful melee weapon attack gains **+2 damage**.

### Lore

The oldest Paladin Orders teach that faith is not measured by the prayers spoken before battle, but by the conviction maintained throughout it. Every trial overcome, every wound endured, and every refusal to yield strengthens the bond between champion and patron, causing divine power to grow with each passing heartbeat.

Veteran Paladins often describe Zeal as a flame carried within the soul. It burns brighter with perseverance, falters when conviction is shaken, yet never truly dies while the oath remains unbroken. Those who master this sacred discipline become living symbols of unwavering devotion, capable of calling upon their faith whenever the battle demands it.

> *"Conviction is not the absence of doubt. It is the choice to keep advancing despite it."*  
> **— Grand Marshal Aurelius, First Champion of the Sacred Host**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your conviction has grown beyond simple determination. Every battle further strengthens the sacred bond between you and your patron, allowing your Zeal to be channelled in new and more disciplined ways. Experienced Paladins no longer rely solely on instinct; they learn to direct their faith with purpose, adapting to the ever-changing flow of battle.

The greatest champions understand that conviction is a resource to be managed wisely. Recklessly expending Zeal may leave a Paladin vulnerable, while patiently building it allows them to unleash greater acts of divine prowess when the moment demands it.

### Effects

- **Paladin's Zeal** continues to follow all Zeal generation and **Broken Conviction** rules established in **Rank I**.
- Maximum Zeal remains **10**.

### Zeal Techniques

#### Divine Guard (4 Zeal Points)

- Spend **4 Zeal**.
- Gain **+2 AC** until the start of your next turn or until you are successfully hit by an attack.

#### Unbreakable Resolve (5 Zeal Points)

- Spend **5 Zeal**.
- Gain **+4 on your next Saving Throw**.
- This bonus must be declared before the Saving Throw is rolled.

### Lore

As Paladins mature in both faith and experience, they discover that true conviction is neither reckless nor impulsive. Every choice made in battle reflects their devotion, and every technique mastered is another lesson in balancing courage with discipline.

Veteran holy orders teach that Zeal is a gift entrusted to worthy champions, not simply a weapon to be unleashed without thought. Those who master its flow become calm within the fiercest storms, answering every challenge with unwavering confidence.

> *"Strength may win a battle, but conviction decides when that strength must be unleashed."*  
> **— Grand Marshal Aurelius, First Champion of the Sacred Host**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your faith has become unwavering, allowing your Zeal to surge beyond simple acts of courage and into feats worthy of the greatest champions. Every battle strengthens your resolve, and every trial overcome reinforces the sacred bond between you and your patron. With growing mastery, you learn to channel that conviction into increasingly powerful manifestations of divine strength.

Where younger Paladins fight with determination, seasoned champions fight with purpose. They know exactly when to conserve their Zeal and when to unleash it, turning the tide of battle through disciplined conviction rather than reckless emotion. To those who witness such mastery, it appears as though the patron themselves guides every movement.

### Effects

- **Paladin's Zeal** continues to follow all Zeal generation and **Broken Conviction** rules established in **Rank I**.
- Maximum Zeal remains **10**.

### Zeal Techniques

#### Righteous Fury (6 Zeal Points)

- Spend **6 Zeal**.
- Your next successful melee weapon attack deals an additional **1d6 Patron Damage**.
- The Patron Damage type is determined by your patron's Religious pages.

#### Divine Endurance (7 Zeal Points)

Choose one:

- Gain **Resistance to all damage** until the start of your next turn.

**OR**

- Gain **Temporary HP equal to your Level + your Charisma Modifier**.
- Temporary HP gained this way lasts until the end of combat or until depleted.

### Lore

The oldest Paladin Orders teach that true conviction is measured not by words, but by the willingness to stand firm when all hope seems lost. Those who master Zeal become living examples of this belief, transforming unwavering faith into miracles of endurance and righteous might upon the battlefield.

Veterans often describe the experience as feeling their patron standing beside them with every step. Whether that presence is seen as radiant light, celestial fire, spectral ravens, blooming life, or another divine manifestation depends upon the deity they serve, but the message is always the same—the faithful are never truly alone.

> *"Faith is forged in hardship. Conviction is revealed when hardship fails to break you."*  
> **— Grand Marshal Aurelius, First Champion of the Sacred Host**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your unwavering faith has become a beacon through which your patron's divine will flows with remarkable clarity. The Zeal you cultivate during battle no longer represents simple determination—it has become a sacred force that can be shaped into miracles of strength, protection, and righteous conviction.

Masters of Zeal are recognised across Asteria not by the brilliance of their armour or the sharpness of their blade, but by the calm certainty with which they face impossible odds. Their faith no longer falters under pressure, allowing them to call upon extraordinary reserves of divine power when the battle demands it most.

Every act of courage further strengthens the sacred bond between Paladin and patron, preparing them for the greatest trials still to come.

### Effects

- **Paladin's Zeal** continues to follow all Zeal generation and **Broken Conviction** rules established in **Rank I**.
- Increase your **Maximum Zeal** from **10** to **15**.

### Zeal Techniques

#### Divine Momentum (8 Zeal Points)

Choose one:

- Gain **1 additional Action Point** this turn.

**OR**

- Immediately move up to **half your Movement Speed** without provoking Opportunity Attacks.

#### Holy Restoration (9 Zeal Points)

Choose one:

- Restore **HP equal to twice your Character Level + your Charisma Modifier**.

**OR**

- End **one negative condition** currently affecting you.

### Lore

Only the greatest champions ever learn to command Zeal with such precision. What begins as determination eventually becomes a living manifestation of faith itself, allowing the Paladin to answer seemingly impossible challenges with miraculous resolve.

The sacred texts describe these warriors as carrying a fragment of their patron's presence into every battle. Though still mortal, they begin to display moments of divine greatness that inspire allies and leave even powerful enemies questioning whether they face a lone warrior or the will of a god.

> *"The greatest miracle is not power. It is the courage to stand again when every reason tells you to fall."*  
> **— Grand Marshal Aurelius, First Champion of the Sacred Host**

## Rank 5
### Description

Your conviction has reached its absolute peak, allowing your patron's divine presence to flow through you without hesitation. Every challenge faced, every oath upheld, and every battle survived has forged an unbreakable spirit capable of inspiring allies and striking fear into those who oppose your patron's divine will.

The greatest Paladins no longer fight through strength alone. Their unwavering faith becomes a beacon upon the battlefield, their determination inspiring those around them while reminding their enemies that no force can extinguish a soul truly devoted to its sacred purpose. To stand beside such a champion is to witness the very embodiment of divine conviction.

Only the most legendary heroes ever reach this level of mastery. Their Zeal is no longer merely a reflection of their faith—it is living proof that the bond between mortal and patron can transcend all doubt.

### Effects

- **Paladin's Zeal** continues to follow all Zeal generation and **Broken Conviction** rules established in **Rank I**.
- Your **Maximum Zeal** remains **15**.

### Zeal Techniques

#### Divine Ascendance (10 Zeal Points)

Choose one of the following effects:

- Gain **+2 Attack Rolls**, **+2 AC**, and **+2 Saving Throws** until the start of your next turn.

**OR**

- Gain **+2d6 Patron Damage** on every successful melee weapon attack until the end of your turn.

**OR**

- Immediately recover **10 Zeal**, up to your maximum of **15**, after reducing a creature aligned with your patron's **Divine Opposition** to 0 HP this turn.

### Lore

Every generation produces only a handful of Paladins whose conviction becomes so complete that their presence alone inspires unwavering courage. These champions are remembered not for the enemies they defeated, but for the hope they carried into battles that others believed impossible to win.

The scriptures teach that such heroes are living reflections of their patron's ideals. Though they remain mortal, their faith burns so brightly that, for a few fleeting moments during battle, it is difficult to distinguish where the champion ends and the patron's divine purpose begins.

> *"Faith is not proven when victory is certain. Faith is proven when conviction alone is enough to carry you through the impossible."*  
> **— Grand Marshal Aurelius, First Champion of the Sacred Host**
