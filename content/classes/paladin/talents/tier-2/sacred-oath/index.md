# Sacred Oath

## Overview
To walk the path of a Religious Class is to enter into a sacred covenant with a higher power. Through solemn vows, unwavering faith, or absolute devotion, you pledge yourself to a patron whose ideals shall guide your actions and shape the divine gifts bestowed upon you.

This covenant is more than a promise—it is a living bond between mortal and deity. As your faith deepens, so too does your patron's willingness to grant divine blessings. Every miracle you perform, every trial you overcome, and every oath you uphold strengthens this sacred connection.

Although every Religious Class forms the same Divine Covenant, each serves their patron in different ways. A Paladin becomes the patron's champion upon the battlefield, a Cleric becomes their vessel of divine miracles, an Inquisitor becomes their righteous judge, while other Religious Classes express that devotion through their own unique calling.

## Rank 1
### Description

To walk the path of a Religious Class is to enter into a sacred covenant with a higher power. Through solemn vows, unwavering faith, or absolute devotion, you pledge yourself to a patron whose ideals shall guide your actions and shape the divine gifts bestowed upon you.

This covenant is more than a promise—it is a living bond between mortal and deity. As your faith deepens, so too does your patron's willingness to grant divine blessings. Every miracle you perform, every trial you overcome, and every oath you uphold strengthens this sacred connection.

Although every Religious Class forms the same Divine Covenant, each serves their patron in different ways. A Paladin becomes the patron's champion upon the battlefield, a Cleric becomes their vessel of divine miracles, an Inquisitor becomes their righteous judge, while other Religious Classes express that devotion through their own unique calling.

### Effects

- Choose a patron to form your **Divine Covenant** with.
- Your chosen patron becomes permanent unless changed through a major story event, divine intervention, or GM approval.
- Gain your patron's **Rank I Class Boon**.
- The Class Boon granted depends on both your **chosen patron** and your **Religious Class**.
- Unlock your patron's **Religious Pages**, including:
  - Divine Resonance
  - Divine Opposition
  - Divine Authority
  - Class Boons
  - Patron Features
- All talents that reference your patron now use the information found within your chosen patron's Religious Pages.
- Future ranks of **Sacred Oath** grant additional Class Boons from your chosen patron.

### Lore

Every faith teaches that divine power cannot simply be taken—it must be entrusted. Through the Sacred Oath, a deity acknowledges a mortal's devotion and accepts them into their service, establishing a covenant that may last a lifetime. From that moment onward, the faithful no longer walk alone, for every miracle they perform is shaped by the ideals of the patron they have chosen to serve.

Across Asteria, countless covenants exist between gods and mortals. Some are forged through unwavering honour, others through sacrifice, wisdom, justice, fate, life, death, or even darker ideals. Though the paths differ, all who swear a Sacred Oath accept the same responsibility—to uphold their patron's divine will within the mortal realm.

> *"A prayer is spoken with the lips. An oath is spoken with the soul. The gods remember the difference."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your bond with your patron grows stronger as your actions continue to honour the covenant you have sworn. Through faithful service, steadfast devotion, and unwavering commitment to your sacred calling, your patron places greater trust in your abilities, rewarding your dedication with a deeper blessing.

The Divine Covenant is a living relationship that strengthens over time. Every miracle performed, every hardship endured, and every trial overcome reaffirms your place within your patron's service. As your faith matures, so too do the gifts bestowed upon you.

Though every Religious Class follows the same covenant, no two paths are identical. A patron grants different blessings to their Paladins, Clerics, Priests, Inquisitors, Oracles, and other faithful, ensuring each servant fulfils their own divine purpose within the world.

### Effects

- Your **Divine Covenant** deepens with your chosen patron.
- Gain your patron's **Rank II Class Boon**.
- The Class Boon granted depends on both your **chosen patron** and your **Religious Class**.
- Unlock any additional **Divine Authority**, **Class Features**, or **Patron Features** granted by your patron at Rank II.
- All talents that reference your patron continue to use the information contained within your chosen patron's Religious Pages.
- Your Sacred Oath remains bound to your chosen patron unless changed through a major story event, divine intervention, or GM approval.

### Lore

The greatest servants of the gods understand that faith is not measured by a single promise, but by the countless choices made after that promise is sworn. Each act of loyalty strengthens the covenant, allowing the patron to reveal greater aspects of their divine purpose to those who have proven worthy.

Ancient scriptures speak of champions whose devotion became so profound that their patrons entrusted them with miracles reserved for only the most faithful. Such blessings are never given lightly, for every gift carries the expectation that it will be used in accordance with the patron's divine will.

> *"The oath is not completed when it is spoken. It is renewed every day through the choices of the faithful."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**

## Rank 3
### Description

The covenant between you and your patron has become an unshakable bond. Your faith is no longer measured solely by your words or deeds, but by the trust your patron has placed within you. Through countless trials, unwavering devotion, and faithful service, you have proven yourself worthy of receiving greater divine gifts.

As your Divine Covenant strengthens, your patron entrusts you with blessings reserved for only their most devoted servants. These sacred boons are not simply rewards—they are symbols of responsibility, reflecting your growing role within your patron's divine purpose.

Though every Religious Class walks beneath the same Divine Covenant, each fulfils a different calling. Whether champion, healer, judge, prophet, or guardian, every faithful servant receives blessings uniquely suited to their sacred role.

### Effects

- Your **Divine Covenant** strengthens with your chosen patron.
- Gain your patron's **Rank III Class Boon**.
- The Class Boon granted depends on both your **chosen patron** and your **Religious Class**.
- Unlock any additional **Divine Authority**, **Class Features**, or **Patron Features** granted by your patron at Rank III.
- All talents that reference your patron continue to use the information contained within your chosen patron's Religious Pages.
- Your Sacred Oath remains bound to your chosen patron unless changed through a major story event, divine intervention, or GM approval.

### Lore

Only the truly devoted ever reach this stage of the Divine Covenant. By now, your patron no longer sees you as merely another follower, but as a trusted servant whose actions carry their ideals into the mortal world. Every miracle performed and every oath upheld strengthens the bond between deity and disciple.

Throughout Asteria's history, many legendary heroes earned their place in history not through unmatched strength, but through unwavering devotion to their patron's will. Their names became synonymous with the virtues they embodied, inspiring generations of faithful who sought to walk the same sacred path.

> *"A covenant is strengthened not through promises, but through every faithful step taken after the promise is made."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your Divine Covenant has matured into an extraordinary bond, one recognised not only by your patron but by the celestial, infernal, primal, or cosmic powers that serve beneath them. Your unwavering faith has carried you through countless trials, proving your devotion beyond question and earning the trust required to wield greater fragments of your patron's divine authority.

Few faithful ever reach this stage of their covenant. Those who do become living representatives of their patron's ideals, entrusted to act with wisdom, courage, and conviction wherever their journey leads. Every decision you make reflects upon the deity you serve, and every miracle performed strengthens the connection between the mortal and the divine.

Although every Religious Class follows the same Divine Covenant, each fulfils a different purpose within their patron's design. The blessings granted to a Paladin differ greatly from those bestowed upon a Cleric, Priest, Inquisitor, Oracle, or other servant, ensuring every champion is equipped to fulfil the role their patron has chosen for them.

### Effects

- Your **Divine Covenant** deepens further with your chosen patron.
- Gain your patron's **Rank IV Class Boon**.
- The Class Boon granted depends on both your **chosen patron** and your **Religious Class**.
- Unlock any additional **Divine Authority**, **Class Features**, or **Patron Features** granted by your patron at Rank IV.
- All talents that reference your patron continue to use the information contained within your chosen patron's Religious Pages.
- Your Sacred Oath remains bound to your chosen patron unless changed through a major story event, divine intervention, or GM approval.

### Lore

The fourth stage of the Divine Covenant is spoken of with great reverence throughout Asteria. It marks the point where a follower ceases to be viewed as merely faithful and instead becomes a true champion of their patron's ideals. Their actions inspire fellow believers, while their presence alone often serves as a reminder that the gods still guide the mortal world.

Ancient records tell of covenant bearers who turned the tide of wars, restored dying kingdoms, or stood alone against impossible darkness—not through unmatched strength, but because their patron's trust had become absolute. Such individuals are remembered for generations as examples of what unwavering devotion can achieve.

> *"When a god places their trust in a mortal, the weight of that trust becomes both their greatest blessing and their greatest responsibility."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**

## Rank 5
### Description

Your Divine Covenant has reached its ultimate expression. Through unwavering faith, selfless service, and absolute devotion, you have become one of your patron's most trusted champions upon the mortal plane. The bond between your soul and your deity has become so profound that your actions are no longer seen as those of a mere follower, but as an extension of your patron's divine will.

The blessings granted through this covenant represent far more than power. They are a sacred responsibility entrusted only to those who have proven themselves worthy through countless trials and steadfast conviction. Every miracle you perform, every judgement you deliver, and every life you touch reflects directly upon the deity you serve.

Although every Religious Class reaches this pinnacle through the same Divine Covenant, each fulfils a unique role within their patron's design. Whether serving as a righteous champion, compassionate healer, fearless inquisitor, devoted priest, enlightened oracle, or another sacred calling, all stand among the greatest representatives of their chosen patron.

### Effects

- Your **Divine Covenant** reaches its final stage.
- Gain your patron's **Rank V Class Boon**.
- The Class Boon granted depends on both your **chosen patron** and your **Religious Class**.
- Unlock your patron's complete **Divine Authority**, **Class Features**, and any remaining **Patron Features** granted at Rank V.
- All talents that reference your patron continue to use the information contained within your chosen patron's Religious Pages.
- Your Sacred Oath is considered complete, marking you as one of your patron's chosen champions.
- Your Sacred Oath remains bound to your chosen patron unless altered through an extraordinary story event or direct divine intervention, at the GM's discretion.

### Lore

Only the rarest individuals ever complete the Divine Covenant. Their names are remembered within temples, recorded in sacred texts, and spoken with reverence by generations of the faithful. They are not worshipped as gods, but honoured as the greatest examples of mortal devotion, proving that absolute faith can bridge the divide between the mortal and the divine.

Legends tell that when such champions walk the world, the presence of their patron can almost be felt by those around them. Whether bringing hope, judgement, wisdom, balance, or even necessary destruction, they become living symbols of the ideals they have sworn to uphold. Though they remain mortal, their legacy often endures long after their final breath.

> *"A covenant does not end when its final blessing is granted. It lives on through every soul inspired by the one who was faithful enough to receive it."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**
