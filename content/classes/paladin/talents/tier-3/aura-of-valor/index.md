# Aura of Valor

## Overview
The courage of a true Paladin is infectious. Through unwavering faith and steadfast resolve, your presence inspires those around you to stand firm against impossible odds. Allies fighting beside you find renewed confidence in their own abilities, emboldened by the certainty that they do not stand alone.

Unlike miraculous blessings that must be consciously invoked, the Aura of Valor is a constant expression of your devotion. Whether defending a village, charging into battle, or standing as the last line between darkness and the innocent, your conviction strengthens the hearts of those who choose to fight at your side.

Though every patron manifests this aura differently, its purpose remains the same—to inspire greatness in those who share your cause. Radiant light, spectral ravens, blooming life, celestial flame, infernal embers, or countless other divine manifestations remind friend and foe alike that true courage can change the course of any battle.

## Rank 1
### Description

The courage of a true Paladin is infectious. Through unwavering faith and steadfast resolve, your presence inspires those around you to stand firm against impossible odds. Allies fighting beside you find renewed confidence in their own abilities, emboldened by the certainty that they do not stand alone.

Unlike miraculous blessings that must be consciously invoked, the Aura of Valor is a constant expression of your devotion. Whether defending a village, charging into battle, or standing as the last line between darkness and the innocent, your conviction strengthens the hearts of those who choose to fight at your side.

Though every patron manifests this aura differently, its purpose remains the same—to inspire greatness in those who share your cause. Radiant light, spectral ravens, blooming life, celestial flame, infernal embers, or countless other divine manifestations remind friend and foe alike that true courage can change the course of any battle.

### Effects

- While you are **conscious**, project a **5 metre Aura of Valor**.
- Allies within the aura gain **+2 on Attack Rolls**.
- Allies within the aura gain **+2 damage** with melee and ranged weapon attacks.
- A creature only benefits from one **Aura of Valor** at a time.
- The visual appearance of the aura is determined by your chosen patron.
- Additional effects granted by your patron's **Divine Authority** apply where applicable.

### Lore

Military commanders throughout Asteria have long sought the companionship of Paladins, not merely for their martial prowess, but for the remarkable effect their presence has upon those around them. Warriors who would normally falter often find themselves standing resolute, driven onward by the unwavering conviction radiating from the Paladin's soul.

Many songs tell of hopeless battles won because a single champion refused to yield. Those who survived rarely speak of overwhelming strength or divine miracles. Instead, they remember the feeling that, so long as the Paladin still stood, victory remained possible.

> *"Valor is not the absence of fear. It is the courage to inspire others even while fear stands beside you."*  
> **— Sir Aldren, First Shield of the Covenant**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your unwavering courage continues to inspire those who fight beside you. Warriors within your presence find themselves acting with greater confidence, striking with renewed determination and refusing to surrender even against overwhelming odds. Your patron's influence strengthens every ally who chooses to stand alongside you, forging a united front that is far greater than the sum of its parts.

As your mastery grows, the Aura of Valor becomes more than simple inspiration—it becomes a symbol of unity. Allies instinctively fight with greater discipline, trusting in one another and drawing strength from your steadfast leadership. Whether charging into battle or holding the line against impossible enemies, your presence reminds every companion that courage shared is courage multiplied.

### Effects

- While you are **conscious**, project a **7 metre Aura of Valor**.
- Allies within the aura gain **+2 on Attack Rolls**.
- Allies within the aura gain **+4 damage** with melee and ranged weapon attacks.
- The first melee weapon attack an ally makes each round while within the aura deals an additional **+1 Patron Damage**.
- A creature only benefits from one **Aura of Valor** at a time.
- The visual appearance and Patron Damage type are determined by your chosen patron.
- Additional effects granted by your patron's **Divine Authority** apply where applicable.

### Lore

Paladins who reach this level of mastery become natural rallying points upon the battlefield. Soldiers often recount that fear fades when they stand beside such champions, replaced instead by an overwhelming desire to protect those fighting alongside them. Entire companies have been known to hold impossible defensive lines simply because a Paladin's banner still flew above the battlefield.

Temples teach that true valor is not measured by the strength of a single warrior, but by the courage they awaken in others. Through unwavering faith, a Paladin becomes the heart of every formation, binding companions together through shared conviction and divine purpose.

> *"One brave soul may begin the charge, but true valor is measured by how many find the courage to follow."*  
> **— Sir Aldren, First Shield of the Covenant**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your presence has become a symbol of unwavering heroism, inspiring extraordinary acts of courage from those who fight beside you. Allies no longer draw strength solely from your conviction—they begin to fight with greater precision, coordination, and confidence, trusting completely in the leadership of your patron's chosen champion.

Every advance becomes more decisive, every strike more deliberate, and every victory more achievable. Warriors who once hesitated now press forward with renewed determination, emboldened by the knowledge that they stand beneath the watchful blessing of your patron. Whether leading a handful of companions or an entire army, your Aura of Valor transforms ordinary soldiers into steadfast heroes.

Though every patron expresses this blessing differently, all share the same purpose: to inspire courage through action and reward those willing to stand together against overwhelming darkness.

### Effects

- While you are **conscious**, project a **10 metre Aura of Valor**.
- Allies within the aura gain **+4 on Attack Rolls**.
- Allies within the aura gain **+4 damage** with melee and ranged weapon attacks.
- The first successful weapon attack each round made by an ally within the aura deals an additional **1d4 Patron Damage**.
- Allies within the aura gain **+2 on Critical Confirmation Rolls** (or **+2 to confirm Critical Hits**, if your system uses confirmation rolls).
- A creature may only benefit from one **Aura of Valor** at a time.
- The appearance of the aura and the Patron Damage type are determined by your chosen patron.
- Additional effects granted by your patron's **Divine Authority** apply where applicable.

### Lore

As legends spread across Asteria, veteran soldiers speak not only of the Paladin's strength, but of the impossible feats accomplished by those who fought beside them. Entire companies have stood firm against impossible odds, inspired by the unwavering conviction radiating from a single champion whose faith never faltered.

Many commanders believe that true valor is contagious. While courage may begin within one heart, it is through shared conviction that armies accomplish the impossible. Those blessed by a master's Aura of Valor often leave the battlefield believing they achieved far more than they ever thought themselves capable of.

> *"A hero may win a duel. A true champion gives others the courage to win a war."*  
> **— Sir Aldren, First Shield of the Covenant**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your unwavering courage has become legendary, inspiring extraordinary acts of heroism from all who fight beside you. Your patron's blessing radiates far beyond your own strength, urging companions to strike boldly, defend one another, and press the attack with unwavering confidence. Those who stand within your presence feel as though victory is always within reach, no matter how overwhelming the enemy.

Veteran warriors often describe this aura as impossible to ignore. Doubt fades, hesitation disappears, and every ally instinctively fights with greater purpose. Your patron's divine will flows through your leadership, transforming a band of adventurers into a unified force capable of overcoming challenges that would otherwise seem impossible.

To enemies, your presence is equally terrifying. Every successful strike delivered by your companions serves as another reminder that they are not facing a lone Paladin, but the united conviction of an entire company blessed by the divine.

### Effects

- While you are **conscious**, project a **12 metre Aura of Valor**.
- Allies within the aura gain **+2 on Attack Rolls**.
- Allies within the aura gain **+6 damage** with melee and ranged weapon attacks.
- The first successful weapon attack each round made by an ally within the aura deals an additional **1d6 Patron Damage**.
- Once per round, when an ally within the aura scores a **Critical Hit**, they immediately gain **+2 AC** until the start of their next turn.
- A creature may only benefit from one **Aura of Valor** at a time.
- The appearance of the aura and the **Patron Damage** type are determined by your chosen patron.
- Additional effects granted by your patron's **Divine Authority** apply where applicable.

### Lore

The greatest generals throughout Asteria have long sought the companionship of Paladins whose Aura of Valor could inspire entire armies to achieve what should have been impossible. Tales tell of defenders who held broken walls, exhausted soldiers who found renewed strength, and desperate charges that became glorious victories simply because one faithful champion refused to yield.

Such Paladins become living banners upon the battlefield. Wherever they stand, courage flourishes, hope endures, and even the darkest conflicts become opportunities for heroes to rise above fear and embrace the ideals their patron represents.

> *"Valor is not measured by the strength of one champion, but by the greatness they awaken in every soul willing to stand beside them."*  
> **— Sir Aldren, First Shield of the Covenant**

## Rank 5
### Description

Your courage has become an unshakable beacon of hope, inspiring extraordinary acts of heroism in all who stand beside you. The battlefield itself seems to rally around your presence as your patron's divine blessing flows through every ally who shares your cause. Fear is replaced by determination, hesitation gives way to decisive action, and even the darkest battles become opportunities to prove the strength of unwavering conviction.

Veteran Paladins who reach this level are remembered not only for their own deeds, but for the heroes they inspire. Entire companies fight harder beneath their banner, companions discover reserves of strength they never knew they possessed, and impossible victories become legendary tales passed down through generations. Wherever your Aura of Valor shines, your patron's ideals become a living force that unites those willing to stand together.

Though every patron manifests this blessing differently, each reminds friend and foe alike that true valor is never the work of one champion alone—it is the courage shared by all who fight with unwavering faith.

### Effects

- While you are **conscious**, project a **15 metre Aura of Valor**.
- Allies within the aura gain **+2 on Attack Rolls**.
- Allies within the aura gain **+8 damage** with melee and ranged weapon attacks.
- The first successful weapon attack each round made by an ally within the aura deals an additional **1d8 Patron Damage**.
- Once per round, when an ally within the aura scores a **Critical Hit**, they immediately gain **+2 AC** until the start of their next turn.
- Once per round, an ally within the aura may **reroll one missed weapon attack**. The second result must be used.
- A creature may only benefit from one **Aura of Valor** at a time.
- The appearance of the aura and the **Patron Damage** type are determined by your chosen patron.
- Additional effects granted by your patron's **Divine Authority** apply where applicable.

### Lore

The greatest Paladins in history are remembered not because they stood alone against impossible odds, but because they inspired others to do the same. Their courage became contagious, their conviction unwavering, and their presence transformed ordinary soldiers into legendary heroes whose names would be remembered for centuries.

The oldest temples teach that this is the highest expression of valor—not personal glory, but the ability to awaken greatness within others. Those blessed with such an aura become living symbols of their patron's ideals, carrying hope wherever they travel and reminding all who witness them that true courage is strongest when it is shared.

> *"A single hero may save a life. A true champion teaches others they were heroes all along."*  
> **— Sir Aldren, First Shield of the Covenant**
