# Bastion of Faith

## Overview
Your faith becomes an unshakable foundation, allowing you to assume a defensive stance that transforms you into a steadfast protector. Rather than seeking swift victory, you prepare yourself to endure the enemy's assault, trusting your patron to strengthen your resolve against every blow.

While Bastion of Faith is active, your movements become deliberate and unwavering. Every step is taken with purpose, every stance rooted in conviction. Though enemies may attempt to force you aside, your devotion anchors both body and spirit, making you increasingly difficult to displace.

To a seasoned Paladin, the greatest victory is not measured by the enemies they defeat, but by the allies who remain standing because they refused to yield.

## Rank 1
### Description

Your faith becomes an unshakable foundation, allowing you to assume a defensive stance that transforms you into a steadfast protector. Rather than seeking swift victory, you prepare yourself to endure the enemy's assault, trusting your patron to strengthen your resolve against every blow.

While Bastion of Faith is active, your movements become deliberate and unwavering. Every step is taken with purpose, every stance rooted in conviction. Though enemies may attempt to force you aside, your devotion anchors both body and spirit, making you increasingly difficult to displace.

To a seasoned Paladin, the greatest victory is not measured by the enemies they defeat, but by the allies who remain standing because they refused to yield.

### Effects

- Spend **25 MP** and **10 SP** to enter **Bastion of Faith**.
- Bastion of Faith lasts for **5 Rounds**.
- Gain **+2 AC** while Bastion of Faith is active.
- Gain **+2 on all checks or Saving Throws made to resist forced movement**, being shoved, knocked back, pulled, grappled, or otherwise repositioned against your will.
- The visual manifestation of Bastion of Faith is determined by your chosen patron.
- Additional defensive blessings granted by your patron's **Divine Authority** apply where applicable.

### Lore

Among every holy order, the Bastion is admired not for overwhelming strength, but for unwavering resolve. These champions willingly stand where others would retreat, trusting that their patron will grant them the courage to endure whatever darkness lies ahead.

The oldest fortresses of Asteria are said to have been inspired by such warriors. Their walls were built to imitate the same unyielding conviction displayed by the first Paladins who stood firm against impossible odds, refusing to surrender even when the world itself seemed determined to move them.

> *"The mountain does not resist the storm through strength alone. It stands because it refuses to move."*  
> **— Sir Garrick, Warden of the Eternal Gate**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your conviction grows stronger with every battle fought in your patron's name. While others rely upon strength alone, you have learned that true resilience comes from unwavering faith and the willingness to place yourself between danger and those who cannot protect themselves.

While Bastion of Faith is active, your presence becomes a rallying point for allies. Enemies find it increasingly difficult to force you from your position, while your steadfast resolve allows you to intervene whenever your companions are threatened. Every successful defence strengthens your reputation as an unyielding guardian whose faith cannot be shaken.

To your allies, you become a reassuring presence upon the battlefield. To your enemies, you become an obstacle that refuses to yield.

### Effects

- Spend **30 MP** and **10 SP** to enter **Bastion of Faith**.
- Bastion of Faith lasts for **5 Rounds**.
- Gain **+3 AC** while Bastion of Faith is active.
- Gain **+4 on all checks or Saving Throws made to resist forced movement**, being shoved, knocked back, pulled, grappled, or otherwise repositioned against your will.
- **Once per round**, when a creature within your melee reach targets an allied creature with a melee attack, you may use your **Reaction** to impose **Disadvantage** on that attack.
- The visual manifestation of Bastion of Faith is determined by your chosen patron.
- Additional defensive blessings granted by your patron's **Divine Authority** apply where applicable.

### Lore

Every great fortress begins with a single stone that refuses to break. Likewise, every legendary guardian begins with a single vow—to stand firm regardless of the danger before them. Through years of faithful service, a Paladin learns that the strongest defence is often their own willingness to become the shield others need.

Many temples teach that a Bastion is not defined by the armour they wear, but by the lives they protect. Those who master this sacred stance become symbols of hope, standing as unwavering guardians while chaos crashes harmlessly against their unbreakable resolve.

> *"Walls are built from stone. A true bastion is built from conviction."*  
> **— Sir Garrick, Warden of the Eternal Gate**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your devotion has become an unbreakable foundation upon which others may stand. While Bastion of Faith is active, every blow endured and every attack turned aside strengthens your resolve, allowing your patron's blessing to reinforce both body and spirit. Your enemies soon realise that attempting to break your defence only serves to strengthen your determination.

Veteran Paladins understand that true endurance is not measured by how little damage they suffer, but by their ability to continue standing when others would have fallen. Through absolute conviction, your patron rewards your steadfast defence with renewed strength, enabling you to hold the line against even the fiercest assault.

As your reputation spreads, allies instinctively rally behind your unwavering presence, while enemies begin to recognise the futility of attempting to force aside a guardian whose faith has become stronger than steel.

### Effects

- Spend **35 MP** and **15 SP** to enter **Bastion of Faith**.
- Bastion of Faith lasts for **6 Rounds**.
- Gain **+4 AC** while Bastion of Faith is active.
- Gain **+6 on all checks or Saving Throws made to resist forced movement**, being shoved, knocked back, pulled, grappled, or otherwise repositioned against your will.
- **Once per round**, when a creature within your melee reach targets an allied creature with a melee attack, you may use your **Reaction** to impose **Disadvantage** on that attack.
- **Once per round**, when you successfully avoid a melee attack, or reduce its damage through a successful defensive action, gain **Temporary HP equal to your Charisma Modifier + Half your Character Level**.
- Temporary HP gained through Bastion of Faith does not stack. If you gain Temporary HP again, keep the higher value.
- The visual manifestation of Bastion of Faith is determined by your chosen patron.
- Additional defensive blessings granted by your patron's **Divine Authority** apply where applicable.

### Lore

The greatest bastions are remembered not because they never fell, but because they endured long enough for hope to triumph. Every strike deflected, every ally protected, and every step held against overwhelming odds becomes another testament to the strength granted through unwavering faith.

The oldest military orders teach that a Bastion's greatest weapon is not their sword, but their refusal to yield. Legends tell of champions who stood alone at broken gates, holding back entire armies until reinforcements arrived, their conviction becoming an inspiration that outlived them by centuries.

> *"Steel may guard the body, but conviction guards the soul. When both stand together, no force can break them."*  
> **— Sir Garrick, Warden of the Eternal Gate**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your devotion has become an immovable bulwark against the darkness. No longer content to simply endure your enemies' assaults, you willingly place yourself between danger and those under your protection, trusting completely in your patron to grant you the strength to bear burdens that would overwhelm lesser warriors.

Within Bastion of Faith, your resolve inspires absolute confidence in your allies. They know that while you continue to stand, they will never face the enemy alone. Every strike intercepted and every wound endured serves as another testament to the sacred covenant you uphold, proving that true faith is measured not by the lives you take, but by the lives you save.

Among the greatest holy orders, this mastery is regarded as the defining quality of a true guardian. To become a Bastion is to accept that your own safety is secondary to the protection of those who fight beside you.

### Effects

- Spend **40 MP** and **15 SP** to enter **Bastion of Faith**.
- Bastion of Faith lasts for **6 Rounds**.
- Gain **+5 AC** while Bastion of Faith is active.
- Gain **+8 on all checks or Saving Throws made to resist forced movement**, being shoved, knocked back, pulled, grappled, or otherwise repositioned against your will.
- **Once per round**, when an allied creature within **3 metres** is hit by a melee or ranged weapon attack, you may use your **Reaction** to become the new target of that attack before damage is rolled.
- Apply your own **AC, resistances, and defensive effects** against the intercepted attack.
- **Once per round**, when you successfully avoid a melee attack or reduce its damage through a successful defensive action, gain **Temporary HP equal to your Charisma Modifier + Half your Character Level**.
- Temporary HP gained through Bastion of Faith does not stack. If you gain Temporary HP again, keep the higher value.
- The visual manifestation of Bastion of Faith is determined by your chosen patron.
- Additional defensive blessings granted by your patron's **Divine Authority** apply where applicable.

### Lore

The legends of Asteria remember many great warriors, yet few are honoured as highly as those who willingly stood between danger and the innocent. Such champions became living shields, placing themselves in harm's way without hesitation, knowing their patron's blessing would grant them the resolve to endure.

Temples teach that this is the highest calling of a guardian. Strength is not measured by how many enemies fall before you, but by how many lives continue because you chose to stand where others could not.

> *"A true shield is never carried for its own protection. It is raised for the sake of another."*  
> **— Sir Garrick, Warden of the Eternal Gate**

## Rank 5
### Description

Your faith has become an unbreakable fortress, allowing you to stand as your patron's ultimate guardian upon the battlefield. Every trial endured, every ally protected, and every oath upheld has forged a spirit that refuses to yield. When Bastion of Faith is invoked, you become the living embodiment of steadfast devotion, a champion whose resolve cannot be shaken by mortal strength alone.

Enemies soon discover that moving you is as futile as attempting to push back the mountains themselves. Your patron's blessing anchors your body and soul, while your unwavering determination allows you to intercept even the deadliest blows intended for those under your protection. So long as you continue to stand, your allies know there is still hope.

Among every holy order, reaching this mastery is regarded as the highest expression of selfless courage. To become a Bastion is to willingly bear the burden of others, trusting that your patron will grant you the strength to endure until the battle is won.

### Effects

- Spend **50 MP** and **20 SP** to enter **Bastion of Faith**.
- Bastion of Faith lasts for **8 Rounds**.
- Gain **+6 AC** while Bastion of Faith is active.
- Gain **Advantage** on all checks and Saving Throws made to resist being shoved, knocked back, pulled, grappled, knocked prone, or otherwise forcibly moved.
- Creatures attempting to forcibly move you have **Disadvantage** on any opposed rolls or ability checks made to do so, where applicable.
- **Once per round**, when an allied creature within **5 metres** is hit by a melee or ranged weapon attack, you may use your **Reaction** to become the new target of that attack before damage is rolled.
- Apply your own **AC, resistances, armour, and defensive effects** against the intercepted attack.
- **Once per round**, when you successfully avoid an attack or reduce its damage through a successful defensive action, gain **Temporary HP equal to your Character Level + your Charisma Modifier**.
- Temporary HP gained through Bastion of Faith does not stack. If gained again, keep the higher value.
- The visual manifestation of Bastion of Faith is determined by your chosen patron.
- Additional defensive blessings granted by your patron's **Divine Authority** apply where applicable.

### Lore

Only the greatest champions of the divine ever attain the title of Bastion. Their unwavering conviction becomes legendary, their names forever remembered alongside the countless lives they saved. To friend and foe alike, they are not simply warriors—they are the immovable foundation upon which hope itself is built.

Many of Asteria's oldest fortresses bear statues of such guardians, depicting them standing resolute against impossible odds. Though kingdoms have risen and fallen around these monuments, they remain enduring symbols of a timeless truth: the strongest shield is not forged from steel, but from a soul that refuses to surrender.

> *"When the storm comes, become the mountain. When darkness advances, become the wall. Let your faith be the fortress that others call home."*  
> **— Sir Garrick, Warden of the Eternal Gate**
