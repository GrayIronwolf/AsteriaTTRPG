# Unyielding Conviction

## Overview
There are moments when even the greatest champions falter. A failed leap, a crippling curse, or a test of will can mean the difference between salvation and ruin. Yet a Paladin does not surrender to failure so easily. Through unwavering faith, you may call upon your patron to grant you one final opportunity to overcome the impossible.

When invoking Unyielding Conviction, you place your trust entirely in your patron. Your own skill gives way to divine guidance as fate itself is rewritten for a single fleeting moment. This blessing is never granted lightly, demanding both physical endurance and divine energy to invoke.

Those who witness this miracle often describe it as impossible to explain. To the faithful, however, the answer is simple—where conviction is absolute, even fate may bend.

## Rank 1
### Description

### Effects

- **Once per Long Rest**, after failing a **Saving Throw** or **Skill Check**, you may invoke **Unyielding Conviction**.
- Spend **10 MP** and **10 SP** to activate this ability.
- Repeat the failed roll using your **Luck Modifier** in place of the modifier normally applied to the roll.
- If the original action required additional **MP, SP, HP, consumables, charges, ammunition, or other resources**, those costs must also be paid again before the second attempt is made.
- If the failed roll involved **multiple dice** (such as **Advantage** or **Disadvantage**), you may reroll **only one** of those dice. The rerolled die replaces its original result before determining the final outcome.
- The repeated attempt is treated as a completely new roll.
- You must accept the result of the second attempt, even if it is lower than the original.
- Additional effects granted by your patron's **Divine Authority** apply where applicable.

### Lore

The scriptures of every faith tell stories of champions who faced certain failure, only to find the strength to rise once more. Some speak of an unseen hand guiding them, while others recall hearing nothing more than a quiet whisper urging them to stand again. Whatever form the miracle takes, all agree that it is not earned through strength alone, but through absolute trust in one's patron.

Among the faithful, Unyielding Conviction is regarded as one of the purest demonstrations of divine favour. A patron does not guarantee success, nor do they erase failure. Instead, they grant their champion one final opportunity to prove that their faith is worthy of the miracle they have received.

> *"The gods do not promise victory. They promise that those who refuse to yield will never stand alone."*  
> **— High Marshal Aurelius, First Champion of the Sacred Host**
