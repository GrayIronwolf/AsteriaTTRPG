# Vigilant Protector

## Overview
A true Paladin never truly lowers their guard. Even during moments of rest, your unwavering faith allows your patron to quietly guide your senses toward approaching danger. Though your body may sleep, your spirit remains ever watchful, instinctively alert to hostile beings that threaten those under your protection.

When battle is joined, that same divine awareness allows you to react with remarkable speed. Rather than watching helplessly as an ally suffers a devastating blow, you instinctively place yourself between them and danger, trusting your faith, armour, and unwavering resolve to endure what others cannot.

To those who travel beside you, your presence offers more than protection—it offers peace of mind. They know that, while you still stand, someone is always watching over them.

## Rank 1
### Description

A true Paladin never truly lowers their guard. Even during moments of rest, your unwavering faith allows your patron to quietly guide your senses toward approaching danger. Though your body may sleep, your spirit remains ever watchful, instinctively alert to hostile beings that threaten those under your protection.

When battle is joined, that same divine awareness allows you to react with remarkable speed. Rather than watching helplessly as an ally suffers a devastating blow, you instinctively place yourself between them and danger, trusting your faith, armour, and unwavering resolve to endure what others cannot.

To those who travel beside you, your presence offers more than protection—it offers peace of mind. They know that, while you still stand, someone is always watching over them.

### Effects

#### Passive — Ever Vigilant

- Even while **resting**, **sleeping**, or otherwise not actively standing watch, you remain spiritually alert.
- You may still attempt **Perception Checks** to detect the approach of **hostile creatures or humanoids** that present an immediate threat to you or your allies.
- These Perception Checks are made with **Disadvantage**.
- **Ever Vigilant** cannot be used to detect traps, poisons, hidden objects, environmental hazards, magical effects, or any other non-creature dangers.
- Additional passive effects granted by your patron's **Divine Authority** apply where applicable.

#### Active — Vigilant Protector

- **Reaction**
- Spend **10 MP** and **30 SP**.
- **Cooldown:** **4 Rounds**.
- When an allied creature within **5 metres** is targeted by a melee or ranged weapon attack, you may activate **Vigilant Protector**.
- Move up to **2 metres** towards the targeted ally without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
- You must end this movement adjacent to the targeted ally.
- You may choose to become the new target of the triggering attack.
- Resolve the attack against your own **AC**, armour, resistances, and defensive effects.
- If you cannot reach the targeted ally, this ability cannot be activated.

### Lore

The oldest Paladin Orders teach that vigilance is the first duty of every guardian. Long before blades are drawn or shields are raised, a watchful protector has already begun safeguarding those entrusted to their care. Through unwavering devotion, a Paladin's patron grants subtle guidance, stirring their senses whenever hostile creatures draw near, even as they rest.

Many tales speak of champions who awoke moments before an ambush or stepped between an ally and certain death with impossible speed. Whether these moments were instinct, discipline, or divine intervention is a matter of faith. To a Paladin, however, the answer is simple—their duty is to stand between darkness and those who cannot defend themselves.

> *"A guardian's greatest strength is not the shield they carry, but the vigilance to raise it before danger strikes."*  
> **— Sir Garrick, Warden of the Eternal Gate**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your patron's blessing has transformed your vigilance into an instinctive act of divine guardianship. Every heartbeat is filled with purpose, every movement guided by an unwavering resolve to protect those who stand beside you. Whether danger comes from blade, arrow, or spell, you answer without hesitation, placing yourself between your allies and certain harm.

Even while your companions sleep, your spirit remains alert, ever watchful for those who would threaten the innocent. In battle, your interventions become almost supernatural, as though your patron guides every step before your conscious mind can react. To your allies, your arrival is a source of hope; to your enemies, it is the crushing realization that their target is no longer unprotected.

### Effects

#### Passive — Ever Vigilant

- Even while **resting**, **sleeping**, or otherwise not actively standing watch, you remain spiritually alert.
- You may still attempt **Perception Checks** to detect the approach of **hostile creatures or humanoids** that present an immediate threat to you or your allies.
- These Perception Checks are made **normally**.
- **Ever Vigilant** cannot be used to detect traps, poisons, hidden objects, environmental hazards, magical effects, or any other non-creature dangers.
- Additional passive effects granted through your patron's **Divine Authority** apply where applicable.

#### Active — Vigilant Protector

- **Reaction**
- Spend **10 MP** and **30 SP**.
- **Cooldown:** **2 Rounds**.
- When an allied creature within **12 metres** is targeted by a **melee attack, ranged attack, or single-target hostile ability**, you may activate **Vigilant Protector**.
- Move up to **8 metres** towards the targeted ally without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
- You must end this movement adjacent to the targeted ally.
- You may choose to become the new target of the triggering attack or ability, provided you are a valid target.
- Resolve the attack or ability against your own **AC**, armour, resistances, Saving Throws, and other defensive effects as appropriate.
- After successfully intercepting the attack or ability, reduce the damage you suffer by **your Charisma Modifier** (minimum 1).
- If you cannot reach the targeted ally, or if you are not a valid target for the triggering ability, this ability cannot be activated.
