# Divine Aegis

## Overview
When all hope seems lost, you may call upon your patron to manifest a Divine Aegis—a magnificent dome of sacred power that shields those within from the chaos beyond. More than a simple magical barrier, the Divine Aegis represents a fragment of your patron's divine domain, standing as an unyielding sanctuary amidst the fury of battle.

Though every Divine Aegis differs in appearance according to the patron who created it, each serves the same purpose. Some shimmer with radiant light, others swirl with celestial fire, sacred tides, spectral ravens, flourishing vines, crimson blood, or countless other divine manifestations. Whatever its form, those within know they stand beneath the protection of something far greater than themselves.

The Divine Aegis is not merely a wall—it is a declaration that, for a short time, the battlefield itself bends to the will of your patron.

## Rank 1
### Description

When all hope seems lost, you may call upon your patron to manifest a Divine Aegis—a magnificent dome of sacred power that shields those within from the chaos beyond. ### Effects

- Spend **250 MP** and **30 SP** to create a stationary **Divine Aegis** centred on yourself.
- **Cooldown:** Long Rest.
- The Divine Aegis forms a **6-metre radius dome**.
- The Divine Aegis has **200 HP**.
- All physical and magical damage directed at the barrier is dealt to the Divine Aegis instead.
- When the Divine Aegis reaches **0 HP**, it immediately shatters and disappears.
- Creatures inside the Divine Aegis may leave it freely.
- Once a creature willingly leaves the Divine Aegis, it cannot re-enter that same Divine Aegis.
- The Divine Aegis blocks:
  - Weapon attacks.
  - Ammunition.
  - Magical projectiles.
  - Breath weapons.
  - Area effects that must pass through the barrier.
- Creatures, spells, enchantments, and effects aligned with your patron's **Divine Resonance** may pass through the Divine Aegis unhindered.
- Interactions with creatures and effects aligned with your patron's **Divine Opposition** are determined by your patron's **Religious Page**.
- Additional interactions granted through your patron's **Divine Authority** apply where applicable.

### Lore

Among the greatest miracles ever witnessed upon the battlefields of Asteria, few inspire more hope than the appearance of a Divine Aegis. Entire villages have survived impossible sieges beneath these sacred domes, while wounded champions found precious moments to recover before returning to the fight. To those sheltered within, the barrier is more than protection—it is a visible reminder that their patron has not abandoned them.

Every Divine Aegis reflects the nature of the deity who grants it. Though no two barriers appear alike, each marks the boundary between the mortal world and a fragment of the patron's divine domain. While it stands, the faithful know they are sheltered beneath the watchful gaze of something eternal.

> *"A wall of stone protects the body. A Divine Aegis protects hope itself."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**

Your mastery over the Divine Aegis deepens, allowing your patron's sanctuary to manifest with greater strength and stability. As your covenant grows stronger, so too does your patron's influence upon the barrier. Its appearance becomes more vivid, its divine presence more overwhelming, and its connection to your patron's element more apparent to both ally and enemy alike.

Many military commanders throughout Asteria have witnessed hopeless battles transformed simply because a Divine Aegis stood between their soldiers and certain destruction.

## Rank 3
### Description

Your Divine Aegis has grown into a true sanctuary of your patron's will. The barrier no longer serves solely as protection from the chaos beyond—it becomes sacred ground where allies may gather, recover, and prepare for the battles yet to come. While the world outside rages with steel, fire, and magic, those within stand beneath the unwavering protection of your patron.

The dome radiates divine authority unique to the deity who empowers it. To the faithful it is a place of hope and refuge; to their enemies it is an unbreakable reminder that the divine still walks beside its chosen champions. Every strike against the barrier weakens its strength, yet every moment it remains standing preserves the lives of those sheltered within.

Whether raised to defend a wounded ally, hold a strategic position, or weather an overwhelming assault, the Divine Aegis stands as one of the greatest miracles a Paladin can bestow upon the battlefield.

## Rank 5
### Description

Your Divine Aegis has reached its ultimate form, becoming a true manifestation of your patron's divine domain upon the mortal battlefield. Within its radiant walls, allies find sanctuary from devastation while the forces of darkness crash endlessly against an unbreakable testament of faith. Entire armies have rallied beneath such miracles, and legends tell of kingdoms surviving impossible sieges because a single Paladin refused to let the Aegis fall.
