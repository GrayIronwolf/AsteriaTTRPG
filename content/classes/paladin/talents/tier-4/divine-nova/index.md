# Divine Nova

## Overview
When the battle reaches its darkest hour, you may call upon your patron to release a devastating surge of divine power from your very soul. This sacred eruption, known throughout Asteria as a Divine Nova, unleashes divine judgement upon those who oppose your patron while simultaneously blessing those who stand within their divine favour.

No two Divine Novae are ever identical. Every patron manifests this miracle according to their own divine nature. Some erupt as radiant light, others as crimson blood, spectral ravens, celestial fire, sacred waters, blooming life, or countless other divine manifestations. Though their appearance differs, every Divine Nova carries the unmistakable presence of the deity who bestowed it.

Those aligned with your patron's Divine Resonance feel warmth, strength, and renewed vitality. Those aligned with your patron's Divine Opposition experience the full weight of divine judgement, suffering the wrath of the covenant you have sworn to uphold.

## Rank 1
### Description

When the battle reaches its darkest hour, you may call upon your patron to release a devastating surge of divine power from your very soul. This sacred eruption, known throughout Asteria as a Divine Nova, unleashes divine judgement upon those who oppose your patron while simultaneously blessing those who stand within their divine favour.

No two Divine Novae are ever identical. Every patron manifests this miracle according to their own divine nature. Some erupt as radiant light, others as crimson blood, spectral ravens, celestial fire, sacred waters, blooming life, or countless other divine manifestations. Though their appearance differs, every Divine Nova carries the unmistakable presence of the deity who bestowed it.

Those aligned with your patron's Divine Resonance feel warmth, strength, and renewed vitality. Those aligned with your patron's Divine Opposition experience the full weight of divine judgement, suffering the wrath of the covenant you have sworn to uphold.

### Effects

- Spend **150 MP** and **40 SP** to unleash a **Divine Nova** centred on yourself.
- **Cooldown:** **Long Rest**.
- The Divine Nova affects all creatures within a **10-metre radius**.
- Creatures aligned with your patron's **Divine Opposition** suffer **4d8 Patron Damage**.
- Creatures aligned with your patron's **Divine Resonance** recover **2d6 HP**.
- The **damage type**, **creatures affected**, and **visual manifestation** are determined by your chosen patron's **Religious Page**.
- Creatures with immunity, resistance, or vulnerability to your patron's damage type resolve those interactions normally.
- Additional effects granted by your patron's **Divine Authority** apply where applicable.

### Lore

Among the greatest miracles recorded throughout Asteria's history, few are spoken of with greater reverence than the Divine Nova. It is said that, for but a single heartbeat, the veil between the mortal realm and the divine grows impossibly thin, allowing a patron's will to manifest through their most devoted champion.

Those who have witnessed such miracles rarely remember the destruction alone. Instead, they speak of the overwhelming presence that accompanied it—a feeling of standing before something infinitely greater than themselves. To the faithful, it is a reminder that while mortals wage war upon the world, the gods still watch over the fate of every soul.

> *"A Divine Nova is not born from anger, nor from vengeance. It is the moment a patron reminds the world that their will still shapes the course of history."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your Divine Nova grows in both power and reach, allowing a greater portion of your patron's will to manifest upon the battlefield. The divine energy surging through your body erupts with overwhelming force, bringing swift judgement to those who oppose your patron while extending a sacred blessing to those who stand within their favour.

Though every Divine Nova reflects the nature of its patron, its purpose remains unchanged—to remind the world that divine power is neither indiscriminate nor without purpose. Those who walk in harmony with your patron are renewed by its presence, while those who embody your patron's Divine Opposition are struck by righteous judgement.

Each invocation strengthens your connection to your patron, allowing more of their divine essence to briefly touch the mortal realm before fading once more.

### Lore

Only those whose faith has deepened through countless trials are capable of unleashing a Divine Nova of this magnitude. Ancient texts describe these miracles as moments where the boundary between deity and disciple becomes almost imperceptible, allowing the patron's judgement to wash across the battlefield in a single overwhelming surge.

Witnesses often describe an indescribable feeling accompanying the Nova—an overwhelming certainty that they stood in the presence of something infinitely greater than themselves. Whether experienced as hope, fear, peace, or awe depends entirely upon the relationship between the observer and the patron whose will has been revealed.

> *"When a Divine Nova blossoms across the battlefield, even kings remember that every throne exists beneath the gaze of the gods."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**

### Lore

Throughout history, only the most devoted Paladins have wielded a Divine Nova of this magnitude. Such miracles remind both ally and enemy alike that the gods do not merely watch the world—they are capable of shaping its destiny through those who have earned their favour.

Within the grand temples of Asteria, these moments are studied not as acts of destruction, but as manifestations of divine purpose. Every Divine Nova serves as a reminder that the covenant between patron and champion is more than faith alone—it is a sacred bond capable of briefly allowing the will of the divine to touch the mortal realm.

> *"When a champion becomes the voice of their patron, even the battlefield falls silent to bear witness."*

## Rank 4
### Description

Your Divine Nova has become one of the greatest miracles your patron is willing to bestow upon a mortal champion. When invoked, divine power erupts from your soul in a breathtaking wave of sacred energy, washing across the battlefield with overwhelming force. For a fleeting moment, your patron's presence becomes undeniable as divine judgement falls upon those who stand in opposition while faithful allies are embraced by a surge of restorative grace.

Though every Divine Nova shares the same purpose, no two manifestations are ever alike. Some blaze with celestial radiance, others surge with raging infernos, spectral ravens, sacred tides, flourishing life, crimson blood, or countless other divine expressions. Regardless of its form, every Nova carries the unmistakable authority of the deity who empowers it.

Veteran warriors often claim that witnessing a Divine Nova changes them forever. Some describe overwhelming peace, others terrifying awe, but all leave the battlefield certain they have stood in the presence of the divine.

### Lore

Only a handful of champions throughout Asteria's history have ever unleashed a Divine Nova of this magnitude. Such miracles are forever remembered within the sacred histories of every temple.

The High Orders teach that a Divine Nova is never an act of anger or vengeance. It is a declaration that the covenant between patron and champion has reached such strength that, for one brief moment, a fragment of the divine is permitted to reshape the battlefield according to the patron's will.

Your Divine Nova has reached its ultimate form, becoming one of the greatest miracles your patron may bestow upon a mortal champion. With a single invocation, an overwhelming surge of divine power erupts from your soul, transforming the battlefield into a fleeting reflection of your patron's domain. For a single, breathtaking moment, the mortal realm bears witness to the majesty of the divine.

Every Divine Nova is unique to the deity who grants it. Some burst forth as blinding celestial radiance, others as roaring infernos, sacred tides, spectral ravens, flourishing life, crimson blood, or manifestations beyond mortal understanding. Though each appears differently, all carry the same unmistakable truth—the will of your patron has descended upon the battlefield.

To allies who walk in harmony with your patron, the Nova is a blessing that restores hope and strength. To those who embody your patron's Divine Opposition, it is a reminder that even the mightiest mortal cannot stand forever against divine judgement.

### Lore

Legends tell of only a handful of Divine Novae ever reaching this level of power. Whether remembered as acts of salvation or judgement depends entirely upon which side of the covenant witnessed the miracle.
