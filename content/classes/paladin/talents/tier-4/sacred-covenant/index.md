# Sacred Covenant

## Overview
Your devotion has matured into a sacred covenant recognised by your patron. Through prayer, sacrifice, and unwavering faith, you may present an offering worthy of the divine, inviting your patron to answer with a miracle beyond the limits of mortal magic. Unlike other divine abilities, Sacred Covenant is not a spell to be controlled—it is a request placed before a god.

Every offering carries meaning. Some patrons value great wealth or powerful relics, while others cherish acts of self-sacrifice, unwavering conviction, or gifts with deep personal significance. The greatest miracles are rarely earned through material value alone, but through the sincerity of the sacrifice offered.

No two Sacred Covenants are ever identical. Every miracle reflects the ideals, personality, and divine portfolio of the patron receiving the offering. Whether answered with overwhelming judgement, miraculous salvation, divine guidance, or an unforeseen blessing, the outcome is always shaped by the will of the deity.

## Rank 1
### Description

Your devotion has matured into a sacred covenant recognised by your patron. Through prayer, sacrifice, and unwavering faith, you may present an offering worthy of the divine, inviting your patron to answer with a miracle beyond the limits of mortal magic. Unlike other divine abilities, Sacred Covenant is not a spell to be controlled—it is a request placed before a god.

The greatest miracles are rarely earned through material value alone, but through the sincerity of the sacrifice offered.

Whether answered with overwhelming judgement, miraculous salvation, divine guidance, or an unforeseen blessing, the outcome is always shaped by the will of the deity.

### Effects

- Once per **Long Rest**, you may perform a **Sacred Covenant**.
- As part of the ritual, present an offering to your patron.
- An offering may consist of **Mana (MP), Stamina (SP), Health (HP), Zeal, currency, Soul Stones, relics, enchanted items, weapons, armour, magical materials, treasured possessions, death saving throws or another meaningful sacrifice approved by the GM.**
- Unless otherwise stated, all offerings are **permanently consumed** when accepted by your patron.
- Your patron may **accept**, **reject**, or **partially accept** the offering.
- If accepted, your patron grants a **Divine Miracle** appropriate to:
  - The value of the offering.
  - The significance of the offering.
  - The sincerity of the sacrifice.
  - Your standing with the patron.
  - The patron's Divine Resonance, Divine Opposition, and Divine Authority.
- The exact miracle is determined by the GM and should always reflect the nature and ideals of the patron.
- Miracles should remain within reasonable narrative and mechanical limits appropriate for the current campaign and the patron's divine influence.
- The appearance of every miracle is determined entirely by your chosen patron.

### Lore

The oldest scriptures teach that every covenant is built upon sacrifice. While many pray for miracles, few are willing to surrender something of true value in return. The gods are not merchants, nor are they compelled by mortal offerings. Instead, they judge the heart behind every gift, weighing conviction above wealth and devotion above pride.

Throughout Asteria's history, Sacred Covenants have shaped the fate of kingdoms. Some champions surrendered legendary weapons to save a city from ruin, while others offered years of accumulated wealth, fragments of their own life, or treasured family heirlooms in exchange for a single miracle. The greatest offerings were never remembered for what was given, but for what they represented.

Among the faithful, it is said that no offering is ever truly forgotten. Every accepted sacrifice strengthens the invisible bond between patron and champion, preparing the soul for the greater destinies that await those chosen to walk the divine path.

> *"The gods do not measure the weight of an offering by what leaves your hands, but by what leaves your heart."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your covenant has deepened, allowing you to present offerings of greater meaning and significance before your patron. No longer limited to simple sacrifices, your devotion is recognised as that of a trusted champion whose prayers carry greater weight within the divine realm. Though your patron remains free to accept or reject every offering, your growing bond allows increasingly extraordinary miracles to be bestowed.

Every covenant is unique. A weapon passed down through generations, a Soul Stone containing a fragment of immense power, a fortune accumulated through years of hardship, or even a portion of your own life force may all become worthy offerings. What matters most is not their value alone, but the conviction with which they are surrendered.

As your faith matures, your patron begins to reveal more of their divine will, answering worthy sacrifices with miracles that can alter the course of battles, save entire communities, or shape the destiny of those who place their trust in the divine.

- The exact miracle is determined by the GM and should always reflect the ideals, personality, and divine domain of the patron.

### Lore

Few champions ever earn the trust required to invoke a Sacred Covenant of this magnitude. Those who do discover that the gods do not merely observe sacrifice—they remember it. Every offering accepted strengthens the invisible thread binding champion and patron, allowing divine power to flow more freely between the mortal and immortal realms.

Many sacred texts recount miracles born from impossible sacrifices. Entire kingdoms were spared because a champion willingly surrendered everything they possessed, trusting completely in the wisdom of their patron. Such stories endure not because of the miracles themselves, but because they remind the faithful that true devotion is measured by what one is willing to give, not what one hopes to receive.

> *"The covenant grows not through the greatness of the miracle, but through the greatness of the sacrifice."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**

## Rank 5
### Description

Your Sacred Covenant has reached its highest expression. The bond between you and your patron has become so profound that your offerings are no longer viewed as mere sacrifices, but as sacred acts of absolute devotion. Few mortals throughout history have ever achieved such unwavering faith, for only those who have proven themselves through conviction, sacrifice, and selflessness are entrusted with miracles of this magnitude.

Every offering placed before your patron carries immense spiritual significance. Wealth, relics, kingdoms, legendary artefacts, fragments of your own soul, or even years of your remaining life may all become worthy sacrifices, provided they are offered with genuine devotion. The gods do not measure value by gold alone—they judge the heart of the one making the sacrifice.

When a Sacred Covenant of this rank is accepted, the miracle that follows becomes the direct expression of your patron's divine will. Such events are remembered for generations, recorded within holy scripture, and often become the foundation of legends that outlive the champion who invoked them.

### Lore

The oldest temples speak of Sacred Covenants with reverence, for they represent the highest form of devotion a mortal may offer before ascending to become a true divine champion. Every Legendary Miracle recorded throughout history began with a single act of faith—a champion willing to surrender something irreplaceable without certainty that anything would be given in return.

The gods have never been bound by mortal expectation. Some answer immediately, others remain silent, and a few respond in ways no mortal could ever foresee. Yet every accepted covenant leaves an indelible mark upon the world, reminding all who witness it that faith is not measured by the miracle received, but by the willingness to sacrifice without expecting one.

Those who master the Sacred Covenant stand upon the threshold of ascension. Beyond this point lies only the path of the **Avatar of Conviction**, where the champion ceases to merely serve their patron and instead becomes the living embodiment of their divine will.

> *"The greatest covenant is not the one that changes the world. It is the one that proves a mortal heart was worthy of changing it."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**
