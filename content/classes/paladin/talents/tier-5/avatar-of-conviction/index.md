# Avatar of Conviction

## Overview
Your unwavering faith has earned the greatest honour a mortal champion can receive. For a brief moment, your patron allows a fragment of their divine essence to flow directly through your body, transforming you into their chosen Avatar upon the mortal plane.

While this transformation endures, your presence alone radiates overwhelming divine authority. Every action you perform reflects your patron's ideals, every strike carries their judgement, and every miracle manifests with unparalleled clarity. Though your mortal form cannot withstand such power for long, those few moments are often enough to alter the fate of an entire battle.

Every Avatar is unique to their patron. Some radiate blinding celestial light, others are surrounded by spectral ravens, crimson blood, divine storms, flourishing life, sacred flames, or manifestations beyond mortal understanding. Regardless of appearance, all who witness an Avatar know they stand before the living champion of a god.

## Rank 1
### Description

Your unwavering faith has earned the greatest honour a mortal champion can receive. For a brief moment, your patron allows a fragment of their divine essence to flow directly through your body, transforming you into their chosen Avatar upon the mortal plane.

While this transformation endures, your presence alone radiates overwhelming divine authority. Every action you perform reflects your patron's ideals, every strike carries their judgement, and every miracle manifests with unparalleled clarity. Though your mortal form cannot withstand such power for long, those few moments are often enough to alter the fate of an entire battle.

Every Avatar is unique to their patron. Some radiate blinding celestial light, others are surrounded by spectral ravens, crimson blood, divine storms, flourishing life, sacred flames, or manifestations beyond mortal understanding. Regardless of appearance, all who witness an Avatar know they stand before the living champion of a god.

### Effects

- Spend **500 MP** and **100 SP** to assume your **Avatar of Conviction**.
- **Cooldown:** **Long Rest**.
- Avatar of Conviction lasts for **5 Rounds**.
- While transformed:
  - Gain **Advantage on all Saving Throws.**
  - Gain **+2 AC**.
  - Increase all **Patron Damage** by **25%**.
  - Increase all healing granted by your patron's abilities by **25%**.
  - Your movement speed increases by **2 metres**.
  - You count as both your normal creature type and a **Divine Avatar**.
  - Your appearance transforms to reflect your patron's divine nature.
- All abilities continue to use your patron's **Divine Resonance**, **Divine Opposition**, and **Divine Authority**.
- Avatar of Conviction ends immediately if you become unconscious.

### Lore

Only a handful of mortals throughout history have ever been recognised as true Avatars. Such champions are remembered not for their own strength, but because the gods themselves chose to act through them. During these fleeting moments, the boundary between the mortal and divine realms grows thin, allowing a fragment of the patron's essence to manifest within their faithful servant.

The transformation is neither comfortable nor without risk. Mortal bodies were never meant to contain divine power, and even the greatest champions can sustain it only briefly before the strain becomes unbearable. Yet for those few moments, hope itself walks beside the faithful, and the enemies of the divine witness the full majesty of a patron's chosen champion.

> *"For a single heartbeat, the heavens do not merely answer—they walk beside us."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your patron now entrusts you with a greater measure of their divine essence. The transformation no longer merely empowers your body—it begins to reshape your very presence, allowing your conviction to radiate across the battlefield as a living symbol of your patron's authority. Allies fight with renewed confidence while enemies instinctively recognise the overwhelming power standing before them.

Though still fleeting, the Avatar endures longer and carries greater divine strength. Every miracle, every strike, and every act of protection reflects the unwavering bond between champion and patron. During these moments, your actions cease to represent your own will alone—they become an extension of the divine purpose you have sworn to uphold.

Every manifestation remains unique to your patron. Whether clothed in celestial radiance, sacred storms, spectral ravens, flourishing life, crimson blood, or another divine form, the Avatar is unmistakably recognised as one chosen by the gods.

### Effects

- Spend **600 MP** and **120 SP** to assume your **Avatar of Conviction**.
- **Cooldown:** **Long Rest**.
- Avatar of Conviction lasts for **6 Rounds**.
- While transformed:
  - Gain **Advantage on all Saving Throws**.
  - Gain **+3 AC**.
  - Increase all **Patron Damage** by **35%**.
  - Increase all healing granted by your patron's abilities by **35%**.
  - Increase your Movement Speed by **4 metres**.
  - Gain **Advantage on Saving Throws against conditions** inflicted by creatures aligned with your patron's **Divine Opposition**.
  - You count as both your normal creature type and a **Divine Avatar**.
  - Your appearance transforms to reflect your patron's divine nature.
- All patron abilities continue to use your patron's **Divine Resonance**, **Divine Opposition**, and **Divine Authority**.
- Additional Avatar blessings granted by your chosen patron apply while transformed.
- Avatar of Conviction ends immediately if you become unconscious.

### Lore

Throughout history, only the greatest champions have sustained the divine presence for more than a fleeting instant. Their names are carved into temple walls not because they possessed unmatched strength, but because they remained worthy vessels through which their patron's will could be expressed. Every transformation is both a blessing and a burden, for the mortal soul was never intended to contain such overwhelming power.

As your covenant deepens, your patron places greater trust in you, allowing more of their divine essence to flow through your body. Each transformation strengthens the bond between deity and champion, preparing you for the day when your name will stand beside the greatest Avatars recorded in the sacred histories.

> *"Power alone does not create an Avatar. Only unwavering conviction can bear the weight of the divine."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your patron's divine essence now flows through you with remarkable clarity, allowing you to briefly transcend the limitations of mortality. During your transformation, you no longer resemble a mere champion of the divine—you become a living extension of your patron's will upon the battlefield.

The divine presence surrounding you inspires unwavering courage in the faithful while causing those who oppose your patron to hesitate before striking. Every movement carries supernatural grace, every miracle is amplified by your unbreakable conviction, and every enemy witnesses the terrifying reality of a mortal chosen to bear divine power.

Although the transformation remains temporary, your body has grown capable of sustaining a greater portion of your patron's essence. Legends tell of champions who stood alone against overwhelming armies during this sacred state, their conviction proving stronger than any blade.

### Effects

- Spend **700 MP** and **140 SP** to assume your **Avatar of Conviction**.
- **Cooldown:** **Long Rest**.
- Avatar of Conviction lasts for **7 Rounds**.

While transformed:

- Gain **Advantage on all Saving Throws**.
- Gain **+4 AC**.
- Increase all **Patron Damage** by **50%**.
- Increase all healing granted by your patron's abilities by **50%**.
- Increase your Movement Speed by **6 metres**.
- Gain **Advantage on Saving Throws** against effects originating from creatures aligned with your patron's **Divine Opposition**.
- Become **immune to the Frightened condition**.
- You count as both your normal creature type and a **Divine Avatar**.
- Your appearance transforms to reflect your patron's divine nature.
- Additional Avatar Blessings granted by your chosen patron apply while transformed.

- Avatar of Conviction immediately ends if you become unconscious.

### Lore

Few champions throughout history have ever attained this degree of divine embodiment. At this stage, the distinction between mortal servant and divine instrument begins to blur, allowing a greater portion of the patron's will to manifest through the Avatar. Entire battlefields have fallen silent upon witnessing such transformations, for even enemies recognise they stand before one chosen by powers far beyond the mortal realm.

Within the oldest temples, Rank III is regarded as the point where conviction itself becomes a weapon. The faithful no longer fight beside an ordinary Paladin—they march beside a living symbol of their patron's unwavering resolve, whose very presence inspires hope and challenges despair.

> *"Faith gives strength. Conviction gives purpose. An Avatar possesses both, and through them the gods change the course of history."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your conviction has reached a level where your mortal body can barely contain the divine essence entrusted to it. When your patron's power flows through you, the distinction between champion and deity begins to fade. Your presence alone inspires unwavering courage within the faithful while striking doubt into the hearts of those who oppose your patron's divine will.

During this transformation, the world itself seems to acknowledge your patron's authority. The ground beneath your feet trembles with sacred energy, your voice carries supernatural weight, and your body radiates the unmistakable presence of the divine. Every movement reflects absolute purpose, every miracle becomes a declaration of your patron's power, and every enemy understands they face more than a mortal warrior.

Although the transformation remains temporary, few beings can witness an Avatar of this magnitude without believing they have stood before a living legend.

### Effects

- Spend **800 MP** and **160 SP** to assume your **Avatar of Conviction**.
- **Cooldown:** **Long Rest**.
- Avatar of Conviction lasts for **8 Rounds**.

While transformed:

- Gain **Advantage on all Saving Throws.**
- Gain **+5 AC.**
- Increase all **Patron Damage** by **65%**.
- Increase all healing granted by your patron's abilities by **65%**.
- Increase your **Movement Speed by 8 metres.**
- Gain **Advantage on Saving Throws** against effects originating from creatures aligned with your patron's **Divine Opposition**.
- Become **immune to the Frightened condition.**
- Gain **Divine Levitation**, allowing you to hover up to **30 centimetres** above the ground. While levitating, you ignore difficult terrain and may move across water, unstable ground, and similar surfaces, but you do **not** gain the ability to fly.
- You count as both your normal creature type and a **Divine Avatar**.
- Your appearance transforms to reflect your patron's divine nature.
- Additional **Avatar Blessings** granted by your chosen patron apply while transformed.

- Avatar of Conviction immediately ends if you become unconscious.

### Lore

The oldest scriptures describe this transformation as the threshold where a champion's soul becomes capable of bearing an extraordinary fragment of divine essence. During these sacred moments, the Avatar no longer walks as an ordinary mortal. Every heartbeat echoes with unwavering conviction, every miracle reflects the ideals of their patron, and every action serves a purpose greater than themselves.

Many who have witnessed such Avatars claim that the champion never truly touched the earth. Whether this was divine illusion or undeniable truth remains the subject of theological debate, but all agree upon one certainty—those chosen to bear such power are remembered long after kingdoms have fallen and empires have faded.

> *"When conviction becomes absolute, the earth itself no longer dares burden the feet of the faithful."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**

## Rank 5
### Description

There exists no greater honour for a mortal champion than to be acknowledged as the living Avatar of their patron. Through absolute faith, unwavering conviction, and countless acts of devotion, you have earned the right to bear a fragment of your patron's divine essence without hesitation or fear. For a brief time, your patron walks the mortal realm through you, transforming your body into a vessel of sacred purpose.

During this transformation, your very existence radiates divine authority. Allies stand emboldened by your presence, while those who oppose your patron feel the crushing weight of an undeniable truth—they do not face an ordinary warrior, but the chosen champion of a god.

Every Avatar reflects the nature of their patron. Some blaze with radiant light, others command celestial storms, crimson blood, sacred waters, spectral ravens, flourishing life, or manifestations beyond mortal understanding. Regardless of form, all who witness an Avatar understand that they stand before a living legend whose conviction has become inseparable from the divine.

### Effects

- Spend **1000 MP** and **200 SP** to assume your **Avatar of Conviction**.
- **Cooldown:** **Long Rest**.
- Avatar of Conviction lasts for **10 Rounds**.

#### While transformed

- Gain **Advantage on all Saving Throws**.
- Gain **+6 AC**.
- Increase all **Patron Damage** by **100%**.
- Increase all healing granted by your patron's abilities by **100%**.
- Increase your **Movement Speed by 10 metres**.
- Become **immune to the Frightened condition**.
- Gain **Divine Levitation**, allowing you to hover above the ground. While levitating, you ignore difficult terrain and may move across water, unstable terrain, and similar surfaces, but you do not gain true flight.
- Your patron's abilities **ignore Resistance** to their damage type (Immunity still applies).
- You count as both your normal creature type and a **Divine Avatar**.
- Your appearance completely transforms to reflect your patron's divine nature.
- All **Avatar Blessings** granted by your chosen patron remain active throughout the transformation.
- Avatar of Conviction immediately ends if you become unconscious.

### Lore

Only a handful of mortals throughout all of Asterian history have attained this sacred state. Their names are preserved within holy scripture, their deeds immortalised through song and legend, and their sacrifices remembered wherever faith endures. To become an Avatar is not to replace one's patron, nor to rival them, but to become the purest expression of their will upon the mortal plane.

Many scholars believe the transformation is less a gift than a burden. Mortal flesh was never meant to contain divine essence, and every moment spent as an Avatar tests the limits of both body and soul. Yet those chosen accept this burden willingly, knowing that their purpose is not to wield power for themselves, but to serve as the instrument through which their patron protects, judges, and inspires the world.

When the transformation finally ends, the memory of witnessing it remains forever etched into the hearts of friend and foe alike.

> *"An Avatar does not ask the gods for strength. They become the strength the gods choose to send."*  
> **— High Hierophant Cassiel, Keeper of the Sacred Covenant**
