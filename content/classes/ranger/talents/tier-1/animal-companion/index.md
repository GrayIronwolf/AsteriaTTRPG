# Animal Companion

## Overview
Through patience, trust, and countless hours spent within the wilderness, the Ranger forms a lasting bond with a loyal animal companion. Unlike magical summons or temporary creatures, this companion is a living beast that willingly chooses to travel alongside the Ranger.

The companion possesses its own personality, instincts, fears, and preferences. Some companions are playful and curious, while others are cautious and protective. Regardless of temperament, the bond forged between Ranger and companion creates a partnership built upon mutual trust rather than domination.

The companion understands simple commands and can assist with many wilderness tasks. Whether scouting ahead, helping track prey, guarding a campsite, or alerting the Ranger to danger, the companion serves as a valuable ally during both travel and adventure.

While loyal, the companion remains a living creature. It can be injured, frightened, exhausted, and even killed. Rangers who walk this path quickly learn that caring for their companion is as important as relying upon it.

## Rank 1
### Description

Through patience, trust, and countless hours spent within the wilderness, the Ranger forms a lasting bond with a loyal animal companion. Unlike magical summons or temporary creatures, this companion is a living beast that willingly chooses to travel alongside the Ranger.

The companion possesses its own personality, instincts, fears, and preferences. Some companions are playful and curious, while others are cautious and protective. Regardless of temperament, the bond forged between Ranger and companion creates a partnership built upon mutual trust rather than domination.

The companion understands simple commands and can assist with many wilderness tasks. Whether scouting ahead, helping track prey, guarding a campsite, or alerting the Ranger to danger, the companion serves as a valuable ally during both travel and adventure.

While loyal, the companion remains a living creature. It can be injured, frightened, exhausted, and even killed. Rangers who walk this path quickly learn that caring for their companion is as important as relying upon it.

---

### Effects

- Gain one Animal Companion approved by the GM.
- The companion must be a non-magical beast appropriate to the Ranger's environment and level.
- The companion uses the statistics of its base creature unless modified by future talent ranks.
- The companion understands up to 5 trained commands.

#### Starting Commands

Choose or train commands such as:

- Follow
- Stay
- Guard
- Track
- Retrieve
- Scout
- Hunt
- Return

The GM may approve additional simple commands.

#### Companion Assistance

When the companion assists with a task appropriate to its species, the Ranger gains:

- +1 to the associated D20 skill check.

Examples include:

- Wolves assisting with Tracking.
- Hawks assisting with Perception.
- Hounds assisting with scent detection.
- Ravens assisting with observation.
- Boars assisting with foraging.

The GM determines whether the companion can reasonably assist.

#### Companion Limitations

- The companion cannot speak.
- The companion cannot understand complex tactics or multi-step plans.
- The companion acts on its own instincts when not given commands.
- The companion may refuse actions that would obviously lead to its death.

#### Death & Injury

- The companion is a living creature and can be injured, poisoned, exhausted, frightened, or killed.
- The companion uses normal Asteria combat and survival rules.
- If reduced to 0 HP, the companion falls unconscious and begins making Death Saving Throws.
- If the companion dies, the bond is lost.
- Forming a new companion requires significant downtime, roleplay, and GM approval.

### Lore

Among Rangers, it is often said that the wilderness chooses its own guardians. While many adventurers seek power through steel or sorcery, Rangers learn that survival often depends upon trust, patience, and companionship.

The bond between Ranger and companion cannot be purchased, summoned, or forced. It is earned through shared hardships, long journeys, and countless moments spent beneath open skies. Some Rangers meet their companions as injured cubs or fledglings, while others earn the loyalty of a fully grown beast through acts of courage and respect.

Throughout Asteria's history, tales of famous Rangers are often inseparable from the companions who stood beside them. Songs tell of wolves that guarded entire villages, hawks that carried warnings across battlefields, and faithful hounds that remained beside fallen masters until the end.

Veteran Rangers understand a simple truth: a companion is not a tool, weapon, or servant. It is a trusted friend who willingly walks the same path.

---

> *"The wilderness gives many gifts. Trust is the rarest among them."*
> — **Kaelen Thornwalker, Master Ranger of the Emerald Paths**

## Rank 2
### Description

As the bond between Ranger and companion deepens, the animal becomes more responsive to its master's intentions and training. What began as simple trust evolves into genuine cooperation, allowing both to work together with greater efficiency and confidence.

The companion becomes accustomed to the dangers of adventuring and learns to remain calm in situations that would send lesser beasts fleeing. Through constant travel, hunting, and shared experiences, the animal develops a stronger understanding of the Ranger's expectations and commands.

Many Rangers describe this stage of the bond as the point where the companion truly becomes a partner rather than simply a trained animal. Commands require less repetition, trust becomes instinctive, and both Ranger and companion begin anticipating one another's actions.

Though still an ordinary creature, the companion's loyalty and experience allow it to perform tasks that would be impossible for an untrained beast of the same species.

---

### Effects

#### Improved Bond

- The companion understands up to 10 trained commands.
- The companion can distinguish between friendly, neutral, and hostile creatures designated by the Ranger.
- The companion gains greater confidence and is less likely to flee from danger.

#### Skilled Assistance

When assisting with a task appropriate to its species, the Ranger gains:

- +2 to the associated D20 skill check.

Examples include:

- Tracking
- Survival
- Perception
- Investigation
- Hunting
- Scouting

The GM determines whether the companion can reasonably assist.

#### Combat Training

The companion has received basic combat training.

- The companion gains +1 to Attack Rolls.
- The companion gains +1 to AC.
- The companion gains +10% Maximum HP.

#### Coordinated Movement

Years of training allow the pair to move more naturally together.

- The companion no longer requires verbal commands for simple actions it has been trained to perform.
- Hand signals, whistles, or visual gestures may be used instead.
- If the companion can clearly see the Ranger, it understands simple non-verbal commands.

#### Companion Limitations

- The companion still cannot speak.
- The companion cannot understand complex strategies or detailed plans.
- The companion retains its natural instincts and personality.
- The companion may refuse actions that would obviously result in certain death.

#### Death & Injury

- The companion remains a living creature and can be injured or killed.
- If reduced to 0 HP, the companion falls unconscious and begins making Death Saving Throws.
- If the companion dies, the bond is broken.
- Forming a new companion requires significant downtime, roleplay, and GM approval.

---

### Lore

Experienced Rangers often claim that a true companion is forged through hardship rather than training. Countless miles traveled together, shared hunts, dangerous encounters, and nights spent beneath the stars create a connection few outsiders truly understand.

Stories from across Asteria tell of companions sensing danger moments before their Rangers, standing guard through blizzards, or defending wounded masters against impossible odds. While scholars argue these tales are exaggerations, Rangers know such loyalty is neither magic nor myth.

As trust grows, the need for spoken commands begins to fade. A glance, gesture, or shift in posture becomes enough to communicate intent. Many veteran Rangers find themselves reacting alongside their companions without conscious thought, moving as though guided by the same instinct.

This growing harmony is often considered the first step toward the legendary bonds possessed by the greatest beastmasters and wilderness guardians of history.

---

> *"Training teaches obedience. Trust teaches understanding."*
> — **Lyria Ashwood, Warden of the Greenwild**

## Rank 3
# 

### Description

The bond between Ranger and companion has grown beyond simple training and obedience. Through countless shared experiences, each has learned to read the other's intentions with remarkable accuracy. What once required commands can now be understood through instinct, body language, and familiarity.

The companion develops greater confidence in dangerous situations and begins acting proactively when threats arise. Rather than waiting for direct instruction, it can recognize familiar dangers, protect its master, and assist in hunts with increasing effectiveness.

Many Rangers consider this rank the moment when their companion truly becomes an adventuring partner. The animal is no longer simply following orders—it is actively participating in the journey, making decisions based upon experience and trust.

This deeper connection allows the Ranger and companion to operate with greater coordination, whether traversing the wilderness, tracking dangerous prey, or facing enemies in combat.

---

### Effects

#### Deepened Bond

- The companion understands up to 15 trained commands.
- The companion can recognize common threats, enemies, and hazards previously encountered alongside the Ranger.
- The companion may perform previously trained tasks without requiring direct commands.

#### Skilled Assistance

When assisting with a task appropriate to its species, the Ranger gains:

- +3 to the associated D20 skill check.

The GM determines whether the companion can reasonably assist.

#### Combat Experience

The companion has become hardened through travel and adventure.

- +2 Attack Rolls.
- +2 AC.
- +20% Maximum HP.
- +2 to all Saving Throws.

#### Protective Instinct

The companion has learned to actively defend its Ranger.

Once per combat:

- When a hostile creature within 2m attacks the Ranger, the companion may immediately move up to its movement speed toward the attacker.
- This movement does not provoke Opportunity Attacks.
- The companion must end its movement adjacent to either the Ranger or the attacker.

This movement does not grant a free attack.

#### Shared Awareness

The Ranger and companion have developed exceptional situational awareness.

- The Ranger gains Advantage on checks made to locate or track their companion.
- The companion gains Advantage on checks made to locate the Ranger.
- The Ranger always knows the general direction of their companion while both remain on the same continent.

#### Companion Limitations

- The companion cannot speak.
- The companion cannot understand advanced language or complex tactical discussions.
- The companion remains subject to fear, injury, exhaustion, and death.
- The companion may refuse actions that would clearly result in certain death.

#### Death & Injury

- The companion remains a living creature and can be injured or killed.
- If reduced to 0 HP, the companion falls unconscious and begins making Death Saving Throws.
- If the companion dies, the bond is broken.
- Forming a new companion requires significant downtime, roleplay, and GM approval.

---

### Lore

Among Rangers, Rank 3 is often called the Awakening Bond. It marks the point where years of trust, hardship, and shared survival begin transforming a simple partnership into something far deeper.

Many wilderness communities believe that companions reaching this stage possess a fragment of the wild itself within their spirit. While scholars dismiss such claims as superstition, few can explain how certain companions seem to sense danger long before it appears or instinctively know where their Ranger can be found.

Ancient records tell of Rangers and companions traversing entire kingdoms to reunite after becoming separated by war, storms, or magical disasters. Some tales even describe companions crossing mountains and oceans guided only by their bond.

Whether mystical or simply the result of extraordinary trust, Rangers universally recognize this stage as a milestone few ever achieve. From this point onward, Ranger and companion begin to function less as two individuals and more as a single hunting pair.

---

> *"One mind commands. Two minds cooperate. The greatest bonds require neither."*
> — **Thalan Greymane, Beastmaster of the Northern Wilds**

## Rank 4
### Description

At this stage, the bond between Ranger and companion has become extraordinary. Years of shared adventures, battles, and survival have forged a connection that few outside the Ranger traditions can fully comprehend. The companion no longer merely follows commands—it actively interprets intent and acts in ways that complement its master's actions.

The companion's instincts become sharpened through experience. It learns to identify ambushes, recognize familiar enemies, and react to threats with remarkable precision. Many Rangers find themselves relying upon their companion's judgment as much as their own when navigating dangerous wildernesses.

The partnership has evolved into a true hunting pair. Ranger and companion naturally coordinate movements, pursue prey together, and defend one another without hesitation. What once required commands is now accomplished through trust and understanding.

Veteran Rangers often say that reaching this stage is proof that both Ranger and companion have survived challenges that would have broken lesser partnerships long ago.

---

### Effects

#### Exceptional Bond

- The companion understands up to 20 trained commands.
- The companion can distinguish between allies, neutral creatures, and hostile targets designated by the Ranger.
- The companion can perform previously trained tasks without requiring direct commands.

#### Skilled Assistance

When assisting with a task appropriate to its species, the Ranger gains:

- +4 to the associated D20 skill check.

The GM determines whether the companion can reasonably assist.

#### Veteran Companion

The companion has become an experienced adventurer.

- +3 Attack Rolls.
- +3 AC.
- +35% Maximum HP.
- +3 to all Saving Throws.

#### Coordinated Assault

Years of fighting together allow Ranger and companion to exploit openings created by one another.

Once per turn:

- When the Ranger successfully hits a creature with an attack, the companion gains Advantage on its next attack against that same target before the end of its next turn.

OR

- When the companion successfully hits a creature, the Ranger gains Advantage on their next attack against that same target before the end of their next turn.

Only one Coordinated Assault bonus may be active against a target at a time.

#### Shared Senses

The bond has grown strong enough that the Ranger can briefly perceive through their companion.

- As an Action, the Ranger may see and hear through the companion's senses.
- While using Shared Senses, the Ranger is unaware of their own surroundings.
- This effect may be maintained for up to 10 minutes.
- Can be used a number of times equal to the Ranger's Wisdom Modifier per Long Rest (minimum 1).

#### Unbreakable Loyalty

The companion has developed unwavering trust in the Ranger.

- The companion gains Advantage on Saving Throws against Fear effects.
- The companion gains Advantage on Saving Throws against Charm effects.
- The companion will not flee from combat unless affected by magic, overwhelming fear, or direct Ranger instructions.

#### Death & Injury

- The companion remains a living creature and can be injured or killed.
- If reduced to 0 HP, the companion falls unconscious and begins making Death Saving Throws.
- If the companion dies, the bond is broken.
- Forming a new companion requires significant downtime, roleplay, and GM approval.

---

### Lore

Among the Ranger lodges of Asteria, Rank 4 companions are viewed with a mixture of admiration and reverence. Such creatures are no longer considered ordinary beasts. Though they remain natural animals, their experience and intelligence often rival that of seasoned adventurers.

Many legends describe companions at this stage saving entire expeditions from disaster. Wolves have detected hidden assassins before they could strike. Hawks have guided lost caravans through deadly storms. Faithful companions have stood against monsters many times their size to protect their wounded Rangers.

Some scholars believe these bonds are simply the result of exceptional training. Rangers disagree. They claim there comes a point where trust becomes something greater—something impossible to teach and impossible to replicate through ordinary means.

Whatever the truth may be, few who witness a Rank 4 bond can deny that Ranger and companion operate with a level of unity that borders on the supernatural.

---

> *"The day you stop seeing a beast at your side and start seeing a partner beside you, the bond is complete."*
> — **Eldric Stormclaw, Grand Warden of the Wild Frontiers**

## Rank 5
### Description

The bond between Ranger and companion has reached its absolute pinnacle. Few Rangers in the history of Asteria ever achieve such a connection, and those who do are remembered in songs, legends, and Ranger teachings for generations.

At this stage, the companion is no longer merely a trusted ally or hunting partner. Ranger and companion have spent years overcoming impossible odds together, surviving battles, traversing dangerous lands, and protecting one another from countless threats. Their trust has become absolute.

The companion's instincts have been sharpened through experience beyond that of ordinary beasts. It anticipates danger, understands complex situations through observation, and reacts with a level of coordination that often appears supernatural to outsiders. While it remains a natural creature, its bond with the Ranger elevates it far beyond its wild origins.

Many veteran Rangers claim that Rank 5 companions embody the very spirit of the wilderness itself. Whether this belief is truth or tradition, none can deny that the greatest Ranger-companion bonds become something extraordinary.

---

### Effects

#### Master Bond

- The companion understands up to 25 trained commands.
- The companion can interpret complex intentions based on context, body language, and prior training.
- The companion may independently perform trained tasks without requiring direct instruction.

#### Skilled Assistance

When assisting with a task appropriate to its species, the Ranger gains:

- +5 to the associated D20 skill check.

The GM determines whether the companion can reasonably assist.

#### Legendary Companion

The companion has become a veteran of countless adventures.

- +5 Attack Rolls.
- +5 AC.
- +50% Maximum HP.
- +5 to all Saving Throws.

#### Perfect Coordination

Ranger and companion move as a unified hunting pair.

Once per turn:

- When either the Ranger or companion successfully hits a creature, the other gains Advantage on their next attack against that target before the end of their next turn.

Additionally:

- If both the Ranger and companion hit the same target during the same round, the second successful attack deals an additional +1d8 damage.
- The damage type matches the triggering attack.

#### Shared Senses Mastery

The Ranger's connection to their companion has reached its peak.

- The Ranger may see, hear, and smell through the companion's senses.
- Maximum range is increased to 1 kilometre.
- This ability may be maintained for up to 1 hour.
- Can be used a number of times equal to the Ranger's Wisdom Modifier per Long Rest (minimum 1).

#### Guardian Bond

The companion's loyalty is unwavering.

Once per Long Rest:

- When the companion would be reduced to 0 HP, it may instead remain at 1 HP.
- This effect occurs after damage is resolved.
- The companion immediately gains one level of Exhaustion.

This ability does not function against effects that cause instant death.

#### Spirit of the Wild

Years spent together have made the companion exceptionally resilient.

- Advantage on Saving Throws against Fear.
- Advantage on Saving Throws against Charm.
- Advantage on Saving Throws against being controlled or compelled by magic.

#### Death & Injury

- The companion remains a living creature and can still be injured or killed.
- If reduced to 0 HP, the companion falls unconscious and begins making Death Saving Throws.
- The Guardian Bond feature may prevent this once per Long Rest.
- If the companion dies, the bond is broken.
- Forming a new companion requires significant downtime, roleplay, and GM approval.

---

### Lore

Among Ranger circles, Rank 5 companions are spoken of with the same reverence reserved for legendary heroes. Such bonds are so rare that entire generations may pass without a single one being witnessed.

Ancient records tell of companions standing beside Rangers during kingdom-shaping events, hunting creatures of myth, crossing oceans, and surviving wars that changed the course of history. Many of these companions became nearly as famous as the Rangers they served beside.

Stories often describe these animals accomplishing feats no ordinary beast should be capable of. Wolves leading survivors through blizzards, ravens carrying warnings across battlefields, and faithful companions defending their masters against overwhelming odds have become common themes within Ranger folklore.

Yet when asked the secret behind such legends, veteran Rangers rarely speak of power. Instead, they speak of trust, loyalty, sacrifice, and years spent walking the same path. To them, the greatest achievement is not creating a powerful companion—it is earning a friend willing to stand beside them until the very end.

---

> *"Steel dulls. Kingdoms fall. Even legends fade. But a bond forged through trust can outlive them all."*
> — **Aric Wildheart, First Beastmaster of Asteria**
