# Camouflage

## Overview
The wilderness teaches that survival is not always determined by strength or speed. Often, the creature that survives is simply the one that remains unseen. Through careful mana manipulation, the Ranger learns to alter their physical appearance to mirror the environment around them.

Unlike illusion magic, Camouflage is a genuine physical adaptation. As mana flows through the Ranger's body, their skin, clothing, armour, and equipment gradually shift in colour and texture to resemble nearby terrain. Mossy greens emerge among forest foliage, rocky greys form upon mountain slopes, and pale whites appear amidst snow-covered landscapes.

The transformation is subtle and natural, making it difficult for observers to distinguish the Ranger from their surroundings. While the effect is not powerful enough to conceal movement, it allows a patient Ranger to become remarkably difficult to spot while remaining still.

Many novice Rangers spend weeks mastering this technique. Learning to remain motionless while channeling mana is often far more challenging than the transformation itself.

## Rank 1
### Description

The wilderness teaches that survival is not always determined by strength or speed. Often, the creature that survives is simply the one that remains unseen. Through careful mana manipulation, the Ranger learns to alter their physical appearance to mirror the environment around them.

Unlike illusion magic, Camouflage is a genuine physical adaptation. Mossy greens emerge among forest foliage, rocky greys form upon mountain slopes, and pale whites appear amidst snow-covered landscapes.

The transformation is subtle and natural, making it difficult for observers to distinguish the Ranger from their surroundings. While the effect is not powerful enough to conceal movement, it allows a patient Ranger to become remarkably difficult to spot while remaining still.

Many novice Rangers spend weeks mastering this technique. Learning to remain motionless while channeling mana is often far more challenging than the transformation itself.

---

### Effects

#### Environmental Adaptation

Upon activation:

- Spend 10 Mana.
- The Ranger must remain stationary for 10 seconds while channeling mana.
- At the end of the channel, Camouflage becomes active.

While active:

- The Ranger's body, clothing, armour, and visible equipment adapt to match the surrounding environment.
- Creatures relying on sight suffer a **-2 penalty to Perception checks** made to visually detect the Ranger.
- The effect remains active as long as the Ranger remains stationary.

#### Compatible Environments

Camouflage may function in:

- Forests
- Jungles
- Swamps
- Grasslands
- Hills
- Mountains
- Deserts
- Tundra
- Snowfields
- Natural cave systems

The GM determines whether sufficient environmental features exist for adaptation.

#### Disruption

Camouflage immediately ends if the Ranger:

- Moves from their current position.
- Makes an attack.
- Casts a spell.
- Performs a strenuous physical action.
- Is forcibly moved from their position.

The Ranger may reactivate Camouflage normally.

#### Limitations

- Camouflage is not invisibility.
- The effect only hinders visual detection.
- Creatures may still locate the Ranger through sound, scent, tracks, tremors, magical detection, or other senses.
- Camouflage grants no bonus to Stealth checks at this rank.
- The Ranger gains no protection against divination or magical detection abilities.

### Lore

The first Rangers to develop Camouflage were said to have spent entire seasons observing the creatures of the wild. They noticed that survival often depended not upon fighting or fleeing, but upon becoming part of the environment itself.

Through experimentation with natural mana, these early hunters discovered a way to encourage their bodies to imitate the colours and patterns of the world around them. While the transformation was initially crude, generations of refinement transformed the technique into one of the most iconic Ranger abilities in Asteria.

Many scholars classify Camouflage as a form of minor transmutation, though Rangers rarely concern themselves with academic labels. To them, the technique is simply another lesson taught by the wilderness: adapt or perish.

Among Ranger lodges, apprentices are often required to remain hidden using Camouflage for hours at a time as part of their training. Those who succeed learn patience. Those who fail learn humility.

---

> *"A predator does not ask the forest to hide it. It learns how to become another part of the forest."*
> — **Elarion Mosscloak, Warden of the Emerald Wilds**

## Rank 2
### Description

The Ranger's understanding of Camouflage deepens, allowing the adaptation process to occur far more quickly and with greater effectiveness. Rather than simply altering colouration, the mana flowing through the Ranger begins subtly affecting texture, shading, and the way light interacts with their body and equipment.

The transformation becomes smoother and more responsive, allowing the Ranger to disappear into their surroundings in a matter of moments. Leaves, bark, stone, sand, and snow are reflected with remarkable accuracy across the Ranger's appearance, making visual identification increasingly difficult.

Experienced Rangers often use this technique while scouting hostile territory, observing prey, or preparing ambush positions. Many creatures can walk within a few metres of a concealed Ranger without ever realizing they are being watched.

Despite this improvement, movement still disrupts the adaptation. The Ranger must remain motionless for the camouflage to maintain its effectiveness.

### Lore

Rank 2 is often called the Watcher's Veil among Ranger lodges. At this stage, the Ranger begins to understand that camouflage is more than changing colours—it is learning how the world itself is perceived.

Veteran Rangers teach that most creatures do not truly see their surroundings. Instead, they search for movement, unusual shapes, and things that seem out of place. Camouflage exploits this instinct by making the Ranger appear as though they belong exactly where they are.

Many frontier settlements owe their safety to Rangers capable of remaining hidden for hours while observing monster migrations, enemy patrols, or dangerous predators. Such information often saves lives long before a weapon is ever drawn.

Among experienced scouts, a common saying persists: if a Ranger is forced to run, the camouflage has already failed. The true purpose of the technique is not escape—it is remaining unnoticed in the first place.

---

> *"Most eyes search for what does not belong. Camouflage ensures that you do."*
> — **Lysandra Thornroot, Scout Captain of the Green Marches**

## Rank 3
### Description

The Ranger's mastery of Camouflage reaches a significant milestone. No longer restricted to complete stillness, the Ranger learns to maintain the adaptation while moving through the environment. Mana continuously flows across the body, adjusting colours, textures, and patterns as terrain changes around them.

The transformation becomes fluid rather than static. Moss-green tones shift to earthy browns beneath changing foliage, rocky textures blend into mountain terrain, and pale colours emerge when crossing snowy ground. The adaptation occurs constantly, allowing the Ranger to remain concealed even while stalking prey or scouting dangerous territory.

Maintaining such an active transformation requires greater focus and mana expenditure than previous ranks. The Ranger must carefully balance movement and concentration to prevent the camouflage from destabilizing.

Among Ranger circles, reaching this stage is often considered the moment when a hunter truly becomes a predator. No longer bound to hiding in one place, the Ranger can now move with the wilderness itself.

---

### Lore

Rank 3 is known among Rangers as the Stalker's Veil. It represents the transition from hiding within the wilderness to moving as part of it. Rangers who achieve this level of mastery are often employed as scouts, hunters, and reconnaissance specialists throughout Asteria.

The ability requires a level of mana control that few outsiders fully appreciate. Rather than maintaining a single transformation, the Ranger must constantly adjust their appearance to match every change in terrain. Even a short journey may require hundreds of subtle adaptations.

Many famous monster hunters developed this technique specifically to stalk dangerous prey without alerting them. Tales speak of Rangers shadowing wyverns through mountain passes, tracking dire beasts through ancient forests, and infiltrating enemy camps without ever being seen.

Among veteran Rangers, Rank 3 is often viewed as the first truly advanced stage of Camouflage. From this point forward, the Ranger no longer merely hides from the wilderness—they move with it.

---

> *"Stillness teaches concealment. Movement teaches mastery."*
> — **Vaelor Greenstride, Hunter of the Northern Wilds**

## Rank 4
### Description

The Ranger's command over Camouflage has evolved into an instinctive extension of their body. Mana no longer struggles to keep pace with movement, instead flowing naturally across the Ranger's skin, clothing, armour, and equipment as they traverse the wilderness. Every step, every shift in terrain, and every change in light is answered by an equally seamless transformation.

At this stage, the Ranger is no longer forced to stalk prey at a cautious pace. They can move freely through forests, mountains, deserts, and other natural environments while remaining remarkably difficult to distinguish from the world around them. To an observer, the Ranger seems to melt into the landscape, their outline constantly changing to match whatever surrounds them.

This technique is demanding, requiring exceptional mana control and awareness of one's surroundings. Rangers who master it become invaluable scouts, infiltrators, and monster hunters, capable of approaching dangerous prey without revealing their presence until the perfect moment.

Only the most experienced Rangers ever reach this level of mastery. Within the lodges of Asteria, Rank 4 is regarded as the point where Camouflage ceases to be a technique and becomes second nature.

### Lore

Among the Rangers of Asteria, Rank 4 is known as the **Hunter's Mantle**. It is said that those who reach this level no longer think about blending into nature—the wilderness accepts them as one of its own.

Veteran Rangers often spend years refining this technique. Every journey across changing landscapes teaches them how light bends through leaves, how shadows stretch across stone, and how even the smallest shift in colour can betray a careless hunter. These lessons become instinct, allowing the Ranger's mana to respond before conscious thought.

Many famous expeditions owe their success to Rangers capable of maintaining Camouflage while traversing dangerous territory. They have infiltrated enemy fortifications built deep within forests, shadowed ancient beasts across mountain ranges, and rescued lost travellers without ever revealing their presence.

To outsiders, such feats appear magical. Rangers know the truth is far simpler. The wilderness hides those who understand it, and Camouflage is merely the highest expression of that understanding.

---

> *"When your footsteps become as natural as falling leaves, even the forest forgets you are there."*  
> — **High Warden Elyndor Ashcloak, Keeper of the Emerald Trail**

## Rank 5
### Description

The Ranger has mastered Camouflage to a level few mortals ever achieve. Their body has become a living conduit for natural mana, instinctively responding to the world around them. Every step, every shift in terrain, and every change in light is mirrored across their skin, clothing, armour, and equipment in moments, making them appear as though they belong wherever they stand.

Unlike earlier ranks, the Ranger no longer consciously maintains the transformation. The mana flows continuously through their body, adapting faster than the eye can comfortably follow. Forest greens become rocky greys, snowy whites become desert browns, and shadows seem to cling naturally to their form as they move through the wilderness.

To those unfamiliar with the art, a master of Camouflage appears almost impossible to follow. One moment they are clearly visible, the next they have seemingly vanished into the landscape despite never leaving sight. Experienced hunters know the Ranger has not disappeared—they have simply become another part of the environment.

Within the Ranger Lodges of Asteria, only a handful of individuals each generation ever earn the title of **Living Veil**. These masters are entrusted with the most dangerous scouting missions, monster hunts, and reconnaissance operations, for few creatures can match their ability to move unseen through the wilds.

---

### Effects

#### Living Camouflage

Upon activation:

- Spend **30 Mana**.
- Camouflage activates after **1 second** of concentration.

While active:

- The Ranger's body, clothing, armour, weapons, and visible equipment continuously adapt to match the surrounding environment.
- The adaptation occurs seamlessly as the Ranger moves through different terrain.
- Creatures relying primarily on sight suffer a **-10 penalty to Perception checks** made to visually detect the Ranger.

#### Master of Concealment

While Camouflage is active:

- Gain **+5 to Stealth checks**.
- The Ranger may move at full movement speed without disrupting Camouflage.
- Natural difficult terrain does not reduce the Ranger's movement speed.

#### Predator's Grace

Years of experience allow the Ranger to move with almost supernatural precision.

While Camouflage is active:

- The Ranger leaves virtually no visible trail.
- Creatures tracking the Ranger by sight suffer **Disadvantage** on related Perception or Investigation checks.
- The Ranger may use natural cover that would normally be considered too small or sparse to hide behind, at the GM's discretion.

#### Adaptive Persistence

Camouflage is no longer easily broken.

The following actions do **not** end Camouflage:

- Drawing or sheathing weapons.
- Opening doors or containers.
- Drinking potions.
- Reloading weapons.
- Quiet communication.
- Interacting with simple objects.
- Normal movement.

If the Ranger makes an attack or casts a spell:

- Camouflage remains active until the attack or spell is fully resolved.
- The effect then ends immediately.

#### Perfect Adaptation

The Ranger instinctively understands how to blend with every natural environment.

Camouflage functions in:

- Forests
- Jungles
- Swamps
- Grasslands
- Mountains
- Hills
- Deserts
- Snowfields
- Tundra
- Natural cave systems
- Coastal cliffs
- Marshlands

The GM may approve additional natural environments where appropriate.

#### Limitations

- Camouflage is **not invisibility**.
- The talent only hinders **visual detection**.
- Creatures using scent, hearing, tremorsense, magical detection, blindsight, or similar abilities may still locate the Ranger.
- Camouflage provides no protection against divination or similar magical effects.

---

### Lore

The greatest Rangers speak of Camouflage not as a spell or technique, but as a conversation between the Ranger and the wilderness itself. Through years of discipline and unwavering respect for nature, the flow of mana becomes so refined that the world instinctively accepts the Ranger as part of its landscape.

Legends tell of Rangers who crossed battlefields unseen beneath the gaze of thousands, stalked Elder Dragons through ancient forests for days without detection, and watched entire armies march past from only a few metres away. Whether every tale is true is impossible to know, but each speaks to the same extraordinary mastery.

Among the oldest Ranger Lodges, masters of this talent are known as the **Living Veils**. Their teachings are reserved for only the most dedicated students, for the discipline demands patience, humility, and absolute control over one's body and mana.

To become a Living Veil is considered one of the highest honours a Ranger can achieve. It is proof that the wilderness no longer sees the Ranger as an outsider, but as one of its own.

---

> *"The greatest camouflage is not becoming invisible. It is becoming so natural that the world forgets you were ever there."*  
> — **Grandmaster Alaric Thornveil, First Living Veil of Asteria**
