# Hawk Eyes

## Overview
A Ranger's greatest weapon is not their bow, but their vision. Through years of studying birds of prey and mastering the art of observation, the Ranger develops eyesight capable of discerning details that others would never notice. Distant movement, subtle changes in terrain, and the smallest opening between obstacles become immediately apparent.

Hawk Eyes is not magical sight. Instead, it is the culmination of relentless training, allowing the Ranger to focus on distant targets with remarkable clarity. By concentrating on a creature or location, they can judge distances, identify the amount of cover protecting a target, and recognise visual details that would normally be lost to the naked eye.

This exceptional vision also makes difficult shots easier to judge. Rather than increasing the Ranger's accuracy directly, Hawk Eyes allows them to better understand the shot before it is taken, reducing the challenges posed by distance, terrain, and other visual obstacles.

## Rank 1
### Description

A Ranger's greatest weapon is not their bow, but their vision. Through years of studying birds of prey and mastering the art of observation, the Ranger develops eyesight capable of discerning details that others would never notice. Distant movement, subtle changes in terrain, and the smallest opening between obstacles become immediately apparent.

Hawk Eyes is not magical sight. Instead, it is the culmination of relentless training, allowing the Ranger to focus on distant targets with remarkable clarity. By concentrating on a creature or location, they can judge distances, identify the amount of cover protecting a target, and recognise visual details that would normally be lost to the naked eye.

This exceptional vision also makes difficult shots easier to judge. Rather than increasing the Ranger's accuracy directly, Hawk Eyes allows them to better understand the shot before it is taken, reducing the challenges posed by distance, terrain, and other visual obstacles.

---

### Effects

#### Enhanced Vision

The Ranger may focus on a creature, object, or location within line of sight.

While focused, the Ranger can:

- Estimate the distance to the target.
- Determine whether the target has **Light, Half, or Three-Quarters Cover**.
- Clearly distinguish visible weapons, armour, and equipment.
- Notice obvious injuries or unusual physical features.

#### Shooter's Eye

The Ranger's exceptional vision reduces the difficulty of ranged attacks.

- Reduce penalties applied to ranged attack rolls caused by **distance, target size, or environmental visibility** by **1** (minimum 0).

This benefit applies to all:

- Ranged weapon attacks.
- Ranged combat talents.
- Ranged combat skills.
- Racial traits that require a ranged attack roll.

#### Limitations

- Hawk Eyes does not grant Darkvision.
- Hawk Eyes does not reveal invisible creatures.
- Hawk Eyes cannot see through solid objects.
- Magical concealment still functions normally.
- The GM determines what information is reasonably visible.

### Lore

Every Ranger is taught that prey is rarely defeated through strength alone. Victory begins with observation. The slightest movement among the trees, a glint of sunlight reflecting from armour, or the faint outline of a figure behind a stone wall may reveal everything a skilled hunter needs to know.

Across Asteria, veteran Rangers spend years training their eyesight by observing wildlife from impossible distances and learning to judge terrain with remarkable precision. Many apprentices fail these lessons, not because they lack talent, but because they never learn the patience required to truly see.

Among the Ranger Lodges, Hawk Eyes is regarded as the first step towards true mastery of ranged combat. Before a Ranger learns how to strike perfectly, they must first learn how to see perfectly.

---

> *"Every perfect shot begins long before the arrow ever leaves the bow."*  
> — **Master Ranger Caelan Skywatch**

## Rank 2
### Description

The Ranger's eyesight continues to develop through years of practice and experience. Focusing on distant targets becomes second nature, allowing them to distinguish details that would be impossible for most people to perceive. Terrain, movement, and obstacles are analysed almost instinctively, giving the Ranger a clearer understanding of the battlefield before combat even begins.

As their vision improves, so too does their ability to judge difficult shots. Changes in elevation, partial cover, and environmental conditions become easier to account for, allowing the Ranger to make shots that would challenge even experienced marksmen.

Veteran Rangers often serve as scouts and lookouts, trusted to detect danger long before it reaches their companions.

### Lore

Reaching the second rank of Hawk Eyes marks the transition from hunter to scout. Rangers possessing this level of vision are often entrusted with leading expeditions, watching over camps, and identifying threats from extraordinary distances before they pose a danger.

Within the Ranger Lodges, students spend countless hours observing wildlife from elevated perches, learning to distinguish the smallest movements across forests, mountains, and open plains. They are taught that the greatest advantage in any hunt is seeing the danger before the danger sees them.

---

> *"Distance is only an obstacle to those who have never trained their eyes."*  
> — **Master Ranger Caelan Skywatch**

## Rank 3
### Description

The Ranger's eyesight has become exceptional, allowing them to observe the world with a level of clarity few mortals can rival. Distant creatures, hidden movement, and subtle imperfections within armour or the landscape become increasingly easy to distinguish. What others dismiss as an indistinct silhouette, the Ranger sees as a wealth of valuable information.

At this stage, the Ranger begins recognising visible weaknesses from afar. Damaged armour, old wounds, poor footing, and exposed gaps stand out through careful observation. This knowledge does not directly improve the Ranger's aim, but it provides vital information that can be exploited through talents such as **Steady Aim** or shared with allies.

Among Ranger Lodges, those who reach this level are often assigned as reconnaissance specialists, capable of gathering intelligence before a battle has even begun.

---

### Lore

Rangers who achieve the third rank of Hawk Eyes are renowned throughout Asteria for their remarkable powers of observation. Many serve as military scouts, monster trackers, and expedition leaders, trusted to uncover dangers long before swords are drawn or arrows are loosed.

Within the Ranger Lodges, students are taught that every creature carries the marks of its life upon its body. Scars, worn armour, tired movements, and careless habits all reveal opportunities to those patient enough to look. Hawk Eyes is not about seeing farther—it is about understanding more.

---

> *"The world reveals its secrets to those who have the patience to truly observe it."*  
> — **High Ranger Elyas Stormwatch**

## Rank 4
### Description

The Ranger's vision approaches the limits of mortal perception. Their eyes effortlessly focus across vast distances, allowing them to study creatures, structures, and landscapes with extraordinary clarity. Tiny movements, subtle flaws, and distant landmarks stand out as though they were only metres away.

Through years of hunting and reconnaissance, the Ranger has learned that information is often the greatest weapon. A single glance can reveal weaknesses in fortifications, exposed paths through enemy positions, or vulnerabilities in powerful beasts long before combat begins.

Those who master this level of Hawk Eyes are highly sought after as scouts, pathfinders, and monster hunters, capable of gathering intelligence that would otherwise require entire expeditions.

---

### Effects

#### Enhanced Vision

- Increase the Ranger's **effective visual identification range by ×12**.
- Accurately estimate distances to creatures, objects, and terrain features.
- Determine whether a target has **Light, Half, Three-Quarters, or Total Cover**.
- Clearly identify visible weapons, armour, shields, equipment, ammunition, and carried items.
- Recognise obvious injuries, physical abnormalities, damaged equipment, signs of exhaustion, and environmental hazards.
- Identify visible weak points, including damaged armour, exposed joints, old wounds, unstable footing, and structural weaknesses in objects or buildings.

#### Shooter's Eye

- Reduce penalties applied to ranged attack rolls caused by:
  - Distance.

  - Environmental visibility.
- Reduce these penalties by **4** (minimum 0).

This benefit applies to:

- Ranged weapon attacks.
- Ranged combat talents.
- Ranged combat skills.
- Racial traits that require a ranged attack roll.

#### Limitations

- Hawk Eyes does not grant Darkvision.
- Hawk Eyes cannot reveal invisible creatures.
- Hawk Eyes cannot see through solid objects or magical barriers.
- Magical concealment and visual illusions function normally.
- Information is limited to what is physically visible.
- The GM determines what details can reasonably be observed.

### Lore

Rank 4 is known among the Ranger Lodges as the **Eagle's Gaze**, a title earned only by those whose perception has become legendary. Rangers of this calibre have guided armies through hostile lands, uncovered hidden monster lairs, and prevented countless ambushes simply by seeing what others could not.

Stories tell of Eagle's Gaze Rangers identifying a cracked bridge from over a kilometre away or spotting a dragon circling high above the clouds long before anyone else noticed. Whether these tales are embellished or not, they all speak to the same truth: knowledge gained through observation is often the deciding factor between victory and defeat.

---

> *"The greatest advantage is not striking first—it is knowing exactly where to strike."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Aelar Windwatch**

## Rank 5
### Description

The Ranger has reached the pinnacle of natural vision, earning the legendary title of **Predator's Vision** among the Ranger Lodges. Their eyesight rivals the greatest birds of prey in Asteria, allowing them to observe creatures and landscapes with astonishing clarity across immense distances. Every movement, flaw, and detail becomes part of a larger picture that only a master Ranger can truly understand.

To the Ranger, no battlefield is ever unknown. They instinctively recognise firing lanes, hidden dangers, structural weaknesses, and visible vulnerabilities before combat begins. What others dismiss as distant shapes, the Ranger sees as opportunities waiting to be exploited.

Predator's Vision is not magical sight—it is the culmination of decades of discipline, patience, and experience. It is the final proof that the Ranger's greatest weapon has always been their ability to observe.

---

### Effects

#### Predator's Vision

- Increase the Ranger's **effective visual identification range by ×20**.
- Accurately estimate the distance to any visible creature, object, or landmark.
- Determine whether a target has **Light, Half, Three-Quarters, or Total Cover**.
- Clearly identify visible weapons, armour, shields, equipment, ammunition, carried items, and significant magical equipment.
- Recognise visible injuries, physical abnormalities, damaged equipment, signs of exhaustion, and structural weaknesses.
- Instinctively identify visible weak points on creatures, objects, and structures.
- Greatly reduce the time required to search an area using sight, at the GM's discretion.

#### Shooter's Eye

- Reduce penalties applied to ranged attack rolls caused by:
  - Distance.
  - Target size.
  - Environmental visibility.
- Reduce these penalties by **5** (minimum 0).

This benefit applies to:

- Ranged weapon attacks.
- Ranged combat talents.
- Ranged combat skills.
- Racial traits that require a ranged attack roll.

#### Battlefield Mastery

While observing an area, the Ranger can quickly identify:

- Hidden firing positions.
- Ambush locations.
- Natural chokepoints.
- Escape routes.
- Structural weaknesses.
- Elevated vantage points.
- Environmental hazards.

The GM determines what information is reasonably visible.

#### Limitations

- Hawk Eyes does not grant Darkvision.
- Hawk Eyes cannot reveal invisible creatures.
- Hawk Eyes cannot see through solid objects or magical barriers.
- Magical concealment, illusions, and divination wards function normally unless another ability bypasses them.
- All information is limited to what is physically visible.
- The GM determines what details can reasonably be observed.

---

### Lore

Predator's Vision is considered the highest achievement of every Ranger. Throughout history, only a select few have mastered this discipline, becoming legendary scouts whose observations have changed the course of wars and brought down creatures thought impossible to hunt.

The oldest Ranger texts teach that the eyes merely collect information—it is the mind that gives it meaning. A master of Hawk Eyes does not simply see farther than others; they understand what they are seeing before anyone else has even recognised the danger.

Among the Ranger Lodges, earning the title of **Predator's Vision** is one of the greatest honours imaginable. It signifies that the Ranger has become the embodiment of patience, awareness, and precision—the perfect hunter whose greatest victories are often won before the first arrow is ever loosed.

---

> *"Before the arrow is drawn, before the bow is raised, before the hunt begins… the Ranger has already seen the outcome."*  
> — **Grandmaster Thalion Skywatch, First Bearer of Predator's Vision**
