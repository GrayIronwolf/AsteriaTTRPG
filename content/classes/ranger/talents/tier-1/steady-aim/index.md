# Steady Aim

## Overview
Every Ranger understands that patience is often deadlier than speed. Rather than firing the moment an opportunity presents itself, they learn to slow their breathing, steady their stance, and carefully observe their target before releasing a single, deliberate shot.

Steady Aim represents the Ranger's growing mastery of precision marksmanship. By sacrificing valuable time to line up an attack, the Ranger greatly improves the likelihood of striking their intended target. This discipline rewards careful planning and creates opportunities that reckless archers often miss.

Although the technique requires concentration, experienced Rangers know that one perfectly placed arrow is often worth far more than a dozen hurried shots.

---

## Rank 1
### Description

Every Ranger understands that patience is often deadlier than speed. Rather than firing the moment an opportunity presents itself, they learn to slow their breathing, steady their stance, and carefully observe their target before releasing a single, deliberate shot.

Steady Aim represents the Ranger's growing mastery of precision marksmanship. By sacrificing valuable time to line up an attack, the Ranger greatly improves the likelihood of striking their intended target. This discipline rewards careful planning and creates opportunities that reckless archers often miss.

Although the technique requires concentration, experienced Rangers know that one perfectly placed arrow is often worth far more than a dozen hurried shots.

---

### Effects

#### Steady Aim

The Ranger may spend **2 or 3 Action Points** carefully aiming at a single creature within the range of a ranged weapon.

The target must remain visible throughout the aiming process.

After aiming, the Ranger gains the following bonus on their **next ranged weapon attack** against that target.

| Action Points Spent | Bonus |
|---------------------|-------|
| **2 AP** | **+1 Attack Roll** |
| **3 AP** | **+2 Attack Roll** |

#### Focus Rules

- The attack must be made **before the end of the Ranger's next turn**.
- The Ranger may only aim at **one creature** at a time.
- Changing targets immediately removes the bonus.
- If the target leaves the Ranger's line of sight, the bonus is lost.
- Becoming blinded or incapacitated immediately ends the effect.
- Steady Aim only functions with **ranged weapon attacks**.

---

### Lore

The first lesson taught to every Ranger marksman is simple: a hurried arrow is a wasted arrow. Apprentices spend countless hours drawing their bow, holding their aim, and waiting for the perfect moment before they are ever permitted to loose a shot.

As their discipline grows, Rangers learn that precision is not a gift, but a skill earned through patience and repetition. Every breath, every heartbeat, and every movement is controlled until releasing the arrow becomes second nature.

Among the Ranger Lodges, mastering Steady Aim is considered the foundation of true marksmanship, forming the basis for every advanced shooting technique that follows.

---

> *"Anyone can fire an arrow. A Ranger waits until missing is no longer an option."*  
> — **Master Marksman Caelan Ashwood**

## Rank 2
### Description

The Ranger's discipline continues to improve, allowing them to remain calm even in the chaos of battle. Every breath, every heartbeat, and every movement is carefully controlled as they analyse their quarry before committing to the shot. What once required intense concentration now becomes second nature.

With greater experience comes greater confidence. The Ranger begins to recognise predictable movement, defensive habits, and fleeting openings that less experienced archers would overlook. By spending a few precious moments studying their target, they dramatically increase the likelihood of striking true.

Among seasoned Rangers, patience is never viewed as hesitation. It is an investment that ensures every arrow has the greatest possible chance of ending the hunt.

---

### Effects

#### Focused Aim

The Ranger may spend **2 or 3 Action Points** studying a single creature within **line of sight**.

The target must remain visible throughout the observation.

|---------------------|-------|

| **3 AP** | **+3 Attack Roll** |

The bonus applies to the Ranger's **next ranged weapon attack** against that target.

- The Ranger may only study **one creature** at a time.
- Changing targets immediately removes all accumulated focus.
- If the target leaves the Ranger's line of sight, the bonus is lost.
- Becoming blinded or incapacitated immediately ends the effect.
- Steady Aim only functions with **ranged weapon attacks**.

---

### Lore

Within the Ranger Lodges, the second rank of Steady Aim marks the point where an apprentice begins to think like a true marksman. Rather than reacting to an enemy's movements, they learn to predict them, patiently waiting for the moment when a successful shot becomes almost inevitable.

Many Rangers become respected hunters through skill alone, but those who master Steady Aim are admired for something greater—the discipline to resist taking the easy shot in favour of the perfect one. Their patience often allows them to succeed where others would fail.

---

> *"The arrow released too soon belongs to chance. The arrow released at the right moment belongs to the Ranger."*  
> — **Master Ranger Caelan Skywatch**

## Rank 3
### Description

By the third rank, the Ranger has developed exceptional battlefield patience. Rather than simply observing a target's movements, they begin recognising subtle flaws in posture, damaged armour, and predictable defensive habits. Every moment spent aiming increases the likelihood of finding the perfect opening.

The Ranger has learned that true precision is not simply striking the target—it is striking where the target is weakest. Careful preparation allows the Ranger to transform an accurate shot into a devastating one, rewarding those willing to sacrifice time for overwhelming effectiveness.

Veteran Rangers often describe this technique as allowing the prey to defeat itself. Given enough time, every creature eventually reveals a weakness.

### Lore

Only experienced Rangers reach the third rank of Steady Aim. By this stage, years of hunting have taught them that every creature, no matter how powerful, carries imperfections waiting to be exploited. Patience reveals what haste conceals.

Across Asteria, many renowned monster hunters owe their greatest victories not to superior strength, but to their ability to wait for a single decisive opportunity. Among the Ranger Lodges, students are often reminded that the perfect shot is not the fastest—it is the one that ends the hunt.

> *"A careless hunter looks for the target. A master hunter looks for the moment."*  
> — **High Ranger Caelan Skywatch**

## Rank 4
### Description

The Ranger has mastered the discipline of controlled precision. Every movement of the target is carefully studied, every breath measured, and every opportunity evaluated before an arrow is released. What appears to others as hesitation is, in reality, absolute confidence in the coming shot.

Years of experience have taught the Ranger how to exploit even the smallest imperfections in an opponent's defences. A raised shield, a gap in armour, or a momentary shift in footing becomes all that is needed to deliver a devastating strike. Few marksmen possess the patience required to reach this level of mastery.

Among veteran Rangers, Steady Aim is regarded as the point where marksmanship becomes an art rather than a skill.

## Rank 5
### Description

The Ranger has reached the pinnacle of precision marksmanship. Every shot is the product of unwavering discipline, exceptional battlefield awareness, and years of relentless practice. Rather than reacting to the battle around them, the Ranger dictates its pace, patiently waiting for the exact moment their quarry exposes even the smallest weakness.

At this level, Steady Aim becomes more than careful observation—it becomes instinct. The Ranger naturally anticipates movement, identifies vulnerable openings, and threads arrows through gaps that most archers would never attempt. No shot is wasted, and every release carries the intent to end the hunt.

Within the Ranger Lodges, those who master Steady Aim are regarded as living legends. Their patience is spoken of with the same reverence as their accuracy, for they understand that the perfect shot is worth waiting for.

### Lore

To master Steady Aim is to earn a place among the greatest Rangers in Asteria. Stories tell of master marksmen ending battles with a single arrow, striking through impossibly small openings or piercing the only vulnerable point on creatures thought impossible to kill. Whether these tales are embellished or not, they all share the same lesson: patience triumphs over haste.

Senior Rangers teach that a master archer is not defined by how many arrows they fire, but by how few they need. Every shot is deliberate, every movement calculated, and every hunt concluded with calm precision. Those who witness Steady Aim at its highest level often believe it to be magic, though Rangers know it is simply the reward for a lifetime of discipline.

---
