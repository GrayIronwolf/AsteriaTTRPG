# Hunter's Mark

## Overview
A skilled Ranger does not simply attack their quarry—they study it, understand it, and exploit every weakness it reveals. By focusing a small amount of mana upon a chosen creature, the Ranger forms a mystical bond that heightens their awareness of the target's movements and vulnerabilities.

Hunter's Mark represents the Ranger's transition from hunter to battlefield specialist. Rather than increasing accuracy, the mark allows the Ranger to strike with greater force whenever they successfully land a hit. Every arrow, thrown weapon, or ranged attack becomes more punishing as the Ranger learns exactly where to wound their quarry.

At this rank, the benefits of Hunter's Mark are known only to the Ranger. As mastery grows, they will learn to communicate these weaknesses to nearby allies, allowing an entire hunting party to bring down even the mightiest prey.

## Rank 1
### Description

---

### Effects

#### Mark Quarry

- Spend **20 MP** to mark one creature within **30 metres** that you can see.
- The mark lasts for **1 minute**.
- Only **one creature** may be marked at a time.
- Marking a new creature immediately ends the previous mark.

#### Hunter's Instinct

While your Quarry is marked:

- Your successful attacks against the marked target deal **+1d4 bonus damage**.
- This bonus applies to:
  - Ranged weapon attacks.
  - Melee weapon attacks.
  - Ranger talents that deal direct damage.
- The bonus damage is applied **once per successful attack**.

#### Limitations

- Allies gain no benefit from Hunter's Mark at this rank.
- The mark immediately ends if the target dies.
- The mark does not reveal the target's location if it leaves your line of sight.
- Hunter's Mark does not stack with another Ranger's Hunter's Mark on the same target. If multiple Rangers mark the same creature, each Ranger only gains the benefit of their own mark.

---

### Lore

The oldest Ranger Lodges teach that every creature has a weakness waiting to be uncovered. Through instinct, experience, and a small infusion of mana, a Ranger learns to focus entirely upon a single quarry, studying its movements until every successful strike lands with greater precision and force.

Many believe Hunter's Mark is a spell of tracking, but experienced Rangers know this is only part of its purpose. The true strength of the technique lies in the unwavering focus it demands. Once the quarry has been chosen, every thought, every movement, and every attack is dedicated to bringing the hunt to its conclusion.

---

> *"Choose your quarry wisely. Every hunt begins with a decision, but only the greatest Rangers see it through to the end."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 2
### Description

The Ranger's connection to their marked quarry deepens, allowing them to exploit weaknesses with increasing efficiency. Rather than simply identifying vulnerable areas, the Ranger instinctively predicts how their prey will move and where the next opening will appear. Every successful strike becomes more punishing as the hunt progresses.

As the mark strengthens, maintaining focus requires less conscious effort. The Ranger's attention remains fixed upon the quarry even amidst the chaos of battle, ensuring every attack is delivered with purpose. While the mark still benefits only the Ranger, it lays the foundation for the cooperative hunting techniques taught to veteran Rangers.

Experienced hunters often say that once a creature has been marked, every wound it suffers becomes easier to inflict than the last.

### Lore

By the second rank, Hunter's Mark has become more than a simple hunting technique—it is a symbol of unwavering determination. Rangers learn to lock their attention onto a single quarry, studying every movement until striking vulnerable areas becomes second nature.

Within the Ranger Lodges, apprentices are reminded that discipline is just as important as skill. A distracted hunter misses opportunities, while a focused Ranger turns every opening into a decisive blow. Those who master Hunter's Mark are respected not for the number of creatures they defeat, but for the precision with which they bring every hunt to its conclusion.

---

> *"A marked quarry has already made its first mistake—it allowed a Ranger to choose it."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 3
# Description

The Ranger's mastery of Hunter's Mark evolves beyond personal gain, becoming a powerful tool for coordinated hunting. By carefully studying the marked quarry, the Ranger identifies weaknesses, damaged armour, and predictable movements, allowing not only themselves but also their companions to strike with greater effectiveness.

Through shouted commands, hand signals, or simple battlefield awareness, the Ranger instinctively guides nearby allies towards vulnerable areas. Every successful attack against the marked quarry contributes to wearing it down, turning even the most formidable foe into prey.

Within the Ranger Lodges, reaching the third rank is seen as the point where a hunter becomes a leader. The Ranger no longer hunts alone—they direct the entire pack.

---

### Effects

#### Mark Quarry

- Spend **40 MP** to mark one creature within **50 metres** that you can see.
- The mark lasts for **5 minutes**.
- Only **one creature** may be marked at a time.
- Marking another creature immediately ends the previous mark.

#### Hunter's Instinct

While your Quarry is marked:

**The Ranger gains:**

- **+1d8 bonus damage** on all successful attacks against the marked target.

**Allied creatures gain:**

- **+1d4 bonus damage** on successful attacks against the marked target.

This bonus applies to:

- Ranged weapon attacks.
- Melee weapon attacks.
- Talents and abilities that deal direct damage.
- Offensive spells that successfully hit the marked target.

The bonus damage is applied **once per successful attack**.

#### Limitations

- Allies must be aware of the marked target to gain the bonus.
- Hunter's Mark does not reveal the target's location if it leaves line of sight.
- The mark immediately ends if the target dies.
- Only one Hunter's Mark may affect a creature at a time. If multiple Rangers mark the same target, only the highest-ranked Hunter's Mark applies.

---

### Lore

By the third rank, Hunter's Mark becomes one of the defining techniques of an experienced Ranger. Their ability to recognise and communicate weaknesses transforms them from a solitary hunter into the leader of a coordinated hunt. Veteran adventuring parties quickly learn to trust a Ranger's judgement, knowing that every command is based upon careful observation rather than guesswork.

Stories tell of Ranger companies bringing down dragons, giants, and Elder Beasts by relentlessly exploiting a single weakness identified by their leader. These victories were not won through overwhelming strength, but through patience, precision, and cooperation—principles that define every true Ranger.

---

> *"One hunter may wound the beast. A guided pack will bring it down."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 4
### Description

The Ranger's Hunter's Mark has become a symbol of battlefield leadership. With only a moment's observation, they identify weaknesses that would otherwise remain unnoticed, directing their allies to strike precisely where the quarry is most vulnerable. Every command, gesture, and call is deliberate, allowing the hunting party to work together with deadly efficiency.

The Ranger's connection to the marked quarry grows stronger as the hunt progresses. Every wound reveals another opportunity, and every successful attack builds momentum for the entire group. Few creatures can withstand a coordinated assault led by a master Ranger.

Veteran Rangers often say that by this stage, the quarry is defeated long before the final blow is delivered—it simply has not realised it yet.

### Lore

Only accomplished Rangers achieve the fourth rank of Hunter's Mark. By this point, they have learned that hunting is rarely an individual pursuit. The greatest victories come from guiding companions toward the same objective, ensuring every strike contributes to bringing the quarry down.

Entire Ranger companies have built their tactics around Hunters capable of maintaining these advanced marks. Their commands carry absolute confidence, allowing warriors, mages, and scouts alike to focus their attacks with remarkable precision. To fight beside such a Ranger is to feel as though every weakness has already been uncovered before the battle has truly begun.

---

> *"The mark is not a promise that the quarry will fall. It is a promise that every hunter now knows where to strike."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 5
### Description

The Ranger has mastered Hunter's Mark, becoming the unquestioned leader of the hunt. A single marked quarry becomes the sole focus of the battlefield, with every ally instinctively recognising where to strike and every attack driving the prey closer to its inevitable end. What began as a personal hunting technique has evolved into one of the most feared battlefield tactics employed by the Ranger Lodges.

A master Ranger no longer simply identifies weaknesses—they orchestrate the downfall of their quarry. Through observation, experience, and unwavering focus, they direct every member of the hunting party towards the target's greatest vulnerabilities. Against such coordination, even legendary beasts struggle to withstand the relentless assault.

Throughout Asteria, stories tell of Dragons, Elder Beasts, and Demon Lords falling beneath the combined might of a hunting party guided by a single master Ranger. Their greatest weapon was never their bow, but their ability to unite every strike into one perfect hunt.

---

### Effects

#### Mark Quarry

- Spend **60 MP** to mark one creature within **80 metres** that you can see.
- The mark lasts for **30 minutes**.
- Only **one creature** may be marked at a time.
- Marking another creature immediately ends the previous mark.

#### Master Hunter

While your Quarry is marked:

**The Ranger gains:**

- **+1d12 bonus damage** on all successful attacks against the marked target.

**Allied creatures gain:**

- **+1d8 bonus damage** on successful attacks against the marked target.

This bonus applies to:

- Ranged weapon attacks.
- Melee weapon attacks.
- Talents and abilities that deal direct damage.
- Offensive spells that successfully damage the marked target.

The bonus damage is applied **once per successful attack**.

#### Hunt Leader

While Hunter's Mark is active:

- Allies always know which creature is marked.
- The Ranger may reapply Hunter's Mark to the same target without paying an additional Mana Cost if the mark expires naturally.
- If the marked target is reduced to **0 HP**, the Ranger may immediately transfer Hunter's Mark to another creature within range **without spending an action**, paying only the Mana Cost.

#### Limitations

- Hunter's Mark does not reveal the target's location if it leaves line of sight.
- Only one Hunter's Mark may affect a creature at a time.
- If multiple Rangers mark the same creature, only the highest-ranked Hunter's Mark applies.
- Bonus damage from Hunter's Mark does not stack with another Hunter's Mark.

---

### Lore

To achieve the fifth rank of Hunter's Mark is to become one of the legendary Huntmasters of Asteria. These Rangers are revered throughout the Lodges for their uncanny ability to coordinate entire hunting parties against seemingly unstoppable foes. Every order they give is calculated, every marked target carefully chosen, and every strike brings the quarry closer to its inevitable demise.

Many of history's greatest hunts have been led by Rangers who possessed this mastery. Their companions often spoke of battles that felt effortless, as though every weakness had already been discovered before the fighting had even begun. Whether facing towering Behemoths, ancient Dragons, or horrors from beyond the veil, a Huntmaster ensured that no attack was wasted.

To the Ranger Lodges, Hunter's Mark is more than a talent—it is the symbol of absolute trust between hunter and hunting party, where many act with the precision of one.

---

> *"One arrow begins the hunt. One mark ensures every arrow thereafter finds its purpose."*  
> — **Grand Huntmaster Elyas Thornwood, First Keeper of the Hunter's Mark**
