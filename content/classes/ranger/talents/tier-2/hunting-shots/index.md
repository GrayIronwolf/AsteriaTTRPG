# Hunting Shots

## Overview
The greatest Rangers are not defined by the weapons they carry, but by the techniques they master. Hunting Shots represent years of specialised training, allowing a Ranger to perform extraordinary feats of marksmanship beyond a standard attack.

Upon learning this talent, the Ranger unlocks their first Hunting Shot and first Trick Shot. Hunting Shots are specialised combat techniques designed to control or weaken enemies, while Trick Shots demonstrate exceptional skill, allowing impossible angles and creative solutions both in and out of combat.

As the Ranger grows stronger, additional ranks unlock new shots rather than replacing old ones, creating an ever-expanding arsenal of precision techniques.

---

## Rank 1
### Description

The greatest Rangers are not defined by the weapons they carry, but by the techniques they master. Hunting Shots represent years of specialised training, allowing a Ranger to perform extraordinary feats of marksmanship beyond a standard attack.

Upon learning this talent, the Ranger unlocks their first Hunting Shot and first Trick Shot. Hunting Shots are specialised combat techniques designed to control or weaken enemies, while Trick Shots demonstrate exceptional skill, allowing impossible angles and creative solutions both in and out of combat.

As the Ranger grows stronger, additional ranks unlock new shots rather than replacing old ones, creating an ever-expanding arsenal of precision techniques.

---

### Effects

#### Hunting Shot — Stunning Shot

**Stamina Cost:** 15 SP

After making a successful ranged weapon attack, you may declare it as a **Stunning Shot**.

- The target must succeed on a **Constitution Saving Throw**.
- On a failure, the target becomes **Staggered** until the end of its next turn.
- While Staggered, the target cannot take Reactions and has Disadvantage on its next attack roll.
- On a successful save, the target suffers normal weapon damage with no additional effect.

#### Trick Shot — Curve Shot

**Stamina Cost:** 10 SP

The Ranger applies exceptional spin and trajectory to a projectile.

- Fire around corners, trees, pillars, or similar obstacles.
- Ignore partial line-of-sight restrictions where a curved trajectory is physically possible.
- Reduce penalties for difficult or unconventional ranged attacks, at the GM's discretion.
- This shot cannot pass through solid objects or total cover.

#### General Rules

- Only one Hunting Shot or Trick Shot may be applied to a single attack unless another ability states otherwise.
- Hunting Shots may only be used with ranged weapons.
- Additional Hunting Shots and Trick Shots are unlocked by increasing this talent's rank.

---

### Lore

Every Ranger Lodge teaches that an arrow is only as effective as the mind guiding it. While ordinary archers rely upon accuracy alone, Rangers develop specialised techniques that allow each shot to serve a unique purpose. Whether disabling prey, striking impossible angles, or controlling the battlefield, every technique reflects countless hours of disciplined practice.

Veteran Rangers often recognise one another not by the bows they carry, but by the techniques they employ. Many legendary Hunting Shots have been passed from mentor to apprentice for centuries, with each generation refining and expanding the art of precision marksmanship.

---

> *"Any archer can loose an arrow. A Ranger chooses exactly what that arrow will accomplish."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 2
### Description

As the Ranger's mastery grows, so too does their arsenal of specialised techniques. Every hunt presents new challenges, and experienced Rangers learn that a single type of shot cannot solve every problem. Through relentless practice, they develop new methods capable of overcoming heavily armoured prey and striking enemies from angles they believe to be impossible.

At this rank, the Ranger unlocks two new techniques: **Drill Shot**, a devastating armour-piercing attack designed to punch through resilient defences, and **Ricochet Shot**, a trick shot that uses hard surfaces to strike targets from unexpected directions. These techniques reward creativity, positioning, and battlefield awareness.

A skilled Ranger is never limited by a straight line. They learn to make every obstacle another tool in their hunt.

---

### Effects

#### Hunting Shot — Drill Shot

**Stamina Cost:** 20 SP

The Ranger fires a specialised armour-piercing projectile designed to bore through armour, scales, and hardened hides.

- Make a ranged weapon attack as normal.
- On a successful hit, the attack **ignores Half Cover**.
- The attack ignores **2 points of Armour Rating or Natural Armour** (or an equivalent value determined by the GM).
- Drill Shot may only affect the initial target and cannot strike additional creatures.

#### Trick Shot — Ricochet Shot

**Stamina Cost:** 15 SP

The Ranger deliberately bounces a projectile off a hard surface before striking the intended target.

- The projectile may ricochet off one solid surface before hitting its target.
- The shot may ignore obstacles that would normally block a direct line of fire, provided a reasonable ricochet path exists.
- Reduce penalties for indirect shots at the GM's discretion.
- Ricochet Shot cannot pass through solid objects or total cover.

#### General Rules

- The Ranger retains access to **Stunning Shot** and **Curve Shot**.
- Only one Hunting Shot or one Trick Shot may be applied to a single attack unless another ability states otherwise.
- Hunting Shots may only be used with ranged weapons.
- Additional Hunting Shots and Trick Shots are unlocked by increasing this talent's rank.

---

### Lore

Among Ranger Lodges, the second rank of Hunting Shots marks the point where an apprentice begins to think beyond conventional marksmanship. Rather than simply aiming at a target, they learn to manipulate both the projectile and the environment, turning armour into a weakness and obstacles into opportunities.

Many famous Rangers earned their reputations through shots that others believed impossible. Tales speak of arrows skipping from stone walls, bolts punching through thick scales, and hunters striking prey that never even realised they had been exposed. Such techniques are only possible through unwavering discipline and countless hours of practice.

---

> *"When the path is blocked, do not change your target—change the path."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 3
### Description

The Ranger has progressed beyond mastering individual techniques and now learns to manipulate both the battlefield and the enemy's movements. Every shot is carefully chosen to restrict, outmanoeuvre, or overwhelm their quarry before the decisive strike is delivered.

At this rank, the Ranger unlocks **Pinning Shot**, a technique designed to hinder an opponent's movement, and **Split Shot**, a remarkable feat of precision that allows a single attack to threaten multiple targets. These techniques showcase the Ranger's growing mastery over both control and precision.

Veteran Rangers know that victory is often determined long before the final arrow is loosed. By controlling where an enemy can move and how they react, the hunt becomes all but inevitable.

---

### Effects

#### Hunting Shot — Pinning Shot

**Stamina Cost:** 25 SP

The Ranger fires a carefully placed shot intended to restrict the target's movement rather than inflict maximum injury.

- Make a ranged weapon attack as normal.
- On a successful hit, the target must succeed on a **Strength Saving Throw** or become **Pinned** until the end of its next turn.
- While Pinned:
  - The target's movement speed is reduced to **0**.
  - The target cannot Dash.
- Flying creatures that fail the saving throw immediately descend safely to the ground if able, or have their flying speed reduced to 0 until the effect ends (GM discretion).
- Creatures that are significantly larger than the projectile may gain Advantage on the saving throw.

#### Trick Shot — Split Shot

**Stamina Cost:** 20 SP

The Ranger performs an extraordinary feat of marksmanship, releasing two projectiles almost simultaneously.

- Make one attack roll against two creatures within **3 metres** of each other.
- Compare the same attack roll against each target's Armour Class.
- Roll damage separately for each creature that is successfully hit.
- Ammunition for both shots is still consumed.

#### General Rules

- The Ranger retains access to **Stunning Shot**, **Curve Shot**, **Drill Shot**, and **Ricochet Shot**.
- Only one Hunting Shot or one Trick Shot may be applied to a single attack unless another ability states otherwise.
- Hunting Shots may only be used with ranged weapons.
- Additional Hunting Shots and Trick Shots are unlocked by increasing this talent's rank.

---

### Lore

By the third rank, a Ranger's techniques become recognised throughout the Lodges as the mark of a seasoned hunter. They no longer rely solely on accuracy but instead shape the flow of battle itself, deciding where prey may run and when it must stand its ground.

Stories speak of master Rangers pinning charging beasts to ancient trees before turning a single fluid movement into two perfectly placed arrows that felled separate enemies. Such feats are impossible without years of disciplined practice, unwavering focus, and complete trust in one's own skill.

---

> *"A true hunter does not simply strike their prey—they decide where it may stand, where it may run, and when the hunt shall end."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 4
### Description

Master Rangers understand that a well-placed arrow can control the battlefield just as effectively as it can wound an enemy. By the fourth rank, they have developed techniques capable of overwhelming even the largest foes, using raw impact and exceptional skill to dictate the flow of combat.

At this rank, the Ranger unlocks **Impact Shot**, a devastating attack that uses tremendous force to drive enemies backwards or knock them from their feet, and **Arcing Shot**, a specialised technique that allows projectiles to travel in a high trajectory, striking enemies hiding behind walls, fortifications, or other obstacles.

These techniques demonstrate that a master Ranger is not limited by distance, cover, or terrain. Every obstacle becomes another opportunity to land the perfect shot.

---

### Effects

#### Hunting Shot — Impact Shot

**Stamina Cost:** 30 SP

The Ranger delivers a powerful shot designed to overwhelm the target through sheer force.

- Make a ranged weapon attack as normal.
- On a successful hit, the target must succeed on a **Strength Saving Throw**.
- On a failed save:
  - The target is pushed **3 metres** directly away from the Ranger.
  - If the target cannot be pushed because it collides with a solid object, it is instead **knocked Prone**.
- On a successful save, the target suffers normal weapon damage but is not pushed or knocked prone.
- **Large** creatures have **Advantage** on the saving throw.
- **Huge** and larger creatures cannot be pushed but may still be knocked **Prone** at the GM's discretion.

#### Trick Shot — Arcing Shot

**Stamina Cost:** 20 SP

The Ranger launches a projectile in a high, controlled arc to strike targets beyond conventional lines of fire.

- Ignore obstacles that block a direct shot provided a clear aerial trajectory exists.
- You may target creatures behind low walls, battlements, trenches, or similar cover.
- The target does not benefit from **Half Cover** provided the arc successfully bypasses it.
- The GM determines whether sufficient overhead clearance exists for the shot.

#### General Rules

- The Ranger retains access to all previously unlocked Hunting Shots and Trick Shots.
- Only one Hunting Shot or one Trick Shot may be applied to a single attack unless another ability states otherwise.
- Hunting Shots may only be used with ranged weapons.
- Additional Hunting Shots and Trick Shots are unlocked by increasing this talent's rank.

---

### Lore

Among the Ranger Lodges, the fourth rank of Hunting Shots is considered the mark of a battlefield master. These Rangers no longer think only about defeating a single opponent—they shape the entire battlefield, forcing enemies into vulnerable positions while striking those who believed themselves safely protected.

Many military commanders have sought the aid of master Rangers during sieges and large-scale battles. Their ability to strike defenders behind walls, scatter charging enemies, and manipulate the flow of combat has turned the tide of countless conflicts throughout Asteria.

---

> *"A wall is only protection until a Ranger learns to shoot over it. Strength is only an advantage until a Ranger learns to throw you backwards."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 5
### Description

The Ranger has reached the pinnacle of ranged weapon mastery. Every arrow, bolt, bullet, or thrown projectile becomes an extension of their will, capable of travelling extraordinary distances and striking with impossible precision. No battlefield is too large, no target too distant, and no obstacle too daunting for a true master of Hunting Shots.

At this rank, the Ranger unlocks **Horizon Shot**, a legendary technique that dramatically extends the effective range of their ranged weapon, allowing them to engage enemies long before those enemies can retaliate. Alongside it, the Ranger also masters **Impossible Shot**, a technique reserved for only the greatest marksmen, capable of accomplishing feats that most would consider beyond belief.

Among the Ranger Lodges, only a handful of hunters throughout history have ever demonstrated these techniques in battle. Their shots are remembered for generations, becoming stories told around campfires as examples of what true dedication can achieve.

---

### Effects

#### Hunting Shot — Horizon Shot

**Stamina Cost:** 35 SP

The Ranger unleashes a perfectly calculated shot designed to travel far beyond the normal limits of their weapon.

- Increase the weapon's **maximum range by 50%** for this attack.
- Ignore penalties associated with attacking at **long range**.
- The attack ignores **Half Cover**.
- Horizon Shot may be used with any ranged weapon.

#### Trick Shot — Impossible Shot

**Stamina Cost:** 25 SP

The Ranger performs a feat of marksmanship bordering on legend.

Choose one of the following when making the attack:

- Thread a projectile through an exceptionally narrow opening.
- Strike a tiny unattended object.
- Cut a rope, chain, or similar object.
- Trigger a distant mechanism.
- Perform another extraordinary ranged feat, subject to GM approval.

The GM may reduce or remove penalties for exceptionally difficult shots when Impossible Shot is used.

#### General Rules

- The Ranger retains access to every previously unlocked Hunting Shot and Trick Shot.
- Only one Hunting Shot or one Trick Shot may be applied to a single attack unless another ability states otherwise.
- Hunting Shots may only be used with ranged weapons.
- Impossible Shot cannot bypass magical barriers or ignore total cover.

---

### Lore

The fifth rank of Hunting Shots is spoken of with near-mythical reverence throughout the Ranger Lodges. Tales tell of Rangers striking enemy commanders from across entire valleys, severing bridge supports with a single arrow, or saving companions by cutting ropes and triggering mechanisms at precisely the right moment.

These feats are not considered luck or magic—they are the culmination of decades of unwavering discipline, flawless technique, and an intimate understanding of both weapon and environment. To witness a master Ranger employ Horizon Shot or Impossible Shot is to witness the very peak of mortal marksmanship.

---

> *"Distance is an obstacle only to those who doubt their aim."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood, Master Marksman of the Emerald Lodge**
