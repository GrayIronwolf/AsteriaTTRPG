# Predator's Roar

## Overview
The wilderness belongs to those who command respect, not through words, but through presence alone. Predator's Roar allows the Ranger to unleash a thunderous battle cry that echoes the dominance of the world's greatest hunters. It is not magic, but the culmination of unwavering confidence, survival instinct, and countless victories over dangerous prey.

When the Ranger roars, nearby allies are reminded that they stand beside a seasoned hunter, while enemies instinctively recognise the arrival of a predator. Even creatures that have never encountered the Ranger may hesitate for a brief moment as instinct tells them they have become the prey.

Though its effects are subtle at first, Predator's Roar becomes increasingly terrifying as the Ranger's reputation and mastery grow.

---

## Rank 1
### Description

The wilderness belongs to those who command respect, not through words, but through presence alone. Predator's Roar allows the Ranger to unleash a thunderous battle cry that echoes the dominance of the world's greatest hunters. It is not magic, but the culmination of unwavering confidence, survival instinct, and countless victories over dangerous prey.

When the Ranger roars, nearby allies are reminded that they stand beside a seasoned hunter, while enemies instinctively recognise the arrival of a predator. Even creatures that have never encountered the Ranger may hesitate for a brief moment as instinct tells them they have become the prey.

Though its effects are subtle at first, Predator's Roar becomes increasingly terrifying as the Ranger's reputation and mastery grow.

---

### Effects

#### Predator's Roar

**Cost:** 30 SP

Unleash a powerful roar affecting all creatures within **10 metres**.

**Enemies**

- Creatures of your choice must make a **Wisdom Saving Throw**.
- On a failure, they become **Shaken** until the end of their next turn.
- While Shaken:
  - They have **Disadvantage on their first attack roll**.
  - They cannot take **Reactions** until the start of their next turn.
- On a successful save, the creature is unaffected.

**Allies**

Friendly creatures within range gain:

- **+1 Morale Bonus** to their next attack roll before the end of their next turn.
- Immunity to the **Shaken** condition caused by your Predator's Roar.

#### Limitations

- Predator's Roar does not affect creatures that cannot hear you.
- A creature can only be affected by your Predator's Roar once per use.
- This is a non-magical ability and may affect creatures normally immune to magical fear, subject to GM discretion.

---

### Lore

Among the Ranger Lodges, Predator's Roar is taught only after a hunter has proven themselves against formidable prey. It is said that every great predator possesses a presence capable of unsettling even the strongest opponent before the hunt has truly begun.

Legends speak of Rangers whose roars halted charging beasts, scattered packs of wolves, and emboldened exhausted companions to fight on. While many dismiss these stories as exaggeration, those who have stood beside a master Ranger know that confidence itself can become a weapon.

To the Ranger, the roar is not a challenge—it is a declaration that the hunt has already begun, and that every creature who hears it has a choice: stand and fight, or flee.

---

> *"The loudest roar is not the one born of anger—it is the one that reminds the forest who sits at the top of the food chain."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 2
### Description

The Ranger's roar carries far greater authority, echoing with the confidence of a hunter that has survived countless battles. It is no longer simply a display of dominance—it becomes an instinctive declaration that the Ranger has claimed the battlefield as their hunting ground.

Those who hear the roar often react before they even realise why. Allies stand taller, emboldened by the presence of an experienced hunter, while enemies hesitate as primal instincts warn them that they have become the prey. Even disciplined warriors can find themselves momentarily shaken by the sheer conviction behind the Ranger's voice.

Among the Ranger Lodges, this level of mastery is often called the **Call of the Alpha**, symbolising a hunter whose presence alone begins to influence the outcome of the battle.

### Lore

As Rangers continue to master Predator's Roar, they begin to understand that true dominance is communicated long before weapons are drawn. A confident stance, unwavering composure, and a commanding roar are often enough to make predators reconsider the hunt and force hostile creatures into moments of hesitation.

Veteran Rangers are known for leading hunting parties through little more than their presence. Companions speak of feeling an overwhelming sense of confidence the moment the roar echoes through the wilderness, while enemies often remember it as the sound that marked the beginning of their defeat.

Within the Lodges, apprentices are taught that a roar should never be wasted. Every use is a declaration that the Ranger intends to control the battlefield from that moment forward.

---

> *"The strongest predator does not roar because it is angry. It roars because it already knows who will survive the hunt."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 3
### Description

The Ranger's roar now carries the authority of an apex predator. It resonates across the battlefield with such conviction that even seasoned warriors instinctively hesitate, while allies rally behind the certainty of a proven hunter. The roar no longer serves merely as intimidation—it establishes the Ranger as the dominant force in the encounter.

At this level, the Ranger has learned to channel every hard-earned victory, every dangerous hunt, and every brush with death into a single commanding cry. Those who stand beside the Ranger find renewed courage, while enemies begin to question whether they are the hunter or the hunted.

Among the Ranger Lodges, achieving the third rank is regarded as the point where a Ranger no longer survives the wilderness—they command it.

## Rank 4
### Description

The Ranger's roar has become the unmistakable call of an apex predator. It echoes across the battlefield with overwhelming authority, disrupting enemy morale while rallying companions to fight with renewed determination. Few creatures can ignore the instinctive warning carried within its thunderous cry.

Years of surviving impossible hunts have forged the Ranger into a symbol of confidence and dominance. Every roar reminds allies why they chose to follow, while enemies begin to realise they are no longer fighting a lone warrior—they are facing the leader of the hunt.

Among the Ranger Lodges, reaching this level is considered the mark of a Hunt Leader, someone capable of directing an entire party through presence alone.

## Rank 5
### Description

The Ranger has become the embodiment of an apex predator. Their roar is more than a battle cry—it is an instinctive declaration of absolute dominance that echoes across the battlefield. Allies are inspired to fight with unmatched confidence, while enemies instinctively recognise they have become the hunted.

At this level, Predator's Roar carries the weight of countless victories and years spent surviving against creatures far stronger than oneself. Even disciplined soldiers and legendary beasts hesitate for a fleeting moment as ancient instincts tell them they stand before a hunter who has never abandoned a pursuit.

Within the Ranger Lodges, mastering Predator's Roar is one of the highest honours a hunter can achieve. Those who hear it know the hunt has already begun, and those who stand beside the Ranger know they are following the leader of the pack.

---

### Effects

#### Predator's Roar

**Cost:** 50 SP

Unleash a terrifying roar affecting all creatures of your choice within **20 metres**.

**Enemies**

- Creatures must succeed on a **Wisdom Saving Throw**.
- On a failed save, they become **Shaken** for **1 minute**.
- Affected creatures may repeat the saving throw at the end of each of their turns, ending the effect on a success.
- While Shaken:
  - They have **Disadvantage on all attack rolls**.
  - They cannot take **Reactions**.
  - Their movement speed is reduced by **6 metres**.
  - They have **Disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception)** checks made to detect or locate the Ranger.
  - They have **Disadvantage on saving throws** against the Ranger's Hunting Shots.

**Allies**

Friendly creatures within range gain:

- **+4 Morale Bonus** to attack rolls.
- **+4 Morale Bonus** to Wisdom Saving Throws against **Fear** and **Charm** effects.
- Immunity to the **Shaken** condition caused by your Predator's Roar.
- **Advantage on Initiative rolls** if combat begins before the end of your next turn.
- The first successful attack each ally makes before the end of their next turn deals **+1d4 bonus damage**.

#### Limitations

- Predator's Roar does not affect creatures that cannot hear you.
- A creature may only be affected once each time Predator's Roar is used.
- This is a non-magical ability and is not considered a magical fear effect.
- Creatures immune to magical fear are not automatically immune to Predator's Roar, as it represents instinctive intimidation rather than supernatural influence. The GM has final discretion.

### Lore

Only a handful of Rangers throughout history have mastered the fifth rank of Predator's Roar. Their names are remembered not only for the monsters they slew, but for the unwavering confidence they inspired in every companion who fought beside them. Entire hunting parties have rallied behind a single roar, turning hopeless battles into legendary victories.

The oldest Ranger traditions teach that every forest, mountain, and wilderness has an apex predator whose presence alone commands respect. Those who master Predator's Roar become the mortal embodiment of that principle. Their roar carries no magic, only the certainty born from countless successful hunts. To friend and foe alike, it is the unmistakable sound of the hunt reaching its inevitable conclusion.
