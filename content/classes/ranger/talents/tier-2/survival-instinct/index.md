# Survival Instinct

## Overview
Years spent surviving in the untamed wilderness have sharpened the Ranger's instincts beyond those of ordinary adventurers. Constant exposure to predators, harsh environments, and unexpected dangers has taught them to recognise subtle signs of trouble long before others become aware of them.

Survival Instinct is not a learned technique, but a collection of natural reactions developed through experience. A snapped twig, an unnatural silence among wildlife, or the faint scent of smoke is often enough to place the Ranger on alert. While others rely on preparation, the Ranger trusts instinct.

This heightened awareness allows the Ranger to react more quickly to danger, avoid natural hazards, and survive in environments that would overwhelm less experienced travellers.

---

## Rank 1
### Description

Years spent surviving in the untamed wilderness have sharpened the Ranger's instincts beyond those of ordinary adventurers. Constant exposure to predators, harsh environments, and unexpected dangers has taught them to recognise subtle signs of trouble long before others become aware of them.

Survival Instinct is not a learned technique, but a collection of natural reactions developed through experience. A snapped twig, an unnatural silence among wildlife, or the faint scent of smoke is often enough to place the Ranger on alert. While others rely on preparation, the Ranger trusts instinct.

This heightened awareness allows the Ranger to react more quickly to danger, avoid natural hazards, and survive in environments that would overwhelm less experienced travellers.

---

### Effects

- Gain **+2** to **Survival** skill checks.
- Gain **+2** to checks made to detect **natural hazards**, including quicksand, unstable ground, rockfalls, thin ice, poisonous plants, and similar environmental dangers.
- Gain **+2** to detect **ambushes** or hidden creatures before combat begins.
- Gain **+2** to resist becoming **Lost** while travelling through natural environments.
- You may forage for food and fresh water at **twice the normal rate**, subject to the GM's discretion.
- You require **half the normal time** to establish a safe campsite or shelter in the wilderness.

---

### Lore

Among the Ranger Lodges, Survival Instinct is considered one of the first signs that a hunter has truly embraced the wilderness. It cannot be taught through books or drills alone. Instead, it is forged through countless nights beneath the open sky, surviving storms, avoiding predators, and learning to read the world as though it were a living companion.

Veteran Rangers often claim they cannot explain how they know danger is near—they simply do. Whether it is the sudden silence of birds, the shift of the wind, or an almost imperceptible feeling that something is wrong, these instincts have saved countless lives throughout Asteria.

To the untrained eye, it appears to be luck. To a Ranger, it is simply experience speaking louder than words.

---

> *"The wilderness whispers before it strikes. A Ranger survives because they learned to listen."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 2
### Description

The Ranger's instincts continue to sharpen, becoming second nature after countless journeys through dangerous wilderness. They no longer simply recognise danger—they begin anticipating it before it fully reveals itself. Subtle changes in weather, wildlife behaviour, or the environment immediately draw the Ranger's attention.

Experience has also taught the Ranger to conserve energy and resources while travelling. They instinctively choose safer paths, identify reliable sources of food and water, and establish secure camps with remarkable efficiency. What would take others hours of careful planning becomes routine for a seasoned Ranger.

Among the Ranger Lodges, reaching this level is considered the point where a Ranger can confidently guide an expedition through even the most hostile regions of Asteria.

---

### Effects

- Gain **+4** to **Survival** skill checks.
- Gain **+4** to checks made to detect **natural hazards**, including quicksand, unstable ground, rockfalls, thin ice, poisonous plants, and similar environmental dangers.
- Gain **+4** to detect **ambushes** or hidden creatures before combat begins.
- Gain **+4** to resist becoming **Lost** while travelling through natural environments.
- You may forage for food and fresh water at **three times the normal rate**, subject to the GM's discretion.
- You require **half the normal time** to establish a safe campsite or shelter.
- Gain **+2** to saving throws against **natural diseases and natural poisons**.

---

### Lore

Veteran Rangers often joke that the wilderness itself begins to guide those who survive long enough. While outsiders dismiss this as superstition, experienced hunters know that years spent living among nature cultivate instincts that cannot be learned from books or training grounds.

Many expeditions owe their survival to a Ranger noticing the smallest warning signs—a sudden change in birdsong, fresh claw marks on a tree, or the scent of rain carried by the wind. Such observations often mean the difference between arriving safely and walking into disaster.

Within the Lodges, Rangers of this rank are frequently entrusted with leading scouting parties and long-distance expeditions, for their instincts have proven reliable time and time again.

---

> *"Maps show where the road was. Instinct tells you where it should never go."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 3
### Description

The Ranger's survival instincts have become an inseparable part of who they are. Every sense is constantly evaluating the world around them, allowing them to react to danger with remarkable speed and confidence. Their experience enables them to notice threats long before they become obvious, often avoiding danger altogether rather than simply surviving it.

By this stage, the Ranger has learned to thrive in environments that would overwhelm most travellers. Whether crossing frozen tundras, scorching deserts, dense forests, or towering mountain ranges, they instinctively adapt to changing conditions while guiding both themselves and their companions safely through the wilderness.

Among the Ranger Lodges, this level of mastery earns the title of **Pathfinder**, recognising those trusted to lead expeditions into the most dangerous regions of Asteria.

---

### Effects

- Gain **+6** to **Survival** skill checks.
- Gain **+6** to checks made to detect **natural hazards**, including unstable terrain, traps, rockfalls, thin ice, poisonous plants, and similar environmental dangers.
- Gain **+6** to detect **ambushes** or hidden creatures before combat begins.
- Gain **+6** to resist becoming **Lost** while travelling through natural environments.
- Gain **+4** to saving throws against **natural diseases** and **natural poisons**.
- You may forage for food and fresh water at **four times the normal rate**, subject to the GM's discretion.
- You require only **one-quarter of the normal time** to establish a safe campsite or wilderness shelter.
- You and creatures travelling with you ignore **non-magical difficult terrain** while travelling outside of combat.

---

### Lore

Rangers who achieve the third rank of Survival Instinct become renowned throughout Asteria as guides capable of crossing regions that others consider impassable. Their understanding of the natural world extends far beyond tracking prey—they read the land itself, predicting dangers before they arise and finding safe passage where none appears to exist.

Many kingdoms employ such Rangers to escort caravans, military expeditions, and explorers into the unknown. Their reputation is built not upon the monsters they have slain, but upon the countless lives they have brought home safely.

Within the Ranger Lodges, apprentices are taught that surviving the wilderness is no longer enough. A true Pathfinder ensures that everyone under their guidance survives as well.

---

> *"The wilderness is neither friend nor foe. It simply rewards those wise enough to understand it."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 4
### Description

The Ranger's instincts now border on supernatural, though they remain entirely the product of experience, discipline, and countless years spent surviving where few others could. Every sound, scent, and subtle movement is unconsciously processed, allowing the Ranger to anticipate danger before it fully reveals itself.

No environment feels truly hostile anymore. Frozen tundras, scorching deserts, ancient forests, and monster-infested swamps become familiar territory where the Ranger can move with confidence. Their instincts extend beyond personal survival, allowing them to guide companions safely through the harshest regions of Asteria.

Within the Ranger Lodges, those who attain this rank are known as **Wayfinders**, trusted to lead expeditions into lands that have never before been explored.

---

### Effects

- Gain **+8** to **Survival** skill checks.
- Gain **+8** to checks made to detect **natural hazards**, including unstable terrain, hidden pitfalls, avalanches, poisonous flora, quicksand, and similar environmental dangers.
- Gain **+8** to detect **ambushes**, hidden creatures, and concealed natural threats before combat begins.
- Gain **+8** to checks made to avoid becoming **Lost** in natural environments.
- Gain **+6** to saving throws against **natural diseases**, **natural poisons**, and **environmental hazards**.
- You may forage for food and fresh water at **five times the normal rate**, subject to the GM's discretion.
- You require only **one-quarter of the normal time** to establish a secure campsite or wilderness shelter.
- You and creatures travelling with you ignore **non-magical difficult terrain** while travelling and suffer no movement penalty from natural terrain outside of combat.
- You have **Advantage** on initiative rolls when combat begins as the result of a natural ambush or environmental encounter.

---

### Lore

Only the most accomplished Rangers ever achieve the fourth rank of Survival Instinct. Their reputation spreads far beyond the Ranger Lodges, and kings, merchants, and explorers alike seek their guidance before venturing into dangerous lands. Stories tell of Wayfinders leading entire expeditions across continents without losing a single traveller, avoiding disasters that others never even realised existed.

To these Rangers, survival is no longer about endurance—it is about understanding the wilderness as a living, ever-changing world. They read the wind like a map, recognise danger in the silence of birds, and instinctively choose the safest path long before others realise there was ever a choice.

---

> *"The greatest guide is not the one who knows every path. It is the one who always knows which path not to take."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 5
### Description

The Ranger has become one with the wilderness. Every sense operates in perfect harmony, constantly reading the environment without conscious thought. A subtle shift in the wind, the sudden silence of wildlife, or a barely visible footprint is enough for the Ranger to recognise danger long before it threatens them.

To a master Ranger, survival is no longer a skill—it is instinct. They move through the harshest regions of Asteria as though they were home, adapting effortlessly to changing conditions while guiding others with absolute confidence. Where others see an impassable wilderness, the Ranger sees opportunity, safety, and countless paths forward.

The greatest Rangers are said to possess an almost mythical awareness of their surroundings. Whether these stories are exaggerated or not, one truth remains unchanged: very few who travel beside a master Ranger fail to return home.

---

### Effects

- Gain **+10** to **Survival** skill checks.
- Gain **+10** to checks made to detect **natural hazards**, including unstable terrain, hidden sinkholes, avalanches, poisonous flora, quicksand, thin ice, and similar environmental dangers.
- Gain **+10** to detect **ambushes**, hidden creatures, concealed traps, and environmental threats before combat begins.
- Gain **+10** to checks made to avoid becoming **Lost** in natural environments.
- Gain **+8** to saving throws against **natural diseases**, **natural poisons**, and **environmental hazards**.
- You may forage for food and fresh water at **six times the normal rate**, subject to the GM's discretion.
- You require only **one-tenth of the normal time** to establish a secure campsite or wilderness shelter.
- You and creatures travelling with you ignore **all non-magical difficult terrain** while travelling.
- You have **Advantage** on Initiative rolls whenever combat begins due to an ambush or environmental encounter.
- Once per **Long Rest**, if you would be **Surprised**, you may ignore the **Surprised** condition.

---

### Lore

Only the legendary Huntmasters of Asteria ever reach the fifth rank of Survival Instinct. Their names become synonymous with exploration, endurance, and impossible journeys. They are the guides sought by kings venturing into forgotten ruins, by scholars crossing uncharted continents, and by adventurers chasing legends beyond the edge of the known world.

The oldest Ranger Lodges teach that a master Ranger no longer survives because they are stronger than nature, but because they have learned to become part of it. The wilderness no longer feels hostile in their presence; instead, it seems to reveal its secrets willingly. Every rustling leaf, shifting cloud, and distant animal call becomes another piece of a story only the Ranger can truly understand.

Many believe these instincts are supernatural gifts. Rangers simply smile and continue walking, knowing that every lesson was earned through years of hardship, perseverance, and respect for the wild.

---

> *"The wilderness never stops speaking. The greatest Rangers are simply the ones who never stopped listening."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood, First Pathfinder of the Emerald Lodge**
