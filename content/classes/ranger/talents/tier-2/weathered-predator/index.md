# Weathered Predator

## Overview
The first lesson every Ranger learns is that nature is neither cruel nor kind—it simply exists. Rain will fall, winds will howl, and bitter cold will claim the unprepared. Through countless journeys beneath open skies, the Ranger's body gradually adapts to these everyday hardships, becoming far more resilient than that of an ordinary traveller.

Weathered Predator reflects the beginning of this adaptation. The Ranger no longer dreads travelling through relentless rain or sleeping beneath the stars. Their body naturally resists common weather-related illnesses, allowing them to continue the hunt while others seek warmth and shelter.

Though far from immune to nature's fury, the Ranger has taken the first steps toward becoming one of the wilderness' true survivors.

---

## Rank 1
### Description

The first lesson every Ranger learns is that nature is neither cruel nor kind—it simply exists. Rain will fall, winds will howl, and bitter cold will claim the unprepared. Through countless journeys beneath open skies, the Ranger's body gradually adapts to these everyday hardships, becoming far more resilient than that of an ordinary traveller.

Weathered Predator reflects the beginning of this adaptation. The Ranger no longer dreads travelling through relentless rain or sleeping beneath the stars. Their body naturally resists common weather-related illnesses, allowing them to continue the hunt while others seek warmth and shelter.

Though far from immune to nature's fury, the Ranger has taken the first steps toward becoming one of the wilderness' true survivors.

---

### Effects

- Gain **Advantage** on saving throws against contracting **weather-related illnesses**, including:
  - Common Cold.
  - Influenza (Flu).
  - Similar non-magical illnesses caused by prolonged exposure to rain, damp conditions, or changing weather.
- Reduce penalties caused by **heavy rain** by **50%**, including penalties to travel, perception, or ranged attacks where appropriate (GM discretion).
- Double the time you can comfortably remain in wet weather before making checks against exposure.
- Gain **+2** to Survival checks made during rainfall or storms.

---

### Lore

Among the Ranger Lodges, apprentices are rarely given comfortable lodgings. Instead, they are encouraged to experience the wilderness in all its forms—walking through days of relentless rain, sleeping beneath soaked cloaks, and learning that discomfort is simply another part of the journey.

Veteran Rangers often joke that they stopped noticing the rain years ago. What once felt cold and miserable has become little more than another companion upon the trail. This quiet resilience is the first step towards enduring the far greater trials that await in Asteria's wild places.

---

> *"Rain only bothers those who still believe they'll stay dry."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 2
### Description

The Ranger's body continues to adapt to the harsh realities of the wilderness. Long days spent traversing frozen trails, enduring relentless snowfall, and surviving bitter mountain nights have hardened both body and mind. Conditions that force most travellers to seek shelter become little more than another challenge to overcome.

While snow and freezing temperatures remain dangerous, the Ranger has learned how to conserve body heat, recognise the earliest signs of exposure, and keep moving when others would falter. This resilience allows them to remain effective during hunts, even when winter itself becomes an enemy.

Among the Ranger Lodges, mastering the frozen wilderness is considered the first true test of a hunter's endurance.

---

### Effects

- Gain **Advantage** on saving throws against **Hypothermia**, **Frostbite**, and other non-magical cold-weather conditions.
- Reduce penalties caused by **snowfall** and **freezing temperatures** by **50%**, including penalties to travel, perception, and ranged attacks where appropriate (GM discretion).
- Double the amount of time you can safely remain in freezing environments before making Constitution or Endurance checks for exposure.
- Gain **+2** to **Survival** checks made in snowy or frozen environments.
- Ignore movement penalties caused by **light snow** and **shallow snow** outside of combat.

---

### Lore

Every Ranger remembers their first true winter. Endless snow, freezing winds, and bitter nights teach lessons that cannot be learned anywhere else. Those who endure emerge with a newfound respect for the cold, understanding that preparation and perseverance matter far more than strength alone.

Veteran Rangers often travel through snowstorms with little concern, reading tracks hidden beneath fresh snowfall and navigating blizzards by instinct. To them, winter is no longer an obstacle—it is simply another season in which the hunt continues.

Within the Ranger Lodges, surviving the frozen wilds marks the transition from apprentice to seasoned hunter, proving that the wilderness cannot easily claim those who have learned to respect it.

---

> *"Winter does not care how strong you are. It only asks how long you can endure."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 3
### Description

Having conquered the relentless cold, the Ranger now learns to endure the opposite extreme. Scorching deserts, blazing grasslands, volcanic plains, and relentless summer heat are no longer insurmountable obstacles. Through experience and discipline, the Ranger has learned to regulate their body, conserve precious water, and recognise the warning signs of heat exhaustion long before they become life-threatening.

Every expedition across sun-scorched landscapes teaches another lesson in survival. The Ranger instinctively seeks shade, travels with the rhythm of the environment, and understands that patience is often more valuable than speed. What would cripple an inexperienced traveller becomes another challenge to overcome.

Within the Ranger Lodges, surviving both the frozen north and the burning south is considered proof that a hunter has begun to master every face of the wilderness.

---

### Effects

- Gain **Advantage** on saving throws against **Heat Exhaustion**, **Heatstroke**, **Dehydration**, and other non-magical heat-related conditions.
- Reduce penalties caused by **extreme heat** and **desert environments** by **50%**, including penalties to travel, perception, and ranged attacks where appropriate (GM discretion).
- Double the amount of time you can safely travel in extreme heat before making Constitution or Endurance checks for exposure.
- Consume **50% less water** while travelling through hot or arid environments.
- Gain **+2** to **Survival** checks made in deserts, badlands, volcanic regions, and other hot environments.

---

### Lore

The deserts of Asteria have claimed more lives than many wars. Endless dunes, shimmering heat, and a complete absence of water quickly humble those who underestimate them. Rangers who survive these harsh lands develop an understanding of the sun that few others ever achieve, learning to work with the environment rather than against it.

Among experienced Rangers, it is often said that true endurance is measured not by how long one can march, but by how well one adapts when the world itself becomes the enemy. Those who master the burning wilderness are respected throughout the Lodges, for they have proven that no climate alone can halt the hunt.

---

> *"The desert offers no mercy. It simply rewards those wise enough to respect it."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 4
### Description

By the fourth rank, the Ranger has endured nearly every climate Asteria can offer. They have crossed frozen mountain passes during blizzards, survived weeks beneath relentless desert suns, navigated torrential monsoons, and hunted through violent storms that would force entire caravans to seek shelter.

The Ranger's body and instincts have adapted to nature's most violent expressions. While ordinary travellers struggle against the elements, the Ranger remains focused on the hunt, treating extreme weather as another obstacle to overcome rather than a reason to turn back.

Within the Ranger Lodges, those who reach this level are known as **Stormwalkers**, hunters whose determination is said to rival the fury of nature itself.

---

### Effects

- Gain **Advantage** on saving throws against all **non-magical weather-related conditions**, including:
  - Hypothermia.
  - Frostbite.
  - Heat Exhaustion.
  - Heatstroke.
  - Dehydration.
  - Weather-related illnesses.
- Reduce penalties caused by **blizzards**, **sandstorms**, **torrential rain**, **high winds**, and similar non-magical weather events by **50%**.
- Gain **+4** to **Survival** checks made during severe weather.
- Double the amount of time you can safely travel through extreme weather before making Constitution or Endurance checks for exposure.
- Natural weather no longer imposes penalties on your ranged weapon attacks unless visibility is completely obscured.
- Ignore movement penalties caused by **deep snow**, **loose sand**, and **heavy mud** outside of combat.

---

### Lore

Only the most accomplished Rangers earn the title of Stormwalker. These hunters have faced nature at its most unforgiving and emerged stronger for it. Their stories tell of tracking prey through raging blizzards, crossing deserts during sandstorms, and pursuing monsters through storms that shattered entire forests.

The Ranger Lodges teach that nature is never an enemy to be conquered, but a force to be understood. Those who survive its greatest trials learn to move with the storm instead of against it. To witness a Stormwalker calmly continue the hunt while lightning tears across the sky is to understand why they are respected throughout Asteria.

---

> *"Most flee when the storm arrives. A Ranger knows the storm often hides the perfect opportunity to hunt."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 5
### Description

The Ranger has become a true master of the wilderness. Years of enduring nature's harshest trials have forged a body and mind capable of thriving where others merely survive. Rain no longer soaks their resolve, freezing winds fail to slow their stride, and blistering deserts cannot break their determination. The wilderness has become their home.

Every environment tells a story, and the Ranger has lived them all. They understand when to move, when to rest, and how to adapt before nature's fury can take its toll. Companions often find themselves surviving impossible journeys simply because they chose to follow a Ranger who has mastered the wild.

Among the Ranger Lodges, those who achieve this rank are honoured as **Wardens of the Wild**, individuals whose knowledge and endurance are considered the pinnacle of mortal survival.

---

### Effects

- Gain **Advantage** on all saving throws against **non-magical environmental effects**, including:
  - Weather-related illnesses.
  - Hypothermia.
  - Frostbite.
  - Heat Exhaustion.
  - Heatstroke.
  - Dehydration.
  - Altitude sickness.
  - Fatigue caused by natural climates.
- Ignore **all penalties** caused by **non-magical weather**, including:
  - Heavy rain.
  - Snowfall.
  - Blizzards.
  - Sandstorms.
  - High winds.
  - Extreme heat.
  - Extreme cold.
- Gain **+6** to all **Survival** checks made in natural environments.
- You require only **half the normal food and water** needed to survive while travelling through natural environments.
- Natural weather never imposes penalties on your ranged weapon attacks or vision unless visibility is completely obscured.
- You automatically succeed on checks to establish a safe campsite or shelter in natural environments, provided suitable materials are available.
- You may travel for **twice as long** in non-magical extreme environments before making Constitution or Endurance checks for fatigue or exposure.

---

### Lore

The title **Warden of the Wild** is rarely bestowed, for it represents the highest expression of a Ranger's relationship with nature. These legendary hunters are spoken of in stories as though they belong to the wilderness itself. They cross frozen tundras without complaint, traverse endless deserts with little more than a waterskin, and emerge from storms that leave entire expeditions stranded.

The Ranger Lodges teach that this mastery is not achieved by conquering nature, but by respecting it. Every storm endured, every mountain crossed, and every frozen night survived becomes another lesson carried for life. Those who reach this level no longer fight against the wilderness—they move with it, understanding its rhythms as naturally as their own heartbeat.

To many, such resilience appears supernatural. Rangers simply smile, knowing that every lesson was earned one difficult journey at a time.

---

> *"Nature does not grant favour to the strongest. It rewards those who learn to endure every season beneath its endless sky."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood, Warden of the Emerald Lodge**
