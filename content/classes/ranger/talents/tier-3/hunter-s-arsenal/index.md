# Hunter's Arsenal

## Overview
A seasoned Ranger understands that every hunt eventually becomes unpredictable. A carefully aimed shot may suddenly become a desperate struggle at arm's length, forcing the hunter to adapt without hesitation. Rather than specialising in a single weapon, Rangers train relentlessly with the tools they carry, ensuring they remain deadly whether their quarry is hundreds of metres away or standing directly before them.

Hunter's Arsenal represents the beginning of this mastery. The Ranger develops the confidence and discipline to fight effectively at any distance, refusing to let close-quarters combat become a weakness. Every weapon carried serves a purpose, and every encounter becomes another opportunity to prove that a true hunter is never caught unprepared.

## Rank 1
### Description

A seasoned Ranger understands that every hunt eventually becomes unpredictable. A carefully aimed shot may suddenly become a desperate struggle at arm's length, forcing the hunter to adapt without hesitation. Rather than specialising in a single weapon, Rangers train relentlessly with the tools they carry, ensuring they remain deadly whether their quarry is hundreds of metres away or standing directly before them.

Hunter's Arsenal represents the beginning of this mastery. The Ranger develops the confidence and discipline to fight effectively at any distance, refusing to let close-quarters combat become a weakness. Every weapon carried serves a purpose, and every encounter becomes another opportunity to prove that a true hunter is never caught unprepared.

---

### Effects

- You **no longer suffer Disadvantage** when making **ranged weapon attacks** against creatures within your melee range.
- Gain a **+2 bonus to attack rolls** when wielding the following hunting weapons:
  - Daggers.
  - Hatchets and Hand Axes.
  - Short Swords.
  - Spears.
  - Other hunting weapons approved by the GM.
- While wielding a ranged weapon, creatures making **Opportunity Attacks** against you suffer a **-2 penalty to their attack roll**.

---

### Lore

Within the Ranger Lodges, apprentices quickly discover that the wilderness rarely allows the perfect engagement. A wounded beast may charge unexpectedly, an ambush may force combat into narrow passages, or an enemy may close the distance before another arrow can be loosed. Those who survive learn to trust every weapon at their side.

Veteran Rangers move confidently between melee and ranged combat without hesitation, understanding that versatility is one of a hunter's greatest strengths. To them, victory does not belong to the weapon—it belongs to the one who knows exactly when to use it.

---

> *"The prey decides the distance. The hunter decides how the fight ends."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 2
### Description

The Ranger's weapon mastery continues to evolve until changing between bow and blade becomes effortless. Every movement has been refined through countless hunts, allowing the Ranger to adapt instantly as enemies close the distance or attempt to retreat. Rather than interrupting the flow of combat, each weapon naturally complements the next.

At this stage, the Ranger no longer thinks about switching weapons—it simply happens. A bow is lowered as a blade is drawn, a spear is cast aside as an axe is gripped, and every transition maintains the relentless momentum of the hunt. Regardless of range, the Ranger always has the right weapon ready.

---

### Effects

- You **no longer suffer Disadvantage** when making **ranged weapon attacks** against creatures within your melee range.
- Gain a **+3 bonus to attack rolls** when wielding the following hunting weapons:
  - Daggers.
  - Hatchets and Hand Axes.
  - Short Swords.
  - Spears.
  - Other hunting weapons approved by the GM.
- While wielding a ranged weapon, creatures making **Opportunity Attacks** against you suffer a **-3 penalty to their attack roll**.
- **Once per turn**, you may **draw, sheathe, or swap between a ranged weapon and a hunting weapon** without spending an **Action** or **Action Point**.

---

### Lore

Experienced Rangers know that hesitation is often the difference between a successful hunt and becoming the hunted. Within the Ranger Lodges, apprentices spend years practising weapon transitions until every movement becomes instinctive. The goal is not speed alone, but seamless adaptation to an ever-changing battlefield.

Veteran hunters often appear to carry only a single weapon until the moment combat changes. In one fluid motion, a bow gives way to a blade, or a spear is replaced by an axe, all without breaking stride. To those watching, it seems effortless. To the Ranger, it is simply another lesson learned from the wilderness.

---

> *"The greatest weapon is the one already in your hand when the moment demands it."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 3
### Description

The Ranger has mastered the seamless transition between ranged and melee combat. Every weapon is an extension of the same fighting style, allowing them to respond instinctively as the battlefield shifts around them. Whether striking with a blade before creating distance or firing an arrow at point-blank range, the Ranger wastes neither movement nor opportunity.

Experienced Rangers understand that versatility is their greatest weapon. They no longer think in terms of melee or ranged combat; instead, they simply choose the right tool for the next moment of the hunt. Their fluidity allows them to pressure enemies relentlessly, denying them the chance to exploit distance as a weakness.

---

### Effects

- You **no longer suffer Disadvantage** when making **ranged weapon attacks** against creatures within your melee range.
- Gain a **+4 bonus to attack rolls** when wielding the following hunting weapons:
  - Daggers.
  - Hatchets and Hand Axes.
  - Short Swords.
  - Spears.
  - Other hunting weapons approved by the GM.
- While wielding a ranged weapon, creatures making **Opportunity Attacks** against you suffer a **-4 penalty** to their attack rolls.
- **Once per turn**, you may **draw, sheathe, or swap** between a ranged weapon and a hunting weapon without spending an **Action** or **Action Point**.
- **After making a melee attack with a hunting weapon, you may move up to 2 metres without provoking an Opportunity Attack from the creature you attacked.**

---

### Lore

Only veteran Rangers reach the point where weapon changes become almost impossible to perceive. Their movements are smooth and uninterrupted, flowing naturally from bow to blade and back again as though every weapon were crafted as part of the same whole.

Within the Ranger Lodges, this level of mastery is considered a defining trait of a true hunter. Apprentices often spend years attempting to imitate these fluid transitions, only to realise that such precision comes not from speed alone, but from absolute confidence in every weapon they carry.

---

> *"Distance is an illusion. A true hunter is always fighting where they are strongest."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 4
### Description

The Ranger has become a master of adaptive combat, effortlessly transitioning between ranged and melee engagements without sacrificing speed or precision. Every weapon carried serves a purpose, and every movement is calculated to keep the Ranger one step ahead of their opponent. Whether striking from afar or fighting face-to-face, the Ranger maintains complete control over the flow of battle.

At this level, the distinction between ranged and melee combat begins to disappear. The Ranger instinctively knows when to create distance, when to close the gap, and how to seamlessly combine both fighting styles into a relentless assault that keeps enemies constantly reacting.

---

### Effects

- You **no longer suffer Disadvantage** when making **ranged weapon attacks** against creatures within your melee range.
- Gain a **+5 bonus to attack rolls** when wielding the following hunting weapons:
  - Daggers.
  - Hatchets and Hand Axes.
  - Short Swords.
  - Spears.
  - Other hunting weapons approved by the GM.
- While wielding a ranged weapon, creatures making **Opportunity Attacks** against you suffer a **-5 penalty** to their attack rolls.
- **Once per turn**, you may **draw, sheathe, or swap** between a ranged weapon and a hunting weapon without spending an **Action** or **Action Point**.
- **After hitting a creature with a hunting weapon**, you may immediately make **one ranged weapon attack** against the same target, provided your ranged weapon is loaded or ready. This attack is made as part of the same Action and may occur only **once per turn**.
- **After making the ranged attack**, you may move **up to 3 metres** without provoking an Opportunity Attack from that creature.

---

### Lore

Among the Ranger Lodges, mastery of Hunter's Arsenal is considered the point where a hunter truly becomes unpredictable. Enemies expecting a drawn-out melee suddenly find themselves struck by an arrow at point-blank range, while those attempting to keep their distance are quickly met with flashing steel.

Veteran Rangers often describe combat as a flowing current rather than a series of isolated attacks. Every movement leads naturally into the next, allowing them to dictate the pace of battle while forcing their quarry to constantly adapt. Those fortunate enough to witness this level of mastery often struggle to identify where one attack ended and the next began.

---

> *"The greatest hunters never choose between bow and blade. They let the hunt decide."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 5
### Description

The Ranger has achieved complete mastery over every weapon carried into the hunt. To them, bows, blades, spears, and axes are no longer separate disciplines but parts of a single, fluid fighting style. Every movement is deliberate, every transition seamless, allowing the Ranger to adapt instantly to changing threats without ever losing momentum.

Whether an enemy charges into melee or attempts to flee beyond bowshot, the Ranger is always prepared. Their combat style becomes impossible to predict as they flow effortlessly between close-quarters combat and ranged precision. Few opponents survive long enough to exploit a weakness, for the Ranger no longer has one.

Within the Ranger Lodges, only the greatest Huntmasters are said to possess such mastery. They are regarded as warriors capable of defeating their prey regardless of the battlefield, the terrain, or the distance separating hunter and quarry.

---

### Effects

- You **no longer suffer Disadvantage** when making **ranged weapon attacks** against creatures within your melee range.
- Gain a **+6 bonus to attack rolls** when wielding the following hunting weapons:
  - Daggers.
  - Hatchets and Hand Axes.
  - Short Swords.
  - Spears.
  - Other hunting weapons approved by the GM.
- While wielding a ranged weapon, creatures making **Opportunity Attacks** against you suffer a **-6 penalty** to their attack rolls.
- **Once per turn**, you may **draw, sheathe, or swap** between a ranged weapon and a hunting weapon without spending an **Action** or **Action Point**.
- **Once per turn**, after successfully hitting with a **melee attack**, you may immediately make **one ranged weapon attack** against the same creature, provided your ranged weapon is loaded or otherwise ready.
- **Once per turn**, after successfully hitting with a **ranged weapon attack**, you may immediately make **one melee attack** against the same creature if it is within your melee reach.
- After resolving either combination, you may immediately move **up to 3 metres** without provoking an **Opportunity Attack** from that creature.

---

### Lore

The fifth rank of Hunter's Arsenal is considered the pinnacle of martial versatility within the Ranger Lodges. Those who achieve it no longer think in terms of melee or ranged combat. Every weapon becomes part of the same rhythm, each strike naturally flowing into the next as though the hunt itself guides their movements.

Stories tell of legendary Huntmasters who felled charging beasts with an arrow before finishing them with a blade in the same heartbeat, or who cut down a foe in melee before drawing their bow to strike another across the battlefield. Whether embellished or not, these tales all share one truth: a master Ranger is never limited by the weapon in their hands.

---

> *"The weapon does not make the hunter. The hunter makes every weapon worthy of the hunt."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood, First Huntmaster of the Emerald Lodge**
