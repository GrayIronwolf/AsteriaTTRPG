# Multi Shot

## Overview
Master Rangers learn that one arrow is not always enough. Through exceptional speed, coordination, and years of disciplined training, the Ranger can nock and release multiple arrows in rapid succession, engaging several enemies before they have time to react.

Unlike Hunting Shots, which focus on specialised techniques, Multi Shot is about overwhelming battlefield presence. The Ranger rapidly transitions between targets, placing accurate shots where they are needed most. Every arrow is individually aimed, ensuring precision is never sacrificed for speed.

While exhausting to perform, Multi Shot allows the Ranger to control groups of enemies without losing the deadly accuracy that defines their craft.

---

## Rank 1
### Description

Master Rangers learn that one arrow is not always enough. Through exceptional speed, coordination, and years of disciplined training, the Ranger can nock and release multiple arrows in rapid succession, engaging several enemies before they have time to react.

Unlike Hunting Shots, which focus on specialised techniques, Multi Shot is about overwhelming battlefield presence. The Ranger rapidly transitions between targets, placing accurate shots where they are needed most. Every arrow is individually aimed, ensuring precision is never sacrificed for speed.

While exhausting to perform, Multi Shot allows the Ranger to control groups of enemies without losing the deadly accuracy that defines their craft.

---

### Effects

#### Multi Shot

**Cost:** 30 SP

As an Action, make **two separate ranged weapon attacks**.

- Each attack may target the **same creature** or **two different creatures** within your weapon's range.
- Roll each attack and damage separately.
- Each attack consumes **one piece of ammunition**.
- Hunting Shots and Trick Shots may only be applied to **one** of the attacks unless another ability states otherwise.

#### Limitations

- Multi Shot may only be used with **bows, crossbows, firearms, or other ranged weapons** capable of making individual attacks.
- You cannot combine Multi Shot with another ability that grants additional attacks unless specifically stated.
- You must have sufficient ammunition for every attack made.

---

### Lore

The first Rangers to master Multi Shot were said to have defended entire mountain passes against overwhelming odds, loosing arrows so quickly that witnesses believed several hunters were fighting side by side. Though these stories have undoubtedly grown with time, every Ranger Lodge teaches that speed without discipline is meaningless.

Apprentices spend years strengthening their draw, refining their posture, and practising seamless transitions between targets before they are entrusted with this technique. A true master never fires wildly. Every arrow is released with purpose, whether protecting companions or bringing multiple foes low before they can close the distance.

---

> *"One arrow wins a duel. Many well-placed arrows win the battle."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 2
### Description

The Ranger's speed and coordination continue to improve, allowing them to engage multiple threats with astonishing efficiency. Years of training have taught the Ranger how to transition seamlessly between targets, loosing arrows with fluid precision while maintaining complete control over every shot.

Rather than relying on reckless speed, the Ranger carefully selects each target before releasing a rapid volley. Every arrow is deliberately placed, allowing the Ranger to protect allies, suppress advancing enemies, or concentrate fire where it is needed most.

Among the Ranger Lodges, mastery of the second rank marks the point where a Ranger is no longer considered a skilled archer, but a battlefield marksman capable of controlling the flow of combat through overwhelming precision.

### Lore

Veteran Rangers often describe Multi Shot as a dance rather than a technique. Every movement flows naturally into the next, allowing the Ranger to draw, nock, and release arrows with such efficiency that onlookers struggle to follow the motion. To the untrained eye, it appears as though several archers are firing simultaneously.

Within the Ranger Lodges, apprentices spend years developing the strength, stamina, and coordination required to master this technique. Instructors teach that speed alone is meaningless—true mastery comes from ensuring every arrow still finds its intended mark.

---

> *"The greatest Rangers are never hurried. They are simply faster than everyone else can comprehend."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 3
### Description

The Ranger has mastered the art of engaging entire groups of enemies without sacrificing precision. Through countless hours of practice, drawing, nocking, and releasing arrows becomes a seamless motion, allowing them to strike multiple targets before their opponents can react.

At this level, the Ranger's awareness of the battlefield is exceptional. They instinctively prioritise dangerous opponents, exploit openings, and shift their focus between enemies with effortless speed. Every arrow is released with purpose, ensuring that no shot is wasted, whether protecting allies or thinning advancing ranks.

Among the Ranger Lodges, Rangers capable of this level of marksmanship are often assigned to defend settlements and lead hunting parties, where controlling multiple threats can mean the difference between victory and defeat.

## Rank 4
### Description

The Ranger's speed has reached a level few can comprehend. Every movement flows into the next with flawless precision, allowing them to loose a devastating volley that can dominate an entire battlefield. Rather than focusing on individual duels, the Ranger effortlessly shifts between multiple threats, delivering accurate fire wherever it is needed most.

At this stage, Multi Shot is no longer simply a demonstration of exceptional archery—it becomes battlefield control through overwhelming precision. The Ranger can suppress advancing enemies, eliminate vulnerable targets, or focus an incredible number of attacks against a single powerful foe.

Only the most accomplished Rangers within the Lodges are entrusted with teaching this technique. It represents years of physical conditioning, unwavering discipline, and countless hours spent perfecting every draw and release until they become instinct.

## Rank 5
### Description

The Ranger has reached the pinnacle of ranged combat, capable of unleashing an extraordinary barrage of precision attacks in the blink of an eye. Years of relentless practice have refined every movement until drawing, nocking, and releasing become one seamless action. To onlookers, the volley appears less like individual shots and more like a relentless storm of perfectly aimed projectiles.

Unlike ordinary archers who rely on speed alone, a master of Multi Shot sacrifices none of their accuracy. Every arrow is deliberately placed, allowing the Ranger to engage entire groups of enemies, overwhelm powerful foes with concentrated fire, or adapt instantly to the changing flow of battle.

Only the greatest Huntmasters of Asteria are capable of performing this technique. Tales tell of legendary Rangers defending entire passes alone, their arrows falling so rapidly and accurately that enemies believed they were facing an entire company of archers rather than a single hunter.

### Lore

The fifth rank of Multi Shot represents one of the greatest achievements a Ranger can attain. Within the Lodges, only a select few ever master the technique, earning recognition as living legends whose skill borders on the impossible. Their arrows fly with such speed and precision that even experienced warriors struggle to follow their movements.

Stories of these Rangers have spread throughout Asteria for centuries. Some speak of Huntmasters holding narrow mountain passes against entire warbands, while others tell of impossible hunts where six beasts fell before the first had even reached the ground. Whether these tales have grown with time matters little—the legends endure because they are rooted in the extraordinary discipline and mastery of those who dedicated their lives to perfecting the bow.

---

> *"Six arrows leave the string, yet each finds its own destiny. That is not speed—it is mastery."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood, First Huntmaster of the Emerald Lodge**
