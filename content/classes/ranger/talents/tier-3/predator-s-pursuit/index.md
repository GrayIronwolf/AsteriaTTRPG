# Predator's Pursuit

## Overview
Predator's Pursuit is the moment a Ranger's hunt becomes something more than skill alone. By calling upon ancient primal magic, the Ranger forms a mystical bond with a single quarry, allowing the wilderness itself to reveal subtle traces of the creature's passage. What once required careful observation now seems to appear naturally before the hunter's eyes.

The marked quarry becomes easier to follow wherever it travels. Faint spectral signs illuminate its trail, while the Ranger instinctively senses the direction of the hunt. Although the quarry may flee, hide, or attempt to erase its tracks, the wilderness quietly guides the Ranger towards its inevitable destination.

---

## Rank 1
### Description

Predator's Pursuit is the moment a Ranger's hunt becomes something more than skill alone. By calling upon ancient primal magic, the Ranger forms a mystical bond with a single quarry, allowing the wilderness itself to reveal subtle traces of the creature's passage. What once required careful observation now seems to appear naturally before the hunter's eyes.

The marked quarry becomes easier to follow wherever it travels. Faint spectral signs illuminate its trail, while the Ranger instinctively senses the direction of the hunt. Although the quarry may flee, hide, or attempt to erase its tracks, the wilderness quietly guides the Ranger towards its inevitable destination.

---

### Effects

- **Cost:** 50 MP.
- Choose one creature you can see to become your **Quarry**.
- The mark lasts for **1 hour**, or until:
  - The Quarry dies.
  - The Quarry becomes unconscious.
  - You willingly end the effect.
  - You mark another creature.

#### Hunter's Mark Synergy

- If a creature is already affected by your **Hunter's Mark**, it automatically becomes your **Quarry** when Predator's Pursuit is activated.
- If **Hunter's Mark** is transferred to another creature, **Predator's Pursuit** immediately transfers to the same creature without requiring another Mana Cost or action.
- If no creature is affected by Hunter's Mark, choose a Quarry normally.

#### While your Quarry is marked

- The Quarry is outlined by a **faint spectral glow** visible only to you while it remains within your line of sight.
- Footprints, blood trails, broken vegetation, disturbed earth, and other signs left by the Quarry emit a **soft magical glow** visible only to you.
- You always know the **general direction** of your Quarry while it remains within **1 kilometre** and on the same plane of existence.
- Gain **Advantage** on **Survival** checks made to track your Quarry.
- While **Hidden** or benefiting from **Cover**, gain a **+2 bonus to attack rolls** against your marked Quarry.
- Your marked Quarry gains **no benefit from Half Cover** against your ranged attacks.
- Non-magical attempts to conceal or erase the Quarry's trail have **Disadvantage** against your tracking attempts.

#### Limitations

- The spectral outline does not reveal the Quarry through solid objects or magical barriers.
- The bond reveals only the Quarry's **general direction**, not its exact location or distance.
- Magical concealment, teleportation, or planar travel may suppress the connection, at the GM's discretion.
- Only one creature may be your Quarry at a time.

### Lore

The oldest Ranger Lodges teach that every hunt leaves an echo within the wilderness. Those who prove themselves worthy may one day learn to see these echoes, following trails that ordinary eyes could never perceive. To outsiders, Predator's Pursuit appears to be remarkable tracking. Rangers know the truth—it is the wilderness choosing to guide the hunter.

Legends tell of Huntmasters pursuing monsters across entire kingdoms, guided only by glowing footprints and an unwavering instinct that never led them astray. Once the wilderness accepts a Ranger's quarry, few creatures escape the hunt for long.

---

> *"The quarry may hide from my eyes, but it cannot hide from the wilderness."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood, First Huntmaster of the Emerald Lodge**

## Rank 2
### Description

The Ranger's bond with their quarry grows stronger, allowing the primal magic of the wilderness to reveal far more than simple tracks. Every footprint, broken branch, and drop of blood shines brighter, while even older trails become easier to follow. The hunt begins to feel less like a search and more like an invisible thread connecting hunter and prey.

Experienced Rangers speak of reaching a point where the wilderness itself refuses to let their quarry disappear. Whether travelling through dense forests, rocky mountains, or open plains, subtle signs continually guide the Ranger forward. The prey may attempt to hide its trail, but nature remembers every step it has taken.

Those who master the second rank of Predator's Pursuit begin to understand that the wilderness does more than reveal tracks—it remembers them. Every trail left by the quarry becomes another page in a story only the Ranger can read, guiding them with unwavering certainty through forests, mountains, and forgotten ruins alike.

Within the Ranger Lodges, hunters capable of maintaining this bond are rarely assigned ordinary prey. They are entrusted with tracking dangerous monsters, escaped criminals, and creatures that others have long since declared impossible to find.

## Rank 3
### Description

The bond between the Ranger and their Quarry has become deeply intertwined with the primal magic of the wilderness. The marked creature can no longer easily disappear, as nature itself continuously reveals subtle signs of its passage. Even when tracks are scarce, the Ranger's instincts are guided by an unseen force that steadily draws them closer to their prey.

Veteran Rangers describe this connection as becoming one with the hunt. Every footprint shines with spectral light, every broken branch tells a story, and even the faintest disturbance in the environment becomes impossible to overlook. Once marked, the Quarry soon learns that escape is far more difficult than survival.

## Rank 4
### Description

The Ranger's connection to the wilderness has become extraordinarily powerful. Once a Quarry has been chosen, the natural world actively reveals its movements, making escape almost impossible. The bond transcends simple tracking, allowing the Ranger to instinctively understand the state of their prey even across vast distances.

Every step the Quarry takes leaves behind an echo only the Ranger can perceive. Ancient trees, flowing rivers, shifting winds, and the creatures of the wild all seem to quietly guide the hunter toward their destination. Though the Quarry may flee through forests, mountains, or deserts, the wilderness refuses to let it disappear completely.

Among the Ranger Lodges, those who master this rank are known as **Wildseekers**, hunters entrusted with tracking the most dangerous beasts and elusive foes across entire regions of Asteria.

The fourth rank of Predator's Pursuit is regarded as one of the greatest honours within the Ranger Lodges. Wildseekers are often dispatched on missions that span entire kingdoms, pursuing monsters, warlords, and fugitives who have escaped every other hunter. Their success comes not from unmatched speed, but from an unwavering connection with the wilderness itself.

Legends tell of Rangers who pursued Dragons across mountain ranges and tracked ancient horrors through forgotten forests without ever losing the trail. To them, the wilderness is no longer simply a place to survive—it has become a trusted companion in the hunt.

## Rank 5
### Description

The Ranger has become the ultimate hunter, forming a bond with their Quarry so profound that the wilderness itself refuses to let the creature vanish. Mountains, forests, deserts, rivers, and plains all become silent allies, revealing fragments of the Quarry's journey through subtle whispers only the Ranger can perceive.

No longer limited to following physical tracks, the Ranger senses the hunt itself. Every movement of the Quarry leaves an echo within the natural world, guiding the Ranger with unwavering certainty. Escape is still possible, but only through extraordinary means. Once marked, the Quarry carries the attention of both the Ranger and the wilderness until the hunt reaches its conclusion.

Among the Ranger Lodges, this mastery is known as **The Eternal Hunt**, a title reserved for those whose prey is said to have nowhere left to hide.

### Lore

Only a handful of Rangers throughout history have ever mastered Predator's Pursuit to this extent. Their names are remembered in legend, not because their prey was weak, but because nothing they pursued ever truly escaped. Kings sought their aid to track assassins, guilds relied upon them to hunt monsters, and entire armies respected the terrifying certainty with which these Rangers followed their chosen Quarry.

The Ranger Lodges teach that this technique is not about domination, but harmony. By earning the trust of the wilderness over a lifetime of respect and perseverance, the Ranger becomes part of nature's endless cycle. Every trail, every breeze, and every whispering tree serves the hunt until hunter and Quarry are finally brought together once more.

---

> *"The hunt does not end when my Quarry disappears. It ends when the wilderness decides our paths shall cross again."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood, First Huntmaster of the Emerald Lodge**
