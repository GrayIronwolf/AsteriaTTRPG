# Whirlwind Evasion

## Overview
A Ranger's greatest defence is not armour, but movement. Through years of surviving against faster, larger, and deadlier predators, Rangers learn to react instinctively the moment danger presents itself. A single step, pivot, or roll is often enough to turn a fatal strike into a harmless miss.

Whirlwind Evasion channels this philosophy into a disciplined combat technique. Rather than standing firm, the Ranger moves like the wind itself, flowing around attacks with graceful precision before slipping into a more advantageous position. Every movement is deliberate, preserving momentum while creating opportunities to continue the hunt.

---

## Rank 1
### Description

A Ranger's greatest defence is not armour, but movement. Through years of surviving against faster, larger, and deadlier predators, Rangers learn to react instinctively the moment danger presents itself. A single step, pivot, or roll is often enough to turn a fatal strike into a harmless miss.

Whirlwind Evasion channels this philosophy into a disciplined combat technique. Rather than standing firm, the Ranger moves like the wind itself, flowing around attacks with graceful precision before slipping into a more advantageous position. Every movement is deliberate, preserving momentum while creating opportunities to continue the hunt.

---

### Effects

- **Cost:** 40 SP.
- Activate this ability as a **Reaction** when you are targeted by an attack.
- Until the **start of your next turn**, gain the following benefits:
  - **+2 AC.**
  - **+2** to **Dexterity Saving Throws**.
  - Immediately move **up to 3 metres** without spending Movement.
  - This movement must end on solid ground.
  - The creature whose attack triggered this ability **cannot make an Opportunity Attack** against you during this movement.
- This movement does not interrupt your current turn or prevent you from taking actions on your next turn.

#### Limitations

- May only be activated **once per round**.
- Cannot move through creatures or solid objects.
- Does not negate attacks that have already hit before this ability is activated.

---

### Lore

Among the Ranger Lodges, students are taught to study the movements of nature's greatest hunters. Wolves circle before striking, hawks ride unseen currents of air, and great cats avoid danger through perfect timing rather than brute strength. Rangers learn that survival belongs to those who keep moving.

Veteran Rangers appear almost impossible to corner in battle. Enemies often believe they have trapped their prey, only to find the Ranger already standing elsewhere before the attack can land. To them, every dodge is simply another step in the hunt.

---

> *"Stand still and become the target. Move with the wind, and become the hunter once more."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 2
### Description

The Ranger's movements become increasingly fluid, allowing them to weave through combat with remarkable precision. Every attack is met with an instinctive sidestep, every swing anticipated moments before it lands. What once required conscious effort has become second nature, allowing the Ranger to maintain complete awareness while repositioning with incredible speed.

At this level, Whirlwind Evasion is more than a defensive technique—it is a seamless transition between defence and opportunity. The Ranger avoids danger without losing momentum, always seeking the next advantageous position from which to continue the hunt.

### Lore

Experienced Rangers understand that survival is measured not by the number of attacks endured, but by the number successfully avoided. Every hunt, every duel, and every close encounter refines their instincts until movement becomes almost effortless. Opponents often find themselves striking only empty air as the Ranger has already slipped beyond reach.

Within the Ranger Lodges, mastering the second rank of Whirlwind Evasion is considered proof that a hunter has begun to move with the rhythm of the battlefield itself. Like the wind through the trees, their path is difficult to predict and even harder to stop.

---

> *"The quickest step is not the one taken first—it is the one your enemy never sees."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 3
### Description

The Ranger has mastered the ability to move with complete harmony amidst the chaos of battle. Every attack is met with fluid, instinctive motion, allowing the Ranger to evade danger while maintaining perfect control over their footing and surroundings. Rather than reacting after an attack begins, the Ranger seems to anticipate it moments before it is unleashed.

As the technique matures, terrain itself becomes less of an obstacle. Dense undergrowth, loose rocks, fallen trees, and uneven ground fail to interrupt the Ranger's movement, allowing them to reposition with graceful precision even in the harshest environments.

---

### Lore

To seasoned Rangers, movement is as important as marksmanship. Every root, stone, and branch becomes part of the battlefield rather than an obstacle upon it. Those who master the third rank of Whirlwind Evasion move with such effortless precision that even experienced warriors struggle to predict where they will be from one heartbeat to the next.

Within the Ranger Lodges, this technique is often demonstrated in dense forests, where apprentices must weave between trees and natural hazards while avoiding attacks from multiple instructors. Only those who learn to trust both their instincts and the wilderness earn the right to progress further.

---

> *"The forest never asks permission before it moves. Neither should a Ranger."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 4
### Description

The Ranger has become almost untouchable in combat, weaving through the battlefield with speed and precision that few can match. Every dodge flows seamlessly into the next, allowing the Ranger to reposition before opponents can adapt. Their movements resemble the wind itself—swift, unpredictable, and impossible to restrain.

At this level, Whirlwind Evasion is no longer simply a defensive manoeuvre. The Ranger has learned to exploit even the smallest opening, slipping between enemies and escaping situations that would trap almost any other combatant. To those watching from afar, it appears as though the Ranger simply vanishes before reappearing elsewhere on the battlefield.

---

### Effects

- **Cost:** 70 SP.
- Activate this ability as a **Reaction** when you are targeted by an attack.
- Until the **start of your next turn**, gain the following benefits:
  - **+5 AC.**
  - **+5** to **Dexterity Saving Throws**.
  - Immediately move **up to 6 metres** without spending Movement.
  - Ignore **non-magical difficult terrain** during this movement.
  - You may move through the spaces of **Medium or smaller creatures**, treating them as difficult terrain. You must end your movement in an unoccupied space.
  - The creature whose attack triggered this ability **cannot make an Opportunity Attack** against you during this movement.
- This movement does not interrupt your current turn or prevent you from taking actions on your next turn.

#### Limitations

- May only be activated **once per round**.
- Cannot move through solid objects or impassable terrain.
- Does not negate attacks that have already hit before this ability is activated.

---

### Lore

Only veteran Rangers achieve the fourth rank of Whirlwind Evasion. Their movements have become so refined that even experienced duelists struggle to follow them. Every dodge is calculated, every step deliberate, allowing the Ranger to escape seemingly impossible situations without sacrificing control of the battlefield.

Within the Ranger Lodges, masters often describe this technique as "becoming the wind." Like a powerful gust slipping effortlessly through the trees, the Ranger cannot be pinned down for long. Their enemies soon discover that every failed strike leaves them more vulnerable than the Ranger they sought to corner.

---

> *"You cannot trap the wind. You can only wonder where it will strike next."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 5
### Description

The Ranger has perfected Whirlwind Evasion, becoming the embodiment of speed, agility, and instinct. Their movements are so fluid that opponents often struggle to comprehend where the Ranger has gone, striking only the space they once occupied. Every dodge flows naturally into another, allowing the Ranger to evade danger while positioning themselves for the next decisive attack.

To the greatest Huntmasters, movement is no longer a reaction—it is prediction. Every shift in an opponent's stance, every tightening of a grip, and every glance betrays an incoming attack before it is unleashed. Guided by instinct and experience, the Ranger slips effortlessly through the battlefield, impossible to corner and incredibly difficult to strike.

---

### Effects

- **Cost:** 80 SP.
- Activate this ability as a **Reaction** when you are targeted by an attack.
- Until the **start of your next turn**, gain the following benefits:
  - **+6 AC.**
  - **+6** to **Dexterity Saving Throws**.
  - Immediately move **up to 9 metres** without spending Movement.
  - Ignore **all non-magical difficult terrain** during this movement.
  - You may move through the spaces of creatures **Large or smaller**, treating them as difficult terrain. You must end your movement in an unoccupied space.
  - The creature whose attack triggered this ability **cannot make an Opportunity Attack** against you during this movement.
- **If the triggering attack misses**, you may immediately make **one ranged weapon attack** against that creature as part of the same Reaction.
- This movement does not interrupt your current turn or prevent you from taking actions on your next turn.

#### Limitations

- May only be activated **once per round**.
- Cannot move through solid objects or impassable terrain.
- The bonus attack may only target the creature whose attack triggered Whirlwind Evasion.
- The bonus attack cannot benefit from **Multi Shot** or similar abilities that grant additional attacks.

---

### Lore

Only the legendary Huntmasters of Asteria ever achieve the fifth rank of Whirlwind Evasion. Their movements have become so refined that stories often exaggerate them into myth, claiming they vanish into the wind itself before reappearing with bow already drawn. While the truth is less magical, their mastery of timing and movement is no less extraordinary.

Within the Ranger Lodges, this technique is regarded as the ultimate expression of survival. The greatest hunters know that victory is not earned by enduring every blow, but by ensuring the enemy never lands one. Those who witness a master Ranger employ Whirlwind Evasion often realise too late that the hunt has already turned against them.

---

> *"The deadliest Ranger is not the one who stands their ground. It is the one you can never catch."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood, First Huntmaster of the Emerald Lodge**
