# Phantom Ranger

## Overview
Among the oldest Ranger Lodges, there are whispered tales of hunters who could vanish in the middle of battle without casting a spell of invisibility. These legendary warriors learned to briefly step into the Spirit of the Wild, allowing the physical world to pass around them before emerging from an entirely unexpected position.

Phantom Ranger is the beginning of that mastery. By channelling primal magic, the Ranger momentarily shifts into a spectral state, slipping free from close-quarters combat before melting back into the surrounding wilderness. This brief transition creates the perfect opportunity to disappear from an enemy's sight and continue the hunt from the shadows.

---

## Rank 1
### Description

Among the oldest Ranger Lodges, there are whispered tales of hunters who could vanish in the middle of battle without casting a spell of invisibility. These legendary warriors learned to briefly step into the Spirit of the Wild, allowing the physical world to pass around them before emerging from an entirely unexpected position.

Phantom Ranger is the beginning of that mastery. By channelling primal magic, the Ranger momentarily shifts into a spectral state, slipping free from close-quarters combat before melting back into the surrounding wilderness. This brief transition creates the perfect opportunity to disappear from an enemy's sight and continue the hunt from the shadows.

---

### Effects

- **Cost:** 80 MP.
- **Duration:** 1 Minute.
- **Uses:** 2 uses per **Short Rest**.

While Phantom Ranger is active:

- **Once per turn**, after leaving a creature's melee reach, you may activate **Phantom Step**.
- During **Phantom Step**:
  - Move up to **6 metres**.
  - **Non-magical melee attacks**, including **Opportunity Attacks**, pass harmlessly through your spectral form.
  - **Magical melee attacks, spells, magical ranged attacks, and magical area effects** affect you normally.
  - You may move through creatures, but **not** through walls, solid objects, terrain, or magical barriers.
- At the end of the movement, you may immediately attempt a **Stealth** check to **Hide**, even while combat is ongoing.
- If successful, you become **Hidden** and gain the normal benefits of attacking from Hidden.
- If you know the **Camouflage** talent, you may immediately enter **Camouflage** after successfully becoming Hidden, ignoring Camouflage's normal requirement of being out of combat.

#### Limitations

- You are **not invisible** while using Phantom Step.
- Phantom Step must begin by **leaving a creature's melee reach**.
- You must end the movement in an **unoccupied space**.
- Creatures with magical detection or similar abilities can still detect you normally.

---

### Lore

Only a handful of Rangers ever awaken the gift of Phantom Ranger. Those who do speak of hearing the wilderness itself calling them between heartbeats, allowing them to step beyond the reach of ordinary steel before returning to continue the hunt. To their enemies, it appears as though the Ranger simply dissolves into mist before reappearing elsewhere on the battlefield.

Within the Ranger Lodges, Phantom Rangers are considered living legends. They do not rely on invisibility or illusion, but on a fleeting harmony with the primal spirits of the wild. Every disappearance is deliberate, every reappearance perfectly timed, reminding their quarry that the most dangerous hunter is the one who is never where you expect them to be.

---

> *"The prey watches where I stood. The wise hunter has already moved."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood, First Phantom Ranger**

## Rank 2
### Description

The Ranger's connection to the Spirit of the Wild grows stronger, allowing them to remain between the physical and spiritual worlds for longer periods. Each Phantom Step becomes more fluid, enabling the Ranger to disappear from one engagement before silently emerging somewhere entirely unexpected. Enemies often struggle to determine whether the Ranger truly vanished or simply moved faster than the eye could follow.

Experienced Phantom Rangers no longer rely solely on the terrain to conceal them. Their spectral movements disrupt an opponent's focus, creating brief moments of uncertainty that can be exploited to regain the advantage. Every successful disappearance allows the Ranger to dictate the flow of battle rather than react to it.

### Lore

To witness a Rank II Phantom Ranger is to question whether they ever occupied the space at all. Hunters trained in this ancient technique weave between the physical and spiritual worlds with remarkable grace, leaving behind only fleeting echoes of their presence. Their movements are so seamless that many foes instinctively strike at where the Ranger once stood, only to find empty air.

Within the Ranger Lodges, these hunters are often entrusted with missions requiring infiltration, reconnaissance, and the elimination of dangerous targets deep behind enemy lines. Their greatest weapon is not their bow or blade, but the uncertainty they leave in the minds of those who seek them.

---

> *"A phantom is not unseen because it is invisible. It is unseen because you looked in the wrong place."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 3
### Description

The Ranger's mastery of Phantom Ranger has deepened, allowing them to walk the boundary between the physical world and the Spirit of the Wild with remarkable ease. Each Phantom Step becomes longer, quieter, and more difficult to follow, leaving enemies striking at fading afterimages while the true hunter has already repositioned.

Veteran Phantom Rangers no longer rely solely on the environment to disappear. Their movements are so fluid that they seem to dissolve into the battlefield itself, using every shadow, tree, and obstacle to remain unseen. To their quarry, the Ranger becomes an ever-present threat that appears only long enough to strike before vanishing once again.

---

### Lore

To master the third rank of Phantom Ranger is to become a living ghost upon the battlefield. The wilderness no longer simply conceals the Ranger—it seems to embrace them, allowing them to drift through the chaos of combat with uncanny precision before emerging where no enemy expected.

Within the Ranger Lodges, these hunters are entrusted with the most dangerous assignments, from infiltrating enemy strongholds to pursuing legendary beasts through dense forests and forgotten ruins. Those who survive an encounter with a Phantom Ranger often struggle to describe what happened, remembering only fleeting glimpses of a spectral figure that seemed to vanish with every heartbeat.

---

> *"The wisest prey never fears the arrow. It fears the silence before it is loosed."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 4
### Description

The Ranger has become a true spectre of the battlefield, able to disappear and reappear with unnerving precision. Their mastery of Phantom Step allows them to break an opponent's focus, reposition through the chaos of combat, and strike before their quarry can recover. To those who face them, every shadow becomes a potential threat.

At this stage, Phantom Ranger is no longer simply an escape technique—it becomes an offensive tool. The Ranger uses every disappearance to create the perfect angle of attack, re-emerging from concealment with devastating precision before once again melting back into the wilderness.

---

### Effects

- **Cost:** 110 MP.
- **Duration:** 4 Minutes.
- **Uses:** 2 uses per **Short Rest**.

While Phantom Ranger is active:

- **Once per turn**, after leaving a creature's melee reach, you may activate **Phantom Step**.
- During **Phantom Step**:
  - Move up to **15 metres**.
  - **Non-magical melee attacks**, including **Opportunity Attacks**, pass harmlessly through your spectral form.
  - **Magical melee attacks, spells, magical ranged attacks, and magical area effects** affect you normally.
  - You may move through creatures of **Large size or smaller**, but not through walls, solid objects, terrain, or magical barriers.
- At the end of the movement, immediately attempt a **Stealth** check to **Hide**, even while combat is ongoing.
- Gain a **+6 bonus** to the Stealth check made as part of Phantom Step.
- If successful, you become **Hidden** and gain the normal benefits of attacking from Hidden.
- **The first attack you make after successfully becoming Hidden through Phantom Step gains a +2 bonus to its attack roll.**
- If you possess the **Camouflage** talent, you may immediately enter **Camouflage** after successfully becoming Hidden, ignoring its normal requirement of being out of combat.

#### Limitations

- You are **not invisible** while using Phantom Step.
- Phantom Step must begin by **leaving a creature's melee reach**.
- You must end the movement in an **unoccupied space**.
- Creatures with magical detection or similar abilities can still detect you normally.

---

### Lore

Only the most accomplished Phantom Rangers ever reach this level of mastery. Their movements become so fluid that enemies often question whether they are pursuing a single hunter or several spectral figures moving through the battlefield. Every disappearance creates uncertainty, and every reappearance reminds their quarry that nowhere is truly safe.

Within the Ranger Lodges, these hunters are entrusted with eliminating the most elusive prey in Asteria. They are taught that the greatest strike is not the strongest, but the one their enemy never saw coming. By mastering both concealment and timing, they transform every battlefield into the perfect hunting ground.

---

> *"A phantom never rushes the hunt. It simply appears where victory is already waiting."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 5
### Description

The Ranger has achieved complete mastery of Phantom Ranger, becoming a living myth among the Ranger Lodges. During battle they appear to exist between the physical world and the Spirit of the Wild, effortlessly slipping through enemy formations before vanishing into the landscape. Every movement is deliberate, every disappearance calculated, and every reappearance marks another step toward the inevitable end of the hunt.

To the Ranger's quarry, combat becomes a nightmare. The hunter never remains in one place long enough to be cornered, striking from impossible angles before fading back into the wilderness. Those who survive rarely agree on what they witnessed—only that the Ranger was never where they expected them to be.

---

### Effects

- **Cost:** 120 MP.
- **Duration:** 5 Minutes.
- **Uses:** 2 uses per **Short Rest**.

While Phantom Ranger is active:

- **Once per turn**, after leaving a creature's melee reach, you may activate **Phantom Step**.
- During **Phantom Step**:
  - Move up to **18 metres**.
  - **Non-magical melee attacks**, including **Opportunity Attacks**, pass harmlessly through your spectral form.
  - **Magical melee attacks, spells, magical ranged attacks, and magical area effects** affect you normally.
  - You may move through creatures of **Huge size or smaller**, but not through walls, solid objects, terrain, or magical barriers.
- At the end of the movement, immediately attempt a **Stealth** check to **Hide**, even while combat is ongoing.
- Gain a **+8 bonus** to the Stealth check made as part of Phantom Step.
- If successful, you become **Hidden** and gain the normal benefits of attacking from Hidden.
- **The first attack you make after successfully becoming Hidden through Phantom Step gains a +4 bonus to its attack roll.**
- If you possess the **Camouflage** talent, you may immediately enter **Camouflage** after successfully becoming Hidden, ignoring its normal requirement of being out of combat.
- If your first attack from Hidden **hits**, you may immediately activate **Phantom Step** again **without consuming your once-per-turn Phantom Step**, provided you still have movement available. This additional Phantom Step cannot trigger another free Phantom Step.

#### Limitations

- You are **not invisible** while using Phantom Step.
- Phantom Step must begin by **leaving a creature's melee reach**.
- You must end each Phantom Step in an **unoccupied space**.
- Creatures with magical detection or similar abilities can still detect you normally.
- The additional Phantom Step may only occur **once per turn**.

---

### Lore

Only a handful of Rangers throughout Asteria's history have ever reached the fifth rank of Phantom Ranger. They are remembered not merely as exceptional hunters, but as legends who seemed to become one with the wilderness itself. Their prey often believed they had escaped, only to discover the Phantom Ranger silently waiting farther along the trail.

Within the Ranger Lodges, these masters are known as the **Ghosts of the Wild**. They embody the Ranger's highest ideal—not overwhelming strength or unmatched speed, but absolute control of the hunt. Every disappearance creates uncertainty, every reappearance creates opportunity, and every strike reminds their quarry that there is no refuge from a hunter who has become part of the wilderness.

---

> *"You cannot pursue a ghost. By the time you find where it stood, it is already deciding where you will fall."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood, First Phantom Ranger**
