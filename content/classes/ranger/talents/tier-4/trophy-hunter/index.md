# Trophy Hunter

## Overview
The greatest Rangers do not simply hunt—they learn from every victory. Each dangerous beast, monstrous predator, and legendary creature defeated becomes another lesson etched into memory. By studying the anatomy, behaviour, and weaknesses of their prey, the Ranger steadily builds an unmatched understanding of the world's most dangerous creatures.

Trophy Hunter marks the beginning of this journey. Rather than collecting trophies for vanity, the Ranger preserves them as reminders of hard-earned knowledge. Every fang, claw, horn, or hide represents another hunt survived and another weakness uncovered. Against creatures they have studied before, the Ranger fights with calm confidence, knowing exactly where to strike and what mistakes to avoid.

## Rank 1
### Description

The greatest Rangers do not simply hunt—they learn from every victory. Each dangerous beast, monstrous predator, and legendary creature defeated becomes another lesson etched into memory. By studying the anatomy, behaviour, and weaknesses of their prey, the Ranger steadily builds an unmatched understanding of the world's most dangerous creatures.

Trophy Hunter marks the beginning of this journey. Rather than collecting trophies for vanity, the Ranger preserves them as reminders of hard-earned knowledge. Every fang, claw, horn, or hide represents another hunt survived and another weakness uncovered. Against creatures they have studied before, the Ranger fights with calm confidence, knowing exactly where to strike and what mistakes to avoid.

---

### Effects

- Whenever you personally participate in defeating a **Beast**, **Monster**, or other GM-approved creature, you may spend **1 hour** harvesting a trophy from its remains.
- Record the harvested trophy in a **Trophy Journal**.
- While fighting a creature of a species recorded within your Trophy Journal:
  - Gain a **+2 bonus to attack rolls** against that species.
  - Gain a **+2 bonus to Survival, Nature, and Investigation** checks made to identify, track, or study that species.
- You may only benefit from **one Trophy Hunter bonus** at a time.
- Harvesting a trophy does not prevent normal harvesting of crafting materials.

---

### Lore

Within the Ranger Lodges, every trophy tells a story. Some display the claw of a cave bear that nearly ended their first hunt, while others preserve the fang of a wyvern or the feather of a great roc. These keepsakes are not symbols of pride alone—they are lessons carried into every future hunt.

Veteran Rangers often possess collections spanning decades, each one representing a creature whose secrets they have uncovered. Apprentices are encouraged to study these trophies, for the wisdom gained from a single successful hunt may one day save countless lives.

---

> *"The greatest reward from every hunt is not the trophy—it is the knowledge that ensures the next one ends the same way."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 2
### Description

The Ranger's collection of trophies has grown into a living record of experience. Every hunt adds another lesson, allowing the Ranger to recognise subtle differences between creatures of the same family and predict their behaviour before battle even begins. The knowledge gained through years of hunting becomes just as deadly as any weapon they carry.

As the Trophy Journal expands, the Ranger develops an instinct for identifying vulnerable anatomy, common attack patterns, and survival habits. Against familiar prey, hesitation disappears, replaced by calm confidence earned through countless successful hunts.

---

### Effects

- Whenever you personally participate in defeating a **Beast**, **Monster**, or other GM-approved creature, you may spend **1 hour** harvesting a trophy from its remains.
- Record the harvested trophy within your **Trophy Journal**.
- While fighting a creature belonging to a species or creature family recorded within your Trophy Journal:
  - Gain a **+3 bonus to attack rolls** against that species or family.
  - Gain a **+3 bonus** to **Survival**, **Nature**, and **Investigation** checks made to identify, track, or study that species or family.
  - Gain **Advantage** on checks made to recall information about that species.
- You may only benefit from **one Trophy Hunter bonus** at a time.
- Harvesting a trophy does not prevent normal harvesting of crafting materials.
- The GM may allow closely related creatures to count as the same creature family (for example, Wolves, Dire Wolves, and Elder Wolves).

---

### Lore

Veteran Rangers often say that every scar tells a story, but every trophy tells a lesson. Over time, the Trophy Journal becomes more than a catalogue of victories—it becomes a field guide filled with hard-earned knowledge gathered through countless dangerous encounters.

Within the Ranger Lodges, apprentices are encouraged to study the journals of experienced hunters before undertaking dangerous expeditions. Many legendary beasts have been defeated not through overwhelming strength, but because a Ranger recognised a familiar weakness preserved within the pages of a Trophy Journal.

---

> *"Strength wins a hunt once. Knowledge wins every hunt that follows."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 3
### Description

Years of relentless hunting have transformed the Ranger into a living bestiary. Every creature added to the Trophy Journal deepens their understanding of the natural world, allowing them to recognise strengths, weaknesses, and behaviours with only a brief observation. To the Ranger, no trophy is merely a keepsake—it is another lesson that may save lives during future hunts.

This accumulated knowledge grants the Ranger remarkable insight into familiar prey. By studying trophies and recalling past encounters, they instinctively recognise where to strike, which attacks to avoid, and how best to exploit the creature's natural weaknesses. Against known prey, experience becomes as valuable as skill.

---

### Effects

- Whenever you personally participate in defeating a **Beast**, **Monster**, or other GM-approved creature, you may spend **1 hour** harvesting a trophy from its remains.
- Record the harvested trophy within your **Trophy Journal**.
- While fighting a creature belonging to a species or creature family recorded within your Trophy Journal:
  - Gain a **+4 bonus to attack rolls** against that species or family.
  - Gain a **+4 bonus** to **Survival**, **Nature**, and **Investigation** checks made to identify, track, or study that species or family.
  - Gain **Advantage** on checks made to recall information about that species.
- After successfully identifying a creature recorded in your Trophy Journal, you learn one of the following (GM's choice if unknown):
  - One Resistance.
  - One Immunity.
  - One Vulnerability.
  - One notable combat behaviour or special ability.
- You may only benefit from **one Trophy Hunter bonus** at a time.
- Harvesting a trophy does not prevent normal harvesting of crafting materials.

---

### Lore

Senior Rangers often spend as much time studying their Trophy Journals as they do hunting. Every page records another hard-earned lesson, allowing future encounters to begin with knowledge instead of uncertainty. To outsiders the collection appears to be a display of impressive trophies; to a Ranger it is a lifetime of experience preserved for the next hunt.

Within the Ranger Lodges, journals belonging to legendary hunters are treasured relics. Entire generations of Rangers have survived impossible encounters simply because a forgotten note revealed a creature's weakness before the battle began.

---

> *"Every beast carries a weakness. A Trophy Hunter simply learns to remember them all."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 4
### Description

The Ranger's Trophy Journal has become one of the finest collections of hunting knowledge in Asteria. Years of studying monsters, beasts, and legendary creatures have taught the Ranger that every family of creatures shares common instincts, weaknesses, and methods of survival. What was once knowledge of individual species now extends to entire bloodlines.

Rather than relying on memory alone, the Ranger instinctively recognises subtle traits shared between related creatures. A dragon's stance, a wolf's hunting pattern, or the posture of an undead guardian immediately reveals valuable information gathered from countless previous hunts. Against familiar creature families, the Ranger rarely enters battle unprepared.

---

### Effects

- Whenever you personally participate in defeating a **Beast**, **Monster**, or other GM-approved creature, you may spend **1 hour** harvesting a trophy from its remains.
- Record the harvested trophy within your **Trophy Journal**.
- While fighting a creature belonging to a **recorded creature family**, gain:
  - **+5 bonus to attack rolls**.
  - **+5 bonus** to **Survival**, **Nature**, and **Investigation** checks made to identify, track, or study that creature family.
  - **Advantage** on checks made to recall information about that creature family.
- After successfully identifying a creature recorded in your Trophy Journal, immediately learn **two** of the following:
  - One Resistance.
  - One Immunity.
  - One Vulnerability.
  - One notable combat behaviour or special ability.
- Once per encounter, you may reroll a failed **Nature**, **Survival**, or **Investigation** check made against a recorded creature family. You must use the new result.
- You may only benefit from **one Trophy Hunter bonus** at a time.
- Harvesting trophies does not prevent normal harvesting of crafting materials.

---

### Lore

Master Trophy Hunters are no longer recognised by the number of trophies they possess, but by the knowledge they carry into every hunt. Entire creature families become familiar to them, allowing even newly encountered variants to be understood through years of accumulated experience. Few creatures can truly surprise a Ranger who has spent decades studying the natural world.

Within the Ranger Lodges, the journals of these hunters are considered priceless archives. Generations of Rangers continue to add new discoveries, ensuring that every successful hunt strengthens those who will one day follow the same path.

---

> *"Every creature leaves behind more than a trophy. It leaves behind the wisdom to defeat the next one."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 5
### Description

The Ranger has become one of Asteria's greatest Trophy Hunters, possessing a Trophy Journal that spans a lifetime of legendary hunts. Every beast, monster, and apex predator defeated has left behind more than a physical trophy—it has contributed to an unparalleled understanding of the natural world. No hunt is wasted, and every encounter strengthens the Ranger's mastery over future prey.

The Trophy Journal is now considered a priceless archive, containing knowledge that cannot be found in books or taught through study alone. When facing a familiar creature, the Ranger instinctively recognises its strengths, weaknesses, behaviours, and patterns of attack before battle has truly begun. To many, it appears almost supernatural, yet it is simply the reward of surviving countless impossible hunts.

---

### Effects

- Whenever you personally participate in defeating a **Beast**, **Monster**, or other GM-approved creature, you may spend **1 hour** harvesting a trophy from its remains.
- Record the harvested trophy within your **Trophy Journal**.
- While fighting a creature belonging to a **recorded creature family**, gain:
  - **+6 bonus to attack rolls**.
  - **+6 bonus** to **Survival**, **Nature**, and **Investigation** checks made to identify, track, or study that creature family.
  - **Advantage** on checks made to recall information about that creature family.
- Immediately learn **all known**:
  - Resistances.
  - Immunities.
  - Vulnerabilities.
  - Combat behaviours.
  - Special abilities.
  - Natural weaknesses.
- Once per encounter, you may reroll a failed **Attack Roll**, **Nature**, **Survival**, or **Investigation** check made against a recorded creature family. You must use the new result.
- You may only benefit from **one Trophy Hunter bonus** at a time.
- Harvesting trophies does not prevent normal harvesting of crafting materials.

---

### Lore

Only a handful of Rangers throughout history have ever completed a Trophy Journal worthy of legend. These individuals are sought by kingdoms, guilds, and scholars alike, for their knowledge of the world's most dangerous creatures surpasses that of nearly every library in existence. Their journals become treasured relics, copied and preserved so future generations may continue the endless pursuit of understanding the wilderness.

Among the Ranger Lodges, these masters are known simply as **Trophy Hunters**—a title that carries immense respect. They do not hunt for glory or recognition, but because every creature overcome adds another page to the ever-growing story of the wild. Their greatest weapon is no longer their bow or blade, but the wisdom earned through every hunt they have survived.

---

> *"The greatest trophy is not the fang upon your wall—it is the knowledge that ensures no creature defeats you twice."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood, First Trophy Hunter**
