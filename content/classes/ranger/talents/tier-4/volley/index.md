# Volley

## Overview
The Ranger channels primal magic into a single arrow before firing it high into the heavens. As the arrow reaches its peak, it bursts into countless spectral copies that descend upon the battlefield in an unrelenting rain of magical arrows. Rather than striking a single target, Volley transforms the chosen area into a deadly storm where every heartbeat brings the threat of another falling arrow.

At its earliest stage, the Ranger has only limited control over this magical barrage. Once unleashed, the spectral arrows cannot distinguish friend from foe, forcing allies and enemies alike to seek shelter or risk being caught beneath the storm. Though dangerous to everyone within its reach, a well-placed Volley can completely reshape the battlefield and halt an advancing force.

---

### Effects

- **Cost:** 120 MP.
- **Uses:** 2 uses per **Long Rest**.
- Choose a point you can see within **60 metres**.
- Spectral arrows rain down in a **10-metre radius** centred on the chosen point.
- The volley remains active **until the start of the second Initiative Order after your own**.
- A creature must make a **DC 13 Agility (Dodge) Check**:
  - The first time it enters the affected area on its turn.
  - Or when it starts its turn within the affected area.
- A creature can only be affected **once** by each use of Volley.
- On a failed check, the creature takes **6d8 Magical Piercing Damage**.
- On a successful check, the creature takes **half damage**.
- **All creatures**, including allies, are affected by Volley.
- Volley requires a **clear path to the sky** and cannot be used underground, beneath a solid roof, or inside enclosed structures unless the GM determines there is sufficient overhead clearance.
- The spectral arrows disappear immediately after striking and cannot be recovered.

---

### Lore

Volley is considered one of the Ranger Lodges' oldest battlefield techniques, created for the rare occasions when a single arrow was not enough to turn the tide of battle. By surrendering precision for overwhelming area control, the Ranger commands the wilderness itself to answer their call, transforming one arrow into a storm capable of scattering entire warbands.

The earliest practitioners quickly learned that such power demanded restraint. Until complete mastery is achieved, the magical arrows recognise no friend or foe, making careful positioning as important as the technique itself. Veteran Rangers often warn apprentices that a poorly placed Volley can be just as dangerous to allies as it is to their enemies.

---

> *"One arrow begins the hunt. A thousand arrows decide its end."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 2
### Description

The Ranger's mastery over Volley continues to grow, allowing them to sustain a larger and more devastating rain of spectral arrows. What was once an uncontrollable barrage now falls with greater precision, covering a wider portion of the battlefield while maintaining its relentless assault. Though allies are still at risk, an experienced Ranger learns to position the storm where it will inflict the greatest harm upon their enemies.

As the enchanted arrow bursts overhead, countless shimmering copies spiral downward in waves, forcing creatures to either abandon their ground or endure the unending rain of magical projectiles. Few formations can remain intact beneath such overwhelming pressure.

### Lore

Those who survive a Rank II Volley often describe it as standing beneath a violent storm rather than an attack. The sky darkens with spectral arrows, each one guided by primal magic instead of a Ranger's hand. Escape becomes the only sensible option, as remaining beneath the barrage invites certain injury.

Within the Ranger Lodges, apprentices are taught that Volley is as much a tool of battlefield control as it is a weapon. A well-placed barrage can scatter enemy formations, deny strategic ground, and force even the most disciplined warriors to abandon their positions.

---

> *"A hunter does not always need to strike the prey. Sometimes, they simply decide where the prey is allowed to stand."*

Although the Ranger's control has improved considerably, the magical storm still shows no favour. Friend and foe alike must respect its destructive power, making careful positioning and battlefield awareness essential to those fighting alongside the Ranger.

Veteran Huntmasters teach that Volley is not intended to eliminate every foe outright. Its true strength lies in forcing the enemy to move exactly where the Ranger wants them to go, allowing allies to exploit the chaos while the storm rages overhead.

## Rank 4
### Description

The Ranger's mastery over Volley has reached a level where the primal magic responds to their intent as much as their aim. The storm of spectral arrows no longer falls as an indiscriminate barrage, but as a controlled rain directed toward those the Ranger wishes to punish. Entire battlefields can be reshaped as allies advance beneath a sky that only their enemies need fear.

The arrow still ascends into the heavens before splitting into countless magical copies, but the Ranger now instinctively guides the storm's path through primal will. Enemies find themselves trapped beneath a relentless downpour while companions move unhindered through the chaos, allowing coordinated assaults that were once impossible.

### Lore

Few Rangers ever develop enough control to command Volley without endangering their companions. Those who do are regarded as battlefield tacticians as much as hunters, capable of directing a magical storm with remarkable precision while their allies advance unharmed beneath it.

Within the Ranger Lodges, this achievement marks the transition from master hunter to battlefield commander. Entire warbands have marched confidently through a storm of spectral arrows, trusting completely in the judgement of the Ranger who called it forth. To witness such mastery is to understand that the wilderness itself now fights beside them.

---

> *"A storm without control is destruction. A storm guided by a Ranger is victory."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 5
### Description

The Ranger has perfected one of the Ranger Lodges' most feared techniques. A single enchanted arrow is loosed into the heavens where it bursts into an immense storm of primal energy, creating an unrelenting rain of spectral arrows across the battlefield. Every arrow is guided by the Ranger's will, striking only those they deem worthy of the hunt.

No longer an indiscriminate barrage, the final form of Volley demonstrates complete mastery over both archery and primal magic. Allies advance unhindered beneath the storm while enemies desperately seek shelter from a relentless rain that seems to pursue every movement. Entire battlefields can be dominated with a single shot, allowing the Ranger to dictate the flow of combat from incredible distances.

Among the Ranger Lodges, only the greatest Huntmasters are entrusted with this technique. To witness a master unleash Volley is to watch the sky itself become the Ranger's greatest weapon.

---

### Effects

- **Cost:** 200 MP.
- **Uses:** 2 uses per **Long Rest**.
- Choose a point you can see within **120 metres**.
- Spectral arrows rain down in a **25-metre radius** centred on the chosen point.
- The volley remains active **until the start of the second Initiative Order after your own**.
- When activating Volley, you may designate **any number of allied creatures** that are **immune** to this ability's effects.
- A creature must make a **DC 21 Agility (Dodge) Check**:
  - The first time it enters the affected area on its turn.
  - Or when it starts its turn within the affected area.
- A creature can only be affected **once** by each use of Volley.
- On a failed check, the creature takes **10d8 Magical Piercing Damage**.
- On a successful check, the creature takes **half damage**.
- Creatures that fail the Dodge Check have their **Movement Speed reduced by half until the end of their next turn** as spectral arrows pin them down and disrupt their advance.
- Volley requires a **clear path to the sky** and cannot be used underground, beneath a solid roof, or inside enclosed structures unless the GM determines there is sufficient overhead clearance.
- The spectral arrows disappear immediately after striking and cannot be recovered.

---

### Lore

The fifth rank of Volley is considered the pinnacle of battlefield archery. Few Rangers throughout history have possessed the discipline, magical control, and experience required to command such a devastating storm without endangering those fighting beside them. Those who achieve this mastery are remembered not only as legendary hunters, but as protectors capable of turning impossible battles into decisive victories.

Stories tell of entire armies brought to a halt beneath a sky filled with spectral arrows while allied forces advanced untouched through the storm. To the Ranger Lodges, this technique is the ultimate expression of the hunter's creed—not simply to defeat the prey, but to control the battlefield so completely that escape becomes impossible.

---

> *"When the heavens answer the hunter's call, even armies learn there is nowhere left to run."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood, First Huntmaster of the Emerald Lodge**
