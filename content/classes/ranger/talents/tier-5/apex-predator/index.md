# Apex Predator

## Overview
The greatest Rangers eventually transcend the role of hunter and become something far more primal. By awakening the ancient instincts that lie dormant within every living creature, the Ranger enters a heightened state where every sense sharpens, every movement becomes deliberate, and every attack is executed with absolute precision. The wilderness itself seems to recognise the Ranger as its dominant predator.

While Apex Predator is active, hesitation disappears. Every sound, scent, movement, and change within the environment is instinctively processed, allowing the Ranger to react before others even recognise danger. To their enemies, the Ranger becomes relentless—an ever-present hunter who patiently waits for the perfect opportunity before striking with overwhelming efficiency.

This transformation does not rely on rage or brute strength. Instead, it is the culmination of a lifetime spent surviving, adapting, and mastering every lesson the wilderness has to offer.

---

### Effects

- **Cost:** 200 MP.
- **Duration:** 5 Minutes.
- **Uses:** 1 use per **Long Rest**.
- Gain **+2 Attack Rolls** with all weapons.
- Gain **+2 AC**.
- Gain **+2 Agility (Dodge) Checks**.
- Gain **+2 Perception**, **Survival**, and **Initiative**.
- Gain **10 metres** of additional movement speed.
- Your **Hunter's Mark** and **Predator's Pursuit** abilities require **no Mana Cost** while Apex Predator is active.
- Gain **Advantage** on checks made to track, detect, or pursue creatures.
- You cannot be **Surprised** while Apex Predator is active.

---

### Lore

Among every Ranger Lodge, Apex Predator is regarded as the pinnacle of the hunter's path. Few ever awaken this state, and fewer still can maintain it for long. Those who do are said to move with the confidence of a wolf leading its pack, the patience of a great serpent waiting to strike, and the precision of an eagle descending upon its prey.

Legends speak of Huntmasters who walked alone into territories ruled by dragons, titans, and ancient beasts, returning days later with nothing but another trophy for their journals. Whether those tales are true or not, one lesson has endured throughout every generation: the wilderness does not recognise titles or crowns—it recognises only the Apex Predator.

---

> *"Every creature believes itself the hunter... until it meets the one at the top of the food chain."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood, First Apex Predator**

## Rank 2
### Description

The Ranger's transformation into the Apex Predator grows stronger, allowing them to command every instinct with absolute confidence. Their awareness extends far beyond ordinary perception, detecting the slightest disturbance in the environment long before others become aware of danger. Every movement becomes efficient, every attack purposeful, and every decision driven by countless years of experience.

While this state endures, the Ranger embodies the natural order of the wilderness. Predators hesitate before engaging, prey instinctively flees, and even intelligent enemies find themselves unnerved by the calm determination of a hunter that never wastes movement or opportunity. Every action brings the Ranger one step closer to a flawless hunt.

---

### Effects

- **Cost:** 220 MP.
- **Duration:** 6 Minutes.
- **Uses:** 1 use per **Long Rest**.
- Gain **+3 Attack Rolls** with all weapons.
- Gain **+3 AC**.
- Gain **+3** to **Agility (Dodge) Checks**.
- Gain **+3** to **Perception**, **Survival**, and **Initiative**.
- Gain **15 metres** of additional movement speed.
- **Hunter's Mark** and **Predator's Pursuit** cost **no Mana** while Apex Predator is active.
- Gain **Advantage** on all checks made to track, detect, identify, or pursue creatures.
- You cannot be **Surprised** while Apex Predator is active.
- Once per turn, after reducing a creature to **0 HP**, you may immediately move up to **6 metres** without provoking **Opportunity Attacks**.

---

### Lore

Only a handful of Rangers ever progress beyond the first awakening of Apex Predator. Those who do no longer rely solely on their senses, but on an instinct refined through countless hunts. They appear to know where danger will emerge before it exists, allowing them to react with unnerving speed and confidence.

The Ranger Lodges teach that the true Apex Predator does not chase blindly or fight recklessly. Instead, they control the rhythm of every hunt, conserving energy until the decisive moment arrives. By the time their quarry realises it has become prey, escape is already impossible.

---

> *"The strongest predator is not the fastest or the fiercest. It is the one that never wastes a single movement."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 3
### Description

The Ranger has ascended beyond mortal instinct, becoming a true embodiment of nature's perfect hunter. Every sense operates in complete harmony, allowing the Ranger to perceive danger, exploit weaknesses, and react with flawless precision. No movement is wasted, no attack is careless, and no opportunity escapes their notice.

When Apex Predator is awakened, the battlefield becomes the Ranger's hunting grounds. Every heartbeat, shifting shadow, and subtle change in the environment is recognised instantly. Opponents soon realise they are no longer fighting an ordinary warrior—they have become the prey of a hunter whose every action is guided by perfected instinct.

### Lore

Among the Ranger Lodges, reaching the third rank of Apex Predator signifies that the Ranger has become one with the rhythm of the hunt. Every encounter is approached with unwavering confidence, knowing that years of discipline and experience have sharpened every instinct beyond ordinary limits.

Legends tell of Huntmasters capable of crossing entire battlefields without slowing, striking down one foe before seamlessly pursuing the next. To them, combat is no longer chaotic—it is simply another hunt, where patience, precision, and instinct always prevail over panic and brute force.

---

> *"The wilderness does not reward the strongest. It rewards the hunter who never misses the moment to strike."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 4
### Description

The Ranger has reached a level of mastery where instinct and action are inseparable. Every movement is purposeful, every strike calculated, and every heartbeat of the hunt unfolds exactly as anticipated. Within this state, the Ranger no longer reacts to the battlefield—they command it through overwhelming awareness and flawless execution.

Predators throughout the natural world possess moments where instinct eclipses thought. Apex Predator embodies this principle, allowing the Ranger to become the perfect hunter for a brief time. Every weakness is recognised, every escape route anticipated, and every attack delivered with relentless precision. Against a marked quarry, there is little hope of escape once the hunt has begun.

---

- **Cost:** 260 MP.
- **Duration:** 8 Minutes.

### Lore

Only the greatest Huntmasters in history have reached the fourth rank of Apex Predator. They become the embodiment of every lesson the Ranger Lodges strive to teach—patience, discipline, precision, and absolute control. To face such a hunter is to realise that every attempt to flee, hide, or outmanoeuvre them has already been anticipated.

Among the oldest Ranger traditions, these individuals are believed to have earned the respect of the wilderness itself. Birds fall silent as they pass, beasts instinctively recognise their dominance, and even legendary monsters are said to hesitate when confronted by a true Apex Predator. Their reputation alone is often enough to make lesser creatures abandon the hunt before it begins.

---

> *"The hunt is won long before the first arrow is loosed. The Apex Predator merely allows the prey to discover it too late."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood**

## Rank 5
### Description

The Ranger has reached the absolute pinnacle of the hunter's path, becoming the living embodiment of nature's ultimate predator. Every lesson learned, every quarry pursued, and every battle survived culminates in a state where instinct surpasses conscious thought. Within this transformation, the Ranger moves with unmatched speed, unwavering precision, and absolute confidence, dominating the battlefield as though every encounter were another carefully orchestrated hunt.

To witness an Apex Predator is to witness the wilderness itself taking form. Every arrow finds its mark, every movement anticipates danger, and every decision is made with perfect clarity. No prey escapes their senses, no ambush catches them unaware, and no battlefield remains beyond their control. Friend and foe alike recognise that they stand before the highest expression of the Ranger's calling.

---

### Effects

- **Cost:** 300 MP.
- **Duration:** 10 Minutes.
- **Uses:** 1 use per **Long Rest**.
- Gain **+6 Attack Rolls** with all weapons.
- Gain **+6 AC**.
- Gain **+6** to **Agility (Dodge) Checks**.
- Gain **+6** to **Perception**, **Survival**, **Initiative**, and **Investigation** checks.
- Gain **30 metres** of additional Movement Speed.
- **Hunter's Mark** and **Predator's Pursuit** cost **no Mana** while Apex Predator is active.
- Gain **Advantage** on all checks made to track, detect, identify, investigate, or pursue creatures.
- You **cannot be Surprised** while Apex Predator is active.
- Once per turn, after reducing a creature to **0 HP**, you may immediately move up to **15 metres** without provoking **Opportunity Attacks**.
- While attacking a creature affected by **Hunter's Mark**, your attacks ignore **all Cover**.
- Your attacks against creatures affected by **Hunter's Mark** score a **Critical Hit on a natural 18–20**.
- Once per turn, when you miss an attack against your **Hunter's Mark**, you may immediately reroll the attack. You must use the new result.

---

### Lore

Only a handful of Rangers throughout Asteria's history have ever attained the fifth rank of Apex Predator. Their names are remembered not because they hunted the greatest monsters, but because no hunt was ever considered impossible. Dragons, ancient beasts, eldritch horrors, and legendary predators all became quarry beneath their watchful gaze.

Within the Ranger Lodges, these masters are revered as the perfect balance between patience and aggression. They do not rely upon overwhelming force, but upon flawless judgement, unrivalled awareness, and an instinct sharpened by a lifetime spent surviving the unforgiving wilderness. To become an Apex Predator is not simply to master the hunt—it is to become the standard by which all future Rangers are measured.

---

> *"There comes a moment when the prey finally understands the truth... it was never running from the hunter. It was running toward its fate."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood, First Apex Predator**
