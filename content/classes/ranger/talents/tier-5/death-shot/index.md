# Death Shot

## Overview
Every hunt has a final moment—the instant when the hunter knows the prey can no longer escape. Death Shot embodies that moment. Rather than relying on overwhelming force, the Ranger waits patiently until their quarry is exhausted, wounded, and vulnerable before loosing a single arrow infused with primal magic to deliver the final blow.

The technique demands exceptional discipline. A Death Shot fired too early wastes its power, while one fired at the perfect moment becomes the inevitable conclusion of the hunt. Among the Ranger Lodges, apprentices are taught that this ability is never used to begin a battle, only to end one.

---

### Effects

- **Cost:** 250 MP.
- **Uses:** 1 use per **Long Rest**.
- Make a single ranged weapon attack against a creature within your weapon's range.
- This attack must hit normally.
- Death Shot may only be used against a creature currently affected by your **Hunter's Mark**.
- If the target has **10% or less of its maximum HP** remaining when the attack hits, it is **immediately reduced to 0 HP**.
- If the target has more than **10% of its maximum HP**, Death Shot deals normal weapon damage plus **6d8 Magical Piercing Damage** instead.
- Legendary creatures, Raid Bosses, and creatures specifically designated by the GM as immune to execution effects are instead reduced to **1 HP** if they would be slain by Death Shot.

#### Limitations

- Death Shot cannot critically hit.
- Death Shot may only be used with bows, crossbows, or other ranged weapons approved by the GM.
- The execution effect only applies if the attack successfully hits.

---

### Lore

Death Shot is considered the ultimate expression of a Ranger's patience. It is not a display of strength, but of judgement. The greatest hunters know that every creature has a moment of weakness, and only those with the discipline to wait for that instant are worthy of learning this technique.

Many legendary Huntmasters became famous not for the number of arrows they loosed, but because every Death Shot marked the end of a hunt that others believed impossible. Their arrows became symbols of certainty—the final chapter in every pursuit.

---

> *"The first arrow begins the hunt. The last arrow writes its ending."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood, First Apex Predator**

## Rank 2
### Description

The Ranger's understanding of the perfect killing shot deepens, allowing them to recognise the exact instant a wounded quarry can no longer survive. Every heartbeat, every stagger, and every desperate movement reveals an opportunity invisible to ordinary hunters. Death Shot is no longer simply an execution—it is the inevitable conclusion of a hunt carried out with absolute precision.

Veteran Rangers know that the greatest hunters do not waste arrows. They wait patiently, allowing exhaustion, injury, and fear to weaken their quarry before releasing a single arrow that ends the battle. Among the Ranger Lodges, mastering the second rank of Death Shot is considered proof that a Ranger has learned patience above aggression.

## Rank 4
### Description

The Ranger's mastery over Death Shot has become legendary. Their understanding of anatomy, instinct, and timing has reached a point where they can identify a quarry's final weakness with only a glance. Every heartbeat, every faltering step, and every sign of fatigue reveals the perfect opportunity to deliver the finishing blow.

The arrow itself becomes a vessel of primal magic, carrying the Ranger's unwavering intent. Once released, it represents the culmination of an entire hunt, guided by years of discipline rather than emotion. To face a Ranger capable of this technique is to know that every mistake brings the inevitable end one step closer.

---

### Effects

- **Cost:** 325 MP.
- **Uses:** 1 use per **Long Rest**.

#### Limitations

---

### Lore

Among the Ranger Lodges, the fourth rank of Death Shot is regarded as the mark of a true Huntmaster. Those who achieve it are no longer simply skilled archers—they are executioners of the wild, capable of ending even the fiercest hunts with a single perfectly placed arrow.

Many of Asteria's greatest monsters met their end beneath this technique. The stories speak not of prolonged battles, but of one final arrow released at precisely the right moment. To those who witnessed it, the hunt did not end with violence—it ended with certainty.

---

> *"Every hunt ends with a final breath. My arrow simply decides when that breath is taken."*

## Rank 5
### Description

Death Shot has become the ultimate expression of the Ranger's purpose. The hunt has reached its inevitable conclusion, and the Ranger requires only a single arrow to deliver judgement. Every lesson learned throughout a lifetime of tracking, observing, and overcoming the world's deadliest creatures culminates in this one technique.

As the arrow is infused with immense primal magic, the Ranger waits with unwavering patience for the exact instant their quarry falters. There is no hesitation, no wasted movement, and no uncertainty. When the arrow is finally released, it carries the certainty that every hunt must eventually end.

Among the Ranger Lodges, mastering the fifth rank of Death Shot is considered the highest honour a hunter can achieve. It is said that once an Apex Predator has chosen the final moment, even the greatest monsters cannot escape their fate.

---

### Effects

- **Cost:** 350 MP.
- **Uses:** 1 use per **Long Rest**.
- Make a single ranged weapon attack against a creature within your weapon's range.
- This attack must hit normally.
- The target must be affected by your **Hunter's Mark**.
- If the target has **30% or less of its maximum HP** remaining when the attack hits, it is immediately reduced to **0 HP**.
- If the target has more than **30%** of its maximum HP remaining, Death Shot instead deals normal weapon damage plus **15d8 Magical Piercing Damage**.
- Legendary creatures, Raid Bosses, Mythic creatures, and creatures designated by the GM as immune to execution effects are instead reduced to **1 HP** if they would be slain by Death Shot.
- If Death Shot reduces a creature to **0 HP**:
  - Immediately regain **150 MP**.
  - You may immediately transfer **Hunter's Mark** to another creature within your weapon's range without paying its Mana Cost.
  - You may immediately make **one standard ranged weapon attack** against the newly marked creature. This attack does **not** benefit from Death Shot.

#### Limitations

- Death Shot cannot critically hit.
- Death Shot may only be used with bows, crossbows, or other GM-approved ranged weapons.
- The execution effect only occurs if the attack successfully hits.
- The bonus ranged attack may only occur once per use of Death Shot.

---

### Lore

Only a handful of Rangers throughout Asteria's history have ever mastered the fifth rank of Death Shot. Their names have become legend not because they hunted the greatest beasts, but because they never failed to finish a hunt once they committed to it. Dragons, eldritch horrors, ancient predators, and creatures thought impossible to slay all eventually fell to a single perfectly timed arrow.

Within the Ranger Lodges, Death Shot is regarded as the final lesson every hunter strives toward. It is not a display of overwhelming strength, but the ultimate reward for patience, discipline, and absolute mastery of the hunt. To wield Death Shot is to understand that every creature, no matter how mighty, has a final moment—and a true Ranger will always recognise it.

---

> *"Every hunt begins with hope. Every hunt ends with one perfect arrow."*  
> — **Grand Ranger Elyas Thornwood, First Apex Predator**
