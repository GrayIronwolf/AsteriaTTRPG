# Arcane Echo

## Overview
A Spellblade's mastery extends beyond blades and spells. Through careful manipulation of arcane energy, they learn to leave behind fragments of their presence, creating convincing images that linger within the world.

These manifestations, known as Arcane Echoes, are not living beings, summoned creatures, or independent entities. They are reflections of the Spellblade's arcane signature given temporary form. To an observer, the Echo appears identical to its creator, standing motionless as though waiting for the perfect moment to act.

At Rank 1, the Echo lacks intelligence, movement, and substance. It cannot attack, speak, cast spells, or interact with the world. Its purpose is deception. Whether used to distract enemies, conceal the Spellblade's true position, or create uncertainty during an encounter, the Echo forces opponents to question what they are seeing.

Many Spellblades consider Arcane Echo one of the most valuable tactical abilities available to them. A well-placed illusion can create opportunities that brute force never could.

## Rank 1
### Description

A Spellblade's mastery extends beyond blades and spells. Through careful manipulation of arcane energy, they learn to leave behind fragments of their presence, creating convincing images that linger within the world.

These manifestations, known as Arcane Echoes, are not living beings, summoned creatures, or independent entities. They are reflections of the Spellblade's arcane signature given temporary form. To an observer, the Echo appears identical to its creator, standing motionless as though waiting for the perfect moment to act.

At Rank 1, the Echo lacks intelligence, movement, and substance. It cannot attack, speak, cast spells, or interact with the world. Its purpose is deception. Whether used to distract enemies, conceal the Spellblade's true position, or create uncertainty during an encounter, the Echo forces opponents to question what they are seeing.

Many Spellblades consider Arcane Echo one of the most valuable tactical abilities available to them. A well-placed illusion can create opportunities that brute force never could.

### Effects

- Spend **20 MP** to create an Arcane Echo.
- The Echo appears within **5 metres** of the Spellblade.
- The Echo remains for **1 minute** or until destroyed.
- Only **one Arcane Echo** may exist at a time.

#### Arcane Echo Properties

The Echo:

- Appears visually identical to the Spellblade.
- Occupies a physical space.
- Remains completely stationary.
- Cannot move.
- Cannot attack.
- Cannot cast abilities.
- Cannot speak.
- Cannot interact with objects.
- Does not provide flanking bonuses.
- Does not block movement.

#### Investigation

A creature that suspects the Echo is not real may use an:

- Investigation Check
- Arcana Check

The creature must bet a DC10

On a success:
- The creature identifies the Echo as an illusion.

On a failure:
- The creature believes the Echo is genuine.

#### Destruction

The Arcane Echo immediately disappears if:

- It is successfully struck by an attack.
- The Spellblade dismisses it.
- The duration expires.
- The Spellblade is reduced to 0 HP.

#### Restrictions

- Maximum of one Echo active at a time.
- The Echo cannot move.
- The Echo cannot perform actions.
- The Echo cannot activate abilities.
- The Echo provides no direct offensive benefits.
- The Echo is clearly magical when physically interacted with.

### Lore

Among the oldest Spellblade traditions, Arcane Echo was originally developed as a dueling technique. Practitioners sought methods of confusing opponents without relying on brute force or overwhelming magical power.

The first Echoes were crude and unstable, often flickering in and out of existence. Over generations, Spellblades refined the technique until their illusions became nearly indistinguishable from the real practitioner at a glance.

Many instructors teach that Arcane Echo embodies an important lesson: victory does not always belong to the strongest combatant. Sometimes it belongs to the one who controls what their opponent believes.

Veteran Spellblades often claim that the greatest strength of an Echo is not convincing an enemy that it is real. It is making them doubt whether anything they see can be trusted.

> "A sword threatens the body. Uncertainty threatens the mind."
>
> — Master Illarian Voss, Keeper of the Mirror Hall

## Rank 2
### Description

As the Spellblade's understanding of illusion and arcane projection improves, their Arcane Echo becomes more stable and convincing. The image no longer flickers as easily, allowing it to remain present for extended periods and over greater distances.

The Echo remains incapable of movement or independent action, but the quality of the illusion improves considerably. Fine details such as armour, clothing, weapons, and posture are replicated with greater accuracy, making it more difficult for observers to immediately identify the deception.

Experienced Spellblades often use Arcane Echo as a scouting aid, distraction, or means of controlling the battlefield. An enemy forced to question what they are seeing is already at a disadvantage.

While still a simple projection, the Echo begins to feel less like a magical construct and more like a genuine reflection of the Spellblade's presence.

### Lore

Among Spellblade academies, Rank II is often referred to as the Stage of Refinement. At this point, the Echo becomes convincing enough that even experienced combatants may hesitate before dismissing it as a simple illusion.

The greatest strength of Arcane Echo has never been the illusion itself. It is the uncertainty it creates. A moment of hesitation can be worth more than any blade strike, and many Spellblades have secured victory through deception rather than force.

Records from the Mirror Hall tell of duelists who won entire tournaments without ever striking first, relying instead on confusion, misdirection, and flawless positioning. Their Echoes forced opponents to second-guess every decision they made.

A wise Spellblade understands that battles are fought in both the mind and the body. Arcane Echo targets the former long before the latter.

> "When your enemy doubts their own eyes, the battle is already half won."
>
> — Master Illarian Voss, Keeper of the Mirror Hall

## Rank 3
### Description

The Spellblade's Arcane Echo evolves beyond a simple stationary illusion. Through greater control over mana and projection, the Echo can now imitate basic movement, making it significantly more difficult to distinguish from the real Spellblade at a glance.

The movement is limited and lacks true intelligence. The Echo cannot make decisions, engage in combat, or interact with the world around it. Instead, it follows simple commands issued by its creator, moving in a deliberate and predictable manner.

For the first time, the Arcane Echo begins to create genuine uncertainty on the battlefield. Enemies can no longer rely on the Echo remaining where it was created, forcing them to spend precious moments determining which Spellblade is real.

Many veteran Spellblades consider this the point where Arcane Echo becomes a true battlefield deception technique rather than a simple distraction.

### Lore

Within the oldest Spellblade traditions, Rank III is known as the Stage of Motion. It represents the point where an Echo ceases to be a simple image and begins to imitate life itself.

Many apprentices are surprised to learn that movement is often more convincing than appearance. A perfectly crafted illusion that never moves quickly becomes suspicious, while even a simple projection can appear genuine when it behaves naturally.

Historical accounts tell of Spellblades using moving Echoes to lure enemies into traps, distract guards, and create confusion during battles. Entire skirmishes have been decided by opponents chasing a false target while the real Spellblade positioned themselves elsewhere.

Masters of Arcane Echo often remind their students that deception is not about creating a perfect lie. It is about creating enough doubt that the truth becomes difficult to see.

> "The eye sees what it expects to see. A moving shadow is often enough."
>
> — Master Illarian Voss, Keeper of the Mirror Hall

## Rank 4
### Description

The Spellblade's mastery of Arcane Echo reaches a level where a single illusion is no longer sufficient. Through refined control over their mana, they learn to divide their arcane signature between multiple projections, creating a web of uncertainty across the battlefield.

Each Echo remains a construct of mana rather than a true duplicate, yet the presence of multiple projections dramatically increases the difficulty of identifying the real Spellblade. Enemies can no longer focus on a single target, forcing them to question every movement and every decision.

The Echoes are still limited in their capabilities, unable to fight or cast spells, but their ability to move independently creates a powerful tool for deception, scouting, distraction, and battlefield manipulation.

Among veteran Spellblades, Rank IV is often considered the point where Arcane Echo becomes genuinely dangerous. Not because the Echoes can harm an opponent, but because they make mistakes far more likely.

### Lore

Rank IV is often referred to as the Stage of Multiplicity within the Mirror Hall traditions. The Spellblade learns that confusion does not come from creating a perfect illusion—it comes from creating too many possibilities.

Military records contain numerous accounts of enemy formations wasting valuable time and resources attempting to identify the true Spellblade among multiple projections. Some commanders even ordered volleys of arrows directed at every visible target, only to discover they had spent precious resources attacking illusions.

Masters of Arcane Echo often teach that deception is strongest when certainty becomes impossible. When an opponent can no longer trust their own eyes, every decision becomes slower, every action becomes hesitant, and every mistake becomes more likely.

To a skilled Spellblade, uncertainty is a weapon as sharp as any blade.

> "One illusion creates doubt. Two create confusion. Beyond that, the enemy defeats themselves."
>
> — Master Illarian Voss, Keeper of the Mirror Hall

## Rank 5
### Description

Arcane Echo reaches its ultimate form, allowing the Spellblade to create projections so convincing that even experienced warriors struggle to distinguish illusion from reality. The Echoes no longer merely stand or move—they imitate the behaviour of their creator with startling accuracy.

Through exceptional control over mana, the Spellblade can command their Echoes to mimic simple combat actions, gestures, and movements. Though still incapable of causing harm, these projections create uncertainty on a scale that few opponents are prepared to face.

Enemies attempting to identify the real Spellblade must now contend with multiple moving targets, each behaving as though it could be genuine. Every hesitation creates an opportunity, and every mistake can shift the outcome of a battle.

Among the ancient Spellblade Orders, Rank V is known as the Stage of Mirrors. It is said that a master of Arcane Echo never enters a battle alone, for every reflection of their presence becomes another threat their enemies must account for.

### Lore

The greatest masters of Arcane Echo are remembered throughout Asteria as impossible opponents to track. Witnesses often described battles where multiple Spellblades appeared to be fighting at once, each moving with such confidence that identifying the real combatant became nearly impossible.

Legends tell of duelists who defeated stronger opponents through deception alone, exhausting their enemies as they chased illusions while the true Spellblade waited for the perfect opportunity to strike. Whether these stories are exaggerated or entirely truthful remains a matter of debate.

Within the Mirror Hall, Rank V is regarded as the pinnacle of illusionary combat. Students who achieve this level are taught that the purpose of Arcane Echo is not to create falsehoods, but to create uncertainty. When certainty disappears, mistakes follow.

A master Spellblade understands that a blade can wound the body, but doubt can unravel an entire battle.

> "The enemy sees four Spellblades. The wise enemy asks which one is real. The fool attacks the first one they see."
>
> — Master Illarian Voss, Keeper of the Mirror Hallv
