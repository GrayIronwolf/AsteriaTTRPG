# Arcane Edge

## Overview
The defining trait of every Spellblade is the ability to merge martial skill with arcane power. Rather than casting magic separately from combat, they learn to channel mana directly through their weapon, temporarily transforming it into a conduit of raw magical energy.

When Arcane Edge is activated, mana flows from the Spellblade's core into the weapon, causing its edge to shimmer with arcane light. Every strike delivered while the enchantment is active carries a pulse of magical force, allowing the Spellblade to inflict wounds that ordinary steel alone could never achieve.

At Rank I, maintaining this enchantment is physically and mentally demanding, limiting how long the weapon can remain empowered. Even so, mastering this technique marks the first true step towards becoming a Spellblade, teaching that the greatest weapon is one where steel and mana strike together as one.

## Rank 1
### Description

The defining trait of every Spellblade is the ability to merge martial skill with arcane power. Rather than casting magic separately from combat, they learn to channel mana directly through their weapon, temporarily transforming it into a conduit of raw magical energy.

When Arcane Edge is activated, mana flows from the Spellblade's core into the weapon, causing its edge to shimmer with arcane light. Every strike delivered while the enchantment is active carries a pulse of magical force, allowing the Spellblade to inflict wounds that ordinary steel alone could never achieve.

At Rank I, maintaining this enchantment is physically and mentally demanding, limiting how long the weapon can remain empowered. Even so, mastering this technique marks the first true step towards becoming a Spellblade, teaching that the greatest weapon is one where steel and mana strike together as one.

### Effects

- Spend **15 MP** to activate **Arcane Edge**.
- Arcane Edge remains active for **2 Turns**.
- While active, your melee weapon deals an additional **1d4 Arcane Damage** on each successful hit.
- The weapon emits a visible arcane glow while Arcane Edge is active.
- Requires a melee weapon to activate. This talent does not affect ranged weapons.
- Arcane Edge ends immediately if you are no longer wielding the infused weapon.
- Arcane Edge cannot be activated again while already active.

### Lore

Arcane Edge is one of the oldest techniques preserved within the Spellblade Orders. Before learning advanced combat magic, every apprentice is taught that a weapon should become an extension of their mana, not merely a piece of sharpened steel.

The earliest practitioners discovered that even a simple infusion of mana dramatically increased the lethality of a weapon without requiring complex enchantments. Although temporary, this technique proved invaluable in battle, allowing Spellblades to adapt instantly to changing situations without relying upon permanently enchanted arms.

To this day, many masters consider Arcane Edge the moment an apprentice truly earns the title of Spellblade, for it is the first time they successfully unite steel and sorcery into a single weapon.

> *"A blade without mana is only steel. A blade with mana carries the will of its master."*
>
> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 2
### Description

As the Spellblade's control over mana improves, so too does their ability to sustain an arcane enchantment upon their weapon. Rather than releasing short bursts of unstable energy, the Spellblade learns to maintain a steadier flow of mana, strengthening both the duration and potency of Arcane Edge.

The weapon's aura burns brighter and the magical energy coursing through its edge becomes more refined. Each successful strike releases a greater surge of arcane power, allowing the Spellblade to carve through both physical armour and magical defences with increasing efficiency.

Rank II represents the first significant refinement of Arcane Edge. The enchantment lasts longer, strikes harder, and requires greater discipline to maintain. Veteran Spellblades often encourage apprentices to master this rank before pursuing more advanced offensive techniques, as a stable Arcane Edge forms the foundation of nearly every magical weapon art practiced within the Order.

### Effects

- Spend **20 MP** to activate **Arcane Edge**.
- Arcane Edge remains active for **3 Turns**.
- While active, your melee weapon deals an additional **1d6 Arcane Damage** on each successful hit.
- The weapon emits a visible arcane glow while Arcane Edge is active.
- Requires a melee weapon to activate. This talent does not affect ranged weapons.
- Arcane Edge ends immediately if you are no longer wielding the infused weapon.
- Arcane Edge cannot be activated again while already active.

### Lore

Spellblade historians often refer to Rank II as the stage where mana and steel begin to trust one another. The unstable surges experienced by novices become controlled currents, allowing the weapon to channel greater power without risking the integrity of the blade.

Many ancient duelling schools considered mastery of this rank a rite of passage. Only once a Spellblade could reliably maintain Arcane Edge throughout an entire duel were they permitted to study the more advanced techniques of their Order.

Although stronger enchantments await those who continue their training, Rank II is where Arcane Edge first begins to resemble the legendary weapon enchantment that made the Spellblade Orders famous across Asteria.

> *"Power without control burns the blade. Control allows the blade to burn the world."*
>
> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 3
### Description

With greater experience, the Spellblade no longer struggles to contain the immense flow of mana coursing through their weapon. Arcane Edge becomes a stable combat enchantment, allowing the Spellblade to maintain overwhelming magical pressure throughout prolonged engagements.

When activated, the weapon radiates a vibrant arcane aura as streams of mana race across its surface. Every strike lands with both physical force and concentrated magical energy, making each blow increasingly difficult to defend against. The weapon itself feels lighter in the Spellblade's hands, as if recognising the mana flowing through it and responding as a natural extension of its wielder.

Rank III is often regarded as the point where Arcane Edge becomes a signature combat technique rather than a simple enhancement. Spellblades capable of maintaining this level of enchantment are recognised as experienced warriors whose mastery of blade and mana has become second nature.

### Effects

- Spend **25 MP** to activate **Arcane Edge**.
- Arcane Edge remains active for **3 Turns**.
- While active, your melee weapon deals an additional **1d8 Arcane Damage** on each successful hit.
- The weapon emits a visible arcane glow while Arcane Edge is active.
- Requires a melee weapon to activate. This talent does not affect ranged weapons.
- Arcane Edge ends immediately if you are no longer wielding the infused weapon.
- Arcane Edge cannot be activated again while already active.

### Lore

Among the Spellblade Orders, Rank III is known as the **Awakened Edge**. It is the stage where apprentices cease relying on conscious effort to maintain the enchantment, allowing mana to flow naturally between themselves and their weapon.

Many renowned Spellblades have described this experience as hearing the blade "sing" for the first time. Whether this is merely poetic tradition or evidence of a deeper bond between mana and steel remains a topic of debate among arcane scholars.

Regardless of the explanation, every master agrees on one truth: once a Spellblade reaches this stage, their weapon is no longer simply carried into battle—it becomes an extension of their own soul.

> *"When your blade begins to answer your mana without hesitation, you no longer wield a weapon—you wield yourself."*
>
> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 4
### Description

The Spellblade has mastered the art of maintaining an Arcane Edge through even the fiercest battles. Mana now flows effortlessly from their core into their weapon, creating a stable enchantment that burns with immense arcane power. The weapon hums with energy, its edge glowing brightly as each strike releases concentrated bursts of magic.

Unlike earlier ranks, maintaining the enchantment no longer demands complete focus. The Spellblade has learned to weave mana into every movement, allowing them to fight naturally while their weapon remains empowered. Every swing reinforces the harmony between blade and magic, making the Spellblade an increasingly dangerous opponent in prolonged combat.

Rank IV is regarded as the hallmark of veteran Spellblades. The enchantment is no longer an experimental technique but a reliable extension of their combat style, capable of carrying them through the most demanding encounters. Few warriors can withstand a sustained assault from a Spellblade whose Arcane Edge has reached this level of refinement.

### Effects

- Spend **30 MP** to activate **Arcane Edge**.
- Arcane Edge remains active for **4 Turns**.
- While active, your melee weapon deals an additional **1d10 Arcane Damage** on each successful hit.
- The weapon emits a visible arcane glow while Arcane Edge is active.
- Requires a melee weapon to activate. This talent does not affect ranged weapons.
- Arcane Edge ends immediately if you are no longer wielding the infused weapon.
- Arcane Edge cannot be activated again while already active.

### Lore

Within the oldest Spellblade Orders, Rank IV is known as the **Veteran's Edge**. Reaching this stage signifies years of disciplined training and countless battles fought with blade and mana working as one.

Many master smiths claim they can recognise a veteran Spellblade simply by examining their weapon. Though Arcane Edge leaves no permanent enchantment behind, repeated infusions gradually leave faint traces of mana within the steel, a testament to the countless times it has served as a conduit for arcane power.

To fellow Spellblades, the sight of a brilliantly glowing blade is both inspiring and intimidating. It is a reminder that true mastery is not measured by the strength of a single strike, but by the ability to maintain perfect harmony between steel and sorcery throughout an entire battle.

> *"A true Spellblade does not borrow power from magic. They teach their blade to breathe it."*
>
> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 5
### Description

Arcane Edge reaches its ultimate form, becoming the signature technique of a true Spellblade Master. The flow of mana between wielder and weapon is now effortless, allowing the Spellblade to maintain an enchantment of extraordinary potency without interrupting the rhythm of combat. Every strike becomes a perfect union of martial precision and arcane might.

When activated, the weapon blazes with brilliant arcane light, its edge surrounded by streams of concentrated mana that shimmer with every movement. The enchantment resonates through the blade, releasing devastating bursts of magical energy with every successful strike. To those who witness it, the weapon appears less like forged steel and more like a living extension of the Spellblade's soul.

Few warriors ever achieve this level of mastery. Rank V is reserved for those who have devoted years to perfecting the harmony between magic and swordsmanship. Every swing demonstrates absolute control, proving that the greatest strength of a Spellblade is not their magic or their blade alone, but the flawless balance between the two.

### Effects

- Spend **35 MP** to activate **Arcane Edge**.
- Arcane Edge remains active for **8 Turns**.
- While active, your melee weapon deals an additional **1d12 Arcane Damage** on each successful hit.
- The weapon emits a visible arcane glow while Arcane Edge is active.
- Requires a melee weapon to activate. This talent does not affect ranged weapons.
- Arcane Edge ends immediately if you are no longer wielding the infused weapon.
- Arcane Edge cannot be activated again while already active.

### Lore

The ancient Spellblade Orders refer to this technique as the **Master's Edge**, the highest expression of their martial philosophy. It is said that the greatest Spellblades no longer distinguish between themselves, their mana, and their weapon. All three become one, moving with perfect unity in battle.

Legends tell of masters whose Arcane Edge shone so brightly that it illuminated entire battlefields, their enchanted blades carving through monsters, enchanted armour, and even powerful magical barriers. Whether these tales have grown through centuries of retelling is unknown, but they remain a source of inspiration for every aspiring Spellblade.

To achieve Rank V is to embody everything the Spellblade path represents. It is the culmination of countless hours of discipline, sacrifice, and unwavering dedication to mastering both steel and sorcery. Few will ever wield an Arcane Edge of this calibre, and fewer still will be remembered for doing so.

> *"When steel and mana can no longer be told apart, you have reached the edge where legends are born."*
>
> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well
