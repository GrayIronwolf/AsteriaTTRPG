# Arcane Reflex

## Overview
A Spellblade survives not by enduring every strike, but by avoiding the ones that matter.

Through training and repeated exposure to combat, the Spellblade learns to channel mana through their nervous system, briefly accelerating their reactions beyond normal limits. This surge of arcane energy allows them to read danger more quickly and adjust their position in the instant before an attack lands.

To outside observers, the movement appears insignificant—a slight shift of the shoulder, a subtle turn of the body, or a single step placed at precisely the right moment. Yet those tiny adjustments often mean the difference between a devastating blow and a harmless miss.

At Rank 1, Arcane Reflex represents the beginning of this discipline. The Spellblade learns to instinctively call upon mana when threatened, increasing their ability to evade incoming attacks.

## Rank 1
### Description

A Spellblade survives not by enduring every strike, but by avoiding the ones that matter.

Through training and repeated exposure to combat, the Spellblade learns to channel mana through their nervous system, briefly accelerating their reactions beyond normal limits. This surge of arcane energy allows them to read danger more quickly and adjust their position in the instant before an attack lands.

To outside observers, the movement appears insignificant—a slight shift of the shoulder, a subtle turn of the body, or a single step placed at precisely the right moment. Yet those tiny adjustments often mean the difference between a devastating blow and a harmless miss.

At Rank 1, Arcane Reflex represents the beginning of this discipline. The Spellblade learns to instinctively call upon mana when threatened, increasing their ability to evade incoming attacks.

### Effects

When you are targeted by an attack, you may activate Arcane Reflex as a Reaction.

- Spend **12 MP**.
- Gain **+1 AC** against the triggering attack.
- This bonus applies only to the attack that triggered the reaction.
- Does not protect against environmental hazards.
- Cannot be used while unable to react.
- Does not affect saving throws.

#### Timing

- Must be declared before the attack roll is resolved.
- May only be used once per turn.
- Uses an action from you next round.

### Lore

The earliest Spellblade instructors taught that survival begins with awareness. Before a warrior can master advanced techniques, they must first learn to recognise danger and respond to it without hesitation.

Arcane Reflex was developed as a means of enhancing this instinct through mana. Rather than creating barriers or relying on heavy armour, the Spellblade learns to trust their reactions and use magic to sharpen them.

Many apprentices underestimate the value of such a simple technique. Veteran Spellblades rarely make the same mistake. They understand that surviving a battle is often more important than winning a single exchange.

Among the older duelling schools, students are often told that every great Spellblade has one thing in common: they lived long enough to become one.

> "Victory belongs to those who survive long enough to claim it."
>
> — Blademaster Kaelis Vorn, Instructor of the Azure Path

## Rank 2
### Description

As the Spellblade's mastery of Arcane Reflex grows, they learn that avoidance is not always possible. Even the fastest reaction cannot prevent every strike from finding its mark.

Rather than relying solely on evasion, the Spellblade learns to reinforce their body with mana at the moment of impact. Arcane energy instinctively flows to vulnerable areas, lessening the force of incoming blows and reducing the harm suffered when an attack cannot be completely avoided.

This technique requires precise timing. Too early and mana is wasted. Too late and the attack lands with full force. Through constant training, the Spellblade develops the instinct to react at exactly the right moment.

At Rank 2, Arcane Reflex evolves from simple avoidance into a layered defensive technique. The Spellblade becomes harder to hit and more resilient when they are struck.

### Effects

When you are targeted by an attack, you may activate Arcane Reflex as a Reaction.

- Spend **15 MP**.
- Gain **+2 AC** against the triggering attack.
- This bonus applies only to the attack that triggered the reaction.
- Does not protect against environmental hazards.
- Cannot be used while unable to react.
- Does not affect saving throws.

#### Timing

- Must be declared before the attack roll is resolved.
- May only be used once per turn.
- Uses an action from you next round.

### Lore

Among veteran Spellblades, Rank 2 is often called the Lesson of Reality. It teaches a simple truth: perfection is impossible.

No matter how skilled a warrior becomes, there will always be attacks that cannot be completely avoided. The difference between a novice and a master is not whether they are hit, but how they respond when they are.

The arcane reinforcement techniques used by Spellblades were originally developed during the Age of Fractured Crowns, when battlefield mages sought ways to survive against heavily armoured opponents. Rather than matching strength with strength, they learned to redirect and disperse force through carefully controlled mana flows.

Modern Spellblades continue this tradition. Every successful use of Arcane Reflex strengthens the connection between instinct and magic, allowing them to survive situations that would cripple lesser combatants.

> "The greatest warriors are not those who never fall. They are those who learn how to fall without breaking."
>
> — High Spellblade Arkanis Thorne, Warden of the Azure Depths

## Rank 3
### Description

A seasoned Spellblade understands that combat is a balance between avoidance and endurance. While Arcane Reflex allows them to evade many attacks, experience teaches that not every strike can be escaped completely.

As their control over mana improves, the Spellblade learns to instinctively direct arcane energy to vulnerable areas of the body at the moment of impact. This reinforcement does not stop an attack, but it can lessen the force behind it and prevent potentially devastating injuries.

The process occurs almost subconsciously. The moment danger is recognised, mana surges through the body, sharpening reflexes while simultaneously preparing for the possibility of failure. If the attack misses, the Spellblade avoids harm entirely. If it lands, the mana absorbs part of the impact.

At Rank 3, Arcane Reflex becomes a complete defensive technique, providing both improved evasion and limited protection when avoidance alone is not enough.

### Effects

When you are targeted by an attack, you may activate Arcane Reflex as a Reaction.

- Spend **18 MP**.
- Gain **+3 AC** against the triggering attack.
- This bonus applies only to the attack that triggered the reaction.
- Does not protect against environmental hazards.
- Cannot be used while unable to react.
- Does not affect saving throws.

#### Timing

- Must be declared before the attack roll is resolved.
- May only be used once per turn.
- Uses your Reaction for the round.

### Lore

Among the ancient Spellblade Orders, Rank 3 is known as the Stage of Resilience. It marks the point where a practitioner learns that survival is not determined solely by avoiding danger, but by enduring it when necessary.

The earliest forms of Arcane Mitigation were developed during prolonged border wars where Spellblades often found themselves outnumbered and unable to evade every attack. By learning to reinforce their bodies with mana at the moment of impact, they dramatically improved their chances of surviving extended engagements.

Veteran instructors often describe this stage as a shift in mindset. Students stop viewing defense as a single action and begin understanding it as a layered system. First avoid the attack. If that fails, lessen the damage. If that fails, remain standing.

Many Spellblades consider Rank 3 the moment Arcane Reflex becomes truly reliable, transforming it from a useful trick into a cornerstone of battlefield survival.

> "The perfect defense is not avoiding every blow. It is surviving the ones you cannot."
>
> — Grand Spellblade Aethon Vale, Master of the Sapphire Hall

## Rank 4
### Description

Arcane Reflex evolves beyond simple defense and becomes a tool of battlefield control. The Spellblade no longer focuses solely on avoiding danger or enduring a strike—they learn to use every attack against them as an opportunity to reposition and reclaim the advantage.

Through years of combat experience, the Spellblade's reactions become intertwined with their mana flow. The moment an attack is launched, their body instinctively prepares not only to evade or mitigate the strike, but also to move into a more favorable position.

To observers, the movement appears unnatural. An attack is thrown, a flash of mana erupts around the Spellblade, and suddenly they are somewhere else entirely. What seemed like a successful attack becomes an opening for the Spellblade to exploit.

At Rank 4, Arcane Reflex transforms from a purely defensive talent into a tactical one. Surviving is no longer the goal—controlling the battlefield is.

### Effects

When you are targeted by an attack, you may activate Arcane Reflex as a Reaction.

- Spend **21 MP**.
- Gain **+4 AC** against the triggering attack.
- This bonus applies only to the triggering attack.
- Does not protect against environmental hazards.
- Cannot be used while unable to react.
- Does not affect saving throws.

#### Timing

- Must be declared before the attack roll is resolved.
- May only be used once per turn.
- Uses your Reaction for the round.

#### Arcane Mitigation

If the triggering attack still hits:

- Reduce the damage taken by **1d4**. Can not reduce damage to 0.

### Lore

Among veteran Spellblades, Rank 4 is known as the Stage of Control. At this point, practitioners stop reacting to the battlefield and begin shaping it.

The technique was refined during the Wars of the Shattered Frontier, where Spellblades frequently fought against numerically superior forces. Rather than remaining locked in place against overwhelming odds, they learned to transform enemy attacks into opportunities for movement.

Masters of Arcane Reflex often appear impossible to pin down. Every attack seems to place them exactly where they wish to be, turning an enemy's aggression into a tool for their own positioning.

Many young Spellblades believe Arcane Reflex exists to avoid damage. Experienced masters know the truth: avoiding damage is merely the beginning. The real purpose is deciding where the battle happens.

> "A master does not avoid danger. They decide where danger is allowed to exist."
>
> — High Spellblade Arkanis Thorne, Warden of the Azure Depths

## Rank 5
### Description

Arcane Reflex reaches its pinnacle, transforming the Spellblade's defensive instincts into a seamless blend of awareness, protection, and opportunity. At this stage, reacting to danger is no longer a conscious decision—it is an extension of the Spellblade's very nature.

The moment an attack is launched, mana surges through the body, enhancing perception, reinforcing vulnerable areas, and preparing a path of escape before the threat has fully materialised. Every motion becomes precise, efficient, and deliberate.

What separates masters from apprentices is not merely their ability to survive an attack, but their ability to turn that survival into an advantage. A failed strike against a master Spellblade often becomes the first step toward an enemy's defeat.

At Rank 5, Arcane Reflex no longer exists solely to prevent harm. It allows the Spellblade to seize control of the battle's momentum and create openings where none previously existed.

### Effects

When you are targeted by an attack, you may activate Arcane Reflex as a Reaction.

- Spend **24 MP**.
- Gain **+4 AC** against the triggering attack.
- This bonus applies only to the triggering attack.
- Does not protect against environmental hazards.
- Cannot be used while unable to react.
- Does not affect saving throws.

#### Timing

- Must be declared before the attack roll is resolved.
- May only be used once per turn.
- Uses your Reaction for the round.

#### Arcane Mitigation

If the triggering attack still hits:

- Reduce the damage taken by **1d6**.

#### Arcane Reposition

After the triggering attack resolves, whether it hits or misses:

- Instantly move up to **3 metres** to an unoccupied space you can see.
- This movement does **not** provoke Opportunity Attacks.
- You may move through enemy spaces but cannot end your movement inside one.
- You cannot move through solid walls, magical barriers, or impassable terrain.

#### Tactical Uses

Arcane Reposition may be used to:

- Break enemy engagement.
- Move behind cover.
- Reposition for your next attack.
- Escape hazardous terrain.
- Create distance from dangerous opponents.

### Lore

The oldest Spellblade records describe Rank 5 as the Stage of Anticipation. Practitioners no longer react to attacks in the traditional sense. Instead, they appear to move before danger fully manifests, as though they instinctively understand the shape of the battle moments before it unfolds.

Stories of legendary Spellblades often speak of warriors who seemed untouchable in combat. Opponents would launch strike after strike, only to find their target already gone, standing elsewhere with blade in hand and a counterattack prepared.

Scholars debate whether this level of Arcane Reflex is simply the result of extraordinary training or whether prolonged exposure to mana grants fleeting glimpses of future possibilities. Most Spellblades dismiss such theories, claiming that experience and discipline are sufficient explanations.

Whatever the truth, masters of Arcane Reflex are feared throughout Asteria. Every missed attack places them in a stronger position than before, turning an enemy's aggression into the very tool that leads to their downfall.

> "The novice reacts to danger. The master has already moved."
>
> — Grandmaster Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well
