# Arcane Rush

## Overview
The Spellblade learns to channel mana directly into movement, allowing them to cross short distances with unnatural speed. Rather than relying purely on physical strength, they infuse their body with arcane energy, creating a sudden burst of acceleration that carries them across the battlefield in the blink of an eye.

To an observer, the movement appears almost impossible to follow. One moment the Spellblade stands still, and the next they have surged forward, leaving only a faint trail of arcane energy in their wake.

At Rank 1, Arcane Rush is a simple but highly effective mobility technique. It allows the Spellblade to rapidly reposition, close the distance to an enemy, escape danger, or reach advantageous terrain without relying on conventional movement.

While the technique lacks the sophistication of higher ranks, it establishes the foundation upon which future mobility techniques will be built.

## Rank 1
### Description

The Spellblade learns to channel mana directly into movement, allowing them to cross short distances with unnatural speed. Rather than relying purely on physical strength, they infuse their body with arcane energy, creating a sudden burst of acceleration that carries them across the battlefield in the blink of an eye.

To an observer, the movement appears almost impossible to follow. One moment the Spellblade stands still, and the next they have surged forward, leaving only a faint trail of arcane energy in their wake.

At Rank 1, Arcane Rush is a simple but highly effective mobility technique. It allows the Spellblade to rapidly reposition, close the distance to an enemy, escape danger, or reach advantageous terrain without relying on conventional movement.

While the technique lacks the sophistication of higher ranks, it establishes the foundation upon which future mobility techniques will be built.

### Effects

- Spend **12 MP** to activate Arcane Rush.
- Instantly move up to **4 metres** to a visible location.
- This movement does not provoke Opportunity Attacks.
- Arcane Rush may be used to:
  - Engage enemies.
  - Escape melee range.
  - Reposition around obstacles.
  - Reach advantageous terrain.
- Does not grant additional attacks.
- Does not increase movement speed outside of activation.

#### Movement Rules

- The destination must be within line of sight.
- You may move through enemy spaces during the rush.
- You cannot activate if you are engaged with an enemy.
- You cannot pass through solid walls, magical barriers, or completely blocked terrain.
- You must end the movement in a valid unoccupied space.

### Lore

Among Spellblades, Arcane Rush is often the first mobility technique taught after basic mana control. The ability demonstrates one of the core principles of the class: mana is not merely used for casting spells, but can be woven into every aspect of combat.

Veteran instructors frequently encourage students to master Arcane Rush before pursuing more advanced techniques. Proper positioning often determines the outcome of a battle long before the first strike is made.

Many duellists describe their first successful Arcane Rush as a transformative experience. For a brief moment, distance ceases to feel absolute, and the battlefield becomes something that can be navigated rather than endured.

Though simple in execution, countless Spellblades have survived impossible situations thanks to a well-timed Arcane Rush.

> "The quickest path is not always the shortest. Sometimes it is the one shaped by mana."
>
> — Blademaster Kaelis Vorn, Instructor of the Azure Path

## Rank 2
### Description

As the Spellblade's mastery of Arcane Rush grows, they learn to channel mana through their body more efficiently, allowing them to travel greater distances with each burst of arcane acceleration.

The technique becomes smoother and more controlled. Rather than forcing mana through the body in a sudden surge, the Spellblade learns to guide it naturally through their movements, extending both the range and reliability of the rush.

At Rank 2, Arcane Rush remains a mobility technique first and foremost. The Spellblade becomes capable of covering greater distances across the battlefield, allowing them to engage enemies, reposition, or escape danger with increased effectiveness.

Experienced Spellblades often describe this stage as the point where Arcane Rush begins to feel natural rather than forced, becoming a regular part of their combat style rather than a technique used only in emergencies.

### Lore

Among seasoned Spellblades, Rank 2 is often called the stage of refinement. The practitioner has moved beyond simply forcing mana into motion and begins to understand how arcane energy can enhance movement without overwhelming the body.

Many instructors encourage students to use Arcane Rush repeatedly during training exercises, not to increase speed, but to improve control. A rushed movement may reach its destination, but a controlled movement arrives exactly where it is needed.

Those who master this stage often find themselves viewing the battlefield differently. Obstacles become opportunities, distance becomes less intimidating, and positioning becomes a weapon in its own right.

> "The first rush teaches movement. The second teaches control."
>
> — Blademaster Kaelis Vorn, Instructor of the Azure Path

## Rank 3
### Description

The Spellblade's command over Arcane Rush reaches a level where distance begins to lose its meaning. Mana flows effortlessly through their body, carrying them across the battlefield with remarkable speed and precision.

What once felt like a burst of movement now feels like a natural extension of combat itself. The Spellblade no longer thinks about where they are standing, but where they need to be. Every engagement, retreat, and reposition becomes smoother as the technique evolves.

At Rank 3, Arcane Rush allows the Spellblade to traverse significant distances in an instant, making them increasingly difficult to pin down. Enemies who rely on controlling positioning often find themselves struggling to keep pace with a practitioner capable of appearing where they are least expected.

Many veterans consider this the stage where Arcane Rush transforms from a useful technique into a defining part of the Spellblade's combat identity.

  - Bypass enemy frontlines.
- Does not grant additional attacks.
- Does not provide offensive bonuses at this rank.

#### Movement Rules

- The destination must be within line of sight.
- You may move through enemy spaces during the rush.
- You may ignore difficult terrain during the movement.
- You cannot pass through solid walls, magical barriers, or completely blocked terrain.
- You must end the movement in a valid unoccupied space.

### Lore

Among experienced Spellblades, Rank 3 is often referred to as the Path Between Moments. Practitioners describe a strange sensation during movement, as though the distance travelled becomes shorter than it should be.

Some scholars argue that Arcane Rush does not truly increase speed, but instead allows the Spellblade to briefly slip through the spaces between mana currents that flow throughout the world. Whether this theory is true remains the subject of debate among arcane researchers.

What is certain is that those who master this stage become exceptionally difficult to contain. Opponents learn quickly that distance offers little protection against a Spellblade capable of crossing the battlefield in the blink of an eye.

Many legendary duels have been decided by a single perfectly timed Arcane Rush, carrying the practitioner to exactly the right place at exactly the right moment.

> "The battlefield belongs to the one who decides where the fight happens."
>
> — High Spellblade Arkanis Thorne, Warden of the Azure Depths

## Rank 4
### Description

The Spellblade has mastered the fundamentals of Arcane Rush, allowing them to move across the battlefield with extraordinary speed and precision. What was once a method of repositioning has evolved into a technique that directly influences combat itself.

Mana now surges through the Spellblade's body with such efficiency that the momentum of the rush can be carried into their next action. The transition between movement and attack becomes seamless, creating openings that slower opponents struggle to react to.

At Rank 4, Arcane Rush no longer serves only as a mobility tool. The sudden acceleration and perfect positioning generated by the technique provide the Spellblade with a brief but valuable combat advantage immediately after arriving at their destination.

Veteran Spellblades often describe this stage as the point where movement and combat become one. Every rush becomes part of an attack, and every attack begins with movement.

### Effects

- Spend **21 MP** to activate Arcane Rush.
- Instantly move up to **12 metres** to a visible location.
- This movement does not provoke Opportunity Attacks.
- Arcane Rush may be used to:
  - Engage distant enemies.
  - Escape melee range.
  - Reposition around obstacles.
  - Reach advantageous terrain.
  - Bypass enemy frontlines.
- Does not grant additional attacks.
- Does not provide offensive bonuses at this rank.

#### Arcane Momentum

After completing Arcane Rush:

- Your next attack before the end of your turn gains:
  - **+1 to Hit**

The bonus is consumed whether the attack hits or misses.

#### Movement Rules

- The destination must be within line of sight.
- You may move through enemy spaces during the rush.
- You may ignore difficult terrain during the movement.
- You cannot pass through solid walls, magical barriers, or completely blocked terrain.
- You must end the movement in a valid unoccupied space.

### Lore

Among the great Spellblade Orders, Rank 4 is often called the Stage of Momentum. At this point, practitioners no longer think of movement and combat as separate disciplines. Every reposition becomes an opportunity, and every opportunity becomes a potential strike.

Masters frequently train their students to attack immediately after an Arcane Rush, teaching them to exploit the brief confusion created by their sudden relocation. To outside observers, the technique can appear almost unfair, as the Spellblade seems to arrive already prepared to attack.

Historical accounts describe veteran Spellblades using Arcane Rush to cross entire battle lines before striking enemy commanders, mages, or siege crews. Their ability to appear where they were least expected often proved more dangerous than the attacks themselves.

Many duelists spend years attempting to predict where a Spellblade will move next. Most eventually learn the same lesson: by the time you see the rush, it is already too late.

> "The greatest advantage is not speed. It is arriving before your enemy understands that you have moved."
>
> — Grand Spellblade Aethon Vale, Master of the Sapphire Hall

## Rank 5
### Description

Arcane Rush reaches its ultimate form, transforming the Spellblade into a blur of motion and arcane energy. Distance becomes little more than a suggestion as the practitioner moves across the battlefield with breathtaking speed and precision.

The mana flowing through the Spellblade no longer merely enhances movement—it becomes movement. Every rush is executed with absolute confidence, carrying the Spellblade exactly where they need to be while creating the perfect opportunity for a decisive strike.

At Rank 5, Arcane Rush embodies one of the core principles of the Spellblade path: superior positioning creates superior opportunities. The Spellblade can appear where they are least expected, exploit weaknesses in enemy formations, and strike before opponents can properly react.

Among Spellblades, reaching this rank is considered a mark of mastery. Few possess the control, experience, and arcane endurance necessary to wield Arcane Rush at its highest level.

### Effects

- Spend **24 MP** to activate Arcane Rush.
- Instantly move up to **15 metres** to a visible location.
- This movement does not provoke Opportunity Attacks.
- Arcane Rush may be used to:
  - Engage distant enemies.
  - Escape melee range.
  - Reposition around obstacles.
  - Reach advantageous terrain.
  - Bypass enemy frontlines.
  - Rapidly relocate across the battlefield.
- Does not grant additional attacks.
- Does not provide offensive bonuses at this rank.

#### Arcane Momentum

After completing Arcane Rush:

- Your next attack before the end of your turn gains:
  - **+2 to Hit**
  - **+1d4 Arcane Damage**

The bonus is consumed whether the attack hits or misses.

#### Movement Rules

- The destination must be within line of sight.
- You may move through enemy spaces during the rush.
- You may ignore difficult terrain during the movement.
- You cannot pass through solid walls, magical barriers, or completely blocked terrain.
- You must end the movement in a valid unoccupied space.

### Lore

The highest masters of Arcane Rush are often spoken of in legends and battlefield tales. Witnesses describe them as appearing and disappearing across the battlefield so quickly that it becomes difficult to determine where they truly are.

Military historians frequently record accounts of elite Spellblades crossing entire engagements to strike critical targets before defenders could react. Commanders learned to fear the sudden appearance of a Spellblade more than the approach of an army.

Among the Azure Orders, Rank 5 is known as the Horizon Step. The name comes from the belief that a true master sees no meaningful difference between where they stand and where they wish to be. Distance becomes an obstacle that can simply be ignored.

Those who achieve this rank have mastered more than movement. They have mastered opportunity itself, arriving precisely where fate demands and striking at the moment their enemies are least prepared.

> "The battlefield is not measured in distance. It is measured in opportunities. Arcane Rush simply ensures I reach them first."
>
> — Grandmaster Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well
