# Arcane Guard

## Overview
Spellblades understand that not every threat can be avoided through speed alone. Against hostile magic, they learn to shape mana into a protective barrier that surrounds their body, disrupting incoming spells before they can fully take hold.

Unlike Arcane Reflex, which relies upon agility and precise movement, Arcane Guard stands firm against magical assault. The Spellblade condenses raw mana into a shimmering defensive aura that absorbs and weakens hostile arcane energies, allowing them to endure attacks that would overwhelm lesser warriors.

At Rank I, the barrier is temporary and can only withstand modest magical punishment. Even so, it provides invaluable protection against enemy spellcasters, allowing the Spellblade to remain in the fight while closing the distance to their greatest threat.

## Rank 1
### Description

Spellblades understand that not every threat can be avoided through speed alone. Against hostile magic, they learn to shape mana into a protective barrier that surrounds their body, disrupting incoming spells before they can fully take hold.

Unlike Arcane Reflex, which relies upon agility and precise movement, Arcane Guard stands firm against magical assault. The Spellblade condenses raw mana into a shimmering defensive aura that absorbs and weakens hostile arcane energies, allowing them to endure attacks that would overwhelm lesser warriors.

At Rank I, the barrier is temporary and can only withstand modest magical punishment. Even so, it provides invaluable protection against enemy spellcasters, allowing the Spellblade to remain in the fight while closing the distance to their greatest threat.

### Effects

- Spend **20 MP** to activate **Arcane Guard**.
- Arcane Guard remains active for **3 Turns**.
- While active, gain **+2 on all Saving Throws against spells and magical abilities.**
- Reduce all magical damage you receive by **1d4**.
- Arcane Guard only protects the Spellblade.
- Arcane Guard cannot be activated again while already active.
- This talent does not reduce damage from non-magical attacks.

### Lore

Arcane Guard was developed after early Spellblades discovered that their greatest weakness was enemy spellcasters. While heavily armoured warriors could withstand blades and arrows, concentrated magical attacks often proved devastating before a Spellblade could close the distance.

Rather than abandoning their offensive style, the founders created a technique that allowed mana to defend as effectively as it could destroy. By weaving a protective current around their bodies, they learned to endure magical assaults long enough to bring the battle back into melee range.

Today, Arcane Guard is considered an essential technique within every Spellblade Order. Apprentices are taught that a master Spellblade should fear neither steel nor sorcery, for both can be answered with disciplined control over mana.

> *"Steel may break against armour, but magic breaks against a disciplined mind."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 2
### Description

As a Spellblade's understanding of defensive magic deepens, Arcane Guard evolves from a simple protective barrier into a resilient mantle of arcane energy. The flow of mana surrounding the Spellblade becomes steadier and more efficient, allowing it to intercept a greater amount of hostile magic without disrupting the warrior's rhythm in combat.

The barrier pulses in harmony with the Spellblade's mana core, weakening incoming spells before they fully manifest. Fire burns colder, lightning loses its bite, and curses struggle to take hold as the guard disperses part of the magical force before it reaches its target.

Rank II marks the beginning of true magical resilience. Veteran Spellblades rely on Arcane Guard to push through barrages of hostile magic, trusting their barrier to carry them safely into melee where their enemies are at their weakest.

### Effects

- Spend **25 MP** to activate **Arcane Guard**.
- Arcane Guard remains active for **4 Turns**.
- While active, gain **+3 on all Saving Throws against magical effects.**
- Reduce all magical damage you receive by **1d6**.
- Arcane Guard only protects the Spellblade.
- Arcane Guard cannot be activated again while already active.
- This rank replaces the effects of **Arcane Guard — Rank I**.

### Lore

Within the Spellblade Orders, Rank II is often called the **Warden's Mantle**. At this stage, apprentices stop fearing enemy spellcasters and begin treating hostile magic as another obstacle to overcome rather than an impossible threat.

Masters teach that Arcane Guard should never be relied upon to make a Spellblade invincible. Instead, it exists to buy precious moments—enough time to survive a devastating spell, close the distance, and bring the battle back to the edge of a blade.

Many famous Spellblades earned their reputations not by avoiding powerful magic, but by walking calmly through it. Their unwavering confidence inspired allies and unsettled enemy mages who watched their greatest spells lose much of their power against the shimmering barrier.

> *"Magic is only frightening until you learn to stand within the storm."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 3
### Description

Arcane Guard has become an instinctive defense, allowing the Spellblade to withstand powerful magical assaults without breaking their offensive momentum. The protective aura surrounding their body strengthens into a dense layer of mana that instinctively intercepts hostile magical energy before it reaches its target.

Rather than simply absorbing spells, the barrier disrupts and weakens the arcane structure holding them together. Flames lose their intensity, bolts of lightning fracture upon impact, and curses struggle to bind themselves to the Spellblade's soul. Although the barrier cannot completely negate powerful magic, it significantly reduces its effectiveness.

Rank III represents a turning point in a Spellblade's training. Having mastered both martial combat and magical resilience, they are capable of advancing directly into enemy spellcasters, trusting Arcane Guard to shield them while they close the distance.

### Effects

- Spend **30 MP** to activate **Arcane Guard**.
- Arcane Guard remains active for **5 Turns**.
- While active, gain **+4 on all Saving Throws against magical effects.**
- Reduce all magical damage you receive by **1d8**.
- Arcane Guard only protects the Spellblade.
- Arcane Guard cannot be activated again while already active.
- This rank replaces the effects of **Arcane Guard — Rank II**.

### Lore

Spellblade Masters refer to Rank III as the **Arcane Bastion**, a milestone where the barrier begins to function as naturally as breathing. The Spellblade no longer consciously maintains the ward; instead, their mana instinctively responds whenever hostile magic threatens them.

Throughout Asteria's history, many legendary Spellblades have survived encounters with powerful archmages through Arcane Guard alone. Rather than attempting to overpower devastating spells, they relied upon discipline, patience, and unwavering control of their own mana until they stood within striking distance.

Among the Orders, reaching Rank III is considered proof that a Spellblade has learned one of their greatest lessons: magic is not only a weapon to command, but a force that can be resisted through mastery and resolve.

> *"The strongest shield is not forged from steel, but from the unwavering will to endure."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 4
### Description

Arcane Guard has become a masterpiece of defensive spellcraft, allowing the Spellblade to stand firm against even the most destructive magical assaults. The protective barrier no longer simply absorbs hostile mana—it actively disrupts and disperses incoming magical energy before it can fully manifest.

When activated, shimmering currents of arcane energy envelop the Spellblade, constantly shifting to intercept hostile spells. Every wave of magical force that collides with the barrier is weakened as the guard redirects unstable mana harmlessly into the surrounding air. Veteran Spellblades often walk directly through magical bombardments, trusting their Arcane Guard to protect them while they close the distance to their prey.

Rank IV is regarded as the hallmark of an elite Spellblade. Those who achieve this level are feared by enemy mages, for they can withstand magical punishment that would overwhelm most warriors and continue advancing without hesitation.

### Effects

- Spend **35 MP** to activate **Arcane Guard**.
- Arcane Guard remains active for **6 Turns**.
- While active, gain **+5 on all Saving Throws against magical effects**.
- Reduce all magical damage you receive by **1d10**.
- Arcane Guard only protects the Spellblade.
- Arcane Guard cannot be activated again while already active.
- This rank replaces the effects of **Arcane Guard — Rank III**.

### Lore

Within the great Spellblade Orders, Rank IV is known as the **Magebreaker Ward**. It represents the point where enemy spellcasters begin to view the Spellblade not as a warrior to defeat, but as an unstoppable force that refuses to yield before arcane power.

Ancient battle records describe elite Spellblades advancing through storms of fire, lightning, and shadow while their shimmering wards steadily unravelled each spell before it could inflict its full devastation. Their resilience became legendary, inspiring allies while sowing doubt among even the most accomplished mages.

Masters teach that Arcane Guard is not about becoming immune to magic. It is about possessing the courage and discipline to continue advancing when every instinct tells others to retreat.

> *"The greatest victory over magic is not to destroy it, but to walk through it without fear."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 5
### Description

Arcane Guard reaches its ultimate form, surrounding the Spellblade in an almost impenetrable mantle of arcane energy. The barrier no longer merely resists hostile magic—it instinctively unravels and disperses magical attacks before they can reach their full potential. To enemy spellcasters, striking a master Spellblade feels like casting into a storm that tears their spells apart.

The barrier pulses in perfect harmony with the Spellblade's mana core, reinforcing itself with every passing moment. Even the most devastating magical assaults lose much of their strength as the guard absorbs, redirects, and dissipates hostile arcane energy. While no defence is absolute, Arcane Guard allows the Spellblade to endure magical punishment that few mortals could survive.

Only the greatest Spellblades achieve this level of mastery. They are renowned as Magebreakers, warriors who charge fearlessly into the heart of magical battlefields, trusting their discipline and mana to shield them from destruction.

### Effects

- Spend **40 MP** to activate **Arcane Guard**.
- Arcane Guard remains active for **8 Turns**.
- While active, gain **+6 on all Saving Throws against magical effects**.
- Reduce all magical damage you receive by **1d12**.
- The first magical effect each round that would inflict a negative status effect on you is made with **Disadvantage** (or the caster suffers a penalty, depending on the effect's rules).
- Arcane Guard only protects the Spellblade.
- Arcane Guard cannot be activated again while already active.
- This rank replaces the effects of **Arcane Guard — Rank IV**.

### Lore

Within the oldest Spellblade Orders, Rank V is known as the **Aegis of the Arcane**, a title reserved for masters whose command of defensive mana borders on legendary. They have learned that true protection is not created through overwhelming power, but through absolute control over the flow of magic itself.

Stories tell of Spellblades who walked unharmed through storms of fire, shattered curses with sheer force of will, and stood against archmages without yielding a single step. Whether these tales have grown through centuries of retelling is unknown, but they remain the ideal every apprentice strives to achieve.

To become a master of Arcane Guard is to embody one of the Spellblade's greatest philosophies: magic is neither feared nor worshipped—it is understood, mastered, and ultimately turned aside by unwavering discipline.

> *"The greatest shield is not forged from steel or stone. It is forged from a will that refuses to yield before magic."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well
