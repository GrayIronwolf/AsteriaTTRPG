# Arcane Tempo

## Overview
A Spellblade's greatest strength is not their sword or their magic, but the rhythm between the two. Arcane Tempo teaches the practitioner to seamlessly flow from spellcasting into swordplay, then back again, creating an ever-changing dance of steel and mana that keeps opponents constantly off balance.

Upon activating Arcane Tempo, mana courses through the Spellblade's body, synchronising every movement with their spellcasting. Each successful spell naturally leads into a more precise weapon strike, while every successful strike prepares the mind and body to cast with greater focus. The technique rewards versatility rather than repetition, encouraging the Spellblade to weave martial combat and magic together as a single discipline.

At Rank I, the rhythm is still fragile and easily broken, but even novice Spellblades quickly discover that maintaining their Tempo allows them to fight with greater efficiency than relying on blade or spell alone.

## Rank 1
### Description

A Spellblade's greatest strength is not their sword or their magic, but the rhythm between the two. Arcane Tempo teaches the practitioner to seamlessly flow from spellcasting into swordplay, then back again, creating an ever-changing dance of steel and mana that keeps opponents constantly off balance.

Upon activating Arcane Tempo, mana courses through the Spellblade's body, synchronising every movement with their spellcasting. Each successful spell naturally leads into a more precise weapon strike, while every successful strike prepares the mind and body to cast with greater focus. The technique rewards versatility rather than repetition, encouraging the Spellblade to weave martial combat and magic together as a single discipline.

At Rank I, the rhythm is still fragile and easily broken, but even novice Spellblades quickly discover that maintaining their Tempo allows them to fight with greater efficiency than relying on blade or spell alone.

### Effects

- Spend **20 MP** to activate **Arcane Tempo**.
- Arcane Tempo remains active for **5 Turns**.
- After successfully casting a spell, your **next melee weapon attack** gains **+1 to its Attack Roll**.
- After successfully hitting with a melee weapon attack, your **next spell** gains **+1 to its Spell Attack Roll** or **Spell Save DC** (whichever is applicable).
- Each bonus is consumed after it is applied.
- Arcane Tempo only functions while alternating between spellcasting and melee weapon attacks.
- Repeating the same action (casting a spell after a spell, or attacking after an attack) does not generate a new bonus.

### Lore

Among the Spellblade Orders, Arcane Tempo is often called the **Rhythm of Battle**. Masters teach that a true Spellblade should never become predictable. Every spell should naturally flow into a strike, and every strike should prepare the next spell, creating a combat style that is fluid, relentless, and impossible to anticipate.

Many apprentices struggle with this lesson, instinctively favouring either magic or swordsmanship. Only through discipline do they learn that the greatest strength lies not in choosing one path, but in mastering the rhythm that unites them.

> *"Do not think of casting, then striking. Think only of the rhythm, and both will become one."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 2
### Description

The Spellblade's understanding of Arcane Tempo deepens, allowing the rhythm between sword and spell to become far more natural. No longer forcing the transition between martial and magical techniques, the Spellblade begins to move instinctively, allowing each action to flow seamlessly into the next.

Mana circulates continuously throughout the Spellblade's body, synchronising every strike and every spell into a single combat rhythm. As this tempo builds, attacks become more accurate and spells are woven with greater precision, making it increasingly difficult for opponents to predict what will come next.

At Rank II, maintaining the rhythm becomes easier and more rewarding. Experienced Spellblades understand that every successful transition strengthens the flow of battle, while hesitation or repetition causes the tempo to falter. Those who master this balance become exceptionally adaptable, shifting effortlessly between blade and sorcery as the battle unfolds.

### Effects

- Spend **25 MP** to activate **Arcane Tempo**.
- Arcane Tempo remains active for **6 Turns**.
- After successfully casting a spell, your **next melee weapon attack** gains **+2 to its Attack Roll**.
- After successfully hitting with a melee weapon attack, your **next spell** gains **+2 to its Spell Attack Roll** or **Spell Save DC** (whichever is applicable).
- Each bonus is consumed after it is applied.
- Arcane Tempo only functions while alternating between spellcasting and melee weapon attacks.
- Repeating the same action (casting a spell after a spell, or attacking after an attack) does not generate a new bonus.
- This rank replaces the effects of **Arcane Tempo — Rank I**.

### Lore

Within the Spellblade Orders, Rank II is known as the **Flowing Rhythm**, where combat begins to resemble a carefully choreographed dance rather than a series of separate actions. Masters teach that the blade should prepare the spell, and the spell should prepare the blade, creating a cycle that never truly ends.

Many apprentices discover that maintaining Arcane Tempo requires discipline rather than speed. A reckless warrior quickly loses their rhythm, while a patient Spellblade allows every action to naturally lead into the next. This philosophy becomes the foundation upon which all advanced Spellblade techniques are built.

> *"A true rhythm cannot be rushed. Every strike has a spell waiting behind it, and every spell already knows where the next strike will fall."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 3
### Description

The Spellblade has begun to master the rhythm between blade and spell, maintaining a continuous flow of martial and arcane techniques throughout combat. Every successful transition strengthens their momentum, allowing attacks to become more precise while spells are cast with unwavering confidence.

Mana pulses through the Spellblade in perfect harmony with each movement, linking every strike and spell into a single, uninterrupted sequence. Rather than viewing swordsmanship and spellcasting as separate disciplines, the Spellblade embraces them as two halves of the same combat style. Opponents quickly find themselves overwhelmed by the relentless change in tempo, unable to predict whether the next attack will be steel or sorcery.

Rank III represents the point where Arcane Tempo becomes instinctive. The Spellblade no longer needs to consciously maintain the rhythm—it simply becomes part of the way they fight.

### Effects

- Spend **30 MP** to activate **Arcane Tempo**.
- Arcane Tempo remains active for **7 Turns**.
- After successfully casting a spell, your **next melee weapon attack** gains **+3 to its Attack Roll**.
- After successfully hitting with a melee weapon attack, your **next spell** gains **+3 to its Spell Attack Roll** or **Spell Save DC** (whichever is applicable).
- Each bonus is consumed after it is applied.
- Arcane Tempo only functions while alternating between spellcasting and melee weapon attacks.
- Repeating the same action (casting a spell after a spell, or attacking after an attack) does not generate a new bonus.
- This rank replaces the effects of **Arcane Tempo — Rank II**.

### Lore

Among veteran Spellblades, Rank III is known as the **Battle Rhythm**. Reaching this stage signifies that the practitioner has stopped thinking about when to attack or when to cast. Instead, every action naturally leads into the next, creating a seamless dance of steel and mana.

Masters often describe watching an accomplished Spellblade as listening to a symphony. Every spell, every strike, and every movement flows together with perfect timing, leaving opponents unable to anticipate the next beat. It is this unpredictable rhythm that has earned the Spellblade their reputation as one of Asteria's most versatile warriors.

> *"The finest battles are never won through strength alone, but through perfect rhythm. When steel and sorcery share the same heartbeat, victory follows."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 4
### Description

The Spellblade's rhythm has become unwavering, allowing them to recover from mistakes without losing the flow of battle. Where lesser practitioners falter after a missed strike or disrupted spell, a master of Arcane Tempo adapts instantly, seamlessly continuing the dance between steel and sorcery.

Every movement is guided by instinct. Mana circulates effortlessly throughout the Spellblade's body, ensuring that every spell naturally transitions into a weapon strike and every successful strike prepares the next spell. Even when an attack fails to find its mark, the Spellblade's rhythm remains intact, denying their opponent the opportunity to disrupt their momentum.

Rank IV represents the mastery of consistency. The Spellblade no longer relies upon perfect execution to maintain their Tempo, making them an exceptionally difficult opponent to slow or interrupt.

### Effects

- Spend **35 MP** to activate **Arcane Tempo**.
- Arcane Tempo remains active for **8 Turns**.
- After successfully casting a spell, your **next melee weapon attack** gains **+4 to its Attack Roll**.
- After successfully hitting with a melee weapon attack, your **next spell** gains **+4 to its Spell Attack Roll** or **Spell Save DC** (whichever is applicable).
- Each bonus is consumed after it is applied.
- If your empowered melee attack misses, the bonus is retained until your next melee weapon attack.
- If your empowered spell fails to hit or the target succeeds on its saving throw, the bonus is retained until your next spell.
- Arcane Tempo only functions while alternating between spellcasting and melee weapon attacks.
- This rank replaces the effects of **Arcane Tempo — Rank III**.

### Lore

Spellblade Masters call this stage the **Endless Rhythm**, for the flow of battle can no longer be broken by simple mistakes. The practitioner has learned that true mastery is measured not by perfection, but by the ability to recover instantly and continue fighting without hesitation.

Veterans often compare Arcane Tempo to the beating of a heart. It does not stop because one step is misplaced or one strike goes astray. Instead, it continues without interruption, carrying the Spellblade forward until the battle is won.

To witness a master of Arcane Tempo is to see steel and sorcery move with absolute harmony, every action flowing effortlessly into the next without pause.

> *"Perfection is not never stumbling. Perfection is never losing the rhythm when you do."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 5
### Description

Arcane Tempo reaches its highest form, becoming more than a combat technique—it becomes the natural rhythm of the Spellblade's soul. Every spell seamlessly transitions into a strike, and every strike naturally prepares the next spell. The distinction between martial skill and spellcasting disappears entirely, replaced by a flawless cycle of steel and sorcery.

The Spellblade no longer fights by making individual decisions. Instead, they surrender to the flow of battle itself, allowing instinct and discipline to guide every movement. Opponents struggle to keep pace as the Spellblade effortlessly shifts between devastating spells and precise weapon strikes, maintaining relentless pressure without ever losing momentum.

Masters of Arcane Tempo are recognised throughout Asteria as living embodiments of the Spellblade philosophy. To face one is to battle a warrior whose every action strengthens the next, creating an unbroken chain of perfectly timed attacks and spells.

### Effects

- Spend **40 MP** to activate **Arcane Tempo**.
- Arcane Tempo remains active for **10 Turns**.
- After successfully casting a spell, your **next melee weapon attack** gains **+5 to its Attack Roll**.
- After successfully hitting with a melee weapon attack, your **next spell** gains **+5 to its Spell Attack Roll** or **Spell Save DC** (whichever is applicable).
- Each bonus is consumed after it is applied.
- If your empowered melee attack misses, the bonus is retained until your next melee weapon attack.
- If your empowered spell fails to hit or the target succeeds on its saving throw, the bonus is retained until your next spell.
- Each time you successfully alternate between casting a spell and landing a melee weapon attack, **extend Arcane Tempo's remaining duration by 1 Turn**, up to its original maximum duration.
- This rank replaces the effects of **Arcane Tempo — Rank IV**.

### Lore

Within the oldest Spellblade Orders, Rank V is known as the **Perfect Tempo**. It is said that those who achieve this level no longer think about whether to cast or strike, for both actions have become a single expression of their will.

Legend speaks of masters who fought for entire battles without ever breaking their rhythm, maintaining an endless cycle of blade and magic that left their opponents unable to predict or interrupt their relentless assault. Every successful transition renewed their momentum, allowing them to dictate the pace of the fight until victory was inevitable.

To master Arcane Tempo is to understand the heart of the Spellblade. Steel and sorcery are not separate disciplines, but two notes within the same song.

> *"Do not master the blade. Do not master the spell. Master the rhythm between them, and both will follow."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well
