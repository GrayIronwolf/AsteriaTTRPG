# Mana Infusion

## Overview
A Spellblade soon learns that victory is not determined by how much mana they possess, but by how efficiently they can recover it. Through Mana Infusion, the Spellblade floods their body with arcane energy, allowing every successful strike to draw lingering magical essence from the battlefield and recycle it back into themselves.

While Arcane Edge channels mana into a weapon, Mana Infusion channels mana into the Spellblade's body. Every swing becomes part of a continuous cycle—mana is spent to enhance their abilities, then partially recovered through the rhythm of combat. The harder the Spellblade presses the attack, the longer they can maintain their momentum.

At Rank I, the infusion is still unstable, allowing only a small amount of mana and stamina to be reclaimed. Even so, this technique marks the beginning of the Spellblade's mastery over sustained combat, rewarding those who remain aggressive without becoming reckless.

## Rank 1
### Description

A Spellblade soon learns that victory is not determined by how much mana they possess, but by how efficiently they can recover it. Through Mana Infusion, the Spellblade floods their body with arcane energy, allowing every successful strike to draw lingering magical essence from the battlefield and recycle it back into themselves.

While Arcane Edge channels mana into a weapon, Mana Infusion channels mana into the Spellblade's body. Every swing becomes part of a continuous cycle—mana is spent to enhance their abilities, then partially recovered through the rhythm of combat. The harder the Spellblade presses the attack, the longer they can maintain their momentum.

At Rank I, the infusion is still unstable, allowing only a small amount of mana and stamina to be reclaimed. Even so, this technique marks the beginning of the Spellblade's mastery over sustained combat, rewarding those who remain aggressive without becoming reckless.

### Effects

- Spend **12 MP & 5 SP** to activate **Mana Infusion**.
- Mana Infusion remains active for **4 Turns**.
- **Once per turn**, the first successful melee weapon attack restores:
  - **3 Mana (MP)**
  - **3 Stamina (SP)**
- The restored resources cannot exceed your maximum MP or SP.
- Requires a melee weapon to trigger the restoration effect.
- Mana Infusion cannot be activated again while already active.

### Lore

The earliest Spellblades believed that mana should never simply be expended. They observed that every clash of enchanted steel disturbed the natural flow of arcane energy, releasing fragments of magical essence into the surrounding world. Through years of experimentation, they discovered how to reclaim a portion of that energy before it dissipated.

This technique became known as Mana Infusion, not because it grants unlimited power, but because it teaches the Spellblade to fight in harmony with the flow of mana itself. Every successful strike becomes an opportunity to recover, allowing experienced Spellblades to remain in battle long after others have exhausted their strength.

Among the veteran Orders, apprentices are taught that Mana Infusion is a lesson in balance. A Spellblade who spends wisely and recovers efficiently will often outlast an opponent who relies only on overwhelming force.

> *"The greatest warriors are not those who spend the most power, but those who know how to reclaim it."*
>
> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 2
### Description

The Spellblade's mastery of Mana Infusion grows as they learn to draw greater amounts of energy from every successful strike. Rather than merely recovering fragments of their own expended mana, they instinctively absorb the lingering arcane energy released through combat, allowing them to sustain both their magical and physical endurance for longer engagements.

With improved control, the infusion becomes more stable and efficient. Mana flows continuously through the Spellblade's body, reinforcing muscles, calming fatigue, and restoring the magical reserves required to fuel their combat techniques. Every successful blow strengthens the cycle, rewarding those who maintain constant pressure against their opponents.

Rank II teaches the Spellblade that victory is not won through overwhelming force alone, but through endurance. A disciplined Spellblade who manages their resources carefully can continue fighting long after others have exhausted themselves.

### Effects

- Spend **12 MP & 5 SP**  to activate **Mana Infusion**.
- Mana Infusion remains active for **5 Turns**.
- **Once per turn**, the first successful melee weapon attack restores:
  - **5 Mana (MP)**
  - **5 Stamina (SP)**
- The restored resources cannot exceed your maximum MP or SP.
- Requires a melee weapon to trigger the restoration effect.
- Mana Infusion cannot be activated again while already active.

### Lore

Among experienced Spellblades, Rank II is often known as the **Flowing Cycle**. At this stage, the practitioner no longer sees mana as a resource that is simply spent and lost. Instead, they begin to understand that battle itself is filled with arcane energy waiting to be reclaimed.

Masters teach that every clash of enchanted steel leaves traces of magical essence within the battlefield. While most warriors allow this energy to fade into the world, Spellblades learn to instinctively draw it back into themselves, creating a continuous cycle of expenditure and recovery.

Many veteran Spellblades attribute their endurance not to larger mana reserves, but to their ability to replenish those reserves while remaining in the heart of battle. It is this relentless efficiency that separates a seasoned Spellblade from an ordinary warrior.

> *"Mana is never truly lost. The wise simply know where to find it again."*
>
> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Wellv

## Rank 3
### Description

The Spellblade's mastery over Mana Infusion has become second nature, allowing them to sustain themselves through the flow of battle with remarkable efficiency. Every successful strike strengthens the connection between their body and the surrounding arcane currents, drawing greater amounts of energy back into their reserves.

Rather than simply recovering spent mana, the Spellblade establishes a continuous cycle of expenditure and restoration. Mana fuels their techniques, their techniques empower their strikes, and those strikes replenish the very energy that was spent. This harmonious cycle allows experienced Spellblades to remain effective even during prolonged engagements where other warriors begin to tire.

At Rank III, Mana Infusion becomes a defining combat technique of the Spellblade. The ability rewards relentless pressure and careful resource management, allowing the Spellblade to maintain both their magical and physical endurance while remaining at the forefront of battle.

### Effects

- Spend **12 MP & 5 SP** to activate **Mana Infusion**.
- Mana Infusion remains active for **6 Turns**.
- **Once per turn**, the first successful melee weapon attack restores:
  - **7 Mana (MP)**
  - **7 Stamina (SP)**
- The restored resources cannot exceed your maximum MP or SP.
- Requires a melee weapon to trigger the restoration effect.
- Mana Infusion cannot be activated again while already active.

### Lore

Spellblade veterans often refer to Rank III as the **Living Current**, the stage where mana begins to flow naturally through every action rather than being consciously directed. The Spellblade no longer fights against exhaustion—they fight alongside the rhythm of mana itself.

Historical records describe legendary warriors who maintained their magical strength throughout battles lasting hours, not because they possessed limitless reserves, but because every successful strike replenished a portion of what had been spent. Their enemies watched in disbelief as the Spellblade continued to cast spells and empower their weapon long after others would have been exhausted.

Within the Spellblade Orders, reaching Rank III is considered proof that a warrior has learned one of the discipline's greatest lessons: true strength comes not from possessing endless power, but from ensuring that power never stops flowing.

> *"The strongest current is not the one that burns brightest, but the one that never ceases to flow."*
>
> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 4
### Description

The Spellblade has mastered the flow of Mana Infusion to the point where every successful strike draws substantial energy back into both body and soul. The infusion becomes remarkably efficient, allowing the Spellblade to maintain an aggressive fighting style without quickly exhausting their magical or physical reserves.

Mana flows continuously throughout the Spellblade's body, reinforcing every movement and creating a powerful cycle of expenditure and recovery. As spells are cast and techniques are unleashed, successful attacks replenish the energy needed to continue fighting, allowing the Spellblade to maintain relentless pressure throughout extended encounters.

At Rank IV, Mana Infusion becomes one of the defining techniques of veteran Spellblades. Masters of the art understand that victory is often earned through endurance as much as raw power, and those who can continuously recover their strength become exceptionally difficult to outlast.

### Effects

- Spend **35 MP** to activate **Mana Infusion**.
- Mana Infusion remains active for **7 Turns**.
- **Once per turn**, the first successful melee weapon attack restores:
  - **10 Mana (MP)**
  - **10 Stamina (SP)**
- The restored resources cannot exceed your maximum MP or SP.
- Requires a melee weapon to trigger the restoration effect.
- Mana Infusion cannot be activated again while already active.
- This rank replaces the effects of **Mana Infusion — Rank III**.

### Lore

Among the Spellblade Orders, Rank IV is known as the **Endless Current**. Reaching this stage signifies that the Spellblade has developed an intuitive understanding of the relationship between combat and mana, allowing them to recover strength almost as quickly as it is spent.

Veteran instructors often teach that this technique is less about reclaiming power and more about refusing to waste it. Every successful strike becomes an opportunity to sustain the flow of battle, ensuring that no fragment of arcane energy is lost without purpose.

Many legendary Spellblades have survived battles against overwhelming odds through Mana Infusion alone. While others slowly succumbed to fatigue, they continued fighting with unwavering determination, drawing strength from every successful blow until victory was finally secured.

> *"Power fades. Endurance wins wars. Learn the difference, and your mana will never truly abandon you."*
>
> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 5
### Description

Mana Infusion reaches its pinnacle as the Spellblade masters the endless cycle of expenditure and recovery. Rather than simply consuming mana to fuel their abilities, they have learned to weave themselves into the natural flow of arcane energy, reclaiming strength with every decisive strike. Their body becomes a living conduit where magical and physical energy exist in perfect harmony.

When Mana Infusion is activated, arcane currents surge through every muscle, vein, and nerve. Each successful blow draws lingering mana from the battlefield and channels it back into the Spellblade, while simultaneously revitalising their physical endurance. The rhythm of combat itself becomes a source of power, allowing them to sustain prolonged engagements that would leave even seasoned warriors exhausted.

Only the greatest Spellblades ever achieve this level of mastery. To them, battle is no longer a race to see who can spend the most power, but who can maintain their strength the longest. Their opponents often watch in frustration as the Spellblade continues to recover mana and stamina throughout the fight, refusing to slow until victory has been secured.

### Effects

- Spend **40 MP** to activate **Mana Infusion**.
- Mana Infusion remains active for **8 Turns**.
- **Once per turn**, the first successful melee weapon attack restores:
  - **15 Mana (MP)**
  - **15 Stamina (SP)**
- The restored resources cannot exceed your maximum MP or SP.
- Requires a melee weapon to trigger the restoration effect.
- Mana Infusion cannot be activated again while already active.
- This rank replaces the effects of **Mana Infusion — Rank IV**.

### Lore

Among the oldest Spellblade Orders, Rank V is known as the **Eternal Flow**, a title reserved for those who have achieved complete harmony between body, weapon, and mana. Masters no longer view mana as a finite resource to be carefully guarded. Instead, they understand that every clash of enchanted steel releases energy that can be reclaimed through discipline and flawless technique.

Legends speak of Spellblades who fought through entire campaigns without once exhausting their magical reserves. While others rested to recover, these masters simply continued forward, their strength renewed with every successful strike. Whether these stories have been exaggerated through centuries of retelling is unknown, but they remain an inspiration to every aspiring Spellblade.

To master Mana Infusion is to embrace one of the core philosophies of the Spellblade: power is not measured by how much mana you possess, but by how efficiently you can wield it. The greatest warriors are those who never allow the flow to stop.

> *"The river does not fear running dry, for it is always fed by its own current. Fight the same way."*  
> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well
