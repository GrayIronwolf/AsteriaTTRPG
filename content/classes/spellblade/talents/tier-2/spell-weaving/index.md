# Spell Weaving

## Overview
Spell Weaving is the defining technique of the Spellblade, allowing them to bind magic directly into their weapon instead of releasing it immediately. Rather than casting a spell from their hands, the Spellblade stores the spell within the blade, waiting for the perfect moment to unleash it through a successful melee strike.

The weapon becomes a temporary vessel for arcane power, humming with magical energy as the woven spell remains suspended within the steel. When the blade finds its target, the stored spell erupts alongside the weapon attack, combining martial skill with devastating magical force.

At Rank I, maintaining a woven spell is difficult. A missed strike can destabilise the enchantment, forcing the Spellblade to concentrate on holding the spell together until another opportunity presents itself.

## Rank 1
### Description

Spell Weaving is the defining technique of the Spellblade, allowing them to bind magic directly into their weapon instead of releasing it immediately. Rather than casting a spell from their hands, the Spellblade stores the spell within the blade, waiting for the perfect moment to unleash it through a successful melee strike.

The weapon becomes a temporary vessel for arcane power, humming with magical energy as the woven spell remains suspended within the steel. When the blade finds its target, the stored spell erupts alongside the weapon attack, combining martial skill with devastating magical force.

At Rank I, maintaining a woven spell is difficult. A missed strike can destabilise the enchantment, forcing the Spellblade to concentrate on holding the spell together until another opportunity presents itself.

### Effects

- Spend **10 MP**, plus the Mana Cost of the chosen spell, to activate **Spell Weaving**.
- Choose **one Cantrip or Weak Spell** that you know.
- The chosen spell is woven into your melee weapon for **5 Turns**.
- The **first successful melee weapon attack** releases the stored spell.
- The target suffers both the weapon's damage and the full effects of the woven spell.
- Only **one woven spell** may be stored at a time.

If the attack misses, make a **Wisdom (Concentration) Check**.

- **DC 10 Success:** The spell remains woven into the weapon.
- **Failure (5–9):** The spell dissipates harmlessly.
- **Failure (2–4):** **Spell Feedback** — You suffer HP damage equal to **25% of the spell's Mana Cost** (minimum 1 HP), and the spell is lost.
- **Natural 1:** **Arcane Misfire** — The woven spell is immediately released in a direction or target determined by the GM.

### Lore

Among the Spellblade Orders, Spell Weaving is regarded as the moment an apprentice truly embraces the union of steel and sorcery. It demands absolute control over both blade and mana, rewarding patience, precision, and discipline while punishing hesitation.

Masters teach that every woven spell is a promise made to the weapon. Those who cannot maintain that promise risk turning their own magic against themselves, a lesson every Spellblade learns sooner or later.

> *"Any mage can cast a spell. Only a Spellblade has the courage to hold one upon their blade."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 2
### Description

The Spellblade's mastery over Spell Weaving continues to grow, allowing them to bind more complex magical energies into their weapon without immediately destabilising the enchantment. Mana flows more smoothly through the blade, creating a stronger bond between the weapon and the woven spell.

At Rank II, the Spellblade has learned to maintain greater control over unstable arcane energy, allowing **Minor Spells** to be woven alongside weaker magic. Although the enchantment remains volatile, experienced Spellblades recover from failed strikes with far greater consistency than novices.

Veterans often describe this stage as the point where Spell Weaving becomes instinctive. The blade no longer feels like a vessel carrying magic—it feels as though the weapon itself is waiting for the perfect moment to cast.

### Effects

- Spend **15 MP**, plus the Mana Cost of the chosen spell, to activate **Spell Weaving**.
- Choose **one Cantrip, Weak, or Minor Spell** that you know.
- The chosen spell is woven into your melee weapon for **6 Turns**.
- The **first successful melee weapon attack** releases the stored spell.
- The target suffers both the weapon's damage and the full effects of the woven spell.
- Only **one woven spell** may be stored at a time.

If the attack misses, make a **Wisdom (Concentration) Check**.

- **DC 8 Success:** The spell remains woven into the weapon.
- **Failure (5–7):** The spell dissipates harmlessly.
- **Failure (2–4):** **Spell Feedback** — You suffer HP damage equal to **25% of the spell's Mana Cost** (minimum 1 HP), and the spell is lost.
- **Natural 1:** **Arcane Misfire** — The woven spell is immediately released in a direction or target determined by the GM.

### Lore

Spellblade Masters teach that Rank II is where confidence begins to replace caution. The practitioner no longer struggles to contain every spell, instead allowing mana to flow naturally through the weapon as though it were an extension of their own body.

Many duels have been decided by a perfectly woven spell concealed within an otherwise ordinary strike. Opponents often focus on the blade itself, never realising that the true danger lies in the magic patiently waiting beneath its edge.

Every successful weave strengthens the bond between Spellblade and weapon, bringing them one step closer to mastering one of Asteria's most iconic martial arts.

> *"The blade does not simply carry the spell... it waits for the perfect moment to speak."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 3
### Description

Spell Weaving has become second nature to the Spellblade, allowing them to bind powerful arcane energies into their weapon with remarkable stability. What was once a risky technique now feels like an extension of their combat style, seamlessly blending spellcasting with swordsmanship.

At Rank III, the Spellblade is capable of weaving **Greater Spells** into their weapon. The mana contained within the blade is denser and more refined, allowing stronger magical effects to remain stable while awaiting release. Although the risk of destabilisation still exists, experienced Spellblades have learned to recover their concentration with far greater consistency.

Many masters consider this the point where a Spellblade truly becomes feared on the battlefield. Every swing carries the possibility of unleashing devastating magic, forcing opponents to respect every attack regardless of its appearance.

### Effects

- Spend **20 MP**, plus the Mana Cost of the chosen spell, to activate **Spell Weaving**.
- Choose **one Cantrip, Weak, Minor, or Greater Spell** that you know.
- The chosen spell is woven into your melee weapon for **7 Turns**.
- The **first successful melee weapon attack** releases the stored spell.
- The target suffers both the weapon's damage and the full effects of the woven spell.
- Only **one woven spell** may be stored at a time.

If the attack misses, make a **Wisdom (Concentration) Check**.

- **DC 6 Success:** The spell remains woven into the weapon.
- **Failure (5):** The spell dissipates harmlessly.
- **Failure (2–4):** **Spell Feedback** — You suffer HP damage equal to **25% of the spell's Mana Cost** (minimum 1 HP), and the spell is lost.
- **Natural 1:** **Arcane Misfire** — The woven spell is immediately released in a direction or target determined by the GM.

### Lore

Among the Spellblade Orders, Rank III is known as the **Bound Edge**, where weapon and spell become so closely intertwined that separating the two is almost impossible. The blade no longer merely carries magic—it becomes the spell's chosen vessel until the perfect moment arrives.

Veteran Spellblades often recount stories of battles won through patience rather than power, waiting for a single decisive strike to release a woven spell that changed the course of the fight. To them, Spell Weaving is not simply an offensive technique but a lesson in discipline, timing, and unwavering control.

> *"The impatient cast a spell. The wise let their blade decide when it is time."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 4
### Description

The Spellblade has achieved exceptional mastery over Spell Weaving, allowing them to bind immensely powerful spells within their weapon while maintaining remarkable control over the volatile mana. The blade becomes a stable conduit capable of holding magical energy that would have overwhelmed lesser practitioners.

At Rank IV, the Spellblade may weave **Strengthened** and **Enhanced Spells** into their weapon. Years of training have refined their concentration to such a degree that an ordinary missed attack no longer threatens the stability of the woven spell. Instead, the Spellblade instinctively reinforces the enchantment, patiently waiting for the next opportunity to deliver its devastating power.

This level of mastery is rarely witnessed outside the great Spellblade Orders. Every strike forces an opponent to respect the possibility that an immensely powerful spell lies hidden within the blade, ready to be unleashed at any moment.

### Effects

- Spend **25 MP**, plus the Mana Cost of the chosen spell, to activate **Spell Weaving**.
- Choose **one Cantrip, Weak, Minor, Greater, Strengthened, or Enhanced Spell** that you know.
- The chosen spell is woven into your melee weapon for **8 Turns**.
- The **first successful melee weapon attack** releases the stored spell.
- The target suffers both the weapon's damage and the full effects of the woven spell.
- Only **one woven spell** may be stored at a time.
- Missed attacks no longer require a Concentration Check.
- A **Natural 1** still causes an **Arcane Misfire**, immediately releasing the woven spell in a direction or target determined by the GM.

### Lore

Within the Spellblade Orders, Rank IV is known as the **Master's Weave**. Few warriors possess the discipline required to maintain such powerful magic within a weapon while continuing to fight with complete confidence. Every movement becomes measured, every strike deliberate, and every decision guided by absolute control over the mana flowing through the blade.

Stories tell of master Spellblades carrying woven spells throughout entire battles, refusing to release them until the perfect opportunity presented itself. To these masters, patience is every bit as deadly as power, for a spell released at precisely the right moment can decide the fate of an entire battlefield.

> *"Power is easy to wield. Restraint is the mark of a true master."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 5
### Description

Spell Weaving reaches its pinnacle as the Spellblade becomes a true master of binding magic to steel. At this level, the weapon is no longer simply a vessel for arcane energy—it becomes an extension of the Spellblade's own mana core, capable of safely containing some of the most destructive spells known throughout Asteria.

The flow of mana between Spellblade and weapon is flawless. Powerful spells can remain dormant within the blade for extended periods without destabilising, allowing the Spellblade to choose the exact moment to unleash their magic. Every strike becomes a calculated threat, forcing enemies to respect every movement of the enchanted weapon.

Only the greatest Spellblades ever achieve this level of mastery. Those who do are feared not because of the strength of their blade or the power of their magic alone, but because they wield both as one inseparable force.

### Effects

- Spend **30 MP**, plus the Mana Cost of the chosen spell, to activate **Spell Weaving**.
- Choose **one Cantrip, Weak, Minor, Greater, Strengthened, Enhanced, Superior, or Grand Spell** that you know.
- The chosen spell is woven into your melee weapon for **10 Turns**.
- The **first successful melee weapon attack** releases the stored spell.
- The target suffers both the weapon's damage and the full effects of the woven spell.
- Only **one woven spell** may be stored at a time.
- Missed attacks no longer require a Concentration Check.
- **Natural 1:** **Arcane Misfire** — the woven spell is immediately released in a direction or target determined by the GM.
- This rank replaces the effects of **Spell Weaving — Rank IV**.

### Lore

The ancient Spellblade Orders refer to Rank V as the **Perfect Weave**, a level of mastery achieved by only the most disciplined warriors. At this stage, the Spellblade no longer thinks of spellcasting and swordsmanship as separate arts. Every spell is delivered through the blade, and every strike carries the possibility of extraordinary magical devastation.

Legends tell of masters who entered battle with weapons already carrying devastating spells, patiently waiting for the perfect opportunity to release them. Their opponents often realised too late that the true danger was never the sword itself, but the immense arcane power sleeping within it.

To master Spell Weaving is to embody the philosophy of the Spellblade: magic and steel are not separate paths, but two halves of the same discipline. When united, they create a warrior unlike any other in Asteria.

> *"When your blade becomes your spellbook, and your spellbook becomes your blade, you have mastered the Perfect Weave."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well
