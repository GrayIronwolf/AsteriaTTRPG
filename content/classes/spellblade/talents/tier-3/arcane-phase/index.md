# Arcane Phase

## Overview
Arcane Phase is one of the most dangerous techniques taught within the Spellblade Orders, allowing the practitioner to partially convert their physical body into raw arcane energy. For a brief moment, the Spellblade exists between the physical and magical planes, enabling them to move through obstacles that would otherwise halt their advance.

While phased, the Spellblade's body becomes intangible to most physical matter. Stone walls, iron bars, enemy warriors, and difficult terrain become little more than passing obstacles. However, this transformation comes at a great risk. By becoming living mana, the Spellblade becomes significantly more vulnerable to hostile magic, as spells can interact directly with their unstable arcane form.

Masters repeatedly warn their apprentices that Arcane Phase is a technique of precision, not recklessness. Countless Spellblades have suffered horrific injuries by allowing the technique to expire while still inside solid matter. Those who master Arcane Phase always know exactly where they intend to return before they ever step beyond the physical world.

## Rank 1
### Description

Arcane Phase is one of the most dangerous techniques taught within the Spellblade Orders, allowing the practitioner to partially convert their physical body into raw arcane energy. For a brief moment, the Spellblade exists between the physical and magical planes, enabling them to move through obstacles that would otherwise halt their advance.

While phased, the Spellblade's body becomes intangible to most physical matter. Stone walls, iron bars, enemy warriors, and difficult terrain become little more than passing obstacles. However, this transformation comes at a great risk. By becoming living mana, the Spellblade becomes significantly more vulnerable to hostile magic, as spells can interact directly with their unstable arcane form.

Masters repeatedly warn their apprentices that Arcane Phase is a technique of precision, not recklessness. Countless Spellblades have suffered horrific injuries by allowing the technique to expire while still inside solid matter. Those who master Arcane Phase always know exactly where they intend to return before they ever step beyond the physical world.

### Effects

- Spend **1 Action Point** and **40 MP** to activate **Arcane Phase**.
- Arcane Phase lasts for **1 Turn**.
- **1 Use per Short Rest.**
- While Arcane Phase is active:
  - Ignore difficult terrain.
  - Move through creatures.
  - Move through thin, non-magical solid objects such as doors, gates, bars and walls.
  - You are immune to Opportunity Attacks.
  - You cannot interact with or manipulate physical objects while phased unless the GM determines otherwise.

#### Arcane Vulnerability

While Arcane Phase is active:

- You have **Disadvantage on Saving Throws against magical effects.**
- You take **25% additional magical damage** (rounded up).

#### ⚠ Phase Instability

Arcane Phase **must not** end while any part of your body occupies a creature or solid object.

If this occurs:

- You are immediately expelled to the nearest unoccupied space.
- You are **Knocked Prone**.
- You suffer **6d6 Arcane Damage**.
- You gain **Arcane Fatigue** until the end of your next turn.

### Lore

Among the Spellblade Orders, Arcane Phase is considered both a marvel and a warning. It represents the ultimate trust between a Spellblade and their mana, requiring absolute confidence that one's body can safely return to the physical world. Those who hesitate often discover too late that the arcane currents show little mercy to careless practitioners.

Veteran masters insist that Arcane Phase should never be viewed as an escape. It is a calculated commitment, demanding complete awareness of one's surroundings before taking even a single step beyond reality. Every apprentice is taught the same lesson before learning the technique: always know where you intend to reappear.

Legends speak of master Spellblades walking unharmed through castle walls, collapsing caverns, and enemy battle lines. Yet those same stories always end with a reminder that even the greatest masters respected the dangers of the technique, for the arcane realm rewards discipline and punishes arrogance in equal measure.

> *"The world will gladly let you pass through it... but it may not welcome you back if you choose your path poorly."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 2
### Description

The Spellblade's control over Arcane Phase has improved, allowing them to maintain their incorporeal form for longer without destabilising their mana. What was once a brief step between worlds becomes a sustained state, enabling the Spellblade to navigate dangerous terrain and bypass physical obstacles with greater confidence.

Although the technique has become more stable, it remains one of the most hazardous abilities within the Spellblade arsenal. While partially transformed into arcane energy, the Spellblade becomes increasingly susceptible to hostile magic. Enemy spellcasters quickly learn to exploit this vulnerability, making careful timing and battlefield awareness essential.

Experienced Spellblades are taught that Arcane Phase should never be used recklessly. A poorly planned phase can leave them trapped between solid objects or exposed to devastating magical attacks. Every use demands discipline, patience, and absolute awareness of one's surroundings.

### Effects

- Spend **1 Action Point** and **35 MP** to activate **Arcane Phase**.
- Arcane Phase lasts for **2 Turns**.
- **2 Uses per Short Rest.**
- While Arcane Phase is active:
  - Ignore difficult terrain.
  - Move through creatures.
  - Move through non-magical doors, gates, walls, bars and other solid objects up to **1 metre thick**.
  - You are immune to Opportunity Attacks.
  - You cannot interact with or manipulate physical objects while phased unless the GM determines otherwise.

#### Arcane Vulnerability

While Arcane Phase is active:

- You have **Disadvantage on Saving Throws against magical effects.**
- You take **25% additional magical damage** (rounded up).

#### ⚠ Phase Instability

Arcane Phase **must not** end while any part of your body occupies a creature or solid object.

If this occurs:

- You are immediately expelled to the nearest unoccupied space.
- You are **Knocked Prone**.
- You suffer **6d6 Arcane Damage**.
- You gain **Arcane Fatigue** until the end of your next turn.

### Lore

Within the Spellblade Orders, Rank II is known as the **Ghost Step**, marking the point where the practitioner begins to trust the flow of mana rather than fear it. Remaining phased for several moments requires exceptional concentration, as even the slightest lapse can cause the arcane currents to become unstable.

Veteran instructors often send apprentices through abandoned ruins and ancient fortresses to teach them a simple lesson: always know where you intend to emerge. Many overconfident students have found themselves painfully expelled from stone walls after losing track of time, a mistake that every Spellblade remembers only once.

Those who master Rank II learn that Arcane Phase is not simply a means of movement. It is a lesson in restraint, precision, and respect for the unpredictable nature of arcane energy.

> *"The greatest danger is not stepping beyond the world—it is forgetting that the world still expects you to return."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 3
### Description

The Spellblade has reached a level of mastery where Arcane Phase is no longer a desperate gamble, but a calculated manipulation of the boundary between the physical and arcane realms. Maintaining an incorporeal form has become second nature, allowing the Spellblade to traverse obstacles and hostile battlefields with remarkable precision.

While phased, the Spellblade exists only partially within the physical world. Weapons, creatures, and solid terrain struggle to impede their movement, making them exceptionally difficult to pin down. However, this freedom comes at a significant cost. Their body, now composed largely of unstable arcane energy, becomes increasingly vulnerable to hostile spells and magical effects capable of disrupting their fragile form.

Spellblade Masters teach that Rank III is where overconfidence claims the lives of many practitioners. The longer one remains phased, the easier it is to forget the dangers of the physical world waiting to reclaim them. Every experienced Spellblade knows that the greatest threat is not entering the arcane state—it is returning from it safely.

### Effects

- Spend **1 Action Point** and **30 MP** to activate **Arcane Phase**.
- Arcane Phase lasts for **3 Turns**.
- **3 Uses per Short Rest.**
- While Arcane Phase is active:
  - Ignore difficult terrain.
  - Move through creatures.
  - Move through most non-magical structures and natural obstacles, including walls, floors, ceilings, doors, gates, and large rock formations up to **2 metres thick**.
  - You are immune to Opportunity Attacks.
  - You cannot interact with or manipulate physical objects while phased unless the GM determines otherwise.

#### Arcane Vulnerability

While Arcane Phase is active:

- You have **Disadvantage on Saving Throws against magical effects.**
- You take **25% additional magical damage** (rounded up).

#### ⚠ Phase Instability

Arcane Phase **must not** end while any part of your body occupies a creature or solid object.

If this occurs:

- You are immediately expelled to the nearest unoccupied space.
- You are **Knocked Prone**.
- You suffer **8d6 Arcane Damage**.
- You gain **Arcane Fatigue** until the end of your next turn.

### Lore

Within the Spellblade Orders, Rank III is known as the **Veil Walker**, a title earned by those capable of walking confidently between the material world and the arcane currents that surround it. At this stage, Arcane Phase ceases to be viewed as a simple movement technique and becomes an expression of complete trust in one's mastery over mana.

Veterans often recount stories of Spellblades slipping effortlessly through fortress walls, collapsing caverns, and enemy shield lines before reappearing exactly where they intended. Yet every tale is accompanied by another, warning of those who misjudged a wall's thickness, underestimated the duration of the technique, or ignored the presence of enemy mages. The arcane realm is unforgiving to those who become complacent.

Masters remind every student that Arcane Phase does not make them invincible. It merely changes the dangers they face. Wisdom lies in knowing not only when to phase, but when to return.

> *"Any fool can step beyond the world. Only a master knows exactly where to step back into it."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 4
### Description

The Spellblade has become a true master of Arcane Phase, effortlessly shifting between the physical world and the arcane plane. Their body now exists only loosely within reality, allowing them to bypass obstacles that even powerful magic was designed to stop. Walls, fortifications, and many magical barriers become little more than temporary inconveniences to a practitioner of this calibre.

Yet with such mastery comes even greater danger. While their connection to the physical world weakens, their arcane form becomes increasingly exposed to hostile magical forces. Enemy spellcasters can exploit this vulnerability, making every use of Arcane Phase a calculated risk rather than a guaranteed escape.

Within the Spellblade Orders, Rank IV is considered the threshold of true mastery. It is said that a master no longer asks whether a path exists—they simply decide to walk through it. Even so, the oldest instructors continually warn that arrogance has claimed more lives than any blade ever has.

### Effects

- Spend **1 Action Point** and **25 MP** to activate **Arcane Phase**.
- Arcane Phase lasts for **4 Turns**.
- **4 Uses per Short Rest.**
- While Arcane Phase is active:
  - Ignore difficult terrain.
  - Move through creatures.
  - Move through most non-magical structures regardless of thickness, provided your movement is sufficient to completely pass through them.
  - You may pass through **lesser magical barriers**, magical walls, force effects, and magically sealed doorways at the GM's discretion.
  - You are immune to Opportunity Attacks.
  - You cannot interact with or manipulate physical objects while phased unless the GM determines otherwise.

#### Arcane Vulnerability

While Arcane Phase is active:

- You have **Disadvantage on Saving Throws against magical effects.**
- You take **25% additional magical damage** (rounded up).

#### ⚠ Phase Instability

Arcane Phase **must not** end while any part of your body occupies a creature or solid object.

If this occurs:

- You are immediately expelled to the nearest unoccupied space.
- You are **Knocked Prone**.
- You suffer **10d6 Arcane Damage**.
- You gain **Arcane Fatigue** until the end of your next turn.

### Lore

Within the ancient Spellblade Orders, Rank IV is known as the **Veil Master**. Very few practitioners ever reach this stage, for it demands absolute trust in one's mastery over both body and mana. Those who succeed no longer view walls or barriers as obstacles, but as temporary boundaries between one step and the next.

Stories tell of Veil Masters infiltrating heavily fortified castles without opening a single gate, escaping collapsing temples by walking through solid stone, and pursuing enemies through magically sealed chambers thought to be impenetrable. Such feats have earned them equal measures of admiration and fear throughout Asteria.

Even so, every master shares the same warning with their apprentices: the arcane plane is unforgiving. A moment of carelessness, a lapse in concentration, or a misjudged path can turn Arcane Phase from a miraculous technique into a fatal mistake.

> *"Stone yields to patience. Magic yields to understanding. Pride yields to neither."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 5
### Description

Arcane Phase reaches its ultimate form, allowing the Spellblade to exist almost entirely as living arcane energy. Through absolute mastery of their mana core, they freely shift between the material world and the Arcane Plane, slipping through obstacles that would stop even the greatest warriors. To onlookers, the Spellblade appears to fade from reality itself, moving effortlessly through walls, fortifications, creatures, and magical barriers before reforming exactly where they intend.

Despite this mastery, Arcane Phase remains one of the most dangerous techniques ever created. While the Spellblade is incredibly difficult for physical forces to hinder, they become exceptionally vulnerable to hostile magic. Every spell threatens to destabilise their arcane form, and the greatest enemy of a master Spellblade is often overconfidence.

The oldest masters teach that Arcane Phase is not an escape from reality—it is a temporary surrender to it. The world will always reclaim those who linger too long beyond its grasp.

### Effects

- Spend **1 Action Point** and **20 MP** to activate **Arcane Phase**.
- Arcane Phase lasts for **5 Turns**.
- **5 Uses per Short Rest.**
- While Arcane Phase is active:
  - Ignore difficult terrain.
  - Move freely through creatures.
  - Move through all non-magical structures regardless of thickness, provided your movement is sufficient to completely pass through them.
  - Move through nearly all magical barriers, force effects, magical walls, and magically sealed structures.
  - **Artifact-grade wards, Divine Magic, and World Magic cannot be phased through unless the GM rules otherwise.**
  - You are immune to Opportunity Attacks.
  - You cannot interact with or manipulate physical objects while phased unless the GM determines otherwise.

#### Arcane Vulnerability

While Arcane Phase is active:

- You have **Disadvantage on Saving Throws against magical effects.**
- You take **25% additional magical damage** (rounded up).

#### ⚠ Phase Instability

Arcane Phase **must never** end while any part of your body occupies a creature or solid object.

If this occurs:

- You are immediately expelled to the nearest unoccupied space.
- You are **Knocked Prone**.
- You suffer **12d6 Arcane Damage**.
- You gain **Arcane Fatigue** until the end of your next turn.
- The GM may apply additional consequences if you attempted to phase through an object or barrier beyond your current mastery.

### Lore

Within the oldest Spellblade Orders, Rank V is known as the **Phantom Ascendant**, a title granted only to those who have mastered the impossible. These legendary warriors no longer perceive walls, fortifications, or magical prisons as barriers. Instead, they see them as temporary imperfections within the fabric of reality, capable of being crossed through perfect harmony with the arcane.

Legends tell of Phantom Ascendants walking unhindered through impregnable citadels, escaping collapsing mountains, and crossing battlefields untouched by volleys of arrows. Yet every tale carries the same warning. Those who forget that they remain bound by the laws of reality inevitably discover that the Arcane Plane shows no mercy to the careless.

To master Arcane Phase is to accept that immense power always demands equal responsibility. A true Spellblade knows that the greatest danger is never the wall before them, but the decision of where they choose to return.

> *"Reality is not your enemy. It is your destination. Never forget to return to it."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well
