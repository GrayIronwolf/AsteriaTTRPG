# Bladestorm

## Overview
Bladestorm is one of the most demanding martial techniques taught within the Spellblade Orders. Rather than relying upon destructive magic, the Spellblade channels a small amount of mana through their body to enhance their speed, allowing them to unleash a rapid succession of perfectly controlled strikes in the time most warriors make a single attack.

The technique demands immense physical endurance. Every muscle is pushed to its limit as the Spellblade flows from one strike to the next with blinding speed. To onlookers, the blade appears to split into shimmering afterimages as steel flashes through the air, leaving enemies struggling to defend against the relentless assault.

At Rank I, Bladestorm is still a taxing technique that can only be sustained for a brief moment. Even so, a skilled Spellblade can overwhelm an opponent's guard or pressure multiple nearby enemies before they have time to react.

## Rank 1
### Description

Bladestorm is one of the most demanding martial techniques taught within the Spellblade Orders. Rather than relying upon destructive magic, the Spellblade channels a small amount of mana through their body to enhance their speed, allowing them to unleash a rapid succession of perfectly controlled strikes in the time most warriors make a single attack.

The technique demands immense physical endurance. Every muscle is pushed to its limit as the Spellblade flows from one strike to the next with blinding speed. To onlookers, the blade appears to split into shimmering afterimages as steel flashes through the air, leaving enemies struggling to defend against the relentless assault.

At Rank I, Bladestorm is still a taxing technique that can only be sustained for a brief moment. Even so, a skilled Spellblade can overwhelm an opponent's guard or pressure multiple nearby enemies before they have time to react.

### Effects

- Spend **1 Action Point**, **5 MP**, and **25 SP** to activate **Bladestorm**.
- Immediately make **2 melee weapon attacks**.
- You may direct both attacks against **one creature**, or split them between **two different creatures** within your weapon's reach.
- Resolve each attack separately using your normal Weapon Attack Roll.
- Each successful attack deals **normal weapon damage**, including any applicable weapon enchantments or active Spellblade talents.
- Bladestorm counts as a single Attack Action.
- This talent cannot be used with ranged weapons.

### Lore

Within the Spellblade Orders, Bladestorm is regarded as a test of martial discipline rather than magical ability. Apprentices quickly discover that speed alone is meaningless without precision, and that every unnecessary movement wastes valuable stamina.

Masters often teach that the technique should resemble the calm eye of a storm rather than uncontrolled fury. Every strike is deliberate, every movement efficient, and every breath measured. Only through absolute control can a Spellblade unleash a true Bladestorm without exhausting themselves.

Those who witness the technique often describe it as though several swords were striking at once, unable to follow the impossible speed of the Spellblade's blade.

> *"A single strike may win a duel. A storm of strikes wins the battle before your enemy can answer."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 2
### Description

The Spellblade's mastery of Bladestorm continues to develop, allowing them to unleash an even greater flurry of attacks without sacrificing precision. Their body has adapted to the immense physical strain, enabling each strike to flow seamlessly into the next while maintaining complete control over their weapon.

Rather than relying on brute force, the Spellblade embraces momentum. Every swing naturally carries into another, creating a relentless assault that overwhelms an opponent's ability to defend. To those witnessing the technique, the Spellblade appears surrounded by a whirlwind of flashing steel, each strike arriving before the last has fully finished.

At Rank II, Bladestorm becomes a feared battlefield technique capable of pressuring multiple enemies or relentlessly driving a single opponent onto the defensive. Though physically exhausting, experienced Spellblades understand that a perfectly timed Bladestorm can end a battle before their enemy has the opportunity to recover.

- Spend **1 Action Point**, **5 MP**, and **30 SP** to activate **Bladestorm**.
- Immediately make **3 melee weapon attacks**.
- You may direct the attacks against **one creature**, or divide them among **multiple creatures** within your weapon's reach.
- Resolve each attack separately using your normal Weapon Attack Roll.
- Each successful attack deals **normal weapon damage**, including any applicable weapon enchantments or active Spellblade talents.
- Bladestorm counts as a single Attack Action.
- This talent cannot be used with ranged weapons.
- This rank replaces the effects of **Bladestorm — Rank I**.

### Lore

Within the Spellblade Orders, Rank II is known as the **Raging Tempest**. Apprentices who achieve this milestone have learned that Bladestorm is not about attacking wildly, but about maintaining perfect control while striking with extraordinary speed.

Masters often compare the technique to a violent storm sweeping across the battlefield. Each strike appears independent, yet every movement is connected through flawless timing and discipline. Opponents quickly discover that attempting to defend against one strike only leaves them exposed to the next.

To veteran Spellblades, Bladestorm is more than a display of martial skill—it is proof that disciplined technique will always surpass reckless aggression.

> *"The untrained swing harder. The master simply strikes more often than the eye can follow."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 3
### Description

The Spellblade has mastered the rhythm of Bladestorm, transforming it into a devastating display of speed and martial precision. Every movement flows effortlessly into the next as mana subtly reinforces the body, allowing the Spellblade to push beyond normal physical limits without relying upon destructive magic.

To observers, the technique resembles a raging storm of flashing steel. The Spellblade seems to appear in several places at once, delivering a relentless sequence of perfectly controlled strikes that leaves opponents with little opportunity to defend themselves. Every swing is deliberate, every step measured, and every strike contributes to an overwhelming offensive rhythm.

At Rank III, Bladestorm has become one of the defining techniques of the Spellblade. Whether facing a single powerful foe or several nearby enemies, the Spellblade can unleash a barrage of attacks capable of shifting the momentum of battle in an instant.

### Lore

Among the Spellblade Orders, Rank III is known as the **Tempest Dance**. It is the point where speed and discipline become inseparable, allowing the practitioner to unleash a continuous flurry of strikes while maintaining flawless technique.

Masters teach that Bladestorm is never about losing control. A true Spellblade remains calm within the chaos, allowing every movement to naturally guide the next. Those who rely on reckless speed quickly exhaust themselves, while those who embrace precision become an unstoppable force upon the battlefield.

Veteran warriors often recognise a master of Bladestorm before the first strike lands, for the silence before the storm is every bit as terrifying as the storm itself.

> *"Speed without discipline is merely panic. A true storm strikes with purpose."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 4
### Description

The Spellblade has pushed Bladestorm beyond mortal swordsmanship, achieving a level of speed and precision that few warriors can comprehend. Every movement flows without hesitation, each strike naturally leading into the next as the Spellblade maintains complete control over the storm they unleash. Only a small amount of mana is required to reinforce the body, while sheer martial discipline drives the technique forward.

To those caught within its reach, Bladestorm becomes a relentless barrage of flashing steel. Opponents struggle to react as strike after strike falls from seemingly every direction, forcing even veteran warriors onto the defensive. Despite the incredible speed, every blow remains measured and deliberate, reflecting years of relentless training.

At Rank IV, the Spellblade can overwhelm groups of enemies or relentlessly pressure a powerful foe with a storm of perfectly executed attacks. Few can withstand the assault once the storm has begun.

### Lore

Within the Spellblade Orders, Rank IV is known as the **Eye of the Storm**. It represents the point where the practitioner no longer forces the technique through sheer effort. Instead, they become the calm centre of the storm, allowing flawless discipline to guide every strike while chaos erupts around them.

Veteran masters teach that Bladestorm is not won through speed alone. Every strike must carry intent, every movement must preserve balance, and every breath must maintain the rhythm that defines the Spellblade. Those who lose themselves within the storm become reckless; those who master it become legends.

Stories of Rank IV practitioners speak of battlefields where enemies believed they faced an entire company, only to discover it was a single Spellblade moving faster than the eye could follow.

> *"Become the calm within the storm, and your enemies will remember only the sound of your blade."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 5
### Description

Bladestorm reaches its ultimate expression, becoming the pinnacle of martial mastery within the Spellblade Orders. The Spellblade no longer thinks about each individual strike; body, blade, and instinct move as one, unleashing a relentless torrent of perfectly executed attacks that few opponents can hope to withstand. Only a faint pulse of mana is required to reinforce the body, while years of discipline and physical conditioning drive the technique beyond mortal limits.

To onlookers, the Spellblade appears as a storm of flashing steel. Their weapon becomes a blur, striking with such speed that enemies struggle to determine where one attack ends and the next begins. Every movement is efficient, every step deliberate, and every strike delivered with unwavering precision.

Only the greatest Spellblades ever achieve this level of mastery. A master of Bladestorm can dominate the space around them, carving through multiple foes or unleashing an overwhelming assault upon a single enemy before they have the opportunity to respond.

### Lore

Within the oldest Spellblade Orders, Rank V is known as the **Endless Storm**. It is a technique so demanding that only the greatest masters are capable of wielding it without collapsing from exhaustion. Every strike flows effortlessly into the next, creating a flawless sequence of attacks that embodies the perfect union of martial discipline and arcane enhancement.

Legends tell of Spellblades who stood alone against overwhelming numbers, their blades moving with such impossible speed that survivors believed they had faced an entire company rather than a single warrior. Whether these stories have grown through centuries of retelling is unknown, but every apprentice dreams of one day reaching the Endless Storm.

To master Bladestorm is to prove that the greatest weapon of a Spellblade is not magic, but the discipline to wield both steel and mana in absolute harmony.

> *"A storm does not ask how many stand before it. It simply sweeps across the battlefield until nothing remains in its path."*
