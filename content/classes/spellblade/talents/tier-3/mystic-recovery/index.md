# Mystic Recovery

## Overview
Mystic Recovery teaches the Spellblade that health, stamina, and mana are not separate energies, but different expressions of the same life force. Through careful control of their mana core, a Spellblade can sacrifice one resource to restore another, allowing them to adapt to the ever-changing demands of battle.

At Rank I, the conversion process is crude and highly inefficient. Much of the energy is lost during the transmutation, requiring the Spellblade to sacrifice far more than they recover. Even so, experienced warriors understand that surviving a battle often depends upon having the right resource available at the right moment, regardless of the cost.

In addition to active transmutation, the Spellblade's growing understanding of their own life force naturally accelerates their recovery while resting, allowing their body and mana to replenish more efficiently between encounters.

## Rank 1
### Description

Mystic Recovery teaches the Spellblade that health, stamina, and mana are not separate energies, but different expressions of the same life force. Through careful control of their mana core, a Spellblade can sacrifice one resource to restore another, allowing them to adapt to the ever-changing demands of battle.

At Rank I, the conversion process is crude and highly inefficient. Much of the energy is lost during the transmutation, requiring the Spellblade to sacrifice far more than they recover. Even so, experienced warriors understand that surviving a battle often depends upon having the right resource available at the right moment, regardless of the cost.

In addition to active transmutation, the Spellblade's growing understanding of their own life force naturally accelerates their recovery while resting, allowing their body and mana to replenish more efficiently between encounters.

### Effects

#### Active

- Spend **1 Action Point** to activate **Mystic Recovery**.
- Instantly perform **one** of the following conversions using a **3:1 ratio**:
  - Spend **3 HP** to restore **1 MP**.
  - Spend **3 MP** to restore **1 SP**.
  - Spend **3 SP** to restore **1 HP**.
- Mystic Recovery may only be used **once per turn**.
- Restored resources cannot exceed their maximum values.
- This talent cannot reduce your HP below **1**.

#### Passive

- Increase your natural **Health, Mana, and Stamina regeneration by 10%** while resting outside of combat.

### Lore

Among the Spellblade Orders, Mystic Recovery is considered one of the first lessons in mastering the body's internal energies. Apprentices are taught that mana, stamina, and life are connected by a single current, and that with sufficient discipline, one form of energy can be transformed into another.

The process is imperfect at first, often leaving the practitioner drained after each conversion. However, every successful transmutation strengthens their understanding of this delicate balance, laying the foundation for far greater mastery in the years to come.

Veteran Spellblades often remind their students that Mystic Recovery is not a technique of abundance, but one of sacrifice. Victory belongs to those willing to give up what they have in order to preserve what they need most.

> *"Strength is not measured by what you possess, but by what you are willing to sacrifice to keep fighting."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 2
### Description

The Spellblade's understanding of internal energy has grown considerably, allowing them to perform resource transmutation with far greater efficiency. By strengthening the connection between their mana core, physical endurance, and life force, they reduce the amount of energy lost during each conversion.

While the process still requires sacrifice, experienced Spellblades learn to redirect energy with greater precision, making Mystic Recovery a dependable technique during prolonged battles. Whether restoring precious mana to continue casting, recovering stamina to maintain relentless attacks, or converting excess stamina into life, the Spellblade gains greater control over their own survival.

As their mastery improves, the Spellblade's body also becomes more efficient at recovering naturally. Even while resting, their internal energies continue to rebalance themselves, allowing them to recover faster than ordinary warriors or mages.

  - Spend **2 SP** to restore **1 HP**.
- Mystic Recovery may only be used **once per turn**.
- Restored resources cannot exceed their maximum values.
- This talent cannot reduce your HP below **1**.

#### Passive

- Increase your natural **Health, Mana, and Stamina regeneration by 20%** while resting outside of combat.

### Lore

Within the Spellblade Orders, Rank II is known as the **Balanced Exchange**. Apprentices have moved beyond crude transmutation, learning instead to guide energy with careful precision rather than brute force. Every successful conversion strengthens the connection between body, mind, and soul.

Masters teach that Mystic Recovery is not merely a survival technique—it is an exercise in discipline. A Spellblade who understands when to sacrifice one resource for another can remain effective long after others have exhausted their strength.

Many veteran Spellblades credit this technique with saving their lives during long campaigns, where careful management of their own life force often proved more valuable than any potion or enchanted relic.

> *"Energy is never truly lost. The wise simply learn how to guide it where it is needed most."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 3
### Description

The Spellblade has mastered the balance between body, mind, and mana, achieving perfect harmony over the three fundamental energies that sustain all life. No longer wasting energy during transmutation, they can redirect their internal reserves with flawless precision, allowing them to adapt instantly to the demands of battle.

At Rank III, Mystic Recovery reaches perfect efficiency. Every resource sacrificed is converted without loss, enabling the Spellblade to replenish exactly what they require in the heat of combat. Whether restoring mana to unleash another spell, replenishing stamina to continue an assault, or converting stamina into vital health, every exchange becomes a calculated decision rather than a desperate gamble.

This mastery also strengthens the Spellblade's natural recovery. Their body instinctively restores itself during periods of rest, allowing them to return to battle sooner than most warriors or spellcasters.

Within the Spellblade Orders, Rank III is known as the **Perfect Exchange**. It is the stage where apprentices become true masters of internal transmutation, no longer sacrificing excess energy during the conversion process. Every movement of mana, every breath, and every heartbeat become part of a perfectly balanced cycle.

Veteran instructors often describe Mystic Recovery as the art of understanding that no energy is ever truly created or destroyed. A disciplined Spellblade merely guides it to where it is needed most, maintaining harmony between body and soul even amidst the chaos of battle.

Those who achieve this level of mastery are respected throughout the Orders, for they have proven that true strength lies not in possessing endless power, but in using every fragment of it with perfect efficiency.

> *"The moment nothing is wasted, every breath becomes a source of strength."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 4
### Description

The Spellblade's mastery over internal transmutation has surpassed perfect balance, allowing them to amplify the energy released during each conversion. Through absolute control of their mana core, they have learned to minimise waste and harness ambient arcane energy, producing greater returns than the energy originally sacrificed.

At Rank IV, Mystic Recovery no longer serves merely as a means of survival—it becomes a powerful tool for maintaining momentum throughout extended battles. A skilled Spellblade can rapidly adapt to changing circumstances, converting the resource they possess into the one they require most with remarkable efficiency.

The constant refinement of this technique also strengthens the Spellblade's natural vitality. Their body, mind, and mana recover in harmony during rest, ensuring they return to battle sooner and in far better condition than most warriors or mages.

- This talent cannot reduce your HP below **1**.

#### Passive

### Lore

Among the greatest Spellblade Orders, Rank IV is known as the **Abundant Exchange**. At this stage, the practitioner has moved beyond simply conserving energy—they have learned to draw upon the natural arcane currents surrounding all living things, allowing each conversion to yield greater results than would otherwise be possible.

Masters caution that this is not the creation of energy from nothing. Instead, it is the result of flawless efficiency, discipline, and an intimate understanding of the world's arcane flow. To outsiders it appears miraculous, but to a Spellblade it is simply another expression of balance.

Veterans often say that a master of Mystic Recovery no longer fears exhaustion, for they have learned to transform every remaining reserve into exactly what the battle demands.

> *"The world is filled with untapped strength. Master yourself first, and the currents of mana will do the rest."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 5
### Description

Mystic Recovery reaches its ultimate expression as the Spellblade becomes a true master of internal transmutation. Their body, mind, and mana core exist in complete harmony, allowing energy to flow effortlessly between health, stamina, and mana with extraordinary efficiency. Every conversion is no longer a desperate sacrifice, but a deliberate manipulation of the life force that binds all things.

At Rank V, the Spellblade is capable of amplifying every exchange through flawless control of ambient mana. By drawing upon the natural arcane currents surrounding them, they recover far more than they expend, making Mystic Recovery one of the defining techniques of the Order. Even in the longest battles, a master Spellblade can adapt to changing circumstances, ensuring the resource they need is always within reach.

Combined with their exceptional natural regeneration, the Spellblade becomes remarkably self-sufficient. Though never immortal, they possess an endurance that few warriors or spellcasters can hope to match.

### Lore

Within the oldest Spellblade Orders, Rank V is known as the **Perfect Cycle**, representing absolute mastery over the three pillars of life: body, spirit, and mana. Those who achieve this level no longer think of health, stamina, and mana as separate resources. To them, they are simply different forms of the same energy, endlessly flowing and changing with purpose.

Legends tell of masters who fought for days without collapsing, continually redirecting their strength to meet the demands of battle. While others exhausted themselves through reckless expenditure, these Spellblades maintained an elegant balance, always possessing exactly what they needed when they needed it most.

To master Mystic Recovery is to embody one of the oldest teachings of the Spellblade: true power is not measured by how much energy you possess, but by how perfectly you can command its flow.

> *"The river never fears running dry, for it understands that every current returns. Become the current, and your strength shall never truly leave you."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well
