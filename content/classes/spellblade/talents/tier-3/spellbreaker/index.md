# Spellbreaker

## Overview
Spellbreaker is one of the most revered techniques practiced by the Spellblade Orders, allowing a warrior to challenge hostile magic with nothing more than steel, mana, and unwavering resolve. Rather than avoiding incoming spells, the Spellblade steps forward, channeling arcane energy through their weapon to sever the spell before it can reach its intended target.

Executing the technique demands exceptional timing and concentration. The Spellblade must accurately read the flow of mana within an incoming spell and strike at precisely the right moment. A perfectly timed cut causes the spell's magical structure to collapse, dispersing its energy harmlessly into the air. However, hesitation or poor judgement can leave the Spellblade fully exposed to the attack, while a catastrophic mistake may cause the unstable mana to erupt against its would-be defender.

At Rank I, only the simplest travelling spells can be intercepted, making Spellbreaker a difficult but immensely rewarding technique for those willing to master its risks.

## Rank 1
### Description

### Effects

- Spend **1 Action Point** and **20 MP** to activate **Spellbreaker**.
- You may only target **hostile travelling magical effects** that pass within your weapon's reach (such as bolts, lances, spears, beams, or magical projectiles).
- Make a **Weapon Attack Roll** against the Spellbreak DC.

#### Spellbreak DC

| Spell Rank | DC |
|------------|---:|
| Cantrip | 14 |
| Weak | 16 |
| Minor | 18 |

#### Results

- **Success:** The spell is severed and immediately dissipates, causing no effect.
- **Failure:** The spell continues normally and resolves against its target.
- **Natural 1 – Arcane Rebound:** The spell continues normally. You suffer an additional **1d8 Arcane Damage**, lose **10 MP**, and suffer any secondary elemental effects the spell would normally inflict, if applicable.

### Lore

Within the Spellblade Orders, Spellbreaker is regarded as the first true test of mastery. Apprentices spend countless hours studying the structure of magic, learning that every spell possesses a fragile core where its mana is held together. Finding that weakness in the heartbeat before impact is what separates a Spellblade from an ordinary warrior.

Many never master the technique. Some hesitate, allowing the spell to pass unhindered, while others strike too soon or too late and suffer the violent backlash of unstable mana. Those who succeed, however, become legends—warriors capable of standing before a charging fireball or lightning spear with complete confidence, trusting only in the edge of their blade.

> *"A Spellblade does not fear magic. They simply teach it where to break."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 2
### Description

Having mastered the fundamentals of Spellbreaker, the Spellblade learns to read the flow of increasingly complex magic. The intricate weave of hostile spells becomes clearer to the trained eye, allowing the Spellblade to identify weaknesses within their structure and strike with greater confidence.

The technique remains dangerous, but experience has refined the Spellblade's judgement. Their weapon moves with unwavering precision, intercepting hostile magic at the precise moment before its arcane structure stabilises. Every successful Spellbreak is a testament to discipline, timing, and an intimate understanding of the forces that shape magic itself.

At Rank II, the Spellblade is capable of challenging **Greater Spells**, proving that even advanced magic can be overcome through skill rather than overwhelming power.

## Rank 3
### Description

Spellbreaker has become an instinctive technique, allowing the Spellblade to challenge powerful magic with confidence and precision. Years of training have taught them to recognise the arcane threads that bind a spell together, revealing fleeting weaknesses invisible to ordinary spellcasters. The Spellblade no longer waits for magic to strike—they meet it head-on. Their blade becomes an instrument capable of severing spells before they fully manifest, dispersing the unstable mana into harmless fragments. Even powerful magic can be undone when the strike lands with perfect timing.

At Rank III, the Spellblade is capable of confronting **Strengthened Spells**, a feat few warriors would dare attempt. Although the risk of failure remains, experienced Spellblades know that a single decisive strike can completely alter the outcome of a battle.

## Rank 4
### Description

The Spellblade has ascended into the ranks of the greatest mage hunters, capable of severing immensely powerful spells that would overwhelm most warriors. Years of relentless training have sharpened their understanding of arcane structure, allowing them to identify even the smallest instability within advanced magic.

To an observer, the technique appears effortless. The Spellblade calmly watches an incoming spell, waiting until the final heartbeat before impact before delivering a single precise strike. Rather than overpowering the spell through brute force, they cut through the delicate threads holding its mana together, causing the entire construct to collapse into harmless fragments.

At Rank IV, the Spellblade can challenge both **Enhanced** and **Superior Spells**, an achievement that places them among the elite of the Spellblade Orders. Enemy mages quickly learn that even their strongest attacks are no longer guaranteed to reach their target.

Stories tell of Spellblades who marched through storms of arcane destruction, their blades flashing as spell after spell shattered into drifting motes of fading mana. To their allies, they appeared untouchable. To enemy mages, they became a nightmare that turned their greatest strength into uncertainty.

Masters teach that Spellbreaker is ultimately an act of understanding rather than destruction. Every spell possesses a weakness, and only those with absolute discipline can recognise it before the opportunity vanishes.

## Rank 5
### Description

Spellbreaker reaches its ultimate form, allowing the Spellblade to challenge some of the most devastating magic known throughout Asteria. Years of unwavering discipline have taught the Spellblade that every spell, no matter how complex, is built upon a delicate framework of mana. At Rank V, they possess the knowledge and precision to find that weakness in an instant and shatter it with a single, perfectly timed strike.

The technique is no longer driven by instinct alone—it has become an art. The Spellblade calmly studies the approaching spell, waiting until the final moment before cutting through its arcane core. To those who witness it, the impossible becomes reality as torrents of magical energy are split apart and dissolved into harmless fragments before they ever reach their target.

Only the greatest Spellblades are capable of wielding Spellbreaker at this level. Their mastery inspires allies and strikes fear into enemy spellcasters, who know that even their strongest spells may be reduced to nothing by the edge of a single enchanted blade.

### Effects

- Spend **1 Action Point** and **40 MP** to activate **Spellbreaker**.

### Lore

Within the oldest Spellblade Orders, Rank V is known as the **World Cleaver's Edge**. It is said that those who attain this mastery no longer see spells as overwhelming forces of destruction, but as intricate patterns of mana waiting to be unravelled. They understand that even the greatest magic possesses flaws—provided one has the courage and skill to strike them.

Legends speak of master Spellblades who stood before dragons, archmages, and ancient horrors, cutting apart torrents of magical destruction before pressing forward without hesitation. While even they cannot sever the reality-shaping power of **World Spells**, few other forms of magic remain beyond their reach.

To master Spellbreaker is to embody one of the oldest philosophies of the Spellblade Orders: magic is not an unstoppable force. Like steel, it can be studied, understood, and ultimately broken.

> *"Every spell has an ending. A master Spellblade simply chooses where it begins."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well
