# Arcane Arsenal

## Overview
Arcane Arsenal is one of the defining techniques of a true Spellblade. Rather than relying upon weapons forged by mortal hands, the Spellblade shapes their own mana into a physical weapon, forging an armament that perfectly reflects both their combat training and magical affinity. To a Spellblade, every weapon is simply another form their mana can assume.

When manifesting an Arcane Weapon, the Spellblade chooses both its physical form and magical affinity. Whether forging a blazing greatsword, a lightning-charged spear, a crystalline bow, or a blade of pure Arcane Mana, every creation is unique to its wielder. The weapon feels perfectly balanced, as though it were crafted specifically for the Spellblade's hand.

Although the technique appears effortless in the hands of a master, creating an Arcane Weapon requires remarkable control over mana. Every manifestation is a fusion of martial discipline and arcane precision, allowing the Spellblade to remain armed even when stripped of every physical possession.

## Rank 1
### Description

Arcane Arsenal is one of the defining techniques of a true Spellblade. Rather than relying upon weapons forged by mortal hands, the Spellblade shapes their own mana into a physical weapon, forging an armament that perfectly reflects both their combat training and magical affinity. To a Spellblade, every weapon is simply another form their mana can assume.

When manifesting an Arcane Weapon, the Spellblade chooses both its physical form and magical affinity. Whether forging a blazing greatsword, a lightning-charged spear, a crystalline bow, or a blade of pure Arcane Mana, every creation is unique to its wielder. The weapon feels perfectly balanced, as though it were crafted specifically for the Spellblade's hand.

Although the technique appears effortless in the hands of a master, creating an Arcane Weapon requires remarkable control over mana. Every manifestation is a fusion of martial discipline and arcane precision, allowing the Spellblade to remain armed even when stripped of every physical possession.

### Effects

- Spend **35 MP** and **1 Action Point** to manifest **one Arcane Weapon**.
- The Arcane Weapon lasts for **10 Minutes**, or until dismissed.
- You may only maintain **one Arcane Weapon** at a time.
- Choose **any weapon type** you are proficient with when the weapon is manifested.
- Choose one of your **known magical affinities** when the weapon is created. The weapon retains this affinity until it disappears.
- The Arcane Weapon counts as a **Magical Weapon**.
- The weapon deals its **normal weapon damage**, plus an additional **2d6** damage matching its chosen magical affinity.
- If disarmed, the Arcane Weapon immediately dissipates.
- Arcane Weapons cannot be traded, sold, permanently stored, or wielded by another creature.

### Lore

Within the oldest Spellblade Orders, Arcane Arsenal is regarded as the moment a student truly becomes self-sufficient. Masters teach that steel can dull, shatter, or be stolen, but knowledge cannot. A Spellblade who understands how to forge a weapon from mana will never truly stand unarmed.

Every Arcane Weapon bears traces of its creator's soul. Weapons forged from fire may glow with molten veins, while those shaped from lightning crackle with contained energy. Pure Arcane weapons shimmer like polished crystal, and no two manifestations are ever exactly alike. Veteran Spellblades are often recognised by the appearance of their Arsenal long before their names are spoken.

To wield Arcane Arsenal is to understand that true mastery is not measured by the weapons one owns, but by the ability to create the perfect weapon whenever it is needed.

> *"A warrior carries steel into battle. A Spellblade carries the knowledge to forge it from their own soul."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 2
### Description

The Spellblade's control over Arcane Arsenal has matured, allowing their manifested weapons to remain stable for far longer while requiring less mana to sustain. Every Arcane Weapon now feels less like a temporary creation and more like a true extension of the Spellblade's body, responding instantly to every movement and intention.

With greater experience comes greater refinement. The Spellblade learns to weave intricate mana patterns throughout the weapon during its creation, strengthening its structure and allowing it to endure prolonged combat without losing cohesion. The chosen magical affinity burns brighter within the weapon, making every strike a seamless combination of martial skill and arcane power.

Although still limited to manifesting a single weapon, experienced Spellblades no longer fear being separated from their equipment. Their weapon can be recreated whenever needed, ensuring they are always prepared for whatever battle lies ahead.

### Effects

- Spend **30 MP** and **1 Action Point** to manifest **one Arcane Weapon**.
- The Arcane Weapon lasts for **20 Minutes**, or until dismissed.
- You may only maintain **one Arcane Weapon** at a time.
- Choose **any weapon type** you are proficient with when the weapon is manifested.
- Choose one of your **known magical affinities** when the weapon is created. The weapon retains this affinity until it disappears.
- The Arcane Weapon counts as a **Magical Weapon**.
- The weapon deals its **normal weapon damage**, plus an additional **2d6** damage matching its chosen magical affinity.
- Arcane Weapons created through this talent are considered exceptionally stable and cannot be destroyed by mundane means.
- If disarmed, the Arcane Weapon immediately dissipates.
- Arcane Weapons cannot be traded, sold, permanently stored, or wielded by another creature.
- This rank replaces the effects of **Arcane Arsenal — Rank I**.

### Lore

Among the Spellblade Orders, Rank II is known as the **Forgemaster's Grip**, marking the stage where a Spellblade no longer creates a weapon through effort alone. Instead, the weapon forms naturally in response to its wielder's will, flowing from raw mana into solid form with remarkable speed and precision.

Masters often challenge apprentices to dismiss and recreate their weapon repeatedly until the process becomes instinctive. They teach that hesitation in battle can be fatal, and that a true Spellblade should never worry about losing their weapon, only about choosing the right one for the moment.

Veteran Spellblades often become recognised by their Arcane Arsenal alone. Whether forged from radiant light, living flame, crackling lightning, or pure Arcane Mana, every weapon reflects the personality and discipline of its creator, making each manifestation as unique as the warrior who wields it.

> *"A forged blade is carried by its master. An Arcane Weapon is carried by its master's will."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 3
### Description

Arcane Arsenal reaches a new level of mastery as the Spellblade learns that a weapon forged from mana is never fixed in form. Instead of dismissing and recreating their Arcane Weapon, the Spellblade can reshape it instantly into another weapon they have mastered, allowing them to adapt to the ever-changing flow of battle without interrupting their momentum.

Veteran Spellblades often transition seamlessly between weapons during combat. A longsword becomes a spear to engage a distant foe, a warhammer forms to crush heavy armour, or twin daggers become a greatsword in preparation for a decisive strike. To an outside observer, the weapon appears to dissolve into streams of mana before reforming almost instantly into its new shape.

This level of mastery marks the point where the Arcane Weapon truly becomes an extension of the Spellblade's will. The weapon no longer dictates the style of combat—the Spellblade simply creates the weapon that best suits the moment.

### Effects

- Spend **30 MP** and **1 Action Point** to manifest **one Arcane Weapon**.
- The Arcane Weapon lasts for **30 Minutes**, or until dismissed.
- You may only maintain **one Arcane Weapon** at a time.
- Choose **any weapon type** you are proficient with when the weapon is first manifested.
- Choose one of your **known magical affinities** when the weapon is created. The weapon retains this affinity until dismissed.
- The Arcane Weapon counts as a **Magical Weapon**.
- The weapon deals its **normal weapon damage**, plus an additional **3d6** damage matching its chosen magical affinity.
- **Once per turn**, you may spend **1 Action Point** to instantly reshape your Arcane Weapon into any other weapon you are proficient with without dismissing it. The weapon retains its chosen magical affinity.
- Arcane Weapons cannot be destroyed by mundane means.
- If disarmed, the Arcane Weapon immediately dissipates.
- Arcane Weapons cannot be traded, sold, permanently stored, or wielded by another creature.
- This rank replaces the effects of **Arcane Arsenal — Rank II**.

### Lore

Within the Spellblade Orders, Rank III is known as the **Living Arsenal**. Masters teach that no single weapon is perfect for every battle, and a true Spellblade should never be limited by the weapon they currently hold. Instead, they learn to view every weapon as merely another shape their mana can assume.

Watching a master of the Living Arsenal is a breathtaking display of martial artistry. Weapons shift effortlessly between strikes, each transformation occurring so fluidly that many enemies fail to realise the change until it is too late. Every form is deliberate, chosen to exploit an opening, counter an opponent's fighting style, or adapt to the changing battlefield.

To master the Living Arsenal is to abandon the idea of favourite weapons entirely. Every weapon becomes your weapon, and every battle becomes an opportunity to demonstrate complete mastery over both steel and mana.

> *"The greatest weapon is not the sword in your hand—it is the one you choose to create next."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 4
### Description

The Spellblade has achieved a level of mastery where a single Arcane Weapon is no longer sufficient. Through exceptional control over their mana, they can now maintain multiple manifestations simultaneously, allowing them to wield paired weapons or combine offence and defence without sacrificing the stability of their creations.

Rather than simply increasing the power of their arsenal, the Spellblade expands its versatility. Twin blades flow together in perfect harmony, a spear and shield appear as one seamless creation, or an axe and dagger allow the Spellblade to adapt instantly to shifting battlefield conditions. Every manifestation remains an extension of the Spellblade's will, responding as naturally as their own limbs.

Among the Spellblade Orders, Rank IV represents complete confidence in one's martial abilities. Masters no longer think about the weapon they require—they simply will it into existence, knowing their Arcane Arsenal will always provide the perfect tool for the task.

### Effects

- Spend **25 MP** and **1 Action Point** to manifest your **Arcane Arsenal**.
- Your Arcane Arsenal lasts for **1 Hour**, or until dismissed.
- Your Arcane Weapons deal their **normal weapon damage**, plus an additional **3d6** damage matching their chosen magical affinity.
- Choose one of your **known magical affinities** when your Arcane Arsenal is manifested. All manifested weapons share this affinity until dismissed.
- Arcane Weapons count as **Magical Weapons**.

When manifesting your Arcane Arsenal, choose one of the following:

- **One Two-Handed Weapon.**
- **Two One-Handed Weapons.**
- **One One-Handed Weapon and one Arcane Shield.**

Additional Benefits:

- You may reshape any manifested weapon into another weapon you are proficient with by spending **1 Action Point**.
- The chosen magical affinity remains unchanged when reshaping.
- Arcane Weapons cannot be destroyed by mundane means.
- If disarmed, only the affected Arcane Weapon dissipates. Remaining manifested equipment is unaffected.
- Arcane Weapons cannot be traded, sold, permanently stored, or wielded by another creature.
- This rank replaces the effects of **Arcane Arsenal — Rank III**.

### Lore

Within the oldest Spellblade Orders, Rank IV is known as the **Master Arsenal**. Reaching this stage signifies that a Spellblade no longer sees weapons as individual tools, but as parts of a complete fighting style. Every weapon exists only as another expression of their mana, ready to be reshaped whenever the battle demands.

Veterans often demonstrate this mastery by flowing effortlessly between weapon combinations without ever breaking their rhythm. A sword becomes twin daggers, twin daggers become a spear, and moments later a shield forms beside them as though it had always existed. To an observer, the transformations appear less like magic and more like the weapon itself is alive.

The greatest masters teach that adaptability is the true strength of Arcane Arsenal. Victory belongs not to the warrior carrying the strongest weapon, but to the one capable of creating the perfect weapon for every challenge.

> *"An ordinary warrior masters a weapon. A Spellblade masters every weapon before the battle has even begun."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 5
### Description

Arcane Arsenal reaches its ultimate expression, becoming the defining symbol of a true Spellblade Master. No longer bound by the limitations of conventional weapons, the Spellblade effortlessly shapes their mana into any weapon they have mastered, seamlessly adapting to every challenge without interrupting the flow of battle. Every manifestation is an extension of their body, mind, and soul, forged not from steel, but from absolute control over arcane energy.

At this level, the Arcane Arsenal no longer feels like a summoned weapon. It becomes a permanent companion while maintained, capable of shifting between countless forms without losing its magical affinity or structural integrity. Whether wielding twin blades, a towering greataxe, a spear and shield, or a finely crafted bow, the Spellblade is never without the perfect weapon for the moment.

Among the oldest Spellblade Orders, Rank V is known as the **Infinite Arsenal**. Masters believe this is the moment where the Spellblade transcends the idea of weapon mastery. They no longer wield weapons—they command them through will alone.

### Effects

- Spend **20 MP** and **1 Action Point** to manifest your **Arcane Arsenal**.
- Your Arcane Arsenal remains until **dismissed**, or until you become **Unconscious**.
- Your Arcane Weapons deal their **normal weapon damage**, plus an additional **3d6** damage matching their chosen magical affinity.
- Choose one of your **known magical affinities** when your Arcane Arsenal is manifested. All Arcane Weapons retain this affinity until dismissed.
- Arcane Weapons count as **Legendary Magical Weapons**.

When manifesting your Arcane Arsenal, you may choose:

- One Two-Handed Weapon.
- Two One-Handed Weapons.
- One One-Handed Weapon and one Arcane Shield.

#### Infinite Arsenal

- Once per turn, you may spend **1 Action Point** to instantly reshape any Arcane Weapon into another weapon you are proficient with.
- Reshaping does not dismiss the weapon or change its chosen magical affinity.
- Arcane Weapons cannot be destroyed by mundane means.
- If disarmed, only the affected Arcane Weapon dissipates and may be immediately recreated on your next turn.
- Arcane Weapons cannot be traded, sold, permanently stored, or wielded by another creature.

#### Weapon Mastery

Each manifested weapon gains an additional passive benefit based on its weapon type.

Examples include:

- **Sword** — +1 Weapon Attack Rolls.
- **Greatsword** — Reroll one weapon damage die once per attack.
- **Axe** — Critical Hits inflict **Bleeding**.
- **Warhammer** — Successful hits force a **Strength Saving Throw** or the target is **Knocked Prone**.
- **Spear** — Gain **+2 metres** weapon reach.
- **Bow** — Increased range and ignores **Half Cover**.
- **Arcane Shield** — Gain **+1 Armour** and Advantage against being **Disarmed**.

The GM may create additional mastery effects for other weapon types.

### Lore

Within the oldest Spellblade Orders, Rank V is spoken of only in legend. Those who achieve the Infinite Arsenal are said to possess perfect harmony between martial discipline and arcane mastery, creating weapons so flawless that they are often mistaken for relics forged by the gods themselves. Every weapon reflects the Spellblade's soul, carrying their chosen magical affinity in both form and function.

Masters of the Infinite Arsenal have been witnessed changing weapons in the blink of an eye, effortlessly adapting to every opponent and every battlefield. Enemies who prepare to face a swordsman suddenly find themselves duelling a spearman, while heavily armoured champions are met with crushing hammers moments later. To fight such a Spellblade is to face every weapon ever mastered by their Order.

The Infinite Arsenal is regarded as the pinnacle of weapon mastery. It teaches that true strength is never found within the weapon itself, but within the warrior capable of creating exactly what is needed at precisely the right moment.

> *"A king commands an army. A master Spellblade commands every weapon that army has ever carried."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well
