# Arcane Cut

## Overview
Arcane Cut is one of the most devastating techniques ever developed by the Spellblade Orders. Rather than empowering the blade itself, the Spellblade channels an immense quantity of mana along the weapon's edge before releasing it in a single overwhelming slash. The stored energy erupts from the blade as a massive crescent of arcane force, carving through the battlefield and striking everything caught within its path.

A Spellblade may unleash Arcane Cut as pure Arcane Mana or shape it into one of their mastered magical affinities. Pure Arcane Cuts are exceptionally stable and devastating against magical constructs, summoned creatures, and enchanted barriers, while elemental Arcane Cuts inherit the properties of their chosen element, allowing the attack to ignite, freeze, shock, or otherwise affect their targets.

Unlike Bladestorm, Arcane Cut is a technique of commitment rather than speed. It requires absolute concentration, physical strength, and precise mana control to release a single strike capable of deciding the outcome of an entire battle.

## Rank 1
### Description

Arcane Cut is one of the most devastating techniques ever developed by the Spellblade Orders. Rather than empowering the blade itself, the Spellblade channels an immense quantity of mana along the weapon's edge before releasing it in a single overwhelming slash. The stored energy erupts from the blade as a massive crescent of arcane force, carving through the battlefield and striking everything caught within its path.

A Spellblade may unleash Arcane Cut as pure Arcane Mana or shape it into one of their mastered magical affinities. Pure Arcane Cuts are exceptionally stable and devastating against magical constructs, summoned creatures, and enchanted barriers, while elemental Arcane Cuts inherit the properties of their chosen element, allowing the attack to ignite, freeze, shock, or otherwise affect their targets.

Unlike Bladestorm, Arcane Cut is a technique of commitment rather than speed. It requires absolute concentration, physical strength, and precise mana control to release a single strike capable of deciding the outcome of an entire battle.

### Effects

- Spend **2 Action Points**, **50 MP**, and **30 SP** to activate **Arcane Cut**.
- Arcane Cut may be used **once per Short Rest**.
- Choose either **Pure Arcane** or one of your known **magical affinities** before releasing the attack.
- Release a **15 metre long, 1 metre wide** wave of mana in a straight line.
- Every creature within the attack's path must make a **Wisdom Saving Throw** against your **Spellblade Save DC**.
- **Failure:** The target suffers your **normal weapon damage** plus an additional **3d10** damage of the chosen damage type.
- **Success:** The target takes **half of all damage**.

#### Arcane Charge

If you do not have **2 available Action Points** during your current turn, you may begin charging Arcane Cut.

- Spend **1 Action Point** to begin charging.
- Your weapon visibly gathers Arcane Mana or your chosen element.
- At the start of your next turn, spend **1 additional Action Point** to immediately release Arcane Cut.
- Mana and Stamina are only spent when the attack is released.

#### Interrupted Charge

If the charge is interrupted before release by being **Disarmed**, **Stunned**, **Unconscious**, your weapon being destroyed or willingly released, or any effect that prevents you from completing the technique:

- Arcane Cut immediately fails.
- The talent use is expended.
- The committed Action Point is lost.
- You lose **50%** of the Mana and Stamina cost as the unstable energy disperses harmlessly.

#### Weapon Conduit

Arcane Cut may only be performed while wielding a melee weapon. The weapon serves as the conduit that shapes and releases the arcane wave. Without it, the technique cannot be completed.

- **Pure Arcane Cuts** are exceptionally effective against magical barriers, magical constructs, summoned creatures, and other magically created objects.
- **Elemental Arcane Cuts** retain the secondary effects of their chosen element where applicable.

### Lore

Within the Spellblade Orders, Arcane Cut is known as the **First Severance**, the technique that proves a Spellblade's reach extends far beyond the length of their blade. Masters teach that steel is merely the vessel—the true weapon is the mana released from its edge.

Each Spellblade develops a unique Arcane Cut reflecting their magical affinity. Some unleash silent crescents of shimmering mana capable of cleaving enchanted stone, while others release roaring infernos, crashing tides, razor winds, or crackling lightning that embody their elemental mastery. Regardless of its appearance, every Arcane Cut is intended to end the battle with a single perfect strike.

Veterans caution apprentices that the greatest danger is not the attack itself, but the moments spent charging it. An impatient Spellblade releases an incomplete strike. An overconfident one may never release it at all.

> *"Steel begins the cut. Mana carries it. Patience decides whether it reaches its destination."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 2
### Description

The Spellblade has refined Arcane Cut into a far more stable and destructive technique. Greater control over the flow of mana allows the arcane wave to travel farther while maintaining its shape, increasing both its reach and its ability to cleave through multiple opponents. What was once a powerful finishing attack becomes a reliable battlefield technique capable of changing the tide of combat.

As mastery improves, the Spellblade learns to compress mana with even greater precision before its release. Whether formed from pure arcane energy or one of the Spellblade's magical affinities, the resulting wave carries tremendous force as it races across the battlefield, leaving little opportunity for enemies to escape its path.

Despite this growing mastery, Arcane Cut remains a technique demanding patience and commitment. Masters continue to remind their students that every charge leaves them vulnerable, and only those who understand the perfect moment to strike will realise the technique's full potential.

- Spend **2 Action Points**, **45 MP**, and **30 SP** to activate **Arcane Cut**.

- Every creature within the attack's path must make a **Wisdom Saving Throw** against your **Spellblade Save DC**.
- **Failure:** The target suffers your **normal weapon damage** plus an additional **4d10** damage of the chosen damage type.
- **Success:** The target takes **half of all damage**.

#### Arcane Charge

If you do not have **2 available Action Points** during your current turn, you may begin charging Arcane Cut.

- Spend **1 Action Point** to begin charging.
- Your weapon gathers Arcane Mana or your chosen magical affinity.
- At the start of your next turn, spend **1 additional Action Point** to immediately release Arcane Cut.
- Mana and Stamina are only spent when the attack is released.

- One use of Arcane Cut is consumed.
- The committed Action Point is lost.
- You lose **50%** of the Mana and Stamina cost as the unstable energy dissipates.

#### Weapon Conduit

Arcane Cut may only be performed while wielding a melee weapon. The weapon acts as the focus through which the arcane wave is shaped and released.

- **Pure Arcane Cuts** deal Arcane Damage and are especially effective against magical barriers, summoned creatures, magical constructs, and other magically created objects.
- **Elemental Arcane Cuts** deal damage matching the chosen affinity and apply any secondary effects associated with that element.

### Lore

Within the Spellblade Orders, Rank II is known as the **Second Severance**, marking the stage where Arcane Cut evolves from a powerful technique into a dependable battlefield weapon. Spellblades no longer struggle to maintain the arcane wave as it leaves their blade, instead shaping it with enough precision to strike distant foes while preserving its destructive power.

Veterans often describe the technique as a lesson in restraint rather than aggression. Charging Arcane Cut teaches patience, awareness, and discipline, for a poorly timed release wastes both energy and opportunity. Those who master Rank II quickly earn a fearsome reputation, as enemies learn that allowing a Spellblade even a brief moment to prepare can have devastating consequences.

> *"Power gathers in silence. The wise recognise the danger before the blade is ever swung."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 3
### Description

Arcane Cut has evolved into a terrifying display of martial and arcane mastery. The Spellblade's command over mana has become so refined that every slash releases an immense wave of compressed energy capable of carving across the battlefield with incredible force. The expanding arc maintains its structure over far greater distances, making escape increasingly difficult for those caught within its path.

At this level, the Spellblade no longer relies solely upon raw power. Every Arcane Cut is carefully prepared, every movement deliberate, and every release perfectly timed. Whether formed from pure Arcane Mana or one of the Spellblade's elemental affinities, the attack becomes a devastating expression of discipline rather than uncontrolled destruction.

Veteran Spellblades teach that Rank III marks the point where Arcane Cut ceases to be simply an attack. It becomes a battlefield technique capable of forcing enemies to reposition, disrupting formations, and punishing those who underestimate the reach of a Spellblade.

## Rank 4

Arcane Cut has become a technique capable of carving through both armies and magic itself. The Spellblade's mastery over mana has reached the point where the arcane wave no longer loses cohesion as it tears across the battlefield. Every slash is compressed into an impossibly dense edge of magical force, capable of cleaving through physical obstacles and lesser magical defences before continuing towards its intended targets.

Whether released as pure Arcane Mana or shaped into one of the Spellblade's elemental affinities, Arcane Cut now carries tremendous destructive force. Entire battle lines have been broken by a single perfectly executed strike, while enemy spellcasters watch their magical barriers split apart beneath the weight of the attack.

Only the greatest Spellblades can safely wield Arcane Cut at this level. The amount of mana compressed into the blade is immense, and even the slightest lapse in concentration can cause the technique to collapse before it is released.

## Rank 5
### Description

Arcane Cut reaches its ultimate form, becoming one of the legendary techniques of the Spellblade Orders. The Spellblade's mastery over both steel and mana has become so absolute that a single swing can release an immense wave of condensed arcane energy capable of tearing across an entire battlefield. Every strike is the culmination of years of discipline, requiring flawless timing, unwavering concentration, and complete harmony between body, weapon, and mana.

At this level, the Spellblade chooses whether to unleash the attack as pure Arcane Mana or shape it into one of their mastered magical affinities. Pure Arcane Cuts are unrivalled against magical creations, wards, and summoned beings, while elemental Arcane Cuts embody the devastating nature of their chosen element, allowing the Spellblade to tailor the attack to the battlefield before them.

Only the greatest masters are entrusted with this technique. To witness a Rank V Arcane Cut is to see the very air split apart as an overwhelming wave of arcane power races forward, leaving destruction in its wake.

- Spend **2 Action Points**, **30 MP**, and **20 SP** to activate **Arcane Cut**.
- Arcane Cut may be used **5 times per Short Rest**.

- Release a **40 metre long, 3 metre wide** wave of arcane energy in a straight line.

- Mana and Stamina are only spent when the technique is successfully released.

If the charge is interrupted before release by becoming **Disarmed**, **Stunned**, **Unconscious**, your weapon being destroyed or willingly released, or any effect that prevents the technique from being completed:

- Arcane Cut immediately fails.
- One use of Arcane Cut is expended.
- The committed Action Point is lost.
- You lose **50%** of the Mana and Stamina cost as the unstable energy violently disperses.

#### Weapon Conduit

Arcane Cut may only be performed while wielding a melee weapon. The weapon serves as the conduit that shapes and releases the arcane wave.

- **Pure Arcane Cuts** continue through creatures and may sever **nearly all magical barriers, magical constructs, summoned creatures, enchanted objects, and magical effects**, only being stopped by **Artifact-grade wards, Divine Magic, or World Magic**.
- **Elemental Arcane Cuts** deal damage matching the chosen magical affinity and apply all secondary effects associated with that element where applicable.
- Arcane Cut ignores **Half Cover** and **Three-Quarters Cover**.

### Lore

Within the oldest Spellblade Orders, Rank V is known as the **World Severance**, a title spoken with reverence by warriors and mages alike. It is said that those who master this technique no longer swing a sword—they command a river of arcane power capable of carving through armies, fortifications, and magical defences with a single flawless motion.

Legends tell of masters who ended sieges by cleaving through enchanted fortress walls, split the attacks of powerful archmages before they reached their allies, and forced entire battalions to scatter before the advancing wave of mana. Yet every tale shares the same lesson: the technique is not born from overwhelming power alone, but from perfect patience and unwavering discipline.

To master Arcane Cut is to embody the highest ideals of the Spellblade Orders. Steel becomes the guide, mana becomes the blade, and the impossible becomes only another obstacle waiting to be severed.

> *"When your blade no longer ends at its edge, the battlefield itself becomes your reach."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well
