# Arcane Dominion

## Overview
Arcane Dominion is one of the highest disciplines practiced by the Spellblade Orders, allowing the Spellblade to claim a portion of the battlefield as their own. By plunging their weapon into the ground or focusing its point before them, they release a surge of arcane energy that spreads across the surrounding area, creating a Dominion where the natural flow of mana bends to their will.

Within this territory, the Spellblade's mastery over both swordsmanship and magic reaches its peak. Mana answers their commands more readily, while hostile spellcasters find their own magic becoming unstable and increasingly difficult to control. Every movement, every spell, and every strike is influenced by the Dominion's overwhelming arcane pressure.

Although incredibly powerful, maintaining a Dominion demands immense concentration and a tremendous amount of mana. Masters teach that the ability should only be invoked when the outcome of a battle hangs in the balance.

## Rank 1
### Description

Arcane Dominion is one of the highest disciplines practiced by the Spellblade Orders, allowing the Spellblade to claim a portion of the battlefield as their own. By plunging their weapon into the ground or focusing its point before them, they release a surge of arcane energy that spreads across the surrounding area, creating a Dominion where the natural flow of mana bends to their will.

Within this territory, the Spellblade's mastery over both swordsmanship and magic reaches its peak. Mana answers their commands more readily, while hostile spellcasters find their own magic becoming unstable and increasingly difficult to control. Every movement, every spell, and every strike is influenced by the Dominion's overwhelming arcane pressure.

Although incredibly powerful, maintaining a Dominion demands immense concentration and a tremendous amount of mana. Masters teach that the ability should only be invoked when the outcome of a battle hangs in the balance.

### Effects

- Spend **1 Action Point** and **80 MP** to activate **Arcane Dominion**.
- Arcane Dominion lasts for **3 Turns**.
- **1 Use per Short Rest.**
- Create a **10 metre radius** Arcane Dominion centred on yourself. The Dominion moves with you.

While inside your Arcane Dominion:

#### You gain:

- **+2** to Weapon Attack Rolls.
- **+2** to Spell Attack Rolls.
- Regain **10 MP** at the start of each of your turns.
- Regain **5 SP** at the start of each of your turns.

#### Enemy creatures gain:

- **Disadvantage** on Concentration Checks.
- **-2** to Spell Attack Rolls.
- Mana restoration is reduced by **50%** while inside the Dominion.

Only one Arcane Dominion may exist at a time.

### Lore

Among the oldest Spellblade Orders, Arcane Dominion is known as the **Master's Circle**, a technique reserved for those whose understanding of mana extends beyond their own body. Rather than merely controlling the magic within themselves, the Spellblade learns to influence the magical currents surrounding them, shaping the battlefield into an extension of their own will.

Those who witness the technique often describe the air itself changing. Arcane runes shimmer across the ground, the atmosphere vibrates with restrained magical energy, and even seasoned spellcasters find their magic becoming sluggish beneath the Dominion's oppressive influence.

Veterans teach that a true Spellblade never seeks to control the entire battlefield. Instead, they create a single place where victory becomes inevitable.

> *"Do not chase the battle. Make the battle come to you."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 2
### Description

The Spellblade's understanding of Arcane Dominion has deepened, allowing them to establish a larger and more stable zone of arcane influence. Rather than simply empowering themselves, the Spellblade begins manipulating the very flow of mana across the battlefield, creating an area where allies flourish and enemy spellcasters struggle to maintain control.

Experienced Spellblades no longer create their Dominion beneath their own feet. Instead, they project it onto the battlefield, placing it exactly where it will have the greatest tactical impact. Whether protecting allies, controlling an objective, or denying enemy mages access to stable mana, every Dominion becomes a carefully planned extension of the Spellblade's strategy.

Veterans often describe Arcane Dominion as an invisible fortress. While walls cannot be seen, every creature entering the area immediately feels the overwhelming presence of disciplined arcane energy pressing against them.

### Effects

- Spend **1 Action Point** and **75 MP** to activate **Arcane Dominion**.
- Arcane Dominion lasts for **4 Turns**.
- **2 Uses per Short Rest.**
- Place an **Arcane Dominion** anywhere within **20 metres**.
- The Dominion creates a **12 metre radius** stationary zone and does **not** move once created.
- Only one Arcane Dominion may exist at a time.

#### Allied Creatures inside the Dominion

- Gain **+2** to Weapon Attack Rolls.
- Gain **+2** to Spell Attack Rolls.
- Recover **15 MP** at the start of their turn.
- Recover **10 SP** at the start of their turn.
- Gain **Advantage** on Concentration Checks.

#### Enemy Creatures inside the Dominion

- Suffer **-2** to Spell Attack Rolls.
- Have **Disadvantage** on Concentration Checks.
- Mana regeneration is reduced by **50%**.
- Teleportation and dimensional movement into or out of the Dominion requires a successful **Wisdom Saving Throw** against your **Spellblade Save DC**. On a failure, the effect is cancelled but the resources spent are still consumed.

### Lore

Within the Spellblade Orders, Rank II is known as the **Commander's Dominion**, marking the point where the battlefield itself begins responding to the Spellblade's presence. Rather than merely strengthening themselves, accomplished Spellblades learn to reshape the magical currents around an entire area, turning favourable ground into an overwhelming strategic advantage.

Military commanders have long sought the support of Spellblades capable of establishing a Dominion. Entire battles have been won simply because allied forces fought within its protective influence while enemy mages found their spells weakened and unreliable. Many armies now consider a veteran Spellblade as valuable as an entire company of elite soldiers.

Masters teach that Arcane Dominion is not simply a display of magical power—it is an expression of battlefield leadership. A wise Spellblade does not chase victory. They create a place where victory naturally follows.

> *"A battlefield belongs to whoever commands its flow. Arcane Dominion ensures that flow is yours."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 3
### Description

Arcane Dominion has evolved into a true nexus of arcane power. The Spellblade no longer simply creates an area of magical influence—they reshape the battlefield itself, drawing surrounding mana into a stable domain where every action is influenced by their mastery. Within this territory, allies fight with renewed confidence while enemy spellcasters struggle against an oppressive tide of arcane resistance.

The Dominion is no longer merely a defensive technique. Veteran Spellblades deliberately establish their Dominion before a battle begins, forcing opponents to either fight on unfavourable ground or surrender valuable territory. Every movement within the Dominion is dictated by the Spellblade's overwhelming control over the surrounding mana currents.

Among the Spellblade Orders, Rank III is regarded as the point where the Dominion becomes an extension of its creator. Allies instinctively rally within its borders, while enemies quickly learn that remaining inside the Dominion only strengthens the Spellblade's advantage.

### Effects

- Spend **1 Action Point** and **70 MP** to activate **Arcane Dominion**.
- Arcane Dominion lasts for **5 Turns**.
- **3 Uses per Short Rest.**
- Place an **Arcane Dominion** anywhere within **25 metres**.
- The Dominion creates a **15 metre radius** stationary zone.
- Only one Arcane Dominion may exist at a time.

#### Allied Creatures inside the Dominion

- Gain **+3** to Weapon Attack Rolls.
- Gain **+3** to Spell Attack Rolls.
- Recover **20 MP** at the start of their turn.
- Recover **15 SP** at the start of their turn.
- Gain **Advantage** on Concentration Checks.
- Increase the range of **Spells**, **Arcane Cut**, and other ranged magical attacks by **5 metres** while cast from within the Dominion.

#### Enemy Creatures inside the Dominion

- Suffer **-3** to Spell Attack Rolls.
- Have **Disadvantage** on Concentration Checks.
- Mana regeneration is reduced by **75%**.
- Teleportation and dimensional movement into or out of the Dominion requires a successful **Wisdom Saving Throw** against your **Spellblade Save DC**. On a failure, the effect is cancelled and all resources spent are still consumed.
- Ranged magical attacks originating from within the Dominion have their maximum range reduced by **5 metres**.

### Lore

Within the Spellblade Orders, Rank III is known as the **Arcane Nexus**. It represents the moment a Spellblade ceases to merely influence the battlefield and instead begins controlling it. Every thread of mana flowing through the Dominion bends towards the Spellblade's will, empowering allies while resisting hostile magic.

Many famous battles throughout Asteria have been won not through superior numbers, but through the careful placement of an Arcane Nexus. Enemy commanders often find themselves forced to abandon favourable positions simply to escape its overwhelming influence, while allied forces fight with renewed determination beneath the Dominion's protective embrace.

Masters teach that the battlefield itself is a weapon. The greatest Spellblades do not overpower their enemies through strength alone—they create a battlefield where victory becomes the only possible outcome.

> *"Master your blade, then your mana. One day you will master the ground beneath your enemies' feet."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 4
### Description

Arcane Dominion has evolved into one of the greatest battlefield techniques known to the Spellblade Orders. No longer content with merely empowering allies and suppressing enemy magic, the Spellblade now commands the very flow of arcane energy throughout the Dominion. Every current of mana bends towards their will, allowing them to influence battles far beyond the reach of their blade.

Within the Dominion, the Spellblade becomes the unquestioned master of the battlefield. Every ally benefits from the stabilising currents of mana, while enemy spellcasters find their magic becoming increasingly unstable. Even space itself begins to obey the Spellblade's command, allowing certain techniques to be unleashed from anywhere within the Dominion rather than solely from the Spellblade's own position.

Veteran Spellblades often establish their Dominion before a battle begins, forcing their enemies to either retreat from valuable ground or fight on terrain completely controlled by the Spellblade.

### Effects

- Spend **1 Action Point** and **65 MP** to activate **Arcane Dominion**.
- Arcane Dominion lasts for **6 Turns**.
- **4 Uses per Short Rest.**
- Place an **Arcane Dominion** anywhere within **30 metres**.
- The Dominion creates an **18 metre radius** stationary zone.
- Only one Arcane Dominion may exist at a time.

#### Allied Creatures inside the Dominion

- Gain **+3** to Weapon Attack Rolls.
- Gain **+3** to Spell Attack Rolls.
- Recover **25 MP** at the start of their turn.
- Recover **20 SP** at the start of their turn.
- Gain **Advantage** on Concentration Checks.
- Increase the range of all spells and magical abilities by **10 metres** while cast from within the Dominion.

#### Enemy Creatures inside the Dominion

- Suffer **-3** to Spell Attack Rolls.
- Have **Disadvantage** on Concentration Checks.
- Mana regeneration is prevented while inside the Dominion.
- Teleportation, dimensional travel and similar magical movement require a successful **Wisdom Saving Throw** against your **Spellblade Save DC**. On a failure, the effect is cancelled and all resources spent are still consumed.
- Magical barriers and summoned creatures within the Dominion have **reduced magical resilience** at the GM's discretion.

#### Arcane Conduit

While standing inside your Arcane Dominion, you may originate compatible **Spellblade Talents** from **any point within the Dominion** instead of your own position.

Examples include:

- Arcane Cut
- Spellbreaker
- Arcane Rush
- Arcane Pursuit

The chosen talent is treated as though it originated from that location.

### Lore

Within the oldest Spellblade Orders, Rank IV is known as the **Master's Dominion**. It is the point where the battlefield itself becomes an extension of the Spellblade's mind. Veteran masters no longer fight from a single position. Instead, they command every corner of their Dominion as though they were physically standing there, making it nearly impossible for enemies to predict where the next attack will come from.

Stories tell of Spellblades defending entire bridges, castle gates and mountain passes alone, not because they were stronger than their enemies, but because every inch of ground within their Dominion belonged to them. Enemy commanders often realised too late that entering the Dominion meant accepting the Spellblade's rules of engagement.

To achieve the Master's Dominion is to understand that true victory is not earned through overwhelming strength, but through absolute control of the battlefield itself.

> *"Do not ask where the Spellblade stands. Ask where their Dominion ends."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 5
### Description

Arcane Dominion reaches its ultimate form, becoming the pinnacle of battlefield manipulation within the Spellblade Orders. The Spellblade no longer influences the flow of mana—they command it. Every current of magical energy bends to their will, transforming a vast section of the battlefield into territory where allies flourish and enemies find their magic weakened beneath overwhelming arcane pressure.

Within the Dominion, the Spellblade's mastery extends beyond their own position. Their awareness of every arcane current allows them to direct attacks, reinforce allies, and suppress hostile spellcasters with unmatched precision. To fight within a master's Dominion is to battle on ground where the Spellblade dictates the rules of engagement.

Only a handful of Spellblades throughout history have achieved this level of mastery. Entire armies have been forced to abandon strategic positions rather than challenge a fully established Dominion, earning its masters the title of battlefield architects.

### Effects

- Spend **1 Action Point** and **60 MP** to activate **Arcane Dominion**.
- Arcane Dominion lasts for **8 Turns**.
- **5 Uses per Short Rest.**
- Place an Arcane Dominion anywhere within **35 metres**.
- The Dominion creates a **20 metre radius** stationary zone.
- Only one Arcane Dominion may exist at a time.

#### Allied Creatures inside the Dominion

- Gain **+4** to Weapon Attack Rolls.
- Gain **+4** to Spell Attack Rolls.
- Recover **30 MP** at the start of their turn.
- Recover **20 SP** at the start of their turn.
- Gain **Advantage** on Concentration Checks.
- Increase the range of spells and magical abilities by **10 metres** while cast from within the Dominion.

#### Enemy Creatures inside the Dominion

- Suffer **-4** to Spell Attack Rolls.
- Have **Disadvantage** on Concentration Checks.
- Cannot naturally regenerate Mana while inside the Dominion.
- Teleportation, dimensional travel and similar magical movement require a successful **Wisdom Saving Throw** against your **Spellblade Save DC**. On a failure, the effect is cancelled and all resources spent are still consumed.
- Magical barriers, summoned creatures and magical constructs within the Dominion have reduced magical resilience at the GM's discretion.

#### Arcane Conduit

While standing inside your Arcane Dominion, you may originate compatible Spellblade Talents from **any point within the Dominion** instead of your own position.

Examples include:

- Arcane Cut
- Spellbreaker
- Arcane Rush
- Arcane Pursuit

#### Dominion Mastery

Once per turn, you may spend **1 Action Point** to relocate your entire Arcane Dominion up to **10 metres** in any direction. All effects immediately move with the Dominion.

### Lore

Within the oldest Spellblade Orders, Rank V is known as the **Grand Dominion**. Masters who reach this stage no longer fight as individuals; they command the battlefield itself. Every rune, every current of mana and every movement within the Dominion becomes part of an intricate design known only to its creator.

Legends tell of Grand Dominion Masters holding bridges against entire armies, silencing circles of archmages, and turning hopeless battles into decisive victories simply by controlling where the fight would take place. Their enemies quickly learn that strength alone means little when every step is taken within the Spellblade's domain.

The highest lesson of Arcane Dominion is simple: victory belongs not to the strongest warrior, but to the one who decides where the battle is fought.

> *"Master your blade, command your mana, and one day even the battlefield will answer your call."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well
