# Arcane Catalyst

## Overview
Arcane Catalyst is one of the most closely guarded techniques of the Spellblade Orders. Rather than drawing mana from within themselves alone, the Spellblade temporarily becomes a living conduit through which the surrounding arcane currents naturally flow. Every spell becomes easier to shape, every weapon strike carries greater magical force, and the connection between blade and mana reaches an extraordinary level of harmony.

As the Catalyst awakens, brilliant streams of arcane energy spiral around the Spellblade, continuously feeding their Arcane Weapon and reinforcing every technique they perform. The air shimmers with unstable magical energy as mana is drawn from the environment itself before being refined into perfectly controlled power.

Unlike Avatar of the Blade, Arcane Catalyst is not about overwhelming strength. It is about absolute efficiency, allowing the Spellblade to accomplish far more with every point of mana they expend.

## Rank 1
### Description

Arcane Catalyst is one of the most closely guarded techniques of the Spellblade Orders. Rather than drawing mana from within themselves alone, the Spellblade temporarily becomes a living conduit through which the surrounding arcane currents naturally flow. Every spell becomes easier to shape, every weapon strike carries greater magical force, and the connection between blade and mana reaches an extraordinary level of harmony.

As the Catalyst awakens, brilliant streams of arcane energy spiral around the Spellblade, continuously feeding their Arcane Weapon and reinforcing every technique they perform. The air shimmers with unstable magical energy as mana is drawn from the environment itself before being refined into perfectly controlled power.

Unlike Avatar of the Blade, Arcane Catalyst is not about overwhelming strength. It is about absolute efficiency, allowing the Spellblade to accomplish far more with every point of mana they expend.

### Effects

Spend **1 Action Point** and **100 MP** to activate **Arcane Catalyst**.

Arcane Catalyst lasts for **5 Turns**.

**1 Use per Long Rest.**

While Arcane Catalyst is active:

- All Spellblade Talent Mana Costs are reduced by **25%** (minimum 1 MP).
- Recover **25 MP** at the start of each of your turns.
- Gain **+2** to Spell Attack Rolls.
- Gain **+2** to Spellblade Save DC.
- Arcane Edge remains active without paying its upkeep cost.
- Arcane Arsenal gains **+1d6** magical damage.
- Arcane Cut deals an additional **1d10** damage.
- Spellbreaker gains **+2** to Spellbreaker Rolls.
- Arcane Dominion restores an additional **10 MP** to allies inside the Dominion.

When Arcane Catalyst ends:

- Gain **Mana Fatigue** until the end of your next turn.
- During Mana Fatigue, your Mana regeneration is reduced by **50%**.

### Lore

Within the Spellblade Orders, Arcane Catalyst is known as the **Living Conduit**, representing complete mastery over the movement of mana itself. Rather than forcing mana to obey, the Spellblade learns to guide it naturally, allowing arcane energy to pass effortlessly through their body before being shaped into devastating techniques.

Only the greatest masters can maintain this delicate balance. Too little control causes the surrounding mana to disperse harmlessly, while too much force risks catastrophic feedback capable of injuring the Spellblade from within. Those who succeed become living nexuses of magical energy, their presence alone strengthening the flow of mana around them.

Legends speak of masters who fought for hours without exhausting their reserves, each spell flowing into the next with effortless precision. To witness Arcane Catalyst is to see mana itself willingly answer the call of a true Spellblade.

> *"Power is not found in how much mana you possess... but in how perfectly you allow it to flow."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 2
### Description

The Spellblade's understanding of Arcane Catalyst has matured, allowing them to refine the flow of mana with remarkable precision. Every spell, every technique, and every weapon enhancement is executed with minimal waste, enabling the Spellblade to sustain their most demanding abilities for extended periods without exhausting their reserves.

As Arcane Catalyst awakens, streams of magical energy continuously circulate around the Spellblade before flowing back into their body, creating a near-perfect cycle of mana. Their Arcane Weapon shines brighter, their spells become easier to control, and every technique feels lighter to perform. Veteran Spellblades describe the sensation as though the world itself is willingly lending them its mana.

While still requiring tremendous focus, Rank II transforms Arcane Catalyst from a powerful enhancement into a reliable source of sustained magical supremacy, allowing the Spellblade to dominate prolonged engagements where other mages would quickly exhaust themselves.

### Effects

- Spend **1 Action Point** and **90 MP** to activate **Arcane Catalyst**.
- Arcane Catalyst lasts for **6 Turns**.
- **2 Uses per Long Rest.**

While Arcane Catalyst is active:

- All Spellblade Talent Mana Costs are reduced by **35%** (minimum 1 MP).
- Recover **35 MP** at the start of each of your turns.
- Gain **+3** to Spell Attack Rolls.
- Gain **+3** to your Spellblade Save DC.
- Arcane Edge remains active without paying its Mana Cost.
- Arcane Arsenal gains an additional **+1d6** magical damage.
- Arcane Cut deals an additional **2d10** damage.
- Spellbreaker gains **+3** to Spellbreaker Rolls.
- Arcane Dominion restores an additional **15 MP** to allied creatures within its area.
- Mystic Recovery restores **25%** additional HP, SP or MP while Arcane Catalyst is active.

#### Mana Conduit

While Arcane Catalyst is active, whenever you successfully defeat a hostile creature or successfully counter a spell using **Spellbreaker**, immediately recover **15 MP**.

When Arcane Catalyst ends:

- Gain **Mana Fatigue** until the end of your next turn.
- During Mana Fatigue, Mana regeneration is reduced by **50%**.

### Lore

Among the Spellblade Orders, Rank II is known as the **Flowing Conduit**. Masters teach that true magical strength is not measured by the size of one's Mana Pool, but by the efficiency with which every fragment of mana is used. Those who achieve this state appear tireless, maintaining devastating techniques long after other warriors and mages have exhausted themselves.

Veterans often describe the Flowing Conduit as a living river of mana. Every spell cast, every weapon strike, and every technique naturally feeds into the next, creating an endless rhythm that few opponents can withstand. Watching a master fight is like witnessing a current that never stops flowing—relentless, graceful, and impossible to interrupt.

To master Rank II is to learn that the greatest source of power is not limitless mana, but the wisdom to never waste it.

> *"A single drop of mana, perfectly placed, outweighs an ocean cast without purpose."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 3
### Description

Arcane Catalyst has evolved beyond simple efficiency, transforming the Spellblade into a living nexus through which vast currents of mana naturally flow. Every spell cast, every technique performed, and every strike delivered becomes part of an unbroken cycle of arcane energy. Rather than consuming mana alone, the Spellblade continuously refines and recycles it, dramatically reducing waste while empowering every technique.

The Spellblade's chosen magical affinity radiates from their body in waves, surrounding them with an ever-changing aura of concentrated mana. Their Arcane Weapon hums with overwhelming magical power, and even experienced mages can feel the surrounding arcane currents bending toward the Catalyst's presence.

Masters of the Spellblade Orders describe Rank III as the point where a Spellblade no longer forces mana to obey. Instead, mana willingly gathers around them, recognising them as a perfect conduit through which it may safely flow.

### Effects

- Spend **1 Action Point** and **80 MP** to activate **Arcane Catalyst**.
- Arcane Catalyst lasts for **7 Turns**.
- **3 Uses per Long Rest.**

While Arcane Catalyst is active:

- All **Spellblade Talent Mana Costs** are reduced by **50%** (minimum 1 MP).
- Recover **45 MP** at the start of each of your turns.
- Gain **+4** to Spell Attack Rolls.
- Gain **+4** to your Spellblade Save DC.
- Arcane Edge remains active without paying its Mana Cost.
- Arcane Arsenal gains an additional **+2d6** magical damage.
- Arcane Cut deals an additional **2d10** damage.
- Spellbreaker gains **+4** to Spellbreaker Rolls.
- Arcane Dominion restores an additional **20 MP** to allied creatures within its area.
- Mystic Recovery restores **50%** additional HP, SP or MP while Arcane Catalyst is active.

#### Arcane Overflow

Whenever you successfully:

- Defeat a hostile creature,
- Successfully counter a spell using **Spellbreaker**, or
- Restore HP, MP or SP using **Mystic Recovery**,

Immediately recover **25 MP**.

This effect may trigger **once per turn**.

When Arcane Catalyst ends:

- Gain **Mana Fatigue** until the end of your next turn.
- During Mana Fatigue, your natural Mana regeneration is reduced by **50%**, and Spellblade Talents cost **25% more MP**.

### Lore

Within the oldest Spellblade Orders, Rank III is known as the **Living Nexus**. Few Spellblades ever achieve this level of mastery, for it requires absolute harmony between body, blade, and mana. Those who succeed become walking centres of magical energy, drawing mana from the world around them and refining it with extraordinary efficiency.

Legends speak of Living Nexus masters continuing to fight long after entire battalions of mages had exhausted their reserves. Every spell seemed to fuel the next, every technique flowed effortlessly into another, and every strike strengthened the Spellblade instead of weakening them. To fight alongside such a master was to witness mana itself choosing its champion.

The Living Nexus is revered as proof that true mastery lies not in possessing limitless power, but in ensuring that no fragment of power is ever wasted.

> *"Mana is never truly spent. The wise simply learn how to make it return."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 4
### Description

Arcane Catalyst has reached a level where the Spellblade no longer relies solely upon the mana within their own body. Instead, they become a living conduit capable of drawing upon the surrounding arcane currents, weaving environmental mana into every strike, spell, and technique. Their presence alone alters the magical landscape, strengthening allies while destabilising hostile magic.

The Spellblade's chosen magical affinity radiates in brilliant waves, surrounding them with an ever-shifting mantle of arcane energy. Every movement leaves shimmering trails of mana behind, and every Spellblade technique flows together without interruption. Even veteran mages can feel the surrounding mana responding instinctively to the Catalyst's presence.

Among the Spellblade Orders, Rank IV is known as the **Grand Catalyst**, a title reserved for those whose mastery allows them to command not only their own mana, but the mana of the battlefield itself.

### Effects

- Spend **1 Action Point** and **70 MP** to activate **Arcane Catalyst**.
- Arcane Catalyst lasts for **8 Turns**.
- **4 Uses per Long Rest.**

While Arcane Catalyst is active:

- All **Spellblade Talent Mana Costs** are reduced by **50%** (minimum 1 MP).
- Recover **60 MP** at the start of each of your turns.
- Gain **+5** to Spell Attack Rolls.
- Gain **+5** to your **Spellblade Save DC**.
- Arcane Edge remains active without paying its Mana Cost.
- Arcane Arsenal gains an additional **+2d6** magical damage.
- Arcane Cut deals an additional **3d10** damage.
- Spellbreaker gains **+5** to Spellbreaker Rolls.
- Arcane Dominion restores an additional **25 MP** to allied creatures within its area.
- Mystic Recovery restores **75%** additional HP, SP or MP while Arcane Catalyst is active.

#### Arcane Convergence

While standing within your **Arcane Dominion**, your first Spellblade Talent used each turn costs **0 MP**.

#### Arcane Overflow

Once per turn, whenever you:

- Defeat a hostile creature.
- Successfully counter a spell with **Spellbreaker**.
- Restore resources using **Mystic Recovery**.

Recover **35 MP** immediately.

#### Mana Mastery

During Arcane Catalyst, your Spellblade Talents cannot suffer **Mana Feedback**, **Spell Feedback**, or similar effects caused by insufficient mana or unstable mana flow.

When Arcane Catalyst ends:

- Gain **Mana Fatigue** until the end of your next turn.
- During Mana Fatigue:
  - Natural Mana regeneration is reduced by **50%**.
  - Spellblade Talents cost **25% more MP**.

### Lore

Within the oldest Spellblade Orders, Rank IV is known as the **Grand Catalyst**, a title earned by only a handful of masters throughout history. At this stage, the Spellblade ceases to be merely a wielder of mana and instead becomes one of its greatest conductors. The surrounding magical currents willingly flow towards them, reinforcing every spell and every swing of their weapon.

Ancient accounts describe Grand Catalysts walking through battlefields where allied mages found their spells strengthened simply by fighting nearby, while hostile spellcasters struggled to maintain even the simplest incantations beneath the overwhelming pressure of the Catalyst's presence.

The greatest masters teach that true magical supremacy is not achieved by possessing endless mana, but by ensuring that every fragment of mana serves a purpose.

> *"The greatest conduit is not the one that holds the most power... but the one through which no power is ever wasted."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 5
### Description

Arcane Catalyst reaches its final evolution, transforming the Spellblade into the ultimate conduit of arcane power. Mana no longer feels like a finite resource but a living force flowing through every movement, every strike and every spell. The Spellblade no longer expends magic inefficiently—instead, they guide it with perfect precision, ensuring every fragment of mana fulfils its purpose before returning to the endless currents of the world.

As the Catalyst reaches its peak, the Spellblade's chosen affinity surrounds them in an overwhelming display of magical energy. Their Arcane Weapon pulses with incredible power, the air hums with condensed mana, and nearby spellcasters instinctively recognise that they stand in the presence of one who has mastered magic itself.

Within the Spellblade Orders, Rank V is known as the **Infinite Catalyst**. It is regarded as the highest achievement of magical discipline, proving that limitless power is not created through endless mana, but through flawless control over every drop that is used.

### Effects

- Spend **1 Action Point** and **60 MP** to activate **Arcane Catalyst**.
- Arcane Catalyst lasts for **10 Turns**.
- **5 Uses per Long Rest.**

While Arcane Catalyst is active:

- All **Spellblade Talent Mana Costs** are reduced by **50%** (minimum **1 MP**).
- Recover **75 MP** at the start of each of your turns.
- Gain **+6** to Spell Attack Rolls.
- Gain **+6** to your **Spellblade Save DC**.
- Arcane Edge remains active without paying its Mana Cost.
- Arcane Arsenal gains an additional **+3d6** magical damage.
- Arcane Cut deals an additional **3d10** damage.
- Spellbreaker gains **+6** to Spellbreaker Rolls.
- Arcane Dominion restores an additional **30 MP** to allied creatures within its area.
- Mystic Recovery restores **100%** additional HP, SP or MP while Arcane Catalyst is active.

#### Infinite Catalyst

Once per turn, the **first Spellblade Talent** you activate costs **0 MP**.

If you are standing within your **Arcane Dominion**, the first **two Spellblade Talents** you activate each turn cost **0 MP**.

#### Arcane Overflow

Once per turn, whenever you:

- Defeat a hostile creature.
- Successfully counter a spell using **Spellbreaker**.
- Restore HP, SP or MP using **Mystic Recovery**.

Recover **50 MP**.

Excess Mana restored while Arcane Catalyst is active may temporarily exceed your normal Maximum MP by **20%**. This excess Mana disappears when Arcane Catalyst ends.

#### Mana Mastery

While Arcane Catalyst is active:

- You cannot suffer **Mana Feedback**, **Spell Feedback**, or **Mana Burn** caused by your own Spellblade Talents.
- Your Spellblade Talents cannot have their Mana Costs increased by enemy effects.
- Your Mana cannot be drained by magical effects unless they originate from **Artifact**, **Divine**, or **World Magic**.

When Arcane Catalyst ends:

- Gain **Mana Fatigue** until the end of your next turn.
- During Mana Fatigue:
  - Natural Mana regeneration is reduced by **50%**.
  - Spellblade Talents cost **25%** more MP.

### Lore

Within the oldest Spellblade Orders, the Infinite Catalyst is considered the final lesson of magical mastery. It is said that those who reach this state cease to think about mana entirely. Every spell, every technique and every movement becomes instinctive, guided by an understanding so complete that the world's own arcane currents willingly answer their call.

Ancient chronicles describe Infinite Catalysts standing beside exhausted armies of mages, continuing to weave spell after spell while their reserves seemed impossible to deplete. Many believed they possessed limitless mana, yet the truth was far more remarkable—they simply wasted none.

The Infinite Catalyst is not remembered as the Spellblade with the greatest Mana Pool, but as the one who demonstrated that perfect control will always surpass limitless power.

> *"The fool seeks more mana. The master seeks to waste none."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well
