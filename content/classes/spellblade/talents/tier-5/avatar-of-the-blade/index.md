# Avatar of the Blade

## Overview
Avatar of the Blade is the ultimate expression of the Spellblade's lifelong pursuit of harmony between steel and mana. Upon activating this legendary technique, the Spellblade ceases to fight as an ordinary warrior. Every movement becomes instinctive, every strike perfectly balanced, and every thread of mana flows effortlessly through their body and weapon.

As the transformation begins, Arcane energy erupts around the Spellblade, surrounding them in a radiant aura matching their chosen magical affinity. Their Arcane Weapon shines with overwhelming power, leaving brilliant trails of mana through the air as reality itself seems to bend around every swing.

Only the greatest Spellblades in history have ever achieved this state. To allies it is a symbol of hope. To enemies it is the terrifying realization that they are no longer facing a warrior—they are facing the living embodiment of the Spellblade Orders.

## Rank 1
### Description

Avatar of the Blade is the ultimate expression of the Spellblade's lifelong pursuit of harmony between steel and mana. Upon activating this legendary technique, the Spellblade ceases to fight as an ordinary warrior. Every movement becomes instinctive, every strike perfectly balanced, and every thread of mana flows effortlessly through their body and weapon.

As the transformation begins, Arcane energy erupts around the Spellblade, surrounding them in a radiant aura matching their chosen magical affinity. Their Arcane Weapon shines with overwhelming power, leaving brilliant trails of mana through the air as reality itself seems to bend around every swing.

Only the greatest Spellblades in history have ever achieved this state. To allies it is a symbol of hope. To enemies it is the terrifying realization that they are no longer facing a warrior—they are facing the living embodiment of the Spellblade Orders.

### Effects

Spend **1 Action Point**, **150 MP**, and **50 SP** to enter **Avatar of the Blade**.

Avatar of the Blade lasts for **3 Turns**.

**1 Use per Long Rest.**

While Avatar of the Blade is active:

- Your Arcane Weapon immediately manifests if one is not already active.
- Your Arcane Weapon deals an additional **+2d6** damage.
- You gain **+2 Weapon Attack Rolls**.
- You gain **+2 Spell Attack Rolls**.
- You gain **+10 Movement Speed**.
- Arcane Edge may be activated without paying its Mana Cost.
- Arcane Arsenal may be reshaped without spending an Action Point.
- Arcane Cut no longer requires charging if both required Action Points are available during your turn.
- Spellbreaker gains **+2** to its Spellbreaker Roll.
- Arcane Phase Mana Cost is reduced by **50%**.

When Avatar of the Blade ends:

- Gain **Arcane Fatigue** until the end of your next turn.

### Lore

Within the oldest Spellblade Orders, Avatar of the Blade is spoken of with almost mythical reverence. It is said that only those who have achieved complete harmony between martial discipline and arcane mastery may awaken this legendary state. Those fortunate enough to witness it often describe the Spellblade as becoming less like a mortal warrior and more like living arcane energy wrapped in the shape of one.

Ancient records speak of legendary masters who single-handedly held fortress gates, shattered armies, and dueled archmages beneath the light of the stars while empowered by Avatar of the Blade. Though these stories have undoubtedly grown through centuries of retelling, every Spellblade agrees on one truth: no ordinary warrior can hope to match someone who has become one with both blade and mana.

To awaken the Avatar is not merely to gain power—it is to embody everything the Spellblade Orders have spent generations striving to achieve.

> *"The blade was never your weapon. It was always your soul waiting to take shape."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 2
### Description

The Spellblade's connection to the Avatar deepens, allowing them to maintain the transformation with greater stability and far less strain upon their body. The Avatar is no longer simply a burst of overwhelming power, but a state of complete harmony where blade, body, and mana act as one.

As the transformation begins, the Spellblade becomes surrounded by a brilliant aura reflecting their chosen magical affinity. Every movement appears effortless, every strike perfectly controlled, and every spell flows naturally through their weapon without hesitation. Veteran Spellblades describe this state as feeling less like wielding mana and more like becoming part of it.

Although still considered an extraordinary technique, Rank II marks the beginning of true mastery. The Avatar no longer merely enhances the Spellblade—it begins awakening the full potential of every technique they have mastered throughout their journey.

### Effects

- Spend **1 Action Point**, **140 MP**, and **45 SP** to activate **Avatar of the Blade**.
- Avatar of the Blade lasts for **4 Turns**.
- **2 Uses per Long Rest.**

While Avatar of the Blade is active:

- Your Arcane Weapon immediately manifests if one is not already active.
- Your Arcane Weapon gains **+2d6** additional damage.
- Gain **+3** to Weapon Attack Rolls.
- Gain **+3** to Spell Attack Rolls.
- Increase your Movement Speed by **15 metres**.
- Arcane Edge may be activated without paying its Mana Cost.
- Arcane Arsenal may be reshaped without spending an Action Point.
- Arcane Cut no longer requires charging if both required Action Points are available during your turn.
- Spellbreaker gains **+3** to Spellbreaker Rolls.
- Arcane Phase Mana Cost is reduced by **50%**.
- Arcane Dominion increases its radius by **5 metres** while Avatar of the Blade is active.

When Avatar of the Blade ends:

- Gain **Arcane Fatigue** until the end of your next turn.

### Lore

Within the oldest Spellblade Orders, Rank II is known as the **Awakened Avatar**. It is said that this is the point where the Spellblade no longer borrows power from the Avatar—they begin to live as one with it. Their mastery over mana becomes instinctive, allowing every technique to flow seamlessly into the next without hesitation or wasted motion.

Ancient journals describe Awakened Avatars as impossible opponents to predict. Every movement appeared effortless, every weapon transformation occurred without thought, and every spell seemed to arrive precisely when it would have the greatest effect. Enemies often believed they were facing several masters at once, when in truth it was a single Spellblade moving with perfect harmony.

The Awakened Avatar represents the first step towards true transcendence, proving that mastery is not measured by strength alone, but by absolute unity between steel and mana.

> *"When your blade follows your thoughts before your hands, the Avatar has truly awakened."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 3
### Description

The Avatar of the Blade has fully awakened within the Spellblade, transforming them into the perfect union of martial discipline and arcane mastery. No longer fighting as separate swordsman and mage, every movement, spell and strike becomes part of a single flawless rhythm. Mana responds instantly to every thought, allowing techniques that once required preparation to flow naturally into one another.

The Spellblade's chosen magical affinity engulfs their Arcane Arsenal, leaving radiant trails of elemental energy with every movement. To allies, the Avatar appears as a legendary guardian of the battlefield. To enemies, it is as though they are fighting a warrior whose blade exists everywhere at once.

Veteran masters describe Rank III as the point where the Avatar ceases to be a transformation and instead becomes the Spellblade's true nature. Every technique learned throughout their life reaches a level of perfection impossible under normal circumstances.

### Effects

- Spend **1 Action Point**, **130 MP**, and **40 SP** to activate **Avatar of the Blade**.
- Avatar of the Blade lasts for **5 Turns**.
- **3 Uses per Long Rest.**

While Avatar of the Blade is active:

- Your Arcane Weapon immediately manifests if one is not already active.
- Your Arcane Weapon gains **+3d6** additional damage.
- Gain **+4** to Weapon Attack Rolls.
- Gain **+4** to Spell Attack Rolls.
- Increase your Movement Speed by **20 metres**.
- Arcane Edge costs **no Mana** to activate.
- Arcane Arsenal may be reshaped **without spending an Action Point**.
- Arcane Cut no longer requires charging if both required Action Points are available.
- Spellbreaker gains **+4** to Spellbreaker Rolls.
- Arcane Phase Mana Cost is reduced by **75%**.
- Arcane Dominion increases its radius by **10 metres** while Avatar of the Blade is active.
- Bladestorm gains **one additional weapon attack** while Avatar of the Blade is active.

When Avatar of the Blade ends:

- Gain **Arcane Fatigue** until the end of your next turn.

### Lore

Within the Spellblade Orders, Rank III is known as the **Perfect Avatar**. It is said that this is the point where a Spellblade no longer commands mana—it willingly answers every command before the thought is fully formed. Their blade moves with impossible speed, their magic flows without hesitation, and every technique reaches heights thought unattainable by ordinary masters.

Ancient texts tell of Perfect Avatars standing alone against impossible odds, effortlessly weaving swordsmanship and magic together until even veteran archmages struggled to distinguish where steel ended and mana began. Those fortunate enough to witness the transformation often claim that the Spellblade no longer appears mortal, but instead resembles a living incarnation of their chosen magical affinity.

The Perfect Avatar is not simply stronger than an ordinary Spellblade. It is the embodiment of everything the Spellblade Orders have spent centuries striving to achieve—a warrior whose body, weapon and mana exist in complete harmony.

> *"When your blade no longer follows your hand, but your very soul, the Avatar has become complete."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 4
### Description

The Avatar of the Blade has become an almost divine manifestation of martial and arcane perfection. Upon awakening the Avatar, the Spellblade enters a state where every strike, every spell, and every movement becomes effortless. Their body is no longer constrained by the hesitation of mortal thought, allowing them to fight with flawless precision and impossible speed.

The Spellblade radiates overwhelming arcane power, their chosen magical affinity engulfing both their body and Arcane Arsenal. Every step leaves shimmering traces of mana, while every swing appears capable of cutting both steel and magic alike. To those who witness it, the Avatar resembles an ancient guardian forged from blade and arcane energy rather than flesh and blood.

Among the Spellblade Orders, Rank IV is regarded as the threshold of legend. Those capable of maintaining this form are said to have mastered every lesson their Order can teach, becoming living examples of perfect harmony between swordsmanship and magic.

### Effects

- Spend **1 Action Point**, **120 MP**, and **35 SP** to activate **Avatar of the Blade**.
- Avatar of the Blade lasts for **6 Turns**.
- **4 Uses per Long Rest.**

While Avatar of the Blade is active:

- Your Arcane Weapon immediately manifests if one is not already active.
- Your Arcane Weapon gains an additional **+3d6** damage.
- Gain **+5** to Weapon Attack Rolls.
- Gain **+5** to Spell Attack Rolls.
- Increase your Movement Speed by **25 metres**.
- Arcane Edge costs **no Mana**.
- Arcane Arsenal may be reshaped freely without spending an Action Point.
- Bladestorm gains **one additional weapon attack**.
- Arcane Cut no longer requires charging if you have **2 Action Points** available.
- Spellbreaker gains **+5** to Spellbreaker Rolls.
- Arcane Phase costs **no Mana** to activate.
- Arcane Dominion increases its radius by **10 metres**, and compatible Spellblade talents may originate from **any point** within the Dominion.

#### Avatar Mastery

While Avatar of the Blade is active:

- Your Spellblade talents ignore effects that would normally interrupt their activation unless you become **Unconscious**.
- Arcane Weapons cannot be disarmed or destroyed.
- You gain **Advantage** on all Concentration Checks.

When Avatar of the Blade ends:

- Gain **Arcane Fatigue** until the end of your next turn.

### Lore

Within the oldest Spellblade Orders, Rank IV is known as the **Ascendant Avatar**. Very few Spellblades ever reach this state, for it demands not only unmatched martial skill but complete unity with one's mana. Masters describe the transformation as becoming the perfect expression of the Order's ideals, where every action is instinctive and every technique flows together without conscious thought.

Legends tell of Ascendant Avatars walking unharmed through storms of magic, reshaping their weapons in the blink of an eye, and answering every attack with flawless precision. To allies they inspire absolute confidence. To enemies they represent the terrifying realisation that they are no longer fighting a mortal warrior, but the living incarnation of the Spellblade's ancient traditions.

To achieve the Ascendant Avatar is to prove that mastery is not measured by power alone, but by absolute control over every aspect of one's blade, body, and soul.

> *"The Avatar is not awakened by power. It is awakened when there is no longer any difference between the warrior, the blade, and the mana that binds them together."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well

## Rank 5
### Description

Avatar of the Blade reaches its ultimate form, transforming the Spellblade into the perfect union of warrior and mage. No longer merely wielding steel or commanding mana, the Spellblade becomes the living embodiment of both. Every movement flows with impossible precision, every spell is cast without hesitation, and every strike carries the overwhelming authority of a true master.

As the Avatar awakens, the Spellblade is engulfed by an immense aura of their chosen magical affinity. Their Arcane Arsenal shines with legendary brilliance, the battlefield itself trembling beneath the pressure of condensed mana. To allies, the Avatar inspires unwavering courage. To enemies, it is a terrifying reminder that they face someone who has surpassed the limits of mortal mastery.

Only the greatest Spellblades in history have ever achieved this transformation. Their names are remembered not for the battles they fought, but for the impossible victories they made appear effortless.

### Effects

- Spend **1 Action Point**, **100 MP**, and **30 SP** to activate **Avatar of the Blade**.
- Avatar of the Blade lasts for **8 Turns**.
- **5 Uses per Long Rest.**

While Avatar of the Blade is active:

- Your Arcane Weapon immediately manifests if one is not already active.
- Your Arcane Weapon gains **+3d6** additional damage.
- Gain **+6** to Weapon Attack Rolls.
- Gain **+6** to Spell Attack Rolls.
- Increase your Movement Speed by **30 metres**.
- Gain **Advantage** on all Concentration Checks.
- Arcane Weapons cannot be disarmed or destroyed.
- Arcane Edge costs **no Mana**.
- Arcane Arsenal may be reshaped freely without spending an Action Point.
- Bladestorm gains **one additional weapon attack**.
- Arcane Cut no longer requires charging if both required Action Points are available.
- Spellbreaker gains **+6** to Spellbreaker Rolls.
- Arcane Phase costs **no Mana** to activate.
- Arcane Dominion gains **+10 metres** radius, and compatible Spellblade talents may originate from **any point** within the Dominion.

#### Perfect Harmony

While Avatar of the Blade is active:

- All **Spellblade Talents** cost **50% less Mana** (minimum cost of 1 MP).
- **Once per turn**, after using a Spellblade Talent, you may activate a **different Spellblade Talent** without paying its Mana Cost. Normal Action Point costs still apply.
- Whenever you reduce a hostile creature to **0 HP**, immediately recover **20 MP** and **10 SP**.

#### Avatar's End

When Avatar of the Blade ends:

- Gain **Arcane Fatigue** until the end of your next turn.
- Arcane Fatigue reduces your Movement Speed by **5 metres** and prevents the activation of **Avatar of the Blade** again until the current cooldown has been refreshed.

### Lore

Within the Spellblade Orders, Rank V is known simply as **The Eternal Avatar**. It is the final lesson taught only through legend, for no master can truly instruct another how to achieve it. The transformation is said to occur only when a Spellblade has completely abandoned the distinction between blade, body and mana, allowing all three to exist as a single perfect expression of their will.

Stories speak of Eternal Avatars standing alone against dragons, breaking the spells of archmages with a single swing, and defending entire kingdoms through unmatched mastery rather than overwhelming force. Every technique they had ever learned flowed together seamlessly, each becoming stronger because of the others. Their enemies often believed they fought many warriors at once, unable to comprehend that every impossible feat came from a single Spellblade.

The Eternal Avatar is regarded as the highest achievement of the Order—not because it grants unmatched power, but because it represents perfect balance. Steel without magic is incomplete. Magic without steel is uncontrolled. Together, they become something worthy of legend.

> *"When there is no longer a difference between your blade, your magic, and your soul... you have become the Avatar."*

> — Grand Spellblade Aetherion Lux, First Blade of the Infinite Well
