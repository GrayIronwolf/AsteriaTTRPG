# Universal Measure System

The **Measure** is Asteria's standardised unit used when recipes, crafting processes, alchemy, enchanting, and other systems require quantities of materials.

A Measure represents a different physical quantity depending on whether the material is a **solid** or **liquid**.

---

## 📏 Measure Conversion

| Material State | 1 Measure |
|---|---:|
| **Solid** | 100 g |
| **Liquid** | 100 mL |

> [!IMPORTANT] Core Rule
> **1 Measure of Solid = 100 g**
>
> **1 Measure of Liquid = 100 mL**

---

## 🪨 Solid Materials

Solid crafting materials are measured by weight.

| Measures | Weight |
|---:|---:|
| 1 Measure | 100 g |
| 2 Measures | 200 g |
| 3 Measures | 300 g |
| 4 Measures | 400 g |
| 5 Measures | 500 g |
| 6 Measures | 600 g |
| 10 Measures | 1 kg |

Examples of solid materials include:

- Powdered Crystal
- Refined Powdered Crystal
- Herbs
- Powders
- Salts
- Ground minerals
- Other solid crafting ingredients

---

## 🧪 Liquid Materials

Liquid crafting materials are measured by volume.

| Measures | Volume |
|---:|---:|
| 1 Measure | 100 mL |
| 2 Measures | 200 mL |
| 3 Measures | 300 mL |
| 4 Measures | 400 mL |
| 5 Measures | 500 mL |
| 6 Measures | 600 mL |
| 10 Measures | 1 L |

Examples of liquid materials include:

- Water
- Oils
- Extracts
- Alchemical solutions
- Magical liquids
- Other liquid crafting ingredients

---

## ⚙️ System Use

When a crafting recipe specifies a material using **Measures**, convert the quantity according to the material's physical state.

### Example — Solid

**2 Measures Powdered Crystal**

`2 × 100 g = 200 g`

### Example — Liquid

**3 Measures Alchemical Oil**

`3 × 100 mL = 300 mL`
