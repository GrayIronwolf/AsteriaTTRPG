# Adamantium Ingot

## Effect
Adamantium is one of the hardest known metals in existence. It is capable of enduring overwhelming force without deforming, breaking, or degrading, making it the ultimate material for defensive structures and high-impact weaponry.

---

## Appearance
Adamantium Ingots are dark metallic grey with a dense, stone-like finish. Unlike polished metals, Adamantium absorbs light rather than reflecting it brightly, giving it a heavy and immovable presence.

When struck, it emits a deep resonant tone instead of a metallic clang.

---

## Lore
Forged from ore created under impossible pressure, Adamantium became known as the “final forge metal” among ancient master smiths. Entire civilizations built fortresses, war machines, and relic armor from it, believing no mortal force could destroy it.

Many ancient Adamantium structures still remain long after the civilizations that created them have vanished.

---

## Material Traits
- Near-indestructible under physical stress  
- Resistant to corrosion, decay, and environmental damage  
- Extremely difficult to forge and shape  
- Maintains structural integrity under immense impact  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Strong affinity with **Earth**  
- Enhances:
  - Reinforcement enchantments  
  - Stability and defensive effects  
  - Force resistance  
- Less effective for precision or high-energy enchantments  

---

## Combat Integration
- +5 Damage Modifier  
- +5 Armor Modifier  
- Favours tank, siege, and defensive builds  

---

## Special Interaction
- Extremely resistant to physical degradation  
- Performs best under sustained impact and prolonged combat  
- Difficult for weaker materials to damage directly  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires 4x [[Adamantium Ore]]  
- Requires divine-grade, deep-earth, or legendary forge conditions  

---

*"The gods forged mountains; we forged what could endure them."*
