## 📝 Description
Alpha Rat Beast Meat is a heavy portion of dense, dark red meat harvested from an Alpha Rat Beast. Tough and strongly flavoured, the flesh retains potent organic properties that make properly preserved cuts useful to alchemists as well as cooks.

## ✨ Material Properties
- Dense, tough, and strongly flavoured.
- Considerably more substantial than [[Dire Rat Meat]].
- Highly perishable when left untreated.
- Requires thorough cleaning and cooking before consumption.
- Can be dried, smoked, salted, or otherwise preserved.
- Retains potent organic properties useful in alchemy.

## ⚒️ Crafting Use
- Alchemy component.
- Cooked meat dishes.
- Stews, soups, and broths.
- Dried or smoked travel rations.
- Ingredient for Cooking recipes.
- Animal feed where appropriate.

## 🧪 Processing
- **Processing Required:** Clean and cook, dry, smoke, salt, or otherwise preserve.
- **Tools Required:** Knife and appropriate cooking or preservation equipment.
- **Required Crafting Rank:** Apprentice
- **Processed Into:** Cooked Alpha Rat Beast Meat, Preserved Alpha Rat Beast Meat, or appropriate food recipes.
- **Processing Yield:** 1x Alpha Rat Beast Meat → 1x prepared portion.

## 🔮 Enchantment Use
- **Enchantment Affinity:** None
- **Elemental Alignment:** None
- **Compatible Enchantments:** None
- **Special Properties:** Can be used as an Alchemy Component.

## 🐾 Resource Source
