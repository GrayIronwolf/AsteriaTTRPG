## 📝 Description
Alpha Rat Beast Tail is a long, exceptionally thick tail harvested from an Alpha Rat Beast. Its heavily segmented skin is covered in sparse coarse hairs and is considerably tougher than the tails of lesser rats, retaining potent organic properties valued by alchemists and spellcasters.

## ✨ Material Properties
- Exceptionally long, thick, and flexible.
- Covered with sparse, coarse hairs.
- Tougher and more durable than [[Dire Rat Tail]].
- Can be dried and preserved for long-term storage.
- Retains potent organic properties after harvesting.
- Provides no meaningful armour or weapon modifier.

## ⚒️ Crafting Use
- Alchemy component.
- Spell component.
- Advanced charms and trinkets.
- Trophy decorations.
- Decorative additions to equipment.
