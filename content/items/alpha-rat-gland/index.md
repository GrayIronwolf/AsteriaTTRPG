## 📝 Description
Alpha Rat Beast Gland is a dense organic gland harvested from an Alpha Rat Beast. Dark reddish-purple in colour and surrounded by tough connective tissue, it contains concentrated biological compounds that make it a valuable component for alchemists and spellcasters.

## ✨ Material Properties
- Dense, soft organic tissue.
- Dark reddish-purple in colour.
- Contains concentrated biological compounds.
- Highly perishable without proper preservation.
- Retains useful properties when carefully extracted and preserved.
- More valuable for specialised crafting than ordinary animal tissue.

## ⚒️ Crafting Use
- Alchemy component.
- Spell component.
- Ingredient for specialised alchemical recipes.
- Ingredient for specialised magical preparations.

## 🧪 Processing
- **Processing Required:** Carefully clean and preserve immediately after harvesting.
- **Tools Required:** Harvesting Knife, Glass Container, and preservation equipment.
- **Required Crafting Rank:** Apprentice
- **Processed Into:** Preserved Alpha Rat Beast Gland
- **Processing Yield:** 1x Alpha Rat Beast Gland → 1x Preserved Alpha Rat Beast Gland
