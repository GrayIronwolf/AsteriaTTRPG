# Aluminum Ingot

## Effect
Aluminum is an extremely lightweight metal that enhances speed, mobility, and responsiveness. It sacrifices durability for agility, making it ideal for fast-paced combat styles and aerial or evasive gear.

---

## Crafting Use
- Light armor
- Mobility gear
- Artificer components
- Flight / movement devices

---

## Material Traits
- Extremely lightweight  
- Highly resistant to corrosion  
- Poor durability under heavy stress  
- Excellent electrical conductivity  

---

## Alloy Use
- Lightweight alloys (future)
- Speed-enhanced equipment

---

## Enchantment Use
- Enhances Air and Lightning effects  
- Boosts movement-based abilities  
- Reduces activation delay in enchantments  

---

## Combat Integration
- +1 Damage Modifier (weapons)
- +1 Armor Modifier (armor)
- Increases mobility-focused builds (system-side)

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires: 4x [[Aluminum Ore]]
