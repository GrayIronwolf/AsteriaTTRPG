# Aluminum Ore

## Effect
Aluminum Ore is unusually light and responsive to energy, making it valuable for crafting materials focused on speed, movement, and agility.

---

## Lore
Often called *“Sky Ore,”* aluminum is believed to form in regions touched by high winds or lightning storms. Ancient artificers sought it to craft tools that defied weight and gravity.

---

## Material & Crafting Traits
- Extremely lightweight compared to other ores  
- Difficult to extract without loss due to fragility  
- Highly resistant to corrosion  
- Found in high-altitude or storm-rich regions  

---

## Refinement
- Smelts into: [[Aluminum Ingot]]
- Ratio: 4 Ore → 1 Ingot
- Notes: Requires controlled heat to prevent material degradation

---

## Alloy Use
- Lightweight alloys (future)

---

## Enchantment Use
- Strong affinity with Air and Lightning magic  
- Enhances speed and movement-based effects  

---

## Quote
*"Light as wind, fleeting as a storm."*
