# Arricallium Ingot

## Effect
Arricallium Ingots produce a refined metal that excels at conducting and amplifying magical energy, making it ideal for spellcasting equipment and magical constructs.

---

## Appearance
Arricallium Ingots are smooth, metallic bars with faint glowing veins running beneath the surface. These veins shift slowly, as if energy is continuously flowing through the metal.

The surface emits a subtle hum when held near magical sources.

---

## Lore
Once refined, Arricallium becomes one of the most valuable materials for magical construction. It is widely used in advanced spellcasting tools, arcane conduits, and high-tier enchanted equipment.

Despite its stability, improper handling during forging can result in violent energy discharge.

---

## Material Traits
- Excellent magical conductivity  
- Maintains stability under sustained magical use  
- Lightweight compared to other Epic metals  
- Sensitive to overload if improperly used  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Exceptional affinity with **Air** and **Fire**  
- Enhances:
  - Spellcasting  
  - Channeling  
  - Energy-based enchantments  
- Poor synergy with purely physical builds  

---

## Combat Integration
- +4 Damage Modifier  
- +3 Armor Modifier  
- Favours caster, hybrid, and magic-focused builds  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires 4x [[Arricallium Ore]]  
- Requires energy-stabilised forge  

---
