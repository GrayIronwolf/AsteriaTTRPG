# Bismuth Ore

## Effect
Bismuth Ore forms in unstable crystalline structures that react unpredictably to magical and environmental input. It carries volatile energy that can either amplify or distort magical effects.

---
## Appearance
Bismuth Ore forms in jagged, geometric clusters with sharp, stepped edges that resemble fractured crystal structures. Its surface displays shifting iridescent colours — purples, blues, greens, and golds — that change depending on the angle of light.

The edges of the ore appear unstable, subtly warping or realigning when viewed from different perspectives, as if the structure is never fully fixed.

---
## Lore
Bismuth is born in places where magic fractures reality — wild magic zones, broken ley lines, and unstable rifts. Its crystalline patterns constantly shift, reflecting a world that refuses to remain still.
