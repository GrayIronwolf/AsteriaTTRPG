# Chromium Ingot

## Effect
Chromium Ingots are used to reinforce metals, increasing durability and resistance to environmental and structural damage. While not suited for raw offensive power, they significantly enhance defensive properties.

---

## Crafting Use
- Armor reinforcement  
- Structural components  
- Alloy enhancement (High Steel)  
- Defensive equipment  

---

## Material Traits
- Excellent corrosion resistance  
- Strengthens alloy integrity  
- Maintains form under stress  
- Slightly heavier than standard metals  

---

## Alloy Use
- [[High Steel Ingot]]

## Enchantment Use
- Improves enchantment durability  
- Reduces wear from repeated magical use  
- Supports defensive enchantments

- Strong defensive-focused material
