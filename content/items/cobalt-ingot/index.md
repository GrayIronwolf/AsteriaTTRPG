# Cobalt Ingot

## Effect
Cobalt is a mana-reactive metal that amplifies energy flow through weapons and armor. It enhances elemental interactions while maintaining structural stability.

---

## Crafting Use
- Enchanted weapons
- Battlemage armor
- Arcane conduits
- Magical cores

---

## Material Traits
- Highly responsive to magical energy  
- Stable under repeated enchantment use  
- Naturally cool and conductive  
- Resistant to arcane corrosion  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Enhances Lightning and Ice effects  
- Improves enchantment efficiency  
- Supports multi-enchantment builds  

---

## Combat Integration
- +2 Damage Modifier  
- +2 Armor Modifier  
- Favours elemental and hybrid builds  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires: 4x [[Cobalt Ore]]
