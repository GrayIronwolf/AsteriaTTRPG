# Copper Ingot

## Effect
A refined metal with excellent conductivity, Copper Ingots are widely used in crafting tools, enchantment bases, and early-stage equipment.

---

## Crafting Use
- Used in:
  - Basic weapons
  - Magical conduits
  - Artificer components

---

## Material Traits
- Soft and easily shaped  
- Low durability under stress  
- High energy conductivity  

---

## Alloy Use
- [[Bronze Ingot]] (with Tin)
- [[Brass Ingot]] (with Zinc)

---

## Enchantment Use
- Boosts Fire and Lightning effects  
- Improves low-tier enchantment stability  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires: 4x [[Copper Ore]]

---

## Quote
*"Refined from flame, shaped by will."*
