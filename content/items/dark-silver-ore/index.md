# Dark Silver Ore

## Effect
Dark Silver Ore is infused with the primordial essence of **Dark itself** — not death, but absence, depth, and shadow. It absorbs light and distorts magical energy, forming a metal that thrives in low-light environments.

---
## Appearance
Dark Silver Ore appears as smooth, shadow-toned metal veined through stone, with a muted, almost matte finish that absorbs surrounding light. Its surface reflects very little, giving it a depth that feels more like a void than a solid material.

In low light, faint ripples of darker shade seem to move across the ore, as though shadows are pooling and shifting within it rather than around it.

---
## Lore
Dark Silver was not mined — it was *formed*. In places where light was stripped away completely, the essence of Dark condensed into physical form. It is neither evil nor corrupt, but a reflection of balance — the quiet counterpart to Light.

---

## Material & Crafting Traits
- Absorbs ambient light and magical energy  
- Strengthens in darkness and shadow-rich environments  
- Naturally dampens radiant and light-based effects  
- Stable during refinement despite its unusual origin  

---

- Notes: Best refined in low-light or magically controlled environments
