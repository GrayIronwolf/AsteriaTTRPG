## 📝 Description
Dire Rat Meat is a dense portion of dark red meat harvested from a Dire Rat. Tough and strongly flavoured, it requires careful preparation before eating. The unusual properties retained within the flesh also make it useful as a component in certain alchemical preparations.

## ✨ Material Properties
- Dense, tough raw meat.
- More substantial than [[Great Rat Meat]].
- Highly perishable when left untreated.
- Requires thorough cleaning and cooking before consumption.
- Can be dried, smoked, salted, or otherwise preserved.
- Retains useful organic properties for alchemical applications.

## ⚒️ Crafting Use
- Alchemy component.
- Cooked meat dishes.
- Stews, soups, and broths.
- Dried or smoked travel rations.
- Animal feed where appropriate.
- Ingredient for Cooking recipes.

## 🧪 Processing
- **Processing Required:** Clean and cook, dry, smoke, salt, or otherwise preserve.
- **Tools Required:** Knife and appropriate cooking or preservation equipment.
- **Required Profession & Rank:** Cooking — Novice
- **Processed Into:** Cooked Dire Rat Meat, Dried Dire Rat Meat, or appropriate food recipes.
- **Processing Yield:** 1x Dire Rat Meat → 1x prepared portion.

## 🔮 Enchantment Use
- **Enchantment Affinity:** None
- **Elemental Alignment:** None
- **Compatible Enchantments:** None
- **Special Properties:** Can be used as an Alchemy Component.
