# Dragon Glass Ingot

## Effect
Dragon Glass is a draconic crystalline alloy created through an advanced variation of the Glass forging process. By combining Pyraxx Sand and Aluminum under intense dragonfire, smiths produce a material infused with primal heat and destructive draconic energy.

The result is a highly aggressive alloy capable of channeling immense thermal force while maintaining razor-sharp precision.

---

## Appearance
Dragon Glass appears as jet-black crystalline metal threaded with glowing crimson and ember-orange veins beneath its surface. These veins pulse faintly like molten magma trapped within dark obsidian.

When exposed to heat, combat, or draconic presence, the internal glow intensifies and spreads throughout the material.

The surface reflects light unevenly, giving the metal a living and volatile appearance.

---

## Lore
Dragon Glass was developed after ancient forge masters discovered that certain volcanic sands reacted violently when exposed to dragonfire. Through repeated experimentation, they refined the Glass forging process into something far more dangerous.

Using Pyraxx Sand — a volcanic crystalline sand formed near dragon nesting grounds and magma fissures — smiths learned to create a material capable of carrying lingering draconic resonance.

Because true dragonfire is required during forging, Dragon Glass remains one of the rarest and most dangerous alloys in existence.
