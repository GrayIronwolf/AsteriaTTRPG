# Duranium Ingot

## Effect
Duranium Ingots produce a highly resilient metal that absorbs, stabilises, and redistributes force. It adapts to repeated stress, making it one of the most reliable defensive materials available.

---

## Appearance
Duranium Ingots are dense, dark grey bars with subtle internal movement beneath the surface. When struck, faint waves ripple across the metal before settling.

---

## Lore
Once refined, Duranium becomes a cornerstone material for defensive equipment and high-end constructs. Its ability to adapt to stress makes it invaluable in prolonged combat and high-impact environments.

---

## Material Traits
- Absorbs and redistributes force  
- Adapts to repeated impact  
- Extremely durable under sustained stress  
- Maintains structural integrity under extreme conditions  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Strong affinity with **Earth** and **Air**  
- Enhances:
  - Defensive enchantments  
  - Stability and reinforcement  
  - Impact resistance  

---

## Combat Integration
- +4 Damage Modifier  
- +5 Armor Modifier  
- Favours defensive, adaptive, and tank builds  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires 4x [[Duranium Ore]]  
- Requires high-energy forge  

---

## Quote
*"It learns every strike — and answers with silence."*
