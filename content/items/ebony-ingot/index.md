# Ebony Ingot

## Effect
Ebony Ingots produce a dense shadow-infused metal that absorbs light, sound, and ambient energy. It enhances overwhelming presence and oppressive force, making it ideal for heavy, unstoppable combat styles.

---

## Appearance
Ebony Ingots are deep black with a matte surface that consumes surrounding light. The metal appears unnaturally dense, with faint violet undertones shifting beneath its surface when exposed to energy.

Even when still, it gives the impression of weight and pressure, as if the space around it is being pulled inward.

---

## Lore
Ebony forms in regions where death energy and shadow accumulate over immense periods of time. Once refined, it becomes one of the densest materials known, carrying the weight of absorbed energy within its structure.

It is often used in weapons and armor designed to dominate through presence rather than speed.

---

## Material Traits
- Extremely dense and heavy  
- Absorbs light and ambient energy  
- Dampens sound and magical signatures  
- Maintains integrity under extreme pressure  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Strong affinity with **Dark** and **Death**  
- Enhances:
  - Suppression effects  
  - Life-drain and decay  
  - Presence-based abilities  
- Weak against Light-aligned enchantments  

---

## Combat Integration
- +5 Damage Modifier  
- +4 Armor Modifier  
- Favours heavy, oppressive, and shadow-dominant builds  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires 4x [[Ebony Ore]]  
- Requires shadow-aligned forge
