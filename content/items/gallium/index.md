# Gallium

## Effect
Gallium is a fluid metal that responds to heat and magical input. It cannot form stable equipment but excels in reactive enchantment systems and arcane circuitry.

---

## Crafting Use
- Arcane circuits
- Enchantment matrices
- Illusion-based devices
- Alchemical systems

---

## Material Traits
- Melts at low temperature  
- Cannot form stable weapons or armor  
- Highly responsive to magical input  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Supports dynamic / shifting enchantments  
- Useful in illusion and transformation systems  

---

## Combat Integration
- Not usable for weapons or armor
