# Glass Ingot

## Effect
Glass is a specialised crystalline alloy formed by fusing Astryx Sand with Aluminum through a controlled magical forging process. The result is a hardened material that combines clarity, sharpness, and precision.

It excels in focused output and cutting power but is vulnerable to heavy impacts.

---
## Appearance
Glass Ingots are smooth, semi-transparent bars with a polished surface that refracts light into shifting prismatic patterns. Internal layers appear suspended within the material, giving it depth and clarity.

Edges form with unnatural sharpness, and the surface reflects light in clean, precise lines.

---
## Lore
Glass is created from **Astryx Sand**, a rare silica found in magically active regions, combined with Aluminum to stabilise its structure during forging. Through careful refinement, this fragile material is transformed into a hardened alloy capable of rivaling rare metals in performance.

Early attempts to create Glass often resulted in catastrophic fractures, and even now, only skilled artisans can produce it reliably.
