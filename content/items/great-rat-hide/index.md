## 📝 Description

Great Rat Hide is a large, thick pelt covered in coarse dark brown fur. Considerably tougher and heavier than ordinary rat hide, its dense skin provides natural protection while remaining flexible enough to be processed into durable leather.

---

## ✨ Material Properties

- Thick and considerably tougher than ordinary [[Rat Hide]].
- Dense hide provides **+1 Armour Modifier** when used as the protective material of crafted armour.
- Flexible enough for leatherworking despite its increased thickness.

---

## ⚒️ Crafting Use

- Light and medium leather armour.
- Reinforced clothing and protective equipment.
- Heavy pouches, straps, bindings, and equipment components.
- Can be processed into [[Great Rat Leather]].

---

## 🧪 Processing

- **Processing Required:** Clean, scrape, cure, and tan the hide.
- **Tools Required:** Skinning Knife, Scraping Knife, Tanning Rack.
- **Required Profession & Rank:** Leatherworking — Novice.
- **Processed Into:** [[Great Rat Leather]]
- **Processing Yield:** 1x Great Rat Hide → 1x Great Rat Leather.

---

## 🔮 Enchantment Use

- **Enchantment Affinity:** Low
- **Elemental Alignment:** None
- **Compatible Enchantments:** Basic enchantments suitable for hides and leather.
- **Special Properties:** Its increased thickness provides a more stable base for enchantment than ordinary Rat Hide.
