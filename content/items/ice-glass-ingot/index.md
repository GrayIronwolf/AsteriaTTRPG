# Ice Glass Ingot

## Effect
Ice Glass is a refined crystalline alloy formed by fusing powdered ice crystals with Aluminum through a controlled cold-forging process. It produces a material that excels in precision and freezing control, allowing it to deliver sharp, controlled strikes that hinder movement.

---

## Appearance
Ice Glass Ingots are translucent with a pale blue tint, resembling perfectly frozen crystal. Light refracts softly through the material, creating layered frost-like patterns within.

The surface appears smooth and cold, with faint mist forming around it in warmer environments.

---

## Lore
Ice Glass is an evolution of the Glass forging process, replacing Astryx Sand with powdered ice crystals sourced from extreme cold regions or magically frozen environments.

Unlike standard Glass, Ice Glass must be forged under controlled cold conditions. Heat destabilises the material, making its creation both rare and difficult.

---

## Material Traits
- Extremely sharp and precise  
- Lightweight and responsive  
- Structurally stable in cold environments  
- Brittle under heat and heavy impact  

---

## Alloy Composition
- Powdered Ice Crystal  
- Aluminum Ingot  
- Controlled cold-forging process  

---

## Enchantment Use
- High affinity with **Water** and **Air**  
- Enhances:
  - Freezing effects  
  - Movement control  
  - Precision-based abilities  
- Weak under Fire-aligned enchantments  

---

## Combat Integration
- +5 Damage Modifier  
- +2 Armor Modifier  
- Favours precision, control, and frost-based combat builds  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires Powdered Ice Crystal  
- Requires Aluminum Ingot  
- Requires cold-forging environment or frost-aligned forge
