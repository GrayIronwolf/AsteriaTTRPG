# Infernal Steel Ingot

## Effect
Infernal Steel is a refined alloy created by infusing High Steel with concentrated infernal essence. It is designed for destruction, amplifying offensive power while maintaining the strength needed for prolonged combat.

---

## Appearance
Infernal Steel appears as a dark, red-black metal with faint ember-like veins glowing beneath its surface. These veins pulse slowly, giving the impression of contained heat and controlled fury.

When exposed to combat or stress, the glow intensifies slightly, as if the metal is responding to conflict.

---

## Lore
Infernal Steel is the evolution of Infernal Bronze, forged within infernal forges where heat and destructive energy are refined into controlled power.

Unlike raw infernal materials, Infernal Steel is shaped and stabilised, allowing it to be wielded effectively without immediately overwhelming its user.

It is often used for weapons and armor designed for conquest, domination, and battlefield superiority.

---

## Material Traits
- Highly resistant to heat and destructive forces  
- Channels aggressive energy efficiently  
- Maintains structure under prolonged combat stress  
- Slightly volatile under extreme magical strain  

---

## Alloy Composition
- High Steel  
- Infernal Essence  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Strong affinity with **Fire** and **Dark**  
- Enhances:
  - Offensive enchantments  
  - Burn and damage-over-time effects  
  - Aggressive combat abilities  
- Less effective for defensive or purification effects  

---

## Combat Integration
- +5 Damage Modifier  
- +3 Armor Modifier  
- Favours aggressive, high-damage combat builds  

---

## Special Interaction
- Increased effectiveness against **Celestial-aligned beings**  
- Reduced effectiveness against Infernal-aligned entities  
- Performs best during sustained combat and high-pressure situations  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires High Steel  
- Requires Infernal Essence  
- Must be forged within the **Infernal Realm**

---
