# Iron Ore

## Effect
Iron Ore is dense and impurity-rich, requiring proper smelting to unlock its strength. Once refined, it forms the backbone of most weapons and armor.

---

## Lore
Called the *“Blood of the World,”* iron runs deep beneath the earth. Civilizations rose and fell by their ability to mine and shape it.

---

## Material & Crafting Traits
- Dense and widely available  
- Requires stronger heat to refine  
- Reliable base material for combat equipment  

---

- Smelts into: [[Iron Ingot]]
- Ratio: 4 Ore → 1 Ingot
- Notes: Requires stable heat for proper refinement
