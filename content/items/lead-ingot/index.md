# Lead Ingot

## Effect
Lead Ingots are used to suppress and contain magical energy rather than enhance it.

---

## Crafting Use
- Anti-magic items
- Sealed containers
- Cursed relics

---

## Material Traits
- Heavy and dense  
- Soft but stable  
- Magic dampening  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Reduces magical output  
- Blocks magical effects
