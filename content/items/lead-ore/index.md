# Lead Ore

## Effect
Lead Ore absorbs magical energy, making it valuable for anti-magic crafting and containment.

---

## Lore
Called the *“Veil Stone,”* lead is said to silence magic itself.

---

## Material & Crafting Traits
- Dense and heavy  
- Toxic if mishandled  
- Used in containment crafting  

---

- Ratio: 4 Ore → 1 Ingot

---

## Alloy Use
- Anti-magic alloys (future)

---

## Enchantment Use
- Suppresses magic  
- Blocks magical detection
