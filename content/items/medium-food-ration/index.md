## Description
A compact travelling meal consisting of dense hard bread, strips of salted and dried jerky, and a portion of dried fruit. Sized for a Medium creature or humanoid, the ration provides a simple, dependable meal while remaining easy to pack and carry during extended travel.
A standard Medium Food Ration contains:
- **Hard Bread** — Dense baked bread designed to remain edible during extended travel.
- **Jerky** — Salted and dried meat providing the main protein portion of the meal.
- **Dried Fruit** — Preserved fruit providing sweetness and variety while remaining lightweight and easy to store.

## Lore
A staple provision carried by travellers, soldiers, merchants, hunters, explorers, and adventurers throughout Asteria.

### Sustenance
One complete **Medium Food Ration provides enough food for one Medium-sized creature or humanoid for one meal**.

### Storage & Spoilage
Food Rations are specifically prepared for travel and remain edible considerably longer than fresh food when properly stored.

Exposure to the following can reduce their usable lifespan:
- Water or excessive moisture
- Extreme heat
- Insects and vermin
- Contaminated storage
- Damaged packaging
- Magical or environmental corruption

A ration that becomes sufficiently spoiled or contaminated is no longer safe to consume.
