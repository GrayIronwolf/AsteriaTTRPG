# Mercury

## Effect
Mercury is a liquid metal that cannot be forged into traditional equipment. Instead, it is used in alchemy, transmutation, and ritual crafting.

---

## Crafting Use
- Alchemy
- Rituals
- Magical infusion
- Transformation systems

---

## Material Traits
- Liquid state  
- Highly toxic  
- Unstable magical resonance  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Enhances transformation and mutation effects  
- Used in cursebinding and ritual magic  
- Cannot hold standard enchantments  

---

## Combat Integration
- Not usable for weapons or armor directly  

---

## Quote
*"It flows — and nothing that touches it remains unchanged."*
