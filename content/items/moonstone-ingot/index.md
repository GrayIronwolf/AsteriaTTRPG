# Moonstone Ingot

## Effect
Moonstone Ingots produce a refined metal that harmonises magical energy, making it ideal for balanced builds that combine offense, defense, and spellcasting.

---
## Appearance
Moonstone Ingots are refined into smooth, softly luminous bars with a pearlescent sheen. The surface reflects light in shifting tones of white, silver, and pale blue, creating a calm, flowing glow rather than a sharp reflection.

When held, the light within the metal seems to pulse gently, as though echoing a distant rhythm, giving the ingot a sense of balance between stillness and motion.

---

- Hybrid weapons (physical + magic)  
- Balanced armor

- Spellcasting focus items
