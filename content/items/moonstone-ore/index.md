# Moonstone Ore

## Effect
Moonstone Ore carries lunar resonance, shifting subtly in response to light cycles and magical presence. It stabilises and harmonises magical energy rather than amplifying or suppressing it.

---
## Appearance
Moonstone Ore appears as pale, semi-translucent stone veined with soft silver and faint blue hues. It emits a gentle, diffused glow that subtly brightens and dims, as if responding to an unseen cycle.

Within the ore, faint patterns resembling drifting light or slow-moving ripples can be seen, giving the impression that the material is quietly alive with lunar energy.

---
## Lore
Moonstone forms where celestial light meets the physical world in cycles — waxing and waning with unseen forces. It is said to reflect not just the moon above, but the balance between Light and Dark within the world itself.

---

## Material & Crafting Traits
- Responds to ambient light and magical cycles  
- Stabilises fluctuating magical energy  
- Maintains structural integrity under magical stress  
- Often found in regions touched by celestial influence
