# Mythril Ingot

## Effect
Mythril is a refined celestial metal prized for its balance of lightness, durability, and magical conductivity. It allows weapons and armor to remain exceptionally agile without sacrificing structural integrity.

---

## Appearance
Mythril Ingots possess a luminous silver-blue finish with a polished surface that reflects light softly rather than sharply.

Under moonlight or magical energy, faint silver radiance moves beneath the metal like flowing mist.

---

## Lore
For centuries, Mythril has been regarded as one of the greatest metals ever discovered by mortal smiths. Entire kingdoms built their elite relics, armor, and enchanted weapons from it due to its unmatched versatility.

Many ancient orders considered Mythril sacred, believing the metal carried traces of celestial harmony within its structure.

---

## Material Traits
- Lightweight and highly durable  
- Excellent mana conductivity  
- Maintains enchantments with exceptional stability  
- Resistant to magical degradation and corruption  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Exceptional affinity with **Air** and **Light**  
- Enhances:
  - Speed and agility enchantments  
  - Mana efficiency  
  - Precision and refinement effects  
- Performs exceptionally well with enchanted equipment  

---

## Combat Integration
- +4 Damage Modifier  
- +5 Armor Modifier  
- Favours agile, refined, and magic-enhanced combat styles  

---

## Special Interaction
- Reduces burden and movement restriction when used in armor  
- Stabilises magical enchantments more effectively than most metals  
- Performs exceptionally well in prolonged magical use  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires 4x [[Mythril Ore]]  
- Requires refined forge conditions and precise temperature control
