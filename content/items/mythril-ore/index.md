# Mythril Ore

## Effect
Mythril Ore is a rare celestial mineral known for its exceptional lightness, strength, and magical conductivity. It naturally channels mana with minimal resistance while maintaining remarkable structural stability.

---

## Appearance
Mythril Ore appears as pale silver-blue stone threaded with reflective metallic veins. Under moonlight or magical illumination, the ore emits a faint soft glow.

Its surface feels unnaturally smooth and lightweight compared to most metallic ores.

---

## Lore
Mythril is believed to form where celestial energy and the deep earth meet in harmony. Ancient legends claim droplets of molten Mythril once fell from celestial forges into sacred lakes, cooling into pure veins beneath the world.

Because of its rarity and versatility, Mythril became one of the most prized materials among master smiths, artificers, and knightly orders.

---

## Material Traits
- Extremely lightweight for its strength  
- Excellent magical conductivity  
- Resistant to corruption and decay  
- Difficult to damage without concentrated force  

---

## Refinement
- Smelts into: [[Mythril Ingot]]  
- Ratio: 4 Ore → 1 Ingot  
- Requires refined high-temperature forge conditions  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Strong affinity with **Air** and **Light**  
- Enhances:
  - Mana flow  
  - Speed-based enchantments  
  - Precision and refinement effects  

---
