# Netherite Ingot

## Effect
Netherite is a powerful infernal metal infused with destructive heat and corruptive energy. It amplifies aggression, domination, and sustained offensive force while resisting weakening effects from holy or light-aligned sources.

---

## Appearance
Netherite Ingots are blackened crimson metal with glowing magma-like fractures beneath the surface. The metal absorbs surrounding light unevenly, creating the impression of burning darkness contained within solid form.

When exposed to combat or infernal energy, the internal glow intensifies.

---

## Lore
Netherite became infamous during the infernal wars, where abyssal forgemasters refined the ore into weapons capable of overwhelming both mortal armies and celestial forces.

Unlike ordinary infernal materials, Netherite does not merely burn — it corrupts and dominates, exerting pressure on both the body and will of its wielder.

Many legendary cursed weapons were forged from Netherite due to its affinity for domination and destructive power.

---

## Material Traits
- Retains infernal heat and aggression  
- Resistant to fire and corruption  
- Difficult to weaken through holy energy  
- Can gradually influence unstable wielders  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Exceptional affinity with **Fire** and **Dark**  
- Enhances:
  - Infernal enchantments  
  - Corruption effects  
  - Aggressive combat abilities  
- Weak interaction with Light and purification enchantments  

---

## Combat Integration
- +5 Damage Modifier  
- +4 Armor Modifier  
- Favours aggressive, domination, and infernal combat styles  

---

## Special Interaction
- Performs best during prolonged combat  
- Resonates strongly with infernal environments or entities  
- Exposure to infernal flame can temporarily strengthen the material  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires 4x [[Netherite Ore]]  
- Requires infernal forge or abyssal flame refinement  

---
