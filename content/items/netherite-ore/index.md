# Netherite Ore

## Effect
Netherite Ore is a corrupted infernal mineral formed where extreme heat, darkness, and demonic energy converge. The ore radiates oppressive heat and carries lingering traces of infernal corruption within its structure.

---

## Appearance
Netherite Ore appears as blackened metallic stone threaded with deep crimson fissures resembling cooled magma. These fissures pulse faintly under heat or exposure to infernal energy.

The surrounding air often feels unnaturally heavy and warm near large deposits.

---

## Lore
Netherite forms deep within infernal regions and cursed volcanic zones where dark energy saturates the land. Ancient records claim the first veins emerged after infernal entities breached the mortal world during the early ages.

Because of its corruptive nature, many civilizations forbid its use despite its immense strength and destructive potential.

---

## Material Traits
- Retains infernal heat for long periods  
- Resistant to fire and dark-aligned corruption  
- Difficult to refine safely  
- Can influence weaker minds through prolonged exposure  

---

## Refinement
- Smelts into: [[Netherite Ingot]]  
- Ratio: 4 Ore → 1 Ingot  
- Requires infernal forge or abyssal flame conditions  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Strong affinity with **Fire** and **Dark**  
- Enhances:
  - Aggressive enchantments  
  - Corruption effects  
  - Infernal abilities
