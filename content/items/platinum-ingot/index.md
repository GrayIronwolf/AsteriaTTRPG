# Platinum Ingot

## Effect
Platinum is a rare and highly stable metal that excels in magical conductivity. It enhances divine and celestial enchantments while maintaining structural integrity.

---

## Crafting Use
- Holy weapons
- Relics
- Enchanted armor
- Magical cores

---

## Material Traits
- Highly resistant to corruption  
- Extremely stable under magical stress  
- Difficult to forge without advanced methods

## Alloy Use
- High-tier enchanted alloys (future)

---

## Enchantment Use
- Strong affinity with Light and Celestial magic  
- Increases enchantment effectiveness  
- High stability under repeated casting  

---

## Combat Integration
- +1 Damage Modifier
- +2 Armor Modifier
- Favours enchanted builds over raw damage
