# Primordrite Ingot

## Effect
Primordrite is a relic-grade creation metal tied directly to the foundational energies of existence itself. It exists in perfect harmony with elemental, magical, and spiritual forces, allowing it to channel and stabilise immense power without degradation.

---

## Appearance
Primordrite Ingots possess a constantly shifting metallic surface that never fully settles into a single colour. Waves of radiant gold, deep crimson, silver-white, ocean blue, and void-black flow slowly beneath the metal like living energy.

Under strong magical influence, the ingot emits a low resonant hum resembling a distant heartbeat.

---

## Lore
Primordrite is considered one of the rarest and oldest materials in existence. Ancient records describe it as “the first metal” — a substance formed when creation itself solidified into physical form.

Only fragments of Primordrite are known to exist, often hidden within relic vaults, celestial ruins, or locations scarred by primordial events.

Many legendary relics, realm-binding constructs, and divine artifacts are believed to contain traces of Primordrite within their core structure.

---

## Material Traits
- Harmonises all elemental and magical forces  
- Resistant to corruption, decay, and magical destabilisation  
- Stable under immense reality-altering pressure  
- Exceptionally difficult to forge or shape  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Perfect affinity with all elemental alignments  
- Enhances:
  - Relic-grade enchantments  
  - Elemental harmony  
  - Mana stability  
  - Creation and binding effects  
- Performs exceptionally well in relics and world-tier artifacts  

---

## Combat Integration
- +6 Damage Modifier  
- +6 Armor Modifier  
- Favours relic weapons, divine artifacts, and primordial constructs  

---

## Special Interaction
- Stabilises conflicting magical forces  
- Resonates strongly near primordial energy or realm fractures  
- Can maintain enchantments that would destabilise ordinary materials  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires 4x [[Primordrite Ore]]  
- Requires primordial forge or relic-grade creation forge conditions
