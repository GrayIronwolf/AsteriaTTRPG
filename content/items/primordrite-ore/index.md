# Primordrite Ore

## Effect
Primordrite Ore is believed to be one of the oldest known materials in existence — a fragment of creation itself formed before the world fully stabilised.

The ore naturally harmonises magical and elemental forces, allowing it to exist in perfect balance with surrounding energies.

---

## Appearance
Primordrite Ore appears as dark metallic stone filled with constantly shifting internal colouration. Veins of ember-red, ocean-blue, radiant gold, deep violet, and silver-white move slowly beneath the surface like living creation energy.

The ore subtly changes depending on nearby elemental or magical influence.

---

## Lore
According to the oldest surviving myths, Primordrite formed during the earliest moments of creation when raw elemental and cosmic forces first stabilised into physical existence.

Unlike ordinary metals, Primordrite does not appear to originate from geological formation. Instead, fragments are occasionally discovered within fallen celestial bodies, shattered realm fractures, or locations touched by immense primordial energy.

Many ancient civilizations believed the material was not forged by the gods — but used by them to forge the world itself.

---

## Material Traits
- Exists in stable harmony with elemental and magical energy  
- Resistant to corruption and destabilisation  
- Maintains structural integrity under immense force and magical stress  
- Extremely difficult to refine or alter
