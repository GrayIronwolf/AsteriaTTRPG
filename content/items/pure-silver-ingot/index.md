# Pure Silver Ingot

## Effect
Pure Silver Ingots produce a refined metal that channels Light with exceptional clarity. It excels in purification, protection, and revealing hidden or corrupted forces.

---
## Appearance
Pure Silver Ingots are smooth and highly refined, with a bright, mirror-like surface that reflects light cleanly and evenly. Unlike other metals, the reflection appears crisp and undistorted, giving it a sense of clarity and purity.

A faint, steady glow lingers along the surface, most noticeable in darkness, as if the metal itself carries a quiet, internal light.

---
## Crafting Use
- Holy weapons  
- Radiant armor  
- Anti-corruption tools  
- Divine or celestial equipment  

---

## Material Traits
- Lightweight and highly refined  
- Maintains purity under normal conditions  
- Slightly softer than combat-focused metals  
- Reacts strongly to corruption and shadow  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Exceptional affinity with **Light** magic  
- Enhances:
  - Purification  
  - Illumination  
  - Anti-corruption effects  
- Weak synergy with Dark-aligned enchantments  

---

## Combat Integration
- +2 Damage Modifier  
- +3 Armor Modifier  
- Favours defensive, support, and anti-corruption builds  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires: 4x [[Pure Silver Ore]]
