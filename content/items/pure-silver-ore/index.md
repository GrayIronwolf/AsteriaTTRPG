# Pure Silver Ore

## Effect
Pure Silver Ore is infused with the essence of **Light itself** — not life, but clarity, purity, and illumination. It emits a faint glow and disrupts shadow and corruption.

---
## Appearance
Pure Silver Ore appears as bright, pale metal veins running through stone, noticeably cleaner and more luminous than common silver. Its surface reflects light with a soft, steady glow, giving it an almost pristine, untouched quality.

Even in low light, the ore maintains a faint brightness, as though holding onto light rather than reflecting it.

---
## Lore
Unlike common silver, Pure Silver is not simply refined — it is *formed*. Where Light condenses into the physical world, veins of Pure Silver emerge, untouched by impurity. It is said to be the metal that Light chose to inhabit.

---

## Material & Crafting Traits
- Emits a faint glow in darkness  
- Naturally repels corruption and shadow  
- Sensitive to impurity during refinement  
- Requires precise heat control to maintain purity  

---

## Refinement
- Smelts into: [[Pure Silver Ingot]]  
- Ratio: 4 Ore → 1 Ingot  
- Notes: Overheating reduces purity and effectiveness
