## 📝 Description
Rat Teeth are small, pale-yellow incisors harvested from common rats. Their curved shape and naturally hard surface make them unsuitable as structural crafting materials, but they are commonly collected for inexpensive decorations, trophies, jewellery, and ornamental detailing.

## ✨ Material Properties
- Small, lightweight, and naturally hard.
- Usually pale yellow or off-white in colour.
- Too small and brittle for practical armour or weapon construction.
- Can be cleaned, polished, drilled, carved, or engraved for decorative use.

## ⚒️ Crafting Use
- Necklaces, bracelets, and simple jewellery.
- Decorative charms and trinkets.
- Trophy ornaments.
- Clothing and equipment embellishments.
- Decorative additions to weapons, armour, tools, and accessories.

## 🧪 Processing
- **Processing Required:** Clean and dry before use; polishing or drilling is optional.
- **Tools Required:** Small Knife, File, Hand Drill, or Polishing Tools depending on the intended decoration.
- **Required Profession & Rank:** None for basic preparation; appropriate crafting profession for finished decorative items.
- **Processed Into:** Polished or Carved Rat Teeth.
- **Processing Yield:** 1x Rat Tooth → 1x Prepared Rat Tooth.
