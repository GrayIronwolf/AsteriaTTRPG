# Silver Ingot

## Effect
Silver Ingots are prized for their ability to channel divine and spiritual energy, making them ideal for enchanted weapons and relics.

- Ritual tools
- Magical components

- High purity  
- Reacts to corruption

## Alloy Use

- Increased effectiveness vs undead

- Requires: 4x [[Silver Ore]]
