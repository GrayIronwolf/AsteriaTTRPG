# Silver Ore

## Effect
Silver Ore carries strong spiritual resonance, making it ideal for crafting anti-entity and divine-aligned materials.

---

## Lore
Known as *“Moon-Touched Stone,”* silver is said to form where celestial light touches the earth.

---

## Material & Crafting Traits
- Rare compared to other common metals  
- High magical purity  
- Used in sacred crafting  

---

## Refinement
- Smelts into: [[Silver Ingot]]
- Ratio: 4 Ore → 1 Ingot

---

## Alloy Use
- Often combined with stronger metals for weapons

---

## Enchantment Use
- Strong affinity with Light and Spirit magic  
- Effective against undead and cursed beings  

---

## Quote
*"Where light lingers, silver forms."*
