# Starite Ingot

## Effect
Starite is a refined astral metal renowned for its exceptional mana conductivity and celestial resonance. It channels magical energy with remarkable stability, making it highly sought after for advanced spellcraft, relic forging, and planar devices.

---

## Appearance
Starite Ingots possess a polished silver-blue finish filled with shifting prismatic reflections resembling a moving night sky.

Under magical energy, faint constellations and flowing star-like patterns appear beneath the surface.

---

## Lore
Throughout history, Starite has been associated with celestial observatories, astral forges, and ancient mage civilizations that studied the movement of the heavens.

Because of its affinity with mana flow and astral resonance, many legendary staffs, relics, and planar mechanisms were forged using Starite as their core material.

Some scholars believe the metal subtly responds to the movement of stars and ley lines across the world.

---

## Material Traits
- Exceptional mana conductivity  
- Stable under planar and astral energy exposure  
- Resistant to magical degradation  
- Performs exceptionally well in magical constructs and relics  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Exceptional affinity with **Light** and **Air**  
- Enhances:
  - Mana-channeling  
  - Astral and teleportation effects  
  - Spell stability and refinement  
- Performs exceptionally well in arcane focuses and relics  

---

## Combat Integration
- +4 Damage Modifier  
- +5 Armor Modifier  
- Favours mages, astral casters, relic weapons, and advanced magical constructs  

---

## Special Interaction
- Resonates strongly near ley lines and celestial phenomena  
- Improves magical stability during prolonged spellcasting  
- Can subtly react to astral or planar disturbances  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires 4x [[Starite Ore]]  
- Requires astral forge or celestial refinement conditions
