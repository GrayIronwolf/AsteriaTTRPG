# Thorium Ore

## Effect
Thorium Ore radiates stored arcane energy, making it highly reactive during refinement. When properly processed, it produces a metal capable of storing and releasing magical power.

---

## Lore
Forged in the deep veins of the world where lightning once struck the earth’s core, thorium is known as the *“Metal of Living Light.”* It is both revered and feared for its raw power.

---

## Material & Crafting Traits
- Emits low levels of magical radiation  
- Extremely dense and heat-resistant  
- Difficult to mine and handle safely  
- Requires skilled refinement to stabilise  

---

## Refinement
- Smelts into: [[Thorium Ingot]]
- Ratio: 4 Ore → 1 Ingot
- Notes: Requires controlled environment to prevent instability

---

## Alloy Use
- High-tier magical alloys (future)

---

## Enchantment Use
- Strong affinity with Radiant and Arcane magic  
- Stores residual magical energy
