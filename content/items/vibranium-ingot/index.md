# Vibranium Ingot

## Effect
Vibranium is a resonant metal capable of absorbing, storing, and redirecting kinetic energy through harmonic vibration.

Weapons and armor forged from Vibranium excel at dispersing force, reducing impact strain, and converting movement into stored resonance energy.

---

## Appearance
Vibranium Ingots possess a smooth dark silver finish with subtle blue-white resonance veins beneath the surface.

When impacted, the metal emits a deep harmonic tone while faint pulses of light ripple across the ingot.

---

## Lore
Vibranium became legendary among advanced artificers and resonance smiths due to its unique ability to interact with force itself.

Unlike traditional defensive metals that rely purely on hardness, Vibranium survives through controlled energy redistribution, allowing it to withstand devastating impacts that would shatter ordinary materials.

Many ancient resonance forges considered Vibranium a “living metal,” claiming it slowly adapted to repeated combat stresses over time.

---

## Material Traits
- Absorbs and redistributes kinetic force  
- Highly resistant to blunt impacts and shattering  
- Resonates with movement and vibration  
- Performs exceptionally well in reactive systems and advanced constructs  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Exceptional affinity with **Air**  
- Enhances:
  - Resonance enchantments  
  - Shockwave and vibration effects  
  - Movement and reaction-based abilities  
- Stable under prolonged magical stress  

---

## Combat Integration
- +4 Damage Modifier  
- +5 Armor Modifier  
- Favours reactive, defensive, and artificer-based combat styles  

---

## Special Interaction
- Performs exceptionally well under repeated impacts  
- Can store resonance energy temporarily  
- Resonates with movement, sound, and kinetic force  

---
