# Voidsteel Ingot

## Effect
Voidsteel is a void-infused alloy forged through the fusion of High Steel and concentrated Void Essence. It suppresses energy, dulls surrounding presence, and destabilises magical structures through sheer entropic resonance.

---

## Appearance
Voidsteel appears as deep black metal fractured with faint violet lines that shift subtly beneath the surface. Rather than reflecting light, the metal seems to consume it, leaving edges blurred and difficult to focus on directly.

When exposed to magical energy, the fractures pulse faintly like cracks in empty space.

---

## Lore
Voidsteel was first forged within the Gloomforges after the fracturing of the Seal of Gloom. By binding Void Essence into High Steel, ancient smiths created a material capable of resisting reality itself.

Unlike other dark-aligned materials, Voidsteel does not feed on death or shadow — it erodes structure, stability, and existence through gradual entropic pressure.

Forging Voidsteel is exceptionally dangerous, as improperly stabilised alloys may collapse into unstable void fractures.

---

## Material Traits
- Suppresses surrounding magical energy  
- Resistant to magical destabilisation  
- Maintains integrity under void pressure  
- Difficult for weaker enchantments to stabilise against  

---

## Alloy Composition
- High Steel  
- Void Essence  
- Gloomforge refinement process  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Exceptional affinity with **Dark**  
- Enhances:
  - Entropy effects  
  - Suppression enchantments  
  - Void-aligned abilities  
- Weak interaction with Life and Light-aligned enchantments  

---

## Combat Integration
- +5 Damage Modifier  
- +4 Armor Modifier  
- Favours void, suppression, and anti-magic combat styles  

---

## Special Interaction
- Weakens unstable magical structures over time  
- Performs best against magic-heavy opponents  
- Dampens surrounding magical resonance in prolonged combat  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires High Steel  
- Requires Void Essence  
- Must be forged within a Gloomforge or void-aligned forge  

---
