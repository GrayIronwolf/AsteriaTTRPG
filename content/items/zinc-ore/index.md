# Zinc Ore

## Effect
Zinc Ore is a reactive and volatile material that plays a critical role in alloy creation. While weak on its own, it enhances flexibility, resonance, and thermal response when combined with other metals.

---

## Lore
Known as the *“Breath of the Forge,”* zinc was discovered not for its strength, but for how it transformed other metals. Ancient artificers prized it for its ability to “awaken” dormant properties within alloys.

---

## Material & Crafting Traits
- Low structural strength  
- Highly reactive when heated  
- Vaporises at high temperatures if mishandled  
- Essential for alloy crafting  

---

## Refinement
- Smelts into: [[Zinc Ingot]]
- Ratio: 4 Ore → 1 Ingot
- Notes: Requires controlled heat to prevent material loss
