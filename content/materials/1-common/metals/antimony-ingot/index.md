# Antimony Ingot

## Effect
Antimony Ingots are brittle but structurally influential, used to reinforce alloys and improve the rigidity of softer metals.

---

## Crafting Use
- Alloy reinforcement
- Structural balancing in metal crafting
- Artificer and alchemical components

---

## Material Traits
- Brittle and rigid  
- Not suitable for direct weapon or armor crafting  
- Improves hardness and form retention in alloys
