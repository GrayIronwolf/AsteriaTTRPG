# Brass Ingot

## Effect
Brass is a flexible, resonance-rich alloy that enhances vibration, sound, and heat-based interactions. It is widely used in mechanisms, constructs, and arcane devices, while still being viable for light equipment.

---

## Crafting Use
- Mechanical components
- Artificer constructs
- Instruments
- Light weapons
- Light armor

---

## Material Traits
- Highly flexible and easy to shape  
- Moderate resistance to corrosion  
- Excellent sound and heat conductivity  
- Cannot withstand sustained heavy impact  

---

## Alloy Composition
- 1x [[Copper Ingot]]
- 1x [[Zinc Ingot]]

---

## Enchantment Use
- Enhances:
  - Sound-based effects
  - Heat / Fire interactions
  - Vibration-based abilities  
- Improves responsiveness of triggered enchantments  
- Less stable under heavy strain  

---

## Combat Notes
- Suitable for:
  - Daggers
  - Light blades
  - Decorative armor  
- Not suitable for:
  - Heavy weapons
  - Heavy armor  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires:
  - 1x [[Copper Ingot]]
  - 1x [[Zinc Ingot]]
