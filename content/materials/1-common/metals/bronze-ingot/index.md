# Bronze Ingot

## Effect
Bronze is the first true crafted alloy, offering improved durability and strength over its base components. It is ideal for early weapons, armor, and durable crafted goods.

---

## Crafting Use
- Weapons
- Armor
- Tools
- Structural components

---

## Material Traits
- Stronger than Copper and Tin  
- Holds shape and edge better  
- Resistant to corrosion  

---

## Alloy Composition
- 1x [[Copper Ingot]]
- 1x [[Tin Ingot]]

---

## Enchantment Use
- Supports basic enchantments  
- More stable than Copper  
- Can hold longer-lasting effects  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires:
  - 1x [[Copper Ingot]]
  - 1x [[Tin Ingot]]
