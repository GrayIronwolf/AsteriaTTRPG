# Pewter Ingot

## Effect
Pewter is a balanced, multi-metal alloy designed for utility, ritual use, and low-tier equipment. While not suited for heavy combat, it offers stability, adaptability, and strong interaction with minor enchantments.

---

## Crafting Use
- Trinkets
- Ritual tools
- Containers
- Light gear (limited)
- Enchanted utility items

---

## Material Traits
- Soft but structurally balanced  
- Resistant to minor deformation due to antimony reinforcement  
- Slightly toxic due to lead content  
- Easy to shape and rework  

---

## Alloy Composition
- 2x [[Tin Ingot]] (Primary Base)
- 1x [[Copper Ingot]]
- 1x [[Lead Ingot]]
- 1x [[Antimony Ingot]]

---

## Enchantment Use
- Accepts low-tier enchantments easily  
- Moderate stability for sustained effects  
- Does not support high-tier enchantments  
- Useful for:
  - Wards
  - Minor charms
  - Ritual casting tools  

---

## Combat Notes
- Limited use in weapons (low durability)  
- Not suitable for armor beyond decorative/light use  
- Best used for non-combat equipment  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires:
  - 2x [[Tin Ingot]]
  - 1x [[Copper Ingot]]
  - 1x [[Lead Ingot]]
  - 1x [[Antimony Ingot]]
