# Tin Ingot

## Effect
A refined but weak metal, Tin Ingots are primarily used as a stabilising component in alloy crafting rather than for direct equipment creation.

---

## Crafting Use
- Core component for Bronze
- Used in beginner alloy recipes

---

## Material Traits
- Very soft and flexible  
- Cannot hold an edge  
- Supports alloy integrity
