# Cast Iron Ingot

## Effect
Cast Iron is a dense and rigid alloy that provides strong structural support but suffers from brittleness under stress. It excels in defensive applications but performs poorly in precision weaponry.

---

## Crafting Use
- Heavy armor
- Defensive structures
- Reinforced components

---

## Material Traits
- Excellent at absorbing blunt force  
- Brittle under sharp or repeated stress  
- Difficult to reshape once formed  
- Dense and heavy  

---

## Alloy Composition
- 1x [[Iron Ingot]]
- Carbon (high concentration)

---

## Enchantment Use
- Weak enchantment compatibility  
- Slight synergy with Earth-aligned effects

## Combat Integration
- +1 Damage Modifier (limited use)

- Best suited for defensive builds
