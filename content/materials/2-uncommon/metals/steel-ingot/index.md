# Steel Ingot

## Effect
Steel is a refined alloy that balances strength, flexibility, and durability. It represents the first major advancement beyond raw metals, providing a reliable foundation for combat equipment and advanced craftsmanship.

---

## Crafting Use
- Weapons (standard tier)
- Armor (medium to heavy)
- Tools and structural components
- Mechanical frameworks

---

## Material Traits
- Stronger and more flexible than Iron  
- Holds an edge significantly better than base metals  
- Resistant to warping and fracture under stress  
- Reliable across both combat and utility applications  

---

## Alloy Composition
- 1x [[Iron Ingot]]
- Carbon (Refinement Catalyst)

---

## Enchantment Use
- Supports low to mid-tier enchantments  
- Stable under repeated magical use  
- Does not amplify magic, but maintains consistency  

---

## Combat Integration
- +2 Damage Modifier (weapons)
- +2 Armor Modifier (armor)
- Baseline Uncommon combat material

  - 1x [[Iron Ingot]]
  - Carbon
