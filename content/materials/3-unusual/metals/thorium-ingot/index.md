# Thorium Ingot

## Effect
Thorium is a mana-charged metal that stores and releases energy. It enhances magical output while providing strong defensive properties.

---

## Crafting Use
- Arcane armor
- Energy weapons
- Magical cores
- Runic systems

---

## Material Traits
- Stores residual magical energy  
- Resistant to heat and arcane decay  
- Requires careful handling due to instability  
- Dense and highly durable  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Enhances Radiant and Arcane effects  
- Supports high-output enchantments  
- Can store spell energy  

---

## Combat Integration
- +3 Damage Modifier  
- +3 Armor Modifier  
- Favours magic-heavy builds  

---

- Requires: 4x [[Thorium Ore]]
