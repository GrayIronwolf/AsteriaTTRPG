# Bismuth Ingot

## Effect
Bismuth Ingots produce a highly unstable metal that dramatically increases power output while introducing unpredictability into both physical and magical performance.

---
## Appearance
Bismuth Ingots retain their distinctive crystalline geometry, forming angular, stepped patterns rather than smooth bars. Their surface shimmers with metallic rainbow hues that seem to move across the metal like a slow pulse.

When exposed to magical energy or stress, faint distortions ripple across the ingot’s surface, giving the impression that the metal is constantly on the verge of shifting or fracturing.
