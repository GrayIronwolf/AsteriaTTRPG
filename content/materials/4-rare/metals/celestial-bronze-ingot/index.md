# Celestial Bronze Ingot

## Effect
Celestial Bronze is a divine alloy infused with pure Light essence, forged within celestial realms where radiant energy shapes and refines the metal. It excels at channeling clarity, protection, and controlled power.

---

## Appearance
Celestial Bronze appears as a warm, golden-bronze metal with a soft, radiant glow. Its surface reflects light in a smooth, halo-like sheen, and faint currents of luminosity can be seen flowing beneath the metal as if it carries living light within it.

---

## Lore
Celestial Bronze is formed in environments where Light condenses into physical form, most commonly within celestial forges or sacred constructs beyond the mortal plane. While it can be worked by skilled smiths, it cannot be created outside of the Celestial Realm, where the necessary conditions do not exist.

Items made from Celestial Bronze are often associated with divine purpose, guardianship, and the preservation of balance.

---

## Material Traits
- Highly stable under magical and radiant influence  
- Resistant to corruption and shadow-based effects  
- Maintains integrity under sustained magical use  
- Balanced between strength, flexibility, and refinement  

---

## Alloy Composition
- Celestial Bronze is formed from celestial-aligned materials within the Celestial Realm  
- Exact composition varies depending on source and forge  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Exceptional affinity with **Light**  
- Enhances:
  - Radiant effects  
  - Purification  
  - Defensive enchantments  
- Weak interaction with Dark-aligned enchantments  

---

## Combat Integration
- +3 Damage Modifier  
- +3 Armor Modifier  
- Favours balanced, defensive, and Light-aligned builds  

---

## Special Interaction
- Increased effectiveness against **Infernal Realm creatures**  
- More effective against corruption-aligned entities  
- Reduced effectiveness when used against Celestial-aligned beings  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires Celestial materials  
- Must be forged within the **Celestial Realm**
