# Dark Silver Ingot

## Effect
Dark Silver Ingots produce a refined metal that enhances shadow-based abilities and thrives in environments where light is diminished. It amplifies precision, concealment, and controlled magical output.

---
## Appearance
Dark Silver Ingots are refined into sleek, dark metallic bars with a soft, light-absorbing sheen. Unlike polished metals, they do not shine — instead, they dull surrounding light, creating a subtle halo of shadow along their edges.

When viewed closely, the surface appears to have depth beneath it, as if layers of darkness are suspended within the metal, shifting slightly as the ingot is moved.

---
## Crafting Use
- Shadow-aligned weapons  
- Assassin or stealth armor  
- Arcane tools for Dark magic users  
- Precision-based equipment  

---

## Material Traits
- Lightweight with strong structural integrity  
- Absorbs and redistributes light across its surface  
- Becomes more effective in darkness  
- Slightly unstable under intense radiant exposure  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Exceptional affinity with **Dark** magic  
- Enhances:
  - Shadow manipulation  
  - Concealment effects  
  - Precision-based enchantments  
- Weak synergy with Light or Radiant enchantments  

---

## Combat Integration
- +3 Damage Modifier  
- +2 Armor Modifier  
- Favours precision, stealth, and shadow builds
