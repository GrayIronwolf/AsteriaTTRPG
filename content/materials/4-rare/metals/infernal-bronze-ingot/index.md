# Infernal Bronze Ingot

## Effect
Infernal Bronze is a corrupted alloy infused with Fire and Dark essences, forged within the Infernal Realm where heat, pressure, and destructive energy shape its form. It excels in raw power, aggression, and overwhelming force.

---

## Appearance
Infernal Bronze appears as a dark, red-bronze metal with veins of glowing ember-like light running through its surface. These veins pulse faintly, as if heat and energy are constantly moving within the metal.

When exposed to stress or combat, the glow intensifies slightly, giving the impression that the metal is feeding on the conflict around it.

---

## Lore
Infernal Bronze forms within infernal forges where extreme heat and dark energy merge, shaping metal through force rather than refinement. Unlike its celestial counterpart, it is not guided — it is driven.

Though it can be crafted, it requires access to the Infernal Realm, where the conditions necessary for its creation exist. Mortal forges cannot replicate its intensity or corruption.

---

## Material Traits
- Highly resistant to heat and destructive forces  
- Channels aggressive and unstable energy  
- Maintains integrity under extreme stress  
- Slightly volatile under prolonged magical strain  

---

## Alloy Composition
- Infernal-aligned materials forged within the Infernal Realm  
- Exact composition varies based on source and forge  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Strong affinity with **Fire** and **Dark**  
- Enhances:
  - Destructive effects  
  - Burn and damage-over-time effects  
  - Offensive enchantments  
- Less effective for defensive or purification-based magic  

---

## Combat Integration
- +3 Damage Modifier  
- +3 Armor Modifier  
- Favours aggressive and high-damage builds  

---

## Special Interaction
- Increased effectiveness against **Celestial Realm creatures**  
- More effective against Light-aligned entities  
- Reduced effectiveness when used against Infernal-aligned beings  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires Infernal materials  
- Must be forged within the **Infernal Realm**
