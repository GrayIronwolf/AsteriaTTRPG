# Quicksilver Ingot

## Effect
Quicksilver Ingots produce a highly adaptive metal that enhances speed, precision, and responsiveness. It reacts to both physical movement and magical input, making it ideal for fluid combat styles.

---
## Appearance
Quicksilver Ingots appear as perfectly refined bars of reflective metal, their surface constantly shifting with subtle waves and distortions. Despite being solid, they give the impression of barely contained motion.

When held, the metal seems to “adjust” slightly, redistributing its form in response to grip, movement, or intent.
