# Tungsten Ingot

## Effect
Tungsten Ingots produce an extremely dense and durable metal that excels in defensive applications and heavy weapon construction. It sacrifices speed and flexibility for raw endurance and impact force.

---
## Appearance
Tungsten Ingots are thick, dark grey bars with a matte, almost stone-like finish. They lack the reflective quality of most metals, instead appearing solid and weighty, with sharp, clean edges that emphasise their density.

When handled, the ingot gives the impression of extreme weight and immovability, as if the material resists being lifted or shifted, reinforcing its nature as a metal built for endurance rather than elegance.

---
## Crafting Use
- Heavy armor  
- Warhammers and blunt weapons  
- Reinforced structures  
- Defensive equipment

## Material Traits
- Extremely heavy and dense  
- Exceptional resistance to heat and pressure  
- Difficult to shape once refined  
- Maintains integrity under extreme stress
