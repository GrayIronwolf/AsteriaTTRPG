# Abyssal Steel Ingot

## Effect
Abyssal Steel is a void-forged alloy created by infusing High Steel with Abyssal essence. It exerts immense pressure on its surroundings, suppressing energy and overwhelming resistance through sheer density and presence.

---

## Appearance
Abyssal Steel appears as a deep, lightless metal with a faint blue-black tone. Its surface absorbs light completely, with subtle ripples forming across it like a calm, endless ocean.

When observed closely, the metal seems to distort depth, as if it extends far beyond its physical form.

---

## Lore
Forged within the depths of the Abyssal Realm, Abyssal Steel is shaped under immense pressure where energy collapses inward rather than radiating outward.

It is said that each ingot carries the weight of the abyss itself, making it one of the most oppressive materials known.

---

## Material Traits
- Extremely dense and pressure-heavy  
- Suppresses surrounding energy  
- Maintains integrity under extreme force  
- Difficult to shape and transport  

---

## Alloy Composition
- High Steel  
- Abyssal Essence  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Strong affinity with **Abyssal** 
- Enhances:
  - Suppression effects  
  - Control and restriction  
  - Anti-energy interactions  

---

## Combat Integration
- +5 Damage Modifier  
- +4 Armor Modifier  
- Favours control, suppression, and heavy combat builds  

---

## Special Interaction
- Reduces effectiveness of Light-aligned effects  
- Performs best against high-energy or magic-heavy opponents  
- Creates a “pressure” effect in close combat  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires High Steel  
- Requires Abyssal Essence  
- Must be forged within the **Abyssal Realm**
