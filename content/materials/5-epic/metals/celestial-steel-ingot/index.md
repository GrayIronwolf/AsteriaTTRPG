# Celestial Steel Ingot

## Effect
Celestial Steel is a refined divine alloy created by infusing High Steel with concentrated celestial essence. It represents a perfected form of Light-aligned material, offering superior protection, clarity, and stability.

---

## Appearance
Celestial Steel appears as a luminous silver-white metal with faint golden undertones. Soft currents of light move beneath its surface, giving it a constant, calm radiance.

The metal reflects light cleanly without glare, creating a sense of clarity and purity rather than brilliance.

---

## Lore
Celestial Steel is the evolution of Celestial Bronze, forged by combining High Steel with celestial essence within divine forges. This process refines the metal beyond mortal limits, creating a material capable of channeling higher-order Light.

It is often reserved for relic weapons, divine armor, and artifacts tied to celestial forces or higher beings.
