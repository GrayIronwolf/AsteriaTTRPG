# Dark Stone Ingot

## Effect
Dark Stone is an evolved form of Moonstone, shaped through prolonged exposure to Dark and Death-aligned environments. It enhances suppression, decay, and control over opposing forces.

---

## Appearance
Dark Stone appears as a deep black-grey metal with faint, dim internal movement beneath its surface. Unlike Ebony, it does not absorb all light, but instead dulls it, creating a muted, twilight-like sheen.

---

## Lore
Dark Stone forms when Moonstone is exposed to environments saturated with darkness and death energy. This transformation strips away balance, leaving a material aligned with suppression and control.

---

## Material Traits
- Absorbs and dampens energy  
- Stable under shadow and death influence  
- Suppresses surrounding magical presence  
- Performs best in low-light or dark environments  

---

## Evolution Path
