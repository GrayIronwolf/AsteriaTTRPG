# Light Stone Ingot

## Effect
Light Stone is an evolved form of Moonstone, refined under continuous exposure to Light. It amplifies clarity, protection, and purification, making it ideal for defensive and support-oriented equipment.

---

## Appearance
Light Stone appears as a luminous, pale metal with a soft white and silver glow. Its surface reflects light cleanly, with faint internal radiance that pulses gently beneath it.

---

## Lore
Light Stone forms when Moonstone is exposed to intense Light-aligned environments over extended periods. This process removes its dual nature, leaving behind a purified material aligned entirely with Light.
