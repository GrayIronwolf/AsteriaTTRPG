# Titan Steel Ingot

## Effect
Titan Steel is a legendary alloy forged by infusing High Steel with the blood of the Titan race. It embodies unmatched physical strength and resilience, allowing it to endure and deliver immense force without compromise.

---

## Appearance
Titan Steel appears as a dense, dark metallic alloy with faint, slow-moving veins of deep amber or gold running beneath its surface. These veins pulse subtly, as if carrying the lingering essence of something immense and ancient.

The metal feels impossibly heavy for its size, and even at rest, it gives the impression of contained force.

---

## Lore
Titan Steel is not merely forged — it is created through sacrifice.

The process requires High Steel to be infused with the essence of a Titan, a race of lesser gods whose existence is rare and dwindling. Because of this, Titan Steel is one of the most difficult materials to create, and its existence is tightly controlled across the realms.

Many believe that each ingot retains a fragment of the Titan’s will, granting it strength beyond mortal materials.

---

## Material Traits
- Extremely dense and heavy  
- Unmatched resistance to physical force  
- Maintains integrity under extreme stress  
- Difficult to shape and nearly impossible to break  

---

## Alloy Composition
- High Steel  
- Titan Blood (or preserved Titan essence)

## Enchantment Use
- Moderate affinity with **Earth**  
- Enhances:

  - Impact and force  
  - Defensive enchantments  
- Poor synergy with delicate or precision-based enchantments

## Combat Integration
- +5 Damage Modifier  
- +5 Armor Modifier  
- Favours heavy, strength-based, and frontline builds  

---

## Special Interaction
- Increased effectiveness against massive or high-tier creatures  
- Cannot be easily broken, sundered, or degraded  
- Performs best under direct physical confrontation

## Crafting Source

- Requires advanced or divine-grade forge
