# Elementium Ingot

## Effect
Elementium is a harmonised elemental metal capable of conducting and stabilising multiple elemental energies simultaneously. It naturally adapts to the elemental affinity of its wielder while maintaining perfect internal balance.

---

## Appearance
Elementium Ingots possess a smooth metallic surface threaded with constantly shifting elemental colouration. Waves of ember-gold, deep ocean blue, storm silver, and emerald-green move beneath the surface like flowing energy.

The metal subtly changes depending on nearby elemental forces.

---

## Lore
Elementium is considered one of the rarest and most refined elemental materials in existence. Ancient records claim it was first forged within the Astral Crucible by the god-smith Auralon the Starborn.

Unlike most materials, Elementium does not force elemental power into shape — it allows the elements to coexist naturally within its structure.

Because of this, many believe the metal itself possesses a primitive awareness of balance and harmony.

---
