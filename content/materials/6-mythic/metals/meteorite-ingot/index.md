# Meteorite Ingot

## Effect
Meteorite Ingots produce a dense celestial metal capable of withstanding immense force and delivering devastating impact power.

The metal retains traces of cosmic compression and atmospheric heat, giving it exceptional strength and resilience.

---

## Appearance
Meteorite Ingots are dark grey-black with scorched crimson streaks and faint silver inclusions beneath the surface.

The metal carries a rough, star-forged appearance rather than the polished finish of refined alloys.

---

## Lore
Meteorite metal has been prized throughout history due to its rarity and celestial origins. Ancient smiths believed weapons forged from fallen stars carried the will of the heavens themselves.

Because impact sites are uncommon and heavily contested, true Meteorite metal remains rare even among advanced civilizations.

---

## Material Traits
- Extremely impact-resistant  
- Retains celestial compression properties  
- Highly resistant to heat and pressure  
- Difficult to forge and refine  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Strong affinity with **Fire** and **Earth**  
- Enhances:
  - Force-based enchantments  
  - Impact effects  
  - Heat-related abilities  
- Less effective for delicate magical refinement  

---

## Combat Integration
- +5 Damage Modifier  
- +3 Armor Modifier  
- Favours heavy weapons, siege equipment, and impact-focused combat styles  

---

## Special Interaction
- Performs exceptionally well under high-impact stress  
- Retains heat longer than most metals  
- Resistant to structural cracking under force  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires 4x [[Meteorite Ore]]  
- Requires starforge or extreme-temperature forge conditions  

---
