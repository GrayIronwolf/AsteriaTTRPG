# Necronium Ingot

## Effect
Necronium is a death-infused metal that directly interacts with living essence. When brought into contact with living beings, it drains vitality and converts it into necrotic energy.

---

## Appearance
Necronium Ingots are dark and muted, with faint grey-violet tones beneath a smooth surface. The metal appears unnaturally still, with subtle distortions resembling drifting ash or fading smoke.

When held by a living creature, the air around it feels colder and heavier.

---

## Lore
Once refined, Necronium becomes one of the most dangerous materials in existence. It is not forged from the earth, but from accumulated death itself.

Weapons and armor made from Necronium are feared across realms, as they do not simply harm — they erode life directly.

---

## Material Traits
- Reacts to living essence  
- Drains vitality on sustained contact  
- Stable under necrotic influence  
- Weak under intense Light-aligned energy  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Exceptional affinity with **Death**  
- Enhances:
  - Life-drain  
  - Decay  
  - Curse-based effects  
- Poor interaction with Light-aligned enchantments  

---

## Combat Integration
- +5 Damage Modifier  
- +3 Armor Modifier  
- Favours drain, sustain, and death-based builds  

---

## Special Interaction
- Damages **living targets on successful strikes or sustained contact**  
- No effect on undead or non-living entities  
- Prolonged exposure weakens living creatures  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires 4x [[Necronium Ore]]  
- Requires necrotic-aligned forge  
- Forms in death-saturated environments or Death-aligned realms  

---

## Quote
*"It does not strike life — it removes it."*
