# Red Duranium Ingot

## Effect
Red Duranium is a corrupted evolution of Duranium, forged through exposure to immense heat, bloodshed, and violent energy. It retains the adaptive resilience of Duranium while converting absorbed force into aggression, regeneration, and destructive output.

---

## Appearance
Red Duranium Ingots are dark metallic bars streaked with deep crimson veins that pulse slowly beneath the surface like molten blood.

The metal radiates faint heat constantly, and during combat or stress, the glow intensifies as energy moves throughout the ingot.

When struck, the metal emits a deep resonance rather than a metallic clang.

---

## Lore
Red Duranium is found only within the Zahram Peaks, surrounded by the deadly Rahzakar Dunes.

Over countless years, Duranium exposed to the extreme conditions of the region mutates into a far more dangerous material. The constant cycle of death, predation, and heat saturates the ore with violent energy, transforming it into a living, reactive alloy.

Weapons and armor forged from Red Duranium are feared for their hunger — growing stronger through prolonged combat and sustained conflict.
