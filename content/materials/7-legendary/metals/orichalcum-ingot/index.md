# Orichalcum Ingot

## Effect
Orichalcum is a sacred divine metal renowned for its ability to channel radiant energy, divine flame, and holy power with exceptional stability.

Weapons and armor forged from Orichalcum are often regarded as relics rather than equipment due to the metal’s purity and celestial resonance.

---

## Appearance
Orichalcum Ingots possess a radiant golden-red metallic surface with soft inner illumination flowing beneath the metal like liquid sunlight.

Under intense light or divine energy, the ingot shines with a warm solar glow.

---

## Lore
Throughout history, Orichalcum has been associated with divine kingdoms, celestial champions, and sacred relic forges.

Ancient forge masters believed the metal carried remnants of the first dawn within its structure, making it one of the few materials capable of enduring immense holy power without instability.

Many legendary relics wielded by paladins, high priests, and divine guardians were forged from Orichalcum.

---

## Material Traits
- Exceptional divine energy conductivity  
- Resistant to corruption and decay  
- Stable under intense radiant or holy energy  
- Difficult to shape without celestial forge conditions  

---

## Enchantment Use
- Exceptional affinity with **Fire** and **Light**  
- Enhances:
  - Radiant enchantments  
  - Blessings and purification effects  
  - Holy flame abilities  
- Weak interaction with Dark and corruption-based enchantments  

---

## Combat Integration
- +5 Damage Modifier  
- +5 Armor Modifier  
- Favours paladins, divine casters, relic weapons, and holy guardians  

---

## Special Interaction
- Performs exceptionally well under sunlight or divine influence  
- Naturally suppresses weak corruption and curses  
- Resonates strongly with holy relics and sacred locations  

---

## Crafting Source
- Requires 4x [[Orichalcum Ore]]  
- Requires celestial forge or divine flame refinement conditions
