# Antimony Ore

## Effect
Antimony Ore is a brittle, crystalline metal-bearing ore that enhances hardness and structural integrity when alloyed. It is rarely used alone but is critical in reinforcing softer metals.

---

## Lore
Known as the *“Bone of the Earth,”* antimony was believed by ancient smiths to give metals their “spine.” While fragile in isolation, it grants resilience to those it strengthens.

---

## Material & Crafting Traits
- Brittle and unstable in raw form  
- Breaks easily under stress  
- Essential for strengthening alloys  
- Requires careful refinement  

---

## Refinement
- Smelts into: [[Antimony Ingot]]
- Ratio: 4 Ore → 1 Ingot
- Notes: Fragile structure leads to material loss if overheated
