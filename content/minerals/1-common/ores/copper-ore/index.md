# Copper Ore

## Effect
Copper Ore contains trace conductive veins that respond strongly to heat and energy. When refined, it produces a metal highly suited for elemental channeling and early enchantment work.

---

## Lore
Known as the *“First Flame Ore,”* copper was the first material mortals learned to draw from the earth and shape with fire. It represents the birth of craft and the beginning of all metallurgy.

---

## Material & Crafting Traits
- Easily mined and refined  
- High impurity content → refinement loss  
- Strong elemental conductivity (Fire / Lightning)  
- Core material for early crafting systems
