# Nickel Ore

## Effect
Nickel Ore contains balanced metallic properties that allow it to stabilise and reinforce other materials when refined and alloyed. It is rarely used alone but is essential in improving durability and magical consistency.

---

## Lore
Known as the *“Silent Binder,”* nickel was long overlooked until master smiths discovered its ability to unify stronger metals without weakening them. It is said to be the metal that prevents failure before it happens.

---

## Material & Crafting Traits
- Moderate density and durability  
- Resistant to corrosion  
- Stabilises alloy structures  
- Commonly found near iron deposits
