# Tin Ore

## Effect
Tin Ore is a soft, low-density material with minimal standalone use, but it plays a crucial role in stabilising alloys and enabling stronger metal compositions.

---

## Lore
Called the *“Silent Catalyst,”* tin lacks strength but enables greatness in others. Entire ages of advancement depended on its quiet presence in alloy crafting.

---

## Material & Crafting Traits
- Very soft and easy to refine  
- High impurity loss during smelting  
- Essential for alloy creation  
- Common training material  

---

- Ratio: 4 Ore → 1 Ingot
