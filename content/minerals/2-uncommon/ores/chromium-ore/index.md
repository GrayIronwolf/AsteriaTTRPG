# Chromium Ore

## Effect
Chromium Ore yields a highly stable metal known for its resistance to corrosion and structural reinforcement. It is essential in crafting advanced alloys that require durability and longevity.

---

## Lore
Known as the *“Shield Vein”* of the earth, chromium was first discovered in mineral deposits untouched by rust or decay. Smiths quickly realised its value — not for power, but for endurance.

---

## Material & Crafting Traits
- Highly resistant to corrosion and environmental damage  
- Reinforces structural integrity of alloys  
- Stable during high-temperature refinement  
- Often found near iron-rich deposits
