# Platinum Ore

## Effect
Platinum Ore contains highly pure metallic veins that conduct magical energy with exceptional stability, making it one of the most valuable refinement materials for enchantment-focused crafting.

---

## Lore
Known as the *“Metal of Divinity,”* platinum is said to form where celestial energy seeps into the earth. Some believe it is the crystallised essence of fallen stars.

---

## Material & Crafting Traits
- High purity with minimal impurities  
- Extremely difficult to extract and refine  
- Resistant to corruption and decay  
- Found in deep or magically saturated environments  

---

## Refinement
- Smelts into: [[Platinum Ingot]]
- Ratio: 4 Ore → 1 Ingot
- Notes: Requires advanced or stable forge conditions

---

## Alloy Use
- High-tier alloys (future)

---

## Enchantment Use
- Strong affinity with Light and Celestial magic  
- Maintains enchantment stability under heavy use
