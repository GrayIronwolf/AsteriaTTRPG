# Duranium Ore

## Effect
Duranium Ore is a dense, energy-reactive metal that forms in regions of extreme pressure and force. It naturally absorbs and stabilises incoming energy, making it highly resilient to both physical and environmental stress.

---

## Appearance
Duranium Ore appears as a dark grey metallic stone with faint, shifting patterns beneath its surface. These patterns move slowly, like energy being redistributed through the material.

When struck, subtle ripples travel across the ore before fading.

---

## Lore
Duranium forms deep beneath the surface of the world or within areas of intense energy convergence. These environments compress and fuse energy into physical form, creating a metal that responds to force rather than resisting it.

It is rare and difficult to extract due to its density and reactive nature.

---

## Material Traits
- Extremely dense and heavy  
- Absorbs and redistributes energy  
- Stable under high pressure and force  
- Difficult to mine and transport  

---
