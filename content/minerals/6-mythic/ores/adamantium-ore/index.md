# Adamantium Ore

## Effect
Adamantium Ore is one of the densest and most resilient materials known. Formed under immense pressure deep beneath the world or within the remains of collapsed stars, it possesses extraordinary structural stability.

---

## Appearance
Adamantium Ore appears as dark grey-black stone threaded with dense metallic veins. Its surface is rough and matte, but when struck, faint silver reflections emerge beneath the stone.

The ore feels unnaturally heavy, even in small quantities.

---

## Lore
Adamantium Ore forms only under impossible pressure and heat, either deep within the earth or in locations touched by the remnants of fallen celestial bodies.

Ancient civilizations believed the metal was the foundation of mountains themselves, and many legendary fortresses were said to be built around Adamantium deposits rather than mined from them.

---

## Material Traits
- Extremely dense and difficult to move  
- Resistant to physical and environmental degradation  
- Maintains structural integrity under immense force  
- Nearly impossible to refine using standard methods  

---
