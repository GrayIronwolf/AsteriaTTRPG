# Elementium Ore

## Effect
Elementium Ore is a rare elemental metal formed where powerful ley lines and elemental forces converge. The ore naturally harmonises surrounding energies, allowing it to stabilise and conduct multiple elemental alignments simultaneously.

---

## Appearance
Elementium Ore appears as dark metallic stone threaded with shifting veins of elemental colour. These veins pulse softly and change depending on the surrounding elemental environment.

Near fire the ore glows amber-red, while near water it shifts toward deep blue and silver tones.

---

## Lore
Elementium Ore forms in regions where elemental energy reaches perfect balance — ancient leyline nexuses, elemental convergence zones, or shattered locations touched by multiple realms.

Many scholars believe the material is not forged by the elements, but created by their agreement to coexist.

Fragments of Elementium can alter the surrounding ecosystem over time, slowly reshaping environments to reflect elemental balance.
