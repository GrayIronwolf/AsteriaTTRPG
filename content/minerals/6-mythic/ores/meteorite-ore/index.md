# Meteorite Ore

## Effect
Meteorite Ore is a dense celestial material formed from fragments of fallen stars and cosmic bodies that survive atmospheric descent and impact with the world.

The ore carries immense internal compression and residual heat from its passage through the heavens.

---

## Appearance
Meteorite Ore appears as dark metallic stone streaked with burnt crimson and silver-grey veins. Portions of the surface appear partially melted or glassed over from atmospheric entry.

Small fragments often remain unnaturally warm long after impact.

---

## Lore
Meteorite Ore forms when celestial bodies survive their descent from the heavens and crash into the world with catastrophic force.

Many ancient civilizations viewed meteorites as omens, gifts from the stars, or weapons cast down by the gods. Entire wars have been fought over large impact sites due to the rarity and strength of the metal.

---

## Material Traits
- Extremely dense and impact-resistant  
- Retains residual celestial heat  
- Difficult to shape using standard forging methods  
- Stable under extreme pressure and force  

---
