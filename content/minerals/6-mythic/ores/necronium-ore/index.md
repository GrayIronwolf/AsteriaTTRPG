# Necronium Ore

## Effect
Necronium Ore is a condensed form of death energy that reacts to living presence. It slowly drains vitality from nearby living creatures, converting it into dormant necrotic energy within the material.

---

## Appearance
Necronium Ore appears as dark, ash-grey stone with faint violet undertones. Its surface seems unnaturally still, with subtle drifting patterns beneath it like slow-moving smoke.

In proximity to living beings, the surrounding air feels colder and quieter.

---

## Lore
Necronium Ore does not form through traditional geological processes. Instead, it manifests in locations where death energy has accumulated over time — battlefields, mass graves, and necrotic domains.

These formations are rare and unstable, often appearing after significant events rather than through natural mining veins.

---

## Material Traits
- Forms from death saturation rather than mining  
- Reacts to nearby life energy  
- Stable under necrotic influence  
- Weak under strong Light-aligned environments
