# Red Duranium Ore

## Effect
Red Duranium Ore is a corrupted evolution of Duranium Ore, transformed by the extreme heat, bloodshed, and violent energy surrounding the Zahram Peaks.

Unlike normal Duranium, the ore actively absorbs heat and vitality from its environment, storing it as unstable internal energy.

---

## Appearance
Red Duranium Ore appears as dense, dark metallic stone threaded with deep crimson veins that pulse faintly beneath the surface.

These veins glow brighter when exposed to combat, heat, or living creatures, giving the ore the appearance of a slow, burning heartbeat trapped within stone.

Fragments of the ore remain unnaturally warm even during cold nights within the desert.

---

## Lore
Red Duranium Ore forms only within the Zahram Peaks, a mountain range hidden deep within the Rahzakar Dunes — known in Common as the Ember Dunes.

The region is infamous for its extreme heat, blood-red sandstorms, and monstrous creatures that move beneath the dunes waiting for prey. Over centuries, Duranium Ore exposed to the violence and environmental pressure of the region mutates into Red Duranium.

Many believe the mountains themselves are alive, feeding the ore through conflict and death.
