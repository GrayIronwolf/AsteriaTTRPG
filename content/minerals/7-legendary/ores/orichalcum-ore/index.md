# Orichalcum Ore

## Effect
Orichalcum Ore is a sacred solar metal formed through the fusion of divine heat and celestial mineral deposits. The ore naturally radiates warmth and carries powerful affinity with holy and radiant energies.

---

## Appearance
Orichalcum Ore appears as luminous golden-red stone threaded with radiant metallic veins. Under sunlight, the veins shimmer softly like molten gold beneath glass.

Fragments of the ore emit faint warmth even when removed from active heat.

---

## Lore
Ancient records claim Orichalcum first formed during the Age of Dawn, when divine flame descended into the mortal world and fused with the deep earth.

The metal became revered among celestial orders and master smiths due to its purity and ability to channel divine energies without corruption.

Many sacred relics and legendary weapons were forged from Orichalcum during the early ages.

---

## Material Traits
- Naturally radiates solar warmth  
- Highly resistant to corruption and decay  
- Excellent conductor of holy and radiant energies  
- Difficult to refine without divine-grade heat
