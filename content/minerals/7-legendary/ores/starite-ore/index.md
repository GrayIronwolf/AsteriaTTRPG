# Starite Ore

## Effect
Starite Ore is a rare astral metal formed from the crystallised remnants of fallen stars and concentrated celestial mana. The ore naturally resonates with magical currents, allowing it to conduct mana and astral energy with exceptional efficiency.

---

## Appearance
Starite Ore appears as dark silver-blue stone threaded with shimmering prismatic veins resembling distant constellations.

Under moonlight or magical illumination, faint star-like patterns move slowly beneath the surface.

---

## Lore
Starite forms where celestial fragments and astral energies converge with the world itself. Ancient astronomer-priests believed the ore carried echoes of the first stars that illuminated creation.

Because of its natural resonance with mana and planar energy, Starite became highly valued among mage orders, celestial smiths, and advanced arcane civilizations.

---

## Material Traits
- Naturally resonates with mana currents  
- Stable under astral and planar energies  
- Excellent magical conductivity  
- Difficult to refine without precise forge control
