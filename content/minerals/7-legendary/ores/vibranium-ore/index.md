# Vibranium Ore

## Effect
Vibranium Ore is a rare resonant metal capable of absorbing, storing, and redistributing kinetic force through internal harmonic vibrations.

The ore naturally reacts to movement, impact, and sound, creating subtle resonance pulses throughout its structure.

---

## Appearance
Vibranium Ore appears as dark silver stone threaded with faint blue-white resonance lines that pulse softly under movement or sound.

When struck, the ore emits a low harmonic tone that lingers briefly in the air.

---

## Lore
Ancient records claim Vibranium first formed after a celestial impact fused cosmic energy with the raw motion of the world itself.

Unlike most metals, Vibranium does not simply endure force — it learns from it, storing kinetic resonance within its structure and releasing it gradually over time.

Because of this property, the metal became highly prized among artificers, engineers, and advanced forge guilds.

---

## Material Traits
- Absorbs and redistributes kinetic force  
- Resonates with movement and vibration  
- Extremely resistant to shattering  
- Requires harmonic refinement techniques
