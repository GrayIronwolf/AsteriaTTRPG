## Racial Traits
### Amphibious Nature
Uniden can breathe both air and water, easily adapting between land and aquatic environments.

### Waterborne Healing
When immersed in a natural source of water (like rivers or rain), Undines are able to regenerate health quicker:
- In combat: +3 HP per round  
- Out of combat: +10 HP per round
