## Racial Traits
### Frost Sense

#### Description
Frostborn can feel changes in temperature, ice formations, snowfall, and cold mana currents around them.

#### Gameplay Effect
- Gain +2 Perception in snowy, icy, or cold environments.
- Can sense approaching blizzards and severe weather several hours in advance.
- Detect nearby sources of ice magic within 18 metres.
- Gain advantage on Survival checks in frozen environments.

---

### Heart of Winter

#### Description
The essence of eternal winter flows through every Frostborn.

#### Gameplay Effect
- Resistant to Cold damage.
- Immune to natural environmental cold.
- Ignore penalties from snow, ice, and freezing temperatures.
- Ice and Frost spells cost 10% less Mana.

---

### Frozen Resolve

#### Description
Like ancient glaciers, Frostborn possess patience and resilience beyond most mortal races.

#### Gameplay Effect
- Gain +2 on rolls against Fear effects.
- Gain +2 on rolls against environmental exhaustion caused by cold climates.
- Recover Stamina 25% faster while in cold environments.
- Gain advantage against effects that force panic or emotional manipulation.

---

### Breath of the Glacier

#### Description
The body of a Frostborn is naturally adapted to survive beneath frozen waters.

#### Gameplay Effect
- Can breathe underwater indefinitely.
- Ignore underwater combat penalties.
- Swimming speed is doubled.
- Can safely swim in freezing waters without harm.
