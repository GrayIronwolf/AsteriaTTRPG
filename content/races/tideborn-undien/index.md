## Racial Traits
### Tide Sense

#### Description
Tideborn possess an innate awareness of the ocean's movements. They can feel shifts in currents, tides, and nearby bodies of water as naturally as breathing.

#### Gameplay Effect
- Gain +2 Perception while within 30 metres of a natural body of water.
- Can determine the direction of nearby coastlines and large bodies of water.
- Gain advantage on Navigation checks while travelling at sea.
- Can sense incoming tidal changes several hours in advance.

---

### Child of the Ocean

#### Description
The blessing of the Ocean Spirits flows strongly through Tideborn bloodlines.

#### Gameplay Effect
- Water spells cost 10% less Mana.
- Gain +1 to Water spell rolls.
- Resistant to Cold damage.
- Gain advantage on checks involving ocean survival and navigation.

---

### Ocean's Grace

#### Description
Tideborn move through water with effortless elegance, as though carried by unseen currents.

#### Gameplay Effect
- Swim speed is doubled.
- Ignore difficult terrain while underwater.
- Gain advantage on Agility checks while swimming.
- Suffer no penalties from rough seas or strong currents.

---

### Breath of the Sea

#### Description
The ocean accepts the Tideborn as one of its own.

#### Gameplay Effect
- Can breathe underwater indefinitely.
- Ignore underwater combat penalties.
- Cannot drown through natural means.
- Gain +2 Endurance while submerged.
