# Stealth

## Overview

Stealth Skill Techniques represent specialised methods of remaining unseen, moving silently, concealing evidence and avoiding detection through mastery of the **Stealth** skill.

Stealth is more than simply hiding behind an object. A skilled practitioner learns how light, sound, movement and attention shape what others perceive. They understand when to move, where to place their weight and how to use terrain, crowds and distractions to disappear from notice.

Novices learn to control their footsteps and remain still within basic concealment. As their mastery grows, they learn to cross exposed spaces, vanish within crowds, conceal their trail and move through guarded areas without alerting nearby creatures.

Legendary practitioners can pass through places others consider impossible to infiltrate, becoming little more than a fleeting shadow or a forgotten movement at the edge of sight.

Whenever a character reaches a new **Stealth Skill Rank**, they may choose **one technique** from the four techniques available at that rank.

Once selected, a technique becomes part of the character's Stealth training unless it is replaced through retraining approved by the GM.

Stealth Techniques are **Skill Techniques**, not Talents. They do not normally have an inherent resource cost. Prolonged concealment, difficult movement, extreme physical control or repeated attempts under pressure may still cause Stamina loss or Exhaustion.

### Novice Techniques

> The Novice learns that stillness is as important as movement.  
> A hidden figure is often revealed not by being seen, but by moving at the wrong moment.

#### Soft Step

You carefully control where and how your feet touch the ground.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when moving quietly across ordinary terrain.
- Reduce noise caused by loose stones, floorboards and light debris.
- Recognise surfaces that are likely to create excessive sound.
- Running or moving through heavily obstructed terrain may remove this benefit.

---

#### Stillness

You remain motionless while observation passes over your hiding place.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when remaining hidden without moving.
- Minor discomfort does not immediately force you to shift position.
- Reduce the chance of being noticed through small involuntary movements.
- The benefit ends when you attack, speak loudly or leave concealment.

---

#### Basic Concealment

You understand how to use shadows, furniture and natural cover to hide your body.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when hiding behind suitable physical concealment.
- Quickly identify nearby locations capable of hiding a creature of your size.
- Reduce penalties caused by imperfect but usable cover.
- This technique cannot conceal you in a completely open and brightly lit area.

---

#### Quiet Hands

You have learned to handle common objects without unnecessary sound.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when opening ordinary doors, containers and windows quietly.
- Reduce noise caused by drawing weapons or retrieving small items.
- Recognise loose fittings, hinges and objects likely to rattle.
- Damaged or trapped mechanisms may still produce unavoidable noise.

---

### Initiate Techniques

> The Initiate learns to move with the rhythm of the world around them.  
> A closing door, passing cart or gust of wind becomes an opportunity to disappear.

#### Shadow Movement

You use dim light and shifting shadows to conceal your movement.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when moving through dimly lit areas.
- Reduce the chance of being noticed when moving between nearby shadows.
- Recognise when changing light will briefly expose your position.
- Bright magical illumination may prevent this technique from functioning.

---

#### Sound Masking

You time your movement to coincide with surrounding noise.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when moving during rain, conversation, machinery or other significant sounds.
- Reduce penalties caused by crossing a noisy surface.
- Recognise repeating sounds that can be used to conceal your movement.
- Sudden silence may reveal you if you continue moving carelessly.

---

#### Low Profile

You reduce the amount of your body exposed while moving.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when remaining hidden behind low cover.
- Move through crouched or confined positions with reduced penalties.
- Reduce the chance of being seen over barriers and furniture.
- Your Movement may be reduced while maintaining the posture.

---

#### Concealed Equipment

You secure your belongings so they do not betray your presence.

**Effects**
- Reduce noise from armour, weapons, containers and carried equipment.
- Gain Advantage when preparing equipment for infiltration.
- Recognise items likely to reflect light or create sound.
- Heavy or poorly fitted equipment may still impose Stealth penalties.

---

### Apprentice Techniques

> The Apprentice understands that attention is limited.  
> The easiest path is not always unseen—it is simply the path no one thinks to watch.

#### Distracted Passage

You move while nearby creatures are focused elsewhere.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when hiding or moving while observers are distracted.
- Reduce penalties when crossing a briefly exposed space during a distraction.
- Recognise when a creature's attention has shifted away from your position.
- The benefit ends if you become the direct focus of observation.

---

#### Crowd Blend

You conceal yourself by adopting the pace and behaviour of those around you.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when hiding within a crowd or busy settlement.
- Reduce the chance of being remembered by casual witnesses.
- Match the movement and apparent purpose of nearby people.
- Distinctive clothing, behaviour or physical features may reduce the benefit.

---

#### Hidden Approach

You carefully close the distance to an unaware creature.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when approaching a creature that has not detected you.
- Reduce the penalty for moving through the edge of its hearing range.
- Recognise the direction in which the creature's attention is focused.
- The benefit ends if you Sprint or cross terrain without concealment.

---

#### Leave No Trace

You minimise signs left behind by your movement.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when concealing footprints, disturbed objects and minor environmental signs.
- Reduce the chance of ordinary trackers identifying your route.
- Recognise when your clothing or equipment has left visible evidence.
- This technique does not erase blood, strong scent or magical residue.

---

### Journeyman Techniques

> The Journeyman no longer relies upon a single hiding place.  
> Every room, alley and forest path becomes a collection of unseen routes.

#### Silent Takedown

You subdue an unaware target before they can raise an alarm.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when attempting to Grapple or incapacitate an unaware adjacent creature.
- On success, the target cannot immediately call out.
- The technique may be used with a suitable non-lethal attack.
- Powerful, alert or physically superior creatures may resist normally.

---

#### Guard Pattern

You study the habits and blind spots of patrolling creatures.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when moving through an area after observing its guards.
- Identify repeating patrol routes, pauses and changes of direction.
- Recognise positions that guards consistently fail to watch.
- Unpredictable or magically coordinated guards may be harder to analyse.

---

#### Silent Climb

You can climb without creating obvious sound or debris.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage on Stealth checks made while climbing.
- Reduce noise caused by ropes, handholds and loose surfaces.
- Recognise unstable sections before placing your weight upon them.
- This technique does not replace an appropriate Climbing check.

---

#### Vanishing Turn

You break line of sight and immediately change direction.

**Effects**
- After leaving an observer's line of sight, gain Advantage on your next Stealth check.
- Creatures searching for you are more likely to continue toward your previous route.
- You may move up to **5 ft** in a different direction after entering concealment.
- The benefit is lost if the observer can clearly track your movement.

---

### Adept Techniques

> The Adept understands perception as a weapon.  
> They hide not only their body, but the very expectation that someone is present.

#### Unseen Movement

You maintain concealment while moving at greater speed.

**Effects**
- Move up to your normal Movement without automatically losing Stealth.
- Reduce penalties caused by moving quickly between concealed positions.
- Gain Advantage when crossing a short gap between areas of cover.
- Sprinting remains difficult to conceal without a suitable distraction.

---

#### Blind-Side Passage

You remain within the areas an observer is least capable of watching.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when moving outside a creature's direct field of vision.
- Recognise when an observer's equipment, anatomy or posture creates a blind side.
- Reduce penalties when passing close to a distracted creature.
- Creatures with supernatural awareness may not possess an exploitable blind side.

---

#### Hidden in Plain Sight

You use posture, timing and environmental familiarity to avoid attention without full cover.

**Effects**
- Attempt to remain unnoticed in an area where you have plausible reason to be present.
- Gain Advantage when disguising your purpose through ordinary behaviour.
- Casual observers are less likely to question or remember you.
- Direct inspection or behaviour that contradicts your surroundings may reveal you.

---

#### Predator's Silence

You conceal the final stages of your approach toward prey.

**Effects**
- Creatures you approach from concealment suffer Disadvantage when attempting to hear you.
- Gain Advantage on Stealth checks made within short range of an unaware target.
- Reduce noise caused by drawing a weapon immediately before attacking.
- The technique ends after you attack or are detected.

---

### Master Techniques

> The Master moves through guarded places as though they were built with hidden paths only they can see.  
> Locks, lanterns and watchful eyes become parts of a pattern rather than obstacles.

#### Ghost Walk

You move through difficult environments with almost no disturbance.

**Effects**
- Ignore ordinary Stealth penalties caused by vegetation, shallow water and loose ground.
- Reduce signs left by your passage.
- Gain Advantage when moving through terrain that would normally reveal a creature.
- Extreme conditions and magical terrain may still impose penalties.

---

#### Perfect Timing

You recognise the exact moment when every observer is least prepared to notice you.

**Effects**
- Once per encounter, gain Advantage on a Stealth check without requiring concealment from every observer.
- Move through a briefly exposed area before nearby creatures can react.
- Observers must be distracted, repositioning or otherwise experiencing a momentary lapse.
- This technique does not function against creatures directly watching you without interruption.

---

#### Misdirected Search

You manipulate evidence so pursuers search in the wrong direction.

**Effects**
- Create a believable false route using disturbed terrain, moved objects or planted evidence.
- Gain Advantage when directing searchers away from your true path.
- Creatures that fail to recognise the deception lose additional time following the false trail.
- Skilled trackers may contest the deception using Tracking.

---

#### Silent Assault

You preserve your concealment during the opening moments of combat.

**Effects**
- After attacking from Stealth, immediately attempt a new Stealth check if suitable concealment is available.
- Reduce the chance that distant enemies identify your exact position.
- You may shift **5 ft** after the attack without provoking an ordinary Reaction.
- Loud weapons, obvious magic or open terrain may prevent concealment.

---

### Grandmaster Techniques

> The Grandmaster leaves no image in the eye, no sound in the ear and no certainty in the mind.  
> Those who sense their presence do so only after the moment has already passed.

#### Presence Without Trace

Your movement leaves almost no ordinary evidence.

**Effects**
- Ordinary observers cannot reliably identify your footprints, scent or route.
- Gain Advantage against Tracking checks made to follow you.
- Minor signs of your passage naturally blend into the environment.
- Blood, powerful magic and deliberate destruction may still reveal your presence.

---

#### Between Heartbeats

You cross an exposed space during a moment too brief for ordinary awareness.

**Effects**
- Once per encounter, move up to half your Movement between two areas of concealment.
- Ordinary observers cannot make Reactions against this movement.
- You remain hidden if no observer possesses an uninterrupted view of the entire route.
- Supernatural perception may still detect your passage.

---

#### Forgotten Face

Your presence becomes extraordinarily difficult for witnesses to remember accurately.

**Effects**
- Casual witnesses struggle to recall your clothing, features and direction of travel.
- Gain Advantage when avoiding later identification.
- Descriptions of you given by ordinary observers are incomplete or contradictory.
- This technique does not alter memories magically or affect someone who studied you closely.

---

#### Living Shadow

You have achieved legendary mastery of concealment and silent movement.

**Effects**
- The first Stealth check you make each encounter has Advantage.
- Ordinary movement does not automatically break Stealth while suitable concealment exists.
- Once per encounter, reroll a failed Stealth check and use the new result.
- Creatures relying only upon ordinary sight and hearing suffer Disadvantage when attempting to locate you.
- Magical detection and specialised senses may still reveal your position.

---

> *"True stealth is not the absence of sound. It is the absence of suspicion."*
