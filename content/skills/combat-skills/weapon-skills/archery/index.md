# Archery

## Overview

Archery Skill Techniques represent specialised methods of aiming, firing, repositioning and controlling the battlefield through mastery of the **Archery** skill.

An archer learns far more than how to release an arrow. Skilled archers judge distance, account for movement, fire from difficult positions and exploit brief openings in an enemy's defence. As their mastery grows, they may pierce armour, strike distant targets, fire several arrows in rapid succession and perform legendary shots that appear almost impossible.

Whenever a character reaches a new **Archery Skill Rank**, they may choose **one technique** from the four techniques available at that rank.

Once selected, a technique becomes part of the character's archery training unless it is replaced through retraining approved by the GM.

Archery Techniques are **Skill Techniques**, not Talents. They do not normally have an inherent resource cost. Particularly demanding techniques may require additional Action Points, specialised ammunition or Stamina when the character is firing repeatedly or pushing beyond normal physical limits.

### Novice Techniques

#### Steady Draw

You control your breathing and maintain a stable draw.

**Effects**
- Gain **+1 Accuracy** with Archery attacks when you have not moved during the current turn.
- Minor environmental distractions do not interrupt your aim.
- The bonus ends after the attack is made.

---

#### Quick Nock

You can prepare an arrow with practiced speed.

**Effects**
- Reduce the time required to draw and nock an arrow.
- Gain Advantage on checks made to ready a bow under pressure.
- You may draw ammunition without interrupting ordinary movement.

---

#### Hunter's Eye

You have learned to recognise exposed areas on a target.

**Effects**
- Gain **+1 Damage** against creatures that are unaware of you.
- Gain Advantage when identifying an obvious unarmoured area on a visible target.
- This technique does not reveal hidden anatomical weaknesses.

---

#### Anchored Stance

You position your body to absorb the bow's strain.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage against effects that would disrupt your Archery attack.
- Reduce penalties caused by minor shaking, unstable footing or strong recoil.
- You lose this benefit if you are knocked Prone.

---

### Initiate Techniques

#### Longshot

You have learned to maintain accuracy over greater distances.

**Effects**
- Increase the effective range of bows you wield.
- Reduce the first range penalty applied to your Archery attacks.
- Gain Advantage when estimating the distance to a visible target.

---

#### Moving Shot

You can fire while repositioning.

**Effects**
- Moving up to half your normal Movement does not impose a penalty on Archery attacks.
- Gain Advantage when maintaining aim while walking or changing position.
- Sprinting or forced movement may still disrupt your shot.

---

#### Pinning Arrow

You aim for clothing, equipment or nearby terrain to restrict movement.

**Effects**
- On a successful attack, you may attempt to pin the target to a nearby surface.
- A pinned target must use an action or succeed on a Strength check to free itself.
- Creatures without clothing, armour or an appropriate nearby surface cannot be pinned.
- The technique may deal reduced damage at the GM's discretion.

---

#### Controlled Release

You release the bowstring at the exact moment your aim settles.

**Effects**
- Once per turn, ignore **1 point of Accuracy penalty** affecting an Archery attack.
- Reduce penalties caused by minor cover or poor visibility.
- This technique cannot overcome complete concealment.

---

### Apprentice Techniques

#### Piercing Shot

You align your arrow to penetrate protective materials.

**Effects**
- Ignore **2 Armour** with Archery attacks.
- Deal increased damage against lightly armoured targets.
- This technique does not ignore Natural Armour unless specifically allowed by the GM.

---

#### Rapid Shot

You can release a second arrow before your opponent reacts.

**Effects**
- Once per round, spend an additional **Action Point** to make a second Archery attack.
- The second attack suffers **-2 Accuracy**.
- Both attacks must target creatures within your bow's effective range.

---

#### Hamstring Shot

You target the legs to hinder an enemy's movement.

**Effects**
- Once per turn, a creature damaged by your arrow loses **10 ft Movement** until the end of its next turn.
- A Critical Hit may extend the movement penalty for an additional turn.
- Creatures without vulnerable limbs may be immune.

---

#### Covering Fire

You fire into an area to limit enemy movement.

**Effects**
- Choose a visible area within your bow's effective range.
- Enemies moving through the area risk an immediate Archery attack.
- The effect lasts until the start of your next turn.
- You must have available ammunition and a clear line of sight.

---

### Journeyman Techniques

#### Ricochet Shot

You can redirect an arrow from a solid surface.

**Effects**
- Make an Archery attack against a target behind partial cover by ricocheting the arrow.
- The attack suffers **-2 Accuracy**.
- The arrow requires an appropriate hard surface and a believable angle.
- This technique cannot strike targets behind complete sealed cover.

---

#### Eagle's Focus

You remain fixed upon a distant target.

**Effects**
- Spend one Action Point to focus on a visible creature.
- Gain **+2 Accuracy** against that creature until you change targets, lose sight of it or move more than half your Movement.
- The bonus does not stack with itself.

---

#### Disarming Shot

You aim for an opponent's weapon or held object.

**Effects**
- Make an Archery attack against an object held by a visible creature.
- On success, the creature must succeed on a Strength or Dexterity check or drop the object.
- The attack may damage fragile objects.
- Large or securely held objects are more difficult to disarm.

---

#### Split Arrow

You strike an incoming projectile or vulnerable object with your own arrow.

**Effects**
- Use your Reaction to attempt an Archery check against a visible non-magical projectile.
- On success, the projectile is deflected or destroyed.
- You must be aware of the projectile and have a prepared bow.
- Siege ammunition and massive projectiles cannot normally be stopped.

---

### Adept Techniques

#### Armour-Piercing Shot

You fire with enough precision and force to penetrate heavy protection.

**Effects**
- Ignore **4 Armour** with Archery attacks.
- Ignore **1 Natural Armour**.
- Deal increased durability damage to shields and armour struck directly.
- This technique does not stack with Piercing Shot.

---

#### Rain of Arrows

You release several arrows into a concentrated area.

**Effects**
- Spend **2 Action Points** and additional ammunition to target a small area.
- Creatures within the area must defend against the attack.
- Each affected target takes reduced weapon damage.
- Cover may reduce or prevent the damage.

---

#### Deadeye

You wait for the perfect moment to release a devastating shot.

**Effects**
- Spend one Action Point aiming before making the attack.
- Gain Advantage on the attack roll.
- On a successful hit, roll one additional weapon damage die.
- Moving or taking damage before firing may interrupt the aim.

---

#### Intercepting Shot

You can disrupt an enemy as they act.

**Effects**
- Use your Reaction to make an Archery attack against a creature beginning a visible action.
- On a Critical Hit, the target must succeed on an appropriate check or have its action interrupted.
- The technique cannot interrupt instantaneous or unseen actions.
- The GM determines whether the action can reasonably be disrupted.

---

### Master Techniques

#### Penetrating Line

Your arrow passes through one enemy and continues into another.

**Effects**
- Choose two creatures standing in a straight line.
- Make one Archery attack against the first creature.
- On a successful hit, make a second attack against the creature behind it.
- The second attack deals **50% weapon damage**.

---

#### Phantom Volley

Your draw and release become difficult for enemies to follow.

**Effects**
- Make up to three Archery attacks by spending additional Action Points.
- Each attack after the first suffers a cumulative **-1 Accuracy**.
- The attacks may target the same creature or different visible creatures.
- You must have sufficient ammunition available.

---

#### Perfect Trajectory

You instinctively account for wind, distance and movement.

**Effects**
- Ignore ordinary penalties caused by wind and long range.
- Reduce penalties against moving targets.
- Gain Advantage when firing from elevated terrain.
- Severe magical weather may still affect your arrows.

---

#### Heartseeker

You target a creature's most vulnerable vital area.

**Effects**
- Spend one Action Point aiming before making the attack.
- Critical Hits deal **two additional weapon damage dice**.
- Creatures without vital organs may be immune to the additional damage.
- The technique requires an unobstructed view of the target.

---

### Grandmaster Techniques

#### Arrow of Certain Death

You release a shot intended to end the battle with one perfect strike.

**Effects**
- Spend **2 Action Points** to make one Archery attack with Advantage.
- Ignore all Armour granted by worn equipment.
- Roll three additional weapon damage dice on a Critical Hit.
- Bosses and legendary creatures may resist instant-kill effects.

---

#### Horizon Shot

Your arrows can strike targets at seemingly impossible distances.

**Effects**
- Greatly increase your bow's maximum range.
- Ignore normal long-range Accuracy penalties.
- You may attack a target you can clearly observe through a suitable viewing aid.
- The arrow still requires a possible physical path to the target.

---

#### Thousand Arrow Discipline

Your mastery allows you to maintain a relentless stream of arrows.

**Effects**
- Once per encounter, enter the Thousand Arrow Discipline until the end of your next turn.
- Your Archery attacks require one fewer Action Point, to a minimum of one.
- You may make no more than four attacks during the technique.
- Each attack still consumes ammunition.

---

#### Unerring Archer

Your understanding of the bow has reached legendary perfection.

**Effects**
- The first Archery attack you make each round has Advantage.
- Archery attacks count as Magical for overcoming resistance.
- Ignore penalties from partial cover.
- Once per encounter, reroll a failed Archery attack and use the new result.
