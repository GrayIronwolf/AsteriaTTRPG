# Crossbows

## Overview

Crossbow Skill Techniques represent specialised methods of loading, aiming, firing and maintaining crossbows through mastery of the **Crossbows** skill.

A crossbow rewards patience, mechanical understanding and precise shot placement. Unlike bows, crossbows rely less upon continuous physical draw strength and more upon preparation, stability and the proper use of their mechanisms.

Novice crossbow users learn how to load safely and fire accurately. As their mastery grows, they learn to penetrate armour, interrupt charging enemies, prepare specialised bolts and control the battlefield through carefully timed attacks.

Legendary crossbow users are capable of striking targets through narrow openings, disabling equipment and delivering devastating bolts before their enemies recognise the danger.

Whenever a character reaches a new **Crossbows Skill Rank**, they may choose **one technique** from the four techniques available at that rank.

Once selected, a technique becomes part of the character's Crossbows training unless it is replaced through retraining approved by the GM.

Crossbow Techniques are **Skill Techniques**, not Talents. They do not normally have an inherent resource cost. Techniques may still require additional Action Points, specialised bolts, suitable loading mechanisms or Stamina when the weapon is used repeatedly under pressure.

### Novice Techniques

> The Novice learns that a crossbow is not simply aimed and fired.  
> Every latch, string and bolt must work together before the shot can be trusted.

#### Steady Stock

You brace the weapon firmly against your body before firing.

**Effects**
- Gain **+1 Accuracy** when firing without moving during the current turn.
- Reduce penalties caused by minor shaking or unstable footing.
- The bonus ends after the attack is made.
- This technique does not function while Prone unless the weapon can be properly supported.

---

#### Safe Loading

You have learned how to load a crossbow without placing your hands or equipment at unnecessary risk.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when loading a crossbow under pressure.
- Reduce the chance of injuring yourself during an ordinary loading failure.
- Recognise when the string, latch or bolt has been positioned incorrectly.
- This technique does not protect against a broken or sabotaged mechanism.

---

#### Bolt Recognition

You understand the basic differences between common crossbow bolts.

**Effects**
- Identify common bolt types and their intended purpose.
- Recognise whether a bolt is damaged, poorly balanced or unsuitable for your crossbow.
- Gain Advantage when selecting suitable ammunition for hunting, combat or practice.
- Magical and specialised bolts may require further knowledge.

---

#### Supported Aim

You use walls, tables, shields or terrain to steady your weapon.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when firing from a properly supported position.
- Reduce one ordinary Accuracy penalty caused by distance or instability.
- The support must be strong enough to hold the weapon steady.
- Moving away from the support ends the benefit.

---

### Initiate Techniques

> The Initiate begins to understand the weapon’s rhythm.  
> Loading, aiming and firing become one continuous mechanical discipline.

#### Practised Reload

You can reset and reload your crossbow with greater efficiency.

**Effects**
- Reduce the Action Point cost or time required to reload by one step, to the minimum allowed by the weapon.
- Gain Advantage when reloading while moving no more than half your Movement.
- You may retrieve a bolt without interrupting the reload.
- Heavy siege crossbows may not benefit fully from this technique.

---

#### Long Bolt

You adjust your firing angle for distant targets.

**Effects**
- Increase the effective range of crossbows you wield.
- Reduce the first long-range Accuracy penalty.
- Gain Advantage when estimating the distance to a stationary target.
- Severe wind or poor visibility may still impose penalties.

---

#### Braced Shot

You lock your stance before releasing a powerful shot.

**Effects**
- Spend an additional **Action Point** before making the attack.
- Gain **+2 Damage** on a successful hit.
- Ignore **1 Armour**.
- You cannot move after preparing the shot until the attack is completed.

---

#### Mechanism Familiarity

You understand how ordinary crossbow components fit and operate.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when inspecting a crossbow for mechanical faults.
- Perform basic repairs on strings, triggers, latches and loading mechanisms.
- Reduce the time required to clear an ordinary jam.
- Complex magical or repeating crossbows may require Engineering or another appropriate skill.

---

### Apprentice Techniques

> The Apprentice no longer waits for an obvious target.  
> They create openings by controlling when and where the bolt is released.

#### Armour-Piercing Bolt

You align a heavy bolt with gaps and weak points in armour.

**Effects**
- Ignore **2 Armour** with Crossbow attacks.
- Gain **+1 Damage** against targets wearing Medium or Heavy Armour.
- This technique requires a suitable solid bolt.
- It does not ignore Natural Armour unless another effect allows it.

---

#### Pinning Shot

You fire through clothing, equipment or limbs to restrict movement.

**Effects**
- On a successful attack, you may attempt to pin the target to a nearby surface.
- The target must use an action or succeed on a Strength check to free itself.
- The attack may deal reduced damage at the GM's discretion.
- The target must be close enough to an appropriate surface.

---

#### Prepared Shot

You wait with the crossbow trained upon a specific area.

**Effects**
- Choose a visible creature, doorway or small area within range.
- Make a Crossbow attack as a Reaction when the chosen trigger occurs.
- Gain **+1 Accuracy** on the triggered attack.
- The preparation ends if you move, become unable to see the area or take another major action.

---

#### Crippling Bolt

You target the legs, wings or other movement structures.

**Effects**
- Once per turn, a creature damaged by your bolt loses **10 ft Movement** until the end of its next turn.
- A Critical Hit prevents the target from Sprinting during that time.
- Flying creatures struck in a wing may be forced to descend at the GM's discretion.
- Creatures without vulnerable movement structures may be immune.

---

### Journeyman Techniques

> The Journeyman treats every bolt as a deliberate answer.  
> They do not fire often, but each release changes the shape of the battle.

#### Interrupting Shot

You fire the moment an enemy exposes itself during an action.

**Effects**
- Use your Reaction to attack a visible creature beginning a deliberate action.
- On a Critical Hit, the target must succeed on an appropriate check or have the action interrupted.
- The technique cannot interrupt instantaneous or unseen actions.
- You must have a loaded crossbow.

---

#### Penetrating Bolt

Your bolt carries enough force to pass through one target and continue onward.

**Effects**
- Choose two creatures standing in a straight line.
- Make one Crossbow attack against the first creature.
- On a successful hit, make a second attack against the creature behind it.
- The second attack deals **50% weapon damage**.
- Particularly large or heavily armoured targets may stop the bolt.

---

#### Disabling Shot

You target weapons, equipment and mechanical components.

**Effects**
- Make a Crossbow attack against a visible held object or exposed mechanism.
- On success, the item may be dropped, damaged or temporarily disabled.
- Gain Advantage when targeting fragile ropes, locks, levers or exposed components.
- The GM determines the durability and exact result.

---

#### Concealed Shooter

You minimise the signs that reveal your firing position.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage on Stealth checks made immediately after firing from concealment.
- Reduce the chance that enemies identify your exact location from a single shot.
- You may reposition up to **5 ft** after firing while remaining concealed.
- Bright muzzle effects, magical bolts or exposed positions may remove this benefit.

---

### Adept Techniques

> The Adept understands that the crossbow’s greatest strength is certainty.  
> The weapon is prepared slowly so that the bolt may act without hesitation.

#### Heavy Penetrator

You fire reinforced bolts capable of defeating substantial protection.

**Effects**
- Ignore **4 Armour** with Crossbow attacks.
- Ignore **1 Natural Armour**.
- Deal increased durability damage to shields and armour.
- This technique does not stack with Armour-Piercing Bolt.

---

#### Repeating Discipline

You have mastered a repeating or rapid-loading crossbow mechanism.

**Effects**
- Make one additional Crossbow attack by spending an additional **Action Point**.
- The second attack suffers **-2 Accuracy**.
- Both attacks must be made using a crossbow capable of rapid or repeated firing.
- Each attack consumes ammunition.

---

#### Anchor Bolt

You fire a heavy bolt attached to a rope, chain or reinforced line.

**Effects**
- Secure a line to suitable wood, stone, structures or large creatures.
- Gain Advantage when creating climbing lines or securing objects at range.
- Against a creature, the bolt may limit movement until removed.
- The line and bolt must be prepared before firing.

---

#### Executioner's Aim

You take additional time to place a bolt through a vital opening.

**Effects**
- Spend one **Action Point** aiming before making the attack.
- Gain Advantage on the attack roll.
- On a successful hit, roll one additional weapon damage die.
- The aim is interrupted if you move, lose sight of the target or suffer significant damage.

---

### Master Techniques

> The Master can read distance, armour and movement through the frame of the crossbow.  
> By the time the target hears the string, the decision has already been made.

#### Siege Bolt

You prepare an exceptionally heavy shot against large creatures and structures.

**Effects**
- Spend **2 Action Points** to make one Crossbow attack.
- Deal two additional weapon damage dice.
- Ignore half of the target's Armour.
- Deal increased damage against structures, siege engines and Large or larger creatures.
- Requires suitable heavy ammunition.

---

#### Perfect Mechanism

You maintain and operate crossbows with almost no wasted motion.

**Effects**
- Ignore ordinary penalties caused by weapon wear or minor mechanical imperfections.
- Once per round, reduce the reload requirement by one additional step.
- Gain Advantage when clearing a jam or repairing a damaged loading mechanism.
- The technique cannot restore a destroyed crossbow during combat.

---

#### Bolt Through Shadow

You strike targets concealed behind narrow openings and partial protection.

**Effects**
- Ignore penalties from partial cover.
- Reduce the penalty from heavy cover when part of the target remains visible.
- Gain Advantage when firing through narrow gaps or prepared firing slits.
- Complete cover cannot be bypassed.

---

#### Relentless Marksman

You maintain pressure by firing as soon as the mechanism is ready.

**Effects**
- When you reduce a creature to zero Health with a Crossbow attack, immediately begin reloading.
- If the crossbow becomes loaded during the same turn, gain **+1 Accuracy** on your next shot.
- The bonus lasts until the end of your next turn.
- This benefit may activate once per round.

---

### Grandmaster Techniques

> The Grandmaster does not merely fire a bolt.  
> They calculate a path through distance, armour and fate, then allow the mechanism to complete the answer.

#### Bolt of Final Judgement

You release a single devastating bolt intended to end the confrontation.

**Effects**
- Spend **2 Action Points** to make one Crossbow attack with Advantage.
- Ignore all Armour granted by worn equipment and shields.
- On a Critical Hit, roll three additional weapon damage dice.
- Bosses and legendary creatures may resist instant-kill or permanent injury effects.

---

#### Impossible Trajectory

You calculate shots through openings that appear too narrow or distant to use.

**Effects**
- Ignore ordinary penalties from long range, wind and partial cover.
- You may ricochet a suitable bolt from one hard surface before striking the target.
- The target must remain within the weapon's maximum range.
- The shot still requires a physically possible path.

---

#### Grand Arbalist

You have achieved legendary mastery over every ordinary crossbow design.

**Effects**
- The first Crossbow attack you make each round has Advantage.
- Crossbow attacks count as Magical for overcoming resistance.
- Reloading cannot be interrupted by ordinary distractions.
- Once per encounter, reroll a failed Crossbow attack and use the new result.

---

#### Dragon-Piercing Bolt

You prepare a shot capable of breaching the hide of enormous creatures.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage against Huge or larger creatures.
- Ignore all worn Armour and **3 Natural Armour**.
- On a Critical Hit, inflict a severe wound or roll two additional weapon damage dice.
- The technique requires a heavy crossbow and specially reinforced ammunition.

---
