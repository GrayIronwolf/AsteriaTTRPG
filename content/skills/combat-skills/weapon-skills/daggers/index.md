# Daggers

## Overview

Dagger Skill Techniques represent specialised methods of close-range striking, rapid movement, concealment and precision learned through mastery of the **Daggers** skill.

A dagger lacks the reach and raw force of larger weapons, but its speed and flexibility make it exceptionally dangerous in skilled hands. Dagger wielders fight within an opponent's guard, target exposed areas and use brief openings that heavier weapons cannot exploit.

Novices learn safe handling, rapid strikes and close defensive movements. As their skill develops, they learn to exploit armour gaps, disable limbs, counter larger weapons and move between enemies before they can react.

Legendary dagger wielders are said to strike between heartbeats, passing through a battlefield while leaving wounds that their enemies do not notice until the blade has already vanished.

Whenever a character reaches a new **Dagger Skill Rank**, they may choose **one technique** from the four techniques available at that rank.

Once selected, a technique becomes part of the character's Dagger training unless it is replaced through retraining approved by the GM.

Dagger Techniques are **Skill Techniques**, not Talents. They do not normally have an inherent resource cost. Particularly demanding techniques may require additional Action Points or Stamina when they involve repeated attacks, extreme movement or sustained precision.

### Novice Techniques

> The Novice learns that a dagger survives through speed and control.  
> A careless wielder reaches too far; a trained hand allows the enemy to enter first.

#### Quick Jab

You make a short, direct strike without unnecessary movement.

**Effects**
- Gain **+1 Accuracy** with Dagger attacks.
- Reduce penalties caused by fighting in confined spaces.
- This benefit does not apply while Restrained or unable to move your weapon arm.
- The technique may be used with either forward or reverse grip.

---

#### Close Guard

You keep the dagger positioned to redirect attacks at extremely close range.

**Effects**
- Gain **+1 AC** against melee attacks made by adjacent creatures.
- The bonus is lost if you are disarmed.
- The bonus does not apply against attacks made with weapons that cannot be reasonably redirected.
- You must be aware of the attacker.

---

#### Concealed Blade

You know how to hide and draw a small blade without obvious preparation.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when concealing a dagger upon your body.
- Draw a concealed dagger without interrupting ordinary movement.
- Gain Advantage when attempting to keep the weapon unnoticed during a casual search.
- A thorough physical search may still discover the weapon.

---

#### Light Footwork

You remain mobile while fighting within an enemy's reach.

**Effects**
- After making a Dagger attack, shift **5 ft** without provoking an ordinary Reaction from the target.
- The movement must end in an unoccupied space.
- You cannot use this movement while Prone or Restrained.
- This technique may be used once per turn.

---

### Initiate Techniques

> The Initiate begins to understand the safety found within an opponent's reach.  
> Where longer weapons become awkward, the dagger moves freely.

#### Armour Gap

You place the blade between plates, scales and protective fittings.

**Effects**
- Ignore **1 Armour** with Dagger attacks.
- Gain **+1 Damage** against creatures wearing Medium or Heavy Armour.
- This technique does not ignore Natural Armour.
- The target must have a physically reachable armour gap.

---

#### Returning Cut

Your blade strikes again as you withdraw it.

**Effects**
- After a successful Dagger attack, deal **+1 Damage** as the blade is pulled free.
- The additional damage is part of the same attack.
- The bonus does not apply to creatures immune to piercing or slashing injuries.
- This technique may activate once per turn.

---

#### Under the Guard

You step inside the effective reach of a larger weapon.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage on your first Dagger attack against a creature wielding a two-handed or reach weapon.
- The target suffers **-1 Accuracy** when attacking you with that weapon while you remain adjacent.
- The effect ends if you leave the creature's reach.
- The creature may change weapons or reposition to remove the penalty.

---

#### Defensive Nick

A small cut disrupts an opponent before their attack fully develops.

**Effects**
- Use your Reaction when an adjacent creature begins a visible melee attack.
- Make a Dagger attack with **-2 Accuracy**.
- On a successful hit, the creature suffers **-2 Accuracy** on the triggering attack.
- The technique does not interrupt the attack completely.

---

### Apprentice Techniques

> The Apprentice does not strike where an enemy is strongest.  
> They search for the hand, the knee, the exposed seam and the instant of hesitation.

#### Bleeding Cut

You open a wound intended to continue weakening the target.

**Effects**
- Once per turn, a successful Dagger attack may inflict **Bleeding**.
- The target may resist or remove the condition according to the normal Bleeding rules.
- Critical Hits increase the severity of the Bleeding by one rank, to the allowed maximum.
- Creatures without blood or vulnerable tissue may be immune.

---

#### Tendon Strike

You target the structures responsible for movement.

**Effects**
- Once per turn, a creature damaged by your dagger loses **10 ft Movement** until the end of its next turn.
- On a Critical Hit, the target cannot Sprint during that time.
- The target must possess vulnerable limbs or equivalent movement structures.
- This technique cannot reduce Movement below the system's minimum.

---

#### Off-Hand Discipline

You train to fight with a dagger alongside another one-handed weapon.

**Effects**
- Reduce the ordinary penalty for using a dagger in your off hand.
- Draw both weapons as part of the same preparation.
- Gain Advantage when resisting attempts to disarm your off-hand dagger.
- This technique does not grant an additional attack by itself.

---

#### Blade Trap

You catch and redirect a weapon using the dagger's guard, spine or reinforced edge.

**Effects**
- After successfully defending against an adjacent melee weapon attack, attempt an opposed Dagger check.
- On success, the attacker suffers **-2 Accuracy** on its next weapon attack.
- You gain Advantage on your next attempt to disarm that weapon.
- Oversized or extremely heavy weapons may gain a bonus when resisting.

---

### Journeyman Techniques

> The Journeyman moves before intention becomes action.  
> A glance, tightened shoulder or shifting foot is enough to reveal where the next opening will appear.

#### Flurry of Steel

You unleash several rapid strikes against a nearby enemy.

**Effects**
- Spend an additional **Action Point** to make a second Dagger attack.
- The second attack suffers **-2 Accuracy**.
- Both attacks may target the same creature or different adjacent creatures.
- Each attack applies damage and conditions separately.

---

#### Disarming Cut

You slash across fingers, wrists and weapon straps.

**Effects**
- Make a Dagger attack against a weapon or the limb holding it.
- On success, the target must pass a Strength or Dexterity check or drop the held item.
- The attack deals reduced damage unless it scores a Critical Hit.
- Creatures with secured or integrated weapons may resist automatically.

---

#### Passing Blade

You strike while slipping beyond an opponent's position.

**Effects**
- Move through a space adjacent to a hostile creature and make one Dagger attack.
- On a successful hit, continue moving without provoking an ordinary Reaction from that creature.
- You must end in an unoccupied space.
- This technique cannot be used if your Movement has been reduced to zero.

---

#### Counterstab

You exploit the opening left by a missed attack.

**Effects**
- When an adjacent enemy misses you with a melee attack, use your Reaction to make one Dagger attack.
- Gain **+1 Accuracy** on the counterattack.
- The attack cannot trigger another counter effect.
- You must be able to see and reach the attacker.

---

### Adept Techniques

> The Adept sees anatomy and armour as a collection of narrow pathways.  
> Their blade needs only a finger's width to find something vital.

#### Deep Thrust

You commit to a precise thrust through an exposed opening.

**Effects**
- Spend one additional **Action Point** before making the attack.
- Ignore **3 Armour**.
- On a successful hit, roll one additional weapon damage die.
- This technique requires a dagger capable of making a piercing attack.

---

#### Nerve Strike

You target a sensitive nerve cluster or equivalent anatomical structure.

**Effects**
- Once per encounter, a successful Dagger attack forces the target to make a Constitution Save.
- On failure, the target becomes **Dazed** until the end of its next turn.
- On a Critical Hit, the target may also lose its Reaction.
- Creatures without suitable anatomy may be immune.

---

#### Shadow Step

You use an enemy's body and attention to conceal your repositioning.

**Effects**
- After successfully striking a creature, move to another unoccupied space adjacent to it.
- The movement does not provoke an ordinary Reaction from that creature.
- Gain Advantage on your next Stealth check before the end of your turn.
- This technique does not make you invisible.

---

#### Opening Barrage

You exploit a target already pressured by another combatant.

**Effects**
- Gain **+2 Accuracy** against a creature adjacent to one of your allies.
- On the first successful hit each turn, deal **+2 Damage**.
- The allied creature must be active and capable of threatening the target.
- This technique does not apply when the ally is incapacitated.

---

### Master Techniques

> The Master no longer reacts to openings.  
> Through pressure, movement and deception, they decide where the opening must appear.

#### Dance of Knives

You move between nearby enemies while striking each in turn.

**Effects**
- Spend **2 Action Points** to move up to half your Movement.
- Make one Dagger attack against up to three different creatures passed during the movement.
- Roll separately against each target.
- These creatures cannot make ordinary Reactions against this movement.
- Each creature may be targeted only once.

---

#### Artery Sever

You deliver a precise cut intended to cause catastrophic blood loss.

**Effects**
- Spend one **Action Point** aiming before making the attack.
- On a Critical Hit, increase the target's Bleeding by two ranks, to the allowed maximum.
- If the target cannot Bleed, deal two additional weapon damage dice instead.
- Bosses and legendary creatures may reduce the resulting condition.

---

#### Vanishing Counter

You strike and immediately leave the enemy's reach.

**Effects**
- When an adjacent enemy misses you, use your Reaction to make one Dagger attack.
- After the attack, move up to **10 ft** without provoking an ordinary Reaction from that enemy.
- The movement must end in an unoccupied space.
- You cannot use this movement while Grappled or Restrained.

---

#### Death by Degrees

Every small wound creates a greater opening for the next.

**Effects**
- Consecutive successful Dagger attacks against the same target gain **+1 Damage**.
- The bonus stacks up to **+5 Damage**.
- The bonus resets when you miss, attack another creature or the combat ends.
- Each hit must deal at least one point of damage to increase the bonus.

---

### Grandmaster Techniques

> The Grandmaster's blade is seen only before the battle begins and after it ends.  
> Between those moments, there is only movement, silence and the sudden understanding of mortality.

#### Strike Between Heartbeats

You attack within an instant too brief for ordinary defence.

**Effects**
- Spend **2 Action Points** to make one Dagger attack with Advantage.
- Ignore all Armour granted by worn equipment.
- On a successful hit, roll two additional weapon damage dice.
- Natural Armour and magical barriers are not ignored.

---

#### Thousand Wounds Discipline

You unleash a sequence of rapid, precisely placed attacks.

**Effects**
- Once per encounter, make up to four Dagger attacks.
- Each attack after the first suffers a cumulative **-1 Accuracy**.
- The attacks may target one creature or several creatures within reach.
- Each successful hit after the first deals reduced weapon damage.

---

#### Unseen Execution

You exploit complete surprise to deliver a devastating opening strike.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when attacking a creature that is unaware of you.
- Ignore all worn Armour on the attack.
- A Critical Hit rolls three additional weapon damage dice.
- Once the target becomes aware of you, this technique cannot be used against it again during that encounter.

---

#### Blade Beyond Sight

You have achieved legendary mastery of speed, placement and dagger control.

**Effects**
- The first Dagger attack you make each round has Advantage.
- Dagger attacks count as Magical for overcoming resistances.
- Once per encounter, turn a failed Dagger attack into a successful hit.
- Critical Hits with daggers inflict **Bleeding** in addition to their normal effects.

---
