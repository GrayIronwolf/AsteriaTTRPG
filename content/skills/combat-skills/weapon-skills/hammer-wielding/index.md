# Hammer Wielding

## Overview

Hammer Wielding Techniques represent specialized combat maneuvers learned through mastery of the **Hammer Wielding** skill. Rather than every skilled hammer user fighting the same way, techniques allow each warrior to develop their own unique combat style.

Whenever a character reaches a new **Hammer Wielding Skill Rank**, they may choose **one** technique from the four techniques available at that rank.

Once learned, a technique becomes a permanent part of the character's fighting style unless retrained through a rare method determined by the GM.

Hammer techniques are considered **Skill Techniques**, not Talents. Any character proficient with hammers may learn them regardless of class, allowing warriors, paladins, clerics, blacksmiths and other adventurers to specialize in their preferred style of combat.

---

**Primary Characteristic:** Strength  
**Secondary Characteristic:** Constitution

---

### Novice Techniques

#### Crushing Blow
Your strikes carry overwhelming force capable of denting armour and breaking bones.

**Effects**
- Gain **+2 Damage** with Hammer attacks.
- Ignore **1 Natural Armour**.

---

#### Brace
You plant yourself before every swing, making yourself difficult to move.

**Effects**
- After making a Hammer attack, gain **+1 AC** until the start of your next turn.
- This bonus is lost if you moved more than half your movement before attacking.

---

#### Bone Rattler
A heavy impact leaves opponents struggling to regain their footing.

**Effects**
- Once per turn, creatures damaged by your Hammer lose **5 ft Movement** until the start of their next turn.

---

#### Mason's Strike
Your understanding of impact allows you to shatter objects with ease.

**Effects**
- Deal double damage against structures, barricades, doors and objects.
- Gain Advantage on Strength checks made to break objects using a Hammer.

---

### Initiate Techniques

#### Armour Breaker
Repeated blows gradually destroy an opponent's protection.

**Effects**
- Once per round, reduce a target's Armour by **1** until combat ends.
- Maximum reduction of **3 Armour**.

---

#### Driving Swing
Each strike forces your opponent backwards.

**Effects**
- Push a creature **5 ft** after a successful Hammer attack.
- Large or larger creatures instead lose **5 ft Movement** next turn.

---

#### Skullcracker
A perfectly placed strike leaves enemies dazed.

**Effects**
- Critical Hits inflict **Dazed** until the end of the target's next turn.

---

#### Momentum Strike
The weight of your hammer carries into another enemy.

**Effects**
- When you defeat a creature, immediately make a free Hammer attack against another adjacent enemy.

---

### Apprentice Techniques

#### Earthshaker
Your hammer sends tremors through the battlefield.

**Effects**
- Once per encounter, strike the ground.
- Creatures within **5 ft** must succeed on an Agility Save or become **Off Balance**.

---

#### Crushing Momentum
Every strike grows heavier than the last.

**Effects**
- Consecutive attacks against the same target gain **+1 Damage**.
- Maximum **+5 Damage**.

---

#### Sunder
You know exactly where equipment is weakest.

**Effects**
- Critical Hits may damage:
  - Armour
  - Shields
  - Weapons

---

#### Giant Slayer
You specialize in bringing down enormous foes.

**Effects**
- Gain **+2 Damage** against Large or larger creatures.
- Ignore the first point of Damage Reduction they possess.

---

### Journeyman Techniques

#### Shockwave
The force of your strikes ripples outward.

**Effects**
- Once every three rounds, create a **10 ft cone**.
- Enemies take minor damage and are pushed backwards.

---

#### Unstoppable Swing
Your attack continues through fallen enemies.

**Effects**
- If your attack defeats an enemy, immediately attack another adjacent creature.
- The second attack deals **50% Damage**.

---

#### Pulverise
Your hammer excels at destroying brittle materials.

**Effects**
- Deal double damage against crystal, bone, ice and similar materials.

---

#### Iron Resolve
Nothing interrupts your attack.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage against effects that would interrupt or prevent your Hammer attacks.

---

### Adept Techniques

#### Quaking Slam
A devastating impact throws enemies from their feet.

**Effects**
- Slam the ground.
- Enemies within **10 ft** must succeed on an Agility Save or fall **Prone**.

---

#### Dragon Breaker
Your strikes are capable of cracking dragon scales.

**Effects**
- Ignore **3 Natural Armour**.
- Deal bonus damage against Dragons and Constructs.

---

#### Avalanche
Your offensive pressure becomes relentless.

**Effects**
- Consecutive Hammer attacks gain **+1 Accuracy**.
- Bonus resets when an attack misses.

---

#### Fortress Breaker
Even castles fear your hammer.

**Effects**
- Deal increased damage against fortifications, siege weapons and magical barriers.

---

### Master Techniques

#### Meteor Hammer
You descend like a falling star.

**Effects**
- Leap to a nearby location.
- Upon landing, enemies in the area take damage and are pushed away.

---

#### Cataclysm Strike
One devastating swing capable of ending battles.

**Effects**
- Requires **2 Action Points**.
- Deals massive bonus damage.
- Ignores half of the target's Armour.

---

#### Mountain Stance
You become an immovable object.

**Effects**
- Cannot be pushed or knocked Prone.
- Gain Resistance against forced movement.

---

#### Echoing Impact
The force of your strike continues beyond the initial blow.

**Effects**
- Adjacent enemies take minor Shockwave damage after a successful Hammer attack.

---

### Grandmaster Techniques

#### Worldbreaker
A legendary strike said to split mountains.

**Effects**
- Create a massive shockwave around the point of impact.
- Structures suffer catastrophic damage.
- Creatures in the area are knocked **Prone** and become **Staggered**.

---

#### Dragon's Doom
No creature is too mighty to withstand your hammer.

**Effects**
- Against Huge or larger creatures:
  - Ignore all Natural Armour.
  - Deal increased Critical Damage.
  - Critical Hits may Stun the target.

---

#### Earthsplitter
The battlefield itself yields beneath your hammer.

**Effects**
- Create a large fissure across the battlefield.
- The fissure becomes Difficult Terrain.
- Smaller creatures may fall into the opening.

---

#### Titan's Legacy
You have achieved perfect mastery of the hammer.

**Effects**
- The first Hammer attack each round has Advantage.
- Critical Hits deal one additional damage die.
- Hammer attacks count as Magical for overcoming resistances.

---
