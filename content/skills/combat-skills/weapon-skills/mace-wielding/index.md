# Mace Wielding

## Overview

Mace Wielding Techniques represent specialized combat maneuvers learned through mastery of the **Mace Wielding** skill. While hammers excel at overwhelming force and destruction, maces are weapons of precision blunt trauma, capable of crushing armour, disabling limbs and wearing opponents down through relentless impacts.

Whenever a character reaches a new **Mace Wielding Skill Rank**, they may choose **one** technique from the four techniques available at that rank.

Once learned, a technique becomes a permanent part of the character's fighting style unless retrained through a rare method determined by the GM.

Mace techniques are considered **Skill Techniques**, not Talents. Any character proficient with maces may learn them regardless of class.

---

**Primary Characteristic:** Strength  
**Secondary Characteristic:** Constitution

### Novice Techniques

#### Crushing Impact

Your strikes are delivered with perfect weight and balance.

**Effects**
- Gain **+2 Damage** with Mace attacks.
- Ignore **1 Natural Armour**.

---

#### Concussive Strike

You target vulnerable areas to leave enemies reeling.

**Effects**
- Once per turn, a damaged creature suffers **-5 ft Movement** until the start of its next turn.

---

#### Guard Break

Your blows force enemies to lower their guard.

**Effects**
- Enemies struck by your mace suffer **-1 AC** until the start of your next turn.

---

#### Iron Grip

Your weapon becomes almost impossible to disarm.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage on checks made to resist being disarmed.
- Gain Advantage when resisting forced movement caused by weapon clashes.

---

### Initiate Techniques

#### Bone Crusher

Every strike threatens to break bone.

**Effects**
- Critical Hits inflict the **Weakened** condition until the end of the target's next turn.

---

#### Shield Breaker

You specialize in destroying defensive equipment.

**Effects**
- Deal double durability damage against Shields.
- Successful attacks may stagger shield users.

---

#### Relentless Assault

You overwhelm opponents through continuous pressure.

**Effects**
- Gain **+1 Accuracy** against a creature you attacked during your previous turn.

---

#### Stunning Blow

A precise strike leaves enemies dazed.

**Effects**
- Once per encounter, a successful attack forces the target to make a Constitution Save or become **Dazed** until the end of its next turn.

---

### Apprentice Techniques

#### Crippling Strike

Your attacks hinder movement.

**Effects**
- Once per turn, choose one:
  - Reduce Movement Speed by 10 ft.
  - Prevent the target from taking Reactions until its next turn.

---

#### Armour Dent

Every successful strike permanently weakens armour during combat.

**Effects**
- Reduce the target's Armour by **1**.
- Maximum reduction of **3 Armour**.

---

#### Heavy Momentum

Each successful hit increases the force behind the next.

**Effects**
- Consecutive attacks against the same target gain **+1 Damage**.
- Maximum **+5 Damage**.

---

#### Skull Splitter

You know exactly where to strike.

**Effects**
- Critical Hits roll one additional weapon damage die.

---

### Journeyman Techniques

#### Ringing Impact

The force of your strike echoes through the target.

**Effects**
- Enemies adjacent to your target take minor concussion damage.

---

#### Break Formation

Your attacks throw enemies out of position.

**Effects**
- Push creatures **10 ft** after a successful attack.
- Medium or smaller creatures must also succeed on an Agility Save or fall **Prone**.

---

#### Crushing Counter

Punish enemies who expose themselves.

**Effects**
- When an adjacent enemy misses a melee attack against you, make a single Mace attack as a Reaction.

---

#### Juggernaut

Nothing slows your advance.

**Effects**
- Ignore Difficult Terrain while moving toward an enemy.
- Gain Resistance against forced movement.

---

### Adept Techniques

#### Pulverising Blow

One strike can crush even the strongest armour.

**Effects**
- Ignore **3 Natural Armour**.
- Deal increased damage against Constructs and heavily armoured enemies.

---

#### Fracture

Your blows leave lasting injuries.

**Effects**
- Critical Hits inflict **Bleeding** or **Weakened** (your choice).

---

#### Warbreaker

Your mace excels against military formations.

**Effects**
- Deal bonus damage against enemies wielding Shields or wearing Heavy Armour.

---

#### Earthbound

Your stance is unwavering.

**Effects**
- Cannot be knocked Prone.
- Gain Advantage against forced movement.

---

### Master Techniques

#### Devastating Smash

A single attack capable of ending a duel.

**Effects**
- Requires **2 Action Points**.
- Deals massive bonus damage.
- Ignores half of the target's Armour.

---

#### Shock Concussion

Your strikes disorient nearby foes.

**Effects**
- Adjacent enemies must succeed on a Constitution Save or become **Disoriented** until the start of your next turn.

---

#### Fortress Crusher

Walls and gates crumble before your weapon.

**Effects**
- Deal triple damage against structures, siege engines and fortifications.

---

#### Iron Tempest

Your mace flows from one enemy to another.

**Effects**
- If an enemy is defeated, immediately attack another creature within reach.

---

### Grandmaster Techniques

#### King's Judgement

A strike worthy of legends.

**Effects**
- Critical Hits ignore all Armour.
- Roll two additional weapon damage dice.

---

#### Cataclysmic Impact

Your attack shakes the battlefield.

**Effects**
- Create a powerful shockwave around the target.
- Nearby enemies are pushed back and knocked **Prone**.

---

#### Tyrant Breaker

Even the strongest champions fall.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage against Elite, Champion and Boss enemies.
- Ignore all Damage Reduction granted by Heavy Armour.

---

#### Unyielding Dominion

You have achieved absolute mastery of the mace.

**Effects**
- The first Mace attack each round has Advantage.
- Mace attacks count as Magical.
- Critical Hits inflict **Staggered** in addition to their normal effects.

---

> *"Where the sword cuts and the hammer shatters, the mace simply ends the fight."*
