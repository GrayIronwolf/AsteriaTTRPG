# Swordsmanship

## Overview

Swordsmanship Skill Techniques represent specialised attacks, guards, counters and movement patterns learned through mastery of the **Swordsmanship** skill.

A swordsman does more than swing a blade. They learn how to control distance, read an opponent's stance, redirect incoming attacks and place strikes where they are most effective. As their mastery grows, their sword becomes an extension of their body, allowing them to shift between offence and defence with little wasted movement.

Whenever a character reaches a new **Swordsmanship Skill Rank**, they may choose **one technique** from the four techniques available at that rank.

Once selected, a technique becomes part of the character's swordsmanship training unless it is replaced through retraining approved by the GM.

Swordsmanship Techniques are **Skill Techniques**, not Talents. Most techniques have no inherent resource cost. Particularly strenuous techniques may require additional Action Points or Stamina when they involve repeated attacks, extreme movement or sustained defensive pressure.

**Secondary Characteristic:** Strength

### Novice Techniques

#### Controlled Slash

You strike with measured force rather than relying on wild swings.

**Effects**
- Gain **+1 Accuracy** with Sword attacks.
- Reduce penalties caused by minor distractions or unstable footing.
- This benefit does not apply while Prone or Restrained.

---

#### Guarded Stance

You keep your blade between yourself and your opponent.

**Effects**
- After making a Sword attack, gain **+1 AC** until the start of your next turn.
- The bonus ends if you are disarmed.
- You cannot benefit while using the sword for another two-handed action.

---

#### Quick Draw

You can draw your weapon and immediately enter combat.

**Effects**
- Draw a sword without interrupting ordinary movement.
- Gain Advantage on checks made to ready your sword under pressure.
- During the first round of combat, drawing your sword does not increase the Action Point cost of your first attack.

---

#### Measured Footwork

You maintain proper distance through careful movement.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when resisting attempts to force you out of melee reach.
- You may shift **5 ft** after a successful Sword attack without provoking an ordinary Reaction from that target.
- This movement cannot pass through occupied spaces.

---

### Initiate Techniques

#### Locking Guard

You trap an opponent's weapon against your blade.

**Effects**
- After successfully defending against an adjacent melee weapon attack, you may attempt to lock the attacking weapon.
- Make an opposed Swordsmanship check against the attacker.
- On success, gain Advantage on your next attack against that creature.
- The lock ends if either creature moves out of reach or releases its weapon.

---

#### Long Slash

You perform a broad, committed cut.

**Effects**
- Spend an additional **Action Point** before making a Sword attack.
- On a successful hit, deal **+2 Damage**.
- The attack may strike a second adjacent creature if it is positioned beside the original target.
- The second creature takes reduced damage.

---

#### Deflecting Blade

You use the flat and edge of your sword to redirect attacks.

**Effects**
- Use your Reaction when targeted by a visible melee weapon attack.
- Gain **+2 AC** against that attack.
- The bonus applies only to the triggering attack.
- You must be holding a sword and able to move your weapon.

---

#### Opening Cut

You target gaps created by an opponent's movement.

**Effects**
- Gain **+2 Damage** against a creature that moved into your melee reach during its previous turn.
- The bonus applies only to your first attack against that creature.
- Forced movement does not activate this technique.

---

### Apprentice Techniques

#### Riposte

You immediately answer a poorly placed attack.

**Effects**
- When an adjacent enemy misses you with a melee attack, use your Reaction to make one Sword attack against it.
- The attack cannot trigger another Riposte.
- You must be able to see the attacker.

---

#### Hampering Cut

You target muscles and joints to restrict movement.

**Effects**
- Once per turn, a creature damaged by your sword loses **10 ft Movement** until the end of its next turn.
- On a Critical Hit, the target also cannot take the Sprint action during that time.
- Creatures without vulnerable limbs may be immune.

---

#### Half-Sword Grip

You grip the blade to guide the point into armour gaps.

**Effects**
- When wielding an appropriate sword, you may enter a Half-Sword Grip.
- While using this grip, ignore **2 Armour**.
- Your weapon damage die is reduced by one step while using the technique.
- Changing grip may be done once during your turn.

---

#### Sweeping Guard

You use broad blade movements to control nearby space.

**Effects**
- Enemies moving through your melee reach provoke a Sword attack as a Reaction.
- On a successful hit, their movement immediately ends.
- You may use this technique once per round.
- Creatures using teleportation or forced movement do not trigger it.

---

### Journeyman Techniques

#### Flowing Assault

Each successful strike carries into the next movement.

**Effects**
- After hitting with a Sword attack, gain **+1 Accuracy** on your next Sword attack during the same turn.
- The bonus stacks up to **+2 Accuracy**.
- All accumulated bonuses are lost when you miss or your turn ends.

---

#### Pommel Strike

You reverse your weapon and strike with the pommel.

**Effects**
- Make a close-range blunt attack using your sword.
- On a successful hit, deal reduced weapon damage.
- The target must succeed on a Constitution Save or become **Dazed** until the end of its next turn.
- This technique may be used once per encounter against the same creature.

---

#### Blade Bind

You engage an opponent's weapon and control its position.

**Effects**
- After hitting a creature wielding a melee weapon, choose to deal reduced damage and initiate a Blade Bind.
- The target suffers **-2 Accuracy** on weapon attacks against creatures other than you.
- The bind lasts until the start of your next turn or until either creature leaves reach.
- Two-handed and oversized weapons may gain a bonus when resisting the bind.

---

#### Passing Strike

You cut an enemy while moving beyond them.

**Effects**
- Move through a space adjacent to a hostile creature and make one Sword attack.
- On a successful hit, continue moving without provoking an ordinary Reaction from that creature.
- You must end your movement in an unoccupied space.
- This technique cannot be used while your movement is reduced to zero.

---

### Adept Techniques

#### Armour-Seeking Thrust

You deliver a precise thrust through a vulnerable opening.

**Effects**
- Spend one additional **Action Point** before making the attack.
- Ignore **4 Armour**.
- On a Critical Hit, roll one additional weapon damage die.
- This technique requires a sword capable of thrusting.

---

#### Blade Arc

Your sword travels through several enemies in one continuous motion.

**Effects**
- Make one Sword attack against up to three adjacent creatures within your reach.
- Roll separately against each target.
- Attacks after the first deal reduced weapon damage.
- This technique may be used once every three rounds.

---

#### Perfect Parry

You turn aside an attack with minimal movement.

**Effects**
- Use your Reaction when hit by a visible melee weapon attack.
- Make a Swordsmanship check against the attacker's result.
- On success, reduce the attack's damage to zero.
- On failure, reduce the damage by an amount determined by your Swordsmanship modifier.
- This technique may be used once per round.

---

#### Duelist's Focus

You concentrate entirely on a single opponent.

**Effects**
- Choose one visible creature within melee reach.
- Gain **+2 Accuracy** and **+1 AC** against that creature.
- You suffer **-1 AC** against attacks made by other creatures.
- The focus ends when you change targets, lose sight of the creature or move away from it.

---

### Master Techniques

#### Whirlwind of Steel

You become the centre of a storm of blades.

**Effects**
- Spend **2 Action Points** to attack every hostile creature within your Sword reach.
- Roll one attack against each creature.
- Each attack deals normal weapon damage.
- You cannot use this technique while Restrained or in an area too narrow to swing your sword.

---

#### Severing Strike

You deliver a powerful cut intended to cripple or remove a vulnerable limb.

**Effects**
- Spend one Action Point aiming before making the attack.
- On a Critical Hit, inflict a severe limb injury appropriate to the target.
- Against creatures immune to limb injuries, deal two additional weapon damage dice instead.
- Bosses and legendary creatures may resist permanent injuries.

---

#### Mirror Guard

Your blade reacts to attacks from every direction.

**Effects**
- Until the start of your next turn, gain **+2 AC** against melee weapon attacks.
- You may use Perfect Parry without consuming your Reaction once during this duration.
- Activating Mirror Guard requires one Action Point.
- The technique ends if you are disarmed or incapacitated.

---

#### Relentless Blade

Defeating one opponent allows you to immediately pressure another.

**Effects**
- When you reduce a creature to zero Health with a Sword attack, move up to half your Movement.
- You may then make one Sword attack against another creature within reach.
- This additional attack may occur only once per round.
- The movement must bring you closer to a hostile creature.

---

### Grandmaster Techniques

#### Sword Saint's Cut

Your blade passes through defence before the enemy recognises the strike.

**Effects**
- Spend **2 Action Points** to make one Sword attack with Advantage.
- Ignore all Armour granted by worn equipment and shields.
- On a successful hit, roll two additional weapon damage dice.
- Natural Armour and magical barriers are not ignored.

---

#### Thousand-Cut Discipline

Your sword moves too quickly for ordinary eyes to follow.

**Effects**
- Once per encounter, make up to four Sword attacks.
- Each attack after the first suffers a cumulative **-1 Accuracy**.
- The attacks may target one creature or several creatures within reach.
- Each attack requires its normal Action Point cost, reduced by one for the duration to a minimum of one.

---

#### Unbroken Counter

Every attack made against you creates an opportunity.

**Effects**
- Once per round, when you successfully avoid or parry a melee attack, immediately make one Sword attack against the attacker.
- This counter does not consume your Reaction.
- The attack cannot trigger another counter effect.
- You must be holding a sword and capable of acting.

---

#### Blade Without Equal

You have achieved legendary mastery of swordsmanship.

**Effects**
- The first Sword attack you make each round has Advantage.
- Sword attacks count as Magical for overcoming resistances.
- Once per encounter, turn a failed Sword attack into a successful hit.
- Critical Hits with swords deal one additional weapon damage die.

---
