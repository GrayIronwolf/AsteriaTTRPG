# Throwing Knives

## Overview

Throwing Knife Skill Techniques represent specialised methods of throwing, retrieving and controlling blades through mastery of the **Throwing Knives** skill.

A throwing knife is a weapon of precision rather than power. Skilled throwers learn to judge distance, rotation and momentum instinctively, allowing every blade to strike exactly where intended. While novices focus on accuracy, masters can disable enemies, pin targets, strike multiple opponents and recover their weapons with remarkable efficiency.

Whenever a character reaches a new **Throwing Knives Skill Rank**, they may choose **one technique** from the four techniques available at that rank.

Once selected, a technique becomes part of the character's Throwing Knife training unless it is replaced through retraining approved by the GM.

Throwing Knife Techniques are **Skill Techniques**, not Talents. Most techniques have no inherent resource cost, though advanced techniques may require additional Action Points or specialised throwing knives.

### Novice Techniques

> Every throw begins with discipline.  
> Before a knife can strike true, the hand must learn consistency.

#### Perfect Grip

You instinctively balance each knife before release.

**Effects**
- Gain **+1 Accuracy** with Throwing Knife attacks.
- Ignore minor penalties caused by awkward grip or hurried preparation.
- Reduce the chance of dropping a knife before throwing.

---

#### Quick Draw

Your knives are always within easy reach.

**Effects**
- Draw a throwing knife without interrupting movement.
- Gain Advantage when drawing hidden throwing knives.
- Reduce the time needed to ready multiple knives.

---

#### Smooth Release

You release each knife with controlled timing.

**Effects**
- Reduce penalties caused by medium-range throws.
- Gain Advantage when throwing at stationary targets.
- Minor distractions do not interrupt your throw.

---

#### Knife Recovery

You quickly recover thrown knives after combat.

**Effects**
- Recover additional intact throwing knives after an encounter.
- Gain Advantage when locating knives embedded in terrain or targets.
- Reduce losses caused by broken or misplaced blades.

---

### Initiate Techniques

> The Initiate understands that distance is measured in instinct, not steps.

#### Long Throw

Your throws maintain accuracy over greater distances.

**Effects**
- Increase the effective range of throwing knives.
- Reduce the first long-range Accuracy penalty.
- Gain Advantage when estimating throwing distance.

---

#### Pinning Knife

You throw to hinder rather than kill.

**Effects**
- On a successful hit, attempt to pin clothing or equipment.
- The target must spend an action or succeed on a Strength check to free itself.
- May deal reduced damage.

---

#### Twin Throw

You can release two knives almost simultaneously.

**Effects**
- Spend one additional **Action Point**.
- Make a second Throwing Knife attack.
- The second attack suffers **-2 Accuracy**.

---

#### Precision Rotation

You instinctively judge blade rotation.

**Effects**
- Ignore penalties caused by unusual throwing angles.
- Gain Advantage when throwing through narrow gaps.
- Reduce failed throws caused by incorrect rotation.

---

### Apprentice Techniques

> Every blade now follows intention rather than motion.

#### Bleeding Throw

You strike to open lasting wounds.

**Effects**
- Once per turn, a successful hit may inflict **Bleeding**.
- Critical Hits increase the Bleeding severity by one rank.
- Creatures without blood may be immune.

---

#### Ricochet Knife

You deliberately bounce a knife from a hard surface.

**Effects**
- Attack creatures behind partial cover.
- The attack suffers **-2 Accuracy**.
- Requires an appropriate hard surface.
- Cannot bypass complete cover.

---

#### Hamstring Throw

You target the legs.

**Effects**
- Target loses **10 ft Movement** until the end of its next turn.
- Critical Hits prevent Sprinting during that time.
- Creatures without vulnerable limbs may be immune.

---

#### Returning Step

You move immediately after throwing.

**Effects**
- After making a Throwing Knife attack, move **5 ft**.
- This movement does not provoke an ordinary Reaction from the target.
- Must end in an unoccupied space.

---

### Journeyman Techniques

> The Journeyman fills every opening with steel.

#### Fan of Knives

You throw several knives across a small area.

**Effects**
- Spend **2 Action Points**.
- Attack up to three creatures within a small cone.
- Roll separately for each attack.
- Each attack deals reduced weapon damage.

---

#### Disarming Throw

You target weapons instead of flesh.

**Effects**
- Attack a held object.
- On success, the target must pass a Strength or Dexterity check or drop the item.
- Fragile objects may be damaged.

---

#### Interrupting Throw

Your knife disrupts an enemy's action.

**Effects**
- Use your Reaction to throw at a creature beginning a visible action.
- On a Critical Hit, the action may be interrupted.
- Instantaneous actions cannot be interrupted.

---

#### Hidden Throw

Enemies struggle to determine where the knife came from.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage on Stealth checks immediately after throwing from concealment.
- Reduce the chance enemies identify your position.
- May reposition **5 ft** after the throw.

---

### Adept Techniques

> The Adept no longer throws blades.  
> They place them exactly where fate intended.

#### Armour Splitter

You throw with exceptional force and precision.

**Effects**
- Ignore **3 Armour**.
- Ignore **1 Natural Armour**.
- Deal increased durability damage to shields.

---

#### Triple Throw

Three knives leave your hand in rapid succession.

**Effects**
- Spend **2 Action Points**.
- Make three attacks.
- Second attack suffers **-1 Accuracy**.
- Third attack suffers **-2 Accuracy**.

---

#### Deadeye Throw

You wait for the perfect opening.

**Effects**
- Spend one **Action Point** aiming.
- Gain Advantage.
- Roll one additional weapon damage die.

---

#### Shadow Knife

Your knife disappears into darkness before striking.

**Effects**
- Ignore penalties from dim light or darkness.
- Gain Advantage against unaware creatures.
- Cannot bypass magical blindness.

---

### Master Techniques

> Every knife thrown by a Master has already reached its destination before it leaves the hand.

#### Thousand Cuts

You overwhelm your opponent with relentless precision.

**Effects**
- Make four Throwing Knife attacks.
- Each attack after the first suffers **-1 Accuracy**.
- May divide attacks between targets.

---

#### Heartseeker Throw

You target a creature's vital point.

**Effects**
- Spend one **Action Point** aiming.
- Critical Hits roll two additional weapon damage dice.
- Creatures without vital anatomy instead take one additional damage die.

---

#### Perfect Recovery

No knife remains lost for long.

**Effects**
- Recover all ordinary throwing knives after combat unless destroyed.
- Gain Advantage when retrieving knives during combat.
- Reduce the Action Point cost of retrieving nearby knives.

---

#### Phantom Volley

Your enemies struggle to follow the path of your blades.

**Effects**
- Ignore partial cover.
- Reduce long-range penalties.
- Gain Advantage when attacking distracted enemies.

---

### Grandmaster Techniques

> The Grandmaster's blades seem alive.  
> Every throw arrives exactly where destiny demands.

#### Storm of Steel

You unleash a devastating barrage.

**Effects**
- Spend **2 Action Points**.
- Make up to six Throwing Knife attacks.
- Each attack after the first suffers cumulative **-1 Accuracy**.
- May divide attacks freely between visible targets.

---

#### Impossible Throw

You strike through impossibly small openings.

**Effects**
- Ignore ordinary cover penalties.
- Ricochet from multiple hard surfaces.
- Gain Advantage against targets behind obstacles.
- Complete cover cannot be bypassed.

---

#### Assassin's Finale

One perfectly placed knife ends the battle.

**Effects**
- Spend **2 Action Points**.
- Make one attack with Advantage.
- Ignore all worn Armour.
- Critical Hits roll three additional weapon damage dice.
- Bosses and Legendary creatures may resist instant-kill effects.

---

#### Master of Blades

You have achieved legendary mastery of thrown weapons.

**Effects**
- The first Throwing Knife attack each round has Advantage.
- Throwing Knife attacks count as Magical for overcoming resistances.
- Once per encounter, reroll a failed Throwing Knife attack.
- Critical Hits automatically inflict **Bleeding**.

---

> *"The greatest throwing knife is never the one you see leaving the hand... it is the one you only notice after it has already struck."*
