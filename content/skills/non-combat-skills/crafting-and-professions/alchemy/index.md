# Alchemy

## Overview

Alchemy Skill Techniques represent specialised methods of identifying ingredients, refining reagents, stabilising mixtures and producing more reliable alchemical creations through mastery of the **Alchemy** skill.

An alchemist does more than follow recipes. They learn how ingredients react, how environmental conditions alter a mixture and how to adjust a formula when materials are imperfect. Skilled alchemists may improve yields, reduce waste, recognise dangerous reactions and eventually create preparations beyond ordinary recipes.

Whenever a character reaches a new **Alchemy Skill Rank**, they may choose **one technique** from the four techniques available at that rank.

Once selected, a technique becomes part of the character's alchemical training unless it is replaced through retraining approved by the GM.

Alchemy Techniques are **Skill Techniques**, not Talents. Techniques do not normally have an inherent Mana, Stamina or Health cost. Costs instead come from ingredients, equipment, preparation time, dangerous substances or the consequences of failed alchemical checks.

**Secondary Characteristic:** Wisdom

### Novice Techniques

#### Careful Measurement

You have learned that even minor errors can ruin an entire mixture.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when measuring ordinary alchemical ingredients.
- Reduce the chance of a mixture failing because of incorrect quantities.
- Quickly recognise when a measured amount is clearly too high or too low.
- Minor interruptions are less likely to ruin your measurements.

---

#### Reagent Recognition

You can identify common ingredients used in basic alchemy.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when identifying common herbs, minerals, fluids and monster materials used in alchemy.
- Recognise whether a basic reagent is fresh, spoiled or contaminated.
- Recall the most common alchemical use of a recognised ingredient.
- Rare, altered or magically disguised materials may still require additional checks.

---

#### Steady Hand

You remain controlled while pouring, grinding and combining delicate materials.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when performing precise physical steps during alchemical crafting.
- Reduce minor spills and accidental waste.
- Your equipment is less likely to be damaged by ordinary handling mistakes.
- This technique does not protect against explosions or dangerous magical reactions.

---

#### Basic Salvage

You know how to recover usable materials from a failed preparation.

**Effects**
- Recover a small portion of ordinary ingredients after a failed Alchemy check.
- Ingredients destroyed by fire, corruption or powerful magical reactions cannot be recovered.
- Gain Advantage when separating uncontaminated materials from a ruined mixture.
- The recovered ingredients may be reduced in quality.

---

### Initiate Techniques

#### Efficient Grinding

You can prepare powders, pastes and crushed reagents more efficiently.

**Effects**
- Reduce the time required to grind or crush alchemical ingredients.
- Gain Advantage when producing a consistent powder or paste.
- Properly prepared materials mix more evenly into the final creation.
- Reduce waste caused by uneven preparation.

---

#### Temperature Control

You understand how heat and cold influence an alchemical reaction.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when maintaining the correct brewing temperature.
- Recognise when a mixture is heating or cooling too quickly.
- Reduce the chance of failure caused by minor temperature fluctuations.
- You may safely adjust ordinary alchemical heat sources during brewing.

---

#### Ingredient Substitution

You can replace a missing common ingredient with a similar reagent.

**Effects**
- Substitute one common ingredient within a known recipe.
- The replacement must have an appropriate alchemical property.
- The resulting item may have slightly reduced potency, duration or yield.
- Gain Advantage when determining whether two common reagents are compatible.

---

#### Clean Preparation

You maintain a controlled and uncontaminated workspace.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when preventing contamination during alchemical crafting.
- Prepared ingredients remain usable for longer when stored in your workspace.
- Quickly identify obvious contamination in tools, containers or work surfaces.
- Reduce complications caused by residue from previous recipes.

---

### Apprentice Techniques

#### Potency Adjustment

You can slightly alter the strength of a known alchemical creation.

**Effects**
- Before crafting, choose to increase either the potency or duration of the item.
- The unselected property may be slightly reduced.
- Gain Advantage when adjusting a recipe you have successfully crafted before.
- This technique cannot raise an item beyond the maximum rank you can produce.

---

#### Extended Shelf Life

You know how to protect alchemical items from gradual deterioration.

**Effects**
- Items crafted by you remain stable for significantly longer.
- Gain Advantage when identifying whether an old potion or mixture is still safe.
- Reduce potency loss caused by ordinary age and storage.
- Severe heat, freezing, magical corruption or broken containers may still spoil the item.

---

#### Refined Extraction

You can draw a greater amount of useful essence from a reagent.

**Effects**
- Gain an increased yield when extracting fluids, oils, powders or essences.
- Reduce waste during the extraction process.
- Gain Advantage when separating useful alchemical properties from unwanted material.
- Poor-quality ingredients still limit the final quality of the extracted substance.

---

#### Controlled Reaction

You know how to stop a mixture before an unstable reaction becomes disastrous.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when attempting to stabilise a failing mixture.
- Reduce the severity of ordinary alchemical crafting failures.
- You may safely abandon a reaction before the final crafting stage.
- Critical failures involving powerful magical or volatile ingredients may still cause serious consequences.

---

### Journeyman Techniques

#### Batch Brewing

You can produce several identical alchemical items in one organised process.

**Effects**
- Craft a small batch of the same known recipe at one time.
- Reduce the total preparation time compared with crafting each item separately.
- All items in the batch share the same quality result.
- A failed check may affect the entire batch.

---

#### Catalytic Understanding

You understand which substances can accelerate or strengthen a reaction.

**Effects**
- Add an appropriate catalyst to reduce crafting time or improve the resulting yield.
- Gain Advantage when identifying a suitable catalyst for a known recipe.
- Incorrect catalysts may destabilise the mixture.
- Rare catalysts may provide stronger benefits at the GM's discretion.

---

#### Toxicological Insight

You can recognise and prepare dangerous poisonous substances more safely.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when identifying common poisons and toxins.
- Reduce the chance of accidentally poisoning yourself during preparation.
- Recognise the likely delivery method and general effect of a known toxin.
- Creating or handling restricted poisons may still have legal or narrative consequences.

---

#### Formula Correction

You can diagnose why an incomplete or flawed recipe is failing.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when examining a damaged, incorrect or partially translated formula.
- Identify one obvious missing step, incompatible ingredient or incorrect measurement.
- Reduce the number of experimental attempts required to repair a formula.
- Severely incomplete or deliberately false recipes may require Research or Analysis.

---

### Adept Techniques

#### Alchemical Purification

You can remove impurities from damaged or contaminated ingredients.

**Effects**
- Restore a portion of the quality lost from contaminated reagents.
- Gain Advantage when separating toxins, dirt or unwanted residue from valuable material.
- Purified ingredients become safer and more reliable to use.
- Eldritch corruption, powerful curses and soul contamination may require specialised methods.

---

#### Concentrated Mixture

You can compress the useful properties of a preparation into a smaller dose.

**Effects**
- Create a concentrated version of a known recipe.
- The item requires less physical volume while maintaining its normal effect.
- Gain Advantage when concentrating recipes you have crafted repeatedly.
- Concentration may increase the danger of spills, overdoses or failed crafting attempts.

---

#### Delayed Reaction

You can prepare an alchemical creation that activates after a trigger or period of time.

**Effects**
- Add a simple delayed activation to an appropriate alchemical item.
- Possible triggers include impact, exposure to air, heat or a short timed delay.
- Gain Advantage when setting a trigger you have previously used.
- Complex magical triggers require Enchanting, Runecrafting or another appropriate skill.

---

#### Adaptive Formula

You can modify a recipe to suit unusual environmental conditions.

**Effects**
- Adjust a known formula for extreme heat, cold, altitude or humidity.
- Gain Advantage when brewing in difficult environments.
- Reduce penalties caused by an unsuitable workspace.
- The adjusted recipe must still have access to appropriate tools and ingredients.

---

### Master Techniques

#### Masterwork Brewing

Your understanding allows you to produce exceptionally consistent alchemical creations.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when crafting any familiar alchemical recipe.
- Reduce ordinary quality loss caused by minor ingredient flaws.
- Items crafted successfully are less likely to contain unwanted side effects.
- This technique cannot bypass recipe rank, ingredient or equipment requirements.

---

#### Essence Separation

You can isolate individual alchemical properties from complex materials.

**Effects**
- Separate one useful property from a reagent containing several properties.
- Gain Advantage when extracting an essence without carrying over unwanted effects.
- The isolated essence may be used in experimental or advanced recipes.
- Powerful magical creatures and soul-bearing materials may require specialised equipment.

---

#### Compound Creation

You can combine compatible alchemical effects within a single preparation.

**Effects**
- Combine two known compatible recipes into one item.
- The resulting creation may have reduced potency compared with each original recipe.
- Gain Advantage when combining effects you have successfully crafted many times.
- Incompatible combinations may become unstable or dangerous.

---

#### Rapid Preparation

You can produce emergency mixtures under severe time pressure.

**Effects**
- Greatly reduce the crafting time of a familiar recipe.
- Gain Advantage when preparing an alchemical item during an emergency.
- Rapidly prepared items may have a reduced shelf life.
- This technique cannot be used without the minimum required ingredients and tools.

---

### Grandmaster Techniques

#### Philosopher's Method

Your mastery allows you to understand the underlying principles shared by all alchemical reactions.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when analysing an unknown alchemical substance.
- Identify the major properties, likely ingredients and method of creation.
- Reconstruct damaged recipes from limited surviving evidence.
- Completely unique, divine or eldritch substances may resist analysis.

---

#### Perfect Transmutation

You can transform one compatible alchemical substance into another through advanced refinement.

**Effects**
- Convert a quantity of one refined reagent into a related reagent.
- The substances must share compatible alchemical properties.
- Some quantity or quality is lost during the transformation.
- Legendary, divine and soul-bound materials cannot normally be created through this technique.

---

#### Grand Synthesis

You can create complex preparations containing several carefully balanced effects.

**Effects**
- Combine multiple compatible alchemical properties within one creation.
- Gain Advantage when balancing effects that would normally interfere with one another.
- You may reduce or remove minor unwanted side effects from the final item.
- Every added property increases the ingredient, time and difficulty requirements.

---

#### Living Formula

You no longer rely solely on written recipes and can adapt mixtures as they develop.

**Effects**
- Alter a brewing process in response to unexpected reactions without automatically failing.
- Gain Advantage when working with unknown, unstable or changing ingredients.
- Replace or adjust one step of a formula during the crafting process.
- Your final creation must still remain within the limits of your Alchemy rank and available materials.
