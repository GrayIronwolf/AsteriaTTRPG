# Enchanting

## Overview

Enchanting Skill Techniques represent specialised methods of inscribing, binding, stabilising and empowering magical effects through mastery of the **Enchanting** skill.

An enchanter transforms ordinary objects into vessels for magic. Through powdered crystal, carefully prepared runes and a known spell or enchantment, they create weapons that burn with elemental power, armour that protects its wearer and tools capable of performing extraordinary functions.

Novice Enchanters begin by preparing surfaces, recognising magical instability and placing simple enchantments upon ordinary items. As their mastery grows, they learn to preserve charges, combine multiple effects, strengthen magical bindings and work with increasingly powerful enchantment ranks.

Legendary Enchanters are capable of creating relics whose magic survives centuries, repairing damaged enchantments and binding powers that ordinary objects were never intended to contain.

Whenever a character reaches a new **Enchanting Skill Rank**, they may choose **one technique** from the four techniques available at that rank.

Once selected, a technique becomes part of the character's Enchanting training unless it is replaced through retraining approved by the GM.

Enchanting Techniques are **Skill Techniques**, not Talents. They do not normally have an inherent resource cost. Enchanting still requires the intended item, knowledge of the enchantment, powdered crystal and any additional materials required by the formula. Spellbooks and skill books may also require blank books, Scribing and knowledge of the spell or skill being recorded.

### Novice Techniques

> The Novice Enchanter learns that magic does not remain within an object simply because it was placed there.  
> Every line, crystal and spoken word must convince the item to hold what it was never born to carry.

#### Careful Inscription

You create cleaner and more reliable runes upon prepared objects.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when inscribing simple enchantment runes.
- Reduce errors caused by uneven symbols, broken lines or poor spacing.
- Recognise when an inscription is incomplete before magical power is applied.
- Minor surface imperfections are less likely to ruin the enchantment.
- This technique does not repair a damaged or unsuitable item.

---

#### Crystal Measurement

You understand how much powdered crystal is required for basic enchantments.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when measuring powdered crystal for an enchantment.
- Reduce minor crystal waste during successful enchanting.
- Recognise when a mixture contains too much, too little or contaminated crystal.
- Recover a small amount of unused powdered crystal after completing a basic enchantment.

---

#### Stable Foundation

You carefully prepare an item before placing magic within it.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when cleaning and preparing an ordinary item for enchanting.
- Identify obvious cracks, weak fittings or structural flaws that may destabilise the enchantment.
- Reduce the chance of a basic enchantment failing because of poor preparation.
- The technique does not improve the physical quality of the item.

---

#### Minor Recharge

You can restore a small amount of expended magical energy to a simple enchanted item.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when recharging a familiar Weak enchantment.
- Reduce the chance of damaging the item during an ordinary recharge.
- Recognise when an enchantment cannot safely accept another charge.
- This technique cannot exceed the item's normal maximum charges.

---

### Initiate Techniques

> The Initiate begins to recognise the rhythm of a magical binding.  
> Every enchantment has a pulse, and every object has a limit to what it can endure.

#### Efficient Channeling

You guide magical energy into an item with less loss.

**Effects**
- Reduce the amount of magical energy wasted during successful enchanting.
- Gain Advantage when maintaining a steady magical flow into an object.
- Minor interruptions are less likely to collapse the binding.
- This technique does not reduce required Soul Point or material costs unless the GM specifically allows it.

---

#### Rune Correction

You can identify and repair minor mistakes before an enchantment is completed.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when inspecting an incomplete rune sequence.
- Correct one minor inscription error without restarting the entire process.
- Recognise when an incorrect rune would alter the enchantment's intended function.
- Major structural errors may still require the enchantment to be removed and begun again.

---

#### Charge Preservation

You create enchantments that retain their stored power more reliably.

**Effects**
- Enchantments created by you are less likely to lose charges through age or minor damage.
- Gain Advantage when stabilising an item with partially depleted charges.
- Reduce accidental charge loss caused by ordinary crafting failures.
- This technique does not increase the item's maximum number of charges.

---

#### Enchantment Appraisal

You can examine a simple enchanted item and recognise its general function.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when identifying familiar Weak or Minor enchantments.
- Determine whether the enchantment is active, depleted, damaged or dormant.
- Estimate the number of remaining charges.
- Hidden conditions, curses and deliberately disguised enchantments may require further analysis.

---

### Apprentice Techniques

> The Apprentice no longer sees runes as isolated symbols.  
> They perceive a complete magical structure in which every line supports the enchantment around it.

#### Reinforced Binding

You strengthen the connection between an enchantment and the item carrying it.

**Effects**
- Enchantments created by you gain increased resistance against accidental disruption.
- Gain Advantage when enchanting weapons, armour or tools expected to endure heavy use.
- Reduce the chance of the enchantment breaking when the object suffers ordinary damage.
- Destruction of the item may still destroy the enchantment.

---

#### Focused Enchantment

You emphasise one part of an enchantment at the expense of another.

**Effects**
- When creating a familiar enchantment, choose to improve one of the following:
  - Potency
  - Duration
  - Charge efficiency
  - Activation reliability
- Another appropriate property may be slightly reduced.
- This technique cannot raise an enchantment beyond the rank you can create.

---

#### Enchanted Script

You can bind known magical instruction into prepared books and scroll-like objects.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when creating enchanted skill books or spellbooks.
- Reduce damage to the book caused by the binding process.
- Preserve magical instructions more clearly within the written work.
- Skill books still require the appropriate known skill and Scribing.
- Spellbooks still require the known spell rank and appropriate magical knowledge.

---

#### Salvaged Essence

You can recover a portion of the magical materials from a failed enchantment.

**Effects**
- Recover some powdered crystal from an unsuccessful enchanting attempt.
- Gain Advantage when separating unused crystal from damaged inscription material.
- Rare reagents may be recovered if they were not consumed by the failure.
- Critical Failures, explosions and corrupted bindings may destroy all materials.

---

### Journeyman Techniques

> The Journeyman Enchanter understands that an object and its enchantment must become one structure.  
> Magic placed upon the surface is temporary; magic woven into purpose may endure.

#### Dual Inscription

You can attempt to place two compatible enchantments upon one suitable item.

**Effects**
- Add a second compatible enchantment to an item capable of supporting it.
- Both enchantments must be within your known rank.
- Gain Advantage when combining enchantments you have successfully used together before.
- The second enchantment increases material cost, difficulty and risk.
- Incompatible enchantments may interfere with one another or cause failure.

---

#### Enchantment Extraction

You can carefully remove a minor enchantment from an item.

**Effects**
- Attempt to separate a Weak or Minor enchantment from its object.
- Gain Advantage when extracting an enchantment you personally created.
- A successful extraction may preserve part of the rune pattern or magical essence for study.
- The original item may be damaged or left permanently unable to hold the same enchantment.
- Curses and soul-bound effects may resist extraction.

---

#### Rapid Inscription

You can complete familiar enchantment patterns more efficiently.

**Effects**
- Reduce the time required to create a familiar enchantment.
- Gain Advantage when reproducing a rune sequence you have successfully completed many times.
- Rapidly created enchantments remain limited by normal material and rank requirements.
- Unfamiliar, experimental or complex enchantments cannot benefit fully from this technique.

---

#### Recharge Matrix

You create clearer pathways through which an enchantment can regain charges.

**Effects**
- Reduce the time or magical effort required to restore a single charge.
- Gain Advantage when recharging enchantments you created.
- Identify damaged sections of a recharge pathway.
- This technique does not allow an item to exceed its maximum charges.
- Enchantments without rechargeable charges gain no benefit.

---

### Adept Techniques

> The Adept treats magical structure as living architecture.  
> Runes become pillars, crystal becomes mortar and essence becomes the force that holds the entire design together.

#### Greater Binding

You can safely contain stronger enchantments within prepared objects.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when creating Greater or Strengthened enchantments.
- Reduce the chance of magical backlash caused by high-rank bindings.
- Identify whether an item is physically or magically incapable of holding the intended enchantment.
- Suitable item quality and materials are still required.

---

#### Layered Enchantment

You weave enchantments into separate magical layers within the same object.

**Effects**
- Reduce interference between multiple compatible enchantments.
- Gain Advantage when adding an enchantment to an item that already carries another effect.
- One enchantment may remain dormant until a defined activation condition is met.
- The item must possess sufficient quality and magical capacity.

---

#### Durable Enchantment

You create bindings capable of surviving damage, weather and prolonged use.

**Effects**
- Enchantments created by you gain increased durability.
- Reduce the chance of charge loss when the item is damaged.
- Gain Advantage when repairing the magical structure of a worn enchanted item.
- Complete destruction of the enchanted section may still end the effect.

---

#### Magical Efficiency

You reduce the amount of charge consumed when an enchantment activates.

**Effects**
- When creating an enchantment with charges, attempt to improve its charge efficiency.
- On success, the enchantment may occasionally activate without consuming a charge.
- Gain Advantage when applying this technique to a familiar enchantment.
- More powerful effects are less likely to conserve a charge.
- The exact activation chance is determined by enchantment rank and GM balance.

---

### Master Techniques

> The Master Enchanter no longer forces magic into an object.  
> They reshape the object’s nature until holding magic becomes as natural as holding weight, edge or form.

#### Masterwork Binding

Your enchantments are exceptionally stable and refined.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when creating any familiar enchantment within your rank.
- Reduce quality loss caused by minor flaws in the item or materials.
- Successfully created enchantments are less likely to develop unwanted side effects.
- This technique cannot bypass item quality, knowledge or material requirements.

---

#### Enchantment Restoration

You can repair enchantments that have been damaged or partially erased.

**Effects**
- Restore broken rune lines, weakened bindings and damaged charge pathways.
- Gain Advantage when repairing enchantments you created or have fully studied.
- Recover some lost function from an enchantment that has not been completely destroyed.
- Unknown, cursed or eldritch enchantments may require specialised research.

---

#### Spell Compression

You condense a known magical effect into a more efficient enchantment structure.

**Effects**
- Reduce the space or rune complexity required by a familiar enchantment.
- Gain Advantage when fitting enchantments into small objects or restricted surfaces.
- A compressed enchantment may be easier to combine with another compatible effect.
- Compression cannot ignore the object's minimum quality or magical capacity.

---

#### Trigger Mastery

You can create more precise conditions for enchantment activation.

**Effects**
- Assign a complex but clearly defined trigger to an enchantment.
- Possible triggers include:
  - A spoken phrase
  - A specific creature touching the item
  - Receiving a certain damage type
  - Entering a defined environment
- Gain Advantage when using a trigger you have previously created.
- Triggers based on thoughts, fate or distant events may require additional magical disciplines.

---

### Grandmaster Techniques

> The Grandmaster Enchanter sees every object as a possibility waiting to awaken.  
> Stone may remember, steel may hunger and crystal may hold the echo of a spell for a thousand years.

#### Eternal Enchantment

You create enchantments designed to endure across generations.

**Effects**
- Enchantments created with this technique resist ordinary decay, corrosion and magical weakening.
- Charge pathways and rune structures remain stable for extremely long periods.
- Gain Advantage when creating relics, monuments and inherited magical items.
- Catastrophic damage, divine intervention and powerful dispelling may still destroy the enchantment.

---

#### Impossible Vessel

You can attempt to place magic into objects normally considered unsuitable for enchanting.

**Effects**
- Attempt to enchant fragile, unusual or unconventional materials.
- Gain Advantage when you have thoroughly studied the material beforehand.
- The enchantment may require additional crystal, reinforcement or specialised preparation.
- Failure may destroy the object or cause magical backlash.
- Living beings and souls require separate skills and are not ordinary vessels.

---

#### Grand Convergence

You can bind several compatible enchantments into one unified magical system.

**Effects**
- Combine multiple enchantments so they share charges, triggers or magical pathways.
- Gain Advantage when all included enchantments serve a related purpose.
- Reduce interference between the combined effects.
- Every additional enchantment significantly increases cost, time and difficulty.
- A failed convergence may damage every enchantment involved.

---

#### Living Enchantment

You create an enchantment capable of adapting within carefully defined limits.

**Effects**
- The enchantment may alter one aspect of its behaviour in response to its wielder or environment.
- Examples include:
  - Changing elemental expression
  - Shifting between offence and defence
  - Adjusting light intensity
  - Prioritising different activation triggers
- The enchantment cannot learn unrestricted abilities or exceed its original rank.
- Living enchantments may develop minor behavioural traits determined by their materials, purpose and creator.

---

> *"The finest enchantment is not magic placed upon an object. It is the moment the object forgets it was ever mundane."*
