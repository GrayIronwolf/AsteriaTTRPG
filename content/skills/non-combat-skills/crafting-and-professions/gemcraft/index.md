# Gemcraft

## Overview

Gemcraft Skill Techniques represent specialised methods of identifying, cutting, polishing, shaping and mounting gemstones through mastery of the **Gemcraft** skill.

A gemcrafter must understand much more than beauty. Every gemstone contains internal structures, natural flaws, colour patterns and hidden tensions that determine how it should be worked. A careless cut may shatter a valuable crystal, while a masterful one can reveal brilliance, improve durability or prepare the stone for magical use.

Whenever a character reaches a new **Gemcraft Skill Rank**, they may choose **one technique** from the four techniques available at that rank.

Once selected, a technique becomes part of the character's gemcraft training unless it is replaced through retraining approved by the GM.

Gemcraft Techniques are **Skill Techniques**, not Talents. They do not normally have an inherent Mana, Stamina or Health cost. Gemcraft still requires appropriate tools, time, suitable materials and a stable workspace. Rare, magical or soul-bearing stones may require additional skills or specialised equipment.

### Novice Techniques

#### Gem Recognition

You can identify common gemstones by sight, colour and texture.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when identifying common gemstones.
- Recognise whether a gemstone is raw, cut, polished or synthetic.
- Recall the ordinary value and common use of a recognised gemstone.
- Rare, magical or altered stones may still require further examination.

---

#### Steady Cutting

You maintain careful control while making simple cuts.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when performing basic gemstone cuts.
- Reduce the chance of damaging a gemstone through uneven pressure.
- Minor interruptions are less likely to ruin the cut.
- This technique does not remove flaws already present inside the stone.

---

#### Fine Polishing

You can restore clarity and shine to worked gemstones.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when polishing common gemstones.
- Remove minor scratches, dullness and surface imperfections.
- Improve the appearance of a poorly maintained gemstone.
- Deep cracks and internal flaws cannot be removed through polishing alone.

---

#### Tool Care

You understand how to maintain delicate gemcraft equipment.

**Effects**
- Reduce wear on cutting wheels, files, clamps and polishing tools.
- Gain Advantage when identifying damaged or misaligned gemcraft equipment.
- Perform basic repairs on ordinary gemcraft tools.
- Properly maintained tools are less likely to damage a gemstone during routine work.

---

### Initiate Techniques

#### Flaw Detection

You can recognise common fractures and weaknesses within gemstones.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when inspecting a gemstone for visible flaws.
- Identify cracks, cloudy sections, weak edges and unstable inclusions.
- Recognise which areas should be avoided during cutting.
- Hidden magical flaws may require Enchanting or another appropriate skill.

---

#### Efficient Shaping

Your cutting process reduces waste and unnecessary work.

**Effects**
- Reduce the time required to shape common gemstones.
- Recover more usable material from a raw stone.
- Gain Advantage when planning the first cuts made to a gemstone.
- Poorly shaped or heavily damaged stones may still produce limited usable material.

---

#### Matched Stones

You can create gemstones with similar size, shape and appearance.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when cutting pairs or sets of matching gemstones.
- Reduce visible differences between stones from the same batch.
- Matching stones are more suitable for jewellery, decoration and symmetrical designs.
- Natural differences in colour or clarity may still remain.

---

#### Secure Mounting

You can prepare gemstones to fit safely into simple settings.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when placing gemstones into rings, pendants or other ordinary fittings.
- Reduce the chance of the gemstone becoming loose during normal use.
- Recognise whether a setting is too tight, weak or poorly aligned.
- Complex mechanical or magical settings may require Jewelcrafting, Engineering or Enchanting.

---

### Apprentice Techniques

#### Precision Faceting

You can create consistent facets that improve brilliance and appearance.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when cutting multi-faceted gemstones.
- Successfully faceted stones reflect light more effectively.
- Reduce asymmetry between corresponding facets.
- The final quality remains limited by the gemstone's natural clarity and colour.

---

#### Material Conservation

You carefully plan cuts to preserve as much valuable material as possible.

**Effects**
- Reduce the amount of gemstone lost during successful cutting.
- Recover small usable fragments from ordinary offcuts.
- Gain Advantage when salvaging gems from damaged jewellery or objects.
- Crushed or powdered material may only be useful for crafting or enchantment components.

---

#### Decorative Engraving

You can carve simple symbols, patterns and lettering into gemstones.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when engraving decorative designs into common gemstones.
- Create clear marks without significantly weakening the stone.
- Reproduce familiar symbols with reliable accuracy.
- Magical runes require appropriate knowledge and may also require Scribing or Enchanting.

---

#### Structural Assessment

You can determine whether a gemstone can survive a planned cut or use.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when judging the physical stability of a gemstone.
- Estimate whether the stone can withstand drilling, engraving or mounting.
- Identify areas likely to fracture under pressure.
- Magical instability may not be visible through Gemcraft alone.

---

### Journeyman Techniques

#### Advanced Faceting

You can create complex cuts that bring out the strongest features of a gemstone.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when performing advanced or unusual gemstone cuts.
- Choose whether the cut emphasises brilliance, colour or apparent size.
- Reduce penalties caused by irregularly shaped raw stones.
- The cut cannot create qualities that the gemstone does not naturally possess.

---

#### Gem Restoration

You can repair or reshape damaged gemstones.

**Effects**
- Remove chips, damaged edges and certain surface cracks.
- Gain Advantage when recutting a gemstone to preserve its remaining value.
- Restore heavily worn gemstones to a usable condition.
- Repairing damage usually reduces the gemstone's overall size.

---

#### Hidden Inclusion

You can preserve or deliberately display valuable internal features.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when working around unusual inclusions inside a gemstone.
- Choose to conceal or highlight a visually interesting inclusion.
- Reduce the chance of exposing a dangerous internal fracture.
- Magical or living inclusions may require specialised handling.

---

#### Socket Preparation

You shape gemstones for use in weapons, armour, tools or magical objects.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when producing stones intended for specialised sockets.
- Create uniform bases and edges that fit prepared housings securely.
- Reduce the chance of a stone cracking when inserted or removed.
- This technique does not create the socket itself.

---

### Adept Techniques

#### Magical Crystal Preparation

You can prepare gemstones and crystals for use in magical crafting.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when shaping gemstones intended for Enchanting, Runecrafting or Artificing.
- Reduce damage to a stone's natural magical structure during cutting.
- Identify whether a visible flaw may interfere with magical energy.
- This technique does not allow you to enchant the gemstone by itself.

---

#### Internal Mapping

You can study the inner structure of a gemstone before making any cut.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when planning cuts around deep internal flaws.
- Determine the safest and most valuable cutting path.
- Reduce the chance of unexpected fractures during crafting.
- Unknown magical structures may require additional magical analysis.

---

#### Micro-Engraving

You can carve extremely small and detailed designs into gemstones.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when engraving miniature symbols, text or patterns.
- Fit more information or detail into a limited surface area.
- Create markings that are difficult to notice without close inspection.
- Complex magical scripts still require the appropriate magical or scribing knowledge.

---

#### Reinforced Cut

You shape a gemstone to better withstand impact and pressure.

**Effects**
- Successfully cut gemstones gain increased durability.
- Gain Advantage when preparing stones for weapons, armour or frequently used tools.
- Reduce the chance of chipping during ordinary impacts.
- This technique cannot make a fragile gemstone indestructible.

---

### Master Techniques

#### Masterwork Gemcutting

Your cuts reveal the finest qualities hidden within a gemstone.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when cutting any familiar gemstone.
- Successful cuts are less likely to contain uneven facets or accidental flaws.
- Reduce quality loss caused by minor imperfections in the raw stone.
- This technique cannot bypass material, equipment or rank requirements.

---

#### Composite Gemwork

You can combine several gemstone pieces into one unified design.

**Effects**
- Join compatible gemstone pieces into mosaics, inlays or composite stones.
- Gain Advantage when aligning several pieces into a seamless pattern.
- Different gems may be arranged to emphasise colour, symbolism or magical purpose.
- The pieces remain distinct materials and do not become a new gemstone.

---

#### Resonant Shaping

You cut gemstones in ways that encourage the flow of magical energy.

**Effects**
- Prepare gemstones to channel, store or direct magical power more efficiently.
- Gain Advantage when working from a known magical gem design.
- Reduce interference caused by poorly aligned facets.
- The final magical effect must still be created through Enchanting, Runecrafting or another appropriate skill.

---

#### Perfect Restoration

You can recover exceptional value from badly damaged gemstones.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when restoring cracked, shattered or poorly cut stones.
- Recover multiple usable gems from a larger broken gemstone when possible.
- Preserve more of the original material than ordinary restoration allows.
- Powdered, melted or magically destroyed gemstones cannot normally be restored.

---

### Grandmaster Techniques

#### Legendary Gemcraft

You understand the hidden structure and potential of nearly every gemstone.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when analysing unknown gemstones or crystalline materials.
- Identify their major physical qualities, weaknesses and likely uses.
- Reconstruct lost cutting methods from surviving examples.
- Divine, eldritch or entirely unique crystals may still resist analysis.

---

#### Living Light

You shape a gemstone so perfectly that light appears to move within it.

**Effects**
- Create a gemstone with extraordinary brilliance and visual depth.
- The stone retains its brilliance even in weak surrounding light.
- Gain Advantage when crafting symbolic, ceremonial or royal gemstones.
- This effect is physical unless further altered through magic.

---

#### Impossible Facet

You can attempt cuts believed to be too complex or dangerous for ordinary gemcrafters.

**Effects**
- Attempt to create a unique multi-layered or internally aligned cut.
- The finished gemstone may display exceptional brilliance, colour or magical potential.
- Every attempt requires specialised equipment, extended crafting time and a difficult Gemcraft check.
- Failure may severely damage or destroy the gemstone.

---

#### Heart of the Crystal

You can reveal and preserve the defining essence of a gemstone through its final form.

**Effects**
- Choose one natural strength of the gemstone, such as colour, clarity, durability or magical conductivity.
- A successful cut greatly emphasises the selected quality.
- Gain Advantage when preparing legendary gemstones for important items or rituals.
- The technique cannot grant a property that the gemstone does not already possess.
