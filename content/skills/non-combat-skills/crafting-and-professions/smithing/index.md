# Smithing

## Overview

Smithing Skill Techniques represent specialised forging methods learned through mastery of the **Smithing** skill.

A smith does more than heat and strike metal. They must understand temperature, pressure, material behaviour, shaping, quenching and structural balance. Skilled smiths reduce waste, repair damaged equipment and create stronger, more reliable metalwork. At higher ranks, they can work rare materials, combine metals and produce legendary creations capable of surviving extraordinary punishment.

Whenever a character reaches a new **Smithing Skill Rank**, they may choose **one technique** from the four techniques available at that rank.

Once selected, a technique becomes part of the character's smithing training unless it is replaced through retraining approved by the GM.

Smithing Techniques are **Skill Techniques**, not Talents. They do not normally have an inherent Mana, Stamina or Health cost. Smithing may still require materials, tools, fuel and crafting time. Prolonged work, extreme heat or forging exceptionally difficult materials may cause Stamina loss or Exhaustion at the GM's discretion.

### Novice Techniques

#### Controlled Hammering

You learn to strike metal with consistent force and placement.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when performing basic shaping and flattening work.
- Reduce mistakes caused by uneven hammer strikes.
- Simple forged objects are less likely to develop obvious weak points.
- Reduce minor material waste during ordinary smithing.

---

#### Heat Judgement

You recognise when common metals are ready to be worked.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when judging the temperature of common metals.
- Recognise when metal is too cold, overheated or beginning to burn.
- Reduce failures caused by working metal at an incorrect temperature.
- This technique does not automatically identify unfamiliar magical materials.

---

#### Forge Maintenance

You understand how to maintain basic smithing tools and equipment.

**Effects**
- Reduce wear on hammers, tongs, anvils and ordinary forge equipment.
- Gain Advantage when identifying damaged or unsafe smithing tools.
- Perform basic repairs on non-magical forge equipment.
- Properly maintained tools are less likely to fail during ordinary crafting.

---

#### Simple Repair

You can restore basic metal items that have suffered minor damage.

**Effects**
- Repair dents, bends, cracks and loose fittings in common metal objects.
- Gain Advantage when repairing simple tools or equipment.
- Reduce the materials required for minor repairs.
- This technique cannot restore completely destroyed items.

---

### Initiate Techniques

#### Efficient Forging

Your workflow reduces wasted movement, fuel and preparation time.

**Effects**
- Reduce the time required to craft common metal items.
- Consume less ordinary forge fuel during crafting.
- Gain Advantage when organising a forge for repeated work.
- This technique does not reduce the time required for cooling or specialised treatments.

---

#### Clean Weld

You can join metal pieces without creating obvious structural weaknesses.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when welding or forge-joining compatible metals.
- Welded areas are less likely to split under ordinary stress.
- Recognise weak, incomplete or contaminated welds.
- Incompatible or magical metals may require specialised techniques.

---

#### Edge Formation

You understand how to establish and maintain a reliable cutting edge.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when shaping the edge of a bladed metal item.
- Reduce the chance of creating an uneven or brittle edge.
- Items sharpened by you retain their edge for longer under ordinary use.
- This technique does not increase the base damage of the weapon by itself.

---

#### Reinforced Fittings

You specialise in strengthening joints, handles, hinges and fastenings.

**Effects**
- Metal fittings crafted by you have increased durability.
- Gain Advantage when attaching metal components to wood, leather or stone.
- Reduce the chance of handles, straps or hinges separating during use.
- Repair damaged fittings using fewer replacement materials.

---

### Apprentice Techniques

#### Material Conservation

You have learned how to minimise waste during cutting, shaping and refinement.

**Effects**
- Reduce the amount of raw metal lost during successful crafting.
- Recover a portion of usable material from ordinary crafting offcuts.
- Gain Advantage when salvaging metal from damaged non-magical objects.
- Contaminated or heavily corroded metal may provide a reduced yield.

---

#### Balanced Construction

You distribute weight throughout an item to improve handling and reliability.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when balancing weapons, tools or armour components.
- Items crafted with this technique are easier to handle during prolonged use.
- Reduce penalties caused by poor weight distribution.
- This technique cannot remove penalties caused by an item being too large for its wielder.

---

#### Tempered Steel

You use controlled heating and cooling to improve the strength of forged metal.

**Effects**
- Successfully tempered items gain increased durability.
- Gain Advantage when preventing warping during the tempering process.
- Reduce the chance of an item becoming excessively brittle.
- The final benefit is limited by the quality of the metal used.

---

#### Structural Assessment

You can identify weaknesses within metalwork before they become catastrophic.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when inspecting weapons, armour, tools or metal structures.
- Identify cracks, poor joins, warping and hidden stress points.
- Estimate whether an item is safe to use.
- Magical concealment or internal enchantments may require additional skills to analyse.

---

### Journeyman Techniques

#### Batch Forging

You can create multiple identical items through one organised process.

**Effects**
- Forge a small batch of simple identical items at the same time.
- Reduce the total crafting time compared with forging each item separately.
- All items share the same crafting quality result.
- A major crafting failure may affect the entire batch.

---

#### Advanced Repair

You can restore heavily damaged equipment without fully reforging it.

**Effects**
- Repair major cracks, broken sections and severe warping.
- Gain Advantage when restoring items with most of their original material intact.
- Repaired items recover more of their original durability.
- Destroyed, melted or fragmented items may still require complete reforging.

---

#### Layered Metalwork

You forge multiple layers of compatible metal into a single construction.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when creating layered weapons, armour or tools.
- Layered items resist cracking and deformation more effectively.
- Different layers may be used to balance hardness and flexibility.
- Every metal used must be compatible with the forging process.

---

#### Tool Specialist

You excel at creating highly reliable tools for a particular trade.

**Effects**
- Choose one category of metal tool when learning this technique.
- Gain Advantage when forging tools from that category.
- Successfully crafted tools gain increased durability.
- Well-crafted tools may grant a situational bonus to their intended skill at the GM's discretion.

---

### Adept Techniques

#### Rare Metalworking

You have learned to work metals that resist ordinary forging methods.

**Effects**
- Forge rare non-magical and minor magical metals appropriate to your rank.
- Gain Advantage when determining the correct working temperature of a known rare metal.
- Reduce failures caused by unusual hardness, heat retention or brittleness.
- Unknown legendary materials may still require research or specialised equipment.

---

#### Precision Forging

You can produce detailed components with extremely small tolerances.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when forging locks, mechanisms, fine fittings or intricate components.
- Reduce errors caused by minor measurement differences.
- Crafted components fit together with exceptional accuracy.
- This technique may support Engineering, Artificing and other precision-based crafts.

---

#### Armour Reinforcement

You know how to strengthen armour without adding excessive weight.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when reinforcing metal armour.
- Reinforced armour gains increased durability.
- Reduce penalties caused by poorly distributed reinforcement.
- This technique cannot raise armour beyond the limits of its material and quality.

---

#### Weapon Reinforcement

You strengthen the areas of a weapon most likely to break during combat.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when reinforcing metal weapons.
- Reinforced weapons gain increased durability.
- Reduce the chance of the weapon breaking from ordinary critical failures.
- This technique does not prevent destruction by overwhelming force or powerful magic.

---

### Master Techniques

#### Masterwork Forging

Your control of heat, material and impact allows you to create exceptional metalwork.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when forging any familiar metal item.
- Successful items are less likely to contain structural flaws.
- Reduce quality loss caused by minor imperfections in raw materials.
- This technique cannot bypass material, recipe or equipment requirements.

---

#### Composite Alloy

You can create an alloy from several compatible metals.

**Effects**
- Combine two or more known compatible metals into a single alloy.
- Choose which suitable qualities of the component metals the alloy emphasises.
- Gain Advantage when producing an alloy you have successfully created before.
- Failed alloying may ruin or contaminate all metals involved.

---

#### Rapid Reforging

You can reshape an existing metal item into a new form more efficiently.

**Effects**
- Reforge a metal item while recovering most of its usable material.
- Reduce the time required compared with smelting and crafting from raw metal.
- Gain Advantage when reforging an item into one of similar size and complexity.
- Enchantments and soul-bound properties may be damaged or destroyed during reforging.

---

#### Forge Without Flaw

You recognise and correct small imperfections throughout the entire crafting process.

**Effects**
- Once during a smithing project, reroll a failed Smithing check.
- You must use the new result.
- Minor flaws can be corrected before the item is completed.
- Critical failures caused by unsuitable materials or destroyed equipment cannot be rerolled.

---

### Grandmaster Techniques

#### Legendary Metallurgy

You understand the hidden qualities of nearly every workable metal.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when analysing unknown metal or alloy compositions.
- Identify the metal's major strengths, weaknesses and likely forging requirements.
- Reconstruct lost forging methods from surviving examples.
- Divine, eldritch or entirely unique materials may still resist analysis.

---

#### Soul of the Forge

You can judge the condition of metal through sound, vibration and touch alone.

**Effects**
- Instantly recognise ordinary structural flaws in metalwork you handle.
- Gain Advantage on all checks made to repair or reforge damaged metal items.
- Detect whether an item has been altered since it was originally forged.
- Magical disguises may conceal information beyond the metal's physical condition.

---

#### Impossible Alloy

You can attempt to combine metals normally considered incompatible.

**Effects**
- Attempt to forge an alloy from materials with conflicting properties.
- The alloy may preserve extraordinary qualities from each component.
- Every attempt requires specialised equipment, extended crafting time and a difficult Smithing check.
- Failure may destroy the materials or create a dangerously unstable result.

---

#### Forge of Ages

Your creations are made to endure beyond ordinary lifetimes.

**Effects**
- Items successfully forged with this technique gain exceptional durability.
- They resist ordinary corrosion, weathering and long-term material fatigue.
- Gain Advantage when creating weapons, armour, monuments or tools intended to survive for generations.
- Powerful magic, deliberate destruction and catastrophic forces may still damage the item.
