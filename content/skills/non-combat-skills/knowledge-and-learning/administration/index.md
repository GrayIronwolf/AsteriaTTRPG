# Administration

## Overview

Administration Techniques represent specialised methods of organisation, recordkeeping, logistics, delegation and bureaucratic control learned through mastery of the **Administration** skill.

Administrators manage the systems that keep guilds, settlements, armies, businesses and kingdoms functioning. They prepare records, distribute resources, coordinate workers, identify inefficiencies and navigate complex institutions.

Whenever a character reaches a new **Administration Skill Rank**, they may choose **one technique** from the four techniques available at that rank.

Once selected, a technique becomes part of the character's administrative training unless it is replaced through retraining approved by the GM.

Administration Techniques are **Skill Techniques**, not Talents. They normally have no inherent resource cost. Costs or consequences may arise when an administrator works under extreme pressure, manages a prolonged crisis or makes a serious administrative error.

**Secondary Characteristic:** Charisma

### Novice Techniques

#### Organised Records

You develop a consistent system for storing and retrieving documents.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when searching records that you personally organised.
- Reduce the time required to locate a known document, account or entry.
- You can quickly identify whether a document has been removed from your filing system.

---

#### Daily Planner

You prepare clear schedules that help others complete routine tasks.

**Effects**
- You may create a daily schedule for yourself or a small group.
- Characters following the schedule are less likely to forget routine duties, appointments or deadlines.
- Gain a bonus on Administration checks made to coordinate simple daily activities.

---

#### Clear Instructions

You know how to explain duties without unnecessary confusion.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when giving instructions for routine work.
- Reduce minor mistakes caused by unclear directions.
- Workers can accurately repeat your instructions to others after receiving them.

---

#### Basic Inventory

You can maintain a reliable record of supplies, equipment and currency.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when counting or cataloguing stored resources.
- Quickly recognise obvious shortages, excess stock or missing items.
- Reduce the time required to perform a basic inventory check.

---

### Initiate Techniques

#### Efficient Filing

Your filing systems remain useful even as the number of records grows.

**Effects**
- Records organised by you can be searched efficiently by other trained characters.
- Gain a bonus when comparing multiple documents or identifying related entries.
- You can recognise when information has been filed incorrectly.

---

#### Resource Allocation

You distribute limited supplies according to immediate needs.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when deciding how to divide food, tools, workers or funds between small groups.
- Reduce minor waste caused by poor distribution.
- You can identify which department or activity is consuming more resources than expected.

---

#### Meeting Coordinator

You can keep discussions focused and properly documented.

**Effects**
- Reduce the time required to complete formal meetings.
- Gain Advantage when preventing a meeting from becoming disorganised.
- You may produce an accurate summary of decisions, responsibilities and deadlines.

---

#### Administrative Etiquette

You understand the expected behaviour within offices, guilds and institutions.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when interacting with clerks, officials and administrative workers.
- Recognise the correct person or office to approach for routine requests.
- Avoid minor penalties caused by improper forms, titles or procedures.

---

### Apprentice Techniques

#### Audit Trail

You can follow the movement of money, resources and official decisions through written records.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when investigating financial or logistical discrepancies.
- Identify where a recorded transaction entered or left an administrative system.
- Detect obvious alterations, missing documents or conflicting entries.
- This technique does not automatically reveal a skilled forgery.

---

#### Delegation

You assign work according to the strengths and responsibilities of each worker.

**Effects**
- Gain a bonus when coordinating a group performing several related tasks.
- Workers under your direction are less likely to duplicate or overlook duties.
- You can quickly identify which individual is best suited to a routine administrative assignment.

---

#### Contingency Planning

You prepare alternatives before predictable problems occur.

**Effects**
- When creating an administrative plan, you may establish one reasonable contingency.
- Gain Advantage when responding to a disruption covered by that contingency.
- Reduce delays caused by common problems such as missing staff, late deliveries or damaged supplies.

---

#### Bureaucratic Navigation

You understand how institutions process requests and make decisions.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when determining the correct procedure for permits, licences, claims or petitions.
- Reduce the time lost by submitting requests to the wrong office.
- Recognise when an official is deliberately delaying a standard process.

---

### Journeyman Techniques

#### Operational Oversight

You can monitor several connected departments or work groups at once.

**Effects**
- Coordinate a medium-sized operation without suffering penalties from ordinary complexity.
- Gain Advantage when identifying delays between different stages of an operation.
- Quickly determine which work group is causing a broader organisational problem.

---

#### Procurement Network

You maintain relationships with suppliers, traders and service providers.

**Effects**
- Gain a bonus when sourcing common goods in an established settlement.
- Reduce the time required to locate reliable suppliers.
- Recognise suspicious pricing, unreliable contracts or unusual shortages.
- This technique does not guarantee that rare or restricted goods are available.

---

#### Crisis Administration

You remain organised when normal procedures begin to collapse.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage on Administration checks made during emergencies.
- Reduce confusion while organising evacuations, emergency supplies or replacement workers.
- Characters following your crisis plan can clearly identify their immediate responsibilities.

---

#### Regulatory Knowledge

You understand the laws, policies and regulations governing an institution or settlement.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when identifying relevant administrative rules.
- Recognise when a demand falls outside an official's normal authority.
- Identify lawful exemptions, appeals or alternative procedures when they exist.
- This technique does not allow you to ignore or automatically overcome the law.

---

### Adept Techniques

#### Strategic Logistics

You can coordinate the movement of large quantities of supplies and personnel.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when planning large deliveries, expeditions or organised movements.
- Reduce avoidable losses caused by poor routes, timing or storage.
- Identify logistical bottlenecks before they halt the operation.
- Your plans may include multiple destinations and supply stages.

---

#### Institutional Memory

You create records that preserve knowledge beyond individual workers.

**Effects**
- Organisations using your system retain essential information when staff members leave or die.
- Gain Advantage when reconstructing previous decisions from archived records.
- Reduce penalties caused by leadership changes or missing personnel.
- You can create clear procedural documents for recurring operations.

---

#### Fraud Detection

You recognise patterns commonly associated with corruption, theft and false reporting.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when examining suspicious accounts, inventories or official reports.
- Detect unexplained inconsistencies that may indicate fraud.
- Identify which records or individuals require further investigation.
- Highly skilled deception may still require Analysis, Investigation or another appropriate skill.

---

#### Command Structure

You establish a clear hierarchy through which information and orders can travel.

**Effects**
- Reduce confusion within large organised groups.
- Gain Advantage when determining responsibility for a failed or incomplete order.
- Messages passed through your structure are less likely to be altered accidentally.
- Workers can continue routine operations during the temporary absence of a leader.

---

### Master Techniques

#### Grand Administrator

You can manage a major organisation, settlement department or regional operation.

**Effects**
- Coordinate several large administrative divisions simultaneously.
- Ignore ordinary penalties caused by the scale of a managed organisation.
- Gain Advantage when identifying organisation-wide inefficiencies.
- Routine operations under your system can continue with minimal direct supervision.

---

#### Master of Provision

Your logistical planning supports communities, armies and major expeditions.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when calculating long-term supply requirements.
- Reduce spoilage, shortages and unnecessary expenditure caused by poor planning.
- Accurately estimate how long stored supplies will support a known population.
- Quickly identify which supplies are essential to maintaining an operation.

---

#### Bureaucratic Authority

Your knowledge of procedure allows you to exert considerable influence within formal institutions.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when presenting a properly supported official request.
- Officials must provide a legitimate procedural reason to dismiss complete documentation.
- Recognise attempts to hide decisions behind false procedures or invented requirements.
- This technique grants influence through process, not automatic authority over an institution.

---

#### System Reform

You can redesign failing administrative systems.

**Effects**
- Identify redundant procedures, unclear responsibilities and wasteful practices.
- Gain Advantage when replacing an inefficient administrative process.
- A successfully reformed system reduces routine delays and errors.
- Major reforms require time, cooperation and access to the organisation being changed.

---

### Grandmaster Techniques

#### Architect of Nations

You can design administrative systems capable of supporting kingdoms and vast organisations.

**Effects**
- Create interconnected systems for taxation, records, logistics and public administration.
- Your administrative structures can function across multiple settlements or regions.
- Gain Advantage when resolving problems created by distance, scale or divided authority.
- Systems created by you remain effective when maintained by properly trained administrators.

---

#### Perfect Coordination

You can unite many departments behind a single complex objective.

**Effects**
- Coordinate a kingdom-scale project, military campaign or major reconstruction effort.
- Participating groups clearly understand their responsibilities, dependencies and deadlines.
- Reduce delays caused by communication failures between cooperating organisations.
- Gain Advantage when correcting disruptions before they affect the entire operation.

---

#### Invisible Hand

Your administration operates so smoothly that most people never notice the work behind it.

**Effects**
- Routine operations under your control continue without requiring constant checks.
- Minor administrative failures are identified and corrected before becoming public problems.
- Gain Advantage when concealing sensitive logistical movements within ordinary activity.
- Detect attempts to secretly interfere with systems you personally established.

---

#### Absolute Accountability

Nothing within your administrative domain escapes the records for long.

**Effects**
- Resources, orders and official decisions managed through your system leave a trace.
- Gain Advantage when investigating corruption, theft or abuse of authority.
- You can reconstruct complex administrative events from incomplete surviving records.
- Deliberate attempts to erase information must overcome the quality of your administrative system.

---
