# Zootomy

## Overview

Zootomy Skill Techniques represent specialised methods of studying, dissecting and harvesting animals, monsters and magical creatures through mastery of the **Zootomy** skill.

To a Zootomist, a fallen creature is not merely flesh and bone. It is a final record of how that creature lived, hunted, channelled magic and interacted with the world around it. Every organ, gland, scale and strand of essence carries part of that history.

Novice Zootomists begin by learning the structure of common animals. As their mastery grows, they learn to trace magical essence through the body, stabilise volatile organs, harvest rare materials and perceive the hidden connection between flesh, mana and spirit.

Whenever a character reaches a new **Zootomy Skill Rank**, they may choose **one technique** from the four techniques available at that rank.

Once selected, a technique becomes part of the character's Zootomy training unless it is replaced through retraining approved by the GM.

Zootomy Techniques are **Skill Techniques**, not Talents. They do not normally have an inherent resource cost. Harvesting still requires suitable tools, time and access to a creature's remains. Magical, poisonous or unstable creatures may create additional dangers.

### Novice Techniques

> The study of life begins at the surface — sinew, hide and bone.  
> Every creature is a final teacher to those willing to look beneath the skin.

#### Clean Incision

You have learned to open a carcass without unnecessarily damaging the structures beneath its hide.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when making the initial incision during a harvest.
- Reduce accidental damage to nearby organs and internal materials.
- A minor harvesting failure does not automatically destroy the first common material.
- This technique does not protect against a Critical Failure.

---

#### Basic Anatomy

You understand the common arrangement of bones, muscles and organs found within ordinary animals.

**Effects**
- Identify the major organs, bones and muscle groups of Common Beasts.
- Gain Advantage when determining whether a visible body part is healthy, injured or malformed.
- Recognise obvious anatomical differences between related animal species.
- Reduce the time required to examine a familiar creature.

---

#### Cause of Death

You can interpret obvious wounds and physical signs left upon a carcass.

**Effects**
- Determine whether a creature died from blood loss, physical trauma, predation or natural causes.
- Identify the most obvious fatal wound.
- Estimate how recently the creature died.
- Gain Advantage when determining whether an injury occurred before or after death.
- Poison, disease and magical causes may require another appropriate skill.

---

#### Careful Harvest

You prioritise preservation over speed, taking additional time to protect every usable material.

**Effects**
- Increase the time required for a harvest to gain Advantage on the Zootomy check.
- Successfully harvested materials suffer less accidental damage.
- Recover a small amount of additional common material from a successful harvest.
- This technique cannot increase the chance of discovering rare materials.

---

### Initiate Techniques

> The Initiate has learned that every living creature carries a subtle rhythm beneath its skin.  
> Flesh is no longer silent; it whispers of health, hunger and lingering essence.

#### Beast Reader

You can read the visible signs left within a creature's remains.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when determining whether valuable organs or glands remain intact.
- Identify obvious spoilage, contamination or internal damage.
- Recognise whether the creature carried an unusual concentration of natural or magical energy.
- Reduce the chance of harvesting a ruined or unusable material.

---

#### Safe Dissection

You take precautions against venom, infection and biological hazards.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when avoiding ordinary toxins and contaminated fluids during dissection.
- Recognise visible venom sacs, poison glands and dangerous bodily fluids.
- Reduce the chance of accidentally rupturing a hazardous organ.
- Supernatural toxins may still require specialised protection.

---

#### Skeletal Reading

You can study bones to learn how a creature lived and moved.

**Effects**
- Identify fractures, deformities and signs of repeated physical strain.
- Estimate the creature's size and posture from partial remains.
- Determine whether skeletal damage occurred before or after death.
- Gain Advantage when identifying bones from a familiar species.

---

#### Tissue Preservation

You know how to slow the decay of harvested flesh and organs.

**Effects**
- Biological samples prepared by you remain usable for longer.
- Gain Advantage when preparing harvested materials for transport.
- Reduce contamination caused by ordinary exposure.
- Magical corruption and extreme environments may still destroy the material.

---

### Apprentice Techniques

> The Apprentice no longer sees separate organs and tissues.  
> They perceive an interconnected pattern through which blood, instinct and essence once flowed.

#### Essence Thread

You recognise the pathways through which magical energy travelled within the creature.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when locating glands, cores and magically active organs.
- Identify visible pathways through which Mana or elemental power flowed.
- Reduce accidental damage to materials connected to magical abilities.
- This technique does not identify the exact magical effect of an unknown organ.

---

#### Refined Extraction

You can remove delicate internal materials without destroying their connections.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when harvesting glands, organs, crystals or internal sacs.
- Increase the amount of usable material recovered from a successful harvest.
- Reduce quality loss caused by rough removal.
- The quality remains limited by the condition of the carcass.

---

#### Injury Reconstruction

You can determine how a wound was created by examining its depth, angle and surrounding damage.

**Effects**
- Identify whether a wound came from a blade, blunt weapon, claw, bite or puncture.
- Estimate the direction and general force of the attack.
- Gain Advantage when determining which injury was inflicted first.
- This technique does not automatically identify the attacker.

---

#### Anatomical Catalogue

You maintain detailed records of creatures you have examined.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when recalling the anatomy of a previously studied creature.
- Reduce the time required to dissect familiar species.
- Compare new specimens against your existing records.
- Other trained Zootomists may use your catalogue as a research reference.

---

### Journeyman Techniques

> The Journeyman moves with calm certainty.  
> Their blade follows the shimmer of essence beneath hide and sinew, preserving secrets others would destroy.

#### Beast Harvest

You stabilise fading biological and magical properties during extraction.

**Effects**
- Reduce the quality loss caused by harvesting a creature long after its death.
- Gain Advantage when extracting materials that are beginning to decay.
- Preserve a harvested material's residual magical properties for longer.
- Severely decomposed or magically destroyed remains cannot be restored.

---

#### Venom Gland Extraction

You specialise in removing poisonous organs without contamination.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when harvesting venom sacs, poison glands and toxic spines.
- Preserve more of the substance's original potency.
- Reduce the chance of contaminating other harvested materials.
- Preparing the toxin for use may still require Alchemy or Poison Use.

---

#### Vital Structure Analysis

You understand which organs and physical systems were essential to the creature's survival.

**Effects**
- Identify major vital organs and vulnerable anatomical structures.
- Determine which visible injuries were likely fatal.
- Recognise whether a creature possessed redundant organs or unusual survival adaptations.
- Gain Advantage when examining a creature from a previously studied family.

---

#### Field Zootomist

You are accustomed to harvesting creatures under imperfect conditions.

**Effects**
- Reduce penalties caused by poor lighting, limited shelter or an improvised workspace.
- Gain Advantage when performing a harvest shortly after combat.
- Organise helpers to assist with moving, cleaning and preserving remains.
- This technique does not remove hazards created by active enemies or unstable magic.

---

### Adept Techniques

> The Adept walks the boundary between anatomy and sorcery.  
> Every monster becomes a pattern of living geometry through which essence once took form.

#### Monster Harvest

You can stabilise volatile organs found within magical and monstrous creatures.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when harvesting from Magical Beasts and monstrous creatures.
- Reduce the chance of volatile organs combusting, collapsing or losing their power.
- Preserve elemental glands, magical cores and similar structures.
- Mythic and legendary creatures may still require specialised tools.

---

#### Weak-Point Study

You translate anatomical knowledge into an understanding of physical vulnerabilities.

**Effects**
- After studying a creature or its species, identify one plausible anatomical weak point.
- Gain a situational bonus when targeting that area.
- The structure must be visible or physically reachable.
- Creatures without conventional anatomy may not possess a usable weak point.

---

#### Regenerative Assessment

You can identify how a creature heals and what may interrupt that process.

**Effects**
- Determine whether regeneration is biological, magical or environmental.
- Gain Advantage when identifying substances or damage types that may suppress it.
- Recognise whether severed tissue remains alive or capable of regrowth.
- This technique does not automatically prevent regeneration.

---

#### Arcane Organ Preparation

You prepare magical organs for use by alchemists, enchanters and artificers.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when cleaning and stabilising magically active organs.
- Reduce damage to internal Mana pathways during extraction.
- Properly prepared organs provide increased Magical Efficiency when used in crafting.
- This technique does not allow you to craft the final magical item.

---

### Master Techniques

> The Master's gaze pierces flesh and spirit alike.  
> They need not cut to understand; the flow of essence reveals the shape hidden beneath untouched skin.

#### Anatomical Ascendancy

You can perceive the internal arrangement of a creature before beginning the dissection.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage on the first Zootomy check made against a creature.
- Identify the approximate location of major organs, cores and essence channels.
- Recognise obvious unnatural additions or alterations within the body.
- Magical concealment may prevent complete analysis.

---

#### Arcane Organ Study

You understand structures that naturally generate or channel magical effects.

**Effects**
- Identify the general purpose of Mana glands, elemental organs and magical cores.
- Gain Advantage when determining how an organ supported a creature's special ability.
- Preserve the organ's magical pathways during extraction.
- Activating or crafting with the organ requires an appropriate skill.

---

#### Complete Reconstruction

You can rebuild the likely anatomy of a creature from incomplete remains.

**Effects**
- Estimate the creature's full physical structure from bones, tissue and partial organs.
- Identify whether remains from several creatures have been mixed together.
- Gain Advantage when determining the location of missing body parts.
- Extremely limited remains may still produce uncertain conclusions.

---

#### Perfect Harvest

Your control allows you to recover nearly everything of value from a suitable carcass.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage on detailed harvesting checks.
- Recover additional usable materials from a successful harvest.
- Reduce accidental quality loss during extraction.
- Once per creature, reroll a failed harvesting check and use the new result.
- Critical Failures cannot be rerolled.

---

### Grandmaster Techniques

> The Grandmaster sees no division between body, Mana and memory.  
> Every fragment of flesh carries the echo of the life that shaped it.

#### Eidolon Extraction

You can attempt to preserve a faint echo of the creature's essence within a suitable vessel.

**Effects**
- Harvest a residual essence fragment from a recently deceased magical creature.
- The fragment may contain instinct, emotion or an incomplete memory.
- Essence fragments may be used in advanced enchantments, relics or research.
- The technique requires a prepared vessel and a successful high-difficulty Zootomy check.
- Failure may cause the essence to disperse or react unpredictably.

---

#### Anatomy of Legends

You can study and harvest creatures whose bodies defy ordinary natural law.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when examining dragons, leviathans and legendary monsters.
- Identify how immense or magical bodies support extraordinary abilities.
- Preserve rare organs and tissues during extraction.
- Legendary remains may require equally rare tools and containment methods.

---

#### Life Map

You create a complete anatomical and essence map of a studied species.

**Effects**
- Record the creature's skeletal, muscular, circulatory, nervous and magical systems.
- Allies consulting the map gain a bonus on relevant Medicine, Harvesting or Research checks.
- Reveal major physical adaptations and known vulnerabilities.
- The map applies only to the studied species and closely related creatures.

---

#### Origin of Flesh

You can interpret the forces that shaped a creature's body and essence.

**Effects**
- Determine whether a creature developed naturally, was bred, mutated, assembled or transformed.
- Gain Advantage when tracing biological traits to an ancestral species or magical influence.
- Recognise evidence of eldritch corruption, divine alteration or artificial creation.
- The exact creator or historical event may still require Research, Lore or magical investigation.
