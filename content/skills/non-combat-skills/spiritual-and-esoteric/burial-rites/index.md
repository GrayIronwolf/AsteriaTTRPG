# Burial Rites

## Overview

Burial Rite Techniques represent specialised funerary practices, spiritual customs and methods of preparing the dead learned through mastery of the **Burial Rites** skill.

A practitioner of Burial Rites understands that death is not merely the end of life. The treatment of a body, the words spoken over the deceased and the ceremonies performed for their soul may determine how they are remembered, whether their spirit finds peace and whether darker forces can disturb their remains.

Whenever a character reaches a new **Burial Rites Skill Rank**, they may choose **one technique** from the four techniques available at that rank.

Once selected, a technique becomes part of the character's spiritual and cultural training unless it is replaced through retraining approved by the GM.

Burial Rite Techniques are **Skill Techniques**, not Talents. Most ceremonies do not have an inherent resource cost. Rare rites involving powerful spirits, cursed remains or supernatural intervention may require ritual materials, Mana or other offerings determined by the GM.

### Novice Techniques

#### Respectful Preparation

You understand how to clean and prepare a body for burial.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when preparing an ordinary humanoid body for burial.
- Prevent unnecessary damage to the remains during preparation.
- Recognise obvious signs that a body has been improperly handled.
- The prepared body remains presentable for a short period under normal conditions.

---

#### Last Words

You know how to speak with grieving families and record the final wishes of the dying.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when comforting someone shortly after a death.
- Accurately record a creature's final message or burial request.
- Reduce misunderstandings regarding simple funeral wishes.
- Recognise when someone is struggling to express an important final request.

---

#### Grave Marker

You can create and interpret basic burial markers.

**Effects**
- Create a simple marker containing the deceased's name, date of death and known affiliations.
- Gain Advantage when identifying common symbols found on graves.
- Recognise when a grave marker has been altered, moved or recently disturbed.
- Reduce the chance of an ordinary burial site being lost or misidentified.

---

#### Simple Blessing

You perform a basic rite intended to grant dignity and peace to the deceased.

**Effects**
- Conduct an appropriate simple funeral for a known culture or faith.
- Mourners gain comfort from a properly completed ceremony.
- Recognise obvious interruptions or mistakes that would invalidate the rite.
- This technique does not prevent magical resurrection, undeath or spirit activity.

---

### Initiate Techniques

#### Cultural Observance

You understand the funeral customs of several common cultures.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when identifying the burial customs of a familiar culture.
- Avoid causing accidental offence during funeral preparations.
- Recognise when multiple cultural traditions have been combined.
- Learn the basic expectations for mourning, burial position, offerings and grave placement.

---

#### Cause of Passing

Careful preparation allows you to recognise signs surrounding a creature's death.

**Effects**
- Identify obvious wounds, poison symptoms, disease or signs of exposure.
- Gain Advantage when estimating whether the death was sudden, violent or prolonged.
- Recognise when a body was moved after death.
- Detailed medical conclusions may still require Medicine, Zootomy or another appropriate skill.

---

#### Mourner's Guidance

You help the living complete the customs required to honour their dead.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when guiding a family or community through a funeral ceremony.
- Reduce confusion during periods of grief.
- Clearly explain the purpose of common burial practices.
- Help prevent disputes caused by minor ceremonial disagreements.

---

#### Seal the Grave

You prepare a grave in a way that reveals later disturbance.

**Effects**
- Create subtle seals, placements or markings around a burial site.
- Gain Advantage when determining whether the grave was reopened.
- Identify whether the disturbance came from outside or beneath the grave.
- Ordinary creatures are less likely to disturb a properly sealed burial site.

---

### Apprentice Techniques

#### Spirit Vigil

You remain beside the dead to watch for signs of spiritual unrest.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when detecting unusual changes around a recently deceased body.
- Recognise signs associated with a spirit refusing or failing to depart.
- Identify whether the unrest appears peaceful, fearful, confused or hostile.
- This technique does not allow direct communication with spirits.

---

#### Preservation Rite

You slow the natural decay of prepared remains.

**Effects**
- A properly prepared body decays at a greatly reduced rate.
- Gain Advantage when preserving remains for transport or delayed burial.
- Protect the body from ordinary insects and minor scavengers.
- Magical corruption, severe heat and powerful disease may still overcome the preservation.

---

#### Funerary Reconstruction

You can rebuild the likely form of a damaged or incomplete burial.

**Effects**
- Determine how a disrupted ceremony was originally intended to proceed.
- Gain Advantage when arranging incomplete remains for respectful burial.
- Identify which important ritual objects or body parts are missing.
- Reconstruct damaged grave markers from surviving fragments.

---

#### Rite of Safe Passage

You perform a fuller ceremony intended to guide the soul onward.

**Effects**
- Reduce minor spiritual disturbances surrounding the deceased.
- Spirits associated with the body are less likely to become confused or linger without cause.
- Gain Advantage when completing the unfinished funeral of a restless spirit.
- Powerful curses, necromancy or divine interference may prevent the rite from succeeding.

---

### Journeyman Techniques

#### Speak for the Dead

Your knowledge of burial custom allows you to represent the wishes and dignity of the deceased.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when negotiating the return, burial or protection of remains.
- Invoke recognised burial laws and customs when they apply.
- Clearly establish the deceased's known wishes before officials or family members.
- Communities familiar with the tradition are more likely to respect your authority during funerary matters.

---

#### Sanctified Rest

You prepare a burial site to resist minor supernatural interference.

**Effects**
- Lesser spirits and weak undead are reluctant to disturb the burial site.
- Gain Advantage when detecting necromantic interference within the grave.
- Remains buried through this rite are harder to animate through weak necromancy.
- Strong magical effects may still overcome the protection.

---

#### Battlefield Requiem

You can organise respectful burial rites after battles and disasters.

**Effects**
- Coordinate the identification and preparation of many bodies.
- Reduce the time required to conduct mass funeral ceremonies.
- Gain Advantage when preventing remains from being lost, confused or dishonoured.
- Survivors gain greater closure when the rite is completed successfully.

---

#### Keeper of Names

You preserve the identities and histories of the dead.

**Effects**
- Create reliable records linking bodies, graves and personal possessions.
- Gain Advantage when identifying unknown remains through indirect evidence.
- Reduce the chance of a buried creature being forgotten or misidentified.
- You can maintain memorial records for large families, settlements or organisations.

---

### Adept Techniques

#### Ward Against Undeath

Your funerary rites interfere with attempts to raise the deceased as undead.

**Effects**
- Remains prepared by you gain resistance against necromantic animation.
- Gain Advantage when opposing a ritual that would raise those remains.
- Identify signs that a body has been marked for future necromancy.
- Powerful necromancers may still overcome the ward through sufficient force or preparation.

---

#### Soul Anchor

You preserve a spiritual connection between a body, its identity and its rightful resting place.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when locating a body or soul that has been displaced from its prepared grave.
- Help prevent weaker effects from separating the deceased's identity from their remains.
- Recognise whether a spirit, body or grave has been magically severed from the others.
- This technique does not trap the soul or prevent willing resurrection.

---

#### Funeral of Reconciliation

You perform rites intended to settle conflict between the living and the dead.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when calming a spirit angered by an improper burial.
- Help identify the unresolved duty, insult or broken promise causing the unrest.
- Allow living participants to formally offer apology, truth or restitution.
- Hostile spirits are not forced to accept the reconciliation.

---

#### Guardian's Interment

You prepare the burial of an important individual, hero or protector.

**Effects**
- The grave becomes a recognised place of remembrance and cultural importance.
- Gain Advantage when invoking the deceased's reputation during related ceremonies.
- Minor desecration is more easily noticed by those connected to the burial.
- Communities that honour the deceased are more willing to defend or maintain the site.

---

### Master Techniques

#### Grand Funeral

You can conduct ceremonies for rulers, legendary heroes and major religious figures.

**Effects**
- Coordinate large funerals involving multiple faiths, factions or communities.
- Gain Advantage when preventing political or cultural disputes during the ceremony.
- Ensure that important titles, deeds and final wishes are represented accurately.
- A successful Grand Funeral may strengthen unity among those who respected the deceased.

---

#### Sever Necromantic Claim

You know how to remove lesser necromantic bindings from remains.

**Effects**
- Identify ritual marks, grave bindings and magical claims placed upon a body.
- Gain Advantage when removing weak or incomplete necromantic preparations.
- Prevent the same removed binding from being restored without a new ritual.
- Powerful curses or active undead transformations may require specialised magic to remove.

---

#### Path of the Departed

You can interpret the spiritual signs surrounding a soul's departure.

**Effects**
- Determine whether a spirit passed peacefully, lingered, was taken or became trapped.
- Gain Advantage when following traces left by a recently departed soul.
- Distinguish between divine, necromantic, eldritch and natural spiritual interference.
- The trail weakens with time and may be concealed by powerful forces.

---

#### Eternal Memorial

You create funerary monuments capable of preserving memory across generations.

**Effects**
- Design a memorial that accurately preserves the deceased's identity and deeds.
- Records and symbols incorporated into the memorial resist ordinary weathering and misunderstanding.
- Gain Advantage when using the memorial to teach descendants about the deceased.
- Communities connected to the memorial are less likely to forget the person or event it represents.

---

### Grandmaster Techniques

#### Gatekeeper of the Final Path

Your rites guide souls through even the most dangerous spiritual transitions.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when guiding a willing soul away from a corrupted or unstable death site.
- Weaker spirits cannot easily divert or mislead a soul under your protection.
- Recognise supernatural forces attempting to claim the deceased.
- Divine entities, powerful soul magic and willing choices may still determine the soul's final destination.

---

#### Rest Beyond Recall

You perform an ancient rite intended to protect the dead from all but the greatest disturbances.

**Effects**
- The prepared remains gain powerful resistance against necromancy, possession and desecration.
- Creatures attempting to disturb the burial are immediately recognised by the rite's spiritual protections.
- Gain Advantage when identifying or opposing magic affecting the resting place.
- This rite does not prevent resurrection when the soul is willing and the magic is sufficiently powerful.

---

#### Voice of the Ancestors

Your mastery allows you to interpret messages carried through ancestral rites.

**Effects**
- During a properly prepared ceremony, you may seek guidance from honoured ancestral spirits.
- The response may appear through dreams, signs, memories or symbolic events.
- Gain Advantage when determining whether an ancestral message is genuine.
- The ancestors are not compelled to answer and may communicate indirectly.

---

#### Keeper of the Eternal Record

No soul entrusted to your rites is allowed to vanish from history.

**Effects**
- Create a complete spiritual and historical record of the deceased.
- Gain Advantage when restoring their identity after magical alteration, forgotten history or damaged records.
- Recognise attempts to erase the deceased's name, lineage or deeds through supernatural means.
- As long as the record survives, evidence of the deceased's existence cannot be completely removed by ordinary means.
