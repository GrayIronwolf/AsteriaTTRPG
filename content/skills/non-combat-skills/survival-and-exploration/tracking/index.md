# Tracking

## Overview

Tracking Skill Techniques represent specialised methods of finding, identifying and pursuing creatures through mastery of the **Tracking** skill.

A tracker learns to read the world as a trail of interconnected signs. A bent blade of grass, displaced stone, broken branch or lingering scent may reveal where a creature travelled, how quickly it moved and what condition it was in.

Novice trackers begin with visible footprints and obvious disturbances. As their mastery grows, they learn to follow trails across difficult terrain, distinguish individuals within a group, read the movement of prey through wind and water and pursue creatures without revealing their own presence.

Legendary trackers are said to follow fish through open oceans, identify travellers by the marks of their passing and find paths where no physical trail remains.

Whenever a character reaches a new **Tracking Skill Rank**, they may choose **one technique** from the four techniques available at that rank.

Once selected, a technique becomes part of the character's Tracking training unless it is replaced through retraining approved by the GM.

Tracking Techniques are **Skill Techniques**, not Talents. They do not normally have an inherent resource cost. Prolonged pursuit, extreme weather, dangerous terrain or travelling without rest may still cause Stamina loss or Exhaustion.

### Novice Techniques

> The Novice learns that a trail is more than footprints.  
> Every disturbed leaf, broken stem and misplaced stone tells part of the journey.

#### Footprint Reader

You can identify and follow clear footprints left across suitable terrain.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when following visible footprints across soil, mud, sand or snow.
- Estimate the general size and weight of the creature that left the tracks.
- Determine the direction in which the creature travelled.
- Recognise when footprints have been deliberately altered or partially concealed.
- Heavy weather or crowded terrain may still obscure the trail.

---

#### Broken Trail

You recognise minor disturbances created by creatures moving through the environment.

**Effects**
- Identify broken branches, crushed vegetation and displaced stones.
- Gain Advantage when tracking through forests, grasslands or overgrown terrain.
- Determine whether the creature was moving carefully or forcing its way through.
- Recognise obvious signs that several creatures followed the same path.

---

#### Recent Passage

You can estimate how recently a visible trail was created.

**Effects**
- Determine whether tracks are fresh, recent or old.
- Gain Advantage when comparing overlapping trails of different ages.
- Recognise whether weather affected the trail before or after it was made.
- Particularly stable or unusual environments may make precise estimates difficult.

---

#### Trail Marker

You leave subtle signs that help you retrace your own route.

**Effects**
- Create natural markers that are difficult for an untrained observer to notice.
- Gain Advantage when returning along a route you previously marked.
- Allies taught your markings can follow the same trail.
- Recognise when one of your markers has been disturbed or removed.

---

### Initiate Techniques

> The Initiate begins to recognise the habits behind the trail.  
> Tracks reveal not only where the creature went, but how it felt as it travelled.

#### Scent Trail

You can identify and follow noticeable scents left by creatures or materials.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when tracking a creature with a strong or familiar scent.
- Recognise blood, smoke, sweat and common animal odours along a trail.
- Determine whether a scent is becoming stronger or weaker.
- Rain, strong wind and overpowering smells may disrupt the trail.
- This technique does not grant supernatural smell.

---

#### Gait Analysis

You study the spacing and depth of tracks to understand how a creature moved.

**Effects**
- Determine whether the creature was walking, running, limping or carrying additional weight.
- Gain Advantage when identifying injuries from a creature's tracks.
- Recognise sudden changes in speed or direction.
- Estimate whether the creature was calm, cautious or fleeing.

---

#### Trail Separation

You can distinguish one trail from several overlapping sets of tracks.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when following a chosen creature through a travelled area.
- Separate tracks based on size, stride, footwear or physical pattern.
- Recognise when another creature joined or left the group.
- Extremely crowded environments may still conceal individual trails.

---

#### Wind Reader

You understand how wind carries scent, sound and signs of your approach.

**Effects**
- Determine whether you are upwind or downwind of the creature being tracked.
- Gain Advantage when choosing an approach that reduces the chance of being scented.
- Recognise sudden wind changes that may expose your position.
- Estimate the direction from which smoke, blood or strong natural scents originate.

---

### Apprentice Techniques

> The Apprentice no longer follows signs individually.  
> Every track becomes part of a larger story written across the land.

#### Mark of the Prey

You focus your senses upon a particular creature or trail.

**Effects**
- After examining a creature's tracks, scent or physical evidence, mark it as your prey.
- Gain Advantage when attempting to follow that creature's trail.
- Recognise new signs belonging to the marked creature more easily.
- The mark ends when you abandon the pursuit or mark another creature.
- Mark of the Prey does not reveal the creature's exact location.

---

#### Hidden Passage

You recognise when a trail leads toward a concealed route or entrance.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when tracking a creature toward hidden paths, dens or concealed doors.
- Identify repeated traffic around an apparently ordinary location.
- Recognise vegetation, dust or debris disturbed by secret passage.
- This technique reveals signs of use, not the mechanism required to open an entrance.

---

#### Blood Trail

You can follow traces left by an injured creature.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when following blood, damaged tissue or signs of injury.
- Estimate the general severity of the creature's wounds.
- Determine whether bleeding is increasing, slowing or has stopped.
- Recognise attempts to conceal or create a false blood trail.
- Creatures that do not bleed may be immune to this technique.

---

#### Counter-Tracking

You recognise techniques used to conceal, confuse or falsify a trail.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when following a creature attempting to hide its passage.
- Identify doubled-back trails, false footprints and deliberately disturbed terrain.
- Recognise when several creatures attempted to disguise their numbers.
- Reduce penalties caused by ordinary attempts to cover tracks.

---

### Journeyman Techniques

> The Journeyman reads the trail with calm certainty.  
> A single broken thread or displaced grain of soil may reveal an entire journey.

#### Environmental Tracker

You adapt your tracking methods to different landscapes.

**Effects**
- Reduce penalties when tracking through rocky ground, shallow water, urban terrain or other difficult surfaces.
- Gain Advantage when identifying signs unique to the current environment.
- Recognise when the target changed its behaviour to suit the terrain.
- Completely trackless magical movement may still leave no physical evidence.

---

#### Silent Pursuit

You have learned to follow prey without revealing your own approach.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage on Stealth checks made while following a known trail.
- Reduce noise caused by moving through vegetation or loose terrain.
- You are less likely to leave obvious signs of your own pursuit.
- Moving at full speed or wearing unsuitable equipment may remove this benefit.

---

#### Behavioural Pattern

You can predict where a creature is likely to travel based on its habits.

**Effects**
- Determine likely destinations such as water, shelter, feeding grounds or nesting sites.
- Gain Advantage when attempting to intercept rather than directly follow the creature.
- Recognise whether the creature is patrolling, hunting, fleeing or returning home.
- Unfamiliar intelligent creatures may intentionally behave unpredictably.

---

#### Group Assessment

You can reconstruct the movement of a travelling group.

**Effects**
- Estimate the number and approximate size of creatures in the group.
- Identify whether the group contained mounts, prisoners, wounded members or heavy cargo.
- Gain Advantage when determining the group's marching order.
- Recognise where members separated from or rejoined the group.

---

### Adept Techniques

> The Adept follows more than movement.  
> They perceive intention within the trail and sense where the hunted creature believes itself safe.

#### Trail Through Water

You can follow signs left along waterways, coastlines and shallow bodies of water.

**Effects**
- Gain Advantage when tracking creatures through streams, marshes or shorelines.
- Recognise disturbed sediment, broken water plants and displaced debris.
- Determine where a creature entered or left the water.
- Estimate the direction of travel from currents and shoreline signs.
- Open deep water remains extremely difficult to track without additional evidence.

---

#### Unseen Hunter

Your pursuit is concealed from the creature you track.

**Effects**
- Creatures you are actively tracking suffer Disadvantage when attempting to detect your trail.
- Gain Advantage when hiding evidence of your resting places and route.
- Recognise when the creature has become aware that it is being pursued.
- This technique does not make you invisible or conceal careless actions.

---

#### Creature Signature

You memorise the unique pattern left by a specific creature.

**Effects**
- After studying sufficient evidence, distinguish that individual from others of the same species.
- Recognise its footprints, stride, scent and recurring behaviours.
- Gain Advantage when determining whether it passed through a location.
- Significant transformation or deliberate magical disguise may alter the signature.

---

#### Terrain Memory

You form a detailed mental map of the terrain crossed during a pursuit.

**Effects**
- Recall major landmarks, hazards and trail intersections along the route.
- Gain Advantage when retracing the pursuit without visible markers.
- Recognise when you have entered an area crossed earlier.
- Reduce the chance of becoming lost while actively tracking.

---

### Master Techniques

> The Master's eyes see a trail where others see untouched wilderness.  
> Even absence becomes evidence when they know what should have been present.

#### Ghost Trail

You can follow trails that have been almost completely erased.

**Effects**
- Reduce penalties caused by old age, weather and deliberate concealment.
- Gain Advantage when reconstructing a trail from scattered surviving evidence.
- Recognise where signs have been unnaturally removed.
- The technique cannot create evidence where none ever existed.

---

#### Predictive Pursuit

You understand the target well enough to anticipate its future movements.

**Effects**
- After studying the trail, identify the creature's most likely next destination.
- Gain Advantage when preparing an ambush or interception along that route.
- Determine whether the creature is attempting to lure followers into danger.
- The prediction may fail if the creature receives new information or changes its goal.

---

#### Trace of Magic

You recognise physical evidence left by magical movement and supernatural creatures.

**Effects**
- Identify unnatural burns, frost, shadows, residue or environmental distortions along a trail.
- Gain Advantage when following creatures whose movement affects the environment magically.
- Determine whether teleportation, transformation or magical concealment interrupted the physical trail.
- Following the magical effect itself may require Arcana or another appropriate skill.

---

#### Relentless Tracker

You maintain a pursuit across extreme distance and difficult conditions.

**Effects**
- Reduce penalties caused by prolonged tracking.
- Gain Advantage when resuming a trail after resting.
- Retain important details about the trail for an extended period.
- Ordinary delays do not cause you to immediately lose a previously established trail.
- The character still requires food, sleep and protection from environmental hazards.

---

### Grandmaster Techniques

> None shall escape the Grandmaster Tracker.  
> They can find constellations in daylight and follow the memory of a creature across the world.

#### Trail Without Tracks

You can pursue a creature through the indirect consequences of its passage.

**Effects**
- Follow changes in wildlife, disturbed routines, missing supplies and witness reports.
- Reconstruct a route even when no direct footprints or scent remain.
- Gain Advantage when connecting scattered evidence across several locations.
- The technique requires that the creature interacted with the world in some detectable way.

---

#### World Hunter

You can maintain a pursuit across entire regions and changing environments.

**Effects**
- A trail does not automatically end when it crosses into a new terrain type.
- Retain your understanding of the target through forests, mountains, settlements and waterways.
- Gain Advantage when locating where a lost regional trail is likely to resume.
- Long-distance magical transportation may still require new evidence at the destination.

---

#### Perfect Quarry

Your understanding of marked prey reaches legendary precision.

**Effects**
- Mark of the Prey remains active until you willingly dismiss it or mark another creature.
- Gain Advantage on all Tracking checks involving the marked creature.
- Recognise deliberate false trails made by the marked creature.
- Once per pursuit, reroll a failed Tracking check and use the new result.
- This technique does not reveal the creature's location through solid barriers or across planes.

---

#### Echo of Passage

You can reconstruct past movement from the faintest surviving signs.

**Effects**
- Examine a location to determine how creatures moved through it.
- Estimate their number, direction, pace and general physical condition.
- Identify important actions performed during their passage, such as fighting, resting or carrying something away.
- Gain Advantage when reconstructing events from incomplete environmental evidence.
- Powerful magic that erases history may prevent the reconstruction.

---
