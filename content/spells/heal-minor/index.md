## Description

Heal channels Life Magic into a living creature, restoring damaged tissue and replenishing lost vitality.

The Minor form of Heal channels a stronger concentration of Life Magic than Weak Heal, allowing the caster to restore more serious wounds and injuries through direct physical contact with the target.

- **Status Effect:** Healing
- **Target:** 1 Living Creature
- **Area of Effect:** None
- **Saving Throw / Check:** None
- **Healing:** 2D10

The target immediately restores the listed amount of **HP** when the spell is successfully cast.

Heal cannot restore HP beyond the target's **Maximum HP**.

# Enchantment

## Enchantment Description & Effects

**Heal — Minor** can be permanently bound to a suitable item, allowing the wielder or wearer to channel Minor Heal through the enchanted item.

When activated, the enchantment reproduces the effects of the corresponding **Heal — Minor** spell.

The user spends the normal Mana Cost of Heal — Minor and must touch the intended creature with the enchanted item.

The target immediately receives the normal healing provided by the spell.

### Enchantment Requirements

- **Enchantment Rank:** Minor
- **Activation Type:** Mana Activated
- **Soul Stone Required:** Weak Soul Stone or higher
- **Powdered Crystal Required:** 1 Measure (100 g)
- **Enchanting Time:** 20 minutes
- **Enchanting Mana Cost:** 2 × Heal — Minor casting Mana Cost

### Enchantment Properties

- Permanently binds Minor Heal to the enchanted item.
- Requires Mana to activate.
- Activation costs the same Mana as casting Heal — Minor normally.
- Does not use Charges.
- Restores the same amount of HP as Heal — Minor.
- Healing cannot exceed the target's Maximum HP.
- The enchanted item acts as the point through which the healing magic is channelled.

### Enchantment Limitations

- Requires physical contact with the target.
- Cannot heal beyond Maximum HP.
- Does not reduce the normal Mana Cost of Heal — Minor.
- Does not automatically remove diseases, poisons, curses, conditions, or other status effects.
- Does not regenerate missing body parts unless specifically stated by another effect.
- The enchantment is permanent once successfully applied.
