# Arcane Exchange

## Overview
Transmutation scope, safety, and value ceiling improve by rank.

## Rank 1
### Description

You have learned the first principles of Arcane Exchange, an ancient Artificer discipline based upon the law of equivalent exchange.

Through the use of mana, physical exertion, and soul catalysts, you may alter the structure of materials and convert them into related substances. While powerful, Arcane Exchange cannot create matter from nothing. Every exchange requires sacrifice, and the value of the resulting material can never exceed the value of the materials consumed.

At Rank 1, you can only safely transmute simple and common materials.

---

### Effects

You may perform a material transmutation through Arcane Exchange.

### Exchange Cost

Each exchange requires:

- 100 MP
- 25 SP
- Soul Stone 
- Target Materials

### Exchange Check

Make an **Wisdom Check**.

Gain:
+2 Arcane Exchange Bonus

## Rank 2
### Description

Your understanding of Arcane Exchange has expanded beyond simple material conversion.

You can now manipulate more advanced material structures, allowing the transmutation of uncommon substances and refined crafting resources. Through greater control of mana flow and soul catalysts, you can maintain the integrity of more complex materials during the exchange process.

While still bound by the laws of equivalent exchange, your ability to reshape matter has become significantly more versatile.

## Rank 3
### Description

Your mastery of Arcane Exchange now extends into the realm of rare materials.

You can manipulate complex material structures, refine exotic resources, and perform transmutations that many craftsmen consider impossible. At this level, the Artificer begins to understand that materials are merely different arrangements of the same fundamental building blocks.

Rare materials remain difficult to control, however, and even minor mistakes can trigger catastrophic transmutation failures.

The greater the value of the material, the greater the price demanded by the universe.

## Rank 5
### Description

You have mastered the ultimate principles of Arcane Exchange.

Legendary and Relic materials now fall within the limits of your understanding. Through overwhelming mana output, immense physical effort, and powerful soul catalysts, you can alter materials once believed impossible to transmute.

Few Artificers in history have reached this level.

Those who do become known as Grand Transmuters, individuals capable of reshaping entire industries through mastery of matter itself.

Yet even now, one law remains absolute.

Nothing is free.

Everything has a cost.
