# Arcane Reactor

## Overview
Reactor capacity, stability, and supported creations improve by rank.

## Rank 1
### Description

You have learned how to construct and integrate a basic Arcane Reactor.

An Arcane Reactor is a self-contained magical power core designed to generate, store, and regulate arcane energy. Unlike personal mana reserves, reactor energy is dedicated entirely to powering Artificer creations and advanced technologies.

The reactor may be installed within armour, a backpack unit, a construct harness, a prosthetic limb, or another suitable Artificer creation.

Though primitive by later standards, even a Basic Reactor dramatically increases an Artificer's ability to maintain inventions in the field.

## Rank 2
### Description

Your Arcane Reactor has undergone significant improvements in both efficiency and storage capacity.

Refined mana channels, improved crystal matrices, and enhanced containment systems allow the reactor to store substantially more energy while maintaining a stable output. Many Artificers begin relying on their reactor as the primary power source for their inventions at this stage.

The reactor no longer feels like an experiment.

It feels like an essential part of your craft.

## Rank 3
### Description

Your Arcane Reactor has become a highly efficient power core capable of sustaining advanced Artificer technologies for extended periods.

At this level, the reactor is no longer simply a backup energy source. It has become the heart of your inventions, supplying power to constructs, dimensional technologies, soul-forged creations, and advanced magical devices.

Many Artificers view Rank 3 as the point where their reactor becomes a true extension of themselves.

## Rank 4
### Description

Your Arcane Reactor has evolved into a superior power core capable of sustaining some of the most demanding Artificer technologies.

The reactor now produces enough energy to support multiple active systems simultaneously, allowing advanced constructs, dimensional technologies, soul-forged creations, and experimental devices to operate without constantly draining your personal mana reserves.

Few Artificers possess a reactor of this quality.

Fewer still understand how it truly works.

## Rank 5
### Description

You have perfected the art of Arcane Reactor design.

Your reactor is no longer merely a power source. It is a self-sustaining arcane engine capable of generating immense quantities of energy for your inventions, constructs, dimensional technologies, and soul-forged creations.

Many master Artificers spend decades attempting to create a reactor of this quality. Those who succeed often become capable of sustaining technologies that rival the magical infrastructure of entire kingdoms.

The reactor has become the heart of your craft.
