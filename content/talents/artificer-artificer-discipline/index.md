# Artificer Discipline

## Overview
Crafting-related D100 skill checks improve as the discipline ranks up.

## Rank 1
### Description

Through study, experimentation, and countless failures, you develop an instinctive understanding of creation itself.

Whether forging a weapon, brewing a potion, assembling a gadget, or constructing a mechanical servant, your growing expertise allows you to identify patterns and solutions that others overlook.

Artificer Discipline represents your mastery of the creative process and serves as the foundation of all Artificer talents.

---

### Effects

- Gain **+5** to all crafting-related **D100 Skill Checks**.
- Applies to:
  - Crafting
  - Smithing
  - Alchemy
  - Enchanting
  - Runecrafting
  - Salvaging
  - Construct Creation
  - Reverse Engineering
  - Item Analysis
  - Other Artificer crafting systems

---

### Lore

The first Artificers were not masters of a single craft.

They were students of every craft.

By studying how things were made, they discovered the hidden principles connecting all creation.

Over time, this understanding became known as **The Discipline**  the belief that anything can be understood, improved, and ultimately recreated.

---

> *"A craftsman learns a trade. An Artificer learns why the trade works."*  
>  **Arch-Artificer Valen Gearwright**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your understanding of creation continues to expand beyond individual crafts and disciplines.

As your experience grows, you begin to recognize common principles shared between alchemy, smithing, enchanting, and engineering. Mistakes become easier to identify, flaws become easier to correct, and solutions reveal themselves long before others would notice them.

The Discipline is no longer simply knowledge.

It is becoming instinct.

---

### Effects

- Gain **+10** to all crafting-related **D100 Skill Checks**.
- Applies to:
  - Crafting
  - Smithing
  - Alchemy
  - Enchanting
  - Runecrafting
  - Salvaging
  - Construct Creation
  - Reverse Engineering
  - Item Analysis
  - Arcane Engineering
  - Other Artificer crafting systems
- Reduce material waste during crafting by **5%**.
- Gain a greater understanding of crafted items, allowing easier identification of flaws, weaknesses, and improvements.

---

### Lore

Many craftsmen spend their entire lives mastering a single trade.

Artificers follow a different path.

By studying dozens of disciplines, they begin to see the invisible connections between them. A formula written in an alchemist's journal may reveal the answer to a smith's problem, while a runic inscription may hold the key to improving a construct's design.

The Discipline teaches that knowledge is never isolated.

Everything is connected.

---

> *"The greatest breakthrough is rarely a new idea. More often, it is recognizing how two old ideas fit together."*  
>  **Arch-Artificer Valen Gearwright**

## Rank 3
### Description

Years of experimentation have transformed knowledge into intuition.

You no longer approach a project as a collection of separate steps. Instead, you instinctively understand how materials, energy, enchantments, and mechanisms interact with one another.

At this stage, your creations begin to reflect your personal style as an Artificer. What once required careful planning now comes naturally, allowing you to identify opportunities, improvements, and solutions with remarkable speed.

The Discipline has become part of how you think.

---

### Effects

- Gain **+15** to all crafting-related **D100 Skill Checks**.
- Applies to:
  - Crafting
  - Smithing
  - Alchemy
  - Enchanting
  - Runecrafting
  - Salvaging
  - Construct Creation
  - Reverse Engineering
  - Item Analysis
  - Arcane Engineering
  - Other Artificer crafting systems
- Reduce material waste during crafting by **10%**.
- Increase the quality of crafted items by **one step** when achieving exceptional success (GM discretion).
- Gain an improved ability to identify weaknesses, hidden functions, and potential upgrades within crafted objects.

---

### Lore

The Third Discipline is often referred to as **The Maker's Eye**.

Master Artificers claim that after enough years of creation, they begin to see the world differently. Machines become collections of systems. Enchantments become patterns. Even ancient relics reveal clues about their purpose and construction.

To an outsider, it may appear as genius.

To an Artificer, it is simply understanding.

---

> *"Most people see an invention and ask what it does. An Artificer asks how it can be improved."*  
>  **Master Artificer Elric Stormforge**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your understanding of creation has reached a level few ever achieve.

The barriers between disciplines begin to disappear as you effortlessly combine principles from alchemy, engineering, enchantment, smithing, and countless other crafts. Complex projects that would challenge entire workshops can now be understood through observation and experience alone.

You no longer simply create objects.

You create systems.

---

### Effects

- Gain **+20** to all crafting-related **D100 Skill Checks**.
- Applies to:
  - Crafting
  - Smithing
  - Alchemy
  - Enchanting
  - Runecrafting
  - Salvaging
  - Construct Creation
  - Reverse Engineering
  - Item Analysis
  - Arcane Engineering
  - Other Artificer crafting systems
- Reduce material waste during crafting by **15%**.
- Gain increased success when analysing, repairing, or improving existing creations.
- Exceptional crafting successes may reveal new methods, schematics, or improvements at GM discretion.

---

### Lore

Among Artificers, Rank 4 is often called **The Architect's Discipline**.

At this stage, an Artificer ceases to think in terms of individual creations. Every invention becomes part of a larger design, every component part of a greater whole.

The greatest workshops of history were built by Artificers who reached this level of understanding. Their inventions shaped kingdoms, transformed industries, and changed the course of history itself.

---

> *"A novice builds an object. A master builds a masterpiece. An Artificer builds the future."*  
>  **Grand Architect Aurelius Voss**

## Rank 5
### Description

You have achieved the pinnacle of Artificer knowledge.

To you, creation is no longer a collection of crafts, techniques, or formulas. Every discipline exists as part of a single, interconnected truth. Smithing, alchemy, enchanting, engineering, and construction are merely different expressions of the same fundamental principles.

Where others see complexity, you see structure.

Where others see impossibility, you see a problem waiting to be solved.

The Discipline is no longer something you study.

It has become part of who you are.

---

### Effects

- Gain **+30** to all crafting-related **D100 Skill Checks**.
- Applies to:
  - Crafting
  - Smithing
  - Alchemy
  - Enchanting
  - Runecrafting
  - Salvaging
  - Construct Creation
  - Reverse Engineering
  - Item Analysis
  - Arcane Engineering
  - Schematic Mastery
  - Portable Construction
  - Other Artificer crafting systems
- Reduce material waste during crafting by **20%**.
- Gain exceptional insight when analysing, repairing, modifying, or creating crafted objects.
- Crafted items, constructs, gadgets, and inventions are more likely to achieve superior results when exceptional success is achieved.

---

### Lore

Only a handful of Artificers throughout history have ever reached the Fifth Discipline.

These legendary creators were responsible for wonders that reshaped the worldwalking cities, self-sustaining constructs, impossible machines, and relics whose secrets remain unsolved centuries later.

Their greatest achievement was not their inventions.

It was their understanding.

For they discovered a truth hidden beneath every forge, laboratory, workshop, and arcane tower:

Everything can be improved.

Everything can be rebuilt.

Everything can be understood.

---

> *"The universe itself is a machine. Learn its design, and nothing remains beyond your reach."*  
>  **High Artificer Aetheron, Creator of the Celestial Engine**
