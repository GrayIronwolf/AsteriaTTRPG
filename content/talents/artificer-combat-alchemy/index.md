# Combat Alchemy

## Overview
Combat alchemy effects improve by rank.

## Rank 1
### Description

An Artificer cannot always afford the time required for careful potion preparation.

Through extensive practice, you have learned how to rapidly deploy your own alchemical creations during combat. Whether consumed, thrown, or shattered, these potions can be activated in moments, allowing you to adapt to changing battlefield conditions without sacrificing your primary actions.

The process is fast, but imperfect.

A skilled Artificer knows that speed often comes at the cost of precision.

---

### Effects

- You may use **Combat Alchemy** as a **Bonus Action**.
- You may use this ability **3 times per Long Rest**.
- Each use costs:
  - **5 MP**
  - **5 SP**
- Only functions with **potions personally crafted by you**.
- You must make an **Alchemy Check** when using Combat Alchemy.
- The result determines the potion's effectiveness.
- Combat Alchemy may be used to:
  - Drink a potion.
  - Apply a potion to yourself.
  - Throw a potion at a target or location.
  - Smash a potion against a target, object, or surface.
- **Apply Potion may only target yourself.**
- Potions intended to affect allies, enemies, or an area must be thrown or smashed.
- Potions thrown using Combat Alchemy have:
  - **5m Normal Range**
  - **10m Maximum Range**
- Throws beyond 5m are made with Disadvantage.
- Throws beyond 10m automatically fail.

### Effectiveness

| Alchemy Check Result | Potion Effectiveness |
|---|---|
| Critical Failure | 50% Effect |
| Failure | 75% Effect |
| Success | 100% Effect |
| Critical Success | 125% Effect |

---

### Lore

Combat Alchemy emerged from necessity rather than theory.

During expeditions, sieges, and monster hunts, Artificers often discovered that traditional potion use was simply too slow. Through trial, error, and a remarkable number of explosions, they developed techniques allowing potions to be deployed almost instantly during combat.

Most failures were painful.

The successful experiments became Combat Alchemy.

---

> *"Anyone can drink a potion. Doing it while a monster is trying to eat you is where the skill comes in."*  
>  **Battle Alchemist Renn Vale**

While still limited to your own creations, you are beginning to understand how to balance speed with consistency.

### Lore

As Combat Alchemists gain experience, they begin to trust their instincts.

The precise measurements and careful timing taught in traditional alchemy give way to muscle memory, intuition, and battlefield experience. Potions are mixed, deployed, and activated almost instinctively, allowing the Artificer to react to threats with remarkable speed.

Veteran Combat Alchemists often claim they can judge a potion's behaviour before it even leaves their hand.

Your understanding of potion composition also allows you to safely utilize creations crafted by other skilled alchemists, greatly expanding your battlefield versatility.

### Lore

Combat Alchemy is often viewed as reckless by traditional alchemists.

Those who practice it know better.

The discipline requires an intimate understanding of ingredients, reactions, magical catalysts, and delivery methods. Experienced Combat Alchemists can identify weaknesses in a potion's formula at a glance and instinctively compensate for imperfections during deployment.

Many battlefield commanders value a skilled Combat Alchemist as highly as an entire squad of healers.

---

> *"A potion is only useful when it reaches the place it is needed."*  
>  **Master Combat Alchemist Veyra Flint**

### Lore

Only a handful of Artificers throughout history have ever achieved true mastery of Combat Alchemy.

These individuals could deploy potions with such speed and accuracy that witnesses often mistook their actions for spellcasting. Their mixtures detonated precisely where intended, their healing draughts were consumed at the perfect moment, and their battlefield adaptability became the stuff of legend.

Many famous Artificers are remembered not for the potions they created.

But for how they used them.

---

> *"A master alchemist brews the potion. A master combat alchemist decides when it changes the battle."*  
>  **Grand Combat Alchemist Veyra Flint**
