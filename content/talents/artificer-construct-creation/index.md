# Construct Creation

## Overview
Construct slots, complexity, and capability improve by rank.

## Rank 1
### Description

The pinnacle of Artificing is not the creation of tools.

It is the creation of workers.

Through the fusion of engineering, arcane circuitry, and artificial design, you learn how to create simple Constructs capable of performing basic tasks. While lacking true intelligence, these creations can follow instructions, carry materials, and assist with everyday work.

Every Construct is a testament to an Artificer's greatest ambition:

To create something that creates alongside them.

---

### Effects

- Unlocks the **Construct Creation** skill.
- You may create and maintain **1 Utility Construct Slot**.
- Basic Constructs can perform:
  - Carrying
  - Transporting materials
  - Simple labour
  - Basic assistance tasks
  - Workshop support
- Constructs require appropriate materials to build.
- Constructs remain active until destroyed, dismantled, or deactivated.

---

### Lore

The first Constructs were crude machines powered by simple enchantments and endless optimism.

Most failed spectacularly.

Some walked into walls.
Others wandered into rivers.
One famously spent three years attempting to organize a pile of rocks.

Yet despite these failures, Artificers persisted.

Because every great invention begins with a terrible first version.

---

> *"The goal was to build a helper. The fact it only carried buckets backwards was merely a temporary setback."*  
>  **Master Artificer Garrick Boltwright**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your understanding of artificial life continues to evolve.

No longer limited to a single creation, you learn how to manage multiple constructs simultaneously, coordinating their tasks and maintaining their operational stability. Your workshop begins to expand beyond your own hands, with loyal creations assisting wherever they are needed.

An Artificer can only accomplish so much alone.

Fortunately, you are no longer alone.

---

### Effects

- Gain **+1 Utility Construct Slot**.
- You may maintain up to **2 Utility Constructs** at the same time.
- Utility Constructs become more efficient at assigned tasks.
- Unlocks advanced Utility Construct designs.
- Utility Constructs may now perform:
  - Resource Gathering
  - Material Transport
  - Workshop Assistance
  - Salvaging Support
  - Basic Maintenance Tasks

---

### Lore

The first lesson of Construct Creation is learning how to build.

The second is learning how to delegate.

As Artificers refine their craft, they quickly discover that a well-designed construct can perform repetitive tasks tirelessly, allowing its creator to focus on more important work.

Many of the great Artificer workshops of history were operated not by a single inventor, but by dozens of carefully maintained constructs working together in perfect harmony.

---

> *"A true Artificer does not work harder than everyone else. They simply have more workers."*  
>  **Master Constructor Aldric Gearwright**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your mastery of Construct Engineering reaches a major milestone.

For the first time, your creations move beyond simple labour and support roles. Through reinforced frames, improved control systems, and enhanced power distribution, you learn how to create Constructs capable of defending themselves and their creator.

While utility remains the foundation of your craft, some creations are built for more demanding tasks.

After all, a workshop is only as safe as the constructs guarding it.

---

### Effects

- Gain **1 Combat Construct Slot**.
- You may maintain:
  - **2 Utility Construct Slots**
  - **1 Combat Construct Slot**
- Unlocks **Combat Constructs**.
- Combat Constructs may perform:
  - Guard Duties
  - Threat Detection
  - Defensive Actions
  - Basic Combat Engagement
  - Escort and Protection Tasks
- Unlocks advanced Construct designs and upgrades.

---

### Lore

The earliest Constructs were designed to carry tools, transport materials, and assist with everyday work.

Unfortunately, workshops tend to attract thieves.

As Artificers spread across Asteria, many began modifying their creations to act as guardians and protectors. What started as simple defensive measures soon evolved into an entirely new branch of Construct Engineering.

Some of the most famous Artificers in history became known not for what they built...

But for what stood beside them.

---

> *"A good construct helps you work. A great construct makes sure nobody interrupts you."*  
>  **Grand Constructor Varric Steelmind**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your expertise in Construct Engineering allows you to maintain an entire network of creations working in unison.

Your constructs are no longer isolated inventions performing individual tasks. They become a coordinated workforce capable of supporting your crafting, gathering resources, defending your allies, and maintaining your workshop without constant supervision.

A skilled Artificer builds tools.

A master Artificer builds a workforce.

---

### Effects

- Gain:
  - **+1 Utility Construct Slot**
  - **+1 Combat Construct Slot**
- You may maintain:
  - **3 Utility Construct Slots**
  - **2 Combat Construct Slots**
- Unlocks **Advanced Utility Constructs**.
- Unlocks **Advanced Combat Constructs**.
- Constructs may now perform more complex tasks requiring planning, coordination, and specialised functions.
- Existing Constructs become more reliable and efficient.

---

### Lore

The legendary workshops of Asteria were rarely staffed by apprentices.

Instead, they were maintained by armies of constructs carrying materials, repairing equipment, managing inventories, defending entrances, and assisting with research.

Visitors often described these workshops as living machines, every construct moving with purpose and precision toward a common goal.

To outsiders it appeared impossible.

To an Artificer, it was simply good engineering.

---

> *"The day your workshop can function without you is the day you truly become an Artificer."*  
>  **High Constructor Magnus Gearforge**

## Rank 5
### Description

You have achieved mastery over Construct Engineering.

Your creations are no longer simple tools, assistants, or guardians. They form a complete mechanical ecosystem capable of supporting every aspect of your work, from gathering resources and maintaining workshops to defending allies and carrying out complex assignments.

Each construct is an extension of your will, built upon years of innovation, experimentation, and refinement.

Where others see machines...

You see a workforce, an army, and a legacy.

---

### Effects

- Gain:
  - **+2 Utility Construct Slots**
  - **+1 Combat Construct Slot**
- You may maintain:
  - **5 Utility Construct Slots**
  - **3 Combat Construct Slots**
- Unlocks **Master Utility Constructs**.
- Unlocks **Master Combat Constructs**.
- Constructs may perform highly specialised tasks requiring advanced coordination and decision-making.
- Construct maintenance costs are reduced.
- Your constructs operate with exceptional reliability and efficiency.

---

### Lore

The greatest Artificers in history were never remembered for a single invention.

They were remembered for the systems they created.

Entire workshops operated without apprentices.
Fortresses were maintained without workers.
Remote settlements survived through the labour of loyal constructs that never tired, never complained, and never abandoned their purpose.

To many, these creations seemed alive.

To the Artificers who built them, they were something even greater.

Proof that ingenuity could outlast its creator.

---

> *"A construct is not a machine. It is a promise that your work will continue long after your hands have grown still."*  
>  **High Constructor Magnus Gearforge**
