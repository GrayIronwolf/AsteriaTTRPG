# Dimensional Warehouse

## Overview
Dimensional storage capacity and safety improve by rank.

## Rank 1
### Description

You have learned how to anchor a small extradimensional space to reality.

By creating a temporary dimensional gateway, you may access a private storage chamber existing outside the normal world. This chamber serves as a secure repository for valuable materials, equipment, resources, and treasures.

At Rank 1, the warehouse is little more than a dimensional shack, but it represents the foundation of a far greater storage network.

Only the creator may access the warehouse.

The door appears where you choose, taking a form influenced by your personality, magical style, and crafting methods.

---

### Effects

You gain access to a personal extradimensional storage space known as a **Dimensional Warehouse**.

### Activation

- Spend **100 MP**.
- Create a dimensional doorway within **3m**.
- The doorway remains open for **10 Minutes**.
- The doorway may be dismissed early at any time.
- The warehouse persists permanently between uses.

## Rank 2

While still inaccessible to others, the warehouse has become far more practical for an active Artificer.

## Rank 3
### Description

Your Dimensional Warehouse has evolved into a true extradimensional storage facility.

The warehouse has expanded dramatically in both size and stability, allowing trusted individuals to access the space under your authorization. Your connection to the warehouse has also deepened, granting an intuitive awareness of everything stored within.

For many Artificers, this marks the point where their warehouse becomes an essential part of their daily operations.

No longer merely a hidden storage room, it has become a personal vault beyond the reach of ordinary thieves.
