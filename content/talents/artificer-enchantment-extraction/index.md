# Enchantment Extraction

## Overview
Extraction safety and quality improve by rank.

## Rank 1
### Description

You have learned the dangerous art of dismantling magical enchantments to uncover the secrets hidden within them.

By flooding an enchanted item with mana and carefully destabilising its enchantment matrix, you may extract fragments of magical knowledge before the enchantment collapses. The process is destructive, unpredictable, and often dangerous.

Whether successful or not, the enchanted item is permanently destroyed.

Only enchantments associated with magical elements known to the Artificer can be studied in this manner.

---

### Effects

- Spend **50 MP** to attempt an **Enchantment Extraction**.
- The enchanted item is **destroyed regardless of success or failure**.
- You may only attempt to extract enchantments associated with magical elements you know.
- Artificers normally know a maximum of **3 magical elements**.
- Make an **Enchantment Extraction Roll**.
- Gain **+2** to all Enchantment Extraction Rolls.
- On success:
  - Gain **1 Knowledge Fragment** for the extracted enchantment.
- On Critical Success:
  - Gain **2 Knowledge Fragments** for the extracted enchantment.
- Once the required number of Knowledge Fragments has been collected, the enchantment is permanently learned.
- Learned enchantments may later be used through the Enchanting Profession system.

### Lore

Most Enchanters learn through study.

Artificers often learn through experimentation.

Enchantment Extraction was originally developed by Artificers frustrated by the slow pace of traditional magical education. By sacrificing enchanted items, they discovered they could study enchantment patterns directly from the source.

The process remains controversial among magical scholars, who view the destruction of enchanted items as reckless and wasteful.

Artificers simply call it efficient.

---

> *"A broken enchantment still has something to teach. The question is whether you're willing to pay the price."*  
>  **Rune-Savant Elric Voss**

### Lore

As Artificers gain experience with Enchantment Extraction, they begin to recognise recurring patterns within magical constructs.

Runes, enchantment anchors, mana pathways, and elemental signatures all leave clues behind. By studying these patterns, skilled Artificers can improve their chances of recovering useful knowledge before the enchantment destabilises.

Unfortunately, understanding an enchantment and surviving its destruction are often two very different skills.

---

> *"The enchantment always leaves clues. Whether you notice them before the explosion is another matter entirely."*  
>  **Rune-Savant Elric Voss**

Few magical scholars willingly destroy valuable enchanted items in pursuit of knowledge.

Of course, every failed extraction still tends to explode.

### Lore

By Rank 4, Enchantment Extraction has become more than experimentation.

It has become a discipline.

Experienced Artificers learn to recognise repeating magical patterns, elemental harmonics, and enchantment anchors hidden within even the most advanced magical creations. While traditional enchanters spend years studying theory, extractors learn by dismantling the work of others and uncovering the principles hidden inside.

The cost is often high.

The knowledge gained is often worth it.

---

> *"Every enchantment contains a secret. Extraction merely forces it to confess."*

### Lore

Only a handful of Artificers throughout history have ever mastered Enchantment Extraction.

These individuals became feared, respected, and often controversial figures within magical communities. Their workshops contained the remnants of countless enchanted items sacrificed in pursuit of understanding. Through destruction they uncovered knowledge that others spent lifetimes searching for.

Many legendary enchantments known today survive only because a master extractor was willing to risk everything to learn them.

Some discoveries changed kingdoms.

Others changed the world.
