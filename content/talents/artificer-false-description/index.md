# False Description

## Overview
Improves crafted misdirection, disguised functions, and misleading item reads.

## Rank 1
### Description

Not every creation should reveal its secrets.

Through the use of false markings, hidden runes, and subtle magical manipulation, you can alter how an item appears to others. An ordinary ring may seem worthless, a powerful relic may appear mundane, or a dangerous artifact may masquerade as something entirely harmless.

To most observers, the false description appears genuine.

Only those with sharp instincts can see through the deception.

---

### Effects

- You may apply a **False Description** to an item.
- Applying a False Description costs **5 Mana**.
- You may alter:
  - Item Name
  - Item Description
  - Apparent Rarity
  - Apparent Purpose
  - Apparent Magical Properties
- Creatures attempting to discern the item's true nature must succeed on a **DC 12 Wisdom Check**.
- On a failed check, the creature believes the False Description is genuine.
- On a successful check, the creature senses the deception and may investigate further.
- Only one False Description may be active on an item at a time.

---

### Lore

Many Artificers learn that protecting an invention is often more important than creating it.

Throughout history, master craftsmen concealed priceless relics beneath the appearance of common trinkets, protecting their work from thieves, rivals, and collectors. Some artifacts remained hidden for centuries simply because nobody believed they were worth examining.

After all, the greatest disguise is not invisibility.

It is being overlooked.

---

> *"The easiest way to hide something valuable is to convince everyone it isn't."*  
>  **Master Artificer Silas Veilgear**

Simple disguises evolve into carefully crafted deceptions. False enchantments, fabricated histories, and misleading magical signatures can now be woven into an item, making it increasingly difficult for others to determine its true purpose.

The best lies contain just enough truth to be believable.

As the art of False Description matured, Artificers began using it for more than simple protection.

Merchants disguised priceless artifacts as common curiosities. Explorers concealed dangerous relics within mundane containers. Some inventors even hid unfinished prototypes from rival craftsmen by making them appear broken or worthless.

The greatest deception is not hiding an item.

At this level, your greatest creations can hide in plain sight.
