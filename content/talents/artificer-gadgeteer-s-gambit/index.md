# Gadgeteer's Gambit

## Overview
Adds stronger and more flexible mechanical effects to crafted items by rank.

## Rank 1
### Description

Not every invention needs to be revolutionary.

Through experimentation and practical engineering, you learn how to embed simple mechanical functions into your creations. Springs, switches, triggers, and timers can now be incorporated into crafted items, allowing them to perform actions beyond their original purpose.

While these modifications remain relatively simple, they mark the beginning of your journey into the art of Gadgeteering.

Sometimes the smallest mechanism can produce the greatest result.

---

### Effects

- Unlocks **Gadgeteering** as an Artificer crafting skill.
- You may add **1 Mechanical Effect** to a crafted item.
- Adding a Mechanical Effect costs **5 Mana**.
- Available Mechanical Effects:
  - Timer
  - Audible Signal
  - Visual Signal
  - Basic Trigger
  - Mechanical Switch

---

### Lore

The earliest gadgets were often dismissed as curiosities and toys.

Yet while master craftsmen focused on creating stronger blades and sturdier armour, Gadgeteers asked a different question:

*"What if it could do more?"*

From that simple question came hidden compartments, alarm systems, automated tools, and countless inventions that changed the world in ways no one expected.

---

> *"The difference between a tool and a gadget is imagination."*  
>  **Tinkermaster Brindle Cogspark**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your growing understanding of mechanical design allows you to create more sophisticated gadgets and mechanisms.

Simple triggers can now be combined into larger systems, allowing your creations to react to their environment in new and interesting ways. What once required direct interaction can now respond automatically through carefully designed triggers and hidden mechanisms.

The line between tool and invention begins to blur.

---

### Effects

- You may add **up to 2 Mechanical Effects** to a crafted item.
- Adding a Mechanical Effect costs **10 Mana**.
- Mechanical Effects may now be combined together within the same item.
- New Mechanical Effects Unlocked:
  - Pressure Trigger
  - Motion Trigger
  - Delayed Activation
  - Hidden Compartment
  - Mechanical Lock
- Previously unlocked effects remain available:
  - Timer
  - Audible Signal
  - Visual Signal
  - Basic Trigger
  - Mechanical Switch

---

### Lore

Most craftsmen create objects that perform a single purpose.

Gadgeteers see every creation as an opportunity.

A lock can become an alarm.
A toolbox can hide a secret compartment.
A lantern can signal allies from afar.

The greatest inventions are rarely the most powerful.

They are the ones nobody expected.

---

> *"Innovation begins the moment someone asks, 'What else can this do?'"*  
>  **Master Gadgeteer Tovik Brassbolt**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your gadgets have evolved beyond simple mechanisms and become interconnected systems.

By combining multiple triggers, relays, and activation methods, your creations can now perform increasingly complex tasks. A single invention may react to multiple conditions, activate other mechanisms, or respond to commands from its creator.

At this stage, your gadgets begin to feel less like crafted items and more like engineered solutions.

---

### Effects

- You may add **up to 3 Mechanical Effects** to a crafted item.
- Adding a Mechanical Effect costs **15 Mana**.
- Mechanical Effects may now activate other Mechanical Effects within the same item.
- New Mechanical Effects Unlocked:
  - Trigger Chain
  - Automatic Reset
  - Directional Trigger
  - Relay System
  - Remote Activation
- Previously unlocked effects remain available.

---

### Lore

The greatest breakthrough in Gadgeteering was not the invention of a new mechanism.

It was the discovery that mechanisms could work together.

A trigger could activate a relay.
A relay could activate a timer.
A timer could activate an entirely different device.

What began as a collection of moving parts slowly became something more.

A system.

---

> *"A single mechanism performs a task. A collection of mechanisms performs a miracle."*  
>  **Inventor-General Corwin Gearlock**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your understanding of mechanical systems approaches true mastery.

Individual gadgets are no longer isolated creations but components within larger designs. Triggers can be layered, conditions can be evaluated, and multiple mechanisms can work together to achieve increasingly sophisticated outcomes.

At this level, your inventions begin to display a form of mechanical intelligence, responding to circumstances through carefully designed logic and engineering.

What once required direct supervision can now function on its own.

---

### Effects

- You may add **up to 4 Mechanical Effects** to a crafted item.
- Adding a Mechanical Effect costs **20 Mana**.
- Mechanical Effects may be linked into complex activation sequences.
- New Mechanical Effects Unlocked:
  - Linked Devices
  - Conditional Trigger
  - Sequential Activation
  - Proximity Detection
  - Mechanical Counter
- Previously unlocked effects remain available.

---

### Lore

Many Artificers spend their lives building individual inventions.

Great Gadgeteers build systems.

A workshop alarm linked to hidden locks.
A pressure plate connected to a timed release.
A signal device that activates a chain of automated responses.

To an outsider these creations appear impossibly complex.

To a Gadgeteer, they are simply a collection of simple mechanisms working together toward a single purpose.

---

> *"Complexity is nothing more than simple ideas working together."*  
>  **Grand Mechanist Veyra Cogwright**

## Rank 5
# 

### Description

You have achieved mastery of Gadgeteering.

Your creations are no longer simple devices or even complex mechanisms. They are fully engineered systems capable of performing intricate tasks through interconnected triggers, responses, and automated functions.

At this level, a gadget can operate independently, react to changing conditions, and execute elaborate sequences without intervention from its creator.

To others, your inventions appear almost magical.

To you, they are simply the logical conclusion of good design.

---

### Effects

- You may add **up to 5 Mechanical Effects** to a crafted item.
- Adding a Mechanical Effect costs **25 Mana**.
- Mechanical Effects may be combined into advanced mechanical systems.
- New Mechanical Effects Unlocked:
  - Autonomous Function
  - Multi-Stage Activation
  - Complex Trigger Network
  - Self-Maintaining Mechanism
  - Programmable Behaviour
- Previously unlocked effects remain available.

---

### Lore

The greatest Gadgeteers in history were often mistaken for powerful mages.

Their workshops operated without servants.
Their vaults defended themselves.
Their inventions performed tasks long after their creators had departed.

Yet no magic powered these marvels.

Only ingenuity.

The true mark of a master Gadgeteer is not the complexity of their creations, but the elegance with which countless mechanisms work together as one.

---

> *"Any sufficiently advanced machine is indistinguishable from magic to those who do not understand it."*  
>  **Arch-Gadgeteer Orion Brassheart**
