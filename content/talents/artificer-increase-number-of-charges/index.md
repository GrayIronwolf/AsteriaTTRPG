# Increase Number of Charges

## Overview
Increases the number of charges available to Artificer devices.

## Rank 1
### Description

Through careful study of arcane circuitry and magical conduits, you learn how to safely increase the amount of energy a creation can store.

Whether powering an enchanted weapon, an arcane tool, or a mechanical construct, your growing understanding of energy storage allows your creations to function longer before requiring replenishment.

Every great invention requires power.

The wise Artificer simply makes sure there is more of it available.

---

### Effects

- Increase the maximum charges of crafted items by **+1**.
- Reduce charge degradation by **10%**.
- Improve the stability of charged items.
- Applies to:
  - Enchanted Items
  - Gadgets
  - Constructs
  - Other charge-based creations

---

### Lore

The earliest Artificers often encountered the same frustrating problem.

Their inventions worked perfectly.

For a few minutes.

As magical engineering advanced, so too did the understanding of energy storage. New conduits, improved crystal matrices, and more efficient circuits allowed creations to store greater amounts of power than ever before.

The result changed Artificing forever.

---

> *"The difference between a failed invention and a successful one is usually a larger battery."*  
>  **Master Engineer Torrin Voltwright**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your understanding of arcane circuitry continues to improve, allowing you to store greater amounts of magical energy while reducing waste during use.

The conduits within your creations become more refined, enabling power to flow more efficiently through enchantments, mechanisms, and magical matrices. What once required frequent recharging can now operate for significantly longer periods.

A skilled Artificer does not simply create power.

They learn how to preserve it.

---

### Effects

- Increase the maximum charges of crafted items by **+2**.
- Reduce charge degradation by **20%**.
- Improve charge efficiency, reducing unnecessary energy loss.
- Charged items become more stable during prolonged use.
- Applies to:
  - Enchanted Items
  - Gadgets
  - Constructs
  - Other charge-based creations

---

### Lore

As Artificers refined their understanding of magical energy, they discovered that most power was never truly lost.

Instead, it leaked.

Tiny imperfections in runes, flawed conduits, and unstable crystal matrices slowly drained energy from even the finest creations.

The pioneers of Arcane Circuitry dedicated themselves to solving this problem, creating designs that could preserve magical power far beyond what was once thought possible.

Their innovations became the foundation of modern Artificing.

---

> *"Most inventions don't need more power. They need less waste."*  
>  **Master Engineer Torrin Voltwright**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your mastery of arcane circuitry reaches a new level, allowing your creations to store and regulate power with remarkable efficiency.

You begin to understand that energy is not simply something to be contained, but something to be guided. Through refined conduits, reinforced matrices, and advanced charge distribution systems, your inventions become capable of sustaining far greater workloads without suffering instability.

To lesser craftsmen, power is a resource.

To an Artificer, it is a system.

---

### Effects

- Increase the maximum charges of crafted items by **+3**.
- Reduce charge degradation by **25%**.
- Improve charge retention, allowing stored energy to remain stable for longer periods.
- Charged items become significantly more reliable during extended operation.
- Applies to:
  - Enchanted Items
  - Gadgets
  - Constructs
  - Other charge-based creations

---

### Lore

The Third Principle of Arcane Circuitry states:

*"Stored energy is wasted energy unless it can be controlled."*

As Artificers pushed the limits of magical engineering, they learned that increasing capacity alone was not enough. Greater reserves demanded greater control, and only those who mastered both could create inventions capable of enduring the test of time.

Many of the most famous Artificer relics still functioning today were built upon these principles, their power preserved through centuries of careful design.

---

> *"Anyone can store power. Mastery begins when you learn where it should flow."*  
>  **Arch-Artificer Helios Voltaris**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your understanding of arcane energy has evolved beyond simple storage and into complete system design.

The circuits, conduits, and matrices within your creations operate with exceptional efficiency, allowing them to sustain greater power loads while minimizing loss. Even complex devices can maintain stable energy reserves for extended periods, enabling longer operation and greater reliability.

At this level, your creations are no longer merely powered.

They are engineered for endurance.

---

### Effects

- Increase the maximum charges of crafted items by **+4**.
- Reduce charge degradation by **30%**.
- Greatly improve charge retention and energy stability.
- Charged items operate more efficiently during prolonged use.
- Applies to:
  - Enchanted Items
  - Gadgets
  - Constructs
  - Other charge-based creations

---

### Lore

The greatest challenge facing early Artificers was never generating power.

It was keeping it.

Many prototypes failed not because they lacked energy, but because they could not retain it long enough to perform their intended purpose. Through centuries of experimentation, new methods of containment were developed, transforming unstable inventions into reliable masterpieces.

The masters of Arcane Circuitry learned a simple truth:

The longer power remains available, the greater the possibilities become.

---

> *"A moment of power is impressive. A lifetime of power changes the world."*  
>  **Grand Engineer Veyron Arcasteel**

## Rank 5
### Description

You have achieved mastery over the flow and containment of arcane energy.

The limitations that once restricted lesser creations no longer apply to your designs. Through flawless circuitry, perfected conduits, and unparalleled understanding of magical power, your inventions can store immense reserves of energy while maintaining remarkable stability.

Where others see charges as a limitation, you see them as a foundation upon which greater creations can be built.

Your devices no longer struggle to contain power.

They are designed to embrace it.

---

### Effects

- Increase the maximum charges of crafted items by **+5**.
- Reduce charge degradation by **40%**.
- Greatly improve charge retention and long-term stability.
- Charged items maintain peak performance for significantly longer periods.
- Applies to:
  - Enchanted Items
  - Gadgets
  - Constructs
  - Other charge-based creations

---

### Lore

Only the greatest Artificers ever reach the Fifth Principle of Arcane Circuitry.

These masters understand that energy itself is neither finite nor infinite. What matters is the ability to store, direct, and preserve it without loss.

Many of the legendary relics scattered throughout Asteria owe their continued existence to Artificers who mastered this discipline. Centuries after their creators have faded into history, their inventions still function, their power reservoirs still intact.

Such creations stand as a testament to a simple truth:

A masterpiece is not measured by its power.

It is measured by how long that power endures.

---

> *"The mark of a true Artificer is not creating something powerful. It is creating something that remains powerful long after you are gone."*  
>  **High Artificer Aetheron, Master of the Eternal Engine**
