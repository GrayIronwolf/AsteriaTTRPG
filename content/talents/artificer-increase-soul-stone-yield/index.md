# Increase Soul Stone Yield

## Overview
Improves Soul Stone yield from eligible harvesting, extraction, and refining work.

## Rank 1
### Description

Through careful study of Soul Engineering, you learn how to preserve more essence during extraction.

While many fragments are normally lost during the harvesting process, your growing understanding allows you to stabilize and capture a greater portion of the energy left behind.

Where others see waste, you see opportunity.

---

### Effects

- Increase Soul Stone yield by **15%**.
- Reduce Soul Corruption chance by **10%**.
- Failed extractions produce **Soul Dust** instead of being completely lost.
- Unlocks the **Soul Engineering** crafting skill.

---

### Lore

Most Artificers avoid working with souls.

Not because it is impossible.

Because it is uncomfortable.

Every Soul Stone contains the remnants of something that once existed. The earliest Soul Engineers learned that preserving those remnants was far easier than creating new power from nothing.

Thus began one of the most controversial disciplines in Artificing.

---

> *"Energy never truly disappears. It simply waits to be collected."*  
>  **Soulforger Kaelis Voss**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your extraction methods become more refined, allowing you to preserve the integrity of harvested essence.

Soul fragments that would normally deteriorate can now be stabilized and processed into higher quality materials suitable for advanced crafting and energy applications.

You are no longer merely harvesting energy.

You are preserving it.

---

### Effects

- Increase Soul Stone yield by **25%**.
- Reduce Soul Corruption chance by **20%**.
- Chance to produce **Refined Soul Stones** during extraction.
- Failed extractions produce **Enhanced Soul Dust**.

---

### Lore

The difference between a crude Soul Stone and a refined one is measured in moments.

A fragment left unattended quickly degrades into unusable residue. Skilled Soul Engineers learn how to stabilize these fragments before that decay occurs, preserving both their value and their potential.

Many of the finest Artificer creations owe their existence to refined essence harvested by patient hands.

---

> *"Anyone can gather power. Preserving it requires skill."*  
>  **Soulforger Kaelis Voss**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your understanding of essence reaches a critical milestone.

Soul energy no longer appears as random fragments and unstable remnants. You begin to perceive patterns within the flow of essence itself, allowing you to harvest larger quantities while maintaining greater control over the final result.

Each extraction becomes more deliberate.

More efficient.

More purposeful.

---

### Effects

- Increase Soul Stone yield by **35%**.
- Reduce Soul Corruption chance by **25%**.
- Increased chance to produce **Refined Soul Stones**.
- Refined Soul Stones degrade more slowly than normal Soul Stones.

---

### Lore

Every Soul Engineer eventually learns a difficult lesson.

The soul is not a single thing.

It is countless fragments of memory, emotion, will, and energy woven together. Those who understand this truth learn to separate and preserve these components with remarkable precision.

Those who do not often discover why Soul Engineering remains feared.

---

> *"The soul is not fragile. Our understanding of it is."*  
>  **Arch-Soul Engineer Veradis Kain**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your mastery of Soul Engineering allows you to extract and stabilize essence with remarkable efficiency.

What was once a delicate process becomes routine as your understanding of soul structure deepens. Very little energy is wasted, and the fragments you recover possess exceptional stability and value.

You no longer harvest what remains.

You preserve what matters.

---

### Effects

- Increase Soul Stone yield by **45%**.
- Reduce Soul Corruption chance by **30%**.
- Refined Soul Stones become significantly more common.
- Soul Stones created by you degrade at a much slower rate.

---

### Lore

The greatest Soul Engineers are not remembered for how much essence they harvested.

They are remembered for how little they wasted.

Through discipline, patience, and an unwavering pursuit of understanding, they transformed a dangerous art into a respected science, laying the foundations for many of the soul-powered technologies used throughout Asteria today.

---

> *"Waste is the enemy of progress. Even a soul deserves better."*  
>  **Grand Soulforger Elowen Thricebound**

## Rank 5
### Description

You have mastered the art of Soul Engineering.

The extraction, stabilization, and preservation of essence have become second nature. Where others recover fragments, you recover nearly everything. The energy you harvest remains stable, useful, and remarkably efficient for crafting, powering constructs, and fueling advanced Artificer systems.

Very little is lost.

Almost everything is preserved.

---

### Effects

- Increase Soul Stone yield by **50%**.
- Reduce Soul Corruption chance by **35%**.
- Refined Soul Stones become your standard extraction result.
- Soul Stones created by you possess exceptional long-term stability.

---

### Lore

Only a handful of Artificers throughout history have achieved complete mastery over Soul Engineering.

Their workshops produced wonders powered by essence harvested centuries earlier. Their constructs endured long after kingdoms had fallen. Their understanding of the soul blurred the boundary between engineering, magic, and philosophy.

To them, a soul was not merely power.

It was potential.

---

> *"You call it a soul. I call it possibility waiting to be shaped."*  
>  **High Soulforger Aether Vhal**
