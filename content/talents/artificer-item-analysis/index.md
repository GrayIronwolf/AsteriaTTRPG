# Item Analysis

## Overview
Analysis accuracy and detail improve by rank.

## Rank 1
### Description

Every item leaves behind clues about its creation.

By channeling a small amount of mana into an object, you can examine its structure, craftsmanship, and magical signature. While your understanding is still developing, even a basic analysis can reveal valuable information hidden from ordinary observers.

For an Artificer, every object is a lesson waiting to be learned.

---

### Effects

- Unlocks the Item Analysis skill.
- Spend 5 MP to analyze a touched item.
- Reveals:
  - Item Name
  - Item Class
  - Item Quality
  - Basic Description
- Gain +2 to all Item Analysis skill checks.
- While focusing, Common items emit a faint glow visible only to you.
- Glowing items may still require an Item Analysis check to determine their properties.

---

### Lore

The first Artificers were explorers, scavengers, and inventors.

Before they could build wonders of their own, they first had to understand the wonders left behind by others. Through careful study they learned that every item contains traces of its maker, its purpose, and its history.

Those who learn to read those traces rarely remain ignorant for long.

---

> *"The difference between junk and treasure is usually knowledge."*  
>  **Archivist Tiber Voss**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your analytical abilities continue to improve, allowing you to identify finer details hidden within an item's construction.

Through practice and experience, you learn to recognise superior craftsmanship, material quality, and subtle signs of magical influence. Objects reveal more of their story to you, though their deepest secrets still remain concealed.

A skilled Artificer sees more than the item itself.

They see the hands that created it.

---

### Effects

- Spend **5 MP** to analyze a touched item.
- Reveals:
  - Item Name
  - Item Class
  - Item Quality
  - Basic Description
  - Material Composition
  - Estimated Value
- Gain **+4** to all **Item Analysis** skill checks.
- While focusing, **Uncommon** items emit a faint glow visible only to you.
- Glowing items still require an Item Analysis check to determine their properties.
- Improved ability to identify damaged, altered, or repaired items.

---

### Lore

As Artificers refine their craft, they begin to recognise details that others miss.

The quality of a forge.
The purity of a metal.
The signature techniques of a master craftsman.

To most people, an item is simply an object.

To an Artificer, it is evidence.

---

> *"Every tool leaves marks. Every creator leaves a signature."*  
>  **Archivist Tiber Voss**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your understanding of magical and mechanical craftsmanship has grown significantly.

No longer limited to identifying an item's physical characteristics, you can now detect the presence of enchantments, energy reserves, and hidden flaws within a creation. Through careful examination, you begin to uncover not only what an item is, but how it functions.

To a skilled Artificer, every object becomes an open book.

---

### Effects

- Spend **5 MP** to analyze a touched item.
- Reveals:
  - Item Name
  - Item Class
  - Item Quality
  - Basic Description
  - Material Composition
  - Estimated Value
  - Known Enchantments
  - Current Charges (if applicable)
- Gain **+6** to all **Item Analysis** skill checks.
- While focusing, **Unusual** items emit a faint glow visible only to you.
- Glowing items still require an Item Analysis check to determine their properties.
- Improved ability to identify:
  - Hidden flaws
  - Damaged enchantments
  - Missing components
  - Charge instability

---

### Lore

The Third Principle of Analysis states:

*"Every creation reveals its purpose to those who know where to look."*

As Artificers grow in experience, they learn to see beyond an item's surface. Scratches reveal usage, enchantments leave traces within the materials, and even the flow of stored mana can expose secrets hidden from ordinary observers.

Many master Artificers claim that by this stage, they can learn more from an item than from its owner.

---

> *"Objects rarely lie. People do."*  
>  **Master Analyst Corvin Runebinder**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your analytical expertise approaches mastery.

Few secrets remain hidden from your scrutiny. By examining an item's construction, enchantments, and arcane signatures, you can uncover details about its origins, purpose, and hidden functions. Even attempts to conceal an item's nature become increasingly difficult to maintain in your presence.

To an experienced Artificer, every item leaves a trail.

The challenge is simply following it.

---

### Effects

- Spend **5 MP** to analyze a touched item.
- Reveals:
  - Item Name
  - Item Class
  - Item Quality
  - Basic Description
  - Material Composition
  - Estimated Value
  - Known Enchantments
  - Current Charges
  - Creator (if known)
  - Origin (if known)
- Gain **+8** to all **Item Analysis** skill checks.
- While focusing, **Rare** and **Epic** items emit a faint glow visible only to you.
- Glowing items still require an Item Analysis check to determine their properties.
- Improved ability to identify:
  - Hidden Properties
  - Concealed Enchantments
  - Altered Items
  - False Descriptions
  - Signs of Tampering or Modification

---

### Lore

The greatest analysts rarely rely on luck.

They rely on observation.

Every tool mark, rune pattern, enchantment signature, and manufacturing technique leaves evidence behind. While others admire an item's appearance, Artificers learn to examine the countless details hidden beneath the surface.

Many famous relic hunters were once Artificers who discovered that the world's greatest treasures often reveal themselves long before they are understood.

---

> *"Every creation leaves fingerprints. The trick is learning how to see them."*  
>  **Grand Analyst Seraphine Gearwright**

## Rank 5
### Description

You have achieved mastery in the art of analysis.

A single touch can reveal details that would take scholars years to uncover. The structure of an item, the methods used to create it, the enchantments bound within it, and even attempts to conceal its true nature become increasingly clear beneath your scrutiny.

To you, every creation carries a history.

Every flaw tells a story.

Every secret leaves a trace.

---

### Effects

- Spend **5 MP** to analyze a touched item.
- Reveals:
  - Item Name
  - Item Class
  - Item Quality
  - Basic Description
  - Material Composition
  - Estimated Value
  - Known Enchantments
  - Current Charges
  - Creator (if known)
  - Origin (if known)
  - Hidden Properties
  - Concealed Enchantments
  - Item Condition
- Gain **+10** to all **Item Analysis** skill checks.
- While focusing, **Mythic** and **Legendary** items emit a faint glow visible only to you.
- Glowing items still require an Item Analysis check to determine their properties.
- Greatly improves your ability to identify:
  - False Descriptions
  - Tampered Items
  - Counterfeit Items
  - Soul-Bound Items
  - Modified Constructs
- Successful Item Analysis checks reveal significantly more information than lower ranks.

---

### Lore

Few Artificers ever reach this level of understanding.

The greatest analysts become living archives, capable of uncovering secrets hidden within relics, artifacts, and inventions long forgotten by history. Entire expeditions have been funded simply to place a mysterious object into the hands of a master analyst.

Many claim that the greatest Artificers do not create knowledge.

They uncover knowledge that was always there.

---

> *"Every item has a purpose. Every purpose leaves evidence. All evidence tells a story."*  
>  **Grand Archivist Aurelius Voss**
