# Portable Structure

## Overview
Portable structure size, deployment stability, and complexity improve by rank.

## Rank 1
### Description

You have mastered the first principles of Structural Compression.

By combining dimensional engineering, arcane transmutation, and spatial stabilization, you can compress an entire building into a compact portable form without damaging the structure itself.

Once compressed, the structure becomes a small object approximately 10cm  10cm in size, allowing it to be carried, transported, stored, or secured until needed.

The building remains fully intact while compressed and may be redeployed at any time provided sufficient space exists.

---

### Effects

You may convert a completed building into a **Portable Structure**.

### Compression Cost

Compressing a structure requires:

- 500 MP
- 100 SP
- 1 Measure of Resplendent Soul Stone Dust
- Physical contact with the structure

## Rank 5
### Description

You have achieved the pinnacle of Structural Compression.

The limitations of conventional architecture no longer apply to your understanding. Through advanced dimensional engineering, arcane stabilization, and reality-binding techniques, you can compress structures infused with powerful magical energies.

Among Artificers, this achievement is considered one of the greatest feats of engineering ever conceived.
