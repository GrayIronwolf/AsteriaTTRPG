# Quick Assembly

## Overview
Reduces setup time and improves field assembly options by rank.

## Rank 1
### Description

An Artificer does not always have the luxury of a workshop.

Through practice and experience, you learn to rapidly assemble simple gadgets in the middle of combat, using whatever materials and components you have available.

These creations are functional rather than elegant, allowing you to adapt to changing situations without preparation.

When survival depends on innovation, speed matters more than perfection.

---

### Effects

- Requires **2 Actions** to use.
- Spend **10 MP** and **10 SP**.
- Instantly assemble **1 Basic Gadget**.
- Requires appropriate materials and components.
- Quick-Assembled Gadgets remain functional for 10 Minutes.  
- When the duration expires, the gadget falls apart and becomes Scrap Materials.
- Cannot create Constructs.
- Cannot add Enchantments.

---

### Lore

The technique known as Quick Assembly was developed by battlefield engineers who often found themselves needing solutions immediately rather than hours later.

What began as a desperate survival technique eventually became a respected Artificer discipline.

Many lives have been saved by a gadget assembled seconds before it was needed.

---

> *"A good engineer plans ahead. A great engineer builds the solution while the problem is happening."*  
>  **Field Engineer Varric Sparks**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your field engineering skills continue to improve, allowing you to assemble more sophisticated gadgets under pressure.

Years of tinkering and experimentation have taught you how to streamline construction processes, letting you rapidly combine components into useful devices even in the middle of combat. While these creations remain temporary, they are significantly more reliable than your earliest attempts.

A prepared Artificer carries tools.

A skilled Artificer carries solutions.

---

### Effects

- Requires **2 Actions** to use.
- Spend **15 MP** and **10 SP**.
- Instantly assemble **1 Advanced Gadget**.
- Requires appropriate materials and components.
- Quick-Assembled Gadgets remain functional for **30 Minutes**.
- When the duration expires, the gadget becomes unstable and breaks apart into Scrap Materials.
- May create gadgets containing:
  - Basic Triggers
  - Timers
  - Mechanical Switches
  - Pressure Triggers
  - Motion Triggers
  - Hidden Compartments
- Cannot create Constructs.
- Cannot add Enchantments.

---

### Lore

The earliest practitioners of Quick Assembly quickly learned that speed alone was not enough.

A poorly assembled gadget may function for a few moments, but true field engineers learned how to balance efficiency with reliability. Through countless failures, broken devices, and near-disasters, they refined techniques that allowed increasingly complex creations to be assembled in mere seconds.

Many Artificers consider this stage the true beginning of battlefield engineering.

---

> *"Anyone can build something quickly. The challenge is making sure it still works when you need it."*  
>  **Field Engineer Varric Sparks**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your mastery of field engineering allows you to combine mechanical design with basic enchantment techniques.

No longer limited to purely mechanical creations, you can rapidly infuse assembled gadgets with temporary magical effects. While these enchantments lack the permanence and refinement of workshop-crafted items, they provide invaluable versatility when time is short and danger is near.

For many Artificers, this is the point where invention becomes improvisation.

---

### Effects
- Requires **2 Actions** to use.
- Spend **20 MP** and **15 SP**.
- Instantly assemble **1 Advanced Gadget**.
- Requires appropriate materials and components.
- Quick-Assembled Gadgets remain functional for **1 Hour**.
- When the duration expires, the gadget becomes unstable and breaks apart into Scrap Materials.
- May apply **1 Minor Enchantment** during assembly.
- Available Minor Enchantments may include:
- May create gadgets containing:
  - Basic Triggers
  - Timers
  - Mechanical Switches
  - Pressure Triggers
  - Motion Triggers
  - Hidden Compartments
  - Trigger Chains
  - Relay Systems
- Cannot create Constructs.

---

### Lore

The invention of Enchanted Assembly revolutionised battlefield engineering.

Before its discovery, Artificers were forced to choose between speed and magical functionality. The breakthrough came when engineers learned how to weave simple enchantments directly into partially assembled devices, creating temporary but highly effective magical tools.

The enchantments rarely lasted long.

They only needed to last long enough.

---

> *"Permanent enchantments are for workshops. Temporary enchantments are for survivors."*  
>  **Battlefield Artificer Lyra Voltweaver**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your expertise in rapid fabrication has reached a remarkable level.

Complex gadgets that once required hours of careful assembly can now be constructed in moments, even amidst the chaos of battle. Your understanding of arcane engineering allows you to weave increasingly sophisticated enchantments into your creations without sacrificing stability.

While still temporary, your inventions begin to rival the quality of workshop-crafted equipment.

The battlefield itself becomes your workshop.

---

### Effects

- Requires **2 Actions** to use.
- Spend **25 MP** and **15 SP**.
- Instantly assemble **1 Advanced Gadget**.
- Requires appropriate materials and components.
- Quick-Assembled Gadgets remain functional for **8 Hours**.
- When the duration expires, the gadget becomes unstable and breaks apart into Scrap Materials.
- May apply **1 Moderate Enchantment** during assembly.
- Available Moderate Enchantments may include:
- May create **1 Basic Utility Construct** through Quick Assembly.
- Utility Constructs created this way share the 8-hour duration and collapse into Scrap Materials when the duration expires.
- May create gadgets containing:
  - Trigger Chains
  - Relay Systems
  - Remote Activation
  - Conditional Triggers
  - Linked Devices
  - Sequential Activation

---

### Lore

The greatest battlefield engineers discovered that preparation has limits.

No amount of planning can predict every challenge, every enemy, or every opportunity. Those who mastered Quick Assembly learned to create solutions in the moment, adapting their inventions to the circumstances before them.

Stories tell of Artificers constructing bridges during sieges, defensive barriers during ambushes, and automated assistants while under attack.

To most people, such feats seemed impossible.

To an Artificer, they were simply efficient.

---

> *"A workshop is useful. Knowing how to work without one is mastery."*  
>  **Master Engineer Arcturus Gearwright**

## Rank 5
### Description

You have mastered the art of battlefield engineering.

What once took hours at a workbench can now be accomplished in moments amidst the chaos of combat. Through unparalleled skill, precision, and understanding of magical engineering, you can rapidly assemble complex gadgets, temporary constructs, and powerful enchanted devices capable of altering the course of an encounter.

Your creations remain temporary, but for the brief time they exist they can rival the work of a fully equipped workshop.

Where others see a battlefield...

You see a construction site.

---

### Effects

- Requires **1 Action** to use.
- Spend **30 MP** and **20 SP**.
- Instantly assemble **1 Master Gadget**.
- Requires appropriate materials and components.
- Quick-Assembled creations remain functional for **24 Hours**.
- When the duration expires, the creation becomes unstable and collapses into Scrap Materials.
- May apply **1 Major Enchantment** during assembly.
- May create:
  - Advanced Gadgets
  - Master Gadgets
  - Utility Constructs
- Constructs created through Quick Assembly share the 24-hour duration and collapse into Scrap Materials when the duration expires.
- May utilize any unlocked Gadgeteer's Gambit mechanical effects during assembly.
- Quick-Assembled creations count toward your normal Construct and Gadget limits.

---

### Lore

Only a handful of Artificers throughout history have achieved mastery over Quick Assembly.

These engineers became legends not because of what they built, but because of when they built it.

Entire fortifications appeared overnight.
Defensive constructs emerged during sieges.
Arcane devices were assembled moments before they were needed.

Their workshops no longer existed within walls and buildings.

Their workshops travelled with them.

To witness a Master Artificer at work was to watch invention happen in real time.

---

> *"Give me one minute, a bag of parts, and a problem worth solving."*  
>  **High Artificer Magnus Gearforge**
