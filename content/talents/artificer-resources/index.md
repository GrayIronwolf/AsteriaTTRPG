# Resources

## Overview
Improves access to, recovery of, and efficient use of crafting resources.

## Rank 1
### Description

The Artificer learns to see value where others see junk.

Broken weapons, damaged constructs, discarded tools, and failed inventions all contain useful materials waiting to be reclaimed. Through careful dismantling and resource recovery, you learn how to recover components that would otherwise be lost.

Every ruined creation is simply the beginning of another.

---

### Effects

- Increase resource gathering efficiency by **15%**.
- Gain **1d4 Components** when salvaging valid targets.
- Reduce material loss during crafting by **10%**.
- Small chance to recover **Mana Dust** from magical items.
- Unlocks the **Salvaging** skill.

---

### Lore

Many craftsmen begin their work with raw materials.

Artificers often begin with scraps.

The ability to recover useful components from broken creations became one of the foundations of Artificing, allowing resourceful inventors to create wonders from what others discarded.

---

> *"Nothing is useless. You just haven't taken it apart yet."*  
>  **Scrapmaster Borin Coghand**v

## Rank 2
### Description

Your understanding of materials continues to grow, allowing you to recover resources with greater efficiency and precision.

What once appeared to be scrap now reveals hidden value. Through careful dismantling and processing, you learn how to separate useful components from worthless debris, preserving valuable materials that less experienced Artificers would overlook.

A skilled Artificer knows that resources are not found.

They are recovered.

---

### Effects

- Increase resource gathering efficiency by **25%**.
- Gain **1d6 Components** when salvaging valid targets.
- Reduce material loss during crafting by **20%**.
- Increased chance to recover **Mana Dust** from magical items.
- Chance to recover **Refined Components** during salvage.

---

### Lore

As Artificers gain experience, they begin to recognise a simple truth:

Most materials are not destroyed.

They are merely transformed.

A cracked blade still contains metal.
A broken construct still contains components.
Even failed inventions contain lessons worth preserving.

The difference between a novice and a master is often measured by how much they leave behind.

---

> *"The finest materials are rarely mined. They're recovered from the mistakes of yesterday."*  
>  **Scrapmaster Borin Coghand**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your ability to recover materials has become second nature.

Years of dismantling, sorting, and repurposing have taught you where value hides within even the most damaged creations. You instinctively identify useful components, recover materials with minimal waste, and uncover resources that others would never notice.

To you, every object has value.

The challenge is simply finding it.

---

### Effects

- Increase resource gathering efficiency by **35%**.
- Gain **1d6 + 1 Components** when salvaging valid targets.
- Reduce material loss during crafting by **25%**.
- Increased chance to recover **Refined Components**.
- Chance to recover **Rare Components** from advanced items and constructs.

---

### Lore

The greatest salvagers are not treasure hunters.

They are observers.

They see opportunity hidden within broken machines, discarded weapons, and forgotten ruins. Where others focus on what an object once was, Artificers focus on what it can become.

Every recovered component is a future invention waiting to happen.

---

> *"Most people search for resources. I prefer to let them come apart naturally."*  
>  **Master Salvager Rennik Geargrip**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your mastery of resource recovery borders on obsession.

Very little escapes your notice. Hidden compartments, rare alloys, damaged enchantments, and valuable components reveal themselves under your scrutiny. What others consider debris, you recognize as untapped potential.

Every salvage operation becomes an opportunity to strengthen future creations, ensuring that almost nothing goes to waste.

To an Artificer of your skill, resources are everywhere.

---

### Effects

- Increase resource gathering efficiency by **45%**.
- Gain **1d6 + 2 Components** when salvaging valid targets.
- Reduce material loss during crafting by **30%**.
- Refined Components become a common salvage result.
- Increased chance to recover **Rare Components** from advanced items, constructs, and magical devices.

---

### Lore

The workshops of master Artificers often appear cluttered to outsiders.

Boxes of gears.
Crates of broken tools.
Shelves filled with scraps and fragments.

Yet every piece has a purpose.

Over time, Artificers learn that the difference between junk and treasure is rarely the material itself.

It is knowing what to do with it.

---

> *"Waste is simply a resource waiting for a better engineer."*  
>  **Grand Salvager Thorne Irongear**

## Rank 5
### Description

You have achieved mastery over the recovery, preservation, and utilisation of materials.

To others, resources are finite. To you, they are part of an endless cycle of creation. Every broken construct, shattered weapon, discarded tool, and ruined machine represents an opportunity waiting to be reclaimed.

Your understanding of materials allows you to recover extraordinary value from even the most damaged creations, ensuring that almost nothing is ever truly wasted.

Where others see scrap...

You see supply.

---

### Effects

- Increase resource gathering efficiency by **50%**.
- Gain **1d8 + 2 Components** when salvaging valid targets.
- Reduce material loss during crafting by **35%**.
- Refined Components become a standard salvage result.
- High chance to recover **Rare Components** from advanced items, constructs, and magical devices.
- Salvaged materials retain exceptional quality and stability.

---

### Lore

The greatest Artificers are not remembered for what they gathered.

They are remembered for what they refused to waste.

Legend speaks of master workshops that operated for decades without purchasing raw materials, fueled entirely by recovered components, reclaimed metals, and repurposed inventions. Their owners understood a truth that few ever learn:

Creation and destruction are merely different stages of the same process.

A broken invention is not an ending.

It is the beginning of the next one.

---

> *"You see scrap. I see tomorrow's invention."*  
>  **Grand Salvager Thorne Irongear**
