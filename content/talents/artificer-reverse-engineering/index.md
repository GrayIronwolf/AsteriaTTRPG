# Reverse Engineering

## Overview
Reverse Engineering rolls and knowledge fragment recovery improve by rank.

## Rank 1
### Description

You have learned how to dismantle crafted objects and alchemical creations in order to understand how they were made.

By carefully deconstructing an item while flooding its internal structure with mana, you can uncover fragments of knowledge hidden within its design. Through repeated study and experimentation, these fragments can eventually be assembled into a complete schematic or formula.

The process is destructive.

Whether successful or not, the item is permanently destroyed.

To most craftsmen this would be considered wasteful.

To an Artificer, it is education.

---

### Effects

- Spend **50 MP** to attempt **Reverse Engineering**.
- The targeted item or potion is **destroyed regardless of success or failure**.
- Gain **+2** to all Reverse Engineering Rolls.
- On Success:
  - Gain **1 Knowledge Fragment**.
- On Critical Success:
  - Gain **2 Knowledge Fragments**.
- Once the required number of Knowledge Fragments has been collected, the schematic or formula is permanently learned.
- Learned schematics may be crafted through their respective crafting professions.
- Learned potion formulas may be brewed through the Alchemy profession.

### Eligible Targets

- Crafted Equipment
- Weapons
- Armour
- Tools
- Gadgets
- Constructs
- Potions
- Crafting Schematics
- Formula-Based Items

### Lore

Reverse Engineering was developed by Artificers who lacked access to formal schematics and formula archives.

Rather than spending years studying under masters, they learned by taking apart existing creations and discovering how they worked from the inside out.

The method is controversial among craftsmen and alchemists alike, as valuable items are often destroyed in the process.

Artificers rarely see this as a problem.

After all, every broken creation contains a lesson.

---

> *"Anyone can follow a blueprint. Understanding why it works is where true mastery begins."*  
>  **Master Engineer Tovik Brassbolt**

### Lore

Experienced Artificers understand that every crafted object tells a story.

Tool marks reveal techniques. Component placement reveals purpose. Material choices reveal intent. By studying these clues, a skilled Artificer can reconstruct the knowledge of another creator piece by piece.

Of course, sometimes the only way to truly understand something is to take it apart.

---

> *"The best way to learn how something works is to discover why it breaks."*  
>  **Master Engineer Tovik Brassbolt**

### Lore

At Rank 3, Reverse Engineering becomes a true discipline of discovery.

Many master craftsmen guard their methods fiercely, believing their secrets can only be learned through apprenticeship. Artificers disagree. Through careful analysis, dismantling, and experimentation, they prove that every creation contains clues left behind by its creator.

To a skilled Artificer, even the most complex invention can become a lesson.

The challenge is surviving long enough to learn it.

---

> *"Every masterpiece is a puzzle. Reverse Engineering is simply the art of finding the missing pieces."*  
>  **Master Engineer Tovik Brassbolt**

Only the most dedicated Artificers reach this level of understanding.

Master craftsmen often spend decades perfecting a single design, believing their secrets safe within the finished product. Rank 4 Reverse Engineers have learned otherwise. Through observation, experimentation, and countless destroyed creations, they can uncover principles that others spent lifetimes developing.

Many guilds actively discourage this practice.

Many Artificers consider that proof it works.
