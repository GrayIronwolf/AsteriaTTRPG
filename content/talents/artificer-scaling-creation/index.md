# Scaling Creation

## Overview
Allows creations to improve through investment, soul energy, or campaign progression.

## Rank 1
### Description

Most crafted items reach their peak the moment they are completed.

You have discovered a method to create items capable of growth.

By binding a soul into a carefully prepared vessel, you may create a **Scalable Item**, a creation capable of evolving over time as its power develops. The soul acts as an Evolution Core, providing the item with the ability to adapt and unlock new potential.

This process is dangerous.

Not every soul willingly accepts imprisonment.

A failed binding can awaken the soul within the item, creating an unpredictable relationship between creator, wielder, and creation.

---

### Effects

You may create **Scalable Items**.

A Scalable Item is a crafted item capable of gaining additional ranks after creation.

### Requirements

To create a Scalable Item, all of the following are required:

- Item must be **Unusual Item Class or higher**
- One **Luminous Soul Stone** (minimum)
- Appropriate crafting materials
- Relevant schematic
- Access to the required crafting profession
- Completion of a Soul Binding Ritual

### Creation Costs

During creation consume:

- 100 MP
- 1 Luminous Soul Stone
- Normal crafting materials

### Scaling Capacity

Rank 1 Scalable Items may possess:

- **2 Evolution Ranks**
- Base Item Rank + 1 additional Evolution Rank

Example:

```text
Evolution Rank I

## Rank 2
### Description

Despite your growing mastery, the soul remains an independent existence. Every binding still carries the risk of awakening something that was never meant to remain trapped.

## Rank 3
### Description

Your mastery of Soul Forging has advanced beyond simple item evolution.

You can now bind more powerful souls into your creations, allowing scalable items to develop deeper growth paths and unlock abilities impossible for ordinary crafted equipment. ## Rank 4
### Description

You have mastered the art of creating living evolutions.

At this level, the soul bound within a scalable item begins to exert a far greater influence over its growth and development. Powerful souls may unlock unique evolution branches unavailable to ordinary creations, causing no two scalable items to develop in exactly the same way.

Many master Artificers consider Rank 4 the point where a scalable item becomes a true companion rather than a tool.

The soul no longer merely fuels growth.

It helps shape it.

## Rank 5
### Description

You have mastered the pinnacle of Soul Forging.

Your creations are no longer simple items imbued with trapped souls. They are living legacies capable of growth, adaptation, and evolution far beyond the limits of ordinary craftsmanship.

At this level, the soul within the item becomes an active force in its development. Powerful souls can shape entire evolution paths, alter the item's abilities, and potentially awaken into fully sentient companions capable of influencing the world around them.

Each creation becomes unique.

Each creation develops its own story.

And some may eventually become legends.
