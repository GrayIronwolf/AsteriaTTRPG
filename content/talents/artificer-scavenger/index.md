# Scavenger

## Overview
Improves salvaging results and material recovery by rank.

## Rank 1
### Description

Years of salvaging and dismantling have taught you a simple truth:

Useful materials are everywhere.

You have developed an instinct for spotting overlooked components, valuable scrap, and reusable materials hidden within ruins, battlefields, workshops, and discarded objects.

Where others see debris, you see opportunity.

---

### Effects

- Gain **+10% Resources** when salvaging.
- Gain **+2** to Salvaging skill checks.
- Common salvageable objects emit a faint glow while focused.
- Small chance to recover additional Scrap Materials during salvaging.
- Can identify potential salvage sources within short range.

---

### Lore

Many Artificers begin their careers with little more than a toolbox and a pile of scrap.

Over time they learn that the world is filled with reusable materials hidden in plain sight. Broken weapons, damaged constructs, abandoned machinery, and forgotten ruins all contain valuable resources for those willing to search.

The best Artificers rarely waste anything.

---

> *"The difference between junk and resources is whether an Artificer has looked at it yet."*  
>  **Scrapmaster Borin Coghand**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your eye for valuable materials continues to sharpen.

Years of recovering parts and dismantling damaged equipment have trained you to spot resources others overlook. Valuable components reveal themselves amongst debris, and even heavily damaged items often contain something worth reclaiming.

To an experienced Scavenger, every ruin is a warehouse waiting to be explored.

---

### Effects

- Gain **+20% Resources** when salvaging.
- Gain **+4** to Salvaging skill checks.
- **Uncommon** salvageable objects emit a faint glow while focused.
- Increased chance to recover additional Scrap Materials.
- Chance to recover **Refined Components** when salvaging.
- Can identify potential salvage sources at a greater distance.

---

### Lore

As their skills improve, Scavengers begin to see patterns others miss.

A cracked rune plate may still hold residual power.
A broken construct may contain reusable gears.
A rusted weapon may contain valuable alloys hidden beneath the corrosion.

Many Artificers become wealthy not through what they create, but through what they recover.

---

> *"The world is filled with resources. Most people simply haven't learned how to find them."*  
>  **Scrapmaster Borin Coghand**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your scavenging instincts have become second nature.

A quick glance is often enough to identify valuable components hidden within wreckage, ruins, and discarded machinery. What others consider worthless debris now reveals opportunities for crafting, repairs, and future inventions.

You no longer search for resources.

You simply notice them.

---

### Effects

- Gain **+30% Resources** when salvaging.
- Gain **+6** to Salvaging skill checks.
- **Unusual** salvageable objects emit a faint glow while focused.
- Increased chance to recover **Refined Components**.
- Chance to recover **Rare Components** from advanced items, constructs, and magical devices.
- Salvaging operations recover additional materials with greater consistency.
- Can identify valuable salvage opportunities from a considerable distance.

---

### Lore

Master scavengers often describe their skill as a change in perspective.

Where others see a collapsed construct, they see gears, plating, arcane conduits, and reusable components. A ruined workshop becomes a stockpile of resources. A battlefield becomes a collection of future projects.

The world has not changed.

Only the way you see it.

---

> *"Every broken machine is simply a collection of spare parts waiting for a purpose."*  
>  **Master Scavenger Rennik Geargrip**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your understanding of resource recovery has reached an expert level.

Years spent exploring ruins, dismantling constructs, and salvaging forgotten technology have taught you how to spot valuable materials almost instantly. Hidden caches, rare components, and overlooked treasures reveal themselves to your trained eye.

What most people call scavenging has become something far more refined.

You are no longer searching for resources.

You are hunting for opportunities.

---

### Effects

- Gain **+40% Resources** when salvaging.
- Gain **+8** to Salvaging skill checks.
- **Rare** salvageable objects emit a faint glow while focused.
- Increased chance to recover **Rare Components**.
- Chance to recover **Epic Components** from advanced constructs, relic sites, and magical devices.
- Recover additional resources from successful salvaging attempts.
- Can identify valuable salvage opportunities from long distances.
- Reduced chance of damaging materials during salvaging.

---

### Lore

Many Scavengers spend their lives searching for treasure.

The greatest among them search for potential.

A rusted machine may contain a legendary alloy.
A ruined construct may house an intact power core.
A forgotten workshop may contain components worth more than the building itself.

The difference between fortune and failure often comes down to a single detail others failed to notice.

---

> *"The treasure was never hidden. Everyone else simply walked past it."*  
>  **Grand Scavenger Thorne Irongear**

## Rank 5
### Description

You have mastered the art of scavenging.

Years spent dismantling constructs, exploring forgotten ruins, and reclaiming the remains of lost civilizations have sharpened your instincts beyond those of ordinary craftsmen. Valuable materials seem to reveal themselves in your presence, while hidden components, rare resources, and forgotten treasures rarely escape your notice.

To others, the world is filled with discarded objects.

To you, it is filled with opportunities waiting to be reclaimed.

---

### Effects

- Gain **+50% Resources** when salvaging.
- Gain **+10** to Salvaging skill checks.
- **Epic, Mythic, and Legendary** salvageable objects emit a faint glow while focused.
- Greatly increased chance to recover **Rare Components**.
- Chance to recover **Epic Components** from advanced constructs, relic sites, magical devices, and legendary creations.
- Recover additional resources from successful salvaging attempts.
- Greatly reduce the chance of damaging materials during salvaging.
- Can identify valuable salvage opportunities from extreme distances.
- Salvaged materials retain exceptional quality and stability.

---

### Lore

The greatest Scavengers are often mistaken for treasure hunters.

The truth is far simpler.

They have merely learned how to see value where others do not.

Across Asteria, stories tell of Artificers who rebuilt workshops from battlefield scrap, forged legendary equipment from forgotten ruins, and uncovered treasures hidden in plain sight for centuries. Their secret was never luck.

It was perspective.

For a master Scavenger knows that nothing is truly worthless.

Only undiscovered.

---

> *"The world throws nothing away. Why should I?"*  
>  **Grand Scavenger Thorne Irongear**
