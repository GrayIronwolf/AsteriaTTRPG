# Soul Bond Object

## Overview
Soul-bound object stability and capability improve by rank.

## Rank 1
### Description

Through a specialized Soul Engineering ritual, you bind a crafted object to a living soul.

The ritual creates a permanent spiritual connection between the bonded individual and the item, allowing them to sense its condition and whereabouts regardless of distance. While the bond is initially weak, it establishes the foundation for more advanced Soul Engineering techniques.

Most Artificers reserve this process for creations they consider irreplaceable.

A Soul Bond is not ownership.

It is connection.

---

### Effects

- You may create a **Soul Bond** between a willing creature and a crafted item.
- The item must have been personally crafted by you.
- Creating a Soul Bond requires:
  - **25 MP**
  - **1 Measure of Powdered Crystal**
  - **1 Luminous Soul Stone** (Consumed)
- The bonded creature automatically knows:
  - Whether the item exists.
  - Whether the item is damaged or intact.
  - The general direction of the item.
- The bond remains active until:
  - The item is destroyed.
  - The bond is willingly broken.
  - The bonded creature dies.
- Rank 1 Soul Bonds may only be applied to:
  - Mundane Items
  - Non-Enchanted Equipment
  - Non-Enchanted Gadgets
  - Basic Crafted Objects
- Cannot be applied to:
  - Enchanted Items
  - Soul-Bound Items
  - Constructs
  - Relics

---

### Lore

The earliest Soul Engineers believed every crafted object carried a fragment of its creator's essence.

Over time, they discovered methods to strengthen this connection through ritual, Soul Stones, and crystalline catalysts. The result was the first Soul Bonda technique allowing an item and its bearer to remain spiritually connected long after the forge had cooled.

Though simple by modern standards, every advanced Soul Bond begins with this first connection.

---

> *"The strongest creations are not held in the hand. They are carried within the soul."*  
>  **Soulforger Kaelis Voss**

## Rank 2
### Description

Your Soul Bonds grow stronger as your understanding of Soul Engineering deepens.

The connection between bearer and item becomes more precise, allowing the bonded individual to sense not only the direction of the item but also its approximate distance. The bond itself also becomes stable enough to support simple enchanted creations.

What was once a thread becomes a tether.

### Lore

As Soul Engineers refined the art of bonding, they discovered that stronger spiritual links allowed more information to pass between item and bearer.

Direction became distance.

Presence became awareness.

Many merchants, adventurers, and explorers sought out Artificers capable of creating such bonds, as losing a prized possession became far less likely when it could still be sensed across vast distances.

---

> *"A lost item is only lost when the bond can no longer find it."*  
>  **Soulforger Kaelis Voss**

## Rank 3
### Description

Your mastery of Soul Engineering allows the bond between bearer and item to become truly interactive.

The spiritual tether connecting the two has grown strong enough to guide the item back to its bonded owner when called. No longer limited to awareness and tracking, the bond can now exert influence over the item itself, drawing it across the world through the invisible pathways of the soul.

For many Artificers, this is considered the first truly extraordinary application of Soul Bonding.

---

### Effects

- You may create a **Soul Bond** between a willing creature and a crafted item.
- The item must have been personally crafted by you.
- Creating a Soul Bond requires:
  - **50 MP**
  - **1 Measure of Powdered Crystal**
  - **1 Luminous Soul Stone** (Consumed)
- The bonded creature automatically knows:
  - Whether the item exists.
  - Whether the item is damaged or intact.
  - The general direction of the item.
  - The approximate distance to the item.
- Once every **24 Hours**, the bonded creature may attempt to **Recall** the bonded item.
- Recalling a bonded item costs:
  - **25 MP**
  - **5 SP**
- The item instantly returns to the bonded creature if:
  - It is not currently wielded, worn, or held by another creature.
  - It is within the same plane of existence.
  - No magical effect prevents teleportation or summoning.
- Rank 3 Soul Bonds may be applied to:
  - Mundane Items
  - Minor Enchanted Items
  - Moderate Enchanted Items
  - Charged Items
  - Advanced Gadgets
- Cannot be applied to:
  - Major Enchanted Items
  - Constructs
  - Relics
  - Soul-Bound Items created by another Artificer

---

### Lore

The invention of Soul Recall changed Soul Engineering forever.

Before its discovery, Soul Bonds were viewed primarily as tracking tools. Everything changed when an Artificer named Veloran Ashforge accidentally summoned his stolen prototype across an entire kingdom during a moment of desperation.

Since then, countless Artificers have refined the technique, ensuring that their most treasured creations can always find their way home.

Some scholars claim the item travels through space.

Others believe it follows the soul itself.

Regardless of the truth, the result remains the same.

A bonded creation remembers where it belongs.

---

> *"Distance may separate creator and creation, but the soul remembers the path between them."*  
>  **Soulforger Kaelis Voss**

## Rank 4
### Description

Your understanding of Soul Engineering reaches a level where powerful magical creations can safely sustain a Soul Bond.

The connection between bearer and item becomes exceptionally stable, allowing even heavily enchanted objects to maintain a permanent spiritual link without disrupting their magical properties.

At this stage, Soul Bonds are no longer limited to tracking and recall. The bond becomes a conduit through which item and bearer remain constantly connected, regardless of distance.

The item knows its owner.

And its owner knows the item.

### Lore

Many master Artificers consider Rank 4 the true pinnacle of practical Soul Engineering.

The bond is stable enough to survive powerful enchantments, advanced arcane circuitry, and even artificial souls embedded within constructs. Entire guilds have relied upon Soul Bonded equipment to track valuable assets, recover lost inventions, and maintain control over priceless creations.

Few thieves enjoy discovering that the item they stole knows exactly who it belongs to.

---

> *"Ownership is a legal concept. A Soul Bond is a spiritual one."*  
>  **High Soulforger Kaelis Voss**

The bonds you create transcend ordinary spiritual connections, allowing even the most powerful creations to remain permanently linked to their chosen bearer. Through years of study and experimentation, you have learned how to weave a Soul Bond into legendary works without disrupting their magical or spiritual nature.

The bond becomes more than a tether.

It becomes a living connection between soul and creation.

No matter where the item travels, no matter who possesses it, the bond endures.

### Lore

Only the greatest Soul Engineers ever achieve mastery over Soul Bonding.

Their creations become famous not only for their craftsmanship, but for the enduring connection they share with their owners. Legendary weapons return to their wielders after being lost for decades. Ancient constructs continue serving families generations after their creators have passed.

Many historians believe that some of the world's most famous heirlooms remain intact solely because of the Soul Bonds woven into them.

A masterpiece may outlive its creator.

A Soul Bond ensures it never forgets them.
