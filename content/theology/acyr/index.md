## ⚔️ **Acyr – God of War**

**Titles:**  
The Crimson General, The Iron Sovereign, The Unbroken, Warlord of the Divine, The Flame of Battle

**Pantheon:**  
Pantheon of Creation (Essence of Conflict & Conquest)

**Alignment:**  
Chaotic Neutral — embodies the chaos, fury, and honor of battle; neither wholly destructive nor just

**Symbol:**  
A blazing sword crossed with a broken spear upon a crimson sun — representing glory, loss, and the eternal clash of wills

**Domains:**  
War, Conflict, Strength, Courage, Strategy, Conquest, Honor, Fire of the Heart

**Appearance:**  
Acyr is a towering god clad in ever-shifting armor — each plate forged from the weapons of fallen champions. His eyes burn like molten iron, and his hair flows like smoke from a battlefield pyre.  
His skin bears countless faint scars that glow faintly with ember-light. In his hands he wields **Avarn**, the Sword of Ten Thousand Wars, which hums with the cries of every soul who has ever fought in his name.

When he walks, the air crackles with tension — like the moment before the first sword strike.

**Personality:**  
Acyr is a god of passion, fury, and valor. He embodies the beauty and tragedy of war: the courage it demands, the destruction it brings, and the unity it forges among those who fight for something greater than themselves.  
He respects strength in all forms — not just physical might, but conviction, loyalty, and unyielding spirit.  
To some, he is a noble general; to others, a bloodthirsty warlord. Both are true. Acyr teaches that **conflict is the crucible of growth** — that only through struggle can mortals discover their true nature.

**Mythic Lore:**  
When the first mortals learned to wield weapons, their cries of rage and fear gave birth to Acyr. From their fire, he rose — a god born not of peace, but of passion and will.  
He was the first to lead divine armies in the **War of Dawn**, when the gods of chaos rose against the pantheon. It was Acyr’s sword that cleaved the sky in two, sealing the rift between the realms.  
It is said that every battle fought since then is but an echo of his first warcry.

Acyr does not revel in senseless slaughter — he despises cowardice, treachery, and cruelty. To him, war is sacred: a dance between fate and choice, where mortals prove their worth.

**Worship:**  
Acyr’s temples are few but grand — built in stone and blood. They often serve as barracks, training halls, or places of oath and remembrance.  
Warriors carve their names into his altars before battle, and when they fall, their weapons are burned in his honor.  
He is worshipped by soldiers, generals, mercenaries, and blacksmiths who forge instruments of war.

**Rituals:**

- **The Oath of Iron:** Warriors dedicate their first blade to Acyr, binding themselves to courage and loyalty.
    
- **The Blood Vigil:** Before great battles, worshippers smear their weapons with a drop of their own blood, symbolizing the bond between warrior and weapon.
    
- **The Flame of Remembrance:** A pyre is lit at dusk after every victory or loss, so that Acyr may carry the spirits of the fallen into glory.
    
- **The Challenge Rite:** Duels fought under Acyr’s gaze are considered sacred — their outcome divinely judged.
    

**Relationships:**

- **Auralis – Goddess of Air:** Often his companion in battle; she grants him the winds of war and carries his banners across the skies.
    
- **Elyndra – Goddess of Life:** They stand opposed yet intertwined — she creates what he tests.
    
- **Thanaros – God of Death:** His silent ally; together they define the cycle of combat — one begins the struggle, the other ends it.
    
- **Azrakar – God of the Netherworld:** Rival in philosophy; Acyr sees death as glory, Azrakar as consequence.
    
- **Vaelion – God of Time & Space:** A distant observer who records Acyr’s wars across the ages.
    
- **Sirona – Goddess of Water:** Her calm tempers his rage; sailors and warriors alike pray to both before embarking on dangerous campaigns.
    

**Holy Day:**  
**The Day of Red Dawn** — celebrated on the first day of spring, marking the divine rebirth of conflict after winter’s stillness. Warriors perform ritual duels, smiths craft new blades, and generals light the **Flame of Acyr** to bless their armies with courage and clarity.v
