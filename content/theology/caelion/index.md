# Caelion

## Overview
### ☀️ **God of Summer — Caelion, the Unyielding Sun**

**Titles:** The Bright Sovereign, Keeper of Days  
**Symbol:** A golden disc with four rays  
**Domains:** Growth, Vitality, Sunlight, Strength  
**Depiction:** A tall, bronze-skinned man with eyes of burning gold, carrying a staff shaped like the sun’s rays.  
**Lore:** Caelion governs the height of life’s vigor — crops ripen, rivers run deep, and beasts thrive beneath his light. Yet he is also relentless; too long in his season brings drought and exhaustion. His power is worshipped and feared in equal measure.
