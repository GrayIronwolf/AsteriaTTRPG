# Eriabella

## Overview
**Titles:** The Whispering Queen, Sky-Mother, The Breath Between Worlds, Lady of the Endless Horizon, The Four Winds’ Voice  
**Pantheon:** Pantheon of Creation (Essence Division)  
**Alignment:** Neutral Good  
**Symbol:** A spiral of four winds surrounding a single feather, or a silver swirl encircling a clouded gem  
**Domains:** Air, Wind, Freedom, Music, Travel, Breath of Life, Inspiration

---

### ✨ **Appearance**

Auralis manifests as a **tall, ethereal woman** with **silver-white hair** that flows endlessly, merging into clouds and light mist. Her **skin glows faintly blue**, like dawnlight touching ice, and her **eyes mirror the color of the open sky**, ever shifting between sapphire, gray, and gold.  
She often appears **barefoot upon air**, her robes woven from **cloudlight, sunrise gold, and starlit vapor**. When she moves, the air sings softly; when angered, her voice becomes the gale.  
At her back, **two vast wings of translucent wind** unfurl — invisible save for the way they bend light and ripple the air.

---

### 💨 **Personality**

Auralis is **free-spirited and compassionate**, yet bound by no law but her own. She values **truth, movement, and renewal**.  
She is the **voice of change** — never still, never silent. Gentle and nurturing to those who honor her winds, she becomes swift and merciless toward those who choke the air, enslave others, or defile the skies.  
Her laughter is said to bring gentle breezes to travelers. Her sorrow births storms.

---

### 🕊️ **Mythic Lore**

In the beginning, when the **Primordials exhaled the Breath of Existence**, that sacred wind gave birth to Auralis — **the living embodiment of air and the first breath of life**.  
She wandered the newborn skies, alone, until she met **Vaelion, God of Time and Space**, who taught her the rhythm of the cosmos. Together, they gave the world **seasons, movement, and sound**.  
When mortals were created, Auralis granted them **the first breath**, binding herself to all living things.  
She is said to **carry the final breath of the dying** to the realm of souls, delivering them to the **Goddess of Spirit**, ensuring the eternal cycle continues.  
Legends tell that when a mortal dies peacefully, their last sigh is a whisper of Auralis calling them home.

---

### 🌫️ **Worship**

Auralis is worshipped by **sailors, travelers, musicians, monks, and skyward peoples**.  
Her worshippers build **open sanctuaries** on mountaintops, cliffs, and floating isles where the wind may freely pass.  
Prayers to her begin and end with **a single deep breath**, symbolizing both birth and death.  
Worshippers adorn themselves with feathers, wind charms, and bells that tinkle softly in the breeze.

**Common Prayer:**

> “Breath of the world, wind of my soul — carry me true and free.”

---

### 🌪️ **Rituals**

- **The Breath Offering:** At dawn, followers exhale into the wind, whispering a personal truth or burden to be carried away.
    
- **The Gale Dance:** A sacred dance performed during strong winds to honor her restless spirit.
    
- **Feast of the Four Currents:** Once per season, followers release sky lanterns to represent the four winds carrying hope and renewal.
    
- **Rite of Last Breath:** Priests of Auralis perform this rite at deathbeds, helping guide a soul’s final breath to her care.
    

---

### ⚡ **Relationships**

- **Consort / Counterpart:** _Zephyron_, God of Storms — her wild and untamed brother-consort, representing the chaos of the skies. Together they maintain balance between gentle breeze and raging tempest.
    
- **Ally:** _Vaelion_, God of Time and Space — her teacher and friend, whose eternal rhythm shapes her winds.
    
- **Friendship:** _Celest_, Goddess of the Celestial — their domains intertwine as starlight guides the winds at night.
    
- **Rivalry:** _Sangran_, God of Blood — whose storms of war offend her skies with smoke and screams.
    
- **Respect:** _Goddess of Soul & Spirit_ — Auralis reveres her as the final destination of her sacred winds.
    

---

### ☀️ **Holy Day**

**Day of the Four Winds (First Breeze of Spring)**  
Once each year, when winter’s frost first melts, worshippers gather in open fields and mountain heights.  
They release **hundreds of white feathers or paper birds** into the air while singing the _Hymn of Renewal_.  
It is said that on this day, Auralis’s true voice can be heard in the sky, whispering blessings of life and freedom.
