# Ethona

## Overview
## 🌟 **Celest — The Goddess of the Celestial**

**Titles:**  
_The Starborn Queen_, _Radiant Mother_, _Keeper of the Heavenly Light_, _The Crown of Dawn_

**Pantheon:**  
Pantheon of Essence — One of the Eight Essence Deities  
(Aspect: Light, Stars, Purity, and Divine Order)

**Alignment:**  
Lawful Good / Neutral Good  
(Depends on interpretation — worshipped as both a guide and a judge of purity)

**Symbol:**  
A radiant eight-pointed star wreathed in a silver-gold halo.  
Sometimes depicted as a crown of stars or a sunburst with a moon at its heart.

**Domains:**

- Celestial Light & Radiance
- Order & Harmony
- Hope & Salvation
- The Cosmic Cycle (Balance between stars and void)
- Divine Judgement & Ascension

**Appearance:**  
Celest manifests as a tall, ethereal woman draped in flowing robes of starlight and silk spun from the dawn’s first glow. Her hair gleams silver-white, threaded with constellations, and her eyes contain shifting galaxies. When she speaks, her voice is layered — like multiple harmonized tones from different realms.

**Personality:**  
Calm, majestic, and infinitely patient. Celest embodies **divine grace**, but also **unforgiving radiance** — her light heals the worthy and burns the wicked. She believes in cosmic balance and is known to both bless and banish depending on one’s truth.

**Mythic Lore:**  
It is said that when the **First Light** pierced the void, it was Celest who gave it form — shaping the first stars from her own essence.  
She wove the heavens as a tapestry to separate the worlds of gods and mortals, creating constellations that guide both travelers and fates alike.

Celest is said to have **bound the forces of chaos** into the night sky, imprisoning them as dark stars to maintain order. Yet, when the stars align in rare celestial events, these ancient forces stir — testing her dominion over the heavens.

She shares a complex relationship with **Vaelion**, God of Time & Space. Together they are said to govern the **Cosmic Weave** — Vaelion shaping time and Celest illuminating its path.

**Worship & Temples:**

- Worshipped by paladins, astronomers, and royal bloodlines seeking divine favor.
    
- Her temples are open-roofed observatories where her followers chart stars to interpret divine will.
    
- Priests of Celest wear white and gold, carrying star-forged mirrors or crystal orbs to focus her light.
    

**Holy Days:**

- **The Dawning Crown** — celebrated on the year’s first sunrise, marking new beginnings.
    
- **The Radiant Convergence** — a celestial event where her followers fast under the stars and offer vows of renewal.
