# Ignaros

## Overview
**Titles:**  
The Eternal Flame, The Infernal Forge, The Heart of Fury, Bringer of Dawnfire, Lord of Embers

**Pantheon:**  
Pantheon of Essence

**Alignment:**  
Chaotic Neutral

**Symbol:**  
A blazing sun pierced by a molten sword, or a flame rising from an anvil

**Domains:**  
Fire, Heat, Passion, War, Rebirth, Destruction, Forge, Light

---

### 🔥 **Appearance**

Ignavar appears as a towering figure of **living flame and molten metal**, his body ever-shifting between **blazing armor and raw fire**. His hair burns like a solar corona, and his eyes are white-hot cores of divine fury.  
When he walks, the ground cracks beneath him, and his voice carries the sound of a roaring furnace.

At times he manifests as a **blacksmith cloaked in smoke**, or a **warrior wreathed in cinders**, wielding a hammer forged from the heart of a star.

When calm, his flames glow **gold and amber**, warm and life-giving. When enraged, they turn **white and blue**, capable of incinerating cities.

---

### ⚔️ **Personality**

Ignavar is **impulsive, passionate, and fiercely independent** — the embodiment of both creation and chaos.  
He inspires **artisans, warriors, and revolutionaries**, driving them to act, to make, to change.  
Yet, his temper is infamous — **quick to ignite, slow to fade**. He believes that destruction is merely the first step toward rebirth, that ashes are the soil of new creation.

He despises stagnation and cowardice, viewing inaction as a sin against existence itself. To Ignavar, the world must burn to grow.

---

### 📜 **Mythic Lore**

In the earliest days of Astria, when the world was cold and still, **Tarron** had shaped the bones of the earth but it lay lifeless and frigid.  
Then Ignavar descended — a comet streaking from the heavens, **his impact setting the world ablaze**.  
From his flames, warmth and light entered existence. He forged the sun in the sky as his eternal forge-fire, declaring it the **Heart of Creation**.

He is credited with teaching **mortals the secret of fire**, a gift meant to bring comfort and power — though he warned it could as easily destroy as create.  
Legends say he once loved a **water goddess**, whose calm soothed his fury, but their union shattered — her tears forming the first seas, his grief birthing volcanoes.

It is also told that **every flame in the world is a reflection of his essence**, from the smallest candle to the mightiest inferno.

---

### 🔨 **Worship**

Ignavar is revered by **blacksmiths, warriors, pyromancers, and visionaries** — those who dare to transform the world through heat and passion.  
His temples are usually found near **volcanoes, forges, or battlefields**, adorned with braziers that must never be allowed to go out.  
Clerics of Ignavar wear **charred steel and flame-colored robes**, bearing scars from ritual burns as marks of devotion.

**Common Prayer:**

> “May my heart burn bright, and my will strike true — by the flame that creates and consumes alike.”

---

### 🔥 **Rituals**

- **The Ember Rite:** A yearly ritual where followers walk barefoot over hot coals to renew courage and burn away doubt.
    
- **The Forge Vigil:** Smiths and artificers leave their forge fires burning overnight, praying that Ignavar blesses their craft with brilliance.
    
- **Ash Renewal:** When someone dies, their ashes are spread on sacred ground, symbolizing rebirth through flame.
    
- **Trial by Flame:** A ritual of truth — the accused places a hand over fire; if they are innocent, the fire cools.
    

---

### 🌋 **Relationships**

- **Rivalry:** _Auralis, Goddess of Air_ — her winds fan his flames but also steal his heat; their dances are both beautiful and destructive.
    
- **Balance:** _Tarron, God of Earth_ — where fire rages, earth endures; together they forged the first metals.
    
- **Former Love:** _Undina, Goddess of Water_ — their union birthed both creation and ruin; their encounters still form storms and steam.
    
- **Ally:** _God of Smithing_ — both share the sacred flame of craft and transformation.
    
- **Enemy:** _Malthera, Hellish Goddess_ — though born of similar fires, she twisted his gift into torment.
    

---

### 🔥 **Holy Day**

**Festival of the Burning Heart (Midsummer Solstice)**  
A day when **bonfires blaze across cities and villages**, representing passion, creation, and cleansing.  
Weapons are reforged, vows renewed, and lovers leap through fire as a symbol of rebirth and courage.  
At dawn, the fires are extinguished — and the ashes are spread across fields to bless the coming harvest.
