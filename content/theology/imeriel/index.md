# Imeriel

## Overview
---

🌙 Imriel, the Veiled Mercy

Titles:

The Veiled Mercy

Keeper of the Lantern

The Twilight Judge

The Redeemer of the Damned

The Whisper in the Dark


Alignment: Neutral Good (with Lawful tendencies)
Pantheon: The Pale Dominion
Symbol: A silver lantern with a broken chain wrapped around its handle.
Sacred Colors: Silver, deep violet, and dusk blue.
Animal: The owl and the black cat — watchers of the boundary between life and shadow.


---

🌌 Domains

Redemption • Judgment • Souls • Mercy • Guidance • Twilight

Imriel stands as the bridge between Tobias’s absolute judgment and Nymera’s guiding compassion.
Where others pass sentence, Imriel listens. She is invoked by those who repent, confess, or seek to undo their wrongs before death claims them.

She walks the Veil with a lantern whose flame is said to be forged from the last hope of the condemned.


---

🕯️ Appearance

Imriel appears as a woman cloaked in twilight, her face hidden behind a porcelain mask that shifts between serenity and sorrow.
The silver lantern she carries never burns bright — it glows softly, illuminating only what the bearer needs to see, never what they desire.

When she speaks, her voice echoes like wind through a cathedral: calm, patient, and heavy with understanding.

> “No light is lost, only forgotten.”




---

💫 Personality and Teachings

Imriel is compassion without naivety. She believes all beings — mortal, spirit, or divine — carry both shadow and light.
She does not forgive easily, but she allows the chance to earn forgiveness.

Her creed:

> “Even in darkness, mercy walks beside judgment.”



Virtues: Compassion • Truth • Reflection • Courage • Redemption

Her faith teaches that mercy must be chosen, not assumed. Those who truly repent may still walk a brighter path, but only through trial and acceptance of consequence.


---

🌑 Worshippers and Clergy

Titles: Lanternbearers, Veilwardens, Keepers of Twilight

Temples:

Small sanctuaries built at crossroads, graveyards, and execution grounds.

Always dimly lit, filled with lanterns rather than candles — symbolizing light carried by will, not luxury.

No idols; her followers believe her presence resides in silence and reflection.


Common Rituals:

The Lantern Vigil: Worshippers light a lantern for each sin confessed and let it burn through the night.

The Walk of Silence: Once a year, followers walk barefoot to the edge of a graveyard to symbolize humility before the Veil.

The Whispered Plea: A dying sinner may whisper a final truth to Imriel’s clerics; if deemed sincere, their soul’s path is lightened.



---

🩸 Artifacts

The Lantern of the Veil: Said to guide lost spirits to their rightful realm — heaven or hell — depending on the flame’s hue.

Mask of Reflection: Allows the wearer to see their truest nature, stripped of lies.

Chains of Penance: Forged from redeemed souls; binding them to service until their atonement is fulfilled.



---

⚖️ Relationships

Deity	Relationship

Tobias	Serves as his counterbalance; where Tobias enforces law, Imriel interprets intent.
Vorneth	Deep mutual respect — her mercy begins where his rites end.
Kelriss	Philosophical contrast; he believes all decay is renewal, while she believes some corruption must be purified first.
Seraxis	Opposites bound by necessity — she redeems where he punishes. Their paths cross often in divine contention.
Nymera	Kindred spirits — together they guide the living and dead toward peace.



---

🌙 Legends and Myths

The Tale of the Fallen Priest: A cleric who betrayed his vows was hunted by Seraxis, but Imriel intervened, granting him one final chance. He died protecting the innocent — his soul was taken to her lantern instead of Tobias’s throne.

The Twilight Bargain: During the Age of Blood, Imriel negotiated with Tobias to create the Twilight Veil, a realm where repentant souls may prove their worth before final judgment.

The Lantern’s Last Flame: It is said that if her lantern ever goes out, mercy itself will fade from the world — marking the beginning of the End Cycle.



---

🕯️ Clergy and Followers

Her clergy consists of mediators, healers, and confessors — those who see worth even in the wicked.
They act as confessors for the dying, guides for the lost, and defenders of the misunderstood.

They are often at odds with Seraxis’s Inquisitors, who see mercy as weakness. Yet, both orders are sanctioned by Tobias himself — to ensure that justice and compassion never walk alone.

Common Prayer:

> “Guide my shadow to rest, that my light may walk freely.”



Taboo Acts:

False confession

Mercy without truth

Ignoring a plea for help



---

🔮 Role in the Underworld

Imriel tends the Twilight Veil, a liminal realm of gray mists and endless horizon where souls seek redemption before final judgment.
Here, the unworthy are tested, reliving their choices and confronting their failures until acceptance is achieved.
Those who pass emerge as pale lights, ready for Nymera’s guidance.
