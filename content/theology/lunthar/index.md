# Lunthar

## Overview
**Titles:**  
The Woodwright • The Green Carpenter • The Builder Beneath the Boughs • Hand of Kaelun • The Grainkeeper

**Pantheon:**  
Pantheon of Creation (Lesser God, Artisan Division) — Divine craftsman of wood, builder of sacred halls, and protector of natural artisanship.

**Alignment:**  
Neutral Good — patient, grounded, and harmonious. He values respect for nature’s gifts and the sanctity of sustainable creation.

**Domains:**  
Craftsmanship • Wood • Nature • Architecture • Harmony • Artisanship • Growth

**Symbol:**  
A chisel crossed with a living branch, framed by a wooden ring — representing the union of creation and life.

**Appearance:**  
Lunthar appears as a tall, broad-shouldered man with bark-like skin and veins of sap that glow faintly gold. His hair is a canopy of dark green leaves that shift with the seasons. He wears simple craftsman’s robes stitched with living vines, and his tools — an adze and mallet — hang from his belt, humming softly with divine energy.

**Personality:**  
Gentle but steadfast, Lunthar is a god of patience and process. He believes that true craftsmanship honors the material — shaping, not conquering, it. He is known to whisper inspiration to carpenters, sculptors, and builders who work with care and reverence. Though slow to anger, he becomes a tempest when forests are destroyed for greed.

**Mythic Lore:**  
Born from a fallen spark of **Kaelun’s forge**, Lunthar was shaped from ash and oak. Kaelun, wishing to create balance between metal and living matter, breathed life into that spark — and Lunthar emerged, singing to the trees.  
It was he who first taught mortals to build with wood, to craft homes that breathed, and to understand the soul of a tree before felling it.  
Lunthar built the **Hall of Rings**, a divine workshop grown from an ancient world-tree, where he carves the designs for every wooden creation that will ever exist.

When mortals craft wooden wonders — ships that defy the sea, bridges that never rot, or instruments that sing like birds — it is said that Lunthar’s blessing guides the craftsman’s hand.

**Worship:**  
Carpenters, woodworkers, shipwrights, and builders revere Lunthar. His shrines are simple — often placed in forest clearings, workshops, or within hollowed trees. Offerings include wooden carvings, shavings, or crafted gifts left to “feed the grain.” His clergy, known as **The Oakwrights**, wear robes of green and brown, carrying polished wooden hammers as symbols of devotion.

**Rituals:**

- **The Blessing of the Beam:** Before raising a wooden structure, builders carve Lunthar’s symbol into the main beam for protection.
    
- **The Whispering Grain:** Woodworkers whisper a prayer of gratitude into the first cut of a log to honor the spirit within.
    
- **Festival of Rings (Spring Equinox):** Craftsmen gather to plant a tree for every great wooden work completed that year, symbolizing renewal.
    

**Relationships:**

- **Kaelun, God of Craft and Creation:** His father and eternal teacher. Lunthar honors Kaelun’s perfection but embodies his softer, organic side.
    
- **Sirona, Goddess of Water:** Close ally; her waters nourish the forests and provide the flexibility of life to his creations.
    
- **Auralis, Goddess of Air:** Her breezes shape the forests he loves; their domains intertwine in the harmony of growth.
    
- **Elyndra, Goddess of Writing:** Friend and collaborator; she records his sacred designs and timber rites.
    
- **Goruun, God of Earth:** Occasional rival — Goruun claims stone endures longer, while Lunthar argues that wood lives truer.
    

**Holy Day:**  
**The Day of the Grain (Spring Equinox):**  
A festival of renewal and respect. Artisans and common folk alike carve small wooden tokens — leaves, birds, or hearts — and hang them on trees or doorways for protection and prosperity.
