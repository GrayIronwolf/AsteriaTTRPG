# Lythera

## Overview
## 🕊️ **Lythera – Goddess of Glasswork**

**Titles:**  
The Shimmering Artisan, Lady of Reflections, The Lightbinder, Mistress of Fragile Beauty

**Pantheon:**  
Pantheon of Creation (Crafting and Professions)

**Alignment:**  
Neutral Good — patient, creative, and deeply empathetic; she finds beauty in imperfection and strength in fragility

**Symbol:**  
A glass rose encased in a silver circle, or a droplet of molten glass catching the light of dawn

**Domains:**  
Craftsmanship, Artistry, Light, Reflection, Beauty, Patience, Transformation

**Appearance:**  
Lythera appears as a woman made of translucent glass and living light. Her skin shimmers like crystal under sunlight, and her hair flows in delicate strands that glint with every color of the spectrum.  
Her robes seem to be woven from the reflections of morning dew — ever-shifting, ever-glowing.  
When she walks, the sound of soft chimes follows her, like cooling glass in a kiln. Her eyes are gentle and prismatic, each blink refracting the world into color.

**Personality:**  
Lythera is quiet but unshakably wise. She teaches that creation is an act of love and patience — that even fragile things can hold divine strength.  
She embodies transformation through trial: sand and fire create glass, just as struggle and time create beauty in mortal souls.  
She despises reckless destruction but honors those who rebuild, refine, and find light in loss.

**Mythic Lore:**  
Lythera was born from **the tears of Sirona, Goddess of Water**, when the sea first touched molten stone. From that divine union of water and fire, she rose — radiant and new, embodying the moment beauty is forged through pain.  
In the **Age of Shards**, she walked the mortal realm, teaching artisans to shape glass for both function and wonder. It was said her first mirror captured a glimpse of the heavens, and her first window allowed mortals to see the divine light safely.  
During the **Shattering War**, she forged the _Aegis Prism_, a sacred shield of glass that could reflect any spell or gaze — even that of gods.

**Worship:**  
Lythera is revered by glassblowers, jewelers, artists, architects, and scholars. Her temples are sanctuaries of light, built with stained-glass walls that depict the stories of creation.  
Every color of glass is sacred to her, but clear crystal — untainted by dye — represents the purest form of truth.

**Rituals:**

- **The Furnace Prayer:** Artisans whisper blessings over molten glass before shaping it, asking Lythera to grant steady hands and inspiration.
    
- **Festival of Reflections:** Held on the longest day of the year, mirrors and glass lanterns are hung throughout towns to scatter sunlight — symbolizing the spread of creativity and divine light.
    
- **The Ceremony of Shards:** Broken glass from failed works is gathered and melted together into communal art — representing resilience and rebirth.
    
- **Blessing of the Lens:** Scholars and astronomers consecrate their lenses under her gaze, seeking clarity in thought and sight.
    

**Relationships:**

- **Sirona – Goddess of Water:** Her divine mother; together they share the dance of flow and form.
    
- **Tauren – God of Earth:** Provides her with sand and minerals — their bond is steady and enduring.
    
- **Ignivar – God of Fire:** A rival and muse; his flames both create and endanger her craft.
    
- **Celest – Goddess of the Celestial:** A friend who gifts her light for her glasswork.
    
- **Vaelion – God of Time & Space:** She crafts prisms through which his stars shine more brilliantly.
    

**Holy Day:**  
**Day of Reflections** — celebrated during midsummer when sunlight is strongest. Artisans display their finest works, and priests hang colored glass in the streets to bless homes with inspiration and peace.
