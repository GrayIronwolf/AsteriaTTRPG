# Marvok

## Overview
## Titles:
The Defiler of the Veil
Lord of Undead
The Whispering Lich
The Undying Heretic
The Grave Betrayer


## Symbol: 
A cracked skull entwined with thorned roots and a black flame emerging from its eye sockets.



## 💀 Appearance

Marvok manifests as a towering lich shrouded in smoke and writhing tendrils of spirit-fire.
His skeletal form is draped in ancient, tattered vestments stitched from human and divine remains.
His eyes burn with black and violet flame — souls he has devoured.

His presence causes decay to pause, rot to reverse, and corpses to rise.

> “I did not defy death — I refined it.”




---

## ⚖️ Personality and Teachings

Marvok is brilliant, cold, and manipulative — the embodiment of divine hubris.
He views life and death as flaws in creation — mistakes to be fixed by intellect and power.
He does not kill out of malice, but out of a twisted mercy, believing that to die without enlightenment is the greatest tragedy.

His creed:

> “The dead are tools. Use them.”



His virtues (perverted): Ambition • Knowledge • Mastery • Defiance

To his followers, he preaches that preservation of flesh and bone


---

🩸 Temples and Worship

Titles: Defilers, Lichborn, Fleshwrights, the Pale Scholars

Temples:

Hidden in catacombs, ruinous towers, and crypts of forgotten gods.

Walls are etched with runes that bleed light when undead are near.

His “altars” are bone forges — places where life is twisted into new forms.


Rituals:

The Hollow Rite: A living sacrifice drained of spirit to fuel a lich’s soulstone.

The Feast of Ash: Followers consume ashes of the dead to “inherit their wisdom.”

The Soul Binding: A ritual that chains mortal essence to relics or phylacteries, preserving intellect at the cost of humanity.



---

⚔️ Artifacts

The Black Phylactery: Marvok’s divine core, said to drift in the void between realms. It whispers to those who study death too closely.

The Tome of Endless Night: Contains every necromantic law known to mortals — and the names of those who broke them.

The Grave Key: Opens any gate between life and death — but at the price of one’s reflection.

💀 Relationships

Deity	Relationship

Tobias	Mortal enemy; Tobias cast Marvok from the Pale Dominion for perverting divine law.
Seraxis	Eternal adversary; Seraxis hunts Marvok’s followers as oathbreakers of life itself.
Kelriss	Once mentor and brother; now bitter rivals. Kelriss sees him as a blight that stole his teachings.
Vorneth	Loathes Marvok; every soul Marvok binds denies Vorneth’s rites.
Imriel	The only god who still pities him, whispering prayers for the day he repents — though even she doubts redemption remains.



---

☠️ Legends and Myths

The Sundering of the Veil: Marvok’s experiment to bind the soul to flesh shattered part of the boundary between the living and the dead — birthing necromancy.

The War of Silent Graves: Tobias’s host of Seraxis’s chains fought Marvok’s undead legions for seven years beneath the Veil.

The Weeping Tree: Where Marvok’s first disciple was hanged; it bleeds black sap that animates bones.



---

⚰️ Clergy and Cults

Marvok’s followers are heretics, liches, necromancers, and deathless kings.
They seek knowledge forbidden by gods — and in doing so, bind themselves closer to his will.
Most wear bone masks or preserve their bodies with alchemical elixirs, striving for undeath.

Common Prayer:

> “I will not kneel to dust. I will not fade. I will become what even death fears.”



Taboo Acts:

Burying the dead naturally

Destroying undead creations

Allowing knowledge of the afterlife to be hidden



---

🩸 Role in the Underworld

Marvok exists outside the dominion now — a divine parasite that leeches souls meant for judgment.
He whispers through cracks in the Veil, offering salvation to those who fear death.
To the Pale Dominion, he is the great corruption, the cancer in eternity — and yet, none can fully destroy him, for death itself sustains his existence.


---

🕯️ Influence and Culture

Scholars secretly pray to him before performing forbidden experiments.

Some mortals bear his mark unknowingly when resurrected through unnatural means.

His sigil sometimes appears burned into tombs where necromancy has been performed.
