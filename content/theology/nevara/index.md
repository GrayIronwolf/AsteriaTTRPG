# Nevara

## Overview
### ❄️ **Goddess of Winter — Nevara, the Pale Veil**

**Titles:** The Frostmother, Keeper of Stillness  
**Symbol:** A snowflake encased in crystal  
**Domains:** Rest, Death, Endings, Preservation  
**Depiction:** A calm, pale woman with silver hair and eyes like frozen lakes, draped in frost.  
**Lore:** Nevara brings stillness to the world, freezing rivers and laying her silent mantle across the land. Yet she is not cruel — she guards the seeds of next spring beneath her snow. In her quiet reign, the world dreams. Her worshippers light fires and offer warmth in her honor, knowing all life must rest before renewal.
