# Ravenna

## Overview
## **Goddess of Guiding Souls and Ravens – Morralis, the Veilkeeper**

## Titles: 
- The Raven Queen, 
- Lady of the Final Path
- Whisperer Beyond the Veil
- She Who Watches the Departing  


**Appearance:** Morralis appears as a tall, pale woman draped in feathers of midnight black. Her hair flows like ink across the stars, and her eyes gleam with silver light — windows to the afterlife itself. Wherever she walks, ravens follow, silent and watchful. Her voice is soft and distant, as if carried by a winter breeze.  
**Personality:** Gentle yet implacable, Morralis is the calm between life and death. She neither punishes nor rewards — she guides. Her compassion lies in helping lost souls find peace, yet her presence always carries solemn weight. She mourns for every spirit, even those forgotten.  
**Mythic Lore:**  
When the first mortal died, their soul wandered, lost between realms. The gods wept, for they had created life but not the path beyond it. From the sorrow of the world’s first death rose Morralis — a goddess born of compassion and shadow. She took the form of a raven and flew through the void, finding the soul and guiding it to its destined rest.  
Since then, her ravens have become omens of passage — appearing to watch over the dying or lead the newly dead. It is said that when a raven caws thrice, Morralis is near.  
She holds dominion over Purgatory’s borders, ensuring that no soul is lost to the Gloom. Even the gods of death respect her role, for she does not judge, only delivers.  
**Worshippers:** Priests of the dead, seers, shamans, and those who tend to graves. Warriors and sailors often wear raven feathers for protection on their final journey.  
**Temples & Worship:** Shrines to Morralis are quiet places of reflection, often located near burial grounds or cliffs overlooking the sea. Offerings include black feathers, candles, and silver coins for safe passage.  
**Holy Days:** _The Night of Silent Wings_ — a yearly vigil where all fires are extinguished, and offerings are left under the open sky for the ravens to carry to the goddess




🕊️ Nymera, the Soul Shepherd

Titles:

The Raven Queen

Lady of Passing

Keeper of the Final Path

The Voice Beyond the Veil

Matron of Mourning



## Symbol: 
A black raven clutching a silver thread in its beak, encircled by a ring of feathers.




Nymera is the divine shepherd who guides souls from mortal life to their destined afterlife.
She does not judge (that belongs to Tobias), nor does she redeem (that is Imriel’s role).
Instead, she ensures the journey continues — protecting souls from corruption, devourers, and those who would bind them unnaturally.

When mortals die, they see her ravens first. When gods fall silent, she whispers them home.


---

🌙 Appearance

Nymera appears as a tall, graceful woman with raven-black wings and hair like flowing night.
Her eyes shimmer with distant starlight — endless empathy mingled with inevitability.
Her garments seem woven from the mists of dawn and twilight, and she walks barefoot, leaving glowing prints that fade like memory.

> “Grief is not the end. It is the echo of love calling home.”




---

🕯️ Personality and Teachings

Nymera is gentle but absolute — she mourns every passing, yet never falters in her duty.
To her, death is sacred, not cruel; a continuation rather than a punishment.

Her creed:

> “Every breath is borrowed, every soul must return.”



Virtues: Compassion • Tranquility • Respect for the dead • Acceptance • Hope

Her worshippers are taught to view death not with fear but reverence — as the moment all things rejoin the cosmic rhythm Elyndra weaves.


---

🕊️ Worshippers and Clergy

Titles: Soul Wardens, Ravenkeepers, Shepherds of Rest, Mourning Choirs

Temples:

Found near graveyards, rivers, and mountain passes — places where the veil is thin.

Always filled with quiet music, flowing water, and hundreds of ravens roosting in open rafters.

Each temple holds a Mirror of Rest, a black glass pool that reflects the faces of departed loved ones to those she blesses.


Common Rituals:

The Raven’s Call: A ritual bell is rung three times at every death, to summon Nymera’s attention.

The Last Vigil: Followers stay by the body through the night, singing hymns that help the soul detach peacefully.

Feast of Departed Light: An annual event where candles are floated on rivers, one for each lost loved one.



---

🌌 Artifacts

The Shroud of Whispering Wings: Said to be made from her own feathers; when placed over a dying body, it guarantees a peaceful passing.

The Silver Thread: A counterpart to Elyndra’s loom — used by Nymera to pull lost souls back onto their destined path.

The Raven’s Lantern: Guides spirits through storm and shadow, ensuring no soul is lost to the Nether.



---

⚖️ Relationships

Deity	Relationship

Tobias	Deep partnership; she delivers the souls that he judges. They share silent understanding — law and compassion entwined.
Imriel	Sisters in purpose; Imriel grants redemption, Nymera grants release. They often walk together at twilight.
Elyndra	Reverence; Nymera views her as the architect of the soul’s journey and follows her weave faithfully.
Kelriss	Mutual respect; his cycle of decay leads naturally to her path of peace.
Seraxis	Uncomfortable alliance; she despises his zeal for punishment, though acknowledges the order he enforces.
Marvok	Her eternal enemy; she despises his perversion of death and seeks to undo his necromantic corruption.



---

🕯️ Legends and Myths

The Weeping of the Moon: When Marvok first tore souls from her grasp, Nymera’s tears became the first ravens — messengers who hunt lost spirits.

The River of Sleep: Nymera once walked the mortal realm to calm a plague of restless dead, leading them across a river that now divides life and afterlife.

The Forgotten King: A ruler who defied death begged Nymera for more time. She granted it — but his shadow aged in his place, until it consumed him.



---

🕊️ Clergy and Followers

Her clergy act as guides for the dying, keepers of graves, and counselors to the grieving.
They wear long black robes with silver feathers embroidered down the spine — representing her wings.
Her monks learn the “Language of Wings”, a silent ritual movement used to calm angry spirits.

Common Prayer:

> “Lady of Wings, carry the lost to peace, and grant the living the strength to let go.”



Taboo Acts:

Desecrating graves

Binding or enslaving souls

Mocking or defying death



---

⚰️ Role in the Realms

Nymera’s domain spans all five realms — her wings stretch from Avalon’s mists to the Netherworld’s gates.
She moves unseen across battlefields, whispering peace to the fallen, and appears in dreams to those near death.
In Avalon, she is called the Raven Matron. In Asteria, The Gentle Death. In the Netherworld, she is The Shepherd Between Gates.

It is said that when the seal between realms shattered, her ravens were the first to fly through, seeking to guide the lost back to their rightful resting places — though many were consumed by corruption, birthing the Ravenwraiths.
