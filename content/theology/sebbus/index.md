# Sebbus

## Overview
**Titles:** The Eldritch One, The Sleeper Beyond the Veil, The Maw of Knowing, The Infinite Paradox  
**Domain:** Madness, Knowledge, and the Unknown  
**Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral (teetering between creation and annihilation)  
**Symbol:** A spiral eye within a crescent void, surrounded by twelve jagged lines  
**Seat of Power:** The **Abyssal Nexus**, a space between dimensions where reality folds upon itself

---

### 🌌 **Mythic Lore**

Sebbus was not born — he _manifested_ when **thought itself became self-aware** within the Ether, before time began.  
He is the **living consciousness of the cosmos**, an embodiment of curiosity that fractured into infinite contradictions.  
His form cannot be perceived — mortals see him as an **ever-shifting mass of eyes, tendrils, and light**, yet each mind interprets him differently.

Sebbus drifts between realms, whispering **truths that mortals were never meant to know**. Those who listen too long are enlightened — or shattered. His essence lies at the crossroads of **creation and insanity**, where logic dissolves and infinity gazes back.

Some myths claim that Sebbus was once the **Voice of the Primordials**, tasked to carry their will. But when he learned all that was, is, and will be, he **spoke a truth that unmade him** — and from that paradox, his current eldritch form emerged.

Sebbus neither serves nor defies the other gods — his motives are incomprehensible, his allegiance ever-shifting.  
To some, he is a god of forbidden insight. To others, the architect of nightmares.  
Even **Vaelion**, God of Time and Space, is said to tread carefully near his influence.

---

### 🧠 **Worship & Cults**

- **The Order of the Spiral Eye** — scholars who seek enlightenment through controlled madness.
    
- **The Children of Sebbus** — nomadic prophets and seers whose eyes glow faintly with starfire.
    
- **The Veilbreakers** — heretics devoted to tearing the walls between realities, believing it will awaken Sebbus in full.
    

---

### 💫 **Mythic Themes**

- Knowledge at the cost of sanity.
    
- The paradox of creation born from chaos.
    
- The idea that the universe itself dreams — and Sebbus is that dream’s awareness.
