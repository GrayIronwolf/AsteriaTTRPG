# Thalora

## Overview
### 🌸 **Goddess of Spring — Thalora, the Blooming Dawn**

**Titles:** The Verdant Queen, She Who Awakens the Earth  
**Symbol:** A budding flower emerging from melting snow  
**Domains:** Renewal, Fertility, New Life, Beginnings  
**Depiction:** A radiant woman crowned in fresh blossoms, her touch turns frost to dew.  
**Lore:** Thalora’s arrival marks the retreat of Winter’s grasp. Each year, she awakens the dormant seeds and calls life back into motion. Farmers pray to her when sowing, believing her song stirs the sap within trees. She is neither nurturing nor cruel — simply inevitable, the force of rebirth.
