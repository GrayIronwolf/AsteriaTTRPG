# Tharvax

## Overview
**Titles:** The Chaos Flame, The Unshaped One, The Laughing Maelstrom, Bringer of Upheaval  
**Domain:** Chaos, Change, and Unbound Creation  
**Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral → (leans Chaotic Evil in times of imbalance)  
**Symbol:** A spiral of flame breaking through a circle — representing destruction birthing creation  
**Seat of Power:** The **Maelstrom Nexus**, a swirling plane of molten energy, stormlight, and infinite motion

---

### ⚡ **Mythic Lore**

Before form, before time, there was only motion — and from that motion **Cha-Char-Lav** was born.  
He is the **living embodiment of raw, unrefined energy**, the primordial force that both builds and tears down. When the Primordials wove the foundations of existence, they bound his chaos into the roots of creation — but never fully contained it.

It is said that **every explosion, every earthquake, every storm that breaks the sky** echoes with his laughter.  
He does not destroy out of cruelty — but because _stillness is death_, and _motion is life._  
To Cha-Char-Lav, chaos is the **only true form of balance**, for through destruction comes rebirth.

He often clashes with **Vaelion, God of Time and Space**, whose order seeks to define what Cha-Char-Lav wishes to keep unbound.  
Yet their dance — chaos and structure — sustains the rhythm of the cosmos. When either grows too strong, existence teeters toward collapse.

---

### 🔥 **Worship & Cults**

- **The Ember Choir** — nomadic zealots who believe fire cleanses stagnation and rebirths the world.
    
- **The Children of the Maelstrom** — warbands and anarchist prophets who see chaos as divine freedom.
    
- **The Unbound Circle** — secretive mages who channel chaotic energy to reshape matter and reality.
    

His followers are both feared and revered — bringers of innovation, destruction, and transformation alike.  
They often wear **spiral-marked ash tattoos**, said to mark them as chosen “sparks” of their god.

---

### 🌋 **Mythic Themes**

- Change as a necessary and natural force.
    
- Creation and destruction as two sides of one eternal flame.
    
- The futility of permanence — all things burn, but from the ashes, something new is born.
    

---

### 🜂 **Divine Relationships**

- **Allied With:** Sebbus (Eldritch God) — both thrive in the unknown, though Sebbus’s madness contrasts Cha-Char-Lav’s primal instinct.
    
- **Opposed By:** Vaelion (Time & Space) — the eternal struggle between structure and flux.
    
- **Respected By:** Malthera & Azrakar — the gods of the Netherworld, who understand the role of destruction in cosmic balance.
