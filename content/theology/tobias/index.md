# Tobias

## Overview
Appearance: Pale stone-like skin, eyes like hollow moons, wears a crown of bone-roots
Personality: Cold, fair, but unforgiving — never lies, never bargains

> “All debts are paid in the end.”


## Titles:
The Veiled King
Keeper of the Final Gate
Lord of Stillness
Warden of the Pale Dominion
Judge of Ten Thousand Souls

## Symbol:
A black crown atop chained scales, surrounded by a ring of pale light.

## 🕯️ Appearance & Presence

Tobias appears as a tall, serene figure carved from pale stone. His eyes are hollow voids lit by a cold blue flame. He wears a crown of bone intertwined with roots that seem to grow and decay in cycles. His voice is neither deep nor loud — it echoes softly, as if spoken through a cavern.

When Tobias manifests, sound dies: no wind, no heartbeats, no breath. Even gods grow uneasy in his presence.


---

## ⚖️ Role in the Pantheon

Tobias presides over the Pale Dominion, the vast underworld where souls are weighed and judged. Unlike many death gods, Tobias does not crave worship — he demands order.

He maintains the Veil Accord, the divine law that separates the realms of the living and the dead. Any god, mortal, or spirit that breaks this law (such as necromancers or soul-thieves) earns his wrath.

He is the eldest sibling of Nymera (Goddess of Guiding Souls) and the eternal rival of Marvok (God of Undeath).


---

⚔️ Divine Philosophy

Tobias believes that death is not punishment — it is justice.
His creed:

> “Life is the question. Death is the answer.”



To Tobias, each soul is a weight on the scales of balance. His followers uphold duty, truth, and inevitability — never pity.


---

🕊️ Followers and Clergy

Titles: Grave Wardens, Oathbinders, Keepers of Silence

Temples:

Found only near catacombs, crossroads, or old battlefields.

Decorated with chains, scales, and black lilies.

Always silent; bells are forbidden.


Rituals:

The Final Whisper: a prayer said over dying souls to bind them to peace.

The Candle Oath: an unbreakable vow lit before a single black flame — if broken, Tobias sends a shade to reclaim the oathbreaker’s soul.

Day of the Veil (Fiends’ Hallow): the one day each year mortals may speak with ancestors under Tobias’ watch.



---

💀 Divine Powers and Artifacts

The Crown of Silence: suppresses all magic of resurrection or undeath within a vast radius.

The Chains of Measure: spectral bindings that can drag even divine beings into judgment.

The Obsidian Throne: centerpiece of the Pale Dominion; every soul stands before it to face Tobias’ silent verdict.



---

🕰️ Mythic History

Tobias was once a Celestial of Avalon, guardian of balance between creation and entropy. When mortals were born and death followed, he descended willingly into the shadow realm to ensure the cycle remained just.

He built the first underworld — The Pale Dominion — from fragments of stars that had died. From there, he carved the Veil itself and chained Death to Law.

It’s said he does not sleep, for every second, a soul is judged somewhere in the cosmos.

⚖️ Relationships

Deity	Relationship

Nymera	Sister, soul guide — his compassion through her voice.
Vorneth	Priest and servant, conducts mortal rituals in his name.
Kelriss	Ally; Tobias allows the Bone-Sower to reclaim flesh for the earth.
Seraxis	Right hand; delivers judgment to those Tobias condemns.
Elyndra	Occasional opponent — she believes in mercy and rebirth, he believes in balance.
Marvok	Greatest enemy. Tobias cast him from the Dominion for raising the dead.
