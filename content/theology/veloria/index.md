# Veloria

## Overview
**Titles:**  
The Loomkeeper, Weaver of Worlds, Lady of Threads, The Soft-Spoken Matron

**Pantheon:**  
Pantheon of Creation (Crafting & Professions)

**Alignment:**  
Lawful Neutral — patient, methodical, and wise, she values order and rhythm above all. She weaves both joy and sorrow into the grand tapestry of existence.

**Symbol:**  
A golden loom with silver threads forming an infinity pattern, or a spindle encircled by three entwined strands

**Domains:**  
Crafting, Fabric, Destiny, Creation, Patience, Community, Balance

**Appearance:**  
Mythera appears as a calm and graceful woman cloaked in an ever-changing robe of woven light and shadow.  
Her hair falls in long braids that shimmer like silk in candlelight, and her eyes are like polished amber threads — warm, steady, and knowing.  
The loom she carries hums with divine resonance, its threads glowing faintly as they weave unseen connections across the world.

**Personality:**  
Mythera is serene but unyielding. Every thread, no matter how small, has meaning in her eyes.  
She rarely intervenes directly, believing that lives must intertwine naturally — yet when she does act, her touch ripples subtly through the fates of many.  
To her, creation is sacred meditation; every act of weaving is a prayer, every garment a fragment of the divine pattern.

**Mythic Lore:**  
It is said Mythera was born from the **first breath of wind through the threads of dawn**, when the gods clothed the newborn world. She spun the **Mantle of the Ages**, the divine tapestry recording all of creation’s history — every event, life, and soul woven into its endless threads.  
During the **Age of Sundering**, when fate itself was torn apart by warring gods, Mythera rewove the strands of time, restoring the flow of destiny at great cost to her own power.  
Her loom, **The Weave Eternal**, is said to hang between the realms of gods and mortals, where each thread represents a living soul.

**Worship:**  
Weavers, tailors, storytellers, and even scholars honor Mythera. Her worship extends to families and guilds who see her as the divine matron of unity and structure.  
Her temples often double as **guild halls or community centers**, filled with looms, fabrics, and murals of interlocking threads — symbolizing the ties between all living beings.

**Rituals:**

- **The Spindle Blessing:** Artisans spin their first thread of the season while whispering prayers to Mythera for guidance and stability.
    
- **The Loom Festival:** Once a year, great tapestries are woven collaboratively to tell the year’s story, then hung in the temples.
    
- **Threading of the Bonds:** Newlyweds or sworn allies exchange braided cords blessed by her clergy, representing unbreakable unity.
    
- **Weaving the Veil:** When a soul departs, mourners weave small tokens of fabric to help guide the spirit through the Loom of Eternity.
    

**Relationships:**

- **Lythera – Goddess of Glasswork:** A close friend and artistic sister; their crafts often intertwine in divine works of beauty.
    
- **Vaelion – God of Time & Space:** She works with him to weave the fabric of reality itself.
    
- **Malthera – Hellish Goddess:** A wary respect; Mythera understands that even dark threads are necessary in the tapestry.
    
- **Celest – Goddess of the Celestial:** Her partner in light; together they weave the dawn’s first radiance into the sky.
    
- **The Fates (if they exist in your world):** Often depicted as her daughters or apprentices.
    

**Holy Day:**  
**Festival of Threads** — celebrated in early spring, marking renewal and the start of new weaving seasons. Communities hang colorful ribbons and woven banners from rooftops to symbolize their bonds and shared destinies.
